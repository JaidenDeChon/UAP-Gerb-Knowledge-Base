The Defense Advanced Research Center (DARC) — not to be confused with [[DARPA]] (Defense Advanced Research Projects Agency) — is an alleged clandestine underground facility located between [[Area 51]] Groom Lake and the Nevada Test Site. According to [[Edgar Fouché]], the DARC was a minimum 10-story underground installation where he worked in 1979 with advanced digital technologies and gained knowledge of human-made reverse-engineered craft.

The DARC's origins can be traced through declassified ARPA documents. A 1973 historical evaluation of ARPA's R&D management noted a Department of Defense directive proposing the transfer of the Army Ballistic Missile Agency into a Defense Advanced Research Center, linking the proposed institution to the Jet Propulsion Laboratory. The Army Ballistic Missile Agency was commanded by [[Wernher von Braun]], the former Nazi V-2 rocket scientist transferred to the US under [[Operation Paperclip]].

Fouché was recruited to Groom Lake and the DARC from his station at [[Edwards Air Force Base]] Jet Propulsion Laboratory. According to the transcript, [[EG&G]] was involved in the construction and operations of the DARC facility. The center's connection to both the [[Strategic Defense Initiative (SDI)]] and UFO legacy programs positions it as a key node in the alleged network of underground facilities used for reverse engineering non-human technology.
