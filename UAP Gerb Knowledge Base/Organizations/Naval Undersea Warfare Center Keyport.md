Naval Undersea Warfare Center Keyport (NUWC Keyport) is a U.S. Navy research, testing, and fleet-support facility in Keyport, Washington, on the Kitsap Peninsula. It operates under [[Naval Sea Systems Command]] (NAVSEA), as does its sister command, NUWC Newport. NUWC facilities are the undersea counterpart to the Naval Surface Warfare Center locations and work on submarine and torpedo systems, undersea sensors, and related test and evaluation. Gerb identifies NUWC Keyport as a supporting site for an alleged [[MITRE Corporation|MITRE]]-managed program to reverse-engineer UFO technology, a program that by his account traces back to material recovered at the 1947 [[Roswell Crash]].

## Alleged Role in MITRE Legacy Programs

Gerb alleges that NUWC Keyport handles data processing within an integrated Navy triage that supports the reverse-engineering program. In his account, [[Naval Surface Warfare Center Crane]] and [[Naval Surface Warfare Center Dahlgren]] hold and analyze the hardware, and [[Naval Air Station Patuxent River]] is the overall integration node for data, hardware, and aircraft.
