Naval Undersea Warfare Center Keyport (NUWC Keyport) is a U.S. Navy research, testing, and fleet-support facility located in Keyport, Washington, on the Kitsap Peninsula, operating under [[Naval Sea Systems Command]] (NAVSEA) alongside its sister command, NUWC Newport. NUWC facilities are the undersea counterpart to Naval Surface Warfare Center locations, focused on submarine and torpedo systems, undersea sensors, and related test and evaluation work. In UAP Gerb's investigative framework, NUWC Keyport is identified as a supporting site for an alleged [[MITRE Corporation|MITRE]]-managed UFO technology reverse-engineering program tracing to material recovered at the 1947 [[Roswell Crash]].

## Alleged Role in MITRE Legacy Programs

UAP Gerb alleges that NUWC Keyport plays the data-processing role within an integrated Navy triage supporting the reverse-engineering program, alongside [[Naval Surface Warfare Center Crane]] and [[Naval Surface Warfare Center Dahlgren]] (hardware holding and analysis) and [[Naval Air Station Patuxent River]] (overall integration node for data, hardware, and aircraft).
