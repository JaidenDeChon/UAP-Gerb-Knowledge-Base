Radiance Technologies is an American defense contractor headquartered in Huntsville, Alabama. It performs engineering, intelligence, reverse-engineering and technology development work for the Department of Defense and the intelligence community.

## Alleged UAP Involvement

In 2023 attorney [[Daniel Sheehan]] publicly named Radiance Technologies as a contractor involved in UAP reverse engineering, claiming that its prompt global strike work drew on reverse-engineered UAP technology for hypersonic missile development. In the same period Sheehan named Radiance among the private interests he said were lobbying against the UAP Disclosure Act of 2023.

In *[[Off-World Technologies Division – UAP Technology Reverse Engineering]]*, UAP Gerb mentions Sheehan's claim alongside a 2023 hypersonic technology transfer between [[Naval Surface Warfare Center Crane]], [[Sandia National Laboratories]] and other participants. The host says there is probably not much of a connection, but finds it notable that [[SAIC]] and Sandia are tied both to each other and to Crane's hypersonics work.
