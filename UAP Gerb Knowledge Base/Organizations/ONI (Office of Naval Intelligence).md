The Office of Naval Intelligence (ONI) is the United States Navy's intelligence agency responsible for maritime intelligence collection, analysis, and counterintelligence operations. ONI is the oldest continuously operating intelligence service in the U.S. military, established in 1882.

## Alleged UAP Task Force Administration

The [[UAP Task Force]], established in August 2020, operated under the Office of Naval Intelligence, placing ONI at the organizational center of recent official U.S. government UAP investigations. This positioning reflects the Navy's prominent role in modern UAP encounters and documentation, including the 2004 USS Nimitz incident and the 2019 series of encounters captured in officially released footage.

## Connection to Alleged Leaked Document

An alleged leaked classified document that surfaced online in 2021-2022 lists ONI alongside [[NSA]], [[DHS]], and DOD SAP-cleared experts as joint contributors to UAP behavioral data analysis. The document, purportedly a section from a classified appendix of a [[UAP Task Force]] report, identifies ONI as one of the "joint elements" examining 1,292 UFO cases. If authentic, this would indicate ONI's participation in comprehensive cross-agency UAP analysis beyond its administrative role in the task force itself.
