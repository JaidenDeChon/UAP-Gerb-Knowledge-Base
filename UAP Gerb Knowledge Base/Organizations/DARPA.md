The Defense Advanced Research Projects Agency (DARPA) is the research and development agency of the U.S. Department of Defense. It reports through the Under Secretary of Defense for Research and Engineering (USD R&E) and is tasked with developing emerging and advanced technologies for military use. DARPA is widely credited with foundational contributions to technologies including the internet, and it is the Pentagon's premier agency for high-risk, high-reward research intended to prevent and create technological surprise.
