The Air Force Test Center (AFTC), formerly the Air Force Flight Test Center (AFFTC), is the [[Air Force Materiel Command (AFMC)]] center headquartered at [[Edwards Air Force Base]], California. It conducts developmental and follow-on test and evaluation of manned and unmanned aircraft and related avionics, flight controls, munitions and weapon systems, and it has flight-tested every aircraft in the Army Air Forces and Air Force inventory since World War II. The [[412th Test Wing]] carries out its flying mission at Edwards, and the center also operates the [[Air Force Test Pilot School]]. AFTC is one of AFMC's six centers; the others include the [[Air Force Research Laboratory]] and the [[Air Force Life Cycle Management Center (AFLCMC)]].

He says pilots from the Test Pilot School flew these craft both manned and unmanned. Gerb's central thesis is that AFTC, the Test Pilot School, AFRL and the 412th Test Wing are the focal point of active Air Force reverse engineering. In his account they work across the [[Nevada Test and Training Range (NTR)]], the [[Utah Test and Training Range]] and Edwards, and draw on [[Antelope Valley]] contractors at [[Air Force Plant 42]]. He calls this test and evaluation portfolio the [[Hidden Wing]]. UAP Gerb's timeline video separately alleges that a 2009-2012 "Go Public" campaign was conceived and dropped within this structure.
