The Air Force Test Center (AFTC), formerly the Air Force Flight Test Center (AFFTC), is the [[Air Force Materiel Command (AFMC)]] center headquartered at [[Edwards Air Force Base]], California. It conducts developmental and follow-on test and evaluation of manned and unmanned aircraft and related avionics, flight controls, munitions and weapon systems, and it has flight-tested every aircraft in the Army Air Forces and Air Force inventory since World War II. Its flying mission at Edwards is carried out by the [[412th Test Wing]], and it operates the [[Air Force Test Pilot School]]. It is one of AFMC's six centers, with the [[Air Force Research Laboratory]] and the [[Air Force Life Cycle Management Center (AFLCMC)]].
