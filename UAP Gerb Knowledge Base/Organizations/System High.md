System High Corporation is a private security and counterintelligence services firm founded in 2005 and headquartered in Chantilly, Virginia. Since 2012 it has held a multi-year security-services contract with [[DARPA Security and Intelligence Directorate (SID)|DARPA's Security and Intelligence Directorate]] (SID). The contract has been renewed in later award cycles, including a five-year renewal in January 2022 that was the company's third consecutive SID award. A further contract modification in 2025 brought the total value of the relationship to roughly $124 million. Outside DARPA, System High also holds Indefinite Delivery/Indefinite Quantity (IDIQ) contract vehicles with the U.S. Navy and the Missile Defense Agency (MDA). The company describes its mission as delivering "the most advanced protection and security solutions to secure and strengthen critical missions, programs, operations, and intelligence activities."

Rob Howe, a 20-year U.S. Air Force veteran and former security chief for the F-35 program office, joined System High in 2013. He was the company's President from approximately 2018 until he stepped down from that role in January 2025.
