The Center for Naval Analyses (CNA) is a federally funded research and development center (FFRDC) for the United States Navy and Marine Corps. It provides in-depth analysis of complex problems in naval operations, force structure, readiness, and strategy, and proposes solutions to them. As an FFRDC, CNA is an independent nonprofit with privileged access to classified military data and operations.

## Hunter Warrior Analysis

CNA analyzed the 1997 [[Hunter Warrior Advanced Warfighting Experiment]], held at [[Marine Corps Air Ground Combat Center 29 Palms]], and published its findings. Its report documented the experiment's objectives, execution, and results across its three phases of operations. The work gave CNA researchers embedded access to the exercise, including its communications networks, force disposition data, and operational outcomes. As a result, CNA is one of the few organizations with a comprehensive documented record of what happened during Hunter Warrior, including the naval adjunct operations that [[Rodrik Castle]]'s encounter may have been connected to.
