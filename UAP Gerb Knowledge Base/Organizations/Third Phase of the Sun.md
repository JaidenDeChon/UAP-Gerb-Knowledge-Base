Third Phase of the Sun is a website dedicated to documenting and analyzing UFO sightings, alleged alien encounters, and UAP-related news. The site serves as both an aggregator of UFO footage from various sources and a platform for commentary on significant cases in the UAP research community.

## Metapod Coverage

On September 11, 2015, just five days after the original sighting and four days after Modesto submitted the footage to [[UFO VNI]], Third Phase of the Sun published a post detailing the Metapod footage and presenting correspondence between UFO VNI and Modesto. This rapid coverage helped amplify awareness of the video within the broader UAP research community.

Some viewers, Third Phase of the Sun among them, say they can make out a bean-shaped figure resting inside the craft's transparent front face. Gerb disagrees, attributing it to sunlight reflecting off the transparent material.

## Role in UAP Documentation

Third Phase of the Sun represents the type of independent media platforms that serve as intermediaries between witnesses, original footage publishers, and the broader public interested in UAP phenomena. While not a scientific research organization, such sites play a role in preserving case documentation and facilitating discussion within the UFO research community.
