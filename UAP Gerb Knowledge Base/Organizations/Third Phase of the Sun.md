Third Phase of the Sun is a website dedicated to documenting and analyzing UFO sightings, alleged alien encounters, and UAP-related news. The site collects UFO footage from various sources and publishes commentary on significant cases in the UAP research community.

## Metapod Coverage

On September 11, 2015, just five days after the original sighting and four days after Modesto submitted the footage to [[UFO VNI]], Third Phase of the Sun published a post detailing the Metapod footage and presenting correspondence between UFO VNI and Modesto. The quick coverage helped spread word of the video through the wider UAP research community.

Some viewers, Third Phase of the Sun among them, say they can make out a bean-shaped figure resting inside the craft's transparent front face. Gerb disagrees, attributing it to sunlight reflecting off the transparent material.

## Role in UAP Documentation

Third Phase of the Sun is one of the independent media sites that sit between witnesses, those who first publish the footage, and the wider public interested in UAP phenomena. Such sites are not scientific research organizations, but they help preserve case records and host discussion in the UFO research community.
