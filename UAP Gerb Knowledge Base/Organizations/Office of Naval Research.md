The Office of Naval Research (ONR) is the United States Navy's primary organization for coordinating, executing, and promoting the science and technology programs of the Navy and Marine Corps. Established in 1946, it funds basic and applied research across a broad range of scientific disciplines with both immediate military applications and long-term strategic value. In UAP research, the ONR is significant as the alleged final recipient and routing authority for non-human craft recovered from US waters and ocean floors before those craft are transferred to defense contractors for material exploitation.

## Role in UAP Legacy Programs

Christopher Sharp of *Liberation Times* reported in September 2024 — based on sources he identified as facing serious personal risk — that undersea craft recovered through CIA, NURO, and SOCOM operations are "transferred to the Office of Naval Research, which subsequently hands them over to defense contractors for detailed analysis." This characterization positions the ONR as the institutional bridge between active recovery operations and the longer-term material exploitation pipeline.

UAP Gerb cites this as consistent with the pattern he has documented across Army and Air Force legacy programs, where a government RDT&E institution acts as an intermediary between the operational recovery apparatus and private-sector contractors. The ONR would ensure that access to materials remains within controlled, need-to-know channels before contractor personnel gain access to specific components or samples.
