Referenced as a parallel example of turf battles between CIA and Navy over control of a classified reconnaissance agency

Joint intelligence program; CIA DS&T was one of its founding agencies; Inman reportedly served as a director

Classified black program focused on undersea reconnaissance and technology acquisition, directed by Inman starting in 1974

Operated submarines Halibut and Seawolf used to transport top secret equipment and industry specialists for clandestine undersea missions

Secretly created in 1969 to consolidate reconnaissance between CIA and Navy; still classified; Inman served as director
