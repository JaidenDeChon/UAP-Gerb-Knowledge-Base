The Directorate of Air Force Test and Evaluation (designated AF/TE) is the Air Staff office at Headquarters Air Force whose director is the senior test executive for both the Air Force and the Space Force. Under Headquarters Air Force Mission Directive 1-52 (20 April 2021), it is the chief test and evaluation (T&E) adviser to Department of the Air Force leadership. It develops T&E policy and guidance, advocates for test resources, and resolves developmental and operational test issues. It also holds authority, delegated to the Secretary of the Air Force under DoD Directive 3200.11, over T&E activities at the Air Force's [[Major Range and Test Facility Bases (MRTFBs)|major range and test facility bases]]. It is separate from [[SAFAQR (Science, Technology and Engineering)|SAF/AQR]] in the acquisition secretariat but works closely with [[SAFAQ|SAF/AQ]]. The transcript of Gerb's video spells the office's name as "AFTTE".

## Subdivisions

- **AF/TEP (Policy, Programs and Resources Division):** the T&E policy focal point for testing by the [[Air Force Operational Test and Evaluation Center (AFOTEC)]], the major commands, Space Force field commands and field operating agencies.
- **AF/TEZ (Special Programs Division):** represents Air Force T&E to the Special Programs Review Group, oversees all T&E special access programs and deals heavily with foreign material. The mission directive tasks AF/TE with managing the life cycle of foreign material acquisition, overseeing exploitation of foreign aerospace weapon systems and components, and directing foreign material program execution.

## Role in the Hidden Wing thesis

In *The Hidden Wing*, Gerb calls AF/TE "the tip of the spear" for Air Force T&E and argues that its programs include reverse engineering and derivation of non-human technologies. He also suggests that "foreign material" may cover material of non-human origin. His strongest claim is a personal one. He says he has held the military records of a witness named Ed, a retired officer who served on a T&E program for reverse-engineered vehicles at the [[412th Test Wing]], and that Ed's office of primary responsibility was AF/TE. In his closing remarks he calls AF/TE the real top of the chain of command for hands-on T&E in the [[Hidden Wing]]. He adds that the directors who set up its infrastructure would be worth examining, and names one of them only by the surname Clark. [[Randall G. Walden]] was director of AF/TE before leading the [[Rapid Capabilities Office (RCO)]].
