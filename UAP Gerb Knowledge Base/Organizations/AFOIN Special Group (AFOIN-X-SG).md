AFOIN-X-SG is the office symbol on a letter dated April 24, 1952, sent to the US Air Force's Air Technical Intelligence Center. The letter forwarded nine reports of submarines making contact with UFOs, specifically flying discs, which had been compiled with the help of the [[Office of Naval Intelligence]]. AFOIN is the Air Force Office of Intelligence. Gerb reads the "SG" suffix as "special group" and argues that the office was likely one of the US government's genuinely secret UFO investigation bodies, working in parallel with [[Project Blue Book]].

## The 1952 Letter

The letter was addressed to a Colonel Dunn and dealt with a possible connection between submarine contacts and flying discs. The nine attached reports are not available.

## A Parallel Investigation

Gerb supports this reading with statements by [[Edward J. Ruppelt]], director of [[Project Grudge]] and Project Blue Book. Ruppelt said AFOIN was conducting a more complete UFO investigation, parallel to Blue Book, in connection with [[Project Stork]], a project run by [[Battelle Memorial Institute]] that was uncovered later. He named two AFOIN offices doing UFO-related work: the technical capabilities branch and the collection control branch.

In 1979 the Computer UFO Network (CUFON) filed Freedom of Information Act requests for information on the special group. Every request was denied under executive order, on the grounds that the information had to stay classified for national security.
