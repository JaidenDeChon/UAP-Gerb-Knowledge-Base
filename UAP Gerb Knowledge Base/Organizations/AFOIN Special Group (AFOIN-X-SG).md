AFOIN-X-SG is the office symbol on an April 24, 1952 letter to the US Air Force's Air Technical Intelligence Center that forwarded nine reports of submarine contacts with UFOs, specifically flying discs, compiled with the help of the [[Office of Naval Intelligence]]. AFOIN is the Air Force Office of Intelligence; UAP Gerb reads the "SG" suffix as "special group" and argues the office was likely one of the US government's genuinely secret UFO investigation bodies, working in parallel with [[Project Blue Book]].

## The 1952 Letter

The letter, addressed to a Colonel Dunn, concerned the possible connection between submarine contacts and flying discs. The nine attached reports are not available.

## A Parallel Investigation

UAP Gerb supports his reading with statements by [[Edward J. Ruppelt]], director of [[Project Grudge]] and Project Blue Book, who said AFOIN was conducting a more complete UFO investigation parallel to Blue Book in connection with the later-uncovered [[Project Stork]] run by [[Battelle Memorial Institute]]. Ruppelt named AFOIN's technical capabilities branch and collection control branch as offices doing UFO-related work.

In 1979 the Computer UFO Network (CUFON) filed Freedom of Information Act requests for information on the special group. All were denied under executive order on the grounds that classification had to be maintained for national security.
