The UAP Caucus is a legislative group focused on UAP (Unidentified Anomalous Phenomena) transparency and disclosure, backed by members of the United States Congress. The caucus coordinates Congressional representatives who want to investigate UAP programs, push for the declassification of UAP-related materials, and bring UAP crash retrieval incidents into official Congressional hearings and the public record.

## Membership

As of 2024, the UAP Caucus included the following Congressional representatives:

- Representative [[Tim Burchett]] (R-TN)
- Representative [[Anna Paulina Luna]] (R-FL)
- Representative [[Eric Burlison]] (R-MO)
- Representative [[Jared Moskowitz]] (D-FL)

Its membership shows bipartisan interest in UAP transparency and in oversight of alleged legacy programs.

## Role in Bolivia UFO Crash Investigation

The UAP Caucus brought the 1978 Bolivia UFO crash to public attention, citing it as one of several historical crash retrieval events that deserve Congressional investigation. In the [[1978 Bolivia UFO Crash|Bolivia incident]], thousands of witnesses saw a cylindrical object crash into a mountainside near El Taire, Bolivia. US Air Force personnel were involved, along with [[Project Moon Dust]], the classified crash retrieval program. Documents obtained through FOIA show that Secretary of State [[Cyrus Vance]] contacted "appropriate government agencies" about the crash, and those agencies were later confirmed to be Moon Dust. The UAP Caucus has pointed to the case as documentary evidence that the US government took part in foreign UFO crash retrieval operations.

## Legislative Initiatives

The UAP Caucus has supported and promoted legislative efforts to mandate UAP disclosure, including backing provisions in the National Defense Authorization Act (NDAA) that would require declassification of UAP-related records and establish mechanisms for whistleblower protection.

The caucus has also worked to draw attention to testimony from UAP whistleblowers such as [[David Grusch]], and to coordinate Congressional hearings that bring UAP evidence into the official record.

## Agenda and Objectives

The UAP Caucus's stated objectives are to:

- Mandate transparency and declassification of historical UAP programs
- Investigate alleged crash retrieval and reverse engineering operations
- Provide Congressional oversight of UAP-related Special Access Programs
- Protect UAP whistleblowers from retaliation
- Bring credible UAP incidents (including historical crashes such as Bolivia, [[Roswell]], and others) into formal Congressional inquiry
