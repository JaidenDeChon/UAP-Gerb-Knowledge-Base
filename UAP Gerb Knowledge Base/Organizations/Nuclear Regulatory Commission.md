The Nuclear Regulatory Commission (NRC) is the independent United States federal agency that licenses and regulates civilian nuclear power plants and the civilian use of nuclear materials, including the security of nuclear facilities. It was created by the Energy Reorganization Act of 1974, which abolished the [[Atomic Energy Commission]] (AEC) and divided its functions: regulation passed to the NRC, and weapons, research and development to the agencies that became the [[Department of Energy]] (DOE).
