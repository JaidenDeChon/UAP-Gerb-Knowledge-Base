The National Reconnaissance Office (NRO) is the US agency that develops, acquires, launches and operates the nation's intelligence satellites and their ground systems, supplying signals, imagery and measurement intelligence to both the Department of Defense and the Intelligence Community. It is one of the "big six" US intelligence agencies, and its director answers to both the Secretary of Defense and the Director of National Intelligence. Founded in 1961 and kept secret for 31 years, it was officially declassified only in September 1992. In UAP research, and above all in UAP Gerb's 2025 investigation, the NRO is argued to be a central but overlooked administrator of the alleged UFO legacy programs.

## History

The NRO grew out of the need to consolidate Air Force and CIA reconnaissance after the Soviet Union shot down a U-2 spy plane in May 1960. President [[Dwight D. Eisenhower]] turned to his science advisers and CIA director Allen Dulles, and the office was established in 1961 with co-directors: Air Force Under Secretary Joseph V. Charyk and CIA officer [[Richard Bissell Jr.]]. By 1962 it had settled into its joint role, with a director appointed by the Secretary of Defense and a deputy director from the CIA.

The office immediately took over the CIA's [[Project Corona|Corona]] film-return satellites (first flown in 1960, declassified in 1995). In 1962 it flew the A-12 Oxcart with the CIA and launched the Poppy signals-intelligence satellites with the Naval Research Laboratory; in 1964 the Quill satellite returned the first radar imagery from orbit. In 1976 it launched the KH-11 Kennen, the first "keyhole" satellite to transmit imagery electronically in near real time. By the 1980s, according to UAP Gerb, keyhole satellites could resolve objects less than 12 inches long.

Until 1992 the NRO was organized into four programs: Program A (Air Force satellites), Program B (CIA [[CIA Directorate of Science and Technology|Directorate of Science and Technology]] satellites, with its ground station at [[Pine Gap, Australia]]), Program C (Navy satellites) and Program D (CIA reconnaissance aircraft such as the A-12 and SR-71). After declassification these were replaced by functional directorates. Its sister agency, the still-classified [[National Underwater Reconnaissance Office]], was created in 1969 and is funded through the same National Reconnaissance Program.

The NRO's workforce is a hybrid of cadre civilians, government civilians from other agencies and military personnel, and a 1996 congressional appraisal said its work is done primarily by contractors while it receives the highest funding of any program in the community. Soon after declassification, Senate Select Committee on Intelligence investigations led by Senator Richard Bryan uncovered an unreported headquarters cost overrun and, in 1995, $1.7-3.7 billion in carryover ("forward") funding; the director and deputy director were then removed by DCI [[John Deutch]]. Today it favors "proliferated architecture", launching many small satellites on partners such as SpaceX and United Launch Alliance; in October 2024 its director said 100 satellites had been launched between June 2023 and December 2024.

## Alleged UAP Involvement

UAP Gerb argues the NRO plays three roles in the legacy programs:

- **Surveillance.** Its constellations and the AI system [[NRO Sentient]] allegedly detect UFOs and route anomalous data away from ordinary analysts' feeds to cleared personnel. Sentient flagged a possible tic-tac-shaped object in May 2021, and the NRO consulted with the UAP Task Force that year, although in 2015 it had said UFOs were outside its responsibilities. Gerb believes Sentient is a primary resource of the [[Immaculate Constellation (IMCON)]] program, which he stresses is a reactive surveillance program and not itself a legacy program.
- **Crash retrieval.** Gerb alleges the NRO is the umbrella authority of a siloed crash-retrieval task force using parallel tier 1 units, [[160th Special Operations Aviation Regiment|160th SOAR]] transport and Department of Energy teams such as [[NEST (Nuclear Emergency Support Team)|NEST]]. An anonymous witness who says he served on a rapid reaction team at the [[Nevada National Security Site (Nevada Test Site)]] described briefing binders marked "Joint Operations Task Force, NRO" and an ID badge bearing the NRO's name. [[Steve Wilson|Colonel Steve Wilson]] earlier claimed an NRO "Delta" team retrieved UFOs under [[Project Pounce]].
- **Operating derivative craft.** Gerb argues the NRO would be among the first agencies to use [[Alien Reproduction Vehicle (ARV)|alien reproduction vehicles]] for reconnaissance in denied airspace, citing [[Edgar Fouche]]'s claim that the TR-3B was operated by the NRO, NSA and CIA.

Older claims tie the office to the subject from its founding. [[Albert Bruce Collins]] asserted in 1990 that the NRO was created because satellite photographs showed orbiting spacecraft of unknown origin, and [[Harold Malmgren]] says Richard Bissell confirmed the 1933 Magenta crash to him.
