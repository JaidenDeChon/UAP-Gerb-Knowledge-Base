The Office of Naval Intelligence (ONI), founded in 1882, is the oldest continuously operating intelligence agency in the United States. It is the U.S. Navy's primary intelligence arm and sits within both the Department of Defense and the wider U.S. intelligence community. Its stated mission is to provide full-spectrum maritime intelligence: analyzing, tracking, and monitoring foreign naval capabilities and vessels in support of fleet operations and the security of the seas. Gerb identifies ONI, alongside the Office of the Secretary of the Navy, as one of the most critical Navy-side administrative parts of an alleged [[MITRE Corporation|MITRE]]-managed program that reverse-engineers UFO technology and monitors USOs.

## Role in Alleged MITRE Legacy Programs

According to Gerb, ONI has been one of the primary Navy mission partners and program administrators for two alleged programs since MITRE's founding in 1958, working together with [[Naval Air Systems Command]] and, to a lesser extent, [[Naval Sea Systems Command]]. The first reverse-engineers technology traced to material recovered at the 1947 [[Roswell Crash]]. The second, which has run for decades, monitors an alleged Atlantic USO nicknamed the "[[Tic Tac Factory]]." In this account, ONI works closely with MITRE on intelligence and counterintelligence. One specific element within ONI allegedly looks for evidence that foreign adversaries have independently exploited the same reverse-engineered technologies. Another provides domestic counterintelligence support, which includes watching for leaked program terminology online. For this counterintelligence work, ONI allegedly draws on the [[Naval Criminal Investigative Service]] (NCIS) and the [[Air Force Office of Special Investigations]]' Office of Special Projects.

Gerb argues that if a U.S. intelligence service studied recovered unmanned discs before the 1947 Roswell event, ONI would almost certainly have been involved. He reasons that it is the oldest such service and that its jurisdiction over maritime and naval matters predates the modern UFO era.
