The Office of Naval Intelligence (ONI), founded in 1882, is the oldest continuously operating intelligence agency in the United States and serves as the U.S. Navy's primary intelligence arm, operating within both the Department of Defense and the wider U.S. intelligence community. ONI's stated mission is to provide full-spectrum maritime intelligence — analyzing, tracking, and monitoring foreign naval capabilities and vessels in support of fleet operations and the security of the seas. In UAP Gerb's investigative framework, ONI is identified as one of the most critical Navy-side administrative components of an alleged [[MITRE Corporation|MITRE]]-managed UFO technology reverse-engineering and USO monitoring program, alongside the Office of the Secretary of the Navy.

## Role in Alleged MITRE Legacy Programs

UAP Gerb alleges that since MITRE's 1958 founding, ONI — together with [[Naval Air Systems Command]] and, to a lesser extent, [[Naval Sea Systems Command]] — has served as one of the primary Navy mission partners and program administrators for an alleged technology reverse-engineering program tracing to material recovered at the 1947 [[Roswell Crash]], as well as for a decades-old program monitoring an alleged Atlantic USO nicknamed the "[[Tic Tac Factory]]." ONI is described as working closely with MITRE in an intelligence and counterintelligence capacity: a specific element within ONI is alleged to scout for evidence that foreign adversaries have independently exploited the same reverse-engineered technologies, while another provides domestic counterintelligence support, including monitoring for leaked program terminology online. For this counterintelligence work, ONI is alleged to draw on the [[Naval Criminal Investigative Service]] (NCIS) and the [[Air Force Office of Special Investigations]]' Office of Special Projects.

If a U.S. intelligence service studied recovered unmanned discs prior to the 1947 Roswell event, UAP Gerb argues ONI — as the oldest such service — would almost certainly have been involved, given its jurisdiction over maritime and naval matters predating the modern UFO era.
