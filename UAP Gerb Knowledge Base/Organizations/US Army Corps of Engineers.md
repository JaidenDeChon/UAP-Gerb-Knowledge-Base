The US Army Corps of Engineers (USACE) is a federal agency and a major command of the US Army. It provides engineering, construction and operations services to the US military and to civilian sectors. In UAP research and investigations of Deep Underground Military Bases (DUMBs), the Corps is identified as the main government body that designed and built classified underground military facilities during the Cold War and after it.

## Classified Underground Construction

In a speech titled "Underground Facilities for Defense: Experience and Lessons" (dated 1989 by Gerb; published in 1987 conference proceedings), [[Lloyd A. Duscha]], the Corps' Deputy Director of Engineering and Construction, acknowledged that after World War II the Corps took part in "some very complex and interesting military projects" involving underground construction, and that "several of the most interesting facilities" remain classified. He named the Corps' work on the Cheyenne Mountain NORAD complex, and acknowledged other classified projects of similar or larger size that he could not identify.

The Corps published several documents on building underground facilities:

- A 1963 manual, "Utilization of Nuclear Power Plants in Underground Installation", discussed self-contained underground facilities powered by nuclear reactors.
- A 1964 proposal called for 12 massive structures up to 4,000 feet underground, including one below Yuma County, Arizona, and one in Inyo County, California, near China Lake.
- A 1985 report, "Literature Survey of Underground Construction Methods for Application to Hardened Facilities", stated that "adequate technology is available to construct hardened underground facilities under virtually any ground conditions," so that economic viability, and not technical feasibility, was the only constraint.
- A five-part training manual from 1959–1961, "Design of Underground Installations in Rock", assumes that underground facilities already exist. It describes tunnels 50x50 feet across and chambers up to 100 feet high, discusses truck and rail traffic, and advises that "landscape scars, roads and portal structures should be as inconspicuous as possible."

Taken together, these documents establish that the Corps built classified DUMBs from at least 1947 through the late 1980s, and that their construction was kept hidden from the public throughout that time.

## National Maglev Initiative Partnership

In the early 1990s the Army Corps of Engineers and the Department of Energy co-sponsored the [[National Maglev Initiative]] (NMI), which studied whether maglev technology could be introduced into US transportation. Several of the contractors that took part, including Grumman, General Dynamics and Battelle, are also implicated in alleged secret research on underground transportation that predates the NMI by decades.
