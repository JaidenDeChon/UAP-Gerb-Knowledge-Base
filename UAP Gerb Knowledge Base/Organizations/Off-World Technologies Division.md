The Off-World Technologies Division is an alleged classified program housed in a deep underground section of [[Naval Surface Warfare Center Crane]] in Crane, Indiana, described by firsthand witness [[RA]] as a non-human intelligence technology exploitation program. The name was used casually by a private contractor guide — likely an employee of [[SAIC]] — while escorting RA and a fellow Green Beret through the facility between 2013 and 2015. If the account is accurate, the division represents an active reverse engineering and exploitation program for recovered non-human technology within the US Navy's premier special warfare weapons laboratory.

## Witness Account

Between 2013 and 2015, US Army Green Beret [[RA]], holding an active TS/SCI clearance, attended a classified weapons training course at NSWC Crane. Of approximately ten soldiers in his class, only RA and one other TS/SCI-cleared soldier were separated and escorted deep underground through multiple security checkpoints after surrendering all electronics. The underground area was strikingly modern and advanced compared to the deliberately deteriorated surface buildings, and large enough to require directional signage.

A private contractor guide referred to the area as the "off-world technologies division" — the only name ever given to RA for the program. The two soldiers were brought into what appeared to be a viewing or observation room containing at least two objects identified as non-human technology.

## Technology Housed

### Seamless Metallic Sphere

A basketball-sized object with no visible joints, welds, or manufacturing marks. Its surface appeared almost liquid yet clearly metallic, with an iridescent quality RA compared to the "mother of pearl effect" described by [[Jonathan Wagant]] from the [[1997 Peru UFO Crash]]. The sphere appeared to float or be repelled off its podium by an unknown force, and featured an indentation on top approximately the size of a hand. Staff stated the object responds to some individuals but not others, possibly based on DNA or consciousness type.

### Gauntlet Weapon System

A device resembling a gauntlet or arm-worn tablet, identified as a weapon system because it had discharged an energy pulse during recovery, injuring at least one person. The device projected hieroglyphic-like symbols with no visible projection mechanism — RA speculated the characters may have been displayed directly into observers' minds. These symbols were later identified by RA as closely matching those described by attorney [[Daniel Sheehan]] from classified [[Project Blue Book]] photographs he reviewed in 1977 under orders from President [[Jimmy Carter]].

Facility personnel discussed both objects casually, suggesting routine institutional familiarity with the materials. Neither the Navy's scientists nor the contracting company's experts appeared able to operate or fully comprehend the technology.

## Cognitive Effects

RA reported that his normally photographic military memory becomes "real fuzzy" specifically when attempting to recall the underground facility — an effect he considers externally induced and unnatural. Both soldiers experienced severe ontological shock upon leaving, repeatedly questioning their understanding of reality. Researcher [[Gary Nolan]] has documented similar cognitive effects in individuals exposed to UAP phenomena.

## Institutional Context

The Off-World Technologies Division is consistent with NSWC Crane's publicly acknowledged mission of reverse engineering unknown technology. Crane's electromagnetic warfare experts successfully reverse engineered an unknown component of the AN/SLQ-32 electronic warfare system whose construction and function were initially incomprehensible, establishing the facility's capability for analyzing materials of unknown origin. Crane also receives foreign weapon systems captured by CIA operatives through the same logistics channels RA describes for technology delivery to the facility.

[[SAIC]], the largest private contractor at Crane with over 2,489 on-site employees, has been identified by multiple sources as a "keeper of the secrets" regarding UAP programs. Former SAIC employee [[Denise McKenzie]] testified in 2001 that black budget money is hidden within legitimate SAIC contracts, and several SAIC contract announcements for Crane programs were removed from the company's public website around 2024. SAIC's documented research into anomalous cognition (remote viewing, psychokinesis) aligns directly with the consciousness-interactive properties of the technology RA observed.

The video also references possible research connections between the Off-World Technologies Division and [[Salvador Pais]]'s patent work at Naval Air Station Pax River, and suggests a potential link to the alleged FDR-era Majestic 12 memo from 1944.

## Corroborating Accounts

A second witness named [[Martin]], who claimed participation in remote viewing programs, independently described scientists presenting a basketball-sized transparent sphere in a briefcase that "chose" who it interacted with — a description functionally identical to RA's account of the Crane sphere.
