Citizens Against UFO Secrecy (CAUS) is a UFO research and advocacy organization that uses legal action and Freedom of Information Act (FOIA) requests to force the government to disclose what it knows about UFOs. Founded in the early 1980s, it was one of the first organized civilian groups to challenge government UFO secrecy systematically through legal and legislative means.

## Co-Founders and Leadership

[[Brad Sparks]] was an early co-founder of CAUS and brought expertise in technical analysis to its investigations. The group brought together researchers, attorneys and advocates who used the Freedom of Information Act and court challenges to get past the classification barriers around UFO data.

## Legal and FOIA Strategy

CAUS pioneered the use of sustained legal pressure and FOIA requests to obtain government documents about UFOs. It filed lawsuits against government agencies and pursued administrative appeals when FOIA requests were denied. The legal precedents and strategies it set were later built on by organizations such as [[The Black Vault]].

## Legacy and Influence

CAUS set the template for the civilian UFO disclosure advocacy that continues today. Its work showed that focused legal action and FOIA expertise could get results where public pressure alone had failed. It also showed how far government agencies would go in invoking national security exemptions to withhold sensitive UFO data, including [[Fast Walkers|Fast Walker]] detection records from systems such as [[NORAD]] and [[Defense Support Program (DSP)]].
