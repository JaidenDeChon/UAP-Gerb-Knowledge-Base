The Blue Angels are the United States Navy's flight demonstration squadron, established in 1946 to demonstrate the skill and precision of naval aviators and to promote recruitment. The team flies highly modified F/A-18 Super Hornets and performs aerobatic maneuvers at air shows and public events worldwide.

## Alleged 2022 UAP Cockpit Leak

In 2022, several frames allegedly leaked from a Blue Angels cockpit recording. They purportedly show a UAP dropping out of clouds in a way that looks like the [[Metapod]] craft. If authentic, they would be among the most significant military UAP footage of recent years, because the images are clear and the alleged source is credible.

### Leaked Frame Analysis

The alleged leaked frames show:
- A crescent-shaped object descending rapidly through clouds
- The craft lying horizontally, with its transparent face turned away from its direction of travel
- The back of the craft facing the ground and the transparent face pointing at the sky
- Four additional signatures appearing alongside the craft in one frame
- A shape and structure that look like the Metapod's distinctive crescent

### Second-Generation Recording

The leaked material is clearly a second-generation recording, because a mouse cursor is visible on screen. The cursor suggests someone photographed or recorded a monitor playing the original cockpit footage, which is how leaked classified or restricted material often surfaces. Gerb compares it to the [[Flyby Footage]], which is also a second-generation recording.

### Authentication Status

Gerb calls the frames possibly the most important corroboration of the Metapod shape. He says he is still working to authenticate the leaked Blue Angels frames: "believe me I'm still trying to get to the bottom of it." If the leak is genuine, he argues, the Metapod shape is a legitimate form of UAP.
