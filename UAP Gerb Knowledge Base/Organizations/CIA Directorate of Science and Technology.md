The CIA's Directorate of Science and Technology (DS&T, sometimes rendered DSMT in captions) is one of the agency's principal directorates, founded in 1963 to centralize the CIA's research and development efforts and technical intelligence collection. UAP Gerb identifies DS&T as a recurring gatekeeper within alleged UFO legacy program administration — not, contrary to claims made in the 2025 documentary *Age of Disclosure*, the sole controlling body or "crash retrieval portfolio director" of the legacy programs, but an office whose "fingers" are in many of the legacy program's administrative pies. DS&T is cited as comparable to the Air Force's SAF/AQ, an internal directorate supporting classified programs within its parent organization.

## Alleged UFO Legacy Program Role

UAP Gerb states that many of the legacy program gatekeepers and personnel discussed across its research have deep ties to DS&T, naming in particular [[Dawn Meyerriecks]], [[Doug Wolfe]], [[Stephanie O'Sullivan]], [[Glenn Gaffney]], and [[Mary K. Sturdivant|Mary Sturdivant]]. [[Bobby Ray Inman]], in a 1989 phone call with NASA mission specialist Bob Echler, identified CIA DS&T Deputy Director and NRO Program B Director R. Everett Heinman as the appropriate contact for questions about recovered UAP technology becoming available for research — cited as evidence connecting DS&T leadership directly to alleged UAP material custody.

## Blocking of the Kona Blue Transfer (2011)

UAP Gerb attributes the 2011 killing of the [[Kona Blue]] proposed special access program — which would have transferred [[Lockheed Martin]]-held UAP materials to the [[AAWSAP]] program via the DHS Science and Technology Directorate — specifically to elements of DS&T, naming [[Glenn Gaffney]] and [[Mary K. Sturdivant|Mary Sturdivant]] as the officials who ultimately blocked the technology transfer agreement. The video theorizes Gaffney and other CIA personnel objected to [[Lockheed Martin Space Systems|Lockheed Martin]] introducing an "uninitiated" contractor — [[Bigelow Aerospace Advanced Space Studies]] — into the UFO materials space outside CIA control, framing DS&T's intervention as an effort to preserve centralized gatekeeping over unregulated groups possessing UAP materials.
