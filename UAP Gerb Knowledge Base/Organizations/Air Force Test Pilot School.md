The U.S. Air Force Test Pilot School (TPS) at [[Edwards Air Force Base]], California, is operated by the [[Air Force Test Center]]. It is where the Air Force's top pilots, navigators and engineers learn to conduct flight tests and produce the data test missions need. With the U.S. Naval Test Pilot School at Naval Air Station Patuxent River, it is one of the two main American military test pilot schools.

## In UAP research

According to witness Ed, as relayed in UAP Gerb's *The Hidden Wing*, pilots of manned reverse-engineered vehicles in the [[Hidden Wing]] test program came from the Edwards TPS. Gerb notes a similar claim by former Air Force master sergeant [[Edgar Fouche]], who said pilots of the alleged TR-3B, a triangle said to use a reverse-engineered [[Magnetic Field Disruptor (MFD)|magnetic field disruptor]], came from both the Naval Test Pilot School and the Air Force Flight Test Center's school at Edwards. In his closing remarks Gerb says pilots for the Hidden Wing flight test program are drawn from both schools. He also notes that the three contractors at [[Air Force Plant 42]] (Boeing, Lockheed Martin and Northrop Grumman) work closely with TPS pilots.
