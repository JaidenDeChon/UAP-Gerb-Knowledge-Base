ITBS is a YouTube channel specializing in video stabilization and enhancement of UAP footage. The channel provides technically improved versions of widely discussed UFO videos to facilitate clearer analysis and reduce visual noise caused by camera shake or unstable recording conditions.

## Metapod Footage Stabilization

ITBS created the stabilized version of the Metapod footage that [[UAP Gerp]] used for his detailed analysis video. Video stabilization is a critical post-processing technique that applies motion compensation algorithms to counteract camera movement, allowing frame-by-frame examination of the subject without the distraction of handheld recording instability.

## Technical Contribution to UAP Analysis

By providing stabilized versions of UAP footage, ITBS enables researchers and analysts to:
- Track object movement more precisely
- Identify subtle flight characteristics and rotational behavior
- Detect visual artifacts or anomalies more easily
- Compare craft details across frames without compensating mentally for camera shake

In the case of the Metapod, the stabilized footage allowed clear observation of the craft's variable rotation along its X-axis, including switches from counterclockwise to clockwise rotation with variable acceleration—details that might be obscured in raw, unstabilized footage.

## Attribution and Use

UAP Gerp explicitly credits ITBS in his analysis, noting that he used "YouTube channel itbs's stabilized footage of the Metapod" due to the original uploads by [[UFO VNI]] and [[UFO Invasions]] having been taken down. ITBS's channel information is provided in UAP Gerp's video description to ensure proper attribution.
