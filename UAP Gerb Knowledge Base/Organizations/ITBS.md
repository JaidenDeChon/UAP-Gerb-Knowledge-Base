ITBS is a YouTube channel that stabilizes and enhances UAP footage. It posts technically improved versions of widely discussed UFO videos, with less of the visual noise that camera shake or unsteady recording causes, so they can be analyzed more clearly.

## Metapod Footage Stabilization

ITBS made the stabilized version of the Metapod footage that Gerb used in his detailed analysis video. Video stabilization is a post-processing technique in which motion compensation algorithms counteract the camera's movement. The subject can then be examined frame by frame without the shake of a handheld recording getting in the way.

## Technical Contribution to UAP Analysis

The stabilized versions of UAP footage from ITBS let researchers and analysts:
- Track object movement more precisely
- Identify subtle flight characteristics and rotational behavior
- Detect visual artifacts or anomalies more easily
- Compare craft details across frames without mentally correcting for camera shake

In the Metapod video, the stabilized footage clearly shows the craft's variable rotation along its X-axis, including switches from counterclockwise to clockwise rotation with variable acceleration. These details might be hidden in the raw, unstabilized footage.
