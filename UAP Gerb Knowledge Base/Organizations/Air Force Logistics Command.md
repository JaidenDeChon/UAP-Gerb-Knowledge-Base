Air Force Logistics Command (AFLC) was the U.S. Air Force major command, headquartered at [[Wright-Patterson Air Force Base]], whose mission was to keep the Air Force's aerospace weapon systems in a constant state of combat readiness through procurement, supply, maintenance and transportation. It was formed in 1961 by redesignating [[Air Materiel Command]], and in 1992 it merged with [[Air Force Systems Command]] to form [[Air Force Materiel Command (AFMC)]].
