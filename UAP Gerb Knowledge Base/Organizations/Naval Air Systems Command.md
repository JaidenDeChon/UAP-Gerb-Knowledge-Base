Naval Air Systems Command (NAVAIR) is one of the U.S. Navy's major systems commands (syscoms), responsible for the acquisition, development, testing, and life-cycle support of naval aviation systems. Headquartered at [[Naval Air Station Patuxent River]] ("Pax River") in Maryland, NAVAIR is the aviation-focused counterpart to [[Naval Sea Systems Command]] (NAVSEA), which handles ships, submarines, and surface combat systems. In UAP Gerb's investigative framework, NAVAIR — and specifically its program executive offices (PEOs) at Pax River — is identified as the primary administrative and operational home for an alleged [[MITRE Corporation|MITRE]]-managed UFO technology reverse-engineering program tracing to the 1947 [[Roswell Crash]].

## Role in Alleged MITRE Legacy Programs

UAP Gerb alleges that NAVAIR's program executive offices at Pax River house, both administratively and operationally, the core of a decades-old Navy-MITRE reverse-engineering effort — a role UAP Gerb argues makes sense given that UAP frequently demonstrate transmedium capabilities spanning both NAVAIR's aviation and [[Naval Sea Systems Command|NAVSEA]]'s maritime domains. Pax River is described as the nexus point for Naval Aviation Research, Development, Test and Evaluation (RDT&E), home to both NAVAIR and the U.S. Navy Test Pilot School (USNTPS), as well as the Naval Air Warfare Center Aircraft Division (NAWCAD). NAVAIR is alleged to work alongside [[Office of Naval Intelligence|ONI]] as a primary program administrator, with counterintelligence support provided through ONI's relationship with the [[Naval Criminal Investigative Service]] and the [[Air Force Office of Special Investigations]].

Pax River was the subject of a 2025 site visit by Representative [[Eric Burlison]] and White House staff investigating possible hangar facilities used to store recovered vehicles, and has separately been named by investigative journalist Chris Sharp, citing sources for the outlet Liberation Times, as a site where a vehicle or vehicles of unknown origin were allegedly stored.
