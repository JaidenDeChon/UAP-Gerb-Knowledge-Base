The Naval Criminal Investigative Service (NCIS) is the primary federal law enforcement and counterintelligence agency for the Department of the Navy. It investigates criminal activity, protects Navy and Marine Corps personnel and assets, and conducts counterintelligence and counterterrorism operations within the naval service. Gerb identifies NCIS as a supporting counterintelligence partner of the [[Office of Naval Intelligence]] (ONI) on an alleged [[MITRE Corporation|MITRE]]-managed program that reverse-engineers UFO technology and monitors USOs.

## Role in Alleged MITRE Legacy Programs

Gerb alleges that ONI uses NCIS, along with the [[Air Force Office of Special Investigations]]' Office of Special Projects, for counterintelligence support on MITRE's reverse-engineering program. Their role, he says, is to secure the program against insider disclosure, foreign intelligence penetration, and accidental leaks of its terminology or content.
