The Naval Criminal Investigative Service (NCIS) is the primary federal law enforcement and counterintelligence agency for the Department of the Navy, responsible for investigating criminal activity, protecting Navy and Marine Corps personnel and assets, and conducting counterintelligence and counterterrorism operations within the naval service. In UAP Gerb's investigative framework, NCIS is identified as a supporting counterintelligence partner to the [[Office of Naval Intelligence]] (ONI) in connection with an alleged [[MITRE Corporation|MITRE]]-managed UFO technology reverse-engineering and USO monitoring program.

## Role in Alleged MITRE Legacy Programs

UAP Gerb alleges that ONI employs NCIS — together with the [[Air Force Office of Special Investigations]]' Office of Special Projects — for counterintelligence support on MITRE's reverse-engineering program, helping to secure the program from insider disclosure, foreign intelligence penetration, and inadvertent leaks of program terminology or content.
