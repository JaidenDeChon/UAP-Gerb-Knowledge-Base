General Dynamics is an American aerospace and defense corporation. It was formed in 1952 as a parent company for the submarine builder Electric Boat and the Canadian aircraft maker Canadair, and in 1954 it bought Consolidated Vultee, which became its Convair division. In the early 1990s it sold off much of its aerospace business. Its Fort Worth aircraft division, maker of the F-16, went to Lockheed in 1993, and its San Diego-based Space Systems Division, known for the Atlas rocket, went to [[Martin Marietta]] in a deal agreed in December 1993.
