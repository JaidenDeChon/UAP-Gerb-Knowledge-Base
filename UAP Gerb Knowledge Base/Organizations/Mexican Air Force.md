The Mexican Air Force (Fuerza Aérea Mexicana) filmed 11 unidentified flying objects on March 5, 2004, over the southern state of [[Campeche, Mexico|Campeche]]. They were recorded with infrared equipment during a routine anti-drug-trafficking surveillance mission. The lights appeared to fly at an altitude of 3,500 meters, and they surrounded the jet during its patrol. Major [[Magdaleno Castanon]] stated that the military jets "chased the lights" and that he believed "they could feel we were pursuing them."

Only three of the eleven objects showed up on the plane's radar. Skeptic [[Michael Shermer]] suggested the lights were burnoff flares on an offshore oil platform in the [[Gulf of Mexico]], though his explanation has not been conclusively confirmed.
