The 24th Special Tactics Squadron (24th STS) is the U.S. Air Force's Tier 1 special operations unit and its component of [[JSOC (Joint Special Operations Command)|Joint Special Operations Command]]. It falls under Air Force Special Operations Command. Its operators include pararescuemen, combat controllers, special reconnaissance and tactical air control party personnel, trained for classified missions including direct action, counterterrorism, counterinsurgency, hostage rescue and special reconnaissance. It serves alongside the other JSOC Tier 1 units: Army Delta Force, Navy SEAL Team 6 (DEVGRU), the Army Intelligence Support Activity and the Ranger Regiment's Regimental Reconnaissance Company.

UAP Gerb has argued that parallel Tier 1 units, operating under their own authority, are the "boots on the ground" and direct-action element of clandestine teams that retrieve crashed, downed or found UFOs worldwide. The [[160th Special Operations Aviation Regiment]] (the Nightstalkers) is said to provide transport for these teams. In *The Hidden Wing* he recalls an anonymous whistleblower account, first covered in his NRO investigation, of a 24th STS veteran whose troop chief allegedly served on such a retrieval team and recovered a bizarre egg-shaped craft.
