Air Combat Command (ACC) is a major command of the U.S. Air Force, headquartered at [[Langley Air Force Base]] in Virginia. It is the Air Force component command of U.S. Strategic Command and U.S. Northern Command, and it manages the service's combat air forces, including fighter, bomber, reconnaissance, and command and control aircraft. Around 2012, witness [[Dylan Borland]] observed an apparent electro-optic cloaking triangular craft hovering over a NASA hangar at Langley AFB. Because ACC is based there, researchers looking into alleged UAP legacy programs have theorized that the command may play a role in test and evaluation work involving reverse-engineered non-human airframes.

## Role in the Air Force

ACC organizes, trains, equips, and maintains combat-ready forces for rapid deployment around the world. Its headquarters at Langley AFB is close to the Atlantic Command and to joint operational planning infrastructure. The command oversees major combat air wings, including those that fly F-22 Raptors, F-35s, B-52 Stratofortresses, and the E-3 Sentry AWACS fleet.
