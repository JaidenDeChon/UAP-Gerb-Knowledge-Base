Honeywell International Inc. is an American publicly traded multinational conglomerate, one of whose main business areas is aerospace. Since 1 May 2017 its subsidiary National Technology and Engineering Solutions of Sandia LLC (NTESS) has managed [[Sandia National Laboratories]] for the [[Department of Energy]]'s [[National Nuclear Security Administration]], succeeding [[Lockheed Martin]] (1993-2017, first as [[Martin Marietta]]) and [[AT&T]] (1949-1993). In 1986 Honeywell acquired the aerospace business of Sperry, the defense electronics contractor formerly known as Sperry Rand.
