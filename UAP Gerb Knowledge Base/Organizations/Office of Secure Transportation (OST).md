The Office of Secure Transportation (OST) is the office of the [[National Nuclear Security Administration]] (NNSA), within the [[Department of Energy]], that moves US nuclear weapons, weapon components and special nuclear material between secure sites inside the continental United States. Its convoys of unmarked tractor-trailers and escort vehicles are crewed by armed NNSA federal agents. UAP Gerb theorizes "with extreme confidence" that the OST also transports recovered UFO material and secrets across the country.

## History and operations

The Department of Energy and its predecessor agencies have moved nuclear weapons and special nuclear material by commercial and government transport since 1947. In the late 1960s the Atomic Energy Commission's Albuquerque Operations Office was directed to protect all shipments involving special nuclear material, and after a rise in political violence the Office of Secure Transportation was established there in 1975. The NNSA, created in 2000, now administers it. Under DOE Order 153.1A the OST may also provide transport for [[NEST (Nuclear Emergency Support Team)|NEST]].

OST convoys carry no markings beyond US government license plates, and escort vehicles always accompany the transports, which are conventional three-axle truck tractors with two-axle trailers. Each federal agent completes more than 1,000 hours of candidate training in paramilitary tactics, firearms and vehicle handling, and agents are authorized to use deadly force. The office logs more than 4.5 million miles a year and operates from Amarillo, Texas, Oak Ridge, Tennessee, and Albuquerque, New Mexico. [[Sandia National Laboratories]] has supplied it with Mobile Guardian Transport units.

## Alleged role in UFO legacy programs

In UAP Gerb's account, the [[Atomic Energy Act of 1954]] lets recovered non-human material be classified as special nuclear material, which in turn lets DOE and NNSA special teams handle it. His theory is that the NNSA, using national laboratories such as Sandia, [[Los Alamos National Laboratory|Los Alamos]], [[Oak Ridge National Laboratory|Oak Ridge]] and [[Lawrence Livermore National Laboratory|Lawrence Livermore]], relies on three teams for recovery, procurement and transport of UFOs: [[DOE Special Response Teams (SRTs)|special response teams]] for security and direct action, NEST for on-site scientific work, and the OST for moving material between sites. Crash retrieval accounts discussed on the channel also name the OST among the organizations involved in moving recovered material.
