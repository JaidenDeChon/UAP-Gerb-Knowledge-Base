UFO Invasions is a YouTube channel that re-uploaded the Metapod UAP footage in 2017, about two years after the video was first published and then removed by [[UFO VNI]]. Once the original was unavailable, the re-upload was a secondary source for the footage.

## 2017 Re-upload

After UFO VNI removed the original Metapod video, researchers and UAP enthusiasts who had not archived the original upload could still watch the footage on UFO Invasions. While the original source was unavailable, the channel's 2017 re-upload helped keep the footage circulating in the UAP research community.

## Subsequent Removal

The UFO Invasions re-upload was eventually taken down as well, like the original UFO VNI upload. The circumstances of this second removal, and the reasons for it, are not publicly documented. It may have come from the same privacy concerns or requests from the original witness, Modesto, that led UFO VNI to remove its upload.

## Current Availability

At the time of Gerb's February 2024 analysis, the stabilized version of the Metapod footage used for detailed examination came from the YouTube channel [[ITBS]]. ITBS stabilized the video to reduce camera shake and make the footage clearer to analyze.
