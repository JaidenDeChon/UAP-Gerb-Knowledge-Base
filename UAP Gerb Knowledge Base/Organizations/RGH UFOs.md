RGH UFOs is an independent UAP research group. Gerb credits it for its 2023 investigation of [[Jonathan Weygandt]]'s 1997 [[Peru UFO Crash Incident]] case. RGH UFOs and [[TAGOM]] each reached many of the same conclusions about the case that Gerb came to independently, including that the [[NEST (Nuclear Emergency Support Team)|DOE NEST]] team was the likely retrieval force. Gerb's comprehensive 2025 analysis of the case lists the group's work as recommended viewing.
