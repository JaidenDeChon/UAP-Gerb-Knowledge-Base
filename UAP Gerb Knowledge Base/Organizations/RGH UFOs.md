RGH UFOs is an independent UAP research entity credited by UAP Gerb for their 2023 investigative work into [[Jonathan Weygandt]]'s 1997 [[Peru UFO Crash Incident]] case. Alongside [[TAGOM]], RGH UFOs arrived at many of the same analytical conclusions about the case that UAP Gerb independently developed, including identification of the [[NEST (Nuclear Emergency Support Team)|DOE NEST]] team as the likely retrieval force. Their work is cited as recommended viewing in UAP Gerb's comprehensive 2025 analysis.
