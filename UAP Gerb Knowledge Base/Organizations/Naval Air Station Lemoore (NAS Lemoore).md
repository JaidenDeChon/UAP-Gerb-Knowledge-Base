Naval Air Station Lemoore (NAS Lemoore), in Northern California, is the US Navy's newest and largest master jet base and its only one on the West Coast. It is the home port of every Navy strike fighter squadron on the West Coast, and those squadrons fly F/A-18 Hornet and Super Hornet aircraft. The station hosts several carrier air wings and the entire Strike Fighter Wing Pacific.

## R2508 Complex Access

NAS Lemoore is one of the few facilities with Poncho-3 clearance for the [[R2508 Complex]]. The complex is an area of restricted airspace and associated land in the upper Mojave Desert, managed by [[Edwards Air Force Base]] (412th Test Wing), [[China Lake|Naval Air Weapons Station China Lake]], and National Training Center Fort Irwin. Under Poncho-3 clearance, only locally based aircraft may enter specific restricted sections of R2508. That puts NAS Lemoore in the same group as Edwards, China Lake, and Palmdale Plant 42, all facilities that Gerb has connected to UFO legacy program operations.

## UAP Connections

The 1997 [[Hunter Warrior Advanced Warfighting Experiment]] was mainly a Marine Corps exercise. NAS Lemoore and [[NAWDC]] in Fallon, Nevada, assembled air crews for it as a naval adjunct. Gerb theorizes that this involvement helped enable clandestine operations with alien reproduction vehicles (ARVs) alongside blue (friendly) assets.

Triangular craft have also been reported at NAS Lemoore itself. Navy Aviation Maintenance Administrator Angelo Acetta testified that in 2001 he saw a glowing triangular object with glowing white points above FRC West at NAS Lemoore. He said he watched it travel slowly for three minutes before it left at seemingly instantaneous speed. Acetta claimed he saw the same vehicle four times between February and October 2012, and the Naval Air Station did not respond.
