Peraton is a US government services and technology contractor owned by the private equity firm Veritas Capital. It was formed in 2017 and grew much larger in 2021, when Veritas took Perspecta private for about $7.1 billion and combined the two companies. Peraton serves defense, intelligence and civilian agencies, and later bought [[Northrop Grumman]]'s federal IT and mission support business for $3.4 billion. Former NGA director [[Robert Cardillo]] joined its advisory board in May 2019.

## The Lockheed lineage and the Sub Rosa theory

Part of Peraton descends from [[Lockheed Martin]]. In 2010 Veritas bought Lockheed's Enterprise Integration Group, a unit deeply involved in classified systems engineering for intelligence agencies, for $815 million in cash, and renamed it the SI Organization, taking on contracts with the NSA, NRO and CIA. After a merger under Veritas, the SI Organization became Vencore in 2014. Vencore was later merged with other firms to form Perspecta, which was folded into Peraton in 2021.

In his paper *Sub Rosa*, researcher Rob Jones proposes that after the failed attempts of 2008 to 2011 to move recovered non-human material out of Lockheed through [[Kona Blue]], Lockheed offloaded the material through this sale. In his Lockheed video, Gerb finds the theory compelling. He notes that Cardillo, whom Jones names as a possible final authority in blocking the official transfer, sits on the board of the company where the former Lockheed unit ended up.
