Peraton is a US government services and technology contractor owned by the private equity firm Veritas Capital, formed in 2017 and greatly enlarged in 2021 when Veritas took Perspecta private for about $7.1 billion and combined the two. It serves defense, intelligence and civilian agencies, and later bought [[Northrop Grumman]]'s federal IT and mission support business for $3.4 billion. Former NGA director [[Robert Cardillo]] joined its advisory board in May 2019.

## The Lockheed lineage and the Sub Rosa theory

Part of Peraton descends from [[Lockheed Martin]]. In 2010 Veritas bought Lockheed's Enterprise Integration Group, a unit deeply involved in classified systems engineering for intelligence agencies, for $815 million in cash, and renamed it the SI Organization, taking on contracts with the NSA, NRO and CIA. The SI Organization was rebranded Vencore in 2014 after a merger under Veritas, Vencore was merged with other firms to form Perspecta, and Perspecta was folded into Peraton in 2021.

In his paper *Sub Rosa*, researcher Rob Jones proposes that after the failed attempts of 2008 to 2011 to move recovered non-human material out of Lockheed through [[Kona Blue]], Lockheed offloaded the material through this sale. UAP Gerb's Lockheed video finds the theory compelling, and notes that Cardillo, whom Jones names as a possible final authority in blocking the official transfer, sits on the board of the company where the former Lockheed unit ended up.
