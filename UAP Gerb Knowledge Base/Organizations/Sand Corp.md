Sand Corp is a private defense contractor whose documented specialization is preventing leaks and stopping whistleblowers. It offers counterintelligence-adjacent services focused on information control and program protection. In 2022, the Department of Defense awarded Sand Corp a $1.9 million contract for "AARO Support Services" to support the [[AARO|All-Domain Anomaly Resolution Office (AARO)]]. The office of [[Ronald S. Moultrie]], who was then Under Secretary of Defense for Intelligence and Security, was involved in securing the contract.

## Significance to UAP Research

UAP researchers cite the award of AARO's contract to a whistleblower suppression firm as direct structural evidence that AARO, the DOD's official UAP investigation office, was never designed as a genuine investigative body. AARO's first major support contract went to a firm whose core competency is identifying and neutralizing insider leaks. The office did not contract for scientific expertise, signals intelligence capability or anomalous materials analysis support. This pattern is consistent with the broader accusation that AARO functioned as a whistleblower honeypot, a mechanism to draw out and neutralize people with knowledge of UAP legacy programs instead of documenting and investigating their claims.
