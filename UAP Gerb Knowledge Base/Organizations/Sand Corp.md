Sand Corp is a private defense contractor whose documented specialization is **preventing leaks and stopping whistleblowers** — counterintelligence-adjacent services focused on information control and program protection. In 2022, the Department of Defense awarded Sand Corp a $1.9 million contract for "AARO Support Services" to provide support to the [[All-Domain Anomaly Resolution Office (AARO)]]. The contract was secured with the involvement of [[Ronald S. Moltry]]'s office, then Under Secretary of Defense for Intelligence and Security.

## Significance to UAP Research

The awarding of a whistleblower suppression contractor to AARO — the DOD's official UAP investigation office — is cited by UAP researchers as direct structural evidence that AARO was never designed as a genuine investigative body. Rather than contracting scientific expertise, signals intelligence capability, or anomalous materials analysis support, AARO's first major support contract went to a firm whose core competency is identifying and neutralizing insider leaks. This pattern is consistent with the broader accusation that AARO functioned as a **whistleblower honeypot**: a mechanism to draw out and neutralize individuals with knowledge of UAP legacy programs rather than to document and investigate their claims.
