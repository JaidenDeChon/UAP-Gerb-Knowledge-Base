The **Applied Research Laboratory (ARL)** at Pennsylvania State University is a Department of Defense University Affiliated Research Center (UARC) sponsored by the [[US Navy]]. It was founded in 1945, at the Navy's request, when Harvard closed its wartime Underwater Sound Laboratory and the Navy moved the laboratory's torpedo work to Penn State. It was first called the Ordnance Research Laboratory. [[Eric A. Walker]], the Harvard laboratory's assistant director, came with it as the new laboratory's first director, a post he held until 1951. He later became president of Penn State in 1956.

## History and work

The laboratory grew out of wartime undersea-warfare research and remains one of the Navy's principal university laboratories. According to UAP Gerb, Walker moved about 100 engineers from Harvard to Penn State to establish it. The laboratory has since won the largest research contract in the university's history, $2.1 billion from the Navy. Its specialties today include novel power and energy systems, electro-optics and directed energy.
