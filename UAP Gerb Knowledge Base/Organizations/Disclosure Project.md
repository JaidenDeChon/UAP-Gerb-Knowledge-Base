The Disclosure Project is a nonprofit research organization that Dr. [[Steven Greer]] founded in 1993. Its aim is to compel the US government to declassify information about UAP, extraterrestrial intelligence, and advanced energy technologies. It is best known for its May 9, 2001 event at the [[National Press Club]] in Washington, D.C., where over 20 credentialed military, government, and intelligence witnesses testified publicly about UAP encounters and alleged cover-up activities.

## 2001 National Press Club Event

The 2001 event drew significant media attention. Dozens of credentialed witnesses testified, among them military officers, intelligence analysts, and government scientists. One of them was aerospace illustrator [[Mark McCandlish]], who presented his technical cutaway blueprint of the [[Alien Reproduction Vehicle (ARV)|ARV Flux Liner]] and discussed his investigation into the craft.

## McCandlish and Brad Sorenson

The Disclosure Project pressed McCandlish to name his primary source, [[Brad Sorenson]], in public. McCandlish described being "prodded by people in the Disclosure Project to name names," and he did so. He said Greer had "almost coerced" him into the decision, which he deeply regretted because it brought Sorenson sustained unwanted attention and damaged his career. McCandlish later said: "Not being a journalist, not having a background in journalism, I didn't really know... that this could be potentially very harmful to the individuals who had been the original source of the material."

## Witness Archive and Anonymous Testimonies

Besides its public events, the Disclosure Project keeps an extensive archive of witness testimonies, including anonymous accounts. Redacted witnesses are listed in the archive by number. Two of these entries matter to Gerb's research:

- Gerb believes witness number 10138 is the anonymous Marine whistleblower "[[RB]]," who claimed to have guarded a recovered disc-shaped craft at an undisclosed military base in December 1963. Whether RB ever spoke directly with Greer is unknown.
- Gerb identifies witness number 101179 as [[Alain Boudier]], president of the [[3af-p (French national aeronautical and astronautical Association UAP division)|3af-p]], who claimed to have seen an uncut NASA film showing six UFOs descending onto the lunar surface during Apollo 8.
