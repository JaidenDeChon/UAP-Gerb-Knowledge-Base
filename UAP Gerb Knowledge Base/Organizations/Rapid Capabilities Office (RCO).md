The Department of the Air Force Rapid Capabilities Office (RCO) is a specialized acquisition office set up to develop, acquire and field critical Air Force and Space Force capabilities faster and with less bureaucratic overhead. Its chain of command is short and narrow: it reports to a governance board chaired by the [[Under Secretary of Defense for Acquisition and Sustainment (USD A&S)]]. The RCO can bypass many standard congressional notification requirements and Air Force acquisition regulations. It can also use carve-out contracting mechanisms, which keep down the number of people who must be read into (given access to) sensitive programs. [[Randall G. Walden]] is the RCO's Director and Program Executive Officer (PEO), a Senior Executive Service position.

## Alleged UAP Involvement

In "The Hidden Wing", Gerb names the RCO as the most likely office to carry out acquisition for the Air Force's alleged UFO legacy programs. He argues that its structure makes it a natural vehicle for managing programs that exploit recovered non-human technology and develop [[Alien Reproduction Vehicle (ARV)|alien reproduction vehicles]] and derivative airframes. In his view, the office fits because its oversight chain is narrow, few people need to be read in, it has access to carve-out contracts, and it has authority to speed up development outside normal service channels. Gerb calls on the current administration to question [[Randall G. Walden]] directly about the RCO's potential role in such programs. He also argues that because the RCO reports to the Under Secretary of Defense for Acquisition and Sustainment (USD A&S), there is an institutional route through which [[Waived Unacknowledged Special Access Programs (USAPs)]] could be managed without triggering broader congressional or Air Force oversight.
