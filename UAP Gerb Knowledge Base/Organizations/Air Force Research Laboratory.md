Handles discovery and development under AFMC, headquartered at Wright Patterson Air Force Base, Ohio

Received the Office of Primary Responsibility from AFSWC in 1976; has major presence at Edwards, Kirtland, and Wright Patterson Air Force Bases

Primary R&D arm of the Air Force under Air Force Materiel Command; commander appointed by SAF/AQ; linked to Dr. Eric H. Wang and legacy programs

Commander allegedly provided information to Tom DeLonge for Secret Machines
