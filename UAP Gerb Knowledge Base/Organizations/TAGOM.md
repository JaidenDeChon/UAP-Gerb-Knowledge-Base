TAGOM is an independent UAP research entity credited by UAP Gerb for their 2023 investigative work into [[Jonathan Wagant]]'s 1997 [[Peru UFO Crash Incident]] case, conducted alongside [[RGH UFOs]]. Both research groups arrived at many of the same conclusions about the DOE retrieval team and the case's significance before UAP Gerb's comprehensive 2025 analysis. Their work is recommended by UAP Gerb as supplementary material.
