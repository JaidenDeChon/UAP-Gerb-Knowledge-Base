TAGOM is an independent UAP research group. Gerb credits it for its 2023 investigation, carried out alongside [[RGH UFOs]], into [[Jonathan Weygandt]]'s 1997 [[Peru UFO Crash Incident]] case. Before Gerb's comprehensive 2025 analysis, both groups had reached many of the same conclusions about the DOE retrieval team and the significance of the case. Gerb recommends their work as supplementary material.
