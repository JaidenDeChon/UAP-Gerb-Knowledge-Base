Bell Labs (Bell Telephone Laboratories) is the research and development laboratory founded in 1925 under the joint ownership of [[AT&T]] and [[Western Electric]]. Under AT&T until 1996 it was credited with the transistor, the laser, the photovoltaic cell, radio astronomy and several programming languages, and its researchers won eleven Nobel Prizes and five Turing Awards in that period. It later passed to Lucent Technologies and is now Nokia Bell Labs.

## Alleged Role in UFO Reverse Engineering

[[Philip J. Corso]] named Bell Labs as the largest of the contractors to which the Army's Foreign Technology Division, under General Arthur Trudeau, passed technology from crash retrievals of the late 1940s. *The Day After Roswell* credits Bell Labs' work on the transistor and fiber optics to recovered technology. UAP Gerb treats those specific attributions as embellishment, since both technologies had clear development histories, and prefers the claim in Corso's manuscript *Dawn of a New Age* that recovered technology helped mature human work already under way. He suggests Bell Labs' large improvements to fiber optics in the 1970s might be such a case. A 1950s report on gravity research also listed Bell Labs, with General Electric, Sperry Rand, Boeing and the Lockheed Corporation, as working on anti-gravity rigs.

## Link to Sandia

When AT&T took over management of [[Sandia National Laboratories]] in 1949 at President [[Harry Truman]]'s request, the laboratory's operations were modelled on Bell Labs. UAP Gerb finds it significant that the national laboratory he regards as central to the UFO legacy programs was built on the pattern of the contractor Corso called the Army's premier partner in exploiting recovered material.
