The Black Group is an alleged clandestine organization within the United States Government that was founded in the 1940s to reverse engineer non-human intelligence technology, according to disclosures made by an anonymous insider to whistleblower [[Michael Herrera]]. Unlike the [[Blue Group]], the Black Group operates without congressional oversight, existing as what the insider described as a parallel structure that mirrors the descriptions in the [[Wilson Davis Memo]] of unacknowledged special access programs with virtually unlimited reach and no accountability.

## Founding and Structure

According to the insider's account:
- **Founded in the 1940s**: The Black Group was established shortly after World War II, coinciding with the alleged UFO crash retrievals of that era
- **Lack of Oversight**: The organization operates without congressional knowledge or authorization, functioning as what some would term an "illegal" or "rogue" government apparatus
- **Endless Reach**: The Black Group maintains influence and control across multiple government agencies, military branches, intelligence services, and private contractors
- **No accountability**: Traditional oversight mechanisms including Congress, inspectors general, and even presidents have no functional authority over Black Group operations

## Alleged Capabilities and Programs

### Reverse Engineering Success

The insider claimed the Black Group has successfully reverse-engineered non-human intelligence craft "to an unknown degree of success" and now utilizes these craft for operational purposes. According to the insider, flying craft are considered "boring tech" — there exist far more advanced technologies that the Black Group considers more exciting and utilitarian for humanity.

### Crash Retrieval Operations

The Black Group allegedly operates active crash retrieval programs using advanced technology:
- **EMP-like Weaponry**: Development of electromagnetic pulse weapons that can target, disrupt, and disable UAP
- **Luring Operations**: Ability to attract UAP to specific locations where they can be disabled and recovered
- **Body Recovery**: Retrieval not only of craft but of non-human biological entities

### P3 Recruitment Program

One of the most disturbing alleged Black Group operations involves recruiting individuals with psionic abilities (referred to as "P3"):
- **Third-World Recruitment**: Individuals with psychic/psionic abilities are recruited primarily from third-world nations
- **Interface Function**: These P3 individuals are needed to interface with and control non-human intelligence technology
- **Bargained Recruitment**: Recruits are offered security and safety for their families in exchange for participation
- **Permanent Isolation**: The recruiting process suggests permanent or semi-permanent separation from their families and former lives

According to Michael Herrera's account, the Indonesia operation he witnessed in 2009 — where armed operators loaded shipping containers with cylinder devices onto an octagonal craft — was a P3 recruitment and transport operation.

## Internal Factions

The insider described internal divisions within the Black Group:
- **Pro-Disclosure Faction**: "Good guys" who advocate for Congressional oversight and public transparency
- **Anti-Disclosure Faction**: "Bad guys" who want to maintain secrecy, retain power, and allegedly continue crimes against humanity
- **Political Enforcement Role**: The anti-disclosure faction allegedly functions as an enforcement arm for political corruption with assets in government, law enforcement, and military institutions

## Relationship to Blue Group

The Black Group operates in parallel to but largely independent from the [[Blue Group]] — the congressionally-overseen UAP investigation programs like [[AARO]] and historical programs like [[Project Blue Book]]. The Blue Group presumably has no knowledge of or access to the Black Group's recovered craft, reverse-engineered technology, or operational capabilities, creating a two-tiered system where:
- The Blue Group investigates UAP sightings and collects reports from military personnel
- The Black Group possesses actual craft, non-human technology, and operational understanding

## Connection to Wilson Davis Memo

The insider explicitly stated that the Black Group's structure and lack of oversight "mirrors almost identically what is spoken about in the [[Wilson Davis Memo]]" — the controversial document describing Admiral Thomas Wilson being denied access to a UFO crash retrieval program run by a private aerospace contractor under unacknowledged special access program protocols.

## Credibility and Context

The Black Group's existence and the details provided come solely from anonymous insider disclosures to Michael Herrera. While investigator [[Joey Is Not My Name]] verified that Herrera was taken to a secure facility and met with someone with apparent high-level access, the insider's claims about the Black Group remain unverified. Analysts have noted parallels to the CIA's disinformation campaign against [[Paul Benowitz]] in 1989, raising the possibility that the insider's disclosures constitute psychological operations rather than genuine whistleblowing.

If the Black Group exists as described, it would represent an extraconstitutional shadow government conducting operations including:
- Kidnapping individuals from foreign nations
- Operating weapons systems without oversight
- Shooting down non-human intelligence craft
- Maintaining operational craft derived from reverse engineering

Whether these claims represent truth, disinformation, or a mixture remains an open question in the UAP research community.
