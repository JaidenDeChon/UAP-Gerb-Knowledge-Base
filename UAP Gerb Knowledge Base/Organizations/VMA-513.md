VMA-513, nicknamed "The Flying Nightmares," is a United States Marine Corps attack squadron that flies AV-8B Harrier II jets. The Harrier is a STOVL (short takeoff and vertical landing) aircraft, and its Rolls-Royce Pegasus engines have four rotating nozzles that can direct thrust for vertical landing and for hovering in place. The squadron was stationed at [[MCAS Yuma]], Arizona, as part of Marine Aircraft Group 13 (MAG-13) of the 3rd Marine Aircraft Wing (3rd MAW).

## UAP Significance

In UAP research, VMA-513 is known as the unit to which [[Rodrik Castle]] was assigned during the 1997 [[Hunter Warrior Advanced Warfighting Experiment]] at [[Marine Corps Air Ground Combat Center 29 Palms]]. Castle led a team of six to eight Marines in aviation ordnance and was part of an experimental special-purpose marine air-ground task force (SPMAGTF-X). During the experiment, Castle's team was performing overnight AV-8B Harrier crash retrieval duties when they encountered an enormous triangular craft and approximately 30 armed, unmarked operators near [[Emerson Dry Lake]].

Castle knew Harrier VTOL/STOVL technology and helicopter flight dynamics well, from his service with VMA-513 and from additional training as a helicopter flight medic. That experience made him well qualified to tell the silent, motionless hovering of the triangular craft apart from any known aircraft, including the Harriers he maintained.
