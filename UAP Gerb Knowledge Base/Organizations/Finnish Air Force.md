The Finnish Air Force (Ilmavoimat) is the aerial warfare branch of the [[Finnish Defense Force]]. The [[Pori Airport UFO Incident]] of April 12, 1969 is the only UFO observation the Finnish Air Force has ever officially acknowledged. That day, pilot-in-training [[Tarmo Tukeva]] was sent up in a Fouga Magister to investigate seven presumed balloons. He reported disc-shaped, pale yellow objects that accelerated away at great speed against a 180 km/h headwind, and a second pilot, [[Jouko Kuronen]], also saw them. According to Gerb, radar 200 km away in [[Vaasa, Finland|Vaasa]] then detected the seven objects moving at an estimated 3,218 metres per second.
