The Finnish Air Force (Ilmavoimat) is the aerial warfare branch of the [[Finnish Defense Force]]. The April 12, 1969 [[Pori Airport UFO Incident]] remains the only UFO observation officially acknowledged by the Finnish Air Force in its history. During that incident, pilot-in-training [[Tarmo Tukeva]], sent up in a Fouga Magister to investigate seven presumed balloons, reported disc-shaped, pale yellow objects that accelerated away at great speed against a 180 km/h headwind; a second pilot, [[Jouko Kuronen]], also saw them. According to Gerb, radar 200 km away in [[Vaasa, Finland|Vaasa]] then detected the seven objects, at an estimated 3,218 metres per second.
