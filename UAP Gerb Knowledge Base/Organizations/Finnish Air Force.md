The Finnish Air Force (Ilmavoimat) is the aerial warfare branch of the [[Finnish Defense Force]]. The April 12, 1969 [[Pori Airport UFO Incident]] remains the only UFO observation officially acknowledged by the Finnish Air Force in its history. During that incident, two pilots — [[Tarmo Tukia]] and [[Juhani Korhonen]] — independently observed seven disc-shaped pale yellow objects that accelerated away at extraordinary speed against a 180 km/h headwind. Radar detection 200 km away in Vaasa confirmed the objects at an estimated speed of 3,218 meters per second.
