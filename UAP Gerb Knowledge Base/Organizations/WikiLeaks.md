WikiLeaks is an international non-profit organization that publishes classified, censored, or otherwise restricted information provided by anonymous sources and whistleblowers. Founded by Julian Assange, the organization has released numerous high-profile document collections related to government and corporate activities.

## 2016 Clinton Campaign Email Leaks

In 2016, WikiLeaks published thousands of emails from the Hillary Clinton presidential campaign, including communications involving campaign chairman [[John Podesta]]. Among these emails was a March 2015 communication from former USG contractor [[Bob Fish]] to Podesta containing significant claims about US military satellite tracking of UFOs.

## Bob Fish Email on DSP and Fast Walkers

The Fish email disclosed to WikiLeaks stated that "one of the government programs that collects hard data on unidentified flying objects is the USAF [[Defense Support Program (DSP)|DSP]] satellite program." Fish described witnessing DSP personnel discuss a [[Fast Walkers|Fast Walker]] detection event in which an object from deep space made a 30-degree course correction, indicating controlled flight.

This email represents one of the few documented instances of UAP-related information — from a cleared contractor with direct access to classified program personnel — being communicated to senior US political leadership and subsequently made public through a leak rather than official disclosure.

## Significance for UAP Disclosure

The WikiLeaks publication of the Fish-Podesta email bypassed traditional classification and FOIA restrictions, providing public evidence of claims about satellite-based UFO tracking that the government has consistently refused to disclose through official channels. [[John Greenewald]]'s repeated FOIA denials from [[NORAD]], [[US Northern Command]], and [[US Space Force]] on Fast Walker data stand in contrast to the information revealed through the WikiLeaks leak.
