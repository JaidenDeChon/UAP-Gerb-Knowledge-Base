WikiLeaks is an international non-profit organization that publishes classified, censored, or otherwise restricted information from anonymous sources and whistleblowers. Julian Assange founded it, and it has released many high-profile collections of documents about government and corporate activities.

## 2016 Clinton Campaign Email Leaks

In 2016, WikiLeaks published thousands of emails from Hillary Clinton's presidential campaign, including messages involving campaign chairman [[John Podesta]]. One of them was a March 2015 email to Podesta from former USG contractor [[Bob Fish]], which made claims about US military satellites tracking UFOs.

## Bob Fish Email on DSP and Fast Walkers

The Fish email disclosed to WikiLeaks stated that "one of the government programs that collects hard data on unidentified flying objects is the USAF [[Defense Support Program (DSP)|DSP]] satellite program." Fish described watching DSP personnel discuss a [[Fast Walkers|Fast Walker]] detection in which an object from deep space made a 30-degree course correction, indicating controlled flight.

The email is one of the few documented cases in which UAP-related information from a cleared contractor with direct access to classified program personnel reached senior US political leaders and was then made public through a leak, rather than through official disclosure.

## Significance for UAP Disclosure

By publishing the Fish-Podesta email, WikiLeaks bypassed the usual classification and FOIA restrictions. It gave the public evidence of claims about satellite-based UFO tracking that the government has consistently refused to disclose through official channels. By contrast, [[John Greenewald]]'s repeated FOIA requests for Fast Walker data were denied by [[NORAD]], [[US Northern Command]], and [[US Space Force]].
