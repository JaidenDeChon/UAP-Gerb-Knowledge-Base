Joint Task Force Bravo (JTF-Bravo) is a forward-based expeditionary task force of [[US SOUTHCOM]]. It promotes stability and security in Central and South America by countering transnational threats, including narcotics trafficking. Throughout the 1990s, JTF-Bravo was headquartered in Panama and Honduras and conducted numerous missions in the region.

## Component Units

JTF-Bravo's main subordinate unit is the [[1st Battalion 228th Aviation Regiment]], which has a fleet of helicopters that includes CH-47 Chinooks. During the 1990s, JTF-Bravo and the [[US Army 7th Special Forces Group]] worked together in support of the 1995 Peru-Ecuador border dispute.

## Theorized Role in UAP Crash Retrieval

In his analysis of the 1997 [[Peru UFO Crash Incident]], Gerb theorizes that the [[NEST (Nuclear Emergency Support Team)|DOE NEST]] rapid recovery team commandeered CH-47 Chinook helicopters from JTF-Bravo's [[1st Battalion 228th Aviation Regiment]] to carry personnel and equipment to the crash site. NEST has documented authority within the Department of Defense to requisition suitable military aircraft for emergency deployments, and JTF-Bravo's helicopters were the nearest CH-47s with a provable presence in the [[US SOUTHCOM]] theater at the time of the incident.
