The Army and Navy Research and Development Board was a US Department of Defense body that coordinated and oversaw research and development programs across the Army and Navy. In UAP history, Gerb names it as one of three bodies required to receive every [[Project Sign]] report, the other two being the [[USAF Scientific Advisory Group]] and the [[Atomic Energy Commission]]. The names come from Lieutenant General [[Nathan Twining]]'s [[Twining Memo|letter of 23 September 1947]], which recommended that the data of a detailed study be made available for comment to the Army, the Navy, the AEC, the JRDB (the Joint Research and Development Board), the Air Force Scientific Advisory Group, NACA, and the RAND and NEPA projects. Gerb reads the list as a sign that in the late 1940s UFO investigation was a concern shared by several agencies at the highest levels of military and nuclear oversight.

> [!edit] Corrected 17 October 2026
> Earlier versions said the board was one of three bodies required to receive every Sign report, and treated that as a distribution rule. The list is the recommendation in Twining's letter of 23 September 1947 (paragraph 3a), which proposed sharing the study's data with the Army, the Navy, the AEC, the JRDB, the Air Force Scientific Advisory Group, NACA, and the RAND and NEPA projects; it was not a rule for Sign's reports.
