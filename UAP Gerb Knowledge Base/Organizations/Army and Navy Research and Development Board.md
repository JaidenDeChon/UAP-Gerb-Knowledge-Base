The Army and Navy Research and Development Board was a US Department of Defense body that coordinated and oversaw research and development programs across the Army and Navy. In UAP history, the board is notable as one of three bodies required to receive every [[Project Sign]] report. The other two were the [[USAF Scientific Advisory Group]] and the [[Atomic Energy Commission]]. This distribution rule indicates that in the late 1940s, UFO investigation was treated as a concern shared by several agencies at the highest levels of military and nuclear oversight.
