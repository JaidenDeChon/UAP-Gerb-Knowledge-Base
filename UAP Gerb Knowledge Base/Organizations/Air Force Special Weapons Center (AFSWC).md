The Air Force Special Weapons Center (AFSWC) was a U.S. Air Force organization at [[Kirtland Air Force Base, New Mexico|Kirtland Air Force Base]], New Mexico, that specialized in atomic, nuclear and other unconventional weapons. It operated from 1949, first as a major command. On 1 April 1952 it became a subordinate of the [[Air Research and Development Command (ARDC)]], and then of its successor, [[Air Force Systems Command]], until it was disestablished in 1976. Its office-of-primary-responsibility role then passed to the Air Force Weapons Laboratory, which was later absorbed into the [[Air Force Research Laboratory]].

## Alleged role in UFO legacy programs

UAP Gerb's *The Hidden Wing* calls AFSWC one of the most interesting threads of the whole investigation. According to the April 1961 *Mechanical Engineering* obituary Gerb uncovered, Dr. [[Eric Henry Wang]], whom witnesses tie to the [[1953 Kingman, Arizona Crash Retrieval]] and the [[Aztec UFO Crash and Recovery|1948 Aztec crash]], worked in AFSWC's research directorate. Gerb also found Wang pictured in the research directorate section of AFSWC's 1956 Kirtland yearbook. Because AFSWC survives in modern [[Air Force Materiel Command (AFMC)]] through two channels, AFRL and Air Force Systems Command, Gerb argues that AFMC inherits a direct institutional link to early crash-retrieval science. He also ties the center's nuclear focus to his view that [[Atomic Energy Act of 1954|Atomic Energy Act]] categories such as special nuclear material were used to hide UFO material.
