UFO VNI is a YouTube channel that publishes UFO and UAP footage sent in by eyewitnesses. It is best known as the original publisher of the "Metapod" footage, one of the most discussed close-up UAP videos of recent years.

## Metapod Footage Publication

On September 7, 2015, UFO VNI received an email from a Spanish witness who signed himself Modesto. The day before, he had filmed an anomalous structured craft near [[El Escorial, Madrid, Spain]]. In the email he described the circumstances of the sighting, asked for strict anonymity, and asked that the exact GPS coordinates not be made public.

UFO VNI published the footage and was the first channel to share what became known as the [[Metapod]] video. The footage spread quickly through UFO research communities. It had reached Portuguese forums by September 8, 2015, and [[Third Phase of the Sun]] covered it on September 11, 2015.

## Footage Removal and Mystery

Some time after first publishing the Metapod video, UFO VNI removed it from its channel. Why it did so is unclear. It is speculated that the channel took the footage down at Modesto's request, to keep the witness anonymous or to keep public attention away from the specific location. In his original email, Modesto had explicitly asked that personal and location information stay private.

After UFO VNI removed the video, other channels, including [[UFO Invasions]], re-uploaded the footage in 2017. Those uploads were later taken down as well.
