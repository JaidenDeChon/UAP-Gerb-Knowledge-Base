Skywatchers is a civilian UAP research group active in 2025, notable for their investigations into [[Psionics|psionic]] phenomena related to UAP encounters. Member Barber presented testimony to the group claiming to have experienced telepathic contact with egg-shaped craft, echoing descriptions from [[Jonathan Wagant]]'s 1997 [[Peru UFO Crash Incident]] testimony. UAP Gerb highlighted the Skywatchers/Barber testimony as a 2025 corroborative data point in the broader pattern of psionic experiences reported during close UAP encounters.
