Skywatchers is a civilian UAP research group, active in 2025, known for investigating [[Psionics|psionic]] phenomena related to UAP encounters. A member named Barber presented testimony to the group, claiming to have experienced telepathic contact with egg-shaped craft. The account echoes descriptions from [[Jonathan Weygandt]]'s 1997 [[Peru UFO Crash Incident]] testimony. Gerb pointed to the Skywatchers testimony from Barber as a 2025 data point that corroborates a broader pattern of psionic experiences reported during close UAP encounters.
