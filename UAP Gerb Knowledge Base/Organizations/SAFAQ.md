SAF/AQ is the Office of the Assistant Secretary of the Air Force for Acquisition, Technology and Logistics, part of the Air Force Secretariat at Headquarters Air Force. In its own words it oversees Air Force research, development, acquisition and program sustainment, with an annual budget of more than $60 billion across more than 550 acquisition programs, including the F-35 and the B-21 Raider. It is responsible for Air Force science and technology, including basic research, applied research and advanced technology development. Its duties also cover prototyping and experimentation, the manufacturing technology program, contracting, and collaboration with [[Air Force Test and Evaluation (AF-TE)]]. It appoints the commander of the [[Air Force Research Laboratory]], and it partners with the [[Under Secretary of Defense for Acquisition and Sustainment (USD A&S)]] on technology-transfer programs.

## Directorates and components

- **[[SAFAQL (Special Programs)|SAF/AQL]]** (Special Programs): directs SAP advanced technology development and monitors new technologies.
- **[[SAFAQR (Science, Technology and Engineering)|SAF/AQR]]** (Science, Technology and Engineering): the Air Force Science and Technology Executive.
- **[[SAFAQX (Acquisition Integration)|SAF/AQX]]** (Acquisition Integration): acquisition policy, reporting, funds release and oversight.
- **[[Rapid Capabilities Office (RCO)]]**: the fast-track acquisition office, reporting to a board chaired by USD(A&S).
