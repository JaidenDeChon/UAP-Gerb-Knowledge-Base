The Daily Mail is a British tabloid newspaper and online media outlet. It has published several articles about UAP whistleblowers and disclosure efforts, and has worked with UAP researchers, among them Dr. Steven Greer, to bring whistleblower testimony to the public.

## Michael Herrera Coverage

In June 2023, before the National Press Club press conference, the Daily Mail published an article on [[Michael Herrera]]'s account of his UAP encounter, based on an interview by reporter Josh Boswell. [[Steven Greer]] helped arrange the article. It included text messages from one of Herrera's fellow Marines who had been present at the 2009 encounter in Indonesia. Asked through Herrera to give anonymous testimony to the Senate Intelligence Committee, the Marine replied "leave me out of that".

The Daily Mail also published one of only two publicly known photographs of Herrera. It was later used to try to identify him in a photograph provided by Nathan, another Marine who contradicted Herrera's account, but that identification was disputed on the basis of an analysis of the camouflage pattern.
