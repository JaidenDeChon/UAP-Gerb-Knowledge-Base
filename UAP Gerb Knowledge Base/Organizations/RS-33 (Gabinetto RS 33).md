**Gabinetto Ricerche Speciali 33** (Special Research Cabinet 33), commonly abbreviated as **RS-33**, was a top-secret Italian government commission that fascist dictator [[Benito Mussolini]] set up in June 1933. Its job was to examine and analyze the unknown craft recovered near [[Magenta, Italy]] on June 13, 1933, and to keep it strictly secret. The group was an Italian precursor to later alleged U.S. crash retrieval organizations such as [[Majestic 12]]. In fascist Italy it worked with complete autonomy and controlled all information on UFO-related matters.

## Leadership and Membership

Mussolini headed RS-33 himself, in his capacity as Prime Minister (Il Duce). Foreign Minister [[Galeazzo Ciano]] and Air Marshal [[Italo Balbo]] provided political and military oversight. Astronomer [[Gino Cecchini]] gave scientific support, possibly along with the Nobel Prize-winning radio pioneer [[Guglielmo Marconi]]. Marconi reportedly believed the craft was extraterrestrial in origin, while Mussolini at first judged it to be an advanced terrestrial vehicle from France, Britain, or Germany.

According to original documents provided to researcher [[Roberto Pinotti]], the protocol memorandum was addressed to a "De Santis" (rendered "Desanti" in translation). Italian historian Arrigo Petacco identified this man as [[Tommaso David]], an officer from the province of Frosinone. David founded the "Volpi Argentate" (Silver Foxes) espionage group and later headed the counter-intelligence service of the Salò republic.

## Operating Protocols

Internal memorandums bearing the seal of the Kingdom Senate set out a systematic nine-step protocol for UFO crash retrievals and for handling sightings:

1. Immediate notification of local authorities (mayors)
2. Immediate recovery of any craft
3. Immediate arrest of all witnesses
4. Appointment of an RS-33 special section from the OVRA (Italian secret police) in each provincial capital
5. Reporting only to the central meteorological office at La Sapienza University in Rome, under the RS-33 designation
6. Ex officio prevention of press coverage
7. Publication of disinformation using the cover stories prescribed in RS-33 form FZ-4 (meteor, shooting star, luminous halo, etc.)
8. Notification of the Air Force only with prior RS-33 approval; RS-33 decisions could not be appealed, up to the level of Il Duce himself
9. Strict exclusion of all other scientific bodies, including pontifical universities, from examining the evidence

These protocols are strikingly similar to the U.S. procedures later codified in [[USAF Regulation 200-2]] in 1953. That regulation also centralized UFO reporting, restricted information sharing with scientific bodies, and kept UAP evidence under strict military control.

## Funding and Infrastructure

Crash retrieval expenses were charged to a specific budget line, "Cap 32, Title XXII, heading 32-2 of the order to the R. Academia de Italia," which indicates that RS-33 operations had dedicated funding.

Through OVRA, the fascist secret police, the group set up a network across Italy's provincial capitals so that it could respond quickly to a UFO incident anywhere in the country. All reports went only to Rome for RS-33 to evaluate, and the group alone decided whether a case warranted Air Force involvement.

## Parallel to US Programs

RS-33's structure, protocols, and autonomous authority closely parallel allegations about the U.S. [[Majestic 12]] group, which allegedly emerged in the late 1940s and early 1950s after the [[1947 Roswell Crash]]. Both organizations allegedly operated:
- Outside normal governmental oversight
- With the authority to manage or detain witnesses immediately
- Through dedicated disinformation campaigns
- With complete control over scientific access to materials
- Via compartmentalized funding mechanisms
- With unappealable decision-making authority at the highest levels

## Dissolution

RS-33 remained operational throughout Mussolini's fascist regime, from 1933 until the Italian state collapsed in 1943. According to some accounts, the Germans dissolved RS-33 after they took over Italian facilities in 1943 during Operation Ox. In 1944-1945, agents of the U.S. [[Office of Strategic Services (OSS)]] ultimately recovered the craft that RS-33 had examined, helped in part by intelligence provided by [[Pope Pius XII]].

## Document Trail

The existence and operating procedures of RS-33 are confirmed by authenticated fascist-era documents that the anonymous source [[Mr. X]] provided to [[Roberto Pinotti]]. They include telegrams stamped with "R/S" markings, internal memorandums bearing the Kingdom Senate seal, and handwritten notes describing UFO encounters between 1933 and 1940.
