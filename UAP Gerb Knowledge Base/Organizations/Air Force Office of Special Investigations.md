The Air Force Office of Special Investigations (AFOSI) is the U.S. Air Force's primary federal law enforcement and counterintelligence agency, responsible for criminal investigations, counterintelligence, and counterespionage across Air Force and Space Force personnel, programs, and facilities. Within AFOSI, the Office of Special Projects — internally designated AFOSI/PJ — provides dedicated security and counterintelligence support for the Air Force's most sensitive Special Access Programs. In UAP research, AFOSI/PJ is repeatedly identified as a program-protection and counterintelligence element embedded within the alleged UFO legacy program apparatus, both as a security enforcer against whistleblowers and as a formal counterintelligence partner to outside agencies.

## Office of Special Projects (PJ)

AFOSI/PJ was historically headed by [[Terry Phillips]], a nearly three-decade member of the Defense Senior Executive Service who also directed the Air Force's department-wide Special Access Program security enterprise before retiring from federal service around January 2022 and being succeeded by [[Lee M. Russ]]. AFOSI/PJ maintains permanent offices both in [[Crystal City, Virginia]] and at [[Hanscom Air Force Base, Massachusetts|Hanscom Air Force Base]] — the base where [[MITRE Corporation|MITRE]] opened its first program office.

## Role in Alleged UFO Legacy Programs

UAP Gerb alleges that AFOSI/PJ has historically provided counterintelligence support to MITRE's alleged UFO technology reverse-engineering and USO-monitoring programs, working alongside the [[Office of Naval Intelligence]] and [[Naval Criminal Investigative Service]] on the Navy side of that alleged effort. Separately, UAP Gerb's *Special Access Required Vol.2* alleges that Phillips, prior to his 2021 retirement, acted as "an enforcer for the UFO activity administrative structure, leveraging administrative terrorism and much worse against prospective whistleblowers," and that he continued similar work in a private security role at [[Northrop Grumman]] following his federal retirement.
