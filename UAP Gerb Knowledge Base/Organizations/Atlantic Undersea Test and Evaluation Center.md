The Atlantic Undersea Test and Evaluation Center (AUTEC) is a U.S. Navy major range and test facility base (MRTFB) located in the "Tongue of the Ocean," a deep-water trench off Andros Island in the Bahamas that plunges to roughly 2,000 meters, immediately adjacent to waters that continue to drop to 4,000 meters or more in the Blake–Bahama Basin further into the Atlantic. AUTEC conducts test and evaluation work on advanced submarines, weapons systems, and sonar technology, taking advantage of the area's exceptional natural depth and acoustic isolation.
