The Security and Intelligence Directorate (SID) is an internal support directorate at the [[DARPA|Defense Advanced Research Projects Agency]]. It is responsible for security across DARPA's high-risk research and development portfolio, covering information, personnel, industrial and physical security at DARPA and at its contractor sites. It provides "risk-managed, proactive program protection" through embedded program security representatives (PSRs). The PSRs support the whole research portfolio instead of being split up by classification category, from fundamental research and controlled unclassified information to collateral classified work, Special Access Programs and sensitive compartmented information activities. References to SID go back to at least 2003. SID reports through the DARPA Director, so its administrative chain runs up to the Office of the Under Secretary of Defense for Research and Engineering (USD R&E).

## Contractor Support

Since 2012, [[System High]], a private counterintelligence and security firm, has provided security services to SID. The firm has held consecutive multi-year SID contracts, renewed in later award cycles, including a renewal in 2022.
