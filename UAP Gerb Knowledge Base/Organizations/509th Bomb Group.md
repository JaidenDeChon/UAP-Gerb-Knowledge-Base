The 509th Bomb Group was a U.S. Army Air Forces strategic bombing unit based at Roswell Army Air Field in 1947. It carried out the atomic missions against Hiroshima and Nagasaki, and it was the institution at the center of the first response at Roswell.

## Roswell-Era Role

People linked to the 509th took a direct part in reporting and handling the claims of recovered debris in July 1947. They included public information officer Walter Haut and intelligence officer Jesse Marcel.

## Significance In UAP Research

Researchers who argue that the Roswell case was handled as a high-priority national security matter, and not as the routine recovery of a weather balloon, often point to the group's elite status and its history of nuclear missions.
