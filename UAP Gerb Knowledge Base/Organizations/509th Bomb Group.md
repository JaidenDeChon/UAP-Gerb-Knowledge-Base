The 509th Bomb Group was a U.S. Army Air Forces strategic bombing unit based at Roswell Army Air Field in 1947. It is historically notable both for carrying out the Hiroshima and Nagasaki atomic missions and for its central institutional role in the initial Roswell response.

## Roswell-Era Role

Personnel associated with the 509th, including public information officer Walter Haut and intelligence officer Jesse Marcel, were directly involved in the July 1947 reporting and handling of recovered debris claims.

## Significance In UAP Research

The group's elite status and nuclear mission background are frequently cited by researchers who argue that Roswell-related handling reflected a high-priority national security process rather than routine weather-balloon recovery.
