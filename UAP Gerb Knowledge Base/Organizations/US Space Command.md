United States Space Command is a unified combatant command of the US military responsible for operations in space. It was first established on 23 September 1985, with headquarters at Peterson Air Force Base, Colorado. In 2002 it was disestablished and its duties were absorbed by US Strategic Command. It was reestablished on 29 August 2019 as the eleventh unified combatant command. It is a separate body from the [[US Space Force]], the military service created in December 2019. In Gerb's videos it appears as a source of tracking records, as a recipient of a 2023 briefing on UAP recovery, and on a list of organizations alleged to take part in retrieval programs.

## Tracking records

Researcher [[Stan Gordon]] obtained records through FOIA requests to US Space Command and the Naval Surveillance Center showing that the Soviet probe [[Cosmos 96]] entered the atmosphere over Canada at about 3:18 a.m. Eastern time on 9 December 1965, roughly 13 hours before the fireball seen over Kecksburg, Pennsylvania. Gordon concluded from them that Cosmos 96 was not the object that came down at [[1965 Kecksburg, Pennsylvania Crash Retrieval|Kecksburg]], and Gerb relays the records in his video on the case.

In his video on [[Jonathan Weygandt]], Gerb cites [[Jeffrey T. Richelson]]'s book on the [[NEST (Nuclear Emergency Support Team)|Nuclear Emergency Support Team]]: when Russia's Mars 96 probe failed to leave Earth orbit, NEST considered deploying to Bolivia or Colombia on the strength of intelligence from US Space Command and [[Defense Support Program (DSP)|Defense Support Program]] satellites, and stood down when the debris fell into the Pacific.

## Kirkpatrick's 2023 briefing

A US Space Command FOIA release, recovered by a researcher in September 2024, records a January 2023 meeting that [[Sean Kirkpatrick]], then director of [[AARO]], scheduled with Space Command and senior joint command staff on "UAP response and recovery and material transfer". Gerb cites it as support for the view that Kirkpatrick was an insider whose job was to squash information. He adds that Kirkpatrick had led an intelligence community task force for Space Command in 2018–2019, and that Kirkpatrick's firm, Nonlinear Solutions LLC, later worked as a subcontractor to [[MITRE Corporation|MITRE]] on its federally funded research center serving the command.

## Alleged role in retrievals

[[Christopher Sharp]]'s 20 September 2024 *Liberation Times* article named Space Command alongside the CIA's science and technology and operations directorates, the [[National Underwater Reconnaissance Office]], the Navy, the [[NRO (National Reconnaissance Office)|NRO]], the [[NGA]] and [[JSOC (Joint Special Operations Command)|JSOC]] as organizations involved in UFO retrieval programs. Gerb calls the article a cornerstone of his research. He also relays a claim by [[Jim Shell]], a former NRO technical director and chief scientist of Air Force Space Command, that a "security control system" is supplanting the authority of the Space Force and Space Command and interfering with space domain awareness. With medium confidence, Shell tied this to misappropriated funds and UAP.
