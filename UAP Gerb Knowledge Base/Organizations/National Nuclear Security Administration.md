The National Nuclear Security Administration (NNSA) is a semi-autonomous agency within the US Department of Energy. It manages the US nuclear weapons stockpile, naval nuclear propulsion and nuclear nonproliferation programs, and is responsible for emergency response to nuclear and radiological incidents. The National Defense Authorization Act established the NNSA in 2000. The agency operates several national laboratories, including Sandia National Laboratories and Lawrence Livermore National Laboratory, and manages the [[NEST (Nuclear Emergency Support Team)|Nuclear Emergency Support Team (NEST)]], its premier rapid radiological response unit. Its Office of Secure Transportation (OST) physically transports nuclear materials and weapons components between secure facilities.

## Alleged UAP Involvement

The NNSA and its subordinate organizations are alleged to play multiple roles in UAP crash retrieval operations. NEST allegedly provides radiological assessment teams at UAP crash sites, which is consistent with the DOE personnel in MOPP gear that [[Jonathan Weygandt]] observed at the alleged 1997 Peru crash. The NNSA's [[Office of Secure Transportation (OST)]] is allegedly used to move recovered UAP materials between secure facilities. Sandia National Laboratories and Lawrence Livermore National Laboratory are also cited in connection with reverse engineering of allegedly recovered non-human technologies, including the magnetic field disruptor components claimed to underlie the TR-3B alien reproduction vehicle program.

## The Sandia Investigation

In his investigation of [[Sandia National Laboratories]], Gerb argues that the NNSA is "severely overlooked" in discussion of UFO legacy programs. He believes that the agency, acting under the [[Atomic Energy Act of 1954]] (the source of the NNSA's core authorities), works with its three multi-mission laboratories, Sandia, [[Los Alamos National Laboratory|Los Alamos]] and [[Lawrence Livermore National Laboratory|Lawrence Livermore]], to recover and move downed craft within the United States. In his theory, NEST provides the on-site scientific team, the [[Office of Secure Transportation (OST)|Office of Secure Transportation]] the transport, and the [[DOE Special Response Teams (SRTs)|Special Response Teams]] armed protection. All three have ties to Sandia. His main evidence is the [[2008 Needles UFO Crash Retrieval|2008 Needles crash]], where witnesses said recovery personnel wore Nevada Test Site uniforms or NNSA badges and George Knapp identified the convoys as OST. Gerb later adds the [[Defense Threat Reduction Agency (DTRA)|Defense Threat Reduction Agency]] as a likely support agency.

The NNSA runs the [[Nevada National Security Site (Nevada Test Site)|Nevada National Security Site]] through its Nevada Field Office. The site includes [[Area 6 (Nevada Test Site)|Area 6]], an area for top secret sensor and drone testing that NNSA spokesperson Darwin Morgan acknowledged in 2016. Gerb proposes it as a possible legacy-program site.
