Majestic 12 (abbreviated MJ-12, also called Magic 12 or MAJIC-12) is the name of an alleged top-secret U.S. government control group established by executive order of President Harry S. Truman on September 24, 1947, to oversee the recovery, analysis, reverse-engineering, and compartmentalization of non-human technology and biological entities. According to leaked documents, the group was formed under recommendation by [[Vannevar Bush]] (head of the Office of Scientific Research and Development) and [[James Forrestal]] (Secretary of Defense) following the [[Roswell Crash]]. Truman's daily appointment calendar contains a confirmed meeting with Forrestal and Bush on September 24, 1947, arranged at Forrestal's suggestion.

The existence of Majestic 12 remains disputed, with approximately 3,500 pages of alleged MJ-12 documents leaked between 1984-1999 from seven different sources. Researchers [[Ryan S. Wood]] and [[Robert Wood]] have conducted extensive forensic authentication analysis, identifying multiple documents—particularly the [[Special Operations Manual (SOM 1-01)]] and [[Eisenhower Briefing Document]]—as exhibiting strong authenticity markers including period-correct typography, verifiable personnel connections, and corroboration from witnesses and official records.

## Organizational Structure and Mission

According to the leaked documents, Majestic 12 consisted of twelve members—six military (two each from Army, Navy, and Air Force) and six civilians—reporting directly to the President. The [[Special Operations Manual]] describes MJ-12's goals as:

1. Recovery for scientific study of all materials and devices of foreign or extraterrestrial manufacture by any and all means
2. Recovery for study of all entities and remains of non-terrestrial origin
3. Establishment and administration of special teams to accomplish recovery operations
4. Establishment and administration of special secure facilities at secret U.S. locations for receiving, processing, analysis, and study of materials and entities classified as extraterrestrial
5. Covert operations carried in collusion with the CIA both domestically and abroad
6. Establishment of absolute secrecy concerning all operations

The [[Eisenhower Briefing Document]] states that MJ-12 was established as a "top secret Research and Development/Intelligence operation reporting only to the President of the United States." As of 1954, according to SOM 1-01, the group did not consider non-human intelligence to be a threat, characterizing it as a benign presence but possessing technology far beyond human science.

## The 1954 Turning Point

[[Ryan S. Wood]] identifies 1954 as a critical transition year. SOM 1-01 was created in 1954.** The [[1954 Atomic Energy Act]] established "restricted data" classification allowing non-government-created information to be born classified, potentially providing legal framework for reclassifying crashed non-terrestrial materials as "trans-classified foreign nuclear materials" (the same mechanism referenced in the 2024 Schumer UAP disclosure amendment).

The concentration of leaked documents from the pre-1954 era, with far less detail in post-1954 materials, supports the theory that this reclassification "pulled the rug out" from under Air Force officers running MJ-12 operations, transferring control elsewhere—possibly motivating leakers to preserve documentation of programs being memory-holed. The **Fifth Annual Majestic Report**, one of the last detailed operational documents, may represent final documentation before reorganization.

Wood suggests the shift moved substantial control to private defense contractors, creating the architecture for modern Special Access Programs operating outside traditional government oversight. He identifies likely current MJ-12 participants/contractors as: [[Aerospace Corporation]], [[Carlyle Group]], [[EG&G]], [[RAND Corporation]] ("up to their eyeballs in this stuff"), and [[Battelle Memorial Institute]].

## Evidentiary Status and Authentication

Documents associated with MJ-12 have been disputed since the first leak in 1984, with the FBI declaring the Eisenhower Briefing Document "bogus" after circulating it to three-letter agencies who claimed no knowledge—though Wood notes this non-denial is precisely the expected response for a legitimately classified SAP.

Forensic authentication work by the Woods has identified multiple strong authenticity markers:
- Period-correct typography, fonts, and printing methods (hot lead press artifacts)
- Verifiable personnel connections (individuals matching document control initials found at correct locations/times)
- Witness testimony ([[Dale Bailey]], [[Dr. Eric A. Walker]])
- Cross-document corroboration (references matching across independently obtained documents)
- National Archives corroboration (Cutler-Twining memo, Paperclip memo with "CIA/MJ-12" notation)
- Government acknowledgment of related entities ([[Interplanetary Phenomenon Unit]] existence confirmed via FOIA)

Ryan Wood states: "I have no credible evidence that any document listed on [Majesticdocuments.com] is fake" and has found "no credible objections" that withstand forensic scrutiny.
