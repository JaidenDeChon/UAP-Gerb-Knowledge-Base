Majestic 12 (abbreviated MJ-12, also called Magic 12 or MAJIC-12) is the name of an alleged top-secret U.S. government control group established by executive order of President Harry S. Truman on September 24, 1947, to oversee the recovery, analysis, reverse-engineering, and compartmentalization of non-human technology and biological entities. According to leaked documents, the group was formed on the recommendation of [[Vannevar Bush]] (head of the Office of Scientific Research and Development) and [[James Forrestal]] (Secretary of Defense) following the [[Roswell Crash]]. Truman's daily appointment calendar contains a confirmed meeting with Forrestal and Bush on September 24, 1947, arranged at Forrestal's suggestion.

The existence of Majestic 12 is still disputed. About 3,500 pages of alleged MJ-12 documents leaked from seven different sources between 1984 and 1999. Researchers [[Ryan S. Wood]] and [[Robert Wood]] have done extensive forensic work to authenticate them. In their analysis, several documents, especially the [[Special Operations Manual (SOM 1-01)]] and the [[Eisenhower Briefing Document]], show strong signs of authenticity, including period-correct typography, personnel connections that can be verified, and corroboration from witnesses and official records.

## Organizational Structure and Mission

According to the leaked documents, Majestic 12 had twelve members who reported directly to the President: six from the military (two each from the Army, Navy and Air Force) and six civilians. The [[Special Operations Manual (SOM 1-01)|Special Operations Manual]] describes MJ-12's goals as:

1. Recovery for scientific study of all materials and devices of foreign or extraterrestrial manufacture by any and all means
2. Recovery for study of all entities and remains of non-terrestrial origin
3. Establishment and administration of special teams to accomplish recovery operations
4. Establishment and administration of special secure facilities at secret U.S. locations for receiving, processing, analysis, and study of materials and entities classified as extraterrestrial
5. Covert operations carried in collusion with the CIA both domestically and abroad
6. Establishment of absolute secrecy concerning all operations

The [[Eisenhower Briefing Document]] states that MJ-12 was established as a "top secret Research and Development/Intelligence operation reporting only to the President of the United States." According to SOM 1-01, as of 1954 the group did not consider non-human intelligence a threat. The manual says the group regarded it as a benign presence, though one with technology far beyond human science.

## Original and Subsequent Members

### Original Twelve (1947)

The Eisenhower Briefing Document names the founding members:
- MJ-1: Admiral [[Roscoe H. Hillenkoetter]], the first CIA Director and the designated briefing officer for Eisenhower
- Dr. Vannevar Bush, head of the Office of Scientific Research and Development and a principal architect of the group
- James Forrestal, Secretary of the Navy and the first Secretary of Defense (died May 22, 1949; replaced by [[Walter Bedell Smith|General Walter B. Smith]])
- General Nathan Twining, of Air Material Command, who led Project Sign and the Roswell recovery
- General Hoyt S. Vandenberg, the second Director of Central Intelligence and Air Force Chief of Staff
- [[Donald Menzel|Donald H. Menzel]], a Harvard astronomer and NSA/CIA consultant
- Admiral Sidney W. Souers
- Gordon Gray
- Dr. Detlev Bronk, who performed the Roswell autopsies and coined the term "EBE" (Extraterrestrial Biological Entity)
- Dr. Jerome Hunsaker
- Dr. Robert Montague
- General Walter B. Smith, who replaced Forrestal on August 1, 1950; he was Ambassador to the USSR and the second CIA Director

Truman's appointment calendar confirms an off-the-record meeting with Walter B. Smith on August 1, 1950, the same date on which, according to the Eisenhower Briefing Document, he replaced Forrestal.

### Extended Network

The White Hot Report, another Majestic document, lists a wider Roswell investigation team beyond the core twelve members, which points to a larger network:
- J. Robert Oppenheimer (Manhattan Project)
- Wernher von Braun (rocket scientist)
- Theodore von Kármán (aerospace engineer)
- James Doolittle (USAF General)

[[Eric A. Walker]], a protégé of Vannevar Bush who eventually headed Bush's organization, confirmed MJ-12's existence to researchers and admitted that he had been present at the [[1965 Kecksburg, Pennsylvania Crash Retrieval|1965 Kecksburg, Pennsylvania crash]]. He also made a cryptic remark that the documents were like "Don Quixote chasing at windmills". [[Ryan S. Wood]] takes this to mean that researchers might obtain the documents but would never fully grasp the program's extent.

## Key Documents

The principal documents and their provenance are described in [[MJ-12 Documents]]. The most closely studied are the [[Eisenhower Briefing Document]] (dated 18 November 1952, leaked to [[Jaime Shandera]] in 1984), the [[Special Operations Manual (SOM 1-01)]] (1954, leaked to [[Don Berliner]] in 1994), the [[Interplanetary Phenomenon Unit]] Field Order and Report, the Cutler–Twining memo and the [[Bowen Manuscript]].

## James Forrestal's Death

On May 22, 1949, James Forrestal fell (or was pushed) from the 13th floor of Bethesda Naval Hospital. Official reports called it a suicide caused by depression, but Forrestal had reportedly been pressing for greater openness on the UFO subject. One Majestic document says it was "necessary and regrettable" that Forrestal had to lose his life. Wood's book *Magic Eyes Only* includes a dedication to those who lost their lives for UFO secrecy, and Forrestal is prominent in it.

## The 1954 Turning Point

[[Ryan S. Wood]] identifies 1954 as a critical year of transition. SOM 1-01 was created in 1954. The [[Atomic Energy Act of 1954]] established the "restricted data" classification, under which information not created by the government can be born classified. This could have provided a legal framework for reclassifying crashed non-terrestrial materials as "trans-classified foreign nuclear materials" (the same mechanism referenced in the 2024 Schumer UAP disclosure amendment).

Most of the leaked documents date from before 1954, and the material from after 1954 has far less detail. This supports the theory that the reclassification "pulled the rug out" from under the Air Force officers running MJ-12 operations and moved control elsewhere, which may have led leakers to preserve records of programs that were being memory-holed. The Fifth Annual Majestic Report, one of the last detailed operational documents, may be the final record made before the reorganization.

Wood suggests the shift moved much of the control to private defense contractors and laid the groundwork for today's Special Access Programs, which operate outside traditional government oversight. He names the likely current MJ-12 participants or contractors as [[Aerospace Corporation]], [[Carlyle Group]], [[EG&G]], [[RAND Corporation]] ("up to their eyeballs in this stuff") and [[Battelle Memorial Institute]].

## Evidentiary Status and Authentication

MJ-12 documents have been disputed since the first leak in 1984. The FBI declared the Eisenhower Briefing Document "bogus" after circulating it to three-letter agencies, which claimed to have no knowledge of it. Wood notes that this non-denial is exactly the response to expect for a legitimately classified Special Access Program (SAP).

The Woods' forensic authentication work has found several strong signs of authenticity:
- Period-correct typography, fonts, and printing methods (hot lead press artifacts)
- Verifiable personnel connections (individuals matching document control initials found at correct locations/times)
- Witness testimony (Dale Bailey, [[Eric A. Walker]])
- Cross-document corroboration (references matching across independently obtained documents)
- National Archives corroboration (Cutler-Twining memo, Paperclip memo with "CIA/MJ-12" notation)
- Government acknowledgment of related entities ([[Interplanetary Phenomenon Unit]] existence confirmed via FOIA)

Ryan Wood states: "I have no credible evidence that any document listed on [Majesticdocuments.com] is fake" and has found "no credible objections" that withstand forensic scrutiny.

## The NSC 5412 Committee Connection

Gerb's investigation of the Manhattan Project 2.0 security architecture puts forward the theory that Eisenhower, soon after taking office, effectively hid the UFO control group (MAJ-12, or whatever its successor was named) inside the National Security Council by setting up the NSC 5412 Committee on March 15, 1954. Formally, the 5412 Committee was an interdepartmental body that reviewed and approved covert US operations and defined covert operations for US policy purposes. Its original members were the Deputy Under Secretary of State, the Deputy Secretary of Defense, the President's Special Assistant for National Security Affairs, and the CIA Director as action officer.

The Eisenhower Briefing Document names Gordon Gray, then Truman's Assistant Secretary of the Army, as an original MJ-12 member. From 1958 to 1961 Gray was also Eisenhower's National Security Advisor, which made him a member of the 5412 Committee. That gives a direct personnel link between the alleged MAJ-12 and the 5412 structure.

Like all informal NSC meetings with members of relevant expertise, the 5412 Committee's informal meetings can be held off the books and go unrecorded. They gave the UFO control group a way to operate without formal documentation, in effect a self-perpetuating structure that could transcend presidential oversight. This group evolved through the 303 Committee and later iterations before fracturing during the SAP reform era of the 1980s.

## Renaming to the 303 Committee (1964)

Gerb's *80 Years of UFO Crash Retrieval and Reverse Engineering* traces how the hidden MJ-12 steering group continued on paper through National Security Action Memorandum (NSAM) 303, issued in June 1964 under President Lyndon B. Johnson. NSAM 303 renamed the 5412 Committee's Special Group the "303 Committee," a change the video characterizes as "little more than a name change" made for bookkeeping purposes rather than a substantive reorganization. According to the video, the renamed body went on housing the alleged MJ-12 function within the National Security Council's structure for overseeing covert activities into the subsequent Nixon administration.

In the video's account, the lineage runs from MJ-12 hidden within NSC 10/2 (1947), to its absorption into the 5412 Committee's Special Group (1954), to its renaming as the 303 Committee through NSAM 303 (1964). The video presents this lineage as essentially intact through the "golden era" of the legacy programs. It says the lineage only fractured, and became genuinely difficult to trace, during the Cold War reorganization period, which the video dates from 1978 to 1994. In that period, centralized legacy leadership is alleged to have progressively lost control over program offices, assets, materials and records.
