Majestic 12 (often abbreviated MJ-12) is the name of an alleged classified U.S. control group said in UFO literature to have overseen retrieval, analysis, and compartmented management of non-human technology after the 1947 Roswell incident. The group is central to modern UAP conspiracy narratives, but its existence remains unverified in mainstream archival history.

## Claimed Function in UAP Narratives

In allegations surrounding legacy UAP programs, Majestic 12 is described as a high-level interagency structure with authority over:

- crash retrieval operations,
- reverse-engineering and materials exploitation,
- witness/intelligence compartmentalization,
- and long-term information control through security classification and disinformation.

This portrayal treats MJ-12 as an umbrella management body rather than a conventional bureaucratic office.

## Relationship to RS-33 Comparisons

In [[Video - The 1933 Magenta, Italy UFO Crash]], the host argues that [[RS-33 (Gabinetto RS 33)]] in fascist Italy functioned as a structural precursor to what Majestic 12 is alleged to have become in the United States. The comparison is based on similar claimed patterns:

- centralized authority over anomalous craft reporting,
- exclusion of outside scientific review,
- and use of controlled public narratives for suppression.

This comparison is interpretive and belongs to the video's analytical framework rather than established state archives.

## Key Documents and Referenced Incidents

### Eisenhower Briefing Document

The alleged MJ-12 Eisenhower Briefing Document, purportedly prepared in 1952 to brief President-Elect Dwight D. Eisenhower, describes several crash retrieval cases including the 1947 Roswell incident and a December 6, 1950 impact in the [[El Indio, Texas]]–Guerrero, Mexico area. The 1950 entry describes a craft that "impacted at high speeds" and "was nearly obliterated," with recovered materials transported to the "Atomic Energy Commission's Sandia facility" in New Mexico. UAP Gerb notes the document combines what may be a mixture of disinformation and factual information, consistent with the position of researcher [[Stanton Friedman]].

## Evidentiary Status

Documents associated with MJ-12 have long been disputed, with ongoing debate over provenance, authenticity, and possible disinformation. As a result, references to Majestic 12 are generally presented as claims within UAP research communities, not settled historical fact.
