E-Systems, Inc. was a major U.S. defense-electronics contractor. It began as LTV Electrosystems, a division spun off from the Ling-Temco-Vought (LTV) conglomerate in December 1964. In 1972 LTV Electrosystems became fully independent and was renamed E-Systems. The company specialized in signals intelligence, reconnaissance, and command-and-control electronics for U.S. intelligence and defense agencies. It developed a public reputation for extreme operational secrecy, including in a 1994 *Washington Post* feature. Its leadership included former NSA Director Admiral Bobby Ray Inman and former CIA Director Admiral William F. Raborn. In 1975 the company acquired Air Asia, a known CIA proprietary airline. E-Systems was acquired by Raytheon in 1995 and later became part of L-3 Communications.
