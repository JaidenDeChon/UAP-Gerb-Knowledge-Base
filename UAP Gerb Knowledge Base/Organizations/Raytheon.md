**Raytheon** is a major American defense contractor, founded in 1922 in Cambridge, Massachusetts, and known for missiles, radar and defense electronics. In 1997 it bought the defense business of [[Texas Instruments]]. In 2020 Raytheon merged with United Technologies to form Raytheon Technologies. That company was renamed RTX in 2023, and Raytheon remains one of its business units.

## UAP Significance

The [[Infographic Show Whistleblower]] names Raytheon, with [[Boeing]], as one of the big defense contractors running "co-op sites" with the government, where pieces of recovered craft were studied. Gerb notes that the astrophysicist [[Bernard Haisch]] also named Raytheon among the aerospace companies where legacy UAP programs are said to be hidden, along with Lockheed Martin, the Aerospace Corporation and TRW.
