The Galactic Federation is a claimed or theorized governing body made up of multiple extraterrestrial species that interact, communicate, and cooperate under a shared organization, much like an interstellar United Nations or federal government. The idea has roots in science fiction, most visibly Star Trek's United Federation of Planets and Star Wars' Galactic Senate. Several figures in UAP and intelligence community disclosure circles have asserted that it really exists, most prominently former Israeli Space Security Chief [[Haim Eshed]] in 2020 and alleged former [[CIA]] officer [[John Ramirez]].

## Claimed Existence

In December 2020, [[Haim Eshed]], who directed Israel's military Space Program for nearly 30 years, said in an interview with the Israeli newspaper Yediot Aharonot that:

- A Galactic Federation of extraterrestrial species exists.
- Some members of the Federation are already present on Earth.
- The Federation had requested that governments delay public disclosure because humanity was not psychologically prepared for contact.
- A joint underground base on Mars hosts both US astronauts and extraterrestrial representatives.

[[John Ramirez]], an alleged former CIA officer, has independently made similar claims: that a Galactic Federation exists today and has an ongoing relationship with elements of the US government.

## Speculation and Context

No government, independent researcher, or physical evidence has corroborated the claims that the Federation exists. Eshed had spent nearly three decades directing classified space defense programs at the highest level of the Israeli government, and those credentials gave his statements unusual weight in UAP discussion compared with the typical anonymous claim.

Some UAP researchers regard the broader idea of a galactic governing body as plausible. They accept that multiple extraterrestrial civilizations are probably interacting with Earth and with each other, and that a structured presence of several species would logically require some kind of diplomatic or organizational framework.
