Lawrence Livermore National Laboratory (LLNL) is a federally funded research and development center ([[FFRDC (Federally Funded Research and Development Centers)|FFRDC]]) and [[Department of Energy]] national laboratory located in Livermore, California. According to [[Edgar Fouché]], LLNL and [[Sandia National Laboratories]] co-developed the reverse-engineered [[Magnetic Field Disruptor (MFD)]], the gravity disruption technology at the core of the [[TR-3B]]'s propulsion system.

Sandia and Livermore both already have expertise in plasma physics, nuclear weapons design and advanced materials science. Those fields bear directly on how the MFD is described as working, by pressurizing mercury-based plasma to 250,000 atmospheres. As DOE FFRDCs, both labs sit outside the traditional military classification channels, and the [[Atomic Energy Act of 1954]] could shield MFD-related programs under the frameworks for transclassified foreign nuclear information.
