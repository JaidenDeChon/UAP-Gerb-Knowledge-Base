The Air Force Operational Test and Evaluation Center (AFOTEC) is the Air Force's independent test agency. It is a direct reporting unit under Headquarters U.S. Air Force, responsible for testing new systems for Air Force and multi-service use under operationally realistic conditions. Founded in January 1974 and headquartered at [[Kirtland Air Force Base, New Mexico|Kirtland Air Force Base]], it has detachments at Eglin Air Force Base, Hill Air Force Base (in the [[Utah Test and Training Range]]), [[Edwards Air Force Base]] and [[Nellis Air Force Base]] (on the [[Nevada Test and Training Range (NTR)]]). Its test teams collect, analyze and evaluate data at these sites and prepare formal reports. It follows T&E policy set by the Policy, Programs and Resources Division of [[Air Force Test and Evaluation (AF-TE)]].
