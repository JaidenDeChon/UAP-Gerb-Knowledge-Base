The American Association for the Advancement of Science (AAAS) is an international nonprofit organization that works to advance science, engineering, and innovation. Founded in 1848, it is one of the world's largest and most influential general scientific societies, and it publishes the prestigious journal *Science*. In UAP history, the AAAS is notable for publishing praise of the [[Condon Report]] in 1969 that called it "the most thorough UFO investigation ever conducted". That endorsement gave the report's dismissive conclusions a good deal of credibility and helped entrench the [[UFO Stigma]].

## Endorsement of the Condon Report

After the [[Condon Report]] was released, *Science*, the official publication of the AAAS, ran a review describing it as "unquestionably the most thorough and sophisticated investigation of the nebulous UFO phenomena ever conducted." Coming from one of the most respected peer-reviewed journals in the world, the review gave the report's conclusions scientific legitimacy. It did so despite criticism from [[J. Allen Hynek]] and [[James McDonald]], who said the report had ignored important evidence and failed to address the 25–30% of cases that remained unexplained.

Because the AAAS had endorsed it, the Condon Report's recommendations, including the end of official UFO investigations, carried significant weight in the scientific and governmental establishment.
