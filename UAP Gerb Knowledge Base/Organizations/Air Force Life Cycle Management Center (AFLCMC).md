The Air Force Life Cycle Management Center (AFLCMC) is one of six major operational centers under [[Air Force Materiel Command (AFMC)|Air Force Materiel Command]] (AFMC), headquartered at [[Wright-Patterson Air Force Base]], Ohio, and activated on July 9, 2012. AFLCMC is the single center responsible for total life-cycle management of Air Force aircraft, engines, munitions, and electronic systems — spanning information technology and networks, command/control/communications/intelligence/surveillance/reconnaissance (C4ISR) systems, armaments, strategic systems, aerial platforms, installation and infrastructure management, and combat readiness programs. Lieutenant General [[Shipton|Donna D. Shipton]] has served as AFLCMC's commander since early 2024, its first female commander; she previously served as Military Deputy in the Office of the Assistant Secretary of the Air Force for Acquisition, Technology and Logistics (SAF/AQ or SAF/ATNL) from 2022 to 2023.

## Information Protection Directorate (AFLCMC/IP)

AFLCMC's Information Protection Directorate (AFLCMC/IP) is the center's program-protection element for Air Force acquisition and sustainment, extending across AFMC's other major operating areas, including the [[Air Force Test Center]] and [[Air Force Research Laboratory]], and interfacing with the Air Force's component-level Special Access Program Central Office, SAF/AAZ. AFLCMC/IP is directed by Terrence L. Reynolds, whose responsibilities span information security, personnel security, industrial security, acquisition security, international program security, operations security, the scientific and technical information program, and program protection planning across the Department of the Air Force.
