The Air Force Life Cycle Management Center (AFLCMC) is one of six major operational centers under [[Air Force Materiel Command (AFMC)|Air Force Materiel Command]] (AFMC). It was activated on July 9, 2012, and is headquartered at [[Wright-Patterson Air Force Base]], Ohio. AFLCMC is the single center responsible for the total life-cycle management of Air Force aircraft, engines, munitions, and electronic systems. That work covers information technology and networks, command/control/communications/intelligence/surveillance/reconnaissance (C4ISR) systems, armaments, strategic systems, aerial platforms, installation and infrastructure management, and combat readiness programs. Lieutenant General [[Donna D. Shipton]] has commanded AFLCMC since early 2024 and is its first female commander. From 2022 to 2023 she was Military Deputy in the Office of the Assistant Secretary of the Air Force for Acquisition, Technology and Logistics (SAF/AQ or SAF/ATNL).

## Information Protection Directorate (AFLCMC/IP)

AFLCMC's Information Protection Directorate (AFLCMC/IP) is the part of the center that handles program protection for Air Force acquisition and sustainment. Its reach extends to AFMC's other major operating areas, including the [[Air Force Test Center]] and [[Air Force Research Laboratory]], and it works with SAF/AAZ, the Air Force's component-level Special Access Program Central Office. Terrence L. Reynolds directs AFLCMC/IP. His responsibilities cover information security, personnel security, industrial security, acquisition security, international program security, operations security, the scientific and technical information program, and program protection planning across the Department of the Air Force.
