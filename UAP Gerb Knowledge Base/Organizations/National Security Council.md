The **National Security Council** (NSC) is the President of the United States' principal forum for national security and foreign policy decisions. It was created by the National Security Act of 1947, the same law that unified the armed forces under what became the [[Department of Defense]] and established the [[CIA]]. The President chairs the council. Its members include the Vice President and the Secretaries of State and Defense, and the Director of National Intelligence and the Chairman of the Joint Chiefs of Staff sit on it as advisers. From its earliest years the NSC also directed and approved covert action through a succession of small subcommittees.

In Gerb's research, the NSC is where the UFO legacy programs historically sat. He argues that crash retrieval and exploitation were organized and hidden under it from the late 1940s until 1994.

## Covert-action groups

Oversight of covert action at the NSC passed through several bodies, which Gerb ties to UFO work:

- **NSC 10/2 (1948)**, which gave the CIA authority for covert operations through the Office of Policy Coordination. The channel alleges that a UFO steering group was hidden within this structure.
- **The 5412 Committee Special Group (1954)**, created under [[Dwight D. Eisenhower]] to control covert action. Gerb argues it was the program's control group from 1954 to 1964, the body he calls [[Majestic 12]] or "MJ-12," and notes that its five members match [[David Grusch]]'s statement that five people on the NSC were involved at one time.
- **The 303 Committee (1964-1970)**, its successor. According to the channel, the line of control after it is "a bit of an unknown" until 1994.

## Alleged role in the legacy programs

In *The Manhattan Project 2.0* and *Special Access Required*, Gerb argues that [[Harry Truman]] relegated control of the UFO portfolio to the NSC after the 1947 Act. He also argues that Truman and Eisenhower, through classified executive orders, vested program management there, with support from the intelligence community, the armed forces, [[Vannevar Bush]]'s Research and Development Board and Atomic Energy Commission laboratories. Because the work began under a covert-action body, he argues it was inherently covert action. Following Grusch, he alleges it was later kept in "non-covert action" White House special access programs that carry no congressional reporting requirement (see [[Content-Only Special Access Program]] and [[Covert Action Program (50 U.S. Code § 3093)]]).

According to the channel, in 1994, out of fear that President Clinton would disclose, top-level control moved out of government into a quasi-government and industry control group of just over two dozen people, which still involves the NSC. The channel also alleges that the NSC blessed later activities, including the [[AATIP (Advanced Aerospace Threat Identification Program)|ATIP]] cover effort under [[James Clapper]] and, from about 2017, [[Immaculate Constellation (IMCON)|Immaculate Constellation]]. It criticizes *[[The Age of Disclosure]]* for leaving the NSC off its chart of the programs.
