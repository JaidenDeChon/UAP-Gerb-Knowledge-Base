The US Army 7th Special Forces Group (Airborne) is a special forces unit of the United States Army. It falls under [[JSOC (Joint Special Operations Command)|Special Operations Command]] (SOCOM) and the 1st Special Forces Command, and was historically headquartered at Fort Bragg, North Carolina. The 7th SFG, known as the "Green Berets," specializes in unconventional warfare, direct action, special reconnaissance, counter-proliferation of weapons of mass destruction, and security force assistance. Its main area of responsibility is Central and South America.

## Latin American Operations

The 7th Special Forces Group has carried out many foreign internal defense, counterdrug, and training missions throughout Central and South America. Notable deployments include:

- [[Operation Snow Cap]]: a [[Drug Enforcement Administration|DEA]] counter-narcotics operation in nine Latin American countries, carried out alongside US Navy SEALs.
- [[Operation Laser Strike]]: [[US SOUTHCOM]] authorized up to 14 special forces counterdrug deployments to [[Peru]] during 1997. The 7th SFG conducted riverine training and security operations alongside the [[MARFORLANT Riverine Training Team|MARFORLAND Riverine Training Team]] in [[Iquitos, Peru]].
- 1995 Peru-Ecuador Border Dispute: the 7th SFG operated alongside the [[1st Battalion 228th Aviation Regiment]] from [[Joint Task Force Bravo]] in northern Peru.

## Alleged UAP Involvement

Gerb theorizes that 7th Special Forces Group personnel already deployed to northern Peru for [[Operation Laser Strike]] were the armed enforcement arm of the [[Department of Energy]] [[NEST (Nuclear Emergency Support Team)|NEST]] crash retrieval team that secured the 1997 [[Peru UFO Crash Incident|Peru UFO crash site]]. [[Jonathan Weygandt]] described the armed operators as older men in their late 30s and 40s who wore all-black fatigues without insignia and spoke with American dialects. According to him, they arrived at the crash site before the CH-47 helicopters carrying the DOE scientific team. Their described appearance, behavior, equipment (MP5s, Glocks, shotguns, M16s), and pre-positioning in the region are consistent with an Army Special Forces detachment.
