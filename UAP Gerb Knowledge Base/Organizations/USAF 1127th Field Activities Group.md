The USAF 1127th Field Activities Group was a United States Air Force intelligence unit and the operational command for [[Project Moon Dust]], the classified program that located, recovered and delivered descended foreign space vehicles and unidentified flying objects. A 1967 review prepared for the Assistant Chief of Staff for the Intelligence Community describes the 1127th's functions and confirms its role in Moon Dust collection operations.

## Operational Authority

A draft policy memo of 3 November 1961 from the Air Force intelligence office AFCIN-1E (which Gerb reads as "AFC 1e draft policy") proposed giving the 1127th USAF Field Activities Group collection responsibility for investigating reliably reported unidentified flying objects within the United States. Gerb reads the memo as listing Moon Dust as a project that had historically held this duty. In transcriptions of the draft, the UFO investigation program and Moon Dust, whose task was to locate, recover and deliver descended foreign space vehicles, are described in separate paragraphs.

> [!edit] Corrected 17 October 2026
> Earlier versions called the document the "AFOSR-16 Draft Policy" and stated as fact that Moon Dust had historically handled UFO investigations. In the video Gerb calls it the "AFC 1e draft policy" (the AFCIN-1E draft of 3 November 1961), and the link between Moon Dust and UFO investigation is his reading; the draft treats the two separately.

## Moon Dust Collection Record

According to the 1967 intelligence review, the 1127th USAF Field Activities Group, operating Project Moon Dust, captured:

- 49 fallen Soviet space objects
- 17 US space objects
- Redacted categories, suggesting further classified collection missions beyond the acknowledged recovery of space debris

The large redacted portion of the 1967 memo indicates that the 1127th collected more than space vehicles that had come down on Earth. That fits Moon Dust's documented UFO investigation and crash retrieval operations.

## UFO Investigation Operations

The 1127th coordinated Moon Dust field operations, including:

- The 1968 investigation of a circular disc in Nepal, for which it received State Department cables about the crashed object
- Coordination with [[Wright-Patterson Air Force Base]] Foreign Technology Division for material analysis
- Liaison with the [[Defense Intelligence Agency]] (DIA) for crashed object monitoring

## Legacy and Compartmentalization

The 1127th USAF Field Activities Group was an operational part of the classified UFO investigation and crash retrieval infrastructure that ran parallel to public programs like [[Project Blue Book]]. When Senators [[Jeff Bingaman]] and [[Pete Domenici]] requested Moon Dust documents in the 1990s, the Air Force first denied that the program existed, then claimed the files had been destroyed. That pattern is consistent with the alleged early-1990s [[Wilson-Davis Memo|SAP reorganization]] that compartmentalized UAP legacy programs beyond normal oversight.
