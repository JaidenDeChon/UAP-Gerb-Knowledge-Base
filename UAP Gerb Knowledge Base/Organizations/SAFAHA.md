SAF/AAH, the Air Force Sensitive Activities directorate (rendered "SAFAHA" in this wiki), sat in the Office of the Administrative Assistant to the Secretary of the Air Force (SAF/AA) at Headquarters Air Force. A 2014 Headquarters Air Force mission directive describes it as the Air Force's office of primary responsibility and single enterprise for addressing immediate and emerging needs of the combat air forces, combat support agencies and combatant commanders that involve sensitive activities and operational concepts. That includes the sensitive activities defined in classified DoD directive S-5210.36, "Provision of DoD Sensitive Support to DoD Components and Other Departments and Agencies of the US Government" (6 November 2008), which involve non-DoD federal departments and agencies. The directive's office of primary responsibility is the [[Office of the Under Secretary of Defense for Intelligence and Security (USDINS)|Under Secretary of Defense for Intelligence and Security]]. In the Department of the Air Force's 2024 "re-optimization", sensitive activities became part of the Office of Competitive Activities (SAF/OC), under its Director of Plans and Effects (SAF/OCX). [[Russell E. Wiler]] directed SAF/AAH from 2009 to 2024 and then held the successor post until about mid-2025.

He also suggests those directives trace back to classified presidential orders of the Truman and Eisenhower eras. He gives two hypothetical examples. One is a combatant command such as SOCOM needing sensitive support to retrieve a UFO abroad, with intelligence, surveillance and reconnaissance from a combat support agency such as the NGA. The other is [[Air Combat Command]] fielding derivative technology on US airframes. He links the second to [[Dylan Borland]]'s 2012 sighting at [[Langley Air Force Base]], Air Combat Command's headquarters. The host notes that the NPMS sits in USD(I&S)'s "sensitive activities and special programs" element, and he points to the shared vocabulary with SAF/AAH (sensitive activities) and [[SAFAQL (Special Programs)|SAF/AQL]] (special programs). Its sister office is [[SAFAAZ|SAF/AAZ]].
