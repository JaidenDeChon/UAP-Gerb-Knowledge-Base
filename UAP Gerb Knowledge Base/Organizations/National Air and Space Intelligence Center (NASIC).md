The National Air and Space Intelligence Center (NASIC) is a US Air Force intelligence organization headquartered at [[Wright-Patterson Air Force Base]], Ohio, responsible for producing foreign air, space, missile, and cyberspace threat assessments for the Department of Defense and national intelligence community. It was formally established in February 2003 as the successor to the Air Force's Foreign Technology Division, which itself operated under Air Force Systems Command from 1961.

## Origins and the Foreign Material Program

NASIC's institutional lineage runs through the Air Force's Foreign Technology Division and, before it, foreign-material exploitation efforts dating to World War II. The formal Foreign Material Program (FMP) — encompassing both foreign material acquisition (FMA, obtaining an item) and foreign material exploitation (FME, analyzing it) — was established in 1961 under the Defense Intelligence Agency's Directorate of Science and Technology. UAP Gerb identifies NASIC as the modern continuation of this program and one of the Air Force's most critical elements within alleged UFO legacy program activity, noting that former NASIC official Matthew "Quake" Sullivan was said to have worked on legacy program matters in a dual-headed, unacknowledged capacity while at the center.
