The National Air and Space Intelligence Center (NASIC) is a US Air Force intelligence organization headquartered at [[Wright-Patterson Air Force Base]], Ohio. It produces assessments of foreign air, space, missile, and cyberspace threats for the Department of Defense and the national intelligence community. It was formally established in February 2003 as the successor to the Air Force's Foreign Technology Division, which had operated under Air Force Systems Command from 1961.

## Origins and the Foreign Material Program

NASIC descends from the Air Force's Foreign Technology Division and, before that, from foreign-material exploitation work dating to World War II. The formal Foreign Material Program (FMP) was established in 1961 under the Defense Intelligence Agency's Directorate of Science and Technology. It covered both foreign material acquisition (FMA), which means obtaining an item, and foreign material exploitation (FME), which means analyzing it.
