The University of California, Berkeley is a flagship public research university in Berkeley, California. It comes up in UAP research because [[Albert Bruce Collins]] claimed that in 1947 he saw a damaged UAP on a flatbed trailer being moved into a warehouse on the campus grounds.

## Collins's 1947 Observation

[[Albert Bruce Collins]] was a physical scientist. According to testimony collected by researcher [[Tim Cooper]] in interviews conducted shortly before Collins's death on December 30, 1990, Collins alleged that in 1947 he saw an oval, damaged UAP on a flatbed trailer being backed into a warehouse at UC Berkeley. He described the craft as having a seam around its edge, a multi-layered honeycomb skin, and a shiny sphere at the center. Gerb noted that these physical details resemble descriptions from other witnesses, including [[Jonathan Weygandt]]'s account of a crashed egg-shaped craft in Peru in 1997 and the multi-layered honeycomb bulkhead described in the 1953 Camp Polk crash retrieval testimony.

Collins also claimed that from 1942 through the late 1950s he studied metal of unknown composition and origin in an official capacity. He alleged that by 1949 multiple agencies were involved in what he called "technologies of unknown origin" research, including the CIA, the RAND Corporation, the U.S. Army, Air Force, and Navy, and even the Vatican. His phrase is an early use of wording that UAP disclosure legislation would formalize decades later.

## Research Context

If Collins's account is accurate, a major academic research campus was used in the years just after the war as a transitional storage or processing site for recovered UAP materials, which would be unusual. 1947 is also the year of both the [[Roswell Incident]] and Kenneth Arnold's sighting, which launched the modern "flying saucer" era. UC Berkeley's closeness to major military installations and its established wartime research relationships with the federal government, through programs like the Manhattan Project, would have made it a plausible short-term transit location for classified materials.

Cooper interviewed Collins as part of his broader research into Majestic 12 documents. Collins received his information and did his work in an official capacity, which suggests his access to the materials was institutional and not a matter of chance. Gerb treats the Collins testimony as one of several early-period accounts that together establish the post-war decade as a time when multiple government agencies were actively studying UAP material in many different places. The others include those of [[Robert Sarbacher]], [[Philip J. Corso]], and the Camp Polk witness [[HJ]].

Collins's inclusion of the RAND Corporation among the agencies involved in "technologies of unknown origin" research is notable. RAND was established in 1948 as an Air Force-funded research organization. If Collins's description is accurate, it is an early pointer to the civilian contractor infrastructure that Gerb argues later expanded into the broader network of defense contractors alleged to hold and study recovered non-human technology.
