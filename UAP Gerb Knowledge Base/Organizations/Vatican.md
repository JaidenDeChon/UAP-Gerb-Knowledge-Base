The **Vatican** (officially **Vatican City State**) is the spiritual and administrative headquarters of the Roman Catholic Church and an independent sovereign city-state enclaved within Rome, Italy. The Pope leads it as head of state and bishop of Rome. The Vatican governs the worldwide Catholic Church and has diplomatic relations with nations across the globe. In UAP history, the Vatican is alleged to have acted as an intelligence go-between, particularly during World War II.

## Alleged UAP Involvement

Several accounts of UAP history cite the Vatican as having known about, or been involved in, non-human intelligence matters.

**1933 Magenta, Italy UFO Crash:**  
According to UAP researchers and whistleblower testimony from [[David Grusch]] and journalist [[Christopher Sharp]], [[Pope Pius XII]] allegedly passed information to the United States through a back channel about the craft from the 1933 crash, which had been recovered under [[Benito Mussolini]]'s regime and was held by Italy. In his 2021 article "All UFO Disclosure Roads Lead to Rome", Sharp wrote that the Pope had reportedly grown uneasy about the craft staying in Italian hands as Italy allied with Nazi Germany, and that this led him to leak intelligence to American authorities. The leak helped [[Office of Strategic Services (OSS)]] agents eventually recover the craft in 1944-1945.
