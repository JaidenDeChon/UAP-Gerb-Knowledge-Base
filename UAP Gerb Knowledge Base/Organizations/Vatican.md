The **Vatican** (officially **Vatican City State**) is both the spiritual and administrative headquarters of the Roman Catholic Church and an independent sovereign city-state enclaved within Rome, Italy. Led by the Pope as head of state and bishop of Rome, the Vatican governs the worldwide Catholic Church and maintains diplomatic relations with nations across the globe. In the context of UAP history, the Vatican has been alleged to have played an intermediary intelligence role, particularly during World War II.
