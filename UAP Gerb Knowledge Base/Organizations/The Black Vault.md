The Black Vault is one of the largest privately run online archives of declassified US government documents. [[John Greenewald]] founded it and still runs it. Since it began, The Black Vault has collected hundreds of thousands of pages of government documents, most of them obtained through Freedom of Information Act (FOIA) requests. Much of the collection concerns UFO/UAP records, national security documents and disclosures about classified programs.

## Fast Walker FOIA Campaign

Through The Black Vault, [[John Greenewald]] has spent several years trying to obtain information about [[Fast Walkers]], the military's term for objects detected entering or leaving Earth's atmosphere. The answers to Greenwald's FOIA requests show the government refusing again and again:

### 2013: US Northern Command / NORAD Denial

A FOIA request to [[US Northern Command]], which oversees [[NORAD]], was refused on classification grounds under Executive Order 13526. Two unclassified research papers were released, however, and they confirm that the [[Defense Support Program (DSP)]] has detected Fast Walkers since 1972.

### 2023: US Space Force Final Denial

A FOIA request to [[US Space Force]] was refused in full under FOIA Exception One (national security). It is the government's most recent refusal to release Fast Walker data.

## Significance

By systematically documenting FOIA responses, The Black Vault has built a clear record of the government classifying and withholding information about UFOs detected from space. Greenwald's work has made these documents publicly accessible and searchable, and it gives researchers evidence of secrecy that has lasted for decades.
