The Office of Strategic Services (OSS) was the United States' intelligence service in World War II. It operated from 1942 to 1945 under the Joint Chiefs of Staff and carried out espionage, sabotage, counterintelligence and special operations in Europe, Asia and North Africa. It was the institutional predecessor of the Central Intelligence Agency (CIA).

In UAP circles, the OSS is often named in claims that U.S. agents recovered a non-human craft from fascist Italy in 1944-1945, during the collapse of Benito Mussolini's regime. According to the same claims, the craft had been seized earlier.

## Historical Role

The OSS was set up to centralize U.S. strategic intelligence in wartime and to coordinate operations behind Axis lines. It both gathered intelligence and ran covert actions, such as supporting resistance movements and targeting enemy infrastructure.

After the war, the work of the OSS was redistributed. It was later rebuilt in successor organizations that grew into the modern U.S. intelligence community.

According to claims discussed in [[Videos/The 1933 Magenta, Italy UFO Crash/summary|The 1933 Magenta, Italy UFO Crash]], OSS personnel allegedly took custody of a craft first recovered near [[Magenta, Italy]] in 1933 and managed by [[RS-33 (Gabinetto RS 33)]].

## Analytical Notes

The OSS itself is well documented. The claim that it recovered non-human technology in Italy is still contested, and UAP sources present it as an allegation, not as established historical consensus.
