Heaven's Gate was an American UFO religious cult that operated from 1974 until March 1997, when it ended in a mass suicide. [[Bonnie Nettles]] and Marshall Applewhite co-founded it in Houston, Texas. The group believed that human beings could transcend their physical bodies and graduate to an immortal extraterrestrial existence on a higher evolutionary plane, which they called the "Next Level" or "The Evolutionary Level Above Human (TELAH)." At its peak the group had scores of members living communally. In March 1997, 39 members, Applewhite among them, died by mass suicide at a rented mansion in Rancho Santa Fe, California.

## Beliefs

Heaven's Gate theology blended Christian eschatology with UFO mythology and New Age spirituality.

- The Next Level was a hierarchy of non-human, immortal extraterrestrial beings on a higher evolutionary plane than humanity. Members could ascend to it by following the group's discipline and completely rejecting their "human nature", including sexuality, personal relationships, diet, and individual identity.
- The group called the physical human body a "container" or "vehicle", temporary housing for a soul-entity working toward graduation to the Next Level.
- Applewhite taught that Jesus Christ had himself been a Next Level being who came to Earth two thousand years ago to demonstrate the graduation process. He also taught that he and Nettles were the biblical "Two Witnesses" described in Revelation, returning for the modern era.
- When [[Bonnie Nettles]] died of cancer in 1985, Applewhite worked her death into the theology as proof that she had successfully graduated to the Next Level.

## 1997 Mass Suicide

In early 1997, the Hale-Bopp Comet made its closest approach to Earth. Amateur astronomer Chuck Shramek claimed to have detected a "Saturn-like object" following the comet, and radio host Art Bell amplified the claim. Heaven's Gate came to believe that a spacecraft trailing Hale-Bopp had been sent by Next Level beings to retrieve those ready to graduate.

Between March 24 and 26, 1997, 39 members of Heaven's Gate ingested a mixture of phenobarbital and vodka over three sessions, in groups of approximately 15. They placed plastic bags over their heads and lay down wearing matching black clothing and Nike shoes. Former members who had been sent a farewell video discovered them. All 39 were dead at the scene.

## Legacy

Heaven's Gate is considered one of the most extensively documented UFO-religious cults in American history. Its videos, websites, and writings were preserved. As of 2024 the official Heaven's Gate website remains online, maintained by two surviving members who did not take part in the 1997 event.
