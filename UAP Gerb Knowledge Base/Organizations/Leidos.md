Leidos is a U.S. defense, intelligence, and technology contractor that emerged from the 2013 corporate split of SAIC and subsequently expanded through major acquisitions, including Lockheed Martin's Information Systems and Global Solutions business in 2016.

## Relationship to SAIC History

The SAIC transcript discusses the SAIC-Leidos restructuring as an example of how federal acquisition rules and conflict-of-interest constraints can reshape contractor identity while preserving substantial mission continuity.
