The MARFORLANT Riverine Training Team was a US Marine Forces Atlantic (MARFORLANT) training element that [[US SOUTHCOM|US Southern Command]] kept at [[Iquitos, Peru]] during the mid-1990s counter-narcotics [[Operation Laser Strike]], where US forces trained Peruvian military units in riverine operations. Transcripts of the channel's videos render the name "Marforland".

## Role in the Peru crash reconstruction

UAP Gerb draws on a 1996–1997 Defense Technical Information Center study, *The U.S. Military and the Drug War in Peru*, which records up to 14 US special forces counterdrug deployments to Peru authorised by Southern Command in 1997, regularly engaged in riverine training. For Gerb, the team's presence at Iquitos is one of three US footholds there, with [[USAF Radar Detachment 5, Iquitos]] and [[NAMRU South, Lima, Peru|NAMRU South]], that make the city the likely hub of the 1997 [[Peru UFO Crash Incident]].

He goes further and suggests that special forces working through the riverine training effort, most likely the [[US Army 7th Special Forces Group]], were the armed men in black fatigues who were already on the ground when Lance Corporal [[Jonathan Weygandt]] and his fellow Marines climbed out of the crash site, and who detained them before the Department of Energy team's helicopters landed.
