UAP-X is a nonprofit UFO field research organization that investigates unidentified aerial phenomena scientifically. [[Kevin Knuth]], a physics professor at the [[University of Albany]], is its vice president and brings academic rigor to its field research.

## Mission and Approach

UAP-X runs field research expeditions that use scientific methods to investigate UFO sightings and phenomena. The organization links academic physics research with practical field investigation, combining:

- Scientific methodology, with rigorous protocols for collecting and analyzing data
- Field operations, investigating sighting locations and phenomena directly
- Academic partnerships, working with credentialed researchers like Knuth
- A nonprofit structure, with independent funding outside government or private corporate interests

## Connection to Academic UAP Research

As vice president, Kevin Knuth connects UAP-X to wider academic UFO research, including:

- The [[Galileo Project]], Avi Loeb's Harvard-based search for extraterrestrial technology, where Knuth is a research affiliate
- The [[Sol Foundation]], an academic UAP research organization where Knuth presents his physics-based analysis
- Peer-reviewed research: Knuth's 2019 paper, co-authored with [[Robert M. Powell]] and [[Peter Reali]], which analyzes UAP flight characteristics

This network is a sign of a growing willingness among academics to apply credentialed scientific expertise to UFO phenomena without career stigma.

## Significance

UAP-X is an example of a newer kind of independent UAP research organization, one that works outside government programs but has scientific credibility. By bringing field researchers and academic physicists together, it tries to combine empirical observation with theoretical analysis and close the gap between the UFO community's enthusiasm and academic rigor.

As a nonprofit with academic partnerships, the organization is in a position to do research with fewer institutional constraints than government-funded programs, while keeping higher standards than purely enthusiast-driven investigation.
