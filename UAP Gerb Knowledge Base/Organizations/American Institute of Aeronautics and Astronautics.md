The American Institute of Aeronautics and Astronautics (AIAA) is the world's largest professional society devoted to aerospace and defense, founded in 1963. In UAP history, the AIAA is known for an official statement it issued in 1970 agreeing with the [[Condon Report]]'s conclusion that "little of value had been uncovered by scientific UFO studies". That endorsement helped entrench the [[UFO Stigma]] in aerospace and engineering communities.

## 1970 Official Statement on UFOs

In 1970, one year after the [[Condon Committee]] published its report recommending an end to official UFO investigations, the AIAA issued an official statement agreeing with the report's findings. It took the same position as the [[National Academy of Sciences]] and the [[American Association for the Advancement of Science]], so the major American scientific and engineering institutions stood together in dismissing UFO research as lacking scientific merit.

The endorsement carried particular weight because the AIAA's members were aerospace engineers, researchers, and military professionals, the people most likely to encounter anomalous aerial phenomena in the course of their work.
