The **388th Range Squadron** (388 RANS) is the US Air Force unit at [[Hill Air Force Base]], Utah, that has administered and maintained the [[Utah Test and Training Range]] (UTTR), the largest contiguous block of supersonic-authorized overland restricted airspace in the continental United States. The unit, since redesignated as Headquarters UTTR, provides the range's infrastructure, instrumentation, targets, data systems, airspace, security and safety for Air Force test and training. The range's south range overlaps [[Dugway Proving Ground]], and the squadron uses Dugway's [[Michael Army Airfield]] and the adjacent [[Avery Technical Center]], making it central to UAP Gerb's thesis of a joint Army and Air Force UFO legacy program at Dugway.

## At Dugway

The squadron is listed among Dugway's partner organizations. According to a July 2012 environmental assessment of the Air Force's exclusive use of Army land at Dugway, the 40-acre Avery area and Michael Army Airfield are a focus of the 388th, and Avery serves partly for its air testing and training. The squadron also runs several sites on and just north of [[Granite Peak, Dugway|Granite Peak]], including mini-mute threat emitter stations used to train pilots to recognize and respond to enemy radar.

## In UAP research

UAP Gerb theorizes that witness [[MS (Dugway Witness)|MS]] stumbled onto a legacy operation run between the Army's [[West Desert Test Center]] and the 388th, using Avery's internal rail system to move recovered craft into unmarked buildings and the UTTR's south range to test extraterrestrial or alien reproduction vehicles. He compares it to witness Ed's account of a joint program between the Edwards 412th Test Wing and the Nevada Test and Training Range, but says he could find few links between the 388th and the 412th. Because MS's source GP places much of Dugway's alleged underground facility under Granite Peak, UAP Gerb wonders whether the squadron's Granite Peak South station connects to it.
