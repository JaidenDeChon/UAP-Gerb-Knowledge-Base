The **388th Range Squadron** (388 RANS) is the US Air Force unit at [[Hill Air Force Base]], Utah, that has administered and maintained the [[Utah Test and Training Range]] (UTTR), the largest contiguous block of supersonic-authorized overland restricted airspace in the continental United States. The unit, since redesignated as Headquarters UTTR, provides the range's infrastructure, instrumentation, targets, data systems, airspace, security and safety for Air Force test and training. The UTTR's south range overlaps [[Dugway Proving Ground]], where the squadron uses [[Michael Army Airfield]] and the adjacent [[Avery Technical Center]]. That presence at Dugway puts the squadron at the center of Gerb's theory that the Army and Air Force ran a joint UFO legacy program there.

## At Dugway

The squadron is listed among Dugway's partner organizations. According to a July 2012 environmental assessment of the Air Force's exclusive use of Army land at Dugway, the 40-acre Avery area and Michael Army Airfield are a focus of the 388th, which uses Avery partly for air testing and training. The squadron also runs several sites on and just north of [[Granite Peak, Dugway|Granite Peak]], including mini-mute threat emitter stations used to train pilots to recognize and respond to enemy radar.

## In UAP research

Gerb theorizes that witness [[MS (Dugway Witness)|MS]] stumbled onto a legacy operation run jointly by the Army's [[West Desert Test Center]] and the 388th. In his theory, the operation used Avery's internal rail system to move recovered craft into unmarked buildings, and the UTTR's south range to test extraterrestrial or alien reproduction vehicles. He compares it to witness Ed's account of a joint program between the Edwards 412th Test Wing and the Nevada Test and Training Range, but says he could find few links between the 388th and the 412th. Because MS's source GP places much of Dugway's alleged underground facility under Granite Peak, Gerb wonders whether the squadron's Granite Peak South station connects to it.
