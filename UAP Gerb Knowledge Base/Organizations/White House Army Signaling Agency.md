The White House Army Signaling Agency was a U.S. Army communications and signals support unit attached to presidential operations in the Eisenhower and Kennedy eras. In UFO whistleblower discussions, it is significant as the organization where Steven Lovekin said he served while accessing classified communications and related briefing channels.

## Function

The agency supported secure signaling, communications, and technical message handling for executive-branch operations. Personnel in this environment could encounter compartmented national security material outside normal public reporting systems.

## Relevance To UAP Claims

Lovekin's testimony places his Project Blue Book and Roswell-adjacent claims within this institutional context, arguing that some UFO-related intelligence products remained available only through classified channels.
