The White House Army Signaling Agency was a U.S. Army communications and signals support unit attached to presidential operations in the Eisenhower and Kennedy eras. It comes up in UFO whistleblower discussions as the unit where Steven Lovekin said he served, and where he said he had access to classified communications and related briefing channels.

## Function

The agency supported secure signaling, communications, and technical message handling for executive-branch operations. Its personnel could come across compartmented national security material that sat outside normal public reporting systems.

## Relevance To UAP Claims

Lovekin's testimony ties his Project Blue Book and Roswell-adjacent claims to his time at the agency. He argues that some UFO-related intelligence products remained available only through classified channels.
