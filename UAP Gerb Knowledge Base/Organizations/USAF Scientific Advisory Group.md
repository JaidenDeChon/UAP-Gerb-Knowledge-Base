The USAF Scientific Advisory Group was a body of civilian scientific consultants who advised the United States Air Force on technical and scientific matters relevant to its operations. In UAP history it is known as one of three bodies that had to receive every [[Project Sign]] report. The other two were the [[Army and Navy Research and Development Board]] and the [[Atomic Energy Commission]]. This distribution rule indicates that UFO investigations in the late 1940s were treated as needing high-level scientific review by both military and nuclear agencies.
