SAF/AAZ is the Air Force Directorate of Security, Special Program Oversight and Information Protection, part of the Office of the Administrative Assistant to the Secretary of the Air Force (SAF/AA) at the Pentagon. It sets strategic policy for the Air Force security enterprise and manages the Air Force information security program, including nuclear information security, as well as the personnel and industrial security programs. It is the Air Force focal point for the insider-threat program, the Air Force Special Access Program Central Office (SAPCO), and the authorizing official for Air Force SAP information systems. Its director is a Senior Executive Service member and the functional manager of the Air Force security community of more than 2,000 people.
