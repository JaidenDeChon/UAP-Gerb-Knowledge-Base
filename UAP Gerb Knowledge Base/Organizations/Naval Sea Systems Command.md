Naval Sea Systems Command (NAVSEA) is the largest of the U.S. Navy's systems commands, responsible for designing, building, maintaining, and modernizing the Navy's fleet of ships, submarines, and combat systems, and commanding a substantial share of the Navy's total acquisition budget. NAVSEA is the maritime counterpart to [[Naval Air Systems Command]] (NAVAIR), which handles naval aviation acquisition and support. In UAP Gerb's investigative framework, NAVSEA is identified as a secondary but significant Navy mission partner — alongside NAVAIR and the [[Office of Naval Intelligence]] — in an alleged [[MITRE Corporation|MITRE]]-managed UFO technology reverse-engineering program.
