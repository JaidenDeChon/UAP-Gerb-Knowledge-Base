Naval Sea Systems Command (NAVSEA) is the largest of the U.S. Navy's systems commands. It designs, builds, maintains, and modernizes the Navy's fleet of ships, submarines, and combat systems, and it controls a substantial share of the Navy's total acquisition budget. NAVSEA is the maritime counterpart to [[Naval Air Systems Command]] (NAVAIR), which handles naval aviation acquisition and support. Gerb names NAVSEA as a secondary but significant Navy mission partner, along with NAVAIR and the [[Office of Naval Intelligence]], in an alleged [[MITRE Corporation|MITRE]]-managed program to reverse-engineer UFO technology.

## Warfare Centers

NAVSEA's warfare enterprise includes several Naval Surface Warfare Center (NSWC) and Naval Undersea Warfare Center (NUWC) locations. Gerb alleges that several of them play supporting roles in MITRE's reverse-engineering program, including:

- [[Naval Surface Warfare Center Dahlgren]], Virginia: allegedly conducts major testing and deployment exercises
- [[Naval Surface Warfare Center Crane]], Indiana: allegedly holds and analyzes hardware within a broader Navy processing "triage"
- [[Naval Undersea Warfare Center Keyport]], Washington: allegedly processes data within the same triage

Gerb describes these three sites and [[Naval Air Station Patuxent River]] as one integrated triage for the reverse-engineering program. In his account, NUWC Keyport processes data, NSWC Crane and NSWC Dahlgren hold and analyze hardware, and Naval Air Station Patuxent River is the integration node for data, hardware, and aircraft.
