The National Aeronautics and Space Administration (NASA) is the United States government agency responsible for civilian space exploration, aerospace research, and aviation technology. NASA's public mission is space science and exploration, but the agency also operates specialized aircraft and runs facilities that have come up in UAP-related testimony and analysis.

## NASA F-18 Chase Aircraft

NASA operates modified F-18A aircraft with cameras mounted inside, behind the pilot's seat. The cameras can film out of the starboard or port side without the cockpit blocking the view. NASA uses these chase planes to film launches, fly test flights, and take aerial photographs for various missions. The F-18A's distinctive internal camera setup matches features seen in the [[Flyby Footage]], which has led to speculation that the footage may have been shot from a NASA chase aircraft rather than a standard military fighter jet.

## UAP-Related Activity

NASA staff have occasionally come up in UAP research. Bob Echler, a NASA mission specialist, reportedly carried out a UFO-related inquiry with [[Bobby Ray Inman|Inman]], but the details of that investigation remain vague. NASA's unique access to aerospace technology, tracking systems, and scientific expertise makes it a potential player in government UAP analysis. Its official involvement in UAP research, however, has been limited and narrowly defined compared with DoD programs.
