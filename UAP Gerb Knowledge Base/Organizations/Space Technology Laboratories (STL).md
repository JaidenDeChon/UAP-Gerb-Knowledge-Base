Space Technology Laboratories (STL) was the Ramo-Wooldridge, and later [[TRW]], organization that led the systems engineering of the first US intercontinental ballistic missiles and, later, the Titan missile programs. It began as a subsidiary and soon became a division of TRW. In 1960 roughly half of it was spun off as the [[Aerospace Corporation]], because rival contractors complained that STL's Air Force advisory role gave TRW unfair access to technology. The remainder was renamed TRW Systems Group, which ran TRW's entire electronics and defense sector and whose units now form most of [[Northrop Grumman]]'s Mission Systems and Space Systems sectors.
