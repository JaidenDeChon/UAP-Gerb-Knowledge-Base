Danny Lampkin was a YouTube channel, since defunct, that uploaded the [[Flyby Footage]] to YouTube on 14 May 2008. The roughly 20-second clip appears to show a disc-shaped craft flying alongside the wing of a jet, and it is one of the best-known pieces of alleged close-range UFO footage in the public realm.

## The Flyby upload

The channel's description of the video was a single sentence, in which the uploader said he was not too sure about the clip but had downloaded it anyway so that viewers could decide for themselves. The upload gives no information on who filmed the footage or where it came from.

The Danny Lampkin upload is not the clip's first appearance online. In his analysis, Gerb traces the footage back to around April 2008, when it was posted on the website UFO Chronicles as "Flying Saucer UFO filmed from jet window", and found no record of it before then. The clip itself is a [[Second-Generation Recording]], filmed off a computer screen playing the original footage.
