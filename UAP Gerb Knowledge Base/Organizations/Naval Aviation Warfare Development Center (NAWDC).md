The Naval Aviation Warfare Development Center (NAWDC), pronounced "NAW-dick," is the US Navy's Center of Excellence for Air Combat Training and Development, based in Fallon, Nevada. NAWDC was established in 1996 by merging the Naval Strike Warfare Center, the famous [[Navy Fighter Weapons School (Top Gun)]], and the [[Carrier Airborne Early Warning Weapons School (Top Dome)]]. It trains naval aviation in advanced tactics, techniques, and procedures in its assigned combat mission areas. It also gives flight training and academic instruction to air crews, squadrons, and air wings, and provides direct operational and intelligence support.

## Key Departments and Facilities

Naval Intelligence is one of NAWDC's most active departments. NAWDC is the primary authority on training and tactics development in integrated strike warfare, maritime and overland air superiority, strike fighter deployment, airborne battle management, combat search and rescue, close air support, and associated planning support systems.

NAWDC also operates the [[Fallon Range and Training Complex (FTRC)]], an enormous training range just north of the [[Nevada Test Site]] and the [[Nevada Test and Training Range]]. The Nevada Test and Training Range is used extensively by [[Area 51|Groom Lake (Area 51)]], a detachment of the [[412th Test Wing]] at [[Edwards Air Force Base]].

## UAP and ARV Connections

NAWDC sits near Area 51 and the Nevada Test and Training Range and has its own large aerial training complex, which puts it within a corridor of facilities that Gerb has linked to alien reproduction vehicle (ARV) operations. [[Edgar Fouche]], who disclosed details of the [[TR-3B]], claimed that the craft's military pilots were elite aviators from both the Air Force Test Pilot School at Edwards and the Navy's Top Gun school. Top Gun is housed within NAWDC.

During the 1997 [[Hunter Warrior Advanced Warfighting Experiment]], NAWDC (then still known as the Naval Strike Air Warfare Center) and [[NAS Lemoore|Naval Air Station Lemoore]] assembled air crews as a naval adjunct "bolted on late in the planning stage." Gerb theorizes that this late addition provided cover for clandestine ARV and special mission unit operations alongside blue (friendly) assets.

Triangle sightings have also been reported near NAWDC. At 11:13 PM one night in 2002, a witness in Fallon, Nevada reported a very large triangular craft with 7-9 dimly lit nodes. It flew silently at about 20 mph, and the witness described it as astonishingly close and enormous.
