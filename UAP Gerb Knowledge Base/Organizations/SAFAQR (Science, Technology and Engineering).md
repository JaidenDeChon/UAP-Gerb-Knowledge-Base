SAF/AQR, the Directorate of Science, Technology and Engineering, is the office under the Assistant Secretary of the Air Force for Acquisition, Technology and Logistics ([[SAFAQ|SAF/AQ]]) that serves as the Air Force Science and Technology Executive. It represents, advocates for and defends the Air Force S&T program to the Office of the Secretary of Defense, the other services and Congress. It works with technology executive officers at the [[Air Force Research Laboratory]] on all Air Force S&T programs, and with OSD's funding and program-management structure on several technology-transfer vehicles. Its published mission says it integrates SAP and non-SAP RDT&E and S&T planning and directs advanced technology development programs, monitoring new technologies in coordination with [[SAFAQL (Special Programs)|SAF/AQL]].

## Role in the Hidden Wing thesis

In *The Hidden Wing*, UAP Gerb stresses that last point: AQR and AQL coordinate on Air Force RDT&E special access programs and together monitor new technologies. He calls this "really important" and suspects deep programs on technologies of unknown origin fall within that remit. He also wonders whether the [[National Security Council]] uses the partnership between the [[Under Secretary of Defense for Acquisition and Sustainment (USD A&S)]] and SAF/AQ, especially SAF/AQR, together with technology-transfer vehicles, to hide budgets or materials.
