SAF/AQR, the Directorate of Science, Technology and Engineering, is the Air Force Science and Technology Executive, an office under the Assistant Secretary of the Air Force for Acquisition, Technology and Logistics ([[SAFAQ|SAF/AQ]]). It represents, advocates for and defends the Air Force S&T program to the Office of the Secretary of Defense, the other services and Congress. It works with technology executive officers at the [[Air Force Research Laboratory]] on all Air Force S&T programs, and with OSD's funding and program-management structure on several technology-transfer vehicles. Its published mission says it integrates SAP and non-SAP RDT&E and S&T planning, directs advanced technology development programs, and monitors new technologies in coordination with [[SAFAQL (Special Programs)|SAF/AQL]].

## Role in the Hidden Wing thesis

In *The Hidden Wing*, Gerb stresses that AQR and AQL coordinate on Air Force RDT&E special access programs and together monitor new technologies. He calls this "really important" and suspects that deep programs on technologies of unknown origin fall within that remit. He also wonders whether the [[National Security Council]] uses the partnership between the [[Under Secretary of Defense for Acquisition and Sustainment (USD A&S)]] and SAF/AQ, especially SAF/AQR, together with technology-transfer vehicles, to hide budgets or materials.
