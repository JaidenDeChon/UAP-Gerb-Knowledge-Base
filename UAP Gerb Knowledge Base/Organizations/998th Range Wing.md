The 998th Range Wing is described in USAF-related testimony as an organizational predecessor tied to the Nevada test-range structure later associated with the [[Nevada Test and Training Range]]. In UAP program allegations, it appears as a command context for range operations at [[Nellis Air Force Base]] and related restricted test environments.

Within the testimony discussed in this knowledge base, witness [[Ed]] is said to have worked under leadership connected to the 998th Range Wing while assigned to Nellis-area operations. The claim is used to support an argument that early-career personnel were exposed to highly compartmentalized projects before formal read-ins at later duty stations.

## Program Context

- Framed as part of the range-control ecosystem around Nellis and adjacent restricted military airspace.
- Referenced in relation to facility-level work around [[Building 200]] and linked operational pathways to [[Area 51]] and [[S4]].
