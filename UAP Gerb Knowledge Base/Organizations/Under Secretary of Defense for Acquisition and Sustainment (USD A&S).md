The Under Secretary of Defense for Acquisition and Sustainment (USD(A&S)) is the senior official in the Office of the Secretary of Defense responsible for acquisition, logistics and sustainment policy across the Department of Defense. The office was created in 2018 when the former Under Secretary of Defense for Acquisition, Technology and Logistics (USD(AT&L)) was split into USD(A&S) and the Under Secretary of Defense for Research and Engineering.

## In UAP research

Gerb has identified USD(A&S) as one of the main administrators of [[NRO (National Reconnaissance Office)|NRO]] and [[National Security Council]] UFO legacy programs, working through NRO acquisition with its intelligence-community counterpart, the deputy director of national intelligence for acquisition, technology and facilities. In *The Hidden Wing* he extends the argument to the Air Force. USD(A&S) partners with Air Force acquisition ([[SAFAQ|SAF/AQ]]) on technology-transfer vehicles. It also chairs the board of directors to which the Department of the Air Force [[Rapid Capabilities Office (RCO)]] reports. That board also includes the Secretary of the Air Force, the Air Force Chief of Staff, the Chief of Space Operations, the Air Force acquisition executive and the Under Secretary of Defense for Research and Engineering. Gerb argues that this short, narrow chain lets Air Force acquisition run UFO legacy work outside normal congressional and service oversight. He also wonders whether the National Security Council uses the USD(A&S)-SAF/AQ partnership, especially with SAF/AQR, to hide budgets or materials.

In the [[Wilson-Davis Memo|Wilson-Davis notes]], [[Marshal Ward|H. Marshal Ward]] pointed Vice Admiral [[Thomas Wilson]] to legacy-program records held in the acquisition under secretary's office. Ward served in the predecessor organization of USD(A&S), OUSD(AT&L).
