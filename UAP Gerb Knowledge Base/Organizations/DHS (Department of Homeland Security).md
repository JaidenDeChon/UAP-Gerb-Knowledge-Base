The Department of Homeland Security (DHS) is a U.S. federal executive department responsible for public security, border control, immigration enforcement, cybersecurity, and disaster prevention and response. DHS was established in 2002 in response to the September 11 attacks, and it brings a number of federal agencies under one command to coordinate domestic security.

## Alleged UAP Analysis Role

An allegedly leaked classified document that surfaced online in 2021-2022 lists DHS as one of the "joint elements" that contributed to an analysis of UAP behavioral data, alongside [[ONI]], [[NSA]], and DOD experts cleared for [[Special Access Program (SAP)|SAP]] access. The document is purportedly a section of a classified appendix to a [[UAP Task Force]] report. It says these agencies examined 1,292 UFO cases and reached conclusions about what UAP are like, including the possibility that some represent "remote or autonomous drones" or "a form of mechanical life."

If the document is authentic, DHS's part in this alleged analysis would take the agency well beyond its usual homeland security work. It would also suggest possible concern about UAP as a threat to domestic security or border integrity.

## Role in the Proposed Kona Blue Transfer

UAP Gerb's *80 Years of UFO Crash Retrieval and Reverse Engineering* names DHS's Science and Technology Directorate as the office that facilitated [[Kona Blue]]. Kona Blue was a proposed prospective special access program (PSAP), meant to be the vehicle through which [[Lockheed Martin]], under [[Lockheed Martin Space Systems]] vice president [[James T. Ryder]], would have divested recovered UAP materials to [[Bigelow Aerospace Advanced Space Studies]]. According to the video, DHS staff working on the effort met resistance from the gatekeepers of legacy programs when they tried to gain access for Kona Blue. The video says the technology transfer agreement was ultimately blocked by [[CIA Directorate of Science and Technology]] officials [[Glenn Gaffney]] and [[Mary K. Sturdivant|Mary Sturdivant]] before Kona Blue could be formally approved as a special access program.
