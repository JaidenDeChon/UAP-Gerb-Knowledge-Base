The Kamchatka Lake USO Sighting took place in August 1970. A group of Soviet hydrologists doing research from a motorboat on a lake on the [[Kamchatka Peninsula]] saw a large dome of water erupt approximately one kilometer away. A gray oval object rose out of it, hovered, and then accelerated away, after suppressing the boat's engine function. The case is sourced from *Russia's USO Secrets* by [[Paul Stonehill]] and [[Philip Mantle]] and from an article by Soviet scientist Valentin Salomashnikov. It is notable for the engine interference, which Gerb connects to the electromagnetic disruption discussed by [[Kevin Knuth]] at the [[Sol Foundation]].

## Incident Description

A group of Soviet hydrologists was doing field research from a motorboat on a lake in the Kamchatka region. Approximately one kilometer from the boat, a massive dome of water erupted from the surface of the lake, and a gray oval object rose from inside it.

The object's length was estimated at 40 to 60 meters. It rose to approximately 100 meters and hovered motionless there while the motorboat's engine stalled. It stayed long enough for the scientists to observe it in detail, then accelerated away rapidly. Once the object had gone, the engine worked normally again.

The object's emergence from a freshwater lake is notable. The Kamchatka Peninsula is a remote volcanic region with numerous lakes that are relatively isolated from maritime observation, and the case is therefore one of the few well-documented USO encounters from a landlocked body of water rather than an ocean or coastal area.
