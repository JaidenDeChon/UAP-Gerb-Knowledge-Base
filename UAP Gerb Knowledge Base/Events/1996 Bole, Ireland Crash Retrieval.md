The **1996 Bole, Ireland crash retrieval** is an alleged recovery of a craft and its living crew in May 1996 at [[Bole, Ireland]]. UAP Gerb identified it as the "near-UK" retrieval described by the [[Infographic Show Whistleblower]], which the witness said coincided with a meteor event in the region.

## Account

Locals reportedly saw a meteor or aerial craft misjudge its approach, shear through trees and come down in a lake. Several occupants were said to have been taken into custody by a retrieval team, and military activity, including American soldiers, went on for about six months. Local police were told to stay away.

The witness says two of the five crew survived with light bruising and three were killed in the hard landing. The door opened on its own. The survivors stood about aimlessly, reacting only to basic stimuli, and were led out "like school children." Following the [[Live Biologics Protocols]], security stayed out of sight and a lead doctor, "the Ambassador," led the medical team. As in almost every live recovery, he says, the beings died within days.

## The European Agreement

The witness says Europe has a default agreement with the United States under which recovered material goes to the US: "that's the price you pay for having a superpower watch your back." UAP Gerb took the presence of American soldiers at Bole as a key point in favour of the match. He rated his confidence in the identification as medium.
