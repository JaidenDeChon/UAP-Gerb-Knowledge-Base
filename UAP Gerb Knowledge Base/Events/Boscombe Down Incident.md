The Boscombe Down Incident is the alleged crash landing of a classified flying triangle aircraft at Boscombe Down, a highly secret UK government facility, on September 26, 1994. The case is documented in [[Ryan S. Wood|Ryan Wood]]'s *Magic Eyes Only* and was first reported in the November 1994 edition of *Air Force Monthly*.

## The Crash

According to the *Air Force Monthly* article, an unusual crash landing occurred on the runway of Boscombe Down. Rumors quickly circulated that the wreck was of a flying triangle matching descriptions given by UFO witnesses for decades. Shortly after the crash, an unmarked civilian-registered ([[CIA]]-operated) Boeing 737 and a similarly anonymous DC-8 visited the facility. Two days later, the wreckage was loaded onto a C-5 Galaxy and flown to [[Air Force Plant 42]] at Palmdale, California.

The magazine speculated that the aircraft was a TR-3A, a type whose existence the US government has not yet officially acknowledged. Although the article uses the TR-3A designation, [[Edgar Fouche|Edgar Fouché]] noted that the names TR-3A and TR-3B are often used interchangeably in public discussion. That leaves open the possibility that the crashed craft was a [[TR-3B]] variant operating under the [[Aurora Program]].

## Astra Connection

The TR-3B's code name is Astra, which stands for Advanced Stealth Technology Reconnaissance Aircraft, and this incident is a possible real-world echo of it. In *Magic Eyes Only*, [[Ryan S. Wood|Ryan Wood]] lists the Boscombe Down crash among cases that suggest advanced US black program aircraft were operating from or over allied territory.
