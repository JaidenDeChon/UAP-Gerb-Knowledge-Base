The USS Nimitz 1991 USO Encounter is an account by US Navy Petty Officer Kevin Thomas, who claimed that in 1991 he saw a huge black triangular craft, nearly a kilometre long, rise silently out of the ocean beside the aircraft carrier USS Nimitz and shoot vertically into the sky. It predates the same carrier group's [[2004 Nimitz UAP Encounter (Tic Tac)|2004 Tic Tac encounter]] by thirteen years.

## The Sighting

According to Thomas, the ship had been ordered to darken and clear its decks, but he was left on deck. He described a "huge black triangle" lifting out of the ocean. He said no water dripped from the craft and it made no sound.

## Aftermath

The next day, men in civilian clothes came aboard the Nimitz and questioned Thomas about why he had been outside and what he had seen. Thomas told them nothing, out of fear of being disciplined.

In [[USO - Unidentified Submerged Objects]], UAP Gerb says he was unable to locate Thomas's service record and asks viewers to weigh the account accordingly.
