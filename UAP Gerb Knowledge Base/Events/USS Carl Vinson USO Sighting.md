The USS Carl Vinson USO Sighting is a 2010 account by US Navy Petty Officer John Bowman, who said he saw a white, Tic Tac-shaped [[Unidentified Submerged Object (USO)|unidentified submerged object]] in the water beneath the flight deck of the aircraft carrier USS Carl Vinson off the coast of Haiti. The object's shape invites comparison with the [[2004 Nimitz UAP Encounter (Tic Tac)|2004 Nimitz Tic Tac encounter]].

## The Sighting

In Bowman's words as quoted in [[USO - Unidentified Submerged Objects]], he was staring into the water from above when a large, fat, white Tic Tac-shaped object about 20 feet long suddenly appeared below him, moving right, and darted into the depths as fast as it had appeared. He described it as definitely a solid object, but said that as it descended its forward end "rapidly collapsed in on itself" and it disappeared.

## Aftermath

Bowman reported the sighting to his supervising officer. He was not debriefed and was told not to file a report; the officer told him that everybody sees weird things in the ocean.
