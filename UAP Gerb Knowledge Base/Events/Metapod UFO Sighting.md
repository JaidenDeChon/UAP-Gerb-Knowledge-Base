On September 6, 2015, two Spanish witnesses—[[Modesto]] and his companion [[Mo]]—observed and filmed a structured crescent-shaped UAP near [[El Escorial, Madrid, Spain]]. The sighting produced what is considered one of the clearest close-up videos of an anomalous craft ever recorded, showing a distinctive pod-like object with a metallic reflective back and transparent glass-like front face.

## The Observation

The sighting occurred in the mountainous region near El Escorial, in the direction of [[Las Navas Del Marquez]]. Modesto, who described himself as somewhat nearsighted but experienced in the countryside, emphasized that he was intimately familiar with the sounds of birds in flight. The observed craft made absolutely no sound during its maneuvers.

The object descended vertically in a controlled manner before moving away horizontally. Mo, characterized by Modesto as having exceptionally sharp eyesight ("can see hairs and quailes in the same way as finding a needle in a hay stack"), reported observing something alive with limbs moving inside the craft through the transparent front face.

## Witness Reaction

Modesto's account conveys genuine surprise and conviction. He stated: "what we saw yesterday I sincerely know was not from here...I personally neither believed nor did I believe in UFOs or extraterrestrials it simply seemed like 'nonsense from Uncle Aer and four other idiots.' From that moment on I knew that it wasn't from here, that it wasn't human."

In contrast, Mo appeared less surprised by the encounter, remarking cryptically "here again" after the observation. When pressed about this expression, Mo revealed it was not the first time he had seen the object, and that his deceased father had also observed it in the area. This suggests a pattern of recurring sightings in the El Escorial region possibly spanning decades.

## Footage Submission and Publication

The following day, September 7, 2015, Modesto emailed the footage to YouTube channel [[UFO VNI]], requesting strict anonymity and declining to reveal the exact GPS coordinates publicly. UFO VNI published the video shortly thereafter, leading to rapid dissemination across UFO research communities. A Portuguese forum discussed the footage by September 8, and [[Third Phase of the Sun]] published a detailed article on September 11, 2015.

## Significance

The Metapod sighting is significant for several reasons:
- **Visual clarity**: Unlike most UAP footage, the Metapod video shows structural details clearly
- **Craft structure**: The transparent front face and reflective back suggest engineered design
- **Flight characteristics**: Silent, controlled vertical descent and maneuvering without visible propulsion
- **Witness credibility**: Modesto's prior skepticism and detailed account add authenticity
- **Pattern recognition**: Mo's familiarity with the object and multi-generational sightings suggest recurring phenomena
- **Global correlation**: The craft matches morphologically similar objects observed in Peru (1998), Mexico (2006), Vancouver (2021), and Texas (2023)

## Misinformation

Following the video's release, some social media accounts incorrectly claimed the footage was filmed in Denver, Colorado. This misinformation was corrected by UAP analysts confirming the Spanish origin.
