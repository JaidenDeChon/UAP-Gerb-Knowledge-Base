The Metapod UFO Sighting is the alleged observation and filming of a small, silent, crescent-shaped craft (the [[Metapod]]) by two friends in the countryside near [[El Escorial, Madrid, Spain]] on 6 September 2015. The resulting clip, a minute or two long, shows a structured pod with a reflective metallic back and a transparent front face at close range, and has become one of the most discussed pieces of alleged UFO footage online.

## The witnesses' account

The account comes from an email sent the next day, 7 September 2015, to the YouTube channel [[UFO VNI]] by a man signing himself Modesto. According to the email:

- He filmed the object with a friend he calls Mo. The object made no noise; it descended vertically and then moved away. The writer, who has spent many years in the countryside, says he knows the sound of birds' wings in flight.
- He is somewhat short-sighted, but his friend has exceptional eyesight, and the friend saw something moving inside the object, "something alive with limbs".
- The site was near El Escorial, in the direction of Las Navas del Marqués. The pair withheld the exact location; the writer offered the GPS data to the channel for its own investigation only. El Escorial, he writes, is a place where strange things happen in the nearby mountains.
- The writer had not believed in UFOs before, but was convinced that what he saw "wasn't human". His friend was not surprised: on seeing the video he said "here again", and said it was not the first time he had seen the object and that his late father had seen it too.

The email later calls the friend "Modesto", which Gerb suggests may be a slip by a sender using pseudonyms.

## Publication

UFO VNI published the clip, which was picked up by a Portuguese forum on 8 September 2015 and by [[Third Phase of the Sun]] on 11 September. UFO VNI later removed the video, and a 2017 re-upload by [[UFO Invasions]] was removed as well; a stabilized version by the channel [[ITBS]] remains in circulation. Some later re-uploads on Reddit and other platforms claimed the clip was filmed in Denver, which Gerb calls a fallacy: the footage comes from Spain.
