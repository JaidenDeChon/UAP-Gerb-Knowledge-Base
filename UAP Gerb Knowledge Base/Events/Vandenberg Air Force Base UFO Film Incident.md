The Vandenberg Air Force Base UFO Film Incident occurred on September 14, 1964, when optical instrumentation operated by US Air Force Lieutenant [[Robert Jacobs]] filmed a disc-shaped craft interacting with a dummy nuclear warhead during an ICBM test launch at [[Vandenberg Air Force Base]], California. The craft fired four directed energy beams at the warhead, which then fell off its trajectory. Government agents confiscated the footage within days, and Jacobs was ordered to attribute the incident to "laser tracking strikes." The case is corroborated by Major [[Florence J. Mansman]] and entered the formal congressional record when Jacobs testified under oath to [[AARO]] in February 2023.

## Background

Jacobs commanded a 100-man optical instrumentation unit at Vandenberg responsible for filming every nuclear payload and ballistic missile launch from the Western Test Range near [[Big Sur, California]]. The unit's 180-inch lenses and high-sensitivity telescopes could resolve missile details from 160 miles away. The day's test used a dummy ICBM warhead designed to separate from [[Missile Chaff Decoy|chaff countermeasures]] to evade Soviet air defense targeting.

## The UFO Encounter

The three-stage ICBM launched without incident. The warhead separated at the fringe of space while traveling at approximately 6,000–8,000 miles per hour, and at that moment an unidentified craft entered the camera frame. It matched the warhead's speed in what appeared to be polar orbit.

Jacobs described the craft as two saucers pressed flat against one another with a rounded hemisphere on top resembling "half of a ping-pong ball," from which a [[Directed Energy Beam|directed energy beam]] was emitted. The craft made four passes around the warhead and fired a beam on each: once from above, once from the side, once from below, and a final pass before departing in the same direction it had entered. The warhead then deviated from its trajectory and exited the camera frame.

## Confiscation and Suppression

Within days of the test, Jacobs was summoned to the office of Major Mansman, who had assembled three men in gray suits. Mansman later identified them as the Director of the Office of the Chief Scientist and two government agent assistants. After a private screening of the film, Mansman furiously demanded to know whether Jacobs and his team had tampered with the launch. When Jacobs identified the object as a UFO, Mansman ordered the incident classified and instructed him to describe the beam of light as "[[Laser Tracking Strikes]]" if ever questioned. Jacobs noted that this cover story was impossible in 1964, when lasers were still in early developmental stages.

The three agents then cut the UFO footage out of the film reel with scissors, put it in their briefcase and left. Mansman was left with only the edited footage. Both Mansman and Jacobs were threatened and ordered to consider the incident closed.

## Disclosure and Corroboration

Jacobs stayed silent for 18 years. In 1982, reasoning that he had never signed a non-disclosure agreement and was never formally told the incident was classified, he disclosed the case publicly. Reprisals followed at once, in the form of sustained threatening phone calls. The case was published in the MUFON journal in January 1989.

Major Mansman later gave written corroboration to Paramount Pictures, which was producing a documentary on the case, confirming Jacobs's account as "absolute fact." Jacobs later testified under oath to AARO and [[Sean Kirkpatrick]] on February 10, 2023, placing the incident in the formal congressional UAP record.

## Significance

The Vandenberg incident is among the best-corroborated cases in the [[UFO Interference with Nuclear Weapons]] pattern. Its strength as evidence comes from four things: (1) the physical film evidence, though it was later destroyed; (2) independent written corroboration from a second military officer who was present; (3) the specificity and technical detail of the warhead interaction; and (4) sworn congressional testimony. It is frequently cited alongside the [[Malmstrom Air Force Base UFO Incident]] as a foundational case for the [[UFO-Nuclear Connection]].
