The SOL Foundation Symposium was the inaugural conference of the [[Sol Foundation]], held in November 2023 at Stanford University, in the Nolan laboratory of the Stanford School of Medicine. It brought together scientists, former officials and whistleblowers who argue for UAP's academic legitimacy and for preparing for a post-disclosure world. Speakers included [[Karl Nell]], [[David Grusch]], [[Chris Mellon]], [[Kevin Knuth]], [[Gary Nolan]] and [[Jacques Vallee]].

## Karl Nell's presentation

Nell's 31-minute talk is the symposium's best-known presentation. One of its slides partially leaked in November 2023, and the full recording only became viewable in February 2024. It covered:

- a close reading of the [[Schumer Amendment (NDAA)|Schumer Amendment]]'s definitions and eminent-domain clause
- the difference between controlled and catastrophic disclosure
- reasons for and against non-disclosure
- the six-phase [[UAP Campaign Plan - Way Forward]]

Two backup slides not shown in the recording, the [[Universal Civilization Development Datum]] and a [[UAP Taxonomy]] of origin hypotheses, were later shared by [[Matthew Pines]].
