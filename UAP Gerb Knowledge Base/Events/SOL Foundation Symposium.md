The SOL Foundation Symposium held at the Nolan laboratory of the Stanford School of Medicine in November 2023, featuring presentations by [[Colonel Karl Nell]], [[David Grusch]], [[Chris Mellon]], [[Kevin Knuth]], [[Gary Nolan]], [[Jacques Vallee]], and other notable UAP researchers and officials. Nell's presentation outlined a comprehensive five-phase UAP disclosure roadmap and detailed analysis of the Schumer Amendment.
