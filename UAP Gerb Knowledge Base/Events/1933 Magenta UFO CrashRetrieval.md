The **1933 Magenta UFO Crash Retrieval** refers to the alleged crash or landing of an unknown bell-shaped craft near [[Magenta, Italy]] on June 13, 1933. According to original Italian government documents provided to researcher [[Roberto Pinotti]], the craft was recovered by [[Benito Mussolini]]'s fascist regime and secretly examined by the [[RS-33 (Gabinetto RS 33)]], a covert scientific commission Mussolini personally headed. The case is notable for the claim that [[Richard Bissell Jr.]] — CIA Deputy Director for Plans under Eisenhower — privately confirmed its authenticity to economist [[Harold Malmgren]].

## Recovery and Italian Custody

The craft — described as approximately 10 meters in diameter and bell-shaped — remained in Italian possession throughout the fascist era. It was allegedly stored first at [[SIAI-Marchetti Aircraft Company]] hangars in [[Varese, Italy]] and later transferred to [[Benito Mussolini|Mussolini]]'s bunker under [[Mount Soratte]]. [[Guglielmo Marconi]] and astronomer [[Gino Cecchini]] reportedly participated in scientific examination of the craft under RS-33's oversight.

## OSS Recovery

Following Italy's collapse in 1943–1944, [[Pope Pius XII]] reportedly back-channeled intelligence about the craft to the United States. US [[Office of Strategic Services (OSS)]] agents recovered the craft in 1944 or 1945. [[David Grusch]] and journalist [[Christopher Sharp]] have cited this case in connection with UAP material held in US government custody that predates Roswell.
