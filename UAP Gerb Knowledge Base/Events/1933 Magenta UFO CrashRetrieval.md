The **1933 Magenta UFO Crash Retrieval** is the alleged crash or landing of an unknown bell-shaped or lenticular disc-shaped craft near [[Magenta, Italy]] on June 13, 1933. According to original Italian government documents provided to researcher [[Roberto Pinotti]], [[Benito Mussolini]]'s fascist regime recovered the craft, and the [[RS-33 (Gabinetto RS 33)]], a covert scientific commission Mussolini personally headed, secretly examined it. The case is notable for the claim that [[Richard Bissell Jr.]], CIA Deputy Director for Plans under Eisenhower, privately confirmed its authenticity to economist [[Harold Malmgren]].

## Recovery and Italian Custody

The craft, described as bell-shaped and approximately 10 meters in diameter, remained in Italian possession throughout the fascist era. A total press blackout followed the retrieval, with Italian authorities threatening "immediate imprisonment" and "maximum penalties" for anyone discussing the recovered "aircraft of unknown nature and origin." The craft was allegedly stored first in the hangars of the [[SIAI-Marchetti Aircraft Company]] in [[Varese, Italy]], and later moved to [[Benito Mussolini|Mussolini]]'s bunker under [[Mount Soratte]]. [[Guglielmo Marconi]], physicist [[Enrico Fermi]] and astronomer [[Gino Cecchini]] reportedly took part in the scientific examination of the craft under RS-33's oversight. Fermi later created the world's first artificial nuclear reactor during the Manhattan Project. Marconi's daughter and grandson have separately confirmed that he was involved with the real RS-33 and was "absolutely convinced that extraterrestrials existed," though according to the family it was Fermi, not Marconi, who led the commission's work.

## OSS Recovery

After Italy's collapse in 1943–1944, [[Pope Pius XII]] and the Vatican reportedly passed intelligence about the craft to the United States through a back channel. It went by way of the OSS, which later became the CIA, and reached President Franklin D. Roosevelt. US [[Office of Strategic Services (OSS)]] agents recovered the craft in 1944 or 1945. [[David Grusch]] and journalist [[Christopher Sharp]] have cited this case in connection with UAP material held in US government custody that predates Roswell.

## Treatment as the Timeline's Preliminary Entry

UAP Gerb's "80 Years of UFO Crash Retrieval and Reverse Engineering" timeline video deliberately treats the Magenta case as a "preliminary entry" placed ahead of 1947, the true start of its US-centric timeline of the legacy program. The video cites [[David Grusch]]'s statement in a 2023 News Nation interview that Roswell was not the first UFO crash retrieval; the Magenta recovery came 14 years before it. Grusch has said he chose the Magenta case to show that crash retrievals are an international phenomenon and not only an American one. He ran his disclosure of it through his own security approval office before speaking publicly. In Gerb's own assessment, the Magenta vehicle was likely unmanned, since the craft appeared to lack control surfaces, visible propulsion systems or other hallmarks of a piloted aircraft as understood at the time. He also reasons that after the recovery, growing Cold War tensions in Europe led the Americans to shelve a dedicated national effort to study it further.
