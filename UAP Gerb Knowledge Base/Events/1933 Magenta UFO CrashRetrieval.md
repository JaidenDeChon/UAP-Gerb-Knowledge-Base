The **1933 Magenta UFO Crash Retrieval** refers to the alleged crash or landing of an unknown bell-shaped or lenticular disc-shaped craft near [[Magenta, Italy]] on June 13, 1933. According to original Italian government documents provided to researcher [[Roberto Pinotti]], the craft was recovered by [[Benito Mussolini]]'s fascist regime and secretly examined by the [[RS-33 (Gabinetto RS 33)]], a covert scientific commission Mussolini personally headed. The case is notable for the claim that [[Richard Bissell Jr.]] — CIA Deputy Director for Plans under Eisenhower — privately confirmed its authenticity to economist [[Harold Malmgren]].

## Recovery and Italian Custody

The craft — described as approximately 10 meters in diameter and bell-shaped — remained in Italian possession throughout the fascist era. A total press blackout followed the retrieval, with Italian authorities threatening "immediate imprisonment" and "maximum penalties" for anyone discussing the recovered "aircraft of unknown nature and origin." The craft was allegedly stored first at [[SIAI-Marchetti Aircraft Company]] hangars in [[Varese, Italy]] and later transferred to [[Benito Mussolini|Mussolini]]'s bunker under [[Mount Soratte]]. [[Guglielmo Marconi]] and physicist [[Enrico Fermi]], creator of the world's first artificial nuclear reactor during the Manhattan Project, reportedly participated in scientific examination of the craft alongside astronomer [[Gino Cecchini]] under RS-33's oversight. Marconi's daughter and grandson have separately confirmed he was involved with the real RS-33 and was "absolutely convinced that extraterrestrials existed," though according to the family it was actually Fermi, rather than Marconi, who spearheaded the commission's work.

## OSS Recovery

Following Italy's collapse in 1943–1944, [[Pope Pius XII]] and the Vatican reportedly back-channeled intelligence about the craft to the United States via the OSS, which later became the CIA, reaching President Franklin D. Roosevelt. US [[Office of Strategic Services (OSS)]] agents recovered the craft in 1944 or 1945. [[David Grusch]] and journalist [[Christopher Sharp]] have cited this case in connection with UAP material held in US government custody that predates Roswell.

## Treatment as the Timeline's Preliminary Entry

UAP Gerb's "80 Years of UFO Crash Retrieval and Reverse Engineering" timeline video treats the Magenta case as a deliberate "preliminary entry" preceding the true 1947 start of its US-centric legacy program timeline, citing [[David Grusch]]'s 2023 News Nation interview statement that Roswell was not the first UFO crash retrieval — the Magenta recovery predated it by 14 years. Grusch has stated he selected the Magenta case specifically to demonstrate that crash retrievals are not solely a US phenomenon but an international one, and ran his disclosure of it through his own security approval office before speaking publicly. Gerb offers his own assessment that the Magenta vehicle was likely unmanned, reasoning that the craft appeared to lack control surfaces, visible propulsion systems, or other hallmarks of a piloted aircraft as understood at the time, and that following the case's recovery, growing Cold War tensions in Europe led the Americans to shelve a dedicated national effort to study it further.
