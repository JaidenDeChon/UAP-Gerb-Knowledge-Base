The Calvine UFO photo is a photograph of a large, diamond-shaped object apparently hovering over the hills near Calvine, Perthshire, Scotland, taken on 4 August 1990, with a military jet visible nearby. It was one of six photographs handed to the *Daily Record*, which passed them to the UK Ministry of Defence for analysis; the negatives were never returned. A surviving print was tracked down and published in 2022 by researcher David Clarke, and it is often described as one of the clearest UFO photographs ever taken.

## Project Condign and "Astra/Aurora"

In "UFOs in the Private Sector - Lockheed Martin", Gerb points out that the Ministry of Defence's [[Project Condign]] study lists the Calvine image as "Astra / Aurora photos", among other 35mm slides that do not appear in the released file. He reads this as a sign that British officials connected the triangle-and-diamond sightings of the period to a US contractor program, the alleged [[Aurora Program|Aurora or Astra]] project he links to [[Lockheed Martin]].
