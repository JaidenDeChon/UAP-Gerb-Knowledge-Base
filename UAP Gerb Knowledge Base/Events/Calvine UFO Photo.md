The Calvine UFO photo was taken on 4 August 1990 near Calvine, Perthshire, Scotland. It shows a large, diamond-shaped object apparently hovering over the hills, with a military jet visible nearby. It was one of six photographs handed to the *Daily Record*, which passed them to the UK Ministry of Defence for analysis, and the negatives were never returned. In 2022 researcher David Clarke tracked down a surviving print and published it. The image is often described as one of the clearest UFO photographs ever taken.

## Project Condign and "Astra/Aurora"

In "UFOs in the Private Sector - Lockheed Martin", Gerb points out that the Ministry of Defence's [[Project Condign]] study lists the Calvine image as "Astra / Aurora photos", among other 35mm slides that do not appear in the released file. He takes this to mean that British officials connected the triangle and diamond sightings of the period to a US contractor program: the alleged [[Aurora Program|Aurora or Astra]] project, which he links to [[Lockheed Martin]].
