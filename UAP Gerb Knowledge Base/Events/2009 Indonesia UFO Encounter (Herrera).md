The 2009 Indonesia UFO Encounter is an incident in which [[United States Marine Corps|USMC]] Rifleman [[Michael Herrera]] and five other Marines saw unmarked US forces loading a large octagonal, pyramid-shaped craft. It happened during a humanitarian relief mission in Indonesia after the October 2009 Sumatra earthquake and tsunami. Armed operators in black fatigues held the Marines at gunpoint and threatened to kill them if they disclosed what they had seen. Herrera stayed silent for 14 years, then testified to [[AARO]] in April 2023 and spoke at the [[National Press Club]] in June 2023.

## Mission Context

In October 2009, Herrera, then 20 years old, was deployed aboard the [[USS Denver]] for a humanitarian relief operation after a major earthquake in Indonesia. He was on officer's chow detail when a female USMC major briefed him and other Marines for a security mission. The briefing included photographs of [[Barack Obama]] as a child and references to his time in Indonesia.

The team was issued M16 rifles, although Herrera normally carried a SAW. A CH-53 Super Stallion helicopter flew them first to Tobing Airport and then to a hasty landing zone in thick vegetation. The mission was described as guarding supply deliveries to remote villages in a region described as the "second-largest terrorist capital in the world." Yet the landing zone (LZ) where Herrera's team was dropped had no other personnel, no radio communications and no clear extraction timeline, which was very unusual for a routine security detail.

## The Craft

After landing, the six Marines climbed a hill overlooking the LZ. From there they saw what looked like a structure changing color in the distance, and they decided to investigate, even though it meant leaving their security post.

Closer in, they found an octagonal, pyramid-shaped craft about the size of a football field. It had these features:

- It seemed to absorb light. Herrera called it "the blackest, darkest thing" he had ever seen, and its surface had a scale-like pattern.
- It changed color, shifting between a light matte gray and a very dark matte black.
- It had vents at the corners, along its edges.
- Beneath it was a raised platform about as long as the craft, with pointed features. The platform slowly rose about 10 feet off the ground before the craft left.
- It was nearly silent. The only sound was a low hum like a guitar amplifier or transformer, and there was no rotor wash and no movement in the surrounding vegetation.
- When it took off, lights at its corners came on in red, yellow, green and blue.

## The Transport Operation

At the same time, Herrera's team watched a logistics operation that included:

- Four black up-armored trucks that looked like Ford F-350s, with aggressive off-road tires and reinforced armor similar to Secret Service vehicles
- Pelican cases (weapons cases) in each truck bed
- Trailers carrying black shipping containers, each with a large black cylinder running the full width of the container
- Night vision equipment mounted on the sides of the trucks, which suggested they operated at night
- Trucks driving onto the raised platform beneath the craft to be loaded

Herrera first told AARO that the cylinders might be humidity control equipment, possibly connected to drug trafficking. He later learned from program insiders that the cylinders were oxygen supply systems and that the containers held human beings. These were allegedly people recruited from disaster zones under false pretenses to serve as "pink assets" (biological subjects) in classified programs.

## Armed Interception

As Herrera's team broke into the clearing, eight armed operators in unmarked black uniforms moved in on them in a tactical formation. The operators:

- Wore all-black gear with no identifying markings or insignia
- Carried M4-style rifles with ACOGs and PEQ-16 illuminators
- Used professional tactics consistent with former military training
- Disarmed the Marines methodically, removing their magazines and slinging the rifles uncomfortably tight on the Marines' backs
- Scanned the Marines' military IDs with thin devices that looked like modern smartphones, more advanced than the technology of 2009
- Threatened the Marines directly: "We will fucking kill you" and "It's easy to get lost out here"
- Knew exactly where the Marines kept their IDs, which suggested former US military experience
- Searched the team and confiscated their camera documentation, then gave back the Marines' weapons loaded in a deliberately awkward configuration and ordered them to leave without looking back

Their tactical skill and their knowledge of Marine equipment protocols suggested they were former US military personnel working in an unofficial capacity.

## Return and Aftermath

Back at the LZ, Herrera's team met an unknown Gunnery Sergeant who was not wearing the red patches that showed a ship assignment. After a radio call, a CH-53 helicopter arrived shortly and took the team back to the USS Denver.

At Subic Bay, Herrera asked a fellow Marine whether he would tell anyone about the incident. The Marine said "absolutely not," and the team kept quiet. None of the other Marines has come forward publicly. When Senate Intelligence Committee representatives asked one of them to give anonymous testimony, he replied by text message: "leave me out of that".

## Herrera's account was allegedly threatened further by a USAF Lieutenant Colonel who stated: "You're not allowed to talk about what happened. You will go to prison or you will die."

## Significance and Implications

If Herrera's account is accurate, the encounter is evidence of four things:

1. Operational [[Alien Reproduction Vehicle (ARV)|Alien Reproduction Vehicles (ARVs)]]. The large octagonal craft had characteristics unlike those of any known conventional aircraft and may have been non-human technology that was successfully reverse-engineered.
2. Shadow military operations. Unmarked US forces ran logistics operations with advanced craft outside the official military chain of command.
3. Human trafficking for classified programs. Disaster zones were allegedly used to recruit "pink assets" under false pretenses for classified biological or technological programs.
4. Advanced weapons. Herrera later learned from insiders that the programs have [[EMP-like weaponry]] that can target and disable UAP so they can be retrieved after a crash.

The encounter falls in the same general period as other alleged ARV sightings, including reports of triangular craft during the 1990 [[Belgian UFO Wave]] and [[Rodrik Castle|Sergeant Roderick Castle]]'s 1997 encounter with a triangular craft at [[Marine Corps Air Ground Combat Center 29 Palms|29 Palms Marine Corps Base]].

## Connection to [[Steven Greer]]'s ARV Theory

Herrera's testimony came out through [[Steven Greer]]'s disclosure efforts. Greer is known for promoting the theory that most UFOs are [[Alien Reproduction Vehicle (ARV)|Alien Reproduction Vehicles (ARVs)]], human-made craft reverse-engineered from non-human technology. Several whistleblowers, including [[Bob Lazar]], [[David Grusch]] and [[Ross Coulthart]], as well as the [[Wilson-Davis Memo|Wilson Davis Memo]], suggest instead that recovered non-human technology is so far beyond human understanding that copying it may be impossible. That casts doubt on whether the craft Herrera saw was really human-made or of non-human origin.
