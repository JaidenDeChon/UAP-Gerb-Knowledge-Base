The MV Marala North Atlantic Sighting took place on August 4, 1950, when the ship master, chief mate, and third mate of the merchant vessel MV Marala saw an anomalous elliptical object during night operations in the North Atlantic. [[Project Blue Book]] classified the case as "unidentified", one of the very few ship-based maritime cases to receive that label. That makes it one of the more significant officially acknowledged events in the Blue Book files that border on USO (unidentified submerged object) cases. Gerb calls it a personal favorite among the cases in the USO Case Book video.

## Incident Description

At approximately 10:00 p.m. EDT, the ship master, chief mate, and third mate of the MV Marala all saw an unidentified elliptical object approach their vessel from the southwest, heading northeast, at the same moment. Details of the sighting:

- The object passed within 1,000 feet of the ship.
- It hovered no more than 100 feet above sea level for the whole encounter.
- Its estimated speed ranged from 25 mph to 500 mph while they watched.
- They described it as ovular and cylindrical, like half an egg cut lengthwise.
- The witnesses variously described the surface as shiny aluminum or metallic white, but all three agreed on the elliptical form.
- All three described a rotary, wobbling motion as the object passed close over the ocean surface.
- The sighting lasted approximately one minute.

The object came in over the ocean, stayed extremely low the whole time, and left without entering the water. Its low flight over the ocean surface still places it within the scope of [[Unidentified Submerged Object (USO)]] research.

## Blue Book Classification

Blue Book listed this case as unidentified. That was an unusually candid label for a maritime UAP report at a time when ship-based cases were routinely explained away or not addressed at all. Of the 258 ship-based sighting reports in Blue Book's 20-year dataset, only 13 met the program's merit standards for reporting, and fewer still received the "unidentified" classification. Its official status as unidentified sets the Marala case apart from most maritime Blue Book cases, which were given prosaic or inconclusive explanations.

Gerb notes that the wobbling or gyrating motion all three witnesses described recurs in multiple USO and transmedium object cases, including [[Trans-Medium Vehicle|transmedium objects]] discussed by researchers at the [[Sol Foundation]].
