The Inman-Echler Telephone Call was a 1989 telephone conversation between NASA mission specialist [[Bob Echler]] and retired Admiral [[Bobby Ray Inman]], in which Echler asked about the possibility of making recovered UFO vehicles available for civilian scientific and technological research. Lord Hill Norton, a senior British naval officer with UAP interests, set up the call by giving Echler Inman's contact information. During the call, Inman told Echler that [[Everett Heinman]], Deputy Director of the CIA Directorate of Science and Technology, was "the best person to ask" about recovered craft in operational condition. UAP researchers treat this specific, unprompted referral as evidence that Inman had direct knowledge of which CIA officials managed the recovered craft program infrastructure.

After the call, [[Bob Echler]] arranged a meeting with [[Everett Heinman]] at CIA Langley, where Heinman denied any knowledge of UFOs or related programs. The exchange stayed a point of interest for UAP researchers for decades. In 2022, Twitter user RGH_UFOs contacted Heinman and mentioned that Inman had named him as the right person to ask about recovered craft. Heinman did not deny having such knowledge; he said only that he was "far removed from that area of work." UAP researchers treat this 2022 non-denial as an ambiguous partial confirmation. The Inman-Echler telephone call is considered one of the clearest documented instances of a senior US intelligence official pointing specifically toward CIA DS&T as the management node for alleged UAP material holdings. Inman's access spanned several agencies: NSA, CIA, NRO, and Naval Intelligence.
