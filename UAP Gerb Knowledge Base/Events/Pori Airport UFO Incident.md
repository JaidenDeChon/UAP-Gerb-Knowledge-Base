name: "Pori Airport UFO Incident"
date: 1969-04-12
tags:

---

Finnish Defense Force pilots Tarmo Tukia and a second pilot observed seven disc-shaped pale yellow objects that accelerated away at great speed; radar 200 km away in Vaasa also detected the objects. This is the only UFO incident officially acknowledged by the Finnish Air Force.
