The Pori Airport UFO Incident of April 12, 1969, is the only UFO observation acknowledged by the [[Finnish Air Force]]. During a Fouga Magister jet training mission at [[Pori Airport]] in western Finland, a [[Finnish Defense Force]] flight controller sent pilot-in-training [[Tarmo Tukeva]] to investigate seven presumed air balloons floating 1,500 to 3,000 metres above the airport. A second pilot, [[Jouko Kuronen]], also saw the objects.

## The Pilots' Observations

As [[Gerb]] recounts it in [[Video - Global Air Force UFO Encounters You've Probably Never Heard of|Global Air Force UFO Encounters You've Probably Never Heard of]], Tukeva reported on approach that the objects were not balloons but disc-shaped and slightly round, "like balls with no extremities", and pale yellow. As he tried to close on them they accelerated away from him at great speed, against a headwind of 180 km/h. Kuronen observed them as well, confirming their anomalous shape and speed.

## Radar

Pori Airport itself recorded no signatures of the objects (Gerb says "sonar"), but, according to Gerb, radar 200 km away in [[Vaasa, Finland|Vaasa]] detected the seven objects after they had accelerated away from Tukeva, giving an estimated speed of 3,218 metres per second.

## Gerb's Assessment

Gerb admits it is a short case whose trail ends there, and includes it because Scandinavian nations are rarely heard from on UFO sightings. He calls it "a bit tricky" but leans toward a UFO explanation, mainly because of the pilot's "ironclad" testimony.
