The Bob Fish email to John Podesta is a March 2015 message in which [[Bob Fish]], a former program manager for classified US government advanced communications projects, told [[John Podesta]] that "one of the government programs that collects hard data on unidentified flying objects is the USAF DSP satellite program." It became public when [[WikiLeaks]] published Podesta's emails during the 2016 US presidential campaign, and it is the centrepiece of UAP Gerb's [[FASTWALKERS – UFOs Outside Earth]].

## What the email says

Fish wrote that he was never briefed into the [[Defense Support Program (DSP)]] directly, but met its people as the US prepared for [[Operation Desert Shield]] and [[Operation Desert Storm]], sometimes lunching with them in the cafeteria of a highly classified organization in [[El Segundo, California]] that no one could enter without a TS/SCI clearance. On one occasion a member of the group, excited, said they had just picked up a [[Fast Walkers|fast walker]]. It had entered the atmosphere from deep space, from the far side of the satellite, and passed the DSP satellite "pretty closely" on its way to Earth. It was very fast, and it made a 30-degree course correction, which to Fish meant it was not on the ballistic re-entry path a meteorite would follow and was "under some sort of control", manned or robotic.

Toward the end, Fish quoted the 2014 DSP fact sheet, which says researchers at the [[Aerospace Corporation]] "have used DSP to develop portions of a hazard support system that will aid public safety in the future", as a sign DSP was still collecting such data. He also linked a since-defunct page titled "Top Secret Spy Satellite Spot UFOs" by [[Tim Schwartz]], saying much of it was correct.
