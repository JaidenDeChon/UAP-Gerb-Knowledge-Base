The Bluegill Triple Prime UFO Recovery is an alleged October 1962 incident in which a US high-altitude nuclear test disabled an unidentified object, which then fell into the Pacific and was recovered by the US Navy. Bluegill Triple Prime was a real test in Operation Fishbowl, the high-altitude series of the 1962 Operation Dominic nuclear tests over Johnston Island. The UFO claim comes from the late presidential adviser [[Harold Malmgren]]. UAP Gerb calls it an acknowledged USO retrieval in which the Navy took part.

## The account

According to Malmgren, the Bluegill Triple Prime detonation accidentally disabled a nearby UFO, which went careening into the ocean. The Navy searched for and recovered the downed craft, referred to as a "pod". UAP Gerb cites the historical logs of the salvage ship USS Safeguard. They describe the Safeguard, with vessels including the USS Engage, steaming to the crash location, searching in a square grid pattern and using divers to recover a "pod". During the net recovery the Safeguard lost power, and the pod was released for pickup by helicopter.

## Significance

UAP Gerb notes that the [[Deep Submergence Systems Project (DSSP)|Deep Submergence Systems Project]], which built the Navy's deep-recovery submersibles, began in 1964, two years after this recovery. He treats the incident as evidence that the [[US Navy]] had experience retrieving objects from the sea well before the later cases attributed to the [[National Underwater Reconnaissance Office]]. He plans to explore it in more detail in a future video.
