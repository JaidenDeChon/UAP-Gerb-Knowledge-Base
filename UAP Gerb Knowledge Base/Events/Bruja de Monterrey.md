The Bruja de Monterrey ("Witch of Monterrey") is a video recorded on 17 May 2006 by members of the OVNI Club Nuevo León, a Mexican UFO group led by researcher Diana Perla Chapa, from the top of Cerro de las Mitras, a mountain on the edge of Monterrey, Nuevo León. It shows a small dark figure drifting in the evening sky, which the group and later viewers likened to a hooded, black-clad humanoid, hence the name "witch". The clip became one of Mexico's best-known UFO videos.

## Link to the Metapod

The Bruja de Monterrey is one of the recordings that UFO commentators have connected to the [[Metapod]], the crescent-shaped craft filmed near [[El Escorial, Madrid, Spain]] in September 2015. A Portuguese forum that discussed the Metapod clip on 8 September 2015 already noted its similarity to the Monterrey footage.

In his Metapod analysis, Gerb counts the Bruja de Monterrey among five recordings from 1998 to 2023 that he argues show the same craft. He reads it as the Metapod's non-transparent back, moving away from the camera through hills and brush while holding the same vertical position seen in the Spanish footage.
