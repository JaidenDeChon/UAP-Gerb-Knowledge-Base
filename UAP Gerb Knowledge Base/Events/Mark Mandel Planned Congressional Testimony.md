[[Mark McCandlish]] was allegedly scheduled to appear before elements of the US Senate on UAP matters in June 2021. According to UAP researcher [[Robert D. Morning Star]], McCandlish had been in direct contact with Senator [[Marco Rubio]] in the months before April 2021 and was preparing to testify under oath about his decades of investigation into the [[Alien Reproduction Vehicle (ARV)|Alien Reproduction Vehicle Flux Liner]], the classified 1988 [[Air Force Plant 42]] exhibit witnessed by [[Brad Sorenson]], and related UAP legacy program matters.

McCandlish died on April 13, 2021, from an apparent self-inflicted gunshot wound, approximately two months before his scheduled testimony. Gerb and other researchers have asked whether the move from informal public disclosure to sworn Senate testimony may have been the "final straw" that ended whatever protection Brad Sorenson alleged he had provided to McCandlish. In Sorenson's words: "I protected him until I could not."

The timing parallels other cases in which people who had committed to formal Congressional testimony died or were otherwise silenced before they could appear.
