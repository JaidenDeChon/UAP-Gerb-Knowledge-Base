On or around February 11, 2024, the [[Sol Foundation]] released a series of interviews and panel discussions with prominent figures in UAP research, whistleblower testimony and disclosure advocacy. The release brought academic credibility and a formal institutional structure to UAP discussion.

## Featured Figures

The released materials included interviews and panels with:

- [[David Grusch]], a former National Geospatial-Intelligence Agency officer and UAP whistleblower who testified before Congress in 2023 about alleged crash retrieval programs and non-human intelligence
- [[Chris Mellon]], a former Deputy Assistant Secretary of Defense for Intelligence who has been instrumental in UAP disclosure efforts and in congressional engagement on the topic
- [[Kevin Knuth]], Professor of Physics at the University at Albany, who has published peer-reviewed research on UAP flight characteristics and applies Bayesian statistical methods to UAP analysis
- [[Hal Puthoff]], a physicist and long-time UAP researcher, whom Gerb also names among the figures in the release

## Significance

The Sol Foundation's release was notable for several reasons:

1. By hosting interviews with both government insiders and credentialed academic researchers, the Sol Foundation helped position UAP research as worthy of serious scholarly attention.

2. The release came roughly six months after Grusch's bombshell congressional testimony, and it gave room for a deeper discussion of the implications than the constraints of formal congressional hearings allow.

3. It brought intelligence community whistleblowers, former defense officials and academic physicists together, which showed how many sides the UAP issue has: national security, scientific inquiry and policy.

4. Individual interviews on podcasts or news programs are one-off; the Sol Foundation offered an organizational framework that suggested sustained, structured inquiry.

## UAP Community Response

Gerb mentioned the release in his Metapod analysis video and said he intended to review and analyze the Sol Foundation materials in a later video: "the soul Foundation just released a bunch of interviews with Grusch, Chris Mellon, Kevin Knuth, Hal Puthoff... so the next video which I'm going to try to get out as quick as possible I'm going to be reviewing all those uh panels and kind of putting out my thoughts."

His comment reflects wider excitement in the UAP research community about the Sol Foundation's work and its potential to move discussion beyond speculation toward rigorous investigation.
