On March 5, 2004, the [[Mexican Air Force]] filmed 11 unidentified flying objects using infrared equipment over the southern state of [[Campeche, Mexico|Campeche]] during a routine anti-drug trafficking vigilance mission. The lights appeared to fly at altitudes of 3,500 meters and surrounded the military jet as it conducted its patrol. Major [[Magdaleno Castanon]] stated that the jets "chased the lights" and believed "they could feel we were pursuing them."

Only three of the eleven objects showed up on the plane's radar. Skeptic [[Michael Shermer]], head of [[Skeptic Magazine]], suggested the lights were burnoff flares from an offshore oil platform in the [[Gulf of Mexico]]. This prosaic explanation has been considered but not conclusively confirmed, and the case remains notable for the multiple infrared detections and partial radar corroboration.
