The Rodrik Castle UAP encounter is an alleged incident in March 1997, during the [[Hunter Warrior Advanced Warfighting Experiment]] at Marine Corps Air Ground Combat Center Twentynine Palms, in which Marine Sergeant [[Rodrik Castle]] and five to six fellow Marines of [[VMA-513]] came upon a large triangular craft hovering near [[Emerson Dry Lake]], guarded by an armed, unmarked ground team that detained them at gunpoint.

## The Encounter

Sent out one night to investigate reported flare activity three to five miles into the desert, Castle's crash-rescue team crested a hill near Emerson Dry Lake and saw a matte black equilateral triangle, an estimated 200 to 300 feet long, hovering silently 150 to 200 feet up, with a spherical light at each corner and a slow counterclockwise rotation. Below it were about 30 armed men in unmarked black uniforms with five to six blacked-out trucks. About half of them ran at the Marines with weapons drawn and forced them to keep their heads down. After two to three minutes the craft climbed away to the northwest at high speed, toward [[Edwards Air Force Base]], and the ground team drove off the same way. Castle's Humvee would not start for about five minutes afterwards, and his superiors told the Marines it had been flare activity.

## Identity of the Ground Team

In his investigation of [[Sandia National Laboratories]], UAP Gerb states that he has theorized with medium to high confidence that the ground team was a Department of Energy [[DOE Special Response Teams (SRTs)|Special Response Team]], the armed federal protective force, formerly trained and staffed by [[Wackenhut Services]], that guards DOE sites and the [[Office of Secure Transportation (OST)|Office of Secure Transportation]]. He regards the craft as a triangular [[Alien Reproduction Vehicle (ARV)|alien reproduction vehicle]] or a derivative vehicle.
