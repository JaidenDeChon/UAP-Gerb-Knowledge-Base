The UAP Campaign Plan — Way Forward is a five-phase roadmap for UAP disclosure drawn up by retired U.S. Army Colonel [[Karl Nell]], who presented it at the [[SOL Foundation Symposium]] in November 2023. It runs along four channels at the same time (public sector, philosophical investigation, scientific research and private sector) and sets a series of phase transition points between January 2024 and October 2034. Phase 3, in October 2030, is identified as the potential inflection point for public disclosure to humanity. On the public record, it is one of the most detailed strategic disclosure frameworks put forward by a credentialed firsthand UAP witness.

## Overview

Nell presented the plan as a deliberate route to a controlled disclosure. He contrasted it with "catastrophic disclosure", in which all UAP information comes out without coordination, possibly released by an adversarial nation or by non-human intelligence itself. He argued that a managed disclosure, though imperfect, would preserve social cohesion and allow proper legislative and scientific oversight, while an uncontrolled one could severely disrupt global institutions, financial markets and religious frameworks.

## The Four Channels

The plan works through four channels at once:

1. Public sector: government acceptance of UAP as a bipartisan political issue, legislative action such as the Schumer Amendment, and the establishment of credible oversight bodies.
2. Philosophical investigation: drawing on the academic philosophy, theology and ethics communities to prepare frameworks for understanding non-human intelligence.
3. Scientific research: building a peer-reviewed scientific literature on UAP signatures and materials, and giving academics verifiable data to work with.
4. Private sector: bringing in commercial interests and private funding to support research and technology development related to UAP phenomena.

## The Five Phases

| Phase | Target Date | Goal |
|-------|-------------|------|
| Phase 0 | Pre-January 2024 | Narrative shaping: NY Times UAP article, DoD video releases, AATIP, UAP Task Force establishment |
| Phase 1 | January 1, 2024 | Government acceptance; UAP established as bipartisan political issue |
| Phase 2 | January 1, 2026 | UAP signature correlation and academic integration |
| Phase 3 | October 2030 | Public acceptance; potential inflection point for controlled disclosure |
| Phase 4 | October 2034 | Answer the five W's of UAP (marked off target on the slide) |
| Phase 5 | Indefinite | Interactive approach; integration of non-human intelligence and NHI technology with Earth civilization |

Phase 3 is the plan's inflection point and possibly the moment of disclosure for humanity. Gerb calls public acceptance "a tall ask". The slide marks Phase 2 as still at risk and Phase 4 (fully answering who, what, when, where and why) as off target. By early 2024 Gerb judged Phase 1 all but complete, ending with the [[Schumer Amendment (NDAA)|Schumer legislation]].

## Significance

Nell is identified as one of the 40 firsthand witness whistleblowers whose testimony informed [[David Grusch]]'s 2023 congressional disclosure. He presented at the [[Sol Foundation]] alongside Grusch, [[Chris Mellon]], [[Kevin Knuth]], [[Gary Nolan]] and [[Jacques Vallee]], one of the densest gatherings of credentialed UAP researchers and witnesses at a single public event. The plan's level of detail, with specific dates, measurable phase targets and analytical channels, sets it apart from most public commentary on UAP.

Gerb notes that [[AARO]] is missing from Phase 0's list of narrative shapers, and imagines [[Sean Kirkpatrick]] "seething" about it.
