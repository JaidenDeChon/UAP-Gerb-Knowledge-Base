Project Sanddollar was a highly compartmented US Navy program to find and recover items of national security interest from the seafloor. It ran as a covert sub-program nested inside the Polaris submarine-launched ballistic missile program. Its existence is documented in *The Silent War: The Cold War Battle Beneath the Sea* (2001) by Dr. John P. Craven, the Navy's Chief Scientist for Special Projects and a founding figure in US deep submergence technology. A naval intelligence officer briefed Craven into Sanddollar in 1965. Even Craven's own staff was not to know where he was going or whom he was meeting.

## Discovery and Structure

Craven was first brought in by a well-known submarine officer. He later learned that this officer's involvement with the Polaris program was itself a cover for Sanddollar. At the briefing, Craven learned that Sanddollar sat inside a complex web of programs hidden under the Polaris SAP umbrella: Polaris led to several layers of cover programs, and those led to Sanddollar. Craven described being shown an inventory of items known to be on the seabed and a map of where they lay around the world. He was asked whether he could organize and manage a recovery program using DSSP (Deep Submergence Systems Project) assets, and run it so that his "family, friends, and closest professional colleagues would have no idea" he was involved.

This arrangement, a compartmented recovery program inside a cover program under a tier-one SAP umbrella, exactly mirrors the SAP tier-1/tier-2/tier-3 structure set out in DoD Directive 5205.07, where an umbrella contains compartments and each compartment contains sub-compartments. Craven described a similar idea himself. He called it the "seventh veil", a system of secrecy around top-secret unacknowledged programs in which no one could ever be sure of having reached the innermost layer of compartmentation.

## Connection to UFO Legacy Programs

UAP Gerb identifies Sanddollar as highly likely to have been a maritime UFO crash retrieval program. The Navy's DSSP, which Sanddollar used as its operational cover, developed Deep Submergence Rescue Vehicles (DSRVs) and Deep Submergence Search Vehicles (DSSVs) that could reach ocean floor depths that had previously been out of reach. The channel has discussed testimony from a DSRV crew member who claimed in 1991 to have retrieved an exotic triangular craft with glyphic writing from the seafloor off Aberdeen, Scotland. That is the kind of operation Sanddollar was equipped to carry out.

UAP Gerb cites Craven's account of Sanddollar in Chapter 10 ("Spooked") of *The Silent War* as one of the most poignant independent descriptions of the layered, onion-like structure of legacy programs. In Craven's account, every special intelligence project needed a real cover project, most people working on the cover project did not know it was a cover, and one or more people cleared for both levels acted as links between the two.

Project Sanddollar is the naval precedent for UAP Gerb's wider thesis that UFO activities were nested inside cover SAP umbrellas. UAP Gerb argues that UFO-related Army activities were hidden inside the [[Yellow Fruit]] SAP umbrella in the same way Sanddollar was hidden inside Polaris, and that similar structures likely existed within Air Force and NRO programs. In each case the structure is the same. A harmless-looking umbrella program provides the SAP cover, and the real mission sits in nested sub-compartments open only to a handful of people cleared for both levels.
