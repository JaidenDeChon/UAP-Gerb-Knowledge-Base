The **Blackjack Team** is an entity named on an allegedly leaked 1991 National Reconnaissance Office document. [[Steven Greer]]'s sources describe it as one half of a reverse-engineering pairing with a sister entity, **Blackjack Control**. Gerb treats the two as a possible joint reverse-engineering group spanning [[Edwards Air Force Base]], [[Nellis Air Force Base]] and the [[Nevada Test and Training Range (NTR)|Nevada Test and Training Range]]. No public record of either has been found.

## The 1991 NRO document

In July 1991 a document said to be a classified [[NRO (National Reconnaissance Office)|NRO]] communication was leaked to the public. It informed need-to-know groups and operations that civilian flying saucer organisations were organising protests in Nevada. Among its addressees are Royal Ops, Cosmic Ops, M Ops and Majic Ops; Air Force units designated 26th, 64th, 65th and 527th; Nellis AFOSI; Groom Lake and Dreamland; [[Area 51]] S4; AquaTech; the Sea Spray special operations group; and the Blackjack Team. Greer says he gave the document to Admiral [[Thomas Wilson]], of the [[Wilson-Davis Memo]]. Wilson allegedly said he recognised one of the entities on it and that a contractor ran it.

## Blackjack Control and Blackjack Team

Greer says sources on the inside, including one within Blackjack Control, told him about two entities working together. The two accounts in UAP Gerb's video disagree about which base each one belongs to:

- In Gerb's narration, Blackjack Control is a facility at Nellis that takes part in reverse engineering, and the Blackjack Team works with it from Edwards.
- In a clip, Greer presents a list of key facilities that he says he updated for the president in 2009. He groups Blackjack Control with a "constellation" of Edwards-related sites, including Haystack Butte, China Lake, George Air Force Base, the retired [[Norton Air Force Base]] and NASA's Table Mountain Observatory, and says the Blackjack Team on the list was at Nellis.

The only other "Blackjack" Gerb found is a DARPA program from the late 2010s and early 2020s that set out to demonstrate a low-cost, high-speed network in low Earth orbit, using contractors owned by Raytheon. Because the NRO document dates to 1991, he doubts the two are related. He links Blackjack to the joint Edwards and Nellis program described by his witness [[Ed (Witness 11063)|Ed]] and calls it a starting point for research into Air Force and contractor legacy programs. He returns to the same document in his investigation of US Navy programs, listing groups such as Majic Ops, Red Flag MOC and Sea Spray.
