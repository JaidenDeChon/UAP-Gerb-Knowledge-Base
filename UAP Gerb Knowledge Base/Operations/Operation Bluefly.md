Operation Bluefly is an unacknowledged US government program mentioned in the MOON DUST video as one of several classified operations dealing with UFO crash retrieval and the collection of foreign aerospace vehicles. The video names Bluefly alongside [[Project Moon Dust]] and [[Operation Zodiac]] as part of a broader classified system for collecting anomalous objects, run by the US Air Force and intelligence agencies.

## Relationship to Project Moondust

Bluefly is described as a program related to [[Project Moon Dust]], which suggests the two had overlapping or complementary missions in UFO crash retrieval and the collection of aerospace objects. Bluefly's specific operational details remain classified or undisclosed. Its grouping with Moon Dust points to similar functions:

- Recovery of foreign or anomalous aerospace vehicles
- Coordination with intelligence agencies for material analysis
- Field operations for crash site assessment and retrieval
- Technical evaluation of recovered materials

## Unacknowledged Program Status

[[Project Blue Book]] was publicly acknowledged as the Air Force's official UFO investigation program. Operation Bluefly, by contrast, was a classified or unacknowledged program. That level of classification suggests Bluefly handled operational parts of UFO investigation that needed more security than the public-facing programs.

Its unacknowledged status fits with what Captain [[Edward J. Ruppelt]] and Dr. [[J. Allen Hynek]] disclosed about parallel classified programs that ran "more complete" UFO investigations alongside the official public ones.

## Planned Future Coverage

In the MOON DUST video, Gerb said that Operation Bluefly, [[Operation Zodiac]] and the [[CIA Office of Global Access]] would be covered in future videos. This indicates that more documentary evidence or testimony about Bluefly's operations exists but was not detailed in the Moon Dust episode. It also suggests that Bluefly has its own documentary trail, reachable through Freedom of Information Act requests or whistleblower testimony, that warrants a dedicated investigation.

## Significance

Operation Bluefly's existence adds to a pattern of compartmentalized UFO programs operating at different classification levels:

- Public Tier: [[Project Blue Book]] (1952-1969), which handled public information management and reached dismissive conclusions
- Classified Tier: [[Project Moon Dust]], Operation Bluefly and [[Operation Zodiac]], which carried out the actual crash retrieval and material collection
- Deeply Compartmentalized Tier: alleged contractor-run reverse engineering programs described in the [[Wilson-Davis Memo]]

Bluefly likely belonged to the "Classified Tier," carrying out field operations while staying unknown to the public and possibly to oversight committees.
