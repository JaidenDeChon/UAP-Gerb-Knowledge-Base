The Guardian Angel program was a security arrangement created in late 1979 by the [[Air Force Office of Special Investigations]] (AFOSI) Office of Special Projects (PJ) and the FBI to protect the Air Force's "acknowledged black programs". AFOSI's own published history describes it as a new approach at the time: a small cadre of FBI personnel was inserted into Air Force special access program (SAP) efforts. About eight FBI agents in Los Angeles and selected staff at FBI headquarters in Washington were briefed into many of the Air Force's most critical SAPs. The program's best-known result was the arrest of Thomas Patrick Cavanagh, a Northrop engineer on the B-2 program who tried to sell classified stealth technology.

In *The Hidden Wing*, a video on the UAP Gerb channel, Gerb notes that Guardian Angel's agents were placed near what he considers the two most critical areas in the continental United States for UFO programs. He links the program to reports from several non-public legacy-program insiders who say the FBI has harassed them from 2021 to the present; one of them worked directly in Air Force UFO research, development, test and evaluation. Gerb also points to Jeff Nuccetelli's claim, made on a UFO podcast in December 2025, that the FBI is infiltrating the UAP podcasting space. He asks whether the FBI element involved is attached to AFOSI/PJ under Guardian Angel or a successor program, and urges the administration to investigate whether PJ is acting against whistleblowers. He ties this to his allegations against former PJ executive director [[Terry Phillips]].
