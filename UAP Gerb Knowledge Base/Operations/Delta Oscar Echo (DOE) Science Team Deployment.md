A team of roughly 30 people in protective gear marked with the three-letter designation Delta, Oscar, Echo arrived at the UAP incident site in CH47 helicopters. Their arrival suggests an organized response operation.

The deployment is part of the [[Peru UFO Crash Incident]] of 1997, as described by Marine Lance Corporal [[Jonathan Weygandt]]. Weygandt says the team, "about 30 of them at least", wore rain jackets with "the big D, you know, Delta Oscar Echo on the back", alongside others in containment suits, and marched down to the crashed craft while he was being restrained. Gerb reads the letters as standing for the [[Department of Energy]], and identifies the team as the on-site retrieval science team of the DOE's [[NEST (Nuclear Emergency Support Team)|Nuclear Emergency Support Team]], deployed during [[Operation Laser Strike]].
