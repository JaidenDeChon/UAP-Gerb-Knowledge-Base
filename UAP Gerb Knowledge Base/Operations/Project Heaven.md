**Project Heaven**, also called Project Blue Heaven, is an alleged special project at [[Edwards Air Force Base]] in which a landed flying saucer and its crew were held and studied. It is known from the testimony of a physicist identified only by the pseudonym "Dr. Epigoni", recorded by researcher [[Leonard Stringfield]] in *UFO Crash Retrievals: The Inner Sanctum*, his Status Report VI (1991).

## Epigoni's account

Epigoni said he had worked closely with Oppenheimer, Einstein and Teller, and that in the late 1960s or early 1970s he worked on Project Heaven as a special project at Edwards. According to him:

- a flying saucer and its crew had landed at Edwards
- the craft was kept in a large, tightly guarded hangar, while its occupants were taken to a secure building for evaluation
- there were twelve beings, about 5 feet tall and dressed in silvery metal suits, with eyes of one colour, a nose, a mouth, teeth, and blood resembling oil
- because the bodies did not deteriorate, the researchers decided to leave them alone in case another craft came to "rejuvenate or reactivate them"
- the craft was a classic 40-foot saucer with a dome on top and no rivets, seams or welds; other scientists told him its materials were beyond anything they had imagined, and no one could find a way inside
- as of 1990 the project was still active and run by the Department of Defense, and the Navy had two or three similar projects

Stringfield noted that Epigoni later changed the number of bodies from twelve to four. He and a fellow investigator agreed that Epigoni appeared to have consulted his superiors and been told to alter key details.

## In UAP Gerb's research

UAP Gerb cites Project Heaven in *UAP Reverse Engineering at Edwards Air Force Base* as one of several Stringfield cases supporting witness [[Ed (Witness 11063)|Ed]]'s account of reverse-engineering work at Edwards. The detail that the beings walked out of the craft, lay down and seemingly turned themselves off reminds him of the craft donation theory, the idea discussed by [[David Grusch]] and others that some craft are found intact and unattended rather than crashed. He compares it with the alleged 1957 landing at Holloman Air Force Base and suggests the beings may have been something like biological robots. He says he will return to Epigoni's more detailed account in future.
