Project Heaven is an alleged special project described in [[Leonard Stringfield]]-related crash retrieval testimony as involving a landed saucer and biological occupants at or near [[Edwards Air Force Base]]. The operation is not publicly documented as an acknowledged US program and appears in the historical record primarily through secondary witness accounts and ufological reporting.

In this knowledge base, Project Heaven is treated as a legacy allegation that the host uses to contextualize modern whistleblower testimony about compartmentalized retrieval and handling pipelines.

## Claimed Scope in Source Accounts

- Custody and technical evaluation of a non-conventional craft.
- Restricted personnel access and controlled handling of biological entities.
- Continued project status claimed into later decades by anonymous sources.
