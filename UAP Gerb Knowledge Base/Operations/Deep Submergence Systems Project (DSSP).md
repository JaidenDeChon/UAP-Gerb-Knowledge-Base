The Deep Submergence Systems Project (DSSP) was a US Navy program begun in 1964, after the 1963 loss of the submarine USS Thresher, to build up the Navy's deep-ocean engineering so it could carry out search, rescue, salvage and recovery at much greater depths. It was led by Dr. [[John P. Craven]], chief scientist of the Navy's Special Projects Office. Its best-known products were the two [[Deep Submergence Rescue Vehicle (DSRV)|Deep Submergence Rescue Vehicles]], *Mystic* and *Avalon*. UAP researchers have argued that it was the engineering arm of [[Project Sanddollar|Sand Dollar]], a compartmented seafloor-recovery program, and that it gave the Navy its means of retrieving craft from the deep ocean.

## Program

The DSSP was founded out of the Navy's Special Projects Office. It aimed to build six DSRVs, plus Deep Submergence Search Vehicles (DSSVs) designed for seafloor exploration and object recovery, with clandestine capability and an operational depth of 20,000 feet. Officially only *Mystic* (DSRV-1) and *Avalon* (DSRV-2) were built, by Lockheed Missiles and Space Company. The project also led to deep clandestine craft such as the nuclear-powered NR-1.

Craven wrote in *The Silent War* that the rescue vehicles were built with intelligence missions in mind: "a DSRV designed, constructed, and deployed for every conceivable rescue mission would also be available for the intelligence 'mission impossible' that were sure to occur".

## Alleged link to Sand Dollar and UFO retrieval

In 1965, a year after the DSSP began, Craven was briefed on Sand Dollar, a program nested inside another secret program within the Polaris program. Its task was recovering militarily sensitive hardware and other items of national-security importance from the world's seafloors. Gerb argues that the DSSP met Sand Dollar's requirements, and that submarines linked to the [[National Underwater Reconnaissance Office]], such as the Halibut and Seawolf, carried its submersibles to their targets. He asks whether Sand Dollar was the Navy's first UFO legacy program, and points out that the DSSP began two years after the alleged 1962 [[Bluegill Triple Prime UFO Recovery]].

Gerb also suggests that the craft in the [[1991 North Atlantic USO Retrieval]], raised from nearly 1.5 miles down, was recovered by an unacknowledged DSSV-type vehicle rather than by *Mystic* or *Avalon*, whose rated depth was only just over a mile.
