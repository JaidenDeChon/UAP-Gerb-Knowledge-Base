Gerb mentions an alleged landing of a non-human craft at [[Holloman Air Force Base]] in several videos without ever examining it. Laid side by side, those mentions give it two different years. They also join two separate pieces of UFO lore. The best-supported claim, that former president George H. W. Bush told [[Eric Davis]] a Holloman landing was "the real thing", appears in Gerb's Corso video as support for an Eisenhower deal in 1954. Davis himself puts the landing Bush described in 1964, and links it to the filmed landing that Gerb covers in a different video.

## The finding

Gerb's coverage gives the Holloman landing as 1954 (*UFO Whistleblowers Vol.1*, the Corso video) and as 1957 (the Edwards video). Both 1954 mentions come with [[Dwight D. Eisenhower]] meeting the visitors and making an agreement. Outside the channel, the 1954 Eisenhower meetings at Holloman are a claim of the British author Timothy Good. Bush's reported confirmation, which Gerb attaches to them, concerns a different story: the Holloman landing said to have been filmed by the Air Force, which Davis says Bush dated to April 1964. In the Dugway video Gerb tells that film story through [[James Fox]] without any date, and he never links it to Bush. This analysis found no outside source for a 1957 landing with occupants.

One limit applies. The exact wording of the journal entry by [[Jacques Vallee]] that Gerb was reading from couldn't be checked from here. If that entry itself says 1954 and Eisenhower, the mix-up is Vallee's or his informants', not Gerb's.

## The evidence

1. [[Videos/UFO Whistleblowers Vol.1/summary|UFO Whistleblowers Vol.1]] (May 2024): "we cannot forget the astonishing story of Eisenhower meeting with a group of extraterrestrials after their landing at Hollowman Air Force Base in 1954 The Story Goes Eisenhower made an agreement with these being I've yet to dive too much into this alleged encounter".
2. [[Videos/Philip J. Corso - US Army UFO Technology Research & Development/summary|The Corso video]] (January 2025), reading from Vallee's *Forbidden Science* on Davis's conversations with Bush: "Bush expanded on the 1954 alleged Landing of non-human intelligence at Hollman Air Force Base where allegedly Eisenhower and nhi brokered a deal according to Bush this event really did happen resulting in a secret project with obscene security".
3. [[Videos/UAP Reverse Engineering at Edwards Air Force Base Redacted List Vol.2/summary|The Edwards video]] (October 2024), in its closing remarks on Dr. Epigoni's account in [[Project Heaven]]: "this story reminded mostely of the alleged 1957 Hollman Air Force Base Landing in which a flying suer saucer touchdown at Hollman Air Force Base and occupants walked out of the craft to interact with USAF Personnel". Earlier in the same video Gerb tells [[Gordon Cooper]]'s 1957 Edwards lakebed landing: "we can go all the way back to 1957 to the testimony of mercury astronaut Gordon cooper".
4. [[Videos/Dugway Proving Ground - UFO Legacy Programs/summary|The Dugway video]] (February 2025): "Fox discusses a film of a halt the Hollman Air Force Base landing and how his friend Allan or yes Allan Sandler viewed this film and told Fox about it well back in the 70s Alan Sandler and his co- video producer Robert emanager were attempting to make a documentary on UAP". No year is given for the landing itself.

## How it fits together

Outside reporting separates the threads that Gerb's mentions run together:

- The 1954 Eisenhower meetings come from Timothy Good, who said in 2012 that Eisenhower met extraterrestrials three times in 1954, the later meetings at Holloman. The February 1954 trip at the centre of the lore was officially a visit to a dentist in Los Angeles, and some versions put that first meeting at Edwards. This matches Gerb's 1954 mentions.
- The film story comes from Robert Emenegger and Allan Sandler. They said that in the early 1970s, while making *UFOs: Past, Present, and Future*, they were promised Air Force footage of a 1964 landing at Holloman, and the offer was then withdrawn. That's Gerb's Dugway story.
- As for Bush, in *The Age of Disclosure* (November 2025), Davis says Bush told him in 2003 about a landing at a New Mexico air base in April 1964 and was refused more detail. Reports on Vallee's March 2004 journal entry, which relays the same story through [[Hal Puthoff]], describe Bush calling the Holloman film "the real thing", with a secret project whose security was "obscene". Gerb's own paraphrase, "a secret project with obscene security", matches that wording.

So the confirmation Gerb uses for an Eisenhower deal in 1954 looks like it belongs to the 1964 film story. Gerb tells that story about seven weeks later in the Dugway video, through Fox, as if it were a separate tale. The 1957 date in the Edwards video matches none of these. The likeliest source, and this is an inference, is Cooper's 1957 Edwards landing, which comes earlier in the same video and is also a craft touching down at an Air Force base.

## What cuts against it

- Vallee's text could not be read here. Secondary accounts of the entry disagree on the year: one gives 1971, and Davis's later tellings give 1964. Neither mentions 1954 or Eisenhower, though that comes from search snippets; the book itself was not read.
- Holloman lore has more than two dates. One researcher, Art Campbell, puts an Eisenhower visit in February 1955. Gerb may have drawn on a source that merges Good's story with the film story.
- The 1957 date may just be a slip of the tongue in an unscripted closing segment, which would make the disagreement real but small.
- [[Philip J. Corso]]'s claimed encounter at Red Canyon on the White Sands range in 1957–58, which might look related, does not help. It's a different place and a different kind of event, and only the year matches.
- None of this bears on whether any landing happened. All three stories rest on witnesses and informants who are reported here, not tested.

## Already known?

The separate strands are well documented: Good's 1954 claim (widely reported in February 2012), Emenegger's 1964 footage (the Wikipedia article on *UFOs: Past, Present, and Future*), and Davis's 1964 Bush account (many outlets after *The Age of Disclosure*). UFO writers have noted that the film story's date wanders between 1964 and 1971. No source found points out that the Bush confirmation is being attached to the 1954 Eisenhower story, or anything about Gerb's dates.

## What would settle it

The March 26, 2004 entry in Vallee's *Forbidden Science* volume covering 2000–2009 (*Pacific Heights*). If it names 1954 or Eisenhower, Gerb read it correctly and the mix-up predates him. If it names only the Holloman film, or 1964 or 1971, Gerb added the Eisenhower deal himself. For the 1957 date, Gerb's own source for the Edwards closing segment is the only thing that would settle it.
