Gerb tells three stories that he never puts together. In his Magenta video, OSS agents take Mussolini's 1933 craft out of Italy in 1944 or 1945. In his 80-year timeline he relays a claim from Marconi's family that [[Enrico Fermi]] led the commission that studied the craft. In his Manhattan Project 2.0 video he presents the [[Alsos Mission]], the Manhattan Project's team for seizing enemy science, as the blueprint for UFO crash retrieval teams. Read together, they meet in one place: Rome in summer 1944, where Alsos and an OSS agent were questioning the physicists of Fermi's old institute.

## The finding

After Rome fell on 4 June 1944, Alsos teams under Colonel Boris Pash moved into the city. OSS agent Moe Berg, who worked alongside them, spent that summer talking to Italian physicists, Edoardo Amaldi and Gian Carlo Wick among them. Both had been members of Fermi's group at the University of Rome. Fermi had been in the United States since January 1939 and moved to Los Alamos in September 1944. So in the year Gerb's sources give for the OSS recovery, the only US team known to be hunting Italian science reported to the program Fermi worked in, and it was questioning his former colleagues.

That is as far as the evidence goes. It does not show that Alsos or Berg ever heard of RS-33 or the craft.

## What cuts against it

- **The Fermi premise is unconfirmed and probably overstated.** Reports of the April 2024 interview with Marconi's family in *Oggi* don't agree. UFO FEED says the grandson put Fermi *under* Marconi, and a podcast quote has him as vice president. Only one Portuguese report makes Fermi the man in charge. Gerb's own sentence first says Marconi spearheaded RS-33. His March 2024 Magenta video, made a month before the interview, names Mussolini as its head and never mentions Fermi.
- **The coincidence is close to automatic.** Rome was the first Axis capital the Allies took, and both Alsos and the OSS went in as soon as it fell. Almost any US intelligence story set in Italy in 1944 would overlap with them.
- **Alsos was hunting atomic work.** Every account says its Italian phase looked for German nuclear progress and learned little.
- **Gerb's chain names a different route.** [[David Grusch]] and [[Christopher Sharp]] credit a back channel from [[Pope Pius XII]]. Grusch speaks of "a secure uh Airbase in Italy", not Rome. Gerb calls the move to [[Mount Soratte]] only "likely". The bunker there was also Kesselring's headquarters until June 1944, and accounts say US bombers hit it on 12 May, which sits awkwardly with Sharp's claim that the facility was spared.

## The evidence

1. [[Videos/The 1933 Magenta, Italy UFO Crash/summary|The 1933 Magenta]]: the craft "was recovered by us agents of the office of strategic services in 1944 or 1945", and "after the war or the end of 1944 the craft was brought to the US". Gerb adds: "this is all based on reporting and I do not have a paper trail".
2. [[Videos/80 Years of UFO Crash Retrieval and Reverse Engineering - A Timeline/summary|80 Years]]: "Though according to his family, it was actually Enrio Fermy, creator of the world's first artificial nuclear reactor during the Manhattan project who spearheaded RS33."
3. [[Videos/The Manhattan Project 2.0 - the Secrecy of UFO Crash Retrieval Programs Vol.1/summary|Manhattan Project 2.0]]: "Phase one of Alsos saw operations in Italy", with orders "to secure all important persons, laboratories, [music] and scientific information immediately upon their becoming available." Gerb adds: "This sounds an awful lot like the intelligence apparatus behind UFO crash retrieval teams, does it not?"
4. The protocol Gerb reads in the Magenta video sends all [[RS-33 (Gabinetto RS 33)|RS-33]] reports to "the central meteorological office at La Sapienza University in Rome", the university whose physicists Alsos and Berg questioned.

## Already known?

Michael Salla has argued publicly that Fermi knew about visiting extraterrestrials, and a few blog posts tie him to RS-33, so that half is partly known. No source found links Alsos, Pash or Berg to the Magenta craft.

## What would settle it

Alsos's Italian reports for June to August 1944 and Berg's OSS files on his Italian interviews (both in the National Archives) would answer it if they mention RS-33, Marconi's papers or anything unexplained. A transcript of the *Oggi* interview would settle what the family actually said about Fermi.
