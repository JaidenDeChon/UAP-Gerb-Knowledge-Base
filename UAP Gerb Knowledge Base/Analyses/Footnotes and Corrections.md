These are small corrections for places where a video probably misspoke: a gloss, a paraphrase or a detail that doesn't match the record or the clip Gerb plays. Each one is also footnoted where it appears, on the video's summary and on the most relevant page.

## The Hottel memo came before El Indio, not after

2026-10-16

In [[Videos/The 1950s Del Rio, Texas UFO Crashes/summary|the Del Rio video]], after the 6 December 1950 alert and the 8 December FBI memo, Gerb says: "does this FBI memo have to do with the Air Force unidentified craft alert or possibly a crash retrieval we do know from the guy hodle memo sent later this year the FBI was in fact discussing crash retrievals and bodies".

The likely correction: "later" was probably meant as "earlier". The Hottel memo is dated 22 March 1950 ([The Black Vault](https://www.theblackvault.com/documentarchive/the-guy-hottel-memo-and-the-crashed-flying-saucers-of-new-mexico-march-22-1950/); [Space.com](https://www.space.com/20409-ufo-hottel-memo-fbi.html)), and Gerb gives that date himself in [[Videos/Dr. Robert Sarbacher & the US Government's Secret UFO Crash Retrieval Group/summary|the Sarbacher video]] ("the hotle memo on March 22nd 1950") and in [[Videos/The 1948 Aztec, New Mexico UFO Crash Retrieval/summary|the Aztec video]] ("On 22 March 1950, Jay Edgar Hoover received an official memorandum"). That puts it nine months before the alleged El Indio crash. Gerb's point that the FBI was hearing crash-retrieval talk in 1950 still stands, but the memo reports three saucers recovered in New Mexico, so it can't be about El Indio.

Footnoted on [[Videos/The 1950s Del Rio, Texas UFO Crashes/summary|The 1950s Del Rio, Texas UFO Crashes]] and [[Hottel Memo]].

## Owens joined SAIC after his SAPOC seat, not before

2026-10-16

In [[Videos/UFO Legacy Programs - Science Applications International Corporation (SAIC)/summary|the SAIC video]], Gerb says: "A permanent SAP member post SAP restructuring is Admiral William A. Owens, former vice chairman of the Joint Chiefs of Staff. Outside of his prestigious naval career and service as senior military assistant to Dick Cheney, Owens additionally served as the CEO for SIC. All in all, the SAPO [...] saw two of six personnel as former SIC alumni."

The likely correction: Owens's SAIC service came after his time on the committee, and he was never its CEO. A [November 1994 DoD bulletin](https://sgp.fas.org/othergov/sapoc.html) lists him as a permanent SAPOC member in his capacity as Vice Chairman of the Joint Chiefs, the post he held from 1994 to 1996. He joined SAIC after retiring from the Navy in 1996 and left in 1998 as president, chief operating officer and vice chairman ([Nextgov, July 1998](https://www.nextgov.com/digital-government/1998/07/intercepts/227594/); [Wipro board biography](https://wipro.com/leadership/william-arthur-owens), which gives June 1996 to August 1998). So Owens wasn't an SAIC alumnus while he sat on SAPOC, and in 1997, the year the Wilson-Davis notes give for Wilson's search, he was at SAIC rather than on the committee. Whether that matters depends on Gerb's wager that SAIC was the contractor in the notes, which nothing here confirms.

Footnoted on [[Videos/UFO Legacy Programs - Science Applications International Corporation (SAIC)/summary|UFO Legacy Programs - Science Applications International Corporation (SAIC)]] and [[William A. Owens]].

## Ruppelt's parallel-programs remark carries the letter's date

2026-10-16

In [[Videos/MOON DUST - The Pentagon's Secret UFO Programs/summary|MOON DUST]], Gerb says: "Rupel concluded UFOs had a quote interplanetary explanation on the 24th of April 1952 ruple suggested programs parallel to the official USG investigation where quote conducting a more complete investigation".

The likely correction: 24 April 1952 is the date of a different document. In [[Videos/USO - Unidentified Submerged Objects/summary|USO]], Gerb gives it to the letter "written by afoi n-x SG regarding the possible connection of Submarine contacts", and a few sentences later cites the same Ruppelt remark ("Rupel stated that afo was conducting a more complete investigation of UFOs parallel to Blue Book") without a date. The date in MOON DUST has probably carried over from the letter. The source is the USO transcript. Web searches found no dated source for Ruppelt's remark, so the remark's real date is still open.

Footnoted on [[Videos/MOON DUST - The Pentagon's Secret UFO Programs/summary|MOON DUST - The Pentagon's Secret UFO Programs]] and [[Edward J. Ruppelt]].

## Grusch's shred party: one agency in the mid '90s

2026-10-16

In [[Videos/80 Years of UFO Crash Retrieval and Reverse Engineering - A Timeline/summary|the 80-year timeline]], Gerb says Grusch spoke about it "in the Judicial Watch podcast, stating the CIA and other agencies had a tremendous burnback and shred party in 1994 to erase as many physical records as possible."

The likely correction: the Judicial Watch clip Gerb plays in [[Videos/Special Access Required - the Secrecy of UFO Crash Retrieval Programs Vol.2/summary|Special Access Required Vol.2]] has Grusch saying "there was a burn bag and shred party um at a particular agency in the mid '90s I'm aware of", with no agency named and no year. ## Condron Field is at White Sands, not Roswell

2026-10-16

In [[Videos/The 1948 Aztec, New Mexico UFO Crash Retrieval/summary|the Aztec video]], Gerb reads the 4 July 1947 IPU field order: "The one-page memo directs a commanding officer to take a counter inelligence team to a UFO crash site and report to the G2 at Condrinfield, New Mexico, which is of course Roswell." ("Condrinfield" is the captions' spelling of Condron Field.)

The likely correction: Condron Field was the airfield of the White Sands range, about 130 miles from Roswell, so the gloss is probably a slip. Sources: the WSMR Museum's [history of White Sands Proving Ground](https://wsmrmuseum.com/2020/09/21/a-brief-history-of-white-sands-proving-ground-1941-1965/5/) and GlobalSecurity's [Condron Army Airfield](https://www.globalsecurity.org/military/facility/condron.htm). One airfield history, [Paul Freeman's](https://www.airfields-freeman.com/NM/Airfields_NM_Alamagordo.htm), doesn't find it on charts before 1948.

Footnoted on [[Videos/The 1948 Aztec, New Mexico UFO Crash Retrieval/summary|The 1948 Aztec, New Mexico UFO Crash Retrieval]] and [[Interplanetary Phenomenon Unit]].
