If you watched Gerb's January 2025 video on [[Philip J. Corso]], you heard him set aside the idea that military radar knocked the 1947 craft out of the sky: "I am still very skeptical on this point". Twenty months later, in his 80-year timeline, he says it is "probably likely" that radar or electromagnetic interference brought down the [[Roswell Crash|Roswell]] craft. He never says his view changed. The videos in between do show how it changed, one step at a time. His understanding may simply have changed as new information reached him: first the [[Scott and Suzanne Ramsey|Ramseys']] book on the Aztec case, then [[Andrew Kissner]]'s 1994 statement.

## The finding

Over five videos from January 2025 to September 2026, Gerb's position on the radar theory moves from very skeptical, through "perhaps there is credence", to "probably likely". The transcripts show this directly. What they also show, and this is the analysis's own reading, is what the shift moved with: the [[Scott and Suzanne Ramsey|Ramseys']] Aztec witness story about radar-killed ducks, which Gerb takes up in July 2025 and cites again in 2026, and [[Andrew Kissner]]'s 1994 statement, whose radar point Gerb first discusses in November 2025 and by May 2026 says he firmly agrees with. Changing a view as new sources arrive is ordinary. The point is only that a viewer of the Corso video was never told it had changed.

He then gives the Ramseys' account of Lee Crane, who said airmen from the El Vado radar site brought "freshly killed half-cooked ducks" into a diner, knocked down by the radar, and ties it to the [[Strategic Defense Initiative (SDI)|SDI]] weapons he has covered before.

He goes straight on to Kissner, "who gosh do I believe and gosh, do I know well at this point".

## How it fits together

The order matters. The softening starts in the Aztec video, four months before Kissner's radar claim appears on the channel, and it rests on the Ramseys' duck story plus Gerb's existing view of SDI as an anti-UFO weapons effort. Kissner then supplies a named source for the radar network itself. By the timeline video, Gerb gives both as his reasons in the same breath. So the scout's reading that the shift simply tracks Kissner is too narrow. The transcripts point to two sources, the Ramseys first.

## What cuts against it

- The middle statements are hedged ("perhaps", "possibly", "bizarre"), and the May 2026 line is about Aztec, not Roswell. Only the January 2025 and September 2026 lines are clear opposites.
- The 80 Years line comes from a loose, spoken section at the end of a long video, not a scripted narration.
- Gerb's 2025 skepticism was voiced right after Corso's mine story, which Gerb treats with doubt throughout that video. Some of that doubt may have been about Corso rather than the radar idea itself.
- The link between the shift and its sources is inferred from where Gerb places them. He never says "this changed my mind".

## Already known?

The radar theory itself is old and widely repeated in Roswell and Aztec literature. Searches found nothing noting the change in Gerb's own coverage, so the point here is about the channel.

## What would settle it

A statement from Gerb on when and why his view changed. On the theory itself, the Ramseys' book *The Aztec UFO Incident* for the Crane account, and the text of Kissner's 1994 statement for its radar passage.
