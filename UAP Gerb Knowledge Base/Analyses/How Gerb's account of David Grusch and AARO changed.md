If you watched Gerb's January 2024 video on [[Sean Kirkpatrick]], you came away with one version of why [[David Grusch]] never testified to [[AARO]]: he tried, and the office never answered. Watch the 2026 videos and the story is different. Grusch kept his distance because he didn't trust AARO, and Gerb says that was the right call. Gerb never puts the two versions side by side or says why one replaced the other.

## The finding

Gerb's own description of the Grusch and AARO dispute changed between January 2024 and 2026, from "AARO ignored him" to "he chose not to engage, with good reason". That much is on the record in the transcripts. The likely reason, which is this analysis's inference, is the FOIA release of AARO's records on its attempts to reach Grusch, published by [[The Black Vault]] in April 2024. Gerb mentions that release in *The Hidden Wing* as the context for Grusch "not talking to Arrow". Changing a view when new documents appear is normal, but viewers of the 2024 video were never told that this one had changed.

## The evidence

1. [[Videos/The Modern Day UFO Disinformation Agent - Dr. Sean Kirkpatrick's Lies/summary|The Kirkpatrick video]] (January 2024), answering Kirkpatrick's claim that no whistleblower in the public eye came to AARO: "gresa stated multiple times that he tried to approach arrow and Kirk Patrick but never heard back at them one of these people has testified to Congress one hasn't that is grush we choose to believe him".
2. [[Videos/The Hidden Wing - US Air Force UFO Reverse Engineering Programs/summary|The Hidden Wing]] (January 2026), on the [[Chris Mellon]] Signal messages that, Gerb says, Kirkpatrick handed over for a FOIA release to [[John Greenewald]]: "I think that was about summer 2024. And this was in relation to David Grush clearly not trusting Arrow and not talking to Arrow and Sean Kirkpatrick throwing a fissy hissy fit about that."
3. [[Videos/Special Access Required - the Secrecy of UFO Crash Retrieval Programs Vol.2/summary|Special Access Required Vol.2]] (June 2026), right after calling AARO "a honeypot for whistleblowers": "Kirkpatre even establishing a pitiful story to paint Grush as a villain by stating he ghosted Arrow for meetings. Gosh, I wonder why."
4. The same video plays Grusch saying: "I certainly uh met with Arrow twice as a staff member about a year ago." In the same clip he mentions an appearance on Megyn Kelly's show "back in uh January", which suggests the clip dates from 2026. If so, the meetings fall in 2025, during his time as special adviser to Representative [[Eric Burlison]], long after Kirkpatrick left AARO in December 2023.

## How it fits together

In 2024 Gerb uses the "never heard back" line to call Kirkpatrick's statement false. In 2026 he no longer disputes that Grusch stayed away. He describes Grusch as not trusting AARO and treats the "ghosted" story as something Grusch had reason to do. Between the two videos sits the April 2024 FOIA release. Reports on it say AARO contacted Grusch's associates from June 2023, booked an interview for 14 November 2023 that Grusch didn't attend, and repeated its invitation after he raised concerns about whether AARO could receive classified material. Gerb's *Hidden Wing* passage ties his 2026 description to those same records, though he dates them to "about summer 2024".

## What cuts against it

- Both versions can be partly true. Grusch has said he spoke to Kirkpatrick in a classified setting in April 2022, before Kirkpatrick took over AARO, and heard nothing more. "Tried and never heard back" may describe that, and "kept away" may describe 2023.
- In the 2024 line Gerb was reporting Grusch, relaying his position as it stood then. According to what the web check found, Grusch's November 2023 statement was that AARO had sent him "zero emails or calls", which isn't quite the same as saying he approached AARO himself.
- Gerb never says why his account changed. The link to the FOIA release is inferred from where he places his own remark; he doesn't state it.

## Already known?

The dispute itself is well covered. NewsNation reported Grusch calling Kirkpatrick's account "a lie" in November 2023. The Black Vault's April 2024 release and Jason Colavito's write-up of it set out AARO's side. No source turned up that notes the change in Gerb's coverage, so the only new point here is about the channel itself.

## What would settle it

A statement from Gerb on when and why his view changed. On the underlying dispute: the full January 2024 AARO memo in the Black Vault release, set against Grusch's own correspondence about the missed 14 November 2023 interview.
