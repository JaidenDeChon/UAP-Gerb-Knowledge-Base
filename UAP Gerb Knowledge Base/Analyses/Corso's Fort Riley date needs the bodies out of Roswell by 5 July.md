[[Philip J. Corso]] swore he saw a body in a crate at [[Fort Riley, Kansas]] around 6 July 1947, trucked from New Mexico. Gerb gives Roswell dates in four other videos but never sets them beside Corso's, and they don't all leave room for him.

## The finding

For Corso's crate to be in Kansas around 6 July, the bodies had to leave the [[Roswell Crash]] area by road about a day earlier. That matches the chronology Gerb gives in UFO Whistleblowers Vol.1 (crash likely 4 July, bodies out of [[Hangar 84]] on 5 July). It doesn't fit the 7 July date Gerb ties to the crash in the Majestic-12 and Kecksburg videos. This is an open question, not a proven contradiction.

## What cuts against it

- Corso says "on or about". A few days' slack dissolves most of the tension.
- The 7 July date is borrowed: the [[Eisenhower Briefing Document]]'s start of recovery in one video, an aside about [[James E. Webb]]'s phone calls in the other.
- Roswell's dates are disputed, and Gerb separates the debris field from the bodies site. The two dates may describe different sites.
- The Hangar 84 account doesn't say the bodies went by road.

## The evidence

1. Corso's affidavit, read in [[Videos/Philip J. Corso - US Army UFO Technology Research & Development/summary|the Corso video]]: "that on or about July 6th 1947 while stationed at Fort Riley Kansas I personally observed a 4-foot non-human creature". The trucks came "from an Airfield in in New Mexico". Gerb adds: "Corso's official records do indeed Place him at Fort Riley at this date".
2. [[Videos/UFO Whistleblowers Vol.1/summary|UFO Whistleblowers Vol.1]]: "on July 4th a UFO disappears off radar scope this is likely the crash date the following day July 5th ... debris and bodies were shipped out of Roswell Hangar 84 to either right Pat or Fort wor North Texas".
3. [[Videos/The Alien and UFO Obscure Oddities Iceberg (Level 2)/summary|Iceberg Level 2]] repeats the 5 July shipment to [[Wright-Patterson Air Force Base]].
4. [[Videos/The Majestic-12 Documents With Ryan S. Wood/summary|The Majestic-12 video]]: "July 7 1947 oversaw the beginning of the mj12 with the Roswell crash".
5. [[Videos/The 1965 Kecksburg, Pennsylvania UFO Crash/summary|The Kecksburg video]]: "I take you to July 9th 1947 just 2 days after the Roswell crash".

## How it fits together

Roswell is about 580 miles from Fort Riley in a straight line, so a convoy leaving on 5 July could plausibly reach Kansas by the 6th. If the recovery began on 7 July, the crate was at Fort Riley before the bodies were picked up.

## Already known?

No source found sets Corso's 6 July date against the Roswell chronologies. Wikipedia notes critics found chronological problems in *[[The Day After Roswell]]* generally. A Black Vault case file on Corso's Form 66 record lists a Fort Riley assignment from 21 April 1947 (one search snippet), which fits Gerb's statement but doesn't confirm July.

## What would settle it

The full Form 66 or Fort Riley duty rosters for 5 to 7 July 1947, and the date in Corso's unedited *Dawn of a New Age* manuscript.
