If you watched Gerb's videos from the first half of 2025, you heard that [[Glenn Gaffney]], head of the CIA's [[CIA Directorate of Science and Technology|Directorate of Science and Technology]] (DS&T), stopped [[Lockheed Martin]] from handing material to [[AAWSAP]] in 2008. In his September 2025 Lockheed video Gerb tells it differently: there were two attempts, a first one in 2008 or 2009 that he links to [[James Clapper]], and a 2011 attempt through [[Kona Blue]] that Gaffney blocked. Gerb never sets the two versions side by side, but Gaffney's career settles which one fits: in 2008 he worked at the ODNI, not the DS&T.

## The finding

Gerb's early version, "DS&T head Gaffney blocked the 2008 transfer", can't be right as stated. In 2008 Gaffney was Deputy Director of National Intelligence for Collection at the [[Office of the Director of National Intelligence (ODNI)|ODNI]], and [[Stephanie O'Sullivan]] led the DS&T until she became the CIA's Associate Deputy Director in December 2009. Gaffney's six years at the head of the directorate began only after that. So either the year in those videos is wrong or the post is. Gerb's later account, with Gaffney at the DS&T for the 2011 Kona Blue block, is the one that matches his record. Gerb's understanding may simply have changed as new information reached him. In the September 2025 video he builds the two-attempt account on [[Christopher Sharp]]'s reporting and AARO's historical report, and reads the document [[Tim Burchett]] entered into the record, which names Gaffney as the DS&T's "sitting deputy director" when the transfer was killed.

The finding goes no further. It doesn't say whether Gaffney blocked anything, or who, if anyone, stopped a 2008 or 2009 attempt.

## The evidence

1. [[Videos/Philip J. Corso - US Army UFO Technology Research & Development/summary|Philip J. Corso]] (January 2025), on the DS&T: "the director of this position Glenn Gaffney was the individual who stopped locked Martin from divesting UAP material to assap in 2008".
2. [[Videos/UFO Legacy Programs - Northrop Grumman/summary|Northrop Grumman part one]] (January 2025): "the 2008 material transfer to assap that was blocked by gen Glenn Gaffney in the CIA uh directorate of Science and Technology".
3. [[Videos/US Navy UFO Crash Retrieval & Reverse Engineering Programs/summary|US Navy programs]] (April 2025): "Glenn Gaffne, former CIA DS&T director, has been labeled as the gatekeeper who ceased Loheed Martin's attempts to divest UFO materials, likely the 1953 Kingman, Arizona crash, to the Osat program in 2008."
4. [[Videos/Lockheed Martin - UFO Reverse Engineering, Material Exploitation, & Legacy Programs/summary|Lockheed Martin]] (September 2025): "Um maybe a DIA program back in 2008/209. I'm running this off the top of my head, but I believe that uh James Clapper was involved in shutting this down if I'm trying to remember correctly. But then of course in the 2011 transfer this was shut down by the CIA DS&T uh Glenn Gaffne and possibly Robert Cardillo." Earlier in the same video Gerb reads the document Representative Tim Burchett entered into the record, which says the technology transfer agreement into the waived program Senator Reid was championing "was killed by Glenn Gaffne, sitting deputy director of the CIA's Directorate of Science and Technology."
5. [[Videos/80 Years of UFO Crash Retrieval and Reverse Engineering - A Timeline/summary|80 Years]] puts Kona Blue in 2011 ("In the same year of 2011, we have the Kona Blue perspective special access program") and says the transfer "was ultimately thwarted by CIA's director of science and technology deputy director Glenn Gaffne".
6. [[Videos/Northrop Grumman & TRW - UFO Reverse Engineering, Material Exploitation, & Legacy Programs Vol.2/summary|Northrop Grumman & TRW Vol.2]] (March 2026) still blurs the two: "Gaffney's claim to fame is shutting down the late 2000s Kona Blue PSAP".
7. No transcript mentions Gaffney's ODNI post.

## How it fits together

- 2005 to 2008: Mary Margaret Graham is the first Deputy DNI for Collection (Wikipedia).
- 2008: Gaffney holds that post, according to a 2016 AFIO notice. His In-Q-Tel and AFCEA biographies list it without dates.
- Until December 2009: O'Sullivan heads the DS&T, then becomes the CIA's Associate Deputy Director (Wikipedia and her NSA biography agree).
- From 2009: Gaffney leads the DS&T for six years, the length he gives in a CBS *Intelligence Matters* interview.
- 2011: the Kona Blue attempt, which the Burchett document and Gerb's later videos credit Gaffney with killing.

A 2008 block by "DS&T head Gaffney" falls more than a year before he took the job. Even a 2009 attempt, the other year Gerb gives, would almost certainly fall in O'Sullivan's time. Only the 2011 attempt lands in Gaffney's tenure, which is how Gerb tells it from September 2025 on. The early videos look like the two attempts run together, with the first one's date and the second one's blocker.


## What cuts against it

- Gaffney's 2008 ODNI post rests on one search snippet (the AFIO notice) plus his undated biographies. O'Sullivan's dates are firmer, and they alone keep Gaffney out of the DS&T's top job in 2008.
- The early videos may only mean that Gaffney, a CIA official who later ran the DS&T, had a hand in 2008. A Deputy DNI for Collection could in principle have objected to something. But Northrop Grumman part one places him "in the CIA uh directorate of Science and Technology" that year, and none of Gerb's sources, as he cites them, put Gaffney at the ODNI.
- The date of the first attempt is itself uncertain. Gerb gives it "off the top of my head" from Christopher Sharp's reporting and AARO's history, and AARO's Kona Blue report, as search results summarize it, records no Lockheed offer or CIA veto at all.
- All of this concerns allegations. Gaffney has denied blocking a transfer, and the document naming him has no known author.

## Already known?

Nothing turned up that points out the clash between Gaffney's career and the 2008 date. Sharp's own reporting, as quoted by others, names Gaffney as the former DS&T head who blocked the transfer without fixing a year. In a 2025 post Sharp says Gaffney has denied blocking a transfer "from Lockheed to DHS", which is the 2011 Kona Blue route.

## What would settle it

An ODNI or CIA announcement of Gaffney's move to the DS&T would fix his start date. The other half needs a dated record of the first Lockheed offer, such as the AARO historical report's chronology or the Liberation Times reporting Gerb draws on. If the first attempt turned out to be in 2010 rather than 2008 or 2009, the early videos would only be off on the year.
