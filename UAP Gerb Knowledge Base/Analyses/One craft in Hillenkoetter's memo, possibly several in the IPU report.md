Two of the papers Gerb reads from the [[Majestic 12]] collection describe the same July 1947 recovery in New Mexico. A memo attributed to [[Roscoe H. Hillenkoetter]] and dated 19 September 1947 says "we recovered one craft". The [[Interplanetary Phenomenon Unit]] report, dated 22 July 1947, refers in Gerb's words to "multiple fallen objects", and in his Aztec video he says this "possibly lends credence to Roswell featuring multiple UFO crash retrievals". If he's right about the IPU report, the two papers disagree on how many craft came down. Gerb never puts them side by side. This post asks whether they actually disagree.

## The finding

The claim is narrow. If the IPU report's "multiple fallen objects" means more than one craft, as Gerb suggests in his 2025 videos, it doesn't match the Hillenkoetter memo's count of one craft. Both papers are unauthenticated, so the clash bears only on whether they tell one consistent story and says nothing about what happened in 1947. Gerb's own earlier description of the IPU report, a single disc found at two sites, would fit the memo.

## What cuts against it

- **Gerb's 2024 reading fits the memo.** In the Majestic-12 video he says the IPU report covers "the two sites of the crashed disc", which is one disc. The debris field and a second site belonging to the same craft is the usual Roswell story, and "multiple fallen objects" could just mean pieces of it.
- **The deciding words are Gerb's paraphrase.** "Multiple fallen objects" is Gerb summarising the document. He doesn't read it out. The IPU report's text couldn't be checked here: the Majestic Documents site didn't resolve, and searches turned up no copy of it.
- **The memo's line is garbled in the captions.** "we recovered one craft captured one endot" may be cut short. "Captured one" could refer to a being, not a second craft, and "one craft" could mean one intact craft found among other wreckage.
- **Neither paper has an archival record.** A CIA FOIA search found no Majestic 12 records. Gerb himself says the Majestic documents "should not be taken as a concrete source of confirmation or evidence". If the papers came from different forgers, or arrived at different times, an inconsistency is no surprise.
- **Other counts are separate sources.** Two other counts came up while this post was researched: [[Andrew Kissner]]'s "two to four" discs and [[Philip J. Corso]]'s Roswell plus San Augustine crashes. They come from different sources and don't show what either paper says.

## The evidence

1. [[Videos/The Majestic-12 Documents With Ryan S. Wood/summary|Majestic-12 Documents]] (August 2024), on the Hillenkoetter memo to the Joint Intelligence Committee: "this Majestic document memo allegedly dated just before Bush and forest's request to create mj12 is titled quote memorandum for the military assessment of the joint intelligence committee end quot AKA magic the memo requests a classified intelligence project we recovered one craft captured one endot".
2. The same video, a few minutes earlier, on the IPU report: "this document explores in great detail the events of the Roswell UFO crash including initial tracking and assessment of the two sites of the crashed disc".
3. [[Videos/The 1948 Aztec, New Mexico UFO Crash Retrieval/summary|Aztec]] (July 2025): "The intelligence assessment references recovery of multiple fallen objects from 4 to 6th July 1947. This possibly lends credence to Roswell featuring multiple UFO crash retrievals."
4. [[Videos/Sandia National Laboratories - UFO Reverse Engineering, Material Exploitation, & Legacy Programs/summary|Sandia]] (November 2025): "This intelligence assessment references recovery of multiple fallen objects from 4 to 6 July 1947 in the state of New Mexico, aka the Roswell crash, and possibly other retrieval operations." Later Gerb says the Sandia team and the AFSWP "secured the immediate area surrounding the crash sites".
5. No transcript mentions the memo and the IPU report's count together. A search of every transcript for "one craft" turns up only the Majestic-12 video.

## How it fits together

Taken together, Gerb's three descriptions give two readings of the IPU report. The 2024 reading, one disc at two sites, matches the memo's "one craft". The 2025 reading, multiple fallen objects and possibly several retrievals, doesn't. Gerb may simply have read the report more closely, or come back to it while working on Aztec, where more than one crash in 1947 to 1948 is the theme. The memo appears only in the 2024 video, so his later reading has never been set against it.

## Already known?

Nothing turned up. Search results describe the Hillenkoetter memo only in outline (a request for a presidential directive and for scientific study of "objects") and call it disputed. One hobbyist newsletter snippet says the IPU document describes bodies at a site near Corona and animal parts at a second site. That fits two sites, but it doesn't settle how many craft there were. No source compared the counts in the two papers.

## What would settle it

The IPU report's own wording on what was recovered between 4 and 6 July would settle one side, by showing whether it numbers or describes separate craft or debris from one craft at two landing zones. The memo's full sentence after "captured one" would settle the other. Both papers are on Ryan S. Wood's Majestic Documents site. A statement from Gerb on which reading he now holds would also answer it.
