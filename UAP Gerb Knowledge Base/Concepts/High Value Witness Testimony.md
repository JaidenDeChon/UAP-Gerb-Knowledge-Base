"High Value Witness Testimony" is a classification designation allegedly used in internal U.S. government UAP documentation to identify incident reports, footage, or testimony from credible military or intelligence personnel that possess significant evidentiary weight for establishing the reality and characteristics of UAP phenomena. The term implies a hierarchical assessment system in which some UAP encounters are deemed more credible, better documented, or strategically more important than others.

## Alleged Use in UAP Task Force Documentation

An alleged leaked classified document that surfaced online in 2021-2022, purportedly a section from an appendix of a [[UAP Task Force]] report prepared for Congress, contains a section titled "Appendix F, Section 4B: DOD 1992 through 2017 High Value Witness Interviews." This section allegedly includes the [[Flyby Footage]] as documented high-value testimony, suggesting the U.S. government considers it authentic military documentation of a close-proximity UAP encounter rather than a hoax or misidentification.

The document also references behavioral data analysis conducted by joint elements of [[ONI (Office of Naval Intelligence)|ONI]], [[NSA]], [[DHS (Department of Homeland Security)|DHS]], and DOD SAP-cleared experts examining 1,292 UFO cases, with specific researchers ([[K Shakoya]], [[M. Harmon]], [[S. McCaron]], [[Hillary S. McCaron]]) cited as contributors to this analysis. If authentic, this would indicate a systematic process for evaluating and categorizing UAP evidence based on witness credibility and documentation quality.

## Authenticity Questions

The authenticity of the document containing this terminology remains unverified, and exhaustive searches for the named researchers and referenced reports have yielded minimal results.
