The Hudson Valley UFO Flap was a concentrated series of UFO sightings reported across the Hudson Valley region of New York in the mid-1980s and early 1990s. Witnesses described large, silent craft, triangular or boomerang-shaped, with distinctive arrays of lights, moving slowly over populated areas. The flap is relevant to the [[TR-3B]] investigation because its timing matches the period of alleged prototype testing described by [[Edgar Fouche|Edgar Fouché]]. Fouché claimed that early anti-gravity warping craft were flying as early as the 1970s and that the first operational TR-3B models had been achieved by the early 1990s. Other triangular craft flaps, including the [[Belgian UFO Wave]] of 1989–1990, show the same concentration of sightings over populated areas and military facilities.

The flap's best-known episode at a sensitive site is the [[1984 Indian Point UFO Incident]]. On 24 July 1984, security personnel at the Indian Point nuclear power plant in Buchanan, New York, reported a huge lighted object over the plant. According to Gerb, the plant's security systems failed as the object passed, and [[Nuclear Regulatory Commission]] agents confiscated the records.
