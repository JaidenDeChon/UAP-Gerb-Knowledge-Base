**Yankee Blue** is the name of an alleged fake alien-technology program used, according to a June 2025 *Wall Street Journal* report, as a hazing ritual in classified US Air Force units. The article, "The Pentagon Disinformation That Fueled America's UFO Mythology," reported that the Pentagon's [[AARO]] had found that, for decades, some newly assigned personnel were shown images of flying saucers and told they were being read into a top-secret program to recover alien technology. The report presented this as one source of the belief in secret crash-retrieval and reverse-engineering programs.

## UAP Gerb's criticism

UAP Gerb treats the Yankee Blue story as narrative control. In *Special Access Required Vol.2*, he accuses former AARO director [[Sean Kirkpatrick]] of falsely telling the *Journal* that the whole impetus for claims of UFO legacy programs was an illegal hazing program. He says Kirkpatrick told podcasters off air that every branch of the armed forces had its own Yankee Blue equivalent, something never mentioned in the article. He adds that [[Steven Greenstreet]] obtained a copy of the Yankee Blue document, "probably from Kirkpatrick," which he says carries inaccurate security controls. He challenges the Pentagon to release the document and invites viewers to see whose signature is at the bottom.

The video pairs this with [[David Grusch]]'s response in his Judicial Watch interview. Grusch said he was aware of hazing, had taken part in hazing as a lieutenant, and set a high evidentiary threshold because of it. The witnesses he took to the Intelligence Community Inspector General had handled the material and been in the facilities, and Yankee Blue was "certainly not the code words" he provided. He called the hazing narrative "gaslighting" by former career bureaucrats.

## Related

The name echoes **Yankee White**, a real White House access designation: the background investigation required for personnel who directly support the President. UAP Gerb cites Yankee White as an example of a White House special access program that is properly reported to Congress (see [[Content-Only Special Access Program]]).
