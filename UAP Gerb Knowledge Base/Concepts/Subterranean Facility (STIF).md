A Subterranean Facility (STIF) is the term reportedly used for the installations themselves by US military veterans who have visited Deep Underground Military Bases (DUMBs). Gerb notes that multiple former military personnel who have traveled to such locations use "STIF" as the insider operational term. "DUMB", by contrast, is mainly used in the research and UFO community rather than by personnel with actual access to the sites.

## Usage Context

Gerb began using "STIF" interchangeably with "DUMB" in his DUMBs investigation, based on firsthand testimony from multiple veterans who said STIF was the word they habitually used for these facilities. The difference is mainly one of wording, but it matters for analysis. A consistent insider term for these installations implies institutional awareness of them and a standardized naming convention within classified military culture, which lends additional credibility to the claim that the facilities exist.

## Relationship to DUMBs

STIFs and DUMBs refer to the same class of installation: deep underground military facilities used for classified purposes, potentially including UAP legacy program operations. Gerb uses the two terms interchangeably throughout his investigation. See [[Deep Underground Military Bases (DUMBs)]] for the full treatment of these facilities.
