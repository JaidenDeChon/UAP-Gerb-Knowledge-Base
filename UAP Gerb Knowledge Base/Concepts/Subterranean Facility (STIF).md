A Subterranean Facility (STIF) is the term reportedly used by US military veterans who have visited Deep Underground Military Bases (DUMBs) to describe the installations themselves. UAP Gerb notes that multiple former military personnel who have traveled to such locations use "STIF" as the insider operational term, contrasting with "DUMB" — which is primarily a term used in the research and UFO community rather than by personnel with actual access to the sites.

## Usage Context

UAP Gerb began using "STIF" interchangeably with "DUMB" in his DUMBs investigation based on firsthand testimony from multiple veterans who described STIFs as their habitual terminology for these facilities. The distinction is primarily linguistic but has analytical significance: the existence of a consistent insider term for these installations implies an institutional awareness and standardized naming convention within classified military culture, lending additional credibility to the existence of the facilities.

## Relationship to DUMBs

STIFs and DUMBs refer to the same class of installation — deep underground military facilities used for classified purposes, potentially including UAP legacy program operations. The terms are used interchangeably throughout UAP Gerb's investigation. See [[Deep Underground Military Bases (DUMBs)]] for the full treatment of these facilities.
