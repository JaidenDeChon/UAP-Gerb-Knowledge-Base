An ooid cross-section is a lens-shaped, or biconvex, profile that is widest at the center and tapers toward the edges. The anonymous Marine whistleblower "[[RB]]" used the term for the cross-sectional shape of the disc-shaped craft he claimed to have guarded in December 1963. An ooid is a small rounded grain with concentric layers, but in geometry the term describes an oblate ellipsoid. Applied to a craft, it corresponds to what is commonly called a "hamburger-shaped" or classic flying saucer profile: a flattened disc that is taller in the center than at the rim.

RB described the 40-foot craft as totally symmetrical with this ooid cross-section. In his account it was silverish and resembled polished aluminum, with no visible seams except for a hatch seam on the lower portion. The shape is consistent with many classic UFO descriptions in the historical record, and with the "sports model" disc that Bob Lazar described in the context of S-4 at Area 51.
