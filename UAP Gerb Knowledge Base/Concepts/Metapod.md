"Metapod" is the nickname of a small, crescent-shaped craft seen in a short daylight video said to have been filmed near [[El Escorial, Madrid, Spain]] on 6 September 2015 (see [[Metapod UFO Sighting]]). The name has come to stand both for that clip and for the craft's shape, which UFO commentators, Gerb among them, argue appears in several other recordings made between 1998 and 2023. If genuine, Gerb calls the footage one of the best close-up views of a UFO ever recorded.

## Description

In the 2015 footage the Metapod is a pod-like body shaped like a crescent moon. Its back and bulk appear to be a solid, metallic, reflective material; one face is a transparent, glass-like material that Gerb suggests may be a window for viewing out of. As it rotates about its long axis the craft holds a vertical position, with the transparent face on one side and the reflective body on the other.

The witnesses reported that it made no sound, descended vertically and moved away. One of them said he saw something alive, with limbs, moving inside it. Some viewers, including the site [[Third Phase of the Sun]], say they can make out a bean-shaped figure inside; Gerb attributes this to sunlight reflecting off the transparent face.

## Flight characteristics

Gerb assesses the footage as showing three of the [[Five UAP Characteristics (AATIP)|five UAP observables]] associated with [[AATIP (Advanced Aerospace Threat Identification Program)|AATIP]] and [[Luis Elizondo]]: instantaneous acceleration (a sudden drop in altitude), anti-gravity or no visible propulsion, and low observability. He also lists what he calls intelligent flight characteristics: holding still in wind, a straight-line traverse away from the camera, and a variable but stable rotation that reverses from counterclockwise to clockwise.

## Other recordings of the shape

Gerb argues that the same craft appears in:

- **Peru, 1998**: footage he renders as the "parachutes", showing five or more pods with the same body and reflective paneling.
- **Mexico, 2006**: the [[Bruja de Monterrey]], which he reads as the pod's non-transparent back moving away in the same vertical position.
- **Vancouver, 2021**: three pods with the same crescent shape and sunlit reflectivity, which reportedly flew north together before splitting up.
- **2022**: frames allegedly leaked from a [[US Navy Blue Angels]] cockpit recording, showing a pod dropping from the clouds with four other signatures nearby.
- **Texas, 2023**: a hang glider apparently observing the pod, though Gerb considers this the clip likeliest to be prosaic.

Some compare the Metapod's outline to the Navy's Gimbal video; Gerb keeps that comparison in mind but does not claim it, since a FLIR image need not show an object's true shape.

## Proposed explanations

Debunkers have described the Metapod as a balloon (a custom heart-shaped balloon or an inflatable tent), as an object hanging on a string, and as CGI, citing three frames of jitter. On [[Metabunk]], balloon proponents have themselves argued that the jitter fits a physical object dropping in air currents. Gerb finds none of the explanations persuasive and hypothesizes that the Metapod is a single-occupant observation craft.
