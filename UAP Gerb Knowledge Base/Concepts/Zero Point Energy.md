Zero Point Energy (ZPE) is the lowest possible energy state of a quantum mechanical system. It is the energy a system still has at absolute zero temperature, and no amount of cooling can remove it. In quantum field theory, the vacuum of space is filled with fluctuating quantum fields, and ZPE is the ground-state energy of those fields throughout spacetime. Physicist James Clerk Maxwell calculated that the energy density of this vacuum fluctuation is so large that the energy in a single cubic yard of empty space, if it could all be extracted, would be enough to boil all of Earth's oceans. More recent theoretical estimates cited by researcher [[Mark McCandlish]] put the ZPE density at approximately 10^109 joules per cubic centimeter.

## Role in ARV Propulsion Claims

[[Brad Sorenson]], in his December 3, 1990 interview with [[William B. Scott]] of [[Aviation Week and Space Technology]], stated that the [[Alien Reproduction Vehicle (ARV)|ARV Flux Liner craft]] drew its energy from "quantum fluctuations", meaning Zero Point Energy, and not from the [[Biefeld-Brown Effect]] (the conversion of electrostatic energy into propulsion). The briefers at the 1988 [[Air Force Plant 42]] exhibit told attendees that the craft had found "a new fuel source that was everywhere in the universe."

McCandlish developed a theory of how the ARV's propulsion system uses ZPE. He argued that the ARV has three overlapping electromagnetic fields, generated by Tesla coil primary windings, center windings and a low-temperature plasma ring. Together they create conditions similar to the electromagnetic pinching effect of an explosively pumped EMP weapon, which taps into the Zero Point Energy field around it. McCandlish argued that drawing on ZPE as a propulsive force gets around the increase in inertial mass that Einstein described near the speed of light. The energy that would otherwise add to the craft's mass is used for propulsion instead, which could allow faster-than-light travel.

McCandlish also linked ZPE theory to the origin of gravity, inertia and mass. He drew on physicist Morus B. King's "Tapping the Zero Point Energy" and on work at Los Alamos National Laboratory, which carried out extensive research into explosive magnetic flux compression generators from 1975 onward.

## Suppression

McCandlish and others say the alleged suppression of Zero Point Energy technology is mainly about its potential as a weapon. A ZPE weapon would make a hydrogen bomb "look like a firecracker," because the energy density of the quantum vacuum is close to infinite. In this view, the technology is kept classified for global security as well as for its strategic advantage in aerospace.
