The "Blue Room" is the informal name for a classified facility at [[Wright-Patterson Air Force Base]] that allegedly holds recovered UFO materials. These may include non-human craft, biological remains and the documents that go with them. The name turns up in several UAP research sources from the 1950s through the 1990s, and it is one of the most persistently referenced alleged UAP storage sites in the historical record.

## Senator Goldwater's Denied Access

Senator [[Barry Goldwater]] (R-AZ), a Major General in the USAF Reserve, tried several times over roughly a decade to get into the Blue Room. In a 1975 public interview, Goldwater said he believed classified UFO materials were stored at Wright-Patterson. In a letter to UFO researcher [[Leonard Stringfield]] dated December 3, 1974, he admitted he had been denied access. A March 28, 1975 letter to an unknown researcher dates his attempt to around 1963 and describes the classification as "Above Top Secret." In a 1981 letter to researcher [[Lee Graham]], Goldwater said he knew of no one with access to the Blue Room and did not know of any materials being moved elsewhere.

When Goldwater asked General [[Curtis LeMay]] in person, LeMay became angry and ordered him never to raise the subject again. That was a striking reaction to a man of Goldwater's senior rank and security clearances. It is consistent with the materials being protected under compartmentalized programs beyond standard Congressional oversight.

## AFOSI Documentation

An April 27, 1953 AFOSI document records Captain [[Plandowski]] interviewing Master Sergeant [[Ralph Brown]], who conveyed that Wright-Patterson held three flying saucers (one damaged, two functional) and the bodies of their pilots. The document is older than Goldwater's attempts to get in. It shows that by the early 1950s, Air Force investigators already knew of Wright-Patterson as a place where recovered craft were stored.

## FOIA Confirmation of Film Destruction

In 1991, UFO researcher [[Brian Parks]] submitted a FOIA request about the Blue Room. In reply, USAF Colonel [[Eddie L. Anderson]] confirmed that materials related to the Blue Room, including film, were destroyed on September 9, 1965. That date falls within LeMay's term as Air Force Chief of Staff (1961–1965) and around the same time as Goldwater's requests for access.

## Connection to Whistleblower RB

Anonymous Marine whistleblower "[[RB]]" claimed that in December 1963 he was flown on a windowless plane from Cherry Point, North Carolina to an undisclosed location within a three-hour flight, a range consistent with Wright-Patterson. He said he guarded a 40-foot disc-shaped craft there for two weeks. Gerb speculates that the facility RB described may be the Blue Room or a building connected to it.
