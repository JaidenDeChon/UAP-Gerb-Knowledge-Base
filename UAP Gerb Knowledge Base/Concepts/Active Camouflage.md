In UAP research, active camouflage is a hypothesized electro-optic or plasma-based cloaking technology observed on alleged reverse-engineered non-human airframes. Passive stealth reduces radar or infrared signatures, while active camouflage is theorized to manipulate light itself, so that an object blends into its surroundings or vanishes from view. The UAP Gerb "Hidden Wing" investigation treats active camouflage as a derivative technology that likely originated with non-human intelligence and may be under test and evaluation in classified U.S. Air Force programs.

## The Borland Sighting

The main witness account of active camouflage in a military context is [[Dylan Borland]]'s description of a large triangular craft observed over [[Langley Air Force Base]] around 2012. Borland described the craft as appearing to "manifest around a light source". It gave the impression of not being fully solid until it became visible, as though its cloaking system was disengaging as the craft de-cloaked. Its exterior showed a gold, lava-like plasma fluid flowing across a black metallic surface, which observers theorize may be part of the active camouflage mechanism rather than a separate feature.

Gerb speculates that the plasma surface could act as an electroactive or photonic medium. It would bend or absorb incoming light around the craft's body to make it optically transparent, then let the plasma state collapse as the craft re-enters visible operation. The exact mechanism is not established, and this explanation is an inference from the witness description.

## Relationship to Non-Human Intelligence

Gerb's central thesis is that active camouflage of the kind Borland described was derived from the study of non-human craft, as an [[Alien Reproduction Vehicle (ARV)]] derivative, instead of being invented independently by a human defense contractor. If that is accurate, active camouflage would belong to the broader family of alleged reverse-engineered technologies managed by programs in the [[Hidden Wing]] portfolio at facilities such as the [[412th Test Wing|Edwards 412th Test Wing]].

## Corroborating Descriptions

[[Michael Herrera]]'s account of the craft observed during the [[2009 Indonesia UFO Encounter (Herrera)]] describes a rotating octagonal object with a color-shifting exterior. Its shape and behavior differ from those of the triangular craft at Langley, but its color shifting is sometimes cited as a related optical effect that could indicate a similar underlying technology. The two accounts are not treated as describing the same technology. They are noted as part of a broader pattern of optical anomalies reported by witnesses in UAP encounters.
