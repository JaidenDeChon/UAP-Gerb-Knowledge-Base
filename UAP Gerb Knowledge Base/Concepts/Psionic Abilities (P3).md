P3 (phonetically "P-three") is the alleged designation for people with psionic or psychic abilities who are recruited by the Black Group. According to what an anonymous insider told whistleblower [[Michael Herrera]], the group needs these people to interface with and control non-human intelligence technology that cannot be operated by conventional mechanical means.

## Recruitment Protocol

### Source Populations

The P3 recruitment program allegedly targets people from third-world nations and disaster zones. According to the insider, the Indonesia operation Herrera witnessed in 2009, in which armed operators loaded shipping containers onto an octagonal craft, was a P3 recruitment and transport mission carried out after the Sumatra earthquake and tsunami.

### Recruitment Method

Recruits are brought in under false pretenses, often during humanitarian crises, and are offered security and safety for their families in exchange for taking part. That family arrangement suggests the separation is permanent or semi-permanent, and that recruits are unlikely to return to their former lives. Herrera saw shipping containers fitted with large black cylinders being used to transport the recruits. The cylinders were at first thought to be for humidity control or oxygen supply.

### Operational Security

Michael Herrera at first believed the containers held narcotics because of their oxygen and humidity control systems. The insider later revealed that the cylinders were indeed oxygen supply equipment, used to keep human beings alive: the P3 recruits, during their transport to classified facilities.

## Interface Function

Recruits are valued for their ability to interface with non-human intelligence (NHI) craft, operating or controlling reverse-engineered vehicles that require consciousness-based interaction. They can also interact with recovered non-human intelligence systems that conventional pilots or operators cannot use. In effect they serve as "biological equipment". Insiders allegedly call them "pink assets", meaning human subjects whose abilities make them equipment rather than personnel.

## Ethical Implications

If the P3 program exists as described, it would amount to international kidnapping, since foreign nationals are abducted from disaster zones, and to human trafficking, since they are transported and exploited under false pretenses. The permanent separation of recruits from their families, their drugging and their use as biological subjects would be crimes against humanity. Covert operations in foreign nations without their governments' knowledge would also violate those nations' sovereignty.

## Connection to Herrera's 2009 Encounter

The insider explicitly stated that the Indonesia operation Herrera stumbled upon was a P3 recruitment and transport mission. On that account, Herrera had seen more than a craft: he had discovered an active human trafficking operation that procured psychic assets.

## Credibility Assessment

The only source for the P3 program is the anonymous insider's claims to Michael Herrera. Investigator [[Joey Is Not My Name]] verified that Herrera met with someone at a secure facility, but the insider's specific claims about P3 recruitment remain unverified. The account has disturbing parallels with historical allegations of government human experimentation programs, though no documentary evidence has emerged to show that the P3 program exists.
