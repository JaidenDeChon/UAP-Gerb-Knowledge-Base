**Course Correction** or **Controlled Flight** is an object's ability to change its trajectory mid-flight in ways that ballistic motion or gravity cannot explain. It is one of the main features that set [[Fast Walkers]] detected by [[Defense Support Program (DSP)]] satellites apart from natural phenomena such as meteors, and from man-made objects such as satellites and space debris, which follow predictable orbital mechanics.

## Bob Fish Fast Walker Description

The clearest documented example comes from [[Bob Fish]]'s 2015 email to [[John Podesta]], which describes a Fast Walker detection seen by DSP personnel in [[El Segundo, California]]:

> "Not only was it going very fast but it **made a 30° course correction turn** which means it did not have a ballistic freefall reentry trajectory that a meteorite might have. So it was under **some sort of control** although whether it was manned or just robotic there's no way to tell."

The 30-degree course change matters because the objects it might otherwise be taken for all move predictably:
1. Meteors follow ballistic paths. Natural objects entering the atmosphere are acted on only by gravity and atmospheric drag, so their trajectories are predictable.
2. Satellites follow orbital mechanics. Man-made spacecraft in orbit move along Keplerian trajectories governed by gravity.
3. Missiles are ballistic after the boost phase. Once powered flight ends, a missile follows a ballistic arc toward its target.

An object that makes a large course correction mid-flight has active propulsion or some form of control authority, which rules out these natural and conventional explanations.

## 1984 Indian Ocean Case

The [[1984 DSP Indian Ocean Detection|May 5, 1984 Fast Walker case]] reported by [[Joe Stefula]] describes an object that:
- Traveled at 22,000 mph toward Earth
- Passed within 1.8 miles of the DSP satellite
- Changed course and flew back into outer space

Reversing direction after approaching Earth, from an inbound to an outbound trajectory, takes extraordinary control authority. The propulsion system would have to overcome Earth's gravitational pull while the object was traveling at extreme velocity.

## Distinction from Ballistic Trajectories

Military analysts and satellite operators know ballistic trajectories well from monitoring missile launches and other space activity. Reports specifically note the Fast Walkers that make course corrections, which suggests these maneuvers are easy to see in the detection data, unusual enough to be given a special designation and tracked, and inconsistent with any known natural or conventional aerospace phenomenon.

## Implications for Origin and Intent

The ability to correct course has several implications:

1. The object is directed by an intelligence. Something that deliberately changes its trajectory is under guidance, which suggests purposeful navigation rather than random motion.

2. It has advanced propulsion. Maneuvering at extreme velocities in the vacuum of space or the upper atmosphere requires propulsion far beyond conventional chemical rockets.

3. It may be on a reconnaissance or surveillance mission. Deliberate maneuvering near Earth could indicate an observational purpose rather than random transit through the solar system.

4. A non-terrestrial origin becomes more likely. No publicly acknowledged terrestrial technology can make 30-degree course corrections at 22,000+ mph in space near Earth.

## As Evidence of Non-Natural Phenomena

Course corrections are the strongest evidence that Fast Walkers are not any of the following:
- Meteors, which follow ballistic paths and cannot maneuver
- Space debris, which follows orbital mechanics and has no propulsion
- Conventional satellites, which maneuver slowly with small thrusters and do not make rapid course changes at extreme speeds
- Ballistic missiles, which follow predictable arcs once the boost phase ends

Controlled flight has been documented in multiple Fast Walker cases over several decades, and it establishes that these objects have capabilities beyond any conventionally explained phenomenon. This is the main reason [[US Space Force]], [[NORAD]], and [[US Northern Command]] keep detection records strictly classified under Executive Order 13526.
