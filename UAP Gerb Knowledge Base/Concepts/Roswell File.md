The Roswell File is retired Lieutenant Colonel [[Philip J. Corso]]'s name for a file drawer of material that, he said, Lieutenant General [[Arthur Trudeau]] handed him in 1961, when Corso was chief of the [[U.S. Army Foreign Technology Division]]. Corso said it held field reports, medical autopsy reports and pieces of debris from the 1947 [[Roswell Crash]]. Trudeau asked him to go through it and come back with a program for exploiting it. Corso described the file in his manuscript *Dawn of a New Age* and swore to having had it in a 1998 affidavit.

## Contents

Gerb's Corso video lists what Corso said he found in the drawer:

- multicoloured "wires"
- a chip
- a laser instrument
- fiber optics
- a paper-thin, high-strength metal
- autopsy reports from Walter Reed

The autopsy reports described decomposed bodies about four feet tall, with very large hearts and lungs and an unusual muscular structure. Their skin and bones seemed built to protect their organs from cosmic radiation or gravitational forces. The reports called the beings "[[Extraterrestrial Biological Entity|extraterrestrial biological entities]]", and Corso said they matched the body he had seen in a crate at [[Fort Riley, Kansas]], in July 1947. A transparent film over the beings' eyes is the detail Corso links to Trudeau's order for [[Passive Night Vision Technology|passive night vision]] work.

## What Corso's team concluded

Corso and Trudeau's office noticed what the file left out. There was no food or water, no refrigeration, no toilet and no place to rest, and the only medical equipment was what the recovery crew took for a first-aid kit. From this the team concluded that the beings were [[Cloned Beings|clones]] or at least expendable creatures made for space travel, and that the craft was a reconnaissance vehicle meant to return to a mothership. Their most important conclusion, in Gerb's account, was that the being was part of the saucer's guidance system, which they thought was why such craft failed. That idea led to studies of [[Brain Wave Aircraft Control|controlling aircraft and anti-missile missiles by brain waves]]. Gerb's SAIC video cites the same claim as an early link between UFOs and cognition: the Foreign Technology Division began a 1961 project on guidance by brain waves after the Roswell files showed the crew were part of the craft's guidance system.

Gerb sets this beside modern testimony about craft flown through a connection between mind and machine. He also cites a report from [[Leonard Stringfield]]'s files in which a [[Bell Labs]] source described seeing a cutaway of a retrieved craft, and the headband transceivers its occupants wore, at a California Air Force base in the early 1950s.

## The affidavit

Shortly before his death in July 1998, Corso signed a sworn affidavit for Peter Gersten of [[Citizens Against UFO Secrecy (CAUS)|Citizens Against UFO Secrecy]] in support of a lawsuit against the Department of the Army. In it he stated that he came into possession of a "Roswell file" in 1961, that its autopsy reports came from Walter Reed hospital, and that they described the creature he had seen at Fort Riley as an "extraterrestrial biological entity".
