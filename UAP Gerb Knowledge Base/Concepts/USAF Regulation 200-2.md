USAF Regulation 200-2 was a United States Air Force regulation, issued in the early Cold War, that set rules for reporting, investigating and handling unidentified aerial sightings and for what the public was told about them. Discussions of UAP history often cite it as one of the main policies used to centralize control of UFO-related information.

## Purpose and Administrative Function

The regulation set out how sightings were to be reported, investigated and passed through official channels. It kept the authority to make decisions within military command and limited what could be released to the public while an investigation was pending.

## Role in UAP Information Control Narratives

Researchers and commentators often point to Regulation 200-2 as evidence that the institutions involved meant to stop reports of anomalous aircraft from spreading unchecked. In their reading, it also sent intelligence through tightly managed chains and kept the technical interpretation of sightings under military jurisdiction.

These accounts treat the regulation as a prototype for the compartmented models later used to govern UAP matters.
