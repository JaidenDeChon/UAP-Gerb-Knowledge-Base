The underground tunnel rail system is the alleged combination of deep subterranean tunnels and high-speed electromagnetic rail (maglev) systems that witnesses and researchers claim connect Deep Underground Military Bases (DUMBs) across the United States. The term covers both the tunnels themselves and the transportation technology that runs inside them.
