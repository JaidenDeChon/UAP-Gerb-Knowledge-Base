Dysonian SETI, also called Cosmic Techno-signature Intelligence (CTI), is a way of searching for extraterrestrial intelligence by looking for physical techno-signatures that technologically advanced civilizations leave behind, instead of listening for intentional radio or other electromagnetic messages. It is named for Freeman Dyson. His 1960 proposal of a megastructure that would harvest a star's energy ([[Dyson Sphere]]) was the first serious scientific proposal that the large-scale activities of advanced civilizations would be detectable across interstellar distances.

## Motivation

Traditional [[SETI]] searches for narrow-band radio signals, on the assumption that extraterrestrial civilizations may communicate by radio, the technology humanity developed in the late 19th century. Critics call this species-centric. A civilization millions or billions of years more advanced than humanity may not use radio at all, and may instead communicate through neutrino pulses, quantum entanglement, gravitational wave modulation, or methods entirely unknown. Traditional SETI would never detect a civilization that does not use radio, however close or large it is.

Dysonian SETI avoids this assumption by looking for physical traces of engineering on the scale of stars or galaxies. Standard astronomical instruments could detect these whether or not the civilization intends to communicate.

## Key Techno-Signatures Sought

- Anomalous stellar spectra. A [[Dyson Sphere]] or Dyson Swarm would make a star give off unusually little visible light and unusually strong infrared emission. Astronomical surveys can pick out stars like this for further study.
- Irregular stellar light curves. Unexplained, non-periodic dimming of a star may point to megastructures in orbit around it. The star KIC 8462852 ("Tabby's Star") drew wide attention for this reason in 2015–2016.
- Waste heat. Any technological civilization that uses energy on a large scale produces waste heat, and at high enough energy use this becomes detectable in a galaxy's infrared emission profile.
- Stellar spectra that point to atmospheric engineering. These would show planetary atmospheric chemistry that natural processes cannot explain, such as unexpected ratios of industrial gases.

## Significance

Dysonian SETI is a broader and potentially more powerful search strategy than radio SETI, because the target civilization does not have to be transmitting. It only has to exist and be building things. The search also does not depend on the civilization knowing it is being observed, or being willing to communicate.
