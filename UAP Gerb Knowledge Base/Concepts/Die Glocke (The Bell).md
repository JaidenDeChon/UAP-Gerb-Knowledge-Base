**Die Glocke** (German for "The Bell") is an alleged Nazi wonder weapon that, according to legend, was developed during World War II. Accounts describe it as shaped like a bell or an acorn. It has become a subject of speculation among alternative historians and UAP researchers.

## Description and Alleged Characteristics

The alleged device is said to have been bell-shaped, roughly like the Liberty Bell. Some accounts credit it with unusual propulsion or anti-gravity properties, but no verified documentation confirms that it existed or had those capabilities.

## UAP Connections

In UAP research, Die Glocke comes up mainly as a visual comparison in descriptions of craft.

The 1933 Magenta, Italy UFO Crash involved a craft recovered near [[Magenta, Italy]] in 1933. It was described as "bell-like, almost resembling a mix between a flying saucer and Die Glocke." The comparison suggests it had the same bell or acorn shape attributed to the alleged Nazi device.

In the 1965 Kecksburg, Pennsylvania UFO Crash, witnesses often described the object that came down in Kecksburg as bell-shaped or acorn-shaped, like the Liberty Bell. That led some researchers to connect it with the Die Glocke mythology.
