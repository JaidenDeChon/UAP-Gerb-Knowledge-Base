The **live biologics protocols** are the rules for handling living non-human beings at a crash site, as described by the [[Infographic Show Whistleblower]]. He calls them "ruthlessly strict."

## The Rules

1. Never level a weapon at a being.
2. Keep security personnel well away from the biologics and out of their sight.
3. Immediately take readings to make sure nothing toxic or hazardous is present near the craft.
4. Then send medical personnel to the craft area or inside it, led by a lead doctor known as **"the Ambassador,"** who always spoke at least English, Chinese, Spanish and Russian.

## In Practice

The witness says the protocols were used at the [[1996 Bole, Ireland Crash Retrieval]], where two of five crew survived. The beings stood about aimlessly, reacted only to basic stimuli, and were led out of the craft "like school children." In almost every live recovery, he says, the beings lived a few days at most. This, and their ability to breathe Earth's atmosphere, led program scientists to see them as biological drones, one basis for the [[Fabrication Hypothesis]].
