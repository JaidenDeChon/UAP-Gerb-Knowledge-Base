Project Stargate was a U.S. government program umbrella used for controlled research into remote viewing and related anomalous cognition claims during the Cold War and post-Cold War period. Program elements were sponsored across different phases by U.S. Army, DIA, and CIA structures, with contractor support.

## Relevance in UAP Discourse

In UAP-focused analysis, Project Stargate is frequently cited as precedent for government interest in cognition-adjacent intelligence methods and for later claims that some classified programs explored mind-technology interfaces.

The SAIC video in this repository references Stargate as part of a broader argument that consciousness research streams overlapped with contractor environments later discussed in legacy UAP program narratives.
