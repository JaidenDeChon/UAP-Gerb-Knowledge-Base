An intercontinental ballistic missile (ICBM) is a long-range ballistic missile designed to deliver nuclear warheads over intercontinental distances, usually defined as ranges greater than 5,500 kilometers (3,400 miles). An ICBM follows a ballistic trajectory through space, and its warheads separate from the boosters at high altitude before re-entry. During the Cold War, ICBMs were the land-based leg of the US nuclear triad, alongside submarine-launched ballistic missiles and strategic bombers. Several documented UAP incidents involve UFOs interacting with ICBMs during test flights or disabling them in their silos.

## Design and Deployment

Cold War US ICBMs included the Minuteman series (Minuteman I, II and III), deployed in hardened underground silos across Montana, Wyoming, North Dakota, South Dakota and Missouri. The Minuteman was the primary ICBM system at [[Malmstrom Air Force Base]], Montana, at the time of the [[Malmstrom Air Force Base UFO Incident]] of 1967. In the 1960s, ICBMs tested from [[Vandenberg Air Force Base]] were used to evaluate warhead design, re-entry vehicle performance and countermeasures such as [[Missile Chaff Decoy|chaff decoys]].

## UAP Interactions

Two formally documented incidents involve direct UFO interaction with US ICBM systems.

In a 1964 test launch from [[Vandenberg Air Force Base]], Lieutenant [[Robert Jacobs]] filmed a dummy ICBM warhead. At the fringe of space, a disc-shaped craft intercepted the warhead and fired four [[Directed Energy Beam|directed energy beams]] at it, knocking it off its trajectory. Government agents confiscated the film. See [[Vandenberg Air Force Base UFO Film Incident]].

In the 1967 Malmstrom shutdown, all 10 Minuteman ICBMs assigned to a launch control facility at Malmstrom lost their guidance and control function at the same time, while a UFO hovered over the base's front gate. Lieutenant [[Robert Salas]] and three other personnel submitted sworn affidavits. See [[Malmstrom Air Force Base UFO Incident]].

Both cases are central to discussion of [[UFO Interference with Nuclear Weapons]] and the [[UFO-Nuclear Connection]] in UAP research.
