The "Hidden Wing" is a theoretical framework and alleged program portfolio introduced by Gerb. It describes a clandestine U.S. Air Force test and evaluation infrastructure that allegedly conducts research, development, test, and evaluation (RDT&E) on recovered non-human vehicles (NHVs), [[Alien Reproduction Vehicle (ARV)|alien reproduction vehicles (ARVs)]], and derivative airframes. The name is a deliberate play on the Air Force's "test wing" organizational structure, specifically the [[412th Test Wing|Edwards 412th Test Wing]] at [[Edwards Air Force Base]]. It suggests that a hidden or undisclosed wing-level program sits inside the Air Force's official test and evaluation apparatus. Gerb first set out the thesis in the video "The Hidden Wing - US Air Force UFO Reverse Engineering Programs" and built on it in later videos.

## Scope and Structure

According to the thesis, the Hidden Wing program portfolio operates across the Air Force's Western Ranges, the network of test ranges and related facilities in the western United States administered through the [[Major Range and Test Facility Bases (MRTFBs)]] system. The main facilities alleged to be involved are:

- **[[Edwards Air Force Base]]** (California): The primary alleged site for T&E of ARVs and derivative craft, carried out through the [[412th Test Wing|Edwards 412th Test Wing]]. Witness "Ed," a retired Air Force officer, claimed to have served on an ARV T&E program at Edwards that involved both manned and unmanned reverse-engineered craft. Pilots of alleged derivative vehicles are said to have trained at the [[Air Force Test Pilot School]], also located at Edwards.
- **[[Dugway Proving Ground]]** (Utah): Alleged storage and exploitation site. A contractor witness, MS, reported encountering a seamless disc-shaped craft being dismantled in a hangar in the Avery area of Dugway. Dugway's Granite Peak installation allegedly contains a deep underground military base, surrounded by radar and sensor infrastructure belonging to the [[388th Range Squadron]].
- **[[Langley Air Force Base]]** (Virginia): Site of witness [[Dylan Borland]]'s 2012 triangular craft observation, consistent with Gerb's theory of deployed derivative or reverse-engineered airframes operating within domestic airspace.
- **[[Nevada Test and Training Range (NTR)]]**: Alleged base of operations for an NRO-led UFO crash retrieval team, according to an anonymous source.

## Organizational Pathway

Gerb argues that the Hidden Wing operates through a specific chain of institutional authority:

1. The [[National Security Council]] and classified presidential executive orders, traced to the Truman and Eisenhower administrations, provide the foundational legal authority.
2. The [[Office of the Under Secretary of Defense for Intelligence and Security (USDINS)]] (USD I&S) and the [[National Program Special Management Staff (NPMS)]] coordinate NSC SAPs with the DoD and the intelligence community.
3. [[SAFAQ]] (Office of the Assistant Secretary of the Air Force for Acquisition, Technology and Logistics) oversees the Air Force acquisition programs in which legacy programs may be embedded, particularly through [[SAFAQL (Special Programs)]], [[SAFAQR (Science, Technology and Engineering)]] and [[SAFAQX (Acquisition Integration)]].
4. The [[Rapid Capabilities Office (RCO)]] is the likely execution arm. Its narrow oversight chain and carve-out contracting authority let it acquire legacy program equities without the standard notification requirements.
5. [[Air Force Materiel Command (AFMC)]], its subordinate [[412th Test Wing|Edwards 412th Test Wing]], and [[Air Force Test and Evaluation (AF-TE)]] conduct the actual RDT&E operations.
6. [[SAFAHA]] (Sensitive Activities) and [[SAFAAZ]] provide the program's administrative and security envelope, and operate under classified DoD directive S-5210.36.

## The hands-on chain

In the video's closing remarks Gerb narrows the thesis. The Hidden Wing covers derivative vehicles, [[Alien Reproduction Vehicle (ARV)|alien reproduction vehicles]] and non-human craft "that humans have found a way to interface with". It runs across the Western Ranges: the [[Utah Test and Training Range]], the [[Nevada Test and Training Range (NTR)]] and the airspace of the [[412th Test Wing]]. Its pilots come from both the U.S. Naval Test Pilot School at Patuxent River and the [[Air Force Test Pilot School]] at Edwards. The 412th runs day-to-day activity, but Gerb names the [[Air Force Test Center]] and, above all, [[Air Force Test and Evaluation (AF-TE)]] as the real administrators and "the real top of the chain of command" for hands-on test and evaluation. He says he has seen witness Ed's records naming AF/TE as Ed's office of primary responsibility. Research and development support comes from the [[Air Force Research Laboratory]] and from contractors at [[Air Force Plant 42]] in the [[Antelope Valley]]. As a parallel he cites [[Rodrik Castle]]'s 1997 black triangle, which left toward Edwards's R-2508 restricted complex.

## The 2009–2012 "Go Public" Campaign

Gerb's "80 Years of UFO Crash Retrieval and Reverse Engineering" timeline video alleges that between 2009 and 2012 an Air Force "Go Public" disclosure campaign was conceived and then killed within the Hidden Wing. The campaign was based at the [[Air Force Test Center]] out of [[412th Test Wing|Edwards 412th Test Wing]], under the direction of a two-star general. It would reportedly have publicly disclosed some of the exotic derivative and reverse-engineered airframes matured on the Western Ranges, without any direct acknowledgment that the technology or influence behind them came from non-human technical vehicles. Gerb states he is uncertain why the campaign was conceived in the first place or why it was killed in 2012. He notes that 2011, within the same window, also saw a further clampdown on legacy program information control, including a briefing embargo within [[MITRE Corporation|MITRE]] that left newcomers to its legacy program work unaware of the program's history or non-human origin.

## Legal Vehicle

Gerb argues that [[Waived Unacknowledged Special Access Programs (USAPs)]] are the most likely legal vehicle for Hidden Wing operations. Under 10 U.S. Code Section 119, at minimum the "Gang of Eight" congressional leaders must be notified of such programs. Gerb contends that UFO legacy programs still operate under some oversight, but through stripped and compartmentalized channels that minimize congressional visibility while keeping a thin legal basis for continued concealment.
