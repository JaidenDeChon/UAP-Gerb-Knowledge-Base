The UAP Taxonomy, titled on its slide "Proposed Taxonomy of UAP Origin Hypotheses", is a classification of candidate explanations for UAP and non-human intelligence (NHI) prepared by retired US Army Colonel [[Karl Nell]]. It was a backup slide to his November 2023 [[SOL Foundation Symposium]] presentation, not shown in the recorded talk, and was shared publicly by [[Matthew Pines]] in February 2024. It is meant to keep every hypothesis on the table while evidence narrows the field.

## Structure

As Gerb reads the slide, the hypotheses fall into three categories: **physical**, **psychophysical** and **metaphysical**.

- **Physical** runs from the expected to the bizarre: natural phenomena, humans, intergalactic extraterrestrials, protohumans, "stranded gods", unknown regions of spacetime, disconnected hyperspace, and 3D avatars or holograms.
- A stranger group follows: colliding universes, parallel universes, macro quantum fluctuations, pangalactic entanglement and "distance recursion". The video doesn't make clear whether these belong to the physical or the psychophysical category.
- **Metaphysical** covers supernatural forces, incorporeal life forms, ascended masters, angelic or demonic beings and artificial realities.

Gerb says he leans toward the physical explanations, and offers to rank the hypotheses in a tier list.

## Related frameworks

The same set of backup slides proposed the [[Universal Civilization Development Datum]] as a replacement for the [[Kardashev Scale]].
