***The Age of Disclosure*** is a 2025 American documentary directed and produced by Dan Farah that alleges an 80-year government cover-up of non-human intelligence and technology of non-human origin. It premiered at South by Southwest on 9 March 2025 and was released in theaters and on Amazon Prime Video on 21 November 2025. It is built around interviews with more than thirty current and former government, military and intelligence figures. Its central figure is [[Luis Elizondo]], and interviewees include [[Jay Stratton]] and former Director of National Intelligence [[James Clapper]].

## UAP Gerb's criticism

Gerb discusses the film at length in *Special Access Required Vol.2*. He stresses that he is criticizing what he calls its revisionist account of the legacy programs, and not Farah or the quality of the film. He argues it is "an amnesty plea and misdirect."

He says the film presents [[AATIP (Advanced Aerospace Threat Identification Program)|ATIP]] as a real, funded program with its own scientists, naming [[Hal Puthoff]] and [[Eric Davis]], and he calls this "brazen nonsense." In his account ATIP was an unfunded informal working group, separate from the DIA's funded [[AAWSAP]], and was a cover for National Security Council activities under Clapper.

The film's organizational chart shows a single legacy program led by the [[CIA Directorate of Science and Technology]], with the directorate's deputy director portrayed as its head and the [[Department of Energy]], the Air Force and contractors side by side below it. Gerb calls this "demonstrably false." He argues that the [[National Security Council]] belongs above the CIA, that DOE runs through every layer and that most prime contractors sit at the bottom. In his view the chart also leaves out the Army, the Navy, the Office of Naval Intelligence, the [[Defense Intelligence Agency]] and other intelligence agencies, FFRDCs, national laboratories and university research centers. He believes it was drawn to protect Elizondo's and Stratton's "legacy friends" and to decide who "gets to catch the live hand grenade" when disclosure comes.

He also objects to Clapper's inclusion in the film, and argues that it continues the partial-disclosure campaign he associates with ATIP and [[To The Stars Academy]].
