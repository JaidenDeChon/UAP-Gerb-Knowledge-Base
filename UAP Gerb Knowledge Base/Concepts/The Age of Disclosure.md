***The Age of Disclosure*** is a 2025 American documentary directed and produced by Dan Farah that alleges an 80-year government cover-up of non-human intelligence and technology of non-human origin. It premiered at South by Southwest on 9 March 2025 and was released in theaters and on Amazon Prime Video on 21 November 2025. It is built around interviews with more than thirty current and former government, military and intelligence figures. Its central figure is [[Luis Elizondo]], and interviewees include [[Jay Stratton]] and former Director of National Intelligence [[James Clapper]].

## UAP Gerb's criticism

UAP Gerb discusses the film at length in *Special Access Required Vol.2*. He stresses that his criticism is not of Farah or the film's quality, but of what he calls its revisionist account of the legacy programs. He argues it serves as "an amnesty plea and misdirect."

- **ATIP.** He says the film presents [[AATIP (Advanced Aerospace Threat Identification Program)|ATIP]] as a real, funded program with its own scientists, naming [[Hal Puthoff]] and [[Eric Davis]], which he calls "brazen nonsense." In his account ATIP was an unfunded informal working group, distinct from the DIA's funded [[AAWSAP]], and served as cover for National Security Council activities under Clapper.
- **The organizational chart.** The film shows a single legacy program led by the [[CIA Directorate of Science and Technology]], whose deputy director is portrayed as its head, with the [[Department of Energy]], the Air Force and contractors side by side below it. Gerb calls this "demonstrably false." He argues the [[National Security Council]] belongs above the CIA, DOE runs through every layer, most prime contractors sit at the bottom, and the chart leaves out the Army, the Navy, the Office of Naval Intelligence, the [[Defense Intelligence Agency]] and other intelligence agencies, FFRDCs, national laboratories and university research centers. He believes the chart was drawn to protect Elizondo's and Stratton's "legacy friends" and to decide who "gets to catch the live hand grenade" when disclosure comes.
- **Clapper.** He objects to Clapper's inclusion and argues the film continues the partial-disclosure campaign he associates with ATIP and [[To The Stars Academy]].
