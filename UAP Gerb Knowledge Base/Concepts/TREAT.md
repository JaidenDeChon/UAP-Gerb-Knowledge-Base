TREAT (Tactical Reconnaissance Engineering Assessment Team) is an alleged clandestine US government unit that [[Edgar Fouche|Edgar Fouché]] described as what some would call the "Men in Black." Fouché learned of TREAT from his close source Gerald, a former [[NSA]] investigator and TREAT team member. Gerald worked under cover for the [[Department of Energy]] and the NSA, and the NSA controlled all his movements.

## Public Mentions

Only one publicly available mention of TREAT has been identified. In a letter to [[Steven Greer]] dated August 31, 1997, an unknown sender accused Liechtenstein's Prince Hans Adam II, whom Gerb calls the Crown Prince, of funding TREAT. The letter states that "Adam's funding should be focused on his support for TREAT and its GRD, who is wed to a former US Army General, ex-head of Army Intelligence and Security Command." The general in question is Major General [[Albert Stubblebine]], who commanded the US Army Intelligence and Security Command ([[Army INSCOM|INSCOM]]) from 1981 to 1984. Stubblebine allegedly intimidated Greer into dropping his investigation of UFO black projects and once personally offered him $2 billion to end his disclosure activities.

## Possible INSCOM Connection

TREAT's link to Stubblebine and Army INSCOM suggests the unit may have been an intelligence and counterintelligence body housed in the Army and focused on UFO matters. Its work may have included monitoring personnel at classified facilities and possibly field operations related to UAP encounters or crash retrievals.
