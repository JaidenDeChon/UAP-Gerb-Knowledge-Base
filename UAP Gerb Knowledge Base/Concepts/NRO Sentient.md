NRO Sentient is an artificial intelligence-driven intelligence collection and analysis program operated by the [[NRO (National Reconnaissance Office)]]. Development of Sentient reportedly began in 2010, with core infrastructure built out by 2016. The program is designed to autonomously task and correlate data from multiple satellite collection streams, providing near-real-time analysis and enabling the NRO to process vastly larger volumes of overhead intelligence than traditional analyst-driven workflows allow. Its capabilities include autonomous satellite retasking, cross-sensor data fusion, and predictive collection scheduling.

Sentient's significance in UAP research stems from its alleged role as the primary intelligence resource for the [[Immaculate Constellation (IMCON)]] USAP established in 2017 — a program specifically designed to surveil non-human craft and derivative technologies. The temporal proximity of Sentient's 2016 buildout completion and IMCON's 2017 establishment is treated as non-coincidental by UAP researchers. NRO Sentient is referenced by name in the Matthew Brown report on IMCON, suggesting it serves as the collection backbone for that program. UAP researcher Gerb argues that an AI-driven system capable of autonomously correlating overhead imagery across the entire NRO constellation would be uniquely suited to tracking UAP events and generating the intelligence that feeds both IMCON and alleged crash retrieval tasking.

## Public Record

Sentient's existence entered the public record through Freedom of Information Act releases obtained by John Greenwald of [[The Black Vault]]. The NRO describes it as an ongoing research and development program combining problem-centric intelligence, end-to-end multi-INT and trusted machine automation to transform the collection tasking, processing, exploitation and dissemination (TCPED) cycle. Former NGA director [[Robert Cardillo]] has described it as using "automated inferencing" to aid collection.

On 2 June 2022 the NRO released a "Recent Sentient Highlights" document based on data from 6 May 2021. It reported a possible airborne object under 10 meters in diameter that "vaguely resembles" detections by US Navy aircraft and surface vessels, which the NRO judged tic-tac shaped and unlikely to be a sensor artifact. That year the NRO also contributed to the ODNI's report on the UAP Task Force, despite a 2015 statement that UFOs were outside the scope of its responsibilities and that it held no records.

## UAP Gerb's Thesis

UAP Gerb argues that raw overhead data is encoded into imagery before it reaches analysts, and that systems such as Sentient likely identify anomalies, such as UFOs or crash sites, and strip them from ordinary analysts' feeds for delivery to more cleared personnel. He suggests this explains why rank-and-file NRO and NGA analysts have not reported seeing UFOs, and wonders whether the 2021 Sentient detection is the same tic-tac over US Southern Command described in the Immaculate Constellation report.
