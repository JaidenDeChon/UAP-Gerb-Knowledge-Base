Deep Underground Military Bases (DUMBs) are military installations that the United States government and its contractors have built at depths from dozens to thousands of feet below ground. Their purposes include continuity of government, ICBM silo hardening, and command and control operations. According to numerous witnesses and researchers, they also include the clandestine storage and exploitation of non-human technology. The existence of DUMBs in acknowledged forms is documented fact. Their alleged role in UAP legacy programs is contested, but a consistent body of firsthand witness testimony, gathered over decades, supports it.

## Acknowledged DUMBs

Several DUMBs are publicly known and confirmed. The [[Cheyenne Mountain Complex]] near Colorado Springs, Colorado, holds the NORAD command center 2,000 feet underground, in six three-story-high tunnels and 15 steel buildings covering 5.1 acres. The [[Raven Rock Complex (Site R)]] beneath [[Fort Detrick]], Maryland, is a classified underground backup Pentagon. It can house up to 3,000 personnel and has its own medical clinic, fire department, and post office.

These facilities show that large-scale underground construction is technically and financially feasible. According to a 1985 US Army Corps of Engineers report, underground installations can be constructed "under virtually any ground conditions," and the main constraint is economic viability rather than technical feasibility.

## Historical Development

The idea behind the modern DUMB program goes back to Nazi Germany's wartime underground manufacturing complexes. The Nazis built the Regenwurmlager ("earthworm camp") beneath western Poland, with miles of tunnels and electric train lines, and the Ohrdruf installations in Germany, with miles of two- to three-story underground facilities arranged in spoke-wheel configurations. These facilities were designed to survive Allied bombing raids. After the war, the US government used [[Operation Paperclip]] to seek out key Nazi engineers who had worked on underground construction. They included [[Xaver Dorsch]], director of the Organisation Todt (founded by Fritz Todt, builder of the Autobahn), who oversaw major underground industrial plants, and [[Hans Kammler]], the SS general who oversaw Nazi secret weapons programs. Both men are alleged to have directly informed early US programs to build underground facilities.

The US Army Corps of Engineers was heavily involved in DUMB construction from the late 1940s onward. [[Lloyd A. Duscha]], the Corps' Deputy Director of Engineering and Construction, gave a speech titled "Underground Facilities for Defense: Experience and Lessons" in which he acknowledged that the Corps had been involved in "some very complex and interesting military projects" that remained classified. Gerb dates the speech to 1989, though it was published in 1987 conference proceedings. A 1964 Army Corps of Engineers study proposed 12 massive structures to be built as deep as 4,000 feet underground. Two of the sites are of particular interest: one below Yuma County, Arizona (near [[Yuma Proving Ground]]), and one in Inyo County, California (sharing territory with [[China Lake Naval Air Weapons Station]]).

[[RAND Corporation]] was also central to early DUMB planning. From 1957, RAND actively investigated the need for "super hard deep underground centers," and in 1960, under a USAF contract, it published a study identifying 12 locations for underground sites, all assumed to be more than 1,000 feet deep.

Most DUMBs built for purposes other than continuity of government are believed to date from about 1961 to the late 1990s. The estimate rests on the timing of Army Corps planning documents, which assume that underground facilities already existed after 1961.

## Alleged UAP Involvement

Several researchers and witnesses allege that some DUMBs in the American Southwest and on the East Coast have been used to store, exploit, and reverse engineer non-human technology:

- [[Leonard Stringfield]] documented testimony, from as early as 1974, alleging that underground facilities at Fort Hood, Texas, and Wright-Patterson Air Force Base held recovered UAP craft and non-human biologics.
- From the late 1980s through the 1990s, [[Bill Hamilton]] compiled firsthand testimony describing a connected network of UAP-focused underground facilities in California's [[Antelope Valley]], including facilities under [[Tehachapi, California|Tehachapi (the "Anthill")]], Helendale, and Edwards AFB.
- A witness identified as MS, cited by Gerb, described a subterranean facility at [[Dugway Proving Ground]] reached through an elevator shaft hidden in a small surface building.
- [[Randy Anderson]] described an off-world technologies division at [[Naval Surface Warfare Center Crane|NSWC Crane]], housed underground.
- Gerb has reported speaking with nearly half a dozen former military personnel who have traveled to STIFs (Subterranean Facilities) around the country. One of them, an Army officer, described traveling by underground rail from Texas to [[White Sands Missile Range]].

## Subterranean Facility (STIF) Terminology

Gerb notes that several military veterans have called these installations "STIFs" (Subterranean Facilities) rather than "DUMBs," which is mainly a term used by researchers. This suggests that people inside the programs call these sites something different from the names used in public discussion.

## Construction Technologies

Three main technologies are believed to have been used to build DUMBs and the tunnel networks that allegedly connect them:

1. Tunnel Boring Machines (TBMs) are conventional cylindrical mechanical excavators that can bore circular tunnels 35 feet or more in diameter. Under good conditions, a TBM run by about 10 men can bore up to 10 miles a year.
2. Nuclear Subterrenes are experimental devices, developed as a concept at Los Alamos National Laboratory, that melt through rock using compact nuclear reactors. The melted rock vitrifies and leaves the tunnel lined with glass. The Atomic Energy Commission filed patents in 1972 and 1975.
3. Explosive or nuclear detonation is an unconventional excavation method that defense literature describes as an alternative to mechanical boring.

## DUMB Network Maps

Researcher [[Bill Hamilton]] compiled the most detailed alleged map of UAP-focused underground facilities in the American West from his network of informants. He published it in his 1992 book *Cosmic Top Secret*. The map shows connected installations across Southern California, New Mexico, Colorado, and Nevada, with nodes at Edwards AFB, Tehachapi, Helendale, Dugway, Los Alamos, Sandia/Kirtland, and elsewhere. Gerb has compared Hamilton's map with the map in RAND Corporation's 1972 "Very High Speed Transit" (VHST) feasibility study, which shows proposed underground transit corridors across the continent, and notes that the two correspond closely in their geography.

## Eastern Network

Besides the well-documented cluster of DUMBs in the Southwest, an eastern network is alleged to exist. Its main alleged sites include a classified facility under the [[Pentagon]] operated by the [[CIA Directorate of Science and Technology]], the [[Warrenton, Virginia|Warrenton Army Training Center]], used by the CIA as cover for an underground installation, and a US Navy underground facility at [[Sugar Grove Naval Communication Center, West Virginia|Sugar Grove, West Virginia]] run for the NSA. Secretary of Defense Robert McNamara first proposed the "Deep Underground Command Center" (DUCC) in 1963, as a facility 3,500 feet below Washington, D.C., that could survive nuclear strikes of 200–300 megatons. Gerb believes it was built, based on firsthand accounts from sources who described an enormous subterranean facility below the Pentagon.

## Funding

The funding mechanisms of UFO Legacy Programs are believed to pay for building and running DUMBs. Financial analyst [[Catherine Austin Fitts]] has documented as much as $21 trillion in unauthorized spending within the DOD and the Department of Housing and Urban Development between 1998 and 2015. The mechanisms include Independent Research and Development (IRAD) overcharging, sole-source contracts, and IDIQ (Indefinite Delivery, Indefinite Quantity) contracting. Each can be used to hide the flow of money inside legitimate contract vehicles.
