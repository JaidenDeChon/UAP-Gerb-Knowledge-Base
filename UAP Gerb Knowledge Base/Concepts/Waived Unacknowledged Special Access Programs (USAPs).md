Waived Unacknowledged Special Access Programs (USAPs) are the highest classification tier in the U.S. government's Special Access Program (SAP) framework. Standard SAPs require regular congressional reporting, but waived USAPs let the Secretary of Defense exempt specific programs from the normal congressional oversight notifications. Under 10 U.S. Code Section 119, these programs report at minimum only to the "Gang of Eight": the eight senior congressional leaders, meaning the majority and minority leaders of both chambers plus the chairs and ranking members of the intelligence committees. Programs designated as waived and unacknowledged may be unknown even to most members of the congressional armed services and defense appropriations committees, so meaningful legislative oversight of them is extremely limited. The structure rests on post-World War II statutory authorities and is carried out through a series of classified DoD directives.

UAP Gerb's *Special Access Required Vol.2* describes unacknowledged SAPs (USAPs) more broadly as programs whose mere existence and purpose are protected. Their funding is typically classified or hidden within the broader federal budget with no direct link to the program, and cleared personnel must hold indoctrination beyond standard Top Secret/SCI access. Citing the Joint Security Commission's 1994 report "Redefining Security," the video states that unacknowledged SAPs run roughly three to ten times the security costs of acknowledged SAPs, and that estimated security costs for some unacknowledged programs reach as high as 40% of total operational costs. The video connects that figure to the [[Wilson-Davis Memo|Wilson-Davis notes']] claim, attributed to General Mike Costanik, that the legacy program's security budget ran two to seven times the size of the underlying program budget.

## Access Approval Authority

Per DoD Instruction 5205.05 and reporting cited in *Special Access Required Vol.2*, access to waived programs can be approved only by the Deputy Secretary of Defense, special Under Secretaries of Defense, or a component head holding relevant "cognizant authority." Waived programs are still nominally required to be reported to the congressional Gang of Eight, though Gerb argues that this requirement is itself frequently circumvented for waived USAPs related to the legacy program. [[David Grusch]] has publicly described meeting privately with former Senate Majority Leader [[Harry Reid]] roughly nine months before Reid's death. At that meeting Reid, a former Gang of Eight member, reportedly told Grusch: "Yeah, I knew we had UFO material. I was denied access for decades."
