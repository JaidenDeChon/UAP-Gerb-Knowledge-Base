Pink Assets is an alleged insider term for human beings used as biological subjects, or "biological equipment", in classified UAP-related programs. According to people claiming to be program insiders who gave this information to [[Michael Herrera]], these people are recruited under false pretenses, heavily drugged so that they are never fully aware of their involvement or able to remember it, and exploited for purposes they neither understand nor consent to.

## Recruitment Methods

### Disaster Zone Exploitation

Herrera learned that one recruitment method targets people in disaster-stricken areas right after natural catastrophes such as earthquakes, tsunamis or other events that leave chaos, displacement and desperation behind. The 2009 Indonesia operation Herrera witnessed was allegedly an example of this method. It took place approximately 9-10 days after a major earthquake, when humanitarian relief operations provided cover for covert activities.

The operators exploit several conditions found in disaster zones. When a population is displaced and confused, missing persons are common and expected. Local law enforcement and government are overwhelmed and focused on immediate survival and relief. People in desperate conditions may be more open to offers of help, transport or employment. The military and humanitarian presence gives the operators cover and plausible deniability. Records are destroyed or in disarray, which makes investigations difficult.

### False Promises and Deception

According to insider accounts shared with Herrera, people recruited as pink assets are:
- Given false promises about the nature of their involvement
- Misled about what they will be doing and what the consequences will be
- Not given informed consent about the true purpose of the programs
- Unable to leave or refuse participation once recruited

## Usage in Programs

Pink assets are allegedly used as biological subjects in experiments or operations related to:
- Interface testing with non-human intelligence technology
- Consciousness-based craft control systems
- Direct contact or communication with non-human entities
- Unknown experimental protocols related to UAP technology exploitation

Insiders report that participants are heavily drugged during their involvement. This prevents them from:
- Fully understanding what they are participating in
- Forming clear memories of their experiences
- Retaining information that could later compromise program security
- Recognizing the true nature or magnitude of what is being done to them

## Physical Evidence: The Transport Operation

During his 2009 encounter, Herrera saw four black shipping containers on trailers attached to up-armored trucks. Large black cylinders spanned the full width of each container. Herrera at first believed they were humidity control equipment, but insiders later identified them as oxygen supply systems, confirming that the containers held living human beings. He watched the containers being loaded onto an elevating platform beneath an octagonal craft for transport to an unknown destination, presumably a classified facility.

The operation's logistics, which included reinforced vehicles, oxygen systems, pelican cases with weapons and night vision equipment, indicate a well-resourced, professional operation designed to move human subjects covertly, and by force if necessary.

## Ethical Concerns

Insiders describe the use of pink assets as one of the most morally objectionable parts of classified UAP programs. Even people working within these programs who support non-human intelligence research and technology development have expressed ethical opposition to:
- Kidnapping or coercing participants
- Denying informed consent
- Using drugs to eliminate autonomy and memory
- Treating human beings as disposable equipment
- Targeting vulnerable populations in crisis situations

This ethical divide has contributed to internal factions within UAP programs. Some insiders are willing to leak information and contact whistleblowers like Herrera specifically because they oppose the treatment of pink assets.

## Motivation for Disclosure

Herrera states that learning about the existence and treatment of pink assets was one of the most devastating things insiders revealed to him, and that it caused him significant emotional distress. He describes feeling helpless, knowing that human beings were being exploited in this way and that he had unknowingly witnessed part of such an operation. This knowledge became a primary motivation for his continued disclosure efforts. He believes proper oversight could end these practices and potentially allow voluntary, informed participation in UAP-related research in place of coercion and exploitation.
