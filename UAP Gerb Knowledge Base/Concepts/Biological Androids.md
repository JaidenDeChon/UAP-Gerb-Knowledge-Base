Biological androids, in UAP research, refers to a theory that some non-human entities recovered from UAP crashes are not naturally evolved life forms but artificially created biological beings designed to perform specific functions. These beings are described as exhibiting limited cognitive ability, drone-like behavior, and crude physiological systems, suggesting they serve as crew members or operators rather than sentient, autonomous beings.

## Evidence from Crash Retrieval Testimony

The concept appears across multiple alleged retrieval cases:

- **Infographic Show Whistleblower** — A claimed crash retrieval operator stated that in several retrievals involving manned craft, teams would have to lead occupants out of the vehicles. These biologics exhibited limited responses to stimuli, "barely reacted to anything," and "were led out like children" — grabbed by the hand and positioned like dolls. They stood where left and showed only basic responses such as turning away from bright lights.

- **1953 [[Camp Polk, Louisiana]] Incident** — Witness [[HJ]] described three living occupants of an egg-shaped craft who walked as if they had no knees, bending only at the hips, in tight-fitting metallic green uniforms. One occupant made strange noises while looking at a dead companion on a stretcher.

- **1997 [[Peru UFO Crash Incident]]** — [[Jonathan Weygandt]] described an arm hanging limply from a hatch and reported telepathic contact from tall gray-type entities requesting help — suggesting more advanced cognitive function than the drone-like beings described in other cases, raising the possibility that different types of biological occupants (ranging from near-autonomous to fully sentient) may operate different craft.
