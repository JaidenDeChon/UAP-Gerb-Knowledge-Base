Extraterrestrial biological entity, usually shortened to EBE, is a term for the non-human occupants said to have been recovered from crashed craft. It appears in the alleged [[Majestic 12]] documents and in the testimony of [[Philip J. Corso]], and in Gerb's videos it is the standard phrase those sources use for the bodies from the 1947 [[Roswell Crash]].

## Origin of the term

The two sources Gerb covers give different origins for the name.

- The [[Eisenhower Briefing Document]], as Gerb reads it with [[Ryan S. Wood]], says Dr. [[Detlev Bronk]] performed autopsies on the Roswell bodies and coined the term.
- In an interview clip in Gerb's Corso video, Corso says his own team chose the name after concluding the beings were clones.

## Corso and the Roswell autopsies

Corso said the Walter Reed autopsy reports in the [[Roswell File]], which [[Arthur Trudeau]] gave him in 1961, called the beings extraterrestrial biological entities. His sworn 1998 affidavit uses the same phrase for the body he said he saw in a crate at [[Fort Riley, Kansas]], in July 1947. The reports described beings about four feet tall with very large hearts and lungs and skin and bones built to resist cosmic radiation or gravitational forces. In Corso's manuscript, a transparent film over their eyes led Trudeau to order work on [[Passive Night Vision Technology|passive night vision]], and the discovery that they were part of their craft's guidance system led to research on [[Brain Wave Aircraft Control|brain-wave control]]. Gerb also notes Corso's view that EBEs had committed acts of war with superior equipment, and that the Army treated them under the same "fig leaf" policy as the Soviets: a threat, but never officially an enemy.

## The two types in SOM 1-01

The [[Special Operations Manual (SOM 1-01)]], an alleged 1954 Majestic 12 manual, describes two types of EBE. Gerb's Majestic-12 video shows renderings of both by [[Bill McDonald]].

- EBE-1 is humanoid, 5 ft to 5 ft 4 in tall and 80 to 100 lb, with a large rounded cranium, chalky yellow, thick and slightly pebbled skin, small wide-set almond-shaped eyes, a wide and nearly lipless mouth, and hands with four long webbed digits and no thumb.
- EBE-2 is the classic "grey": humanoid but 3 ft 5 in to 4 ft 2 in tall and 25 to 50 lb, with a large elongated head, large black eyes that nearly wrap around the head, pale blue-grey skin and no hair. See [[Grays]].

The manual also sets packaging and shipping rules for EBEs and states that "loss of EBE life is acceptable". Its classification table sends deceased entities to the [[Blue Room]] at [[Wright-Patterson Air Force Base]].

Gerb ties EBE-1 to two other cases. In his Corso video he notes that EBE-1 closely matches the body Corso described at Fort Riley. In his Aztec video he says the bodies described in the 1948 [[Aztec UFO Crash and Recovery|Aztec]] case, with no digestive or reproductive organs and a colourless fluid in place of blood, sound almost identical to EBE-1.

## Background

The Eisenhower Briefing Document, which reached researchers on a roll of film in December 1984 and is dated 18 November 1952, says Bronk's team suggested "Extra-terrestrial Biological Entities", or "EBEs", as the standard term for the creatures until a more definitive name could be agreed.
