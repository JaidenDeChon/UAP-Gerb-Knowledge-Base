Solar Warden is the name of an alleged classified United States military program of space-based operations. According to hacker [[Gary McKinnon]], it appeared in Pentagon computer files he saw in 2001–2002, when he accessed 97 US Army and NASA systems without authorization. If the documents McKinnon claims to have seen were genuine, Solar Warden would be a secret space fleet run by the US Navy Network and Space Operations Command (NNSO). The fleet would have multiple spacecraft staffed by military personnel serving off-planet, and it would operate entirely outside any publicly disclosed government space program.

## McKinnon's Account

During his 2001–2002 hack of US military and NASA systems, [[Gary McKinnon]] claims to have found the following material on Solar Warden in Pentagon files:

- A program explicitly labeled "Solar Warden."
- A roster of approximately 300 military personnel listed as "non-terrestrial officers". McKinnon took the term to mean US military officers whose duty assignments were off Earth, aboard spacecraft operating beyond the planet, rather than aliens.
- References to material transfers between vessels.
- Two ship names with the prefix USSS (United States Space Ship, instead of the usual USS): the USSS *Hillenkoetter* and the USSS *LeMay*.

McKinnon emphasized that the files described these people as human military officers with off-planet assignments inside a structured naval command, and not as alien entities.

## Context and Corroborating Claims

[[Donna Hare]], a former NASA contractor, separately alleged that NASA systematically airbrushed UAPs out of high-resolution satellite imagery at Building 8 of Johnson Space Center. That imagery would overlap with any program of space-based operations. [[Chris Mellon]], a former senior intelligence official, has said that classified 4K satellite footage of UAPs exists but has been suppressed. His account is consistent with a broader infrastructure of classified observation and operations in space.

Researchers who believe that part of the UAP phenomenon is witnesses seeing classified human-operated spacecraft and mistaking them for non-human craft occasionally cite the program name. Others cite Solar Warden as evidence of an advanced human space program developed in parallel to NASA's publicly known activities.

## Status

Solar Warden has never been confirmed or acknowledged by any US government agency. McKinnon has consistently maintained the accuracy of his account. The hack itself is confirmed, which supports his credibility: US prosecutors verified that it was real and extensive. The specific contents of the files he claims to have read, however, cannot be independently verified.
