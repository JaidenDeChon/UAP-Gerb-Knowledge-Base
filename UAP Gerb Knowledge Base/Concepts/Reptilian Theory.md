The Reptilian Theory is a conspiracy theory first set out in full by the British author [[David Icke]] in his 1999 book *The Big Secret*. It holds that a race of shape-shifting reptilian extraterrestrials from the [[Alpha Draconis]] star system takes human form and secretly controls human civilization from within. Icke claims that prominent political dynasties, royal families, and corporate and media elites either belong to a reptilian bloodline or are under direct reptilian control.

## Core Claims

According to Icke, the reptilians, whom he sometimes calls "Archons" or "Anunnaki", have manipulated human civilization for thousands of years through:
- Genetic engineering and interbreeding to create human-reptilian bloodlines that hold power
- Control of banking systems, major governments, and media organizations
- Management of human consciousness through manipulation of low-vibrational emotional states such as fear
- Maintenance of underground bases from which they operate covertly

Icke names many historical and present-day figures as members of the reptilian bloodline, among them members of the British Royal Family, the Bush presidential dynasty, and prominent financial families. As evidence, he points to videos in which politicians' eyes seem to blink sideways or change color. These effects are easily explained as video compression artifacts.

## Criticism

Mainstream researchers, political scientists, and historians reject the Reptilian Theory. Watchdog organizations, including the Anti-Defamation League, have documented that parts of Icke's theory recast anti-Semitic conspiracy tropes as a story about extraterrestrials, although Icke denies this. The Denver International Airport is noted for having taken up the reptilian conspiracy as a theme in its public art and marketing, which shows how far the theory has spread in popular culture even though there is no evidence for it.

Serious UAP researchers regard the Reptilian Theory as harmful to the field's credibility. Gerb dismisses it outright as a baseless distraction.
