The Universal Civilization Development Datum (UCDD) is a proposed replacement for the [[Kardashev Scale]]. It appears on a backup slide to retired U.S. Army Colonel [[Karl Nell]]'s November 2023 [[SOL Foundation Symposium]] presentation. The slide was not shown in the recorded talk; analyst [[Matthew Pines]] shared it publicly in February 2024. The Kardashev scale ranks civilizations only by energy consumption, as planetary, stellar or galactic. The UCDD instead proposes a framework of several variables that assesses civilizations on three dimensions: what they *are*, what they *know*, and who they *are*. It is meant as a tool for forecasting the motives and technological capabilities of non-human intelligences (NHIs) as part of the UAP disclosure effort.

## Framework

The three dimensions are meant to capture attributes that measures of energy consumption ignore:

- What a civilization is: its physical nature, substrate and mode of existence, whether biological, post-biological, energy-based or otherwise.
- What a civilization knows: its accumulated scientific and technological knowledge, including mastery of physics, spacetime manipulation and material engineering.
- Who a civilization is: its values, motivations, ethical orientation and stance toward other civilizations.

The proposed end states, the highest levels of development on the scale, include spacetime manipulation, understanding the nature of reality itself, and immortality or physical transcendence. They describe a path toward capabilities that are post-material, a step beyond the merely post-industrial.

## Motivation

The slide starts from the premise that any meaningful policy response to UAP disclosure needs a way to reason about what NHI civilizations might want, what they can do, and how they might relate to humanity. Because the Kardashev scale looks only at energy, it implies that a Type III civilization (galactic-scale energy use) is necessarily the most dangerous or powerful, yet it says nothing about motivations or values. The UCDD is designed to let analysts reason about NHI entities across a wider set of attributes, which would support more calibrated risk assessment and diplomatic or scientific strategy.

The slide also notes that, given the diversity of UAP sightings, it is not implausible that many civilizations are interacting with Earth at the same time. A single sliding scale cannot express that. (Nell's separate call to engineer materials from 339 isotopes rather than 118 elements comes from the lines of effort in his campaign plan, not from this slide.)

## Context

The UCDD was presented as backup-slide material at the [[Sol Foundation]] Symposium. Nell's main presentation covered the [[UAP Campaign Plan - Way Forward]], the legislative significance of the Schumer Amendment, and a three-category [[UAP Taxonomy]] that sorts UAP into physical, psychophysical and metaphysical hypotheses. The framework was not published as a formal academic paper at the time of the symposium. Instead it circulated through the UAP disclosure community after the event.
