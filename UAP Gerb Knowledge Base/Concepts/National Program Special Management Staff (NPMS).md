The National Program Special Management Staff (NPMS) is a staff function that [[Luis Elizondo]] has described in his own public biography and in interviews. By his account, it operated under the Under Secretary of Defense for Intelligence and Security (USD I&S) and coordinated access and communication between National Security Council special access programs and the Department of Defense and intelligence community. Elizondo has stated that he was NPMS director from about 2013 to 2017, before he left government service to join [[To The Stars Academy]]. The documentation of NPMS as a formal, named DoD office comes mainly from Elizondo's own accounts and from UAP-research commentary, not from official government organizational charts or directives.

## Alleged Significance

UAP Gerb's *Special Access Required Vol.2* gives a lot of weight to Elizondo's self-described NPMS directorship. The video describes the position as "usually the most cleared person in the entire DoD besides the secretary of defense themselves", and in Gerb's account it carries more clearance than the director of the DoD Special Access Program Central Office (SAPCO). Gerb argues that Elizondo's tenure in this role, reporting under [[James Clapper]]'s former USD(I&S) office, shows that Elizondo had far greater access to National Security Council-controlled Special Access Programs than his public [[AATIP (Advanced Aerospace Threat Identification Program)|ATIP]]-era persona has acknowledged. Gerb cites the NPMS role as central evidence for the video's broader skepticism toward Elizondo's public account of his own history within the UFO legacy program apparatus.
