A set of alleged classified government documents related to UFO/UAP crash retrievals and secret committees, including references to the [[Majestic 12]] group. Significant drops of MJ-12 documents were received by researcher Tim Cooper in the 1980s. Many documents within the set are considered forgeries, though some researchers believe certain aspects or individual documents may be legitimate.
