A Moondust Officer is a military officer role in [[Project Moon Dust]], the classified USAF crash retrieval and UFO investigation program. Moondust Officers served in the [[USAF 1127th Field Activities Group]]. They were responsible for field collection operations, for coordination with intelligence agencies, and for handling classified UAP-related documents. The term is tied in particular to documents that researcher [[Tim Cooper]] received through anonymous mailbox drops. This suggests that Moondust Officers held classified material about the program's UFO collection activities and occasionally leaked it.
