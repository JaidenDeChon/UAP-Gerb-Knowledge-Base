A Moondust Officer is a military officer role associated with [[Project Moondust]], the classified USAF crash retrieval and UFO investigation program. Moondust Officers served within the [[USAF 1127th Field Activities Group]] and were responsible for field collection operations, coordination with intelligence agencies, and handling classified UAP-related documents. The term is specifically linked to documents delivered to researcher [[Tim Cooper]] via anonymous mailbox drops, suggesting Moondust Officers possessed and occasionally leaked classified materials related to the program's UFO collection activities.
