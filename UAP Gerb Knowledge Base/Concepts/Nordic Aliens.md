The Nordic aliens, or Nordics, are a supposed race of human-looking extraterrestrials who resemble Scandinavians: taller than most people, at about 6 to 7 feet, with fair skin, blue eyes and long blond hair. They are usually said to come from the Pleiades star cluster. After the [[Grays]], they are one of the best-known alien archetypes in UFO lore.

## In contactee accounts

The Nordic archetype grew out of the contactee movement of the 1950s, when many people claimed meetings with visitors of this kind. In those accounts the Nordics are benevolent, almost magical beings who care about humanity and the world's prosperity. They communicate by telepathy and have a calm, youthful, affectionate and kind manner. They are said to belong to a [[Galactic Federation]] and to travel the cosmos in craft ranging from spherical to cigar-shaped.

## In abduction accounts

Nordic-type beings also appear in some abduction reports alongside the Grays. The best known is [[Travis Walton]]'s 1975 account, dramatized in the film *Fire in the Sky*: Walton described several types of being aboard the craft, among them Grays and a race of tall, human-looking figures matching the Nordic description.

## Gerb's view

Covering the entry in the first episode of his iceberg series, Gerb says he does not know whether the Nordics are real. He prefers not to dwell on abduction stories or on sorting extraterrestrial races. His starting point is that something of non-human intelligence is in the sky, and he says there is a great deal to establish before deciding what these things are, or whether they are extraterrestrial at all.
