The **craft donation theory** is the idea that some non-human craft are deliberately left for humans to recover, landing or crashing so that their technology can be studied. Within UAP research the recovered objects are sometimes called "donation craft."

## In the Infographic Show Account

The [[Infographic Show Whistleblower]] says many craft "just landed and didn't take off, like whatever was doing this wanted us to recover the craft." According to him, the program strongly supported this theory, perhaps because gifting technology this way would not massively disrupt human development. He also gives the program's "more disturbing" alternative: "maybe they just left these crafts lying around because they were easy to build." The theory sits alongside the program's [[Fabrication Hypothesis]], that the craft and their crews are manufactured.

## Related Accounts

UAP Gerb connects the idea to [[David Grusch]]'s description of non-human vehicles "that have either landed or crashed," and to [[Eric Taber]]'s account to AARO of an egg-shaped craft found untouched in the desert and kept at [[Area 51]] (see [[Egg-shaped UAP]]).
