The Anunnaki (also spelled Annunaki or Anunaki) are the principal deities of the ancient Mesopotamian religious pantheons of the Sumerians, Akkadians, Assyrians, and Babylonians. Mainstream scholars treat them as a group of major gods, and their name translates roughly to "those of heavenly descent" or "princely offspring." In fringe writing and popular culture, the Anunnaki are the central subject of [[Zecharia Sitchin]]'s highly contested extraterrestrial reading of ancient Sumerian texts, which recasts them as physical alien beings who genetically engineered modern humans.

## Academic Understanding

In Mesopotamian religion, the Anunnaki were a pantheon of fifty or more deities, among them major figures such as Enlil, Enki, Anu, and Inanna. They governed domains including the sky, earth, storms, water, and the underworld. Their myths survive in thousands of clay tablet inscriptions excavated at sites including Ur, Nippur, and Nineveh. These make up the oldest substantial body of written religious literature in human history, and they include narratives that parallel later flood myths, notably the Epic of Gilgamesh.

## Sitchin's Extraterrestrial Interpretation

[[Zecharia Sitchin]] claimed in his 1976 book *The 12th Planet* and later works that his translations of Sumerian cuneiform tablets showed the Anunnaki to be real, physical extraterrestrial beings. In his account they came from an undiscovered planet called [[Nibiru]], which follows an extremely elongated elliptical orbit around the Sun. According to Sitchin:

- The Anunnaki arrived on Earth approximately 450,000–500,000 years ago.
- They set up operations on Earth mainly to extract gold.
- Unable to sustain their mining workforce, they genetically engineered *Homo sapiens* as a species of slave labor, combining their own DNA with that of existing *Homo erectus* populations.
- Catastrophic flooding, caused by the collapse of Antarctic glaciers, forced the Anunnaki to leave Earth. That departure is the basis for flood myths around the world, including the Biblical account of Noah.

Academic Assyriologists and Sumerian linguists have rejected Sitchin's translations as fundamentally inaccurate. His interpretation has no basis in peer-reviewed philology and is not recognized as a legitimate alternative reading of the texts.

## Connection to Other Theories

[[David Icke]] has claimed that the Anunnaki are the same entities as his proposed [[Reptilian]] extraterrestrial controlling class, a position Sitchin himself rejected. The Anunnaki mythology also overlaps with alternative interpretations of human genetic origins and ancient religion, and with the hypothesis that the UAP phenomenon has been present in human experience for millennia.
