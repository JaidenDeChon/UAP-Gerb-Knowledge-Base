The Eisenhower Briefing Document (EBD) is the first and best known of the [[MJ-12 Documents]]: a purported Top Secret MAJIC Eyes Only briefing dated 18 November 1952, prepared for President-elect [[Dwight D. Eisenhower]] with [[Roscoe H. Hillenkoetter]] named as briefing officer. It describes [[Majestic 12]] as a top secret research and development and intelligence operation reporting only to the President. According to the document, the group was set up by a classified executive order of [[Harry Truman]] on 24 September 1947, on the recommendation of [[Vannevar Bush]] and [[James Forrestal]].

## Leak and reception

Television producer [[Jaime Shandera]] received the document on undeveloped 35 mm film in December 1984, the start of a series of leaks that ran until 1999. Copies were passed to the FBI, which is responsible for leaks of classified material. The FBI circulated it to other agencies, which said they had not lost it, and then declared it bogus. [[Ryan S. Wood]] notes that later copies surfaced from other sources with different marginalia and hole punching.

## Contents

The document lists the twelve MJ-12 members, including General Walter B. Smith as [[Walter Bedell Smith|Smith]]'s replacement for the late Forrestal on 1 August 1950. It recounts the 1947 saucer sightings, then the July 1947 [[Roswell Crash]], in which [[Nathan Twining]] and Bush directed the recovery of craft and bodies and [[Detlev Bronk]] performed autopsies and coined the term "EBE". A team including [[Donald Menzel]] concluded that the craft was non-human and probably from a distant solar system. It says [[Project Sign]], Grudge and [[Project Blue Book]] had only limited contact with MJ-12, and it describes a December 1950 crash near the Mexico–Texas border between El Indio and Guerrero involving the [[Atomic Energy Commission]] and [[Sandia National Laboratories]] (see [[1950 El Indio UFO Crash]]). It ends by citing Contingency Plan MJ-1949-04P/78 for public disclosure, a plan the [[Special Operations Manual (SOM 1-01)]] also references.

## Authentication

[[Stanton Friedman]] made the EBD the centre of his book *Top Secret/Majic*. In 1989 he found, in Office of the Secretary of Defense files at the National Archives, several 18 November 1952 briefings for Eisenhower; none mentions MJ-12, but they confirm the Truman administration was briefing him that day. He also established that Hillenkoetter was in New York in late 1952 as head of the Third Naval District, answering critics who placed him in Korea. The video notes that Truman's appointment records show meetings with Forrestal and Bush on 24 September 1947 and with Smith on 1 August 1950, the dates the document gives. Ryan and Robert Wood rate the EBD among the documents with the highest authenticity.
