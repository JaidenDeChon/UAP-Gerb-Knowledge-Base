Immaculate Constellation (IMCON) is allegedly a US Unacknowledged Special Access Program (USAP), set up in 2017 to watch non-human craft and technologies derived from them, including alleged alien reproduction vehicles (ARVs), that operate in or near the airspace of the US and its allies. The program was first described publicly in the [[Immaculate Constellation Report]], an unsigned whistleblower report that Gerb attributes to [[Matt Brown|Matthew Brown]] and that was entered into the record of a House Oversight hearing on 13 November 2024. Gerb argues that [[NRO Sentient]] is an important intelligence source behind IMCON's data collection, though the report does not name it. IMCON is described as a reactive surveillance program that monitors phenomena the government already knows about. It is separate from the much older UFO legacy retrieval programs, which have allegedly operated since at least 1947.

> [!edit] Corrected 16 October 2026
> Earlier versions said Brown's report named NRO Sentient as an important intelligence source for IMCON. The report, as entered into the hearing record, mentions neither Sentient nor the NRO; the link is Gerb's argument in his NRO video.

IMCON was set up shortly after the core buildout of NRO Sentient in 2016. UAP researchers treat this timing as no coincidence and take it to suggest that Sentient's analytical capabilities made a dedicated surveillance mission against non-human craft possible. According to the report, IMCON draws on "tasked and untasked U.S. military-intelligence resources" in low Earth orbit, the upper atmosphere, aviation altitudes and maritime environments. Gerb believes these include [[NRO (National Reconnaissance Office)]] satellite constellations and space-based OPIR (Overhead Persistent Infrared) systems through the JOPC (Joint Overhead Persistent Infrared Center). The video gives one example of alleged IMCON-type collection: a space-based OPIR recording, over Indo-Pacific Command, of a saucer-shaped craft approximately 210–420 feet in diameter that reversed direction when it apparently detected it was being watched. IMCON is a collection and surveillance program that runs on top of a separate legacy program infrastructure, and it is explicitly not described as a UFO recovery program.

> [!edit] Attribution clarified 16 October 2026
> Earlier versions said IMCON "reportedly" draws on tasked and non-tasked NRO satellite constellations and on OPIR through the JOPC. The report speaks only of tasked and untasked US military-intelligence resources; the NRO and JOPC are Gerb's inference.

## Triangular ARV Designation

The Immaculate Constellation report was released to Congress and the American public on November 13, 2024, during the fall UAP hearings. It described a large equilateral triangle, captured by Indo-Pacific Command (INDOPACOM), that appeared directly over intelligence-collecting vessels. The triangle hovered and rotated slowly 500 to 1,000 meters above the ocean, with three bright points visible at each bottom corner. Triangles are among the rarest UAP configurations in the IMCON report, and the USAP has confirmed at least some of them to be ARV (alien reproduction vehicles). Program members used the same term for the flux liner saucer that [[Brad Sorenson]] saw at the 1988 Norton Air Force Base show. Representative Eric Burlison confirmed to Gerb that he had heard the term ARV before from within the UFO community.

> [!edit] Corrected 16 October 2026
> Earlier versions said the report's historical data went back to 2009, as Gerb says in his black-triangle video. The report gives no date for the triangle footage; its only date range is the 1991 to 2022 span of its HUMINT dataset.

## Possible Connection to MITRE's Reverse-Engineering Portfolio

Gerb's investigation into [[MITRE Corporation|MITRE]] speculatively links IMCON's 2017 establishment to MITRE's alleged loss of control over a Navy-administered UFO technology reverse-engineering program that same year. It theorizes that [[James Clapper]] may have sought to fold MITRE's reverse-engineered assets into the National Security Council's broader crash-retrieval and surveillance portfolio for better situational awareness. Gerb calls this connection unconfirmed, but consistent with IMCON's timing relative to both MITRE's alleged 2017 leadership transition and the roughly contemporaneous 2010–2011 origins of [[NRO Sentient]].
