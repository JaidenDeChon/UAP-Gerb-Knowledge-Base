Non-Human Intelligence (NHI) is a broad term used in contemporary UAP discussion to describe potentially intelligent actors that are not human, regardless of origin. The term is intentionally more inclusive than "extraterrestrial," allowing for hypotheses that include off-world, interdimensional, cryptoterrestrial, or other non-anthropogenic sources.

## Terminology and Scope

The NHI label gained prominence because it separates the intelligence question from a single origin model. Instead of assuming visitors from another planet, it functions as a neutral descriptor for observed or reported phenomena interpreted as controlled, intentional, and technologically advanced.

## Use in Disclosure-Era Testimony

Public whistleblower testimony and media reporting frequently use NHI when discussing alleged craft retrievals, biological claims, and advanced aerospace capabilities. The wording is often chosen to avoid overcommitting to specific biological or cosmological conclusions.

In [[Video - The 1933 Magenta, Italy UFO Crash]], statements by [[David Grusch]] and [[Luis Elizondo]] are presented as support for the claim that an NHI-associated craft was recovered in Italy and later transferred to U.S. control.

## Distinction from Related Terms

- "Extraterrestrial" implies non-Earth origin.
- "Alien" is colloquial and less precise.
- "NHI" is analytic and source-agnostic.

Because the term carries fewer assumptions, it is now standard in many policy and investigative discussions related to UAP.
