The "Anthill" is the informal name UFO researchers use for an alleged network of deep underground tunnels and facilities beneath the Northrop Tehachapi Ranch radar cross-section (RCS) test facility in Kern County, California. The name comes from the large body of testimony suggesting that the underground complex is so extensive, reportedly with up to 42 levels, that its size and interconnected layout resemble an anthill. The Anthill is considered the central underground hub of the broader [[Antelope Valley]] UAP legacy program complex.

## Physical Description from Witness Testimony

Firsthand accounts, compiled mainly by researcher [[Bill Hamilton]], describe the Anthill as follows:

- A local resident reported that the ground near Tehachapi would open "like a missile silo" and that a flying saucer took off from the opening. Edwards AFB OSI personnel silenced the witness.
- A contractor, "Chuck", said he had dug the underground tunnels beneath the Anthill himself. He reported round doorways with few doors, and red and green lights used for identification and entry.
- A source who claimed to have worked on "Project Star Talk" described a large underground building that used lasers "for the purpose of constructing systems to bring in UFOs," and mentioned scalar technology consistent with what [[Steven Greer]] later discussed publicly as a way of downing UAP.
- The same Star Talk informant claimed he had also worked at the McDonald Douglas STIF, where he saw a UAP held in an underground hangar.
- An informant called "Gober" claimed to work with alien technology at Tehachapi, Helendale, and the Nevada Test and Training Range, and said it "deeply frightened him." According to Gober, on-site personnel rotate every 14–16 days, living and working on site, and a command center under Haystack Butte connects to other facilities.
- Multiple informants described ground electric vehicles and maglev train systems within the underground complex.

## Command Structure and Geography

In various accounts, [[Bill Hamilton]] and independent researchers including [[Steven Greer]] and forensic artist [[Bill McDonald]] place the Anthill command center beneath Haystack Butte at Edwards Air Force Base, about 40 miles east of Tehachapi. This suggests the Anthill is one node in a larger command-and-control structure anchored at Edwards, rather than an isolated facility.

Some sources cite an estimated 42 underground levels. That would make the Anthill among the deepest alleged underground facilities in the US, deeper even than the Deep Underground Command Center (DUCC) proposed below the Pentagon at 3,500 feet. [[Steven Greer]] has claimed personal knowledge of the Anthill's entrance. He has also claimed that he was flown over RCS facilities in the Antelope Valley, where he saw what appeared to be trough-like structures from which craft emerge vertically.

## Radar Cross-Section Facility Cover

The Tehachapi Ranch facility on the surface is an acknowledged Northrop Grumman radar cross-section (RCS) test range. RCS facilities have large runways, pylons raised from underground on concrete diamonds, and underground storage for test equipment, all of which give natural cover for underground construction. Much of the Cold War RCS facility was built underground specifically to hide pylon equipment and models from Soviet spy satellites. This existing underground infrastructure at RCS sites is cited as a possible ready route to deeper underground expansion.
