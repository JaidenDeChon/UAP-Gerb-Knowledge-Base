EMP-like weaponry is the term for the alleged electromagnetic pulse or directed energy weapons that the Black Group has reportedly developed to target, disrupt, and disable unidentified anomalous phenomena (UAP) so that the craft can be retrieved after they crash. According to an anonymous insider who spoke to whistleblower [[Michael Herrera]], these weapons have moved the effort from passively observing UAP to actively capturing them.

## Capabilities

In the insider's account, the weapons are designed or tuned to affect non-human intelligence craft and their propulsion and control systems. They can cause temporary or permanent malfunctions in a UAP's flight systems and force the craft to land or crash, which makes it recoverable. This lets the Black Group's crash retrieval program acquire intact or semi-intact craft and their occupants.

## Operational Use

### Luring Operations

The insider claimed that crash retrieval programs have developed techniques to lure UAP to specific locations where the EMP-like weapons can be deployed effectively. This suggests that the programs know what attracts UAP (possibly nuclear facilities, weapons testing, or electromagnetic signatures), that they can set up bait scenarios to draw craft into range, and that they run coordinated operations combining surveillance, luring, and weapon deployment.

### Active Shootdowns

The insider allegedly told Herrera that "non-human intelligence craft are being shot down in desert locations worldwide," which indicates that the weapons are used around the world and not only on U.S. territory. It also indicates that desert locations are preferred, because they have few witnesses and make recovery easier, and that the program is actively shooting craft down instead of only responding to natural crashes.

## Strategic Implications

### Provocation Risk

The insider warned that shooting down non-human intelligence (NHI) craft "risks provocation of an advanced species", and acknowledged that actively targeting craft from a potentially superior intelligence could trigger retaliation or conflict. This raises questions about whether civilian and military leadership know these operations are happening, what risk assessment is used to justify continuing the shootdowns, and what could follow if the targeted intelligence responds defensively.

### Technological Advantage

If EMP-like weapons can reliably disable UAP, it suggests an understanding of weaknesses in UAP propulsion or control systems, and that electromagnetic disruption affects even highly advanced technology. It also suggests that the reverse engineering program has advanced far enough to turn recovered technology into a weapon against its creators.

## Historical Context

Earlier allegations have also described directed energy weapons aimed at UAP:
- Researcher [[Philip J. Corso]] claimed that weapons were developed during the Cold War to intercept UFOs.
- Multiple witnesses have described craft behaving erratically near military installations before crashing.
- The [[1953 Kingman, Arizona Crash Retrieval|1953 Kingman Arizona UFO crash]] and other incidents allegedly involved radar or signal interference that caused the craft to crash.

The insider's claims are a more explicit and operational version of these long-standing allegations. In this account, crash retrievals are orchestrated by bringing craft down with weapons instead of waiting for chance crashes.

## Credibility Assessment

The claim about EMP-like weaponry comes from what the anonymous insider told Michael Herrera, and it remains unverified. If true, it would be an act of war against non-human intelligences and a violation of any diplomatic or tacit agreements with NHI, and it could pose an existential risk if retaliation occurs.

No documentary evidence, weapons specifications, or independent corroboration has emerged. The claim rests on insider testimony alone and should be treated with skepticism, weighed alongside the insider's other disclosures.
