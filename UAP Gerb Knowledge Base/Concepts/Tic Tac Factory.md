The "Tic Tac Factory" is the nickname UAP Gerb uses for an alleged enormous, permanent or semi-permanent [[Unidentified Submerged Object (USO)|unidentified submerged object]] (USO) stationed in the Atlantic Ocean near the Bahamas, which is monitored, tracked, and tasked for intercept under a decades-old U.S. Navy Waived Unacknowledged Special Access Program alleged to be managed by [[MITRE Corporation|MITRE]]. The object is described as roughly the size of ten city blocks and is alleged to deploy and recover smaller "subordinate" vehicles — tic-tac-shaped, cylindrical, discoid, and irregular "blobby" craft — capable of transitioning between underwater, surface, and airborne flight (see [[Trans-Medium Vehicle]]).

## Description and Location

Engineers on the alleged program describe the object as appearing on sensors as an opaque blob or cloud, a signature they are reportedly uncertain represents the object's true physical form rather than a product of active sensor management or cloaking capability. The object is said to maintain a largely fixed presence east of the Bahamas, though it is believed capable of traveling to other locations and depths. UAP Gerb identifies the likely operating area as the Blake–Bahama Basin, where the Atlantic floor drops to roughly 4,000 meters, near the "Tongue of the Ocean" — a submarine trench reaching approximately 2,000 meters that is also home to the U.S. Navy's [[Atlantic Undersea Test and Evaluation Center]] (AUTEC), a major range and test facility base for submarine, weapons, and sonar research. UAP Gerb argues this immediate geographic overlap with AUTEC is unlikely to be coincidental, and connects it to congressional statements by Representative Tim Burchett referencing "deep underwater USO bases," while noting Burchett's terminology may not precisely match what UAP Gerb alleges is actually there.

## Alleged Behavior

The Tic Tac Factory is alleged to deploy and recover subordinate craft that travel in a transmedium capacity, sometimes returning to the larger object. UAP Gerb reports that engineers on the program describe the vessel as largely non-hostile ("more or less harmless") toward observers, and states it does not have direct knowledge of whether the object or its subordinate craft are crewed.

## Corroborating and Related Testimony

- **2004 USS Nimitz encounter**: UAP Gerb connects the Tic Tac Factory concept to Commander [[David Fravor]]'s well-documented 2004 encounter off the coast of San Diego, in which a tic-tac-shaped object was observed above churning ocean water — interpreted by some observers as evidence of a larger submerged object disturbing the surface from below.
- **Senior Chief Wiggins testimony**: UAP Gerb cites testimony from a Navy senior chief identified as Wiggins describing a self-luminous tic-tac-shaped object emerging from the ocean and linking up with three similar objects before a synchronized, near-instantaneous departure with no sonic boom, exhaust plume, or visible control surfaces.
- **2023 anonymous 4chan account**: In April 2023, an anonymous individual posted to 4chan claiming direct knowledge of USOs, describing UFOs as primarily unmanned drones built to specification each time they are deployed by a "mobile construction facility that hides in the ocean." UAP Gerb states it puts significant stock in aspects of this testimony because of its own independent knowledge of MITRE's tracking program, while explicitly disagreeing with several of the poster's specific claims — including that the facility is hostile and destroys anything approaching it, that shootdown attempts against its subordinate craft have failed, and that the craft are powered by "element 115." The poster, in response to a question about a gap in their knowledge, stated they had "moved on from the project because of new management that didn't trust the older proven methods" — a detail UAP Gerb connects to its broader thesis of a 2011 security clampdown across MITRE's legacy programs.

## Program Administration

Per UAP Gerb, the Tic Tac Factory monitoring program is administered similarly to MITRE's Roswell-derived reverse-engineering program (see [[MITRE Corporation]]): out of MITRE's "hidden" Special Security Office at its McLean, Virginia campus, with the Navy — via the [[Office of Naval Intelligence]] and [[Naval Air Systems Command]] — as the primary government mission partner. MITRE is alleged to hold an extensive volume of paper and electronic signals, imagery, and measurement-and-signature intelligence records on the object, subject to the same alleged periodic records-destruction practices UAP Gerb attributes to MITRE's broader legacy program activity.
