The "Tic Tac Factory" is Gerb's nickname for an alleged enormous [[Unidentified Submerged Object (USO)|unidentified submerged object]] (USO) that sits, permanently or semi-permanently, in the Atlantic Ocean near the Bahamas. Under a decades-old U.S. Navy Waived Unacknowledged Special Access Program, allegedly managed by [[MITRE Corporation|MITRE]], the object is monitored and tracked, and intercepts are tasked against it. It is described as roughly the size of ten city blocks. It allegedly deploys and recovers smaller "subordinate" vehicles, including tic-tac-shaped, cylindrical, discoid and irregular "blobby" craft, which can move between underwater, surface and airborne flight (see [[Trans-Medium Vehicle]]).

## Description and Location

Engineers on the alleged program describe the object as an opaque blob or cloud on their sensors. They are reportedly unsure whether this signature shows the object's true physical form or is a product of active sensor management or cloaking. The object is said to stay largely in one place east of the Bahamas, though it is believed able to travel to other locations and depths. Gerb identifies the likely operating area as the Blake–Bahama Basin, where the Atlantic floor drops to roughly 4,000 meters. This is near the "Tongue of the Ocean", a submarine trench about 2,000 meters deep that also holds the U.S. Navy's [[Atlantic Undersea Test and Evaluation Center]] (AUTEC), a major range and test base for submarine, weapons and sonar research. Gerb argues that this direct overlap with AUTEC is unlikely to be a coincidence. He connects it to congressional statements by Representative Tim Burchett about "deep underwater USO bases," while noting that Burchett's wording may not exactly match what Gerb alleges is there.

## Alleged Behavior

The Tic Tac Factory allegedly deploys and recovers subordinate craft that travel between water and air, and these craft sometimes return to the larger object. Gerb reports that engineers on the program describe the vessel as largely non-hostile ("more or less harmless") toward observers. He states that he has no direct knowledge of whether the object or its subordinate craft are crewed.

## Corroborating and Related Testimony

- 2004 USS Nimitz encounter: Gerb connects the Tic Tac Factory to Commander [[David Fravor]]'s well-documented 2004 encounter off the coast of San Diego. In that encounter a tic-tac-shaped object was seen above churning ocean water, which some observers take as evidence of a larger submerged object disturbing the surface from below.
- Senior Chief Wiggins testimony: Gerb cites testimony from a Navy senior chief identified as Wiggins. Wiggins describes a self-luminous tic-tac-shaped object rising from the ocean and joining three similar objects, after which all of them left together almost instantly, with no sonic boom, exhaust plume or visible control surfaces.
- 2023 anonymous 4chan account: In April 2023, an anonymous poster on 4chan claimed direct knowledge of USOs. The poster described UFOs as mostly unmanned drones, built to specification for each deployment by a "mobile construction facility that hides in the ocean." Gerb states that he puts significant stock in parts of this account because of his own independent knowledge of MITRE's tracking program. He explicitly disagrees with several of the poster's specific claims: that the facility is hostile and destroys anything that approaches it, that attempts to shoot down its subordinate craft have failed, and that the craft are powered by "element 115." Asked about a gap in their knowledge, the poster said they had "moved on from the project because of new management that didn't trust the older proven methods". Gerb connects this detail to his broader thesis of a 2011 security clampdown across MITRE's legacy programs.

## Program Administration

According to Gerb, the Tic Tac Factory monitoring program is run much like MITRE's Roswell-derived reverse-engineering program (see [[MITRE Corporation]]). It is administered out of MITRE's "hidden" Special Security Office at its McLean, Virginia campus, and the Navy, through the [[Office of Naval Intelligence]] and [[Naval Air Systems Command]], is the primary government mission partner. MITRE allegedly holds a large volume of paper and electronic records on the object, covering signals, imagery, and measurement-and-signature intelligence. These records are subject to the same alleged periodic destruction that Gerb attributes to MITRE's wider legacy program activity.
