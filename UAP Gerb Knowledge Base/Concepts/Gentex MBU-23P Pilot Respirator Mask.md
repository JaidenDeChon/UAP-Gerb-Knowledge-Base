The Gentex MBU-23/P is a standard-issue military aircraft oxygen mask used by U.S. Air Force and Navy pilots flying fighter jets and other tactical aircraft. It supplies breathable oxygen at altitude and has built-in communication systems. Its hard shell components and flexible rubber seals give it a distinctive profile.

## Role in Flyby Footage Analysis

Reflections in the [[Flyby Footage]], a [[Second-Generation Recording]] filmed from a computer screen, have been analyzed as possibly showing a Gentex MBU-23/P pilot respirator mask reflected in the aircraft's cockpit glass. If that identification is accurate, it would strongly support the conclusion that the original footage was shot from inside a military jet cockpit by a mounted camera filming through the canopy glass, with the pilot's oxygen mask creating a partial reflection in the frame.

Such a reflection would be consistent with the footage's apparent origin on an F-18 platform, specifically the [[NASA]] F-18A Chase aircraft variant, which is known to have cameras mounted inside the cockpit behind the pilot seat. A camera in that position would naturally pick up reflections of cockpit equipment, including the pilot's mask, in the canopy glass when filming outside the aircraft.

## Significance for Authentication

If verified, reflections of military-specific equipment in the footage would substantially strengthen the case for its authenticity, because they would show that the recording came from a real military cockpit rather than a civilian simulation or a CGI recreation. The Gentex MBU-23/P's distinctive shape, and the way it would reflect in curved aircraft glass under particular lighting, could serve as forensic markers. The low quality of the second-generation recording, however, limits how confidently such identifications can be made.
