The Special Operations Manual, designated SOM 1-01 and titled *Extraterrestrial Entities and Technology, Recovery and Disposal*, is one of the [[MJ-12 Documents]]: an alleged 1954 field manual, classified Top Secret MAJIC Eyes Only, written for "Majestic 12 units" and "authorized personnel" of the [[Majestic 12]] group. It is said to describe MJ-12's purpose, the craft and beings it recovered, and how recovery teams were to secure, ship and conceal them. Researchers [[Ryan S. Wood]] and [[Robert Wood]] rate it among the most authentic of the Majestic documents, and [[Stanton Friedman]] called it "very likely genuine".

## Provenance

The manual reached aviation writer [[Don Berliner]] anonymously in 1994 as a roll of undeveloped 35 mm Tri-X film in a small green box. Berliner and Robert Wood developed it themselves. The film was dated to 1954, and the photographed pages show an original binder stamped Top Secret MAJIC Eyes Only. Its document-control page gives [[Kirtland Air Force Base, New Mexico|Kirtland Air Force Base]], Unit KB-88, Building 21, and records changes between 1954 and 1958 by MJ-01 and MJ-04, initialled "EWL" and "JRT". Navy yeoman Dale Bailey alleges he saw the manual in 1976 while helping an admiral destroy classified UFO material.

## Contents

According to the video's reading of the manual:

- **Purpose.** MJ-12 treats UFOs as a matter of the highest national security; the group was established on 24 September 1947 on the recommendation of [[Vannevar Bush]] and [[James Forrestal]]. Its goals are recovery of all craft, devices and entities of non-terrestrial origin, special recovery teams, secure facilities at secret US locations, covert operations with the [[CIA]] at home and abroad, and absolute secrecy. As of 1954, MJ-12 did not regard the visitors as a threat.
- **Craft.** Four configurations: elliptical or disc-shaped (50–300 ft across, with a dome and three sets of landing gear); cigar-shaped (up to 2,000 ft long, very rare, over 7,000 mph, not seen in the lower atmosphere); ovoid or "ice cream cone" shapes (30–40 ft long, travelling point down); and airfoil or triangular craft (longest side near 300 ft, described as new technology).
- **Entities.** Two types of extraterrestrial biological entity (EBE): Type 1, a humanoid of 5 ft to 5 ft 4 in with chalky yellow skin and four webbed, thumbless digits; and Type 2, a classic "grey" of 3 ft 5 in to 4 ft 2 in with wrap-around black eyes and no hair.
- **Materials.** Metallurgical analysis of debris from 1947–1953 crashes found materials unknown to terrestrial science, extremely strong for their weight, resembling aluminium foil or magnesium sheeting and bearing inscriptions, which matches [[Jesse Marcel]]'s Roswell description.
- **Procedures.** Press blackout (media denial, discrediting witnesses, deceptive statements), securing and cleaning a site, and a rule that any craft or device that appears functional goes to "Red Team". If public disclosure becomes necessary, teams are to invoke Contingency Plan MJ-1949-04P/78, which the [[Eisenhower Briefing Document]] also cites.
- **Classification table (Section 17).** Intact craft, devices, fragments and weapons go to [[Area 51]] S-4; media, recordings, maps and charts to Kirtland Building 21; supplies, provisions and deceased entities to the [[Blue Room]] at [[Wright-Patterson Air Force Base]]; living entities only to "OPNAV BBS-01". The manual states that "loss of EBE life is acceptable" during shipping.

## Authentication

A retired Government Printing Office supervisor examined the pages for the Woods. He found raised letter Zs, an artifact of hot-lead printing, and judged the fonts, indentation, dashes and the capitalised "First Aid" correct for the period. The Woods add other period details: "N.Mex" rather than "NM", "craft tape" rather than "duct tape", and "screw driver" as two words. Using Albuquerque phone books and personnel records, they found men with the initials EWL and JRT living on Perimeter Road inside Kirtland in the mid-1950s. They also report that the UFOB Identification Guide, missing from the manual's appendix but later released through FOIA, fits the table of contents' page numbers exactly once retyped to the manual's format.

Critics, including Jan Aldrich in 1996, object to what the manual leaves out, its use of "satellites" before Sputnik, a "Restricted" marking beside "Top Secret", and the listing of Area 51 in 1954. The Woods reply that restricted data under the [[Atomic Energy Act of 1954]] could carry any classification level, and they cite 1951 press reports of a large government concrete contract in "Area 1" as evidence of early construction. [[John B. Alexander]] has written that Robert Wood told him the manual was a 1954 disinformation plant aimed at Soviet intelligence, a claim Ryan Wood rejects.
