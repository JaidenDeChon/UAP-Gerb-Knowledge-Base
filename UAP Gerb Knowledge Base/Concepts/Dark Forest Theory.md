The Dark Forest Theory is a proposed resolution to the [[Fermi Paradox]]. It holds that extraterrestrial civilizations do not communicate or reveal their existence because the universe is like a dark forest full of hidden hunters, where any civilization that gives away its location risks being preyed on by more technologically advanced ones. In this view, the silence across the universe is a rational survival strategy and says nothing about how rare civilizations are. Every intelligent species knows that announcing itself could lead to its annihilation.

## Origin

The theory is most closely associated with Chinese science fiction author Liu Cixin, who made it a central idea of *The Dark Forest* (2008), the second volume of his *Remembrance of Earth's Past* trilogy. Liu's formulation rests on two axioms: (1) the survival instinct is fundamental to all life, and (2) resources in the universe are finite. From these he concludes that a civilization that meets another must in the end destroy it before being destroyed, because trust cannot be guaranteed and the chance that the other side is more capable cannot be ruled out. The logical result is that every civilization hides.

## Implications for SETI and UAP Discourse

The Dark Forest Theory affects how both [[SETI]] results and UAP behavior can be interpreted:

- The lack of detectable alien radio signals fits with civilizations deliberately suppressing their electromagnetic emissions.
- If non-human intelligences are visiting Earth, one of their interests may be judging whether humanity is a threat. They may also be following Dark Forest logic themselves and choosing concealment over open contact.
- Some UAP researchers speculate that the observed behavior of UAPs, such as strategic evasion, operational concealment and non-communication, fits a Dark Forest-type intelligence that has chosen not to reveal itself.

The theory complements the [[Fermi Paradox]] in two ways. It gives a logical explanation for the silence, and it offers a sobering framework for judging what open contact would mean.
