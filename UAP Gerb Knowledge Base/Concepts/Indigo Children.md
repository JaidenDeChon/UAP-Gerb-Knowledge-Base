Indigo Children is a New Age concept that [[Nancy Ann Tappe]] originated in the early 1970s. It describes a purported new type of child with extraordinary spiritual sensitivity, psychic abilities and non-conformist behavior. According to Tappe, these children can be identified by an indigo-colored aura, which people with synesthetic perceptual ability can see. The concept gained mainstream New Age traction in the 1990s. Some fringe theorists have invoked it as evidence of extraterrestrial genetic influence on humanity, though its connection to UAP research is tenuous.

## Origins

Tappe, who claimed to perceive human auras as colors, said she noticed a new aura color, indigo, appearing in children born from the late 1960s onward. She published her framework in *Understanding Your Life Through Color* (1982). Authors Lee Carroll and Jan Tober expanded the concept substantially and took it to a global audience with *The Indigo Children* (1998). Their book described these children as unusually resistant to authority, spiritually aware and empathic, and said they were often misdiagnosed with ADHD.

Carroll and Tober were skeptical of ADHD diagnoses. They suggested that many children labeled with the disorder were in fact Indigo Children, whose advanced spiritual nature made conventional schooling incompatible with their development. Mainstream psychology and medicine reject this position.

## Claimed Characteristics

Proponents describe Indigo Children as having some or all of the following traits:

- Heightened psychic or intuitive sensitivity
- Possible telekinetic or other paranormal abilities
- Deep empathy and emotional sensitivity
- Strong resistance to authority and rigid systems
- A sense of entitlement or "specialness" combined with deep purpose

## Claimed UAP Connection

Indigo Children usually turn up in UFO and alien iceberg discussions because of speculation that these children may be human-extraterrestrial hybrids, recipients of extraterrestrial genetic modification, or souls from advanced civilizations incarnated in human form. None of these claims is backed by credible evidence. In [[Videos/The Alien and UFO Obscure Oddities Iceberg (Level 2)/summary|The Alien and UFO Obscure Oddities Iceberg (Level 2)]], Gerb explicitly dismissed the Indigo Children entry as poorly connected to the UAP phenomenon.
