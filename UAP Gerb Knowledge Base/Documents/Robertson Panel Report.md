The Robertson Panel Report is the CIA's record of the [[Robertson Panel]], the scientific advisory panel on unidentified flying objects that met at CIA headquarters in mid-January 1953 under physicist [[Howard P. Robertson]]. Its cover dates the meetings January 14 to 18, 1953. The copy archived here is the "Durant Report", a memorandum of February 16, 1953, from Frederick C. Durant, one of the panel's associate members, to the CIA's Assistant Director for Scientific Intelligence. It summarizes the meetings, the evidence the panel was shown and the comments of its members, and it attaches the panel's own short report as Tab A. The CIA released this copy through its CREST database, stamped "Approved For Release 2013/06/26".

## What it contains

- **The panel's report (Tab A).** The conclusions and recommendations that later made the panel notorious: no evidence that the reports showed a direct threat to national security, and a recommendation that the government reduce public interest in UFOs through education and "debunking", and keep watch on civilian UFO groups.
- **The evidence reviewed.** Status reports of [[Project Grudge]] and [[Project Blue Book]], the Tremonton, Utah and Great Falls, Montana films, and work by [[Project Stork]], the [[Battelle Memorial Institute]] study (Tab B).
- **The participants.** Robertson, with panel members including [[Thornton Page]], and [[J. Allen Hynek]] among the associate members.
- **Later pages.** The CREST copy ends (pages 33 to 38) with excerpts from [[Project Blue Book Special Report No. 14|Blue Book Special Report No. 14]], dated 5 May 1955, filed with the memorandum.

> [!edit] Corrected 16 October 2026
> Earlier versions said the panel reviewed case files and films from Grudge and Blue Book. Tab B lists Grudge and Blue Book status reports and, separately, the Tremonton and Great Falls films. The note on the copy's last pages is new, from the transcription.

## In Gerb's videos

In his [[National Reconnaissance Office - UFO Crash Retrievals, Surveillance, and Legacy Program Gatekeepers|National Reconnaissance Office video]], Gerb shows the report's list of the evidence presented to the panel on screen: material from Grudge, Blue Book and Stork. He mentions the panel in about a dozen other videos, most fully in [[The Origin of the UFO Stigma]], where he treats it as the start of the official campaign to make UFOs a subject for ridicule.
