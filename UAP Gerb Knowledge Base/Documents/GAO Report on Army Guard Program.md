In April 2006 the Government Accountability Office published GAO-06-284, *Contract Security Guards: Army's Guard Program Requires Greater Oversight and Reassessment of Acquisition Approach*, on the Army's use of contract guards at its installations after 2001. Dugway Proving Ground appears in its table of guard contracts.

> [!edit] Corrected 16 October 2026
> Earlier versions called it a General Accounting Office report on contracted security at Dugway. By 2006 the office was the Government Accountability Office, and the report covers the Army's guard program as a whole, with Dugway one entry in a table.
