"Craft Using an Inertial Mass Reduction Device" is US Patent 10,144,532 B2, granted on 4 December 2018 to [[Salvatore Pais|Salvatore Cezar Pais]] of Leonardtown, Maryland, and assigned to the United States of America as represented by the Secretary of the Navy. It is the best known of the Navy patents nicknamed the "UFO patents". Pais filed the application on 28 April 2016 (application no. 15/141,270), and the USPTO published it on 2 November 2017 as US 2017/0313446 A1. The patent runs to six pages, with four claims and one drawing sheet. Its attorney of record is Mark O. Glut of NAWCAD, the Naval Air Warfare Center Aircraft Division at [[Naval Air Station Patuxent River]]. The copy hosted here is the USPTO's own.

## What the patent describes

The craft is built around a resonant cavity: an electrically charged outer wall and an electrically insulated inner wall, with the space between them filled with a noble gas such as xenon. Microwave emitters inside the cavity, working between 300 megahertz and 300 gigahertz, make the outer wall "vibrate in an accelerated mode and create a local polarized vacuum outside the outer resonant cavity wall". According to the patent, polarizing the vacuum next to the hull cuts the craft's inertial mass, and so its resistance to acceleration, and lets it reach "extreme speeds". The crew compartment, power plant and cargo bay sit inside the cavity, each shielded in a Faraday-type cage. The craft steers by vibrating different sections of the cavity shroud; vibrating the top edges, for example, moves it upward.

The background section sets out the theory. It argues that high-energy electromagnetic fields can polarize the quantum vacuum, gives equations for the energy flux of Pais's "high energy electromagnetic field generator" (HEEMFG), and draws on the Schwinger limit for electron-positron pair production, Ilya Prigogine's work on far-from-equilibrium thermodynamics, the Gertsenshtein effect, and a disputed 1989 report by Hayasaka and Takeuchi of gyroscopes losing weight. The front page cites H. David Froning, [[Hal Puthoff]]'s 2002 paper on the "polarizable-vacuum" approach to general relativity, Prigogine's Nobel lecture, the Hayasaka and Takeuchi paper and two 2015 papers by Pais. The text also proposes a "hybrid aerospace/undersea craft (HAUC)" that, wrapped in "a vacuum plasma bubble/sheath", could move through air, space and water and travel underwater at extreme speeds.

Its one drawing sheet has two figures. FIG. 1 is a triangular craft, pointed at the front and flat at the back. FIG. 2 is a diamond, made by mirroring the leading half to form the trailing half. Both are drawn in outline, with the cavity along the hull and a wavy line round the outside marking the polarized vacuum. The text says the craft can also be "a cone or lenticular triangle/delta wing configuration".

The four claims cover the craft with its inner and outer cavity walls and microwave emitters (claim 1), a cavity filled with a noble gas (claim 2), an electrically charged outer wall (claim 3), and a cavity "axially rotated in an accelerated mode" (claim 4).

## Pais's other Navy patents

The craft patent names its preferred microwave emitter as the generator in Pais's earlier application, "Electromagnetic Field Generator and Method to Generate an Electromagnetic Field". That application was filed on 24 July 2015 and granted on 20 November 2018 as [US 10,135,366 B2](https://image-ppubs.uspto.gov/dirsearch-public/print/downloadPdf/10135366). A third Navy patent, "High Frequency Gravitational Wave Generator", was filed on 14 February 2017 and granted on 18 June 2019 as [US 10,322,827 B2](https://image-ppubs.uspto.gov/dirsearch-public/print/downloadPdf/10322827). Both are assigned to the Secretary of the Navy. Gerb refers only to "his patents" in general, and these have no pages of their own.

## How Gerb uses it

The patent comes up in three videos:

- In *[[Off-World Technologies Division – UAP Technology Reverse Engineering]]*, Gerb says Pais filed the patent in 2017 at "Navy Air Station Pax River" for a triangle- or diamond-shaped craft that "strongly resembles" the alleged [[TR-3B]]. He says NAVAIR chief technology officer James Sheehy supported it, and cites [[Ross Coulthart]]'s account of [[Nat Kobitz]], whose inquiries at NAVAIR found no one who knew of Pais or his patents. Gerb raises Pax River as a possible counterpart to [[Naval Surface Warfare Center Crane]].
- In *[[UFOs in the Private Sector - Lockheed Martin]]*, he says Pais and the Naval Air Station "were awarded" the patent in 2017. He compares it to [[Bernard Haisch]] and Hal Puthoff's paper on zero-point energy for Lockheed's [[Advanced Technology Center (ATC)|Advanced Technology Center]].
- In *[[1997 ALIEN REPRODUCTION VEHICLE ENCOUNTER AT 29 PALMS, CA - THE TESTIMONY OF SGT RODERICK CASTLE]]*, he says the patent, "I think ... in 2017", shows "a triangle with a large concave center", like the craft [[Rodrik Castle]] described.

The patent differs from these accounts in a few details. 2017 is the year the application was published; Pais filed it in April 2016, and it was granted in December 2018. Gerb places Pax River in Virginia, but the base is in St. Mary's County, Maryland, and the patent gives Pais's address as Leonardtown, Maryland. The patent's figures are flat outlines of a triangle and a diamond, and neither they nor the text describe a concave centre. Sheehy's support is not recorded in the patent itself. And the paper the patent cites is Puthoff's own 2002 paper, not the Haisch and Puthoff paper Gerb compares it to.
