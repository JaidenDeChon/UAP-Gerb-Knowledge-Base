## Page 2

*[header and footer: ~~TOP SECRET//SI/TK/REL~~]*

**(U) BLUF**

~~(S//REL)~~ At 0038:17Z 6 May 2021, Sentient [redacted (b)(1), (b)(3)] image processing detected a possible airborne object ~78km southeast of [redacted (b)(1), (b)(3)]

~~(S//REL)~~ The object was small (<10m), and did not match the visual signature of typical aircraft detections

- The object did, however, vaguely resemble similar detections of airborne objects by US Navy aircraft and surface vessels in the [redacted (b)(1), (b)(3)] Operating Areas ("Unidentified Aerial Phenomena")
- There is a rough similarity to the previously-reported "tic tac" shape
- The object was likely not a sensor artifact or focal plane anomaly (although more in-depth imagery analysis is warranted)

~~(TS//SI/TK/REL)~~ There were no correlating tracks present in [redacted (b)(1), (b)(3)] reporting, nor was there any correlating ELINT/SIGINT in [redacted (b)(1), (b)(3)] reporting, despite time-coincident [redacted (b)(1), (b)(3)] access/collection

- Sentient detections did, however, detect the presence of the prob [redacted (b)(1), (b)(3)] in the same imagery ~25km to the west
- In recent reporting¹, the [redacted (b)(1), (b)(3)] has been associated with command-and-control (C2) activities, as well as more traditional telemetry and space functions – the simultaneous presence of this high-interest vessel is notable, although possible [sic] merely coincidental

~~(TS//SI/TK/REL)~~ Confidence is relatively low in this detection, but the potential linkage to similar phenomenon off of [redacted (b)(1), (b)(3)] may warrant further investigation
