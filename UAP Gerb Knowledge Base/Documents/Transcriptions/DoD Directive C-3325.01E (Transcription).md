1. (U) REISSUANCE AND PURPOSE

(U) This Directive:

1.1. (U) Reissues Reference (a) to update policy and responsibilities for the management and operation of the DoD FMP under the authority vested in the Secretary of Defense by Reference (b).

1.2. (U) Designates the Director, Defense Intelligence Agency (DIA), as the DoD Executive Agent for the FMP according to Reference (c).

2. (U) APPLICABILITY AND SCOPE

(U) This Directive applies to:

2.1. (U) The Office of the Secretary of Defense (OSD), the Military Departments, the Chairman of the Joint Chiefs of Staff, the Combatant Commands, the Office of the Inspector General of the Department of Defense, the Defense Agencies, the DoD Field Activities, and all other organizational entities in the Department of Defense (hereafter referred to collectively as the "DoD Components").

Classified by: Stephen A. Cambone, Under Secretary of Defense for Intelligence

Reason: 1.4(b), (c), (d)

Declassify on: August 1, 2031

2.2. (U) All activities of the DoD Components that acquire and exploit foreign military and commercial materiel with potential military application, technologies, and related documents except:

2.2.1. (U) Activities that complement the FMP addressed by DoD Instruction 2015.4 (Reference (d)) and DoD 5000.3-M-2 (Reference (e)).

2.2.2. (U) Purchases of foreign manufactured materiel acquired through open competition and used to equip the DoD Components or to support operational elements of the Military Departments.

3. (U) DEFINITIONS

3.1. (U) Foreign Materiel. Any item of foreign origin including technical and operational documentation that has military or potential military application.

3.2. (U) Foreign Materiel Acquisition (FMA). FMP activities that include gaining physical possession of, or access to, an item of foreign materiel or technology.

3.3. (U) Foreign Materiel Exploitation (FME). FMP activities that include analysis, testing, evaluation, and documentation of the scientific and technical intelligence (S&TI) characteristics of an item of foreign materiel.

4. (U) POLICY

(U) It is DoD Policy that:

4.1. (U) The Department of Defense shall acquire and exploit foreign weapons systems and components, foreign commercial items with potential military application, and related technologies, documents, and software to enhance national security.

4.2. (U) The DoD Components authorized to conduct FMA or FME activities shall be responsive to guidance provided by the Under Secretary of Defense for Intelligence (USD(I)), as advised by the FMP Steering Committee (FMPSC) according to DoD Instruction S-3325.4 (Reference (f)), and coordinate such activities with the Director, DIA.

4.3. (U) The DoD Components shall program funds through the DoD Planning, Programming, and Budgeting process to support the FMP.

4.4. (U) Information derived from exploitation of foreign materiel shall receive immediate and widest possible dissemination and use to ensure maximum possible benefit consistent with need and security.

5. (U) RESPONSIBILITIES

5.1. (U) The USD(I) shall serve as the Principal Staff Assistant and advisor to the Secretary and Deputy Secretary of Defense on all FMP matters and shall:

5.1.1. (U) Exercise oversight of the DoD FMP and the activities of the DoD Executive Agent for FMP, assessing the need, currency, effectiveness, and efficiency of the DoD Executive Agent arrangement in satisfying end-user requirements..

5.1.2. (U) Develop, coordinate, and implement policy and planning for the FMP activities of the DoD Components.

5.1.3. (U) Establish and designate the chair of the FMPSC according to Reference (f).

5.1.4. (U) Serve as the principal DoD point of contact for Congressional matters affecting the DoD FMP, in consultation with the Assistant Secretary of Defense for Legislative Affairs, according to DoD Directive 5142.1 (Reference (g)).

5.1.5. (U) Control special access programs applicable to the FMP using required compartmented procedures.

5.1.6. (U) Establish, program, budget, and execute the OSD FMA and FME (FMA&E) fund in support of DoD FMP activities.

5.1.7. (U) Coordinate planned activities and FMP Acquisition Operational Plans (OPLANs) with the appropriate policy elements within OSD.

5.1.8. (U) Submit a report of DoD FMP activities to the Secretary of Defense by February 1 of each year.

5.2. (U) The Director, DIA, as the DoD Executive Agent for FMP is subject to the authority, direction, and control of the Secretary of Defense through the USD(I), and shall:

5.2.1. (U) Coordinate the execution of all FMP activities of the Military Departments and the Combatant Commands through the Chairman of the Joint Chiefs of Staff, the Defense Agencies, and the DoD Field Activities.

5.2.2. (U) Ensure that the Military Departments' S&TI Centers, the Marine Corps Intelligence Activity, the DIA Missile and Space Intelligence Center, and the Armed Forces Medical Intelligence Center are funded, according to overall priorities, to support FMA&E activities.

5.2.3. (U) Review and assess the adequacy of all FMP acquisition proposals in which the DoD Components participate.

5.2.4. (U) Review and coordinate requests from the DoD Components for allocations from the OSD FMA&E fund using criteria established by the FMPSC.

5.2.5. (U) Acquire and exploit foreign materiel in accordance with this Directive.

5.2.6. (U) In coordination with the DoD Components designate the organizations responsible for acquisition and exploitation of foreign materiel based on overall intelligence requirements, operational requirements, and test and evaluation requirements.

5.2.7. (U) Establish and maintain a comprehensive foreign materiel data base (i.e., Foreign Materiel Management System).

5.2.8. (U) Announce all FMA opportunities to the Military Departments.

5.2.9. (U) Coordinate OPLANs submitted by the Military Departments for all overt acquisitions with the Department of State and the Deputy Under Secretary of Defense for Intelligence and Warfighting Support.

5.2.10. (U) Annually, by January 15, submit to the USD(I) the "Foreign Materiel Program Report" and, by September 15, the "Foreign Materiel Program Annual Plan."

5.3.1. (U) Exercise oversight for all matters concerning the acquisition and exploitation of foreign cryptologic/cryptographic materiel and information.

5.3.2. (U) Comply with the provisions of this Directive for acquisition and exploitation of foreign materiel not governed by Reference (e).

5.3.3. (U) Disseminate the results of FMP acquisitions and exploitations in a timely manner.

5.5.1. (U) Acquire foreign materiel in cooperation with the Director, DIA, as set forth in this Directive.
