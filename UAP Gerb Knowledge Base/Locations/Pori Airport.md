Pori Airport (Porin lentoasema) is an airport in Finland and the site of the [[Pori Airport UFO Incident]] on April 12, 1969. During a Fouga Magister jet training mission, [[Finnish Defense Force]] flight controllers at Pori Airport sent pilot-in-training [[Tarmo Tukeva]] to investigate seven objects they assumed were air balloons, floating 1,500 to 3,000 meters above the airport. A second pilot, [[Jouko Kuronen]], also observed them. The pilots reported that the objects were disc-shaped and pale yellow, and that they accelerated away at extraordinary speed against a 180 km/h headwind. Pori Airport did not record sonar signatures (airports typically use radar, not sonar). Radar 200 km away in [[Vaasa, Finland|Vaasa]] confirmed the objects, at an estimated speed of 3,218 meters per second.
