Pori Airport (Porin lentoasema) is a Finnish airport where the April 12, 1969 [[Pori Airport UFO Incident]] occurred. During a Fouga Magister jet training mission, [[Finnish Defense Force]] flight controllers at Pori Airport directed pilots [[Tarmo Tukia]] and [[Juhani Korhonen]] to investigate seven assumed air balloons floating at 1,500 to 3,000 meters altitude above the airport. The pilots reported the objects were disc-shaped, pale yellow, and accelerated away at extraordinary speed against a 180 km/h headwind. While Pori Airport did not record sonar signatures (note: airports typically use radar, not sonar), radar detection 200 km away in [[Vaasa, Finland|Vaasa]] confirmed the objects at an estimated speed of 3,218 meters per second.
