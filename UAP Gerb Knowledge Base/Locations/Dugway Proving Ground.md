Dugway Proving Ground (DPG) is a United States Army test and evaluation facility located in Tooele County, Utah, approximately 85 miles southwest of Salt Lake City in the Great Salt Lake Desert. Operated by the U.S. Army Test and Evaluation Command (ATEC), Dugway's primary mission has historically centered on chemical and biological defense testing, though it also supports a range of other classified military test activities. The facility encompasses over 800,000 acres of remote desert terrain and includes the West Desert Test Center (WDTC). The remote Granite Peak installation within the Dugway range area is a particularly restricted subzone alleged to host highly sensitive activities.
