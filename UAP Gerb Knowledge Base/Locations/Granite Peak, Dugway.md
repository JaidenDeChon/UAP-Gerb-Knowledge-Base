**Granite Peak** is a restricted sub-installation within [[Dugway Proving Ground]] in Utah. It is surrounded by radar stations, threat emitter systems, and sensor infrastructure that the [[388th Range Squadron]] operates out of [[Hill Air Force Base]]. In UAP research, Granite Peak is alleged to sit directly above the main entrance to the deep underground military base (DUMB) at [[Dugway Proving Ground]]. Satellite imagery and witness testimony identify a small, inconspicuous surface building nearby as the cover for an elevator shaft, which is the only way for personnel to enter the underground facility.

## Physical Description and Infrastructure

Although it is a significant installation, Granite Peak is left out of Dugway Proving Ground visitor guides and public facility maps. The 388th Range Squadron infrastructure around the peak includes radar tracking stations and threat emitter arrays, the same equipment used for advanced aerospace testing across the [[Utah Test and Training Range]]. These layers of sensors support the official mission of range safety and threat simulation. According to Gerb's analysis, they also protect clandestine operations below ground.

## Alleged Deep Underground Military Base

According to witness "GP," a senior Department of Defense chemist who claimed to work in legacy operations at Dugway, the underground facility at Granite Peak:

- Spans approximately 1,300 square miles beneath Dugway's surface footprint
- Is located primarily beneath Granite Peak at an undisclosed depth
- Is accessed via a single personnel-only elevator shaft concealed within a small surface building identified in satellite imagery between [[Avery Technical Center]] and the southern area of the Utah Test and Training Range (UTTR)
- Receives no logistics or materiel through the surface entrance; all supplies, equipment, and food travel internally on an underground [[Maglev Underground Transportation|maglev light rail system]] that connects the facility to other DUMBs nationwide

Gerb notes that Steven Greer, working independently, had identified the same surface entrance on the Disclosure Project Archive before this witness did.

## Witness MS Encounter

In 2009, "MS", a contractor and former Navy serviceman employed at Dugway by C Martin Company, accidentally entered an unmarked building at [[Avery Technical Center]]. Inside he saw a seamless disc craft, about 10 feet tall, hovering. He was interrogated for six hours. MS later spoke with GP, who confirmed that the craft was extraterrestrial and gave details about Granite Peak's underground infrastructure, including the maglev tunnel network.
