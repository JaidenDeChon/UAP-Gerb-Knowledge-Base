Indonesia is a Southeast Asian nation made up of thousands of islands. Several significant UAP-related events have occurred there, the most notable being [[Michael Herrera]]'s 2009 encounter during a US Marine Corps humanitarian relief operation.

## 2009 Earthquake and Humanitarian Relief Operation

In late September 2009, a major earthquake struck Indonesia, and an international humanitarian relief effort followed. The [[USS Denver]], an Austin-class amphibious transport dock carrying US Marines including Michael Herrera, arrived on approximately October 9, 2009, roughly 9-10 days after the initial earthquake. From the ship, CH-53 Super Stallion heavy lift helicopters carried supplies to remote villages whose roads had been blocked by landslides.

Military briefings described the region as the second-largest terrorist capital in the world, with a predominantly Muslim population. This was cited as the justification for Marines providing armed security on supply delivery missions. Marines were also told that US Navy SEAL elements might be operating in the area. A wardroom briefing included photographs of Barack Obama as a child and references to his time in Indonesia, though how this related to the mission remains unclear.

## Michael Herrera UAP Encounter

During the relief operation, Herrera and his Marine fire team encountered an octagonal craft, armed operators in unmarked uniforms, and what appeared to be a human trafficking operation in a jungle clearing. A helicopter inserted them at a hasty landing zone in hilly terrain covered in thick tropical vegetation. The area was remote enough that Herrera's team went in without radio communications or nearby support.

Herrera later learned from alleged program insiders that the operation was recruiting people from disaster-stricken areas under false pretenses to serve as "pink assets" (biological subjects) in classified programs. According to those insiders, it used the chaos and desperation after the earthquake to acquire human subjects with little chance of detection or investigation.

## Historical Connection: Barack Obama

Barack Obama lived in Indonesia as a child, spending approximately four years in Jakarta, from 1967 to 1971, with his mother and Indonesian stepfather. The mission briefing Herrera attended aboard the USS Denver pointed this out explicitly: the female USMC major showed pictures of Obama as a child and of his family members. No clear operational reason was given for including this in a security mission briefing.
