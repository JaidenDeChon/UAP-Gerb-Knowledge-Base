New Mexico's largest city and home to Kirtland Air Force Base and Sandia National Laboratories, both of which are central nodes in the alleged UFO legacy program infrastructure. Albuquerque also features in documentary research into the Special Operations Manual (SOM 1-01), a purported Majestic 12 document.

## SOM 1-01 Document Research

Albuquerque city phone directories from the years 1954–1957 were searched by researchers attempting to identify individuals whose initials appeared on the document control page of the [[SOM 1-01 Manual (Psalm 101)|Special Operations Manual]]. This targeted cross-referencing was part of broader efforts to authenticate the document by linking its listed personnel to verifiable New Mexico addresses during the relevant period.

## Kirtland Air Force Base and Sandia

Albuquerque is the home city of [[Kirtland Air Force Base, New Mexico|Kirtland Air Force Base]], which houses the Sandia National Laboratories — a Department of Energy–managed nuclear weapons research facility. Both Kirtland and Sandia have been identified in UFO legacy program research as key nodes in the classification and storage of UAP-related materials, with Kirtland specifically named as the location of the alleged Aphotech program.

## Geographic Significance

Albuquerque's position within New Mexico, surrounded by major military research installations including [[Kirtland Air Force Base, New Mexico|Kirtland]], [[Holloman Air Force Base]], and [[White Sands Missile Range]], makes it a recurring reference point in discussions of the post-Roswell UAP research infrastructure developed in the American Southwest.
