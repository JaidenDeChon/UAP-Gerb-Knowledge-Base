Malmstrom Air Force Base is a US Air Force installation near Great Falls, Montana. It is a Strategic Air Command (SAC) base that has historically been responsible for maintaining a large portion of the US intercontinental ballistic missile (ICBM) deterrent force, specifically the Minuteman missile fleet. In UAP research, Malmstrom is best known as the site of the [[Malmstrom Air Force Base UFO Incident]] of March 24, 1967. That night a pulsating red oval craft hovered over the base's front gate while all 10 Minuteman ICBMs on site lost their guidance and control systems at the same time.

## Role as an ICBM Installation

Malmstrom hosts several missile squadrons. They are responsible for underground launch control facilities and their silo complexes, which are spread across a large area of central Montana. During the Cold War, when Malmstrom was a Strategic Air Command base, the nuclear readiness of its missiles was critical to the US deterrence posture. The base hosted the 490th Minuteman Missile Squadron, where [[Robert Salas]] was assigned in 1967.

## The 1967 UFO Incident

On the night of March 24, 1967, Lieutenant Robert Salas was the on-duty launch commander at an underground facility assigned to Malmstrom. His flight security controller reported to him that a craft was performing anomalous maneuvers. The craft, a glowing red oval about 30–40 feet in diameter, was later reported hovering directly over the base's front gate. During this period, all 10 Minuteman missiles under Salas's control showed a red fault status at the same time, indicating that their guidance and control systems had failed.

Air Force security intelligence classified the event Secret. Four personnel later submitted sworn affidavits attesting to the event: Salas, missile targeting officer Robert Jamison, Airman First Class Patrick McDonough, and communications officer Dwynne Arneson. No Air Force investigation produced a conventional explanation for the simultaneous missile failures.

## Significance

Malmstrom is the most formally corroborated instance of UFO interaction with an operational nuclear missile installation. The shutdown of 10 ICBMs at a single facility at the same time, while the presence of an unidentified craft was documented, is the defining case in the [[UFO Interference with Nuclear Weapons]] pattern. David Grusch directly confirmed the Malmstrom incident in his NewsNation interview with Ross Coulthart.
