Detroit is the largest city in Michigan and a major industrial and population center in the Great Lakes region. In UAP research it appears as one of multiple documented locations with reported triangular craft sightings, referenced within UAP Gerb's broader survey of the black triangle phenomenon and its possible connection to reverse-engineered non-human airframes.

## Triangular Craft Sighting Context

Detroit is cited alongside a global pattern of triangular UAP sightings that UAP Gerb analyzes in connection with allegations about man-made alien reproduction vehicles (ARVs) and classified triangular craft programs. The triangular craft sightings catalogued in this video span multiple countries and decades, with consistent morphological features: large, silent or near-silent, slow-moving equilateral triangles with lights at each vertex and often a central glow.

The Detroit area falls within the dense population corridor of the Midwest and Great Lakes region, which has produced numerous multi-witness triangular craft reports. UAP Gerb contextualizes these reports against the claims of figures including USAF Master Sergeant [[Edgar Fouché]], who described the TR-3B as a triangular craft developed at classified facilities including [[Area 51]] and related ranges, and against comparable sightings in Wisconsin, England, and elsewhere in the same era.

## Source Limitations
