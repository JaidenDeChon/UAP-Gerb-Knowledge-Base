Ent Air Force Base was a United States Air Force installation in Colorado Springs, Colorado. From 1948 to 1975 it was the headquarters of the Air Defense Command (ADC), the command responsible for defending North American airspace. In research into UAP legacy programs, the base matters because Harry B. Cooper was posted there between 1957 and 1963. During that period he is documented as photographing UFOs and printing classified documents as part of the USAF UFO program.

## Harry B. Cooper and the USAF UFO Program

The base's place in UAP research rests on a signed commendation from General Curtis LeMay to Harry B. Cooper. It praises Cooper for "outstanding work as NCO in charge of camera section, publishing division" at Ent Air Force Base from 1957 to 1963. The document is examined in [[Videos/The Majestic-12 Documents With Ryan S. Wood/summary|The Majestic-12 Documents With Ryan S. Wood]]. It confirms Cooper's operational role in both photographing UFOs and printing classified documents at one of the most sensitive air defense command centers in the United States, during the height of the Cold War UAP program period.

Harry B. Cooper also matters because he is the father of Timothy Cooper, who later received the largest known cache of alleged [[Majestic 12]] documents. The material reached Timothy Cooper by post from Sacramento, California, as items put into his mailbox by hand without postage, and by post from the Fort Meade FOIA office. Researcher Ryan S. Wood identifies the LeMay commendation to Harry Cooper as strong circumstantial evidence linking the elder Cooper to the institutional infrastructure through which Majestic documents might plausibly have originated.

## Air Defense Command Headquarters

As the headquarters of Air Defense Command from 1948 onward, Ent AFB was at the center of U.S. military monitoring of, and response to, unidentified aerial incursions during the most active documented period of UAP sightings over U.S. territory. Because the ADC's mission was to track and intercept unknown aerial objects, it falls directly among the institutions that Gerb identifies as having had routine contact with UAP phenomena as part of their work, and therefore as likely nodes in the classification and compartmentation of UAP information during the 1950s and 1960s.

Cooper's work photographing and printing classified documents at ADC headquarters during this period, together with the LeMay commendation that explicitly praises his "outstanding work" on a USAF UFO program, is direct documentary evidence of a UAP-connected function at Ent Air Force Base.
