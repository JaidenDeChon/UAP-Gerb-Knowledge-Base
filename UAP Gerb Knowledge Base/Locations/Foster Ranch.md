Foster Ranch is the New Mexico ranch property where Mac Brazel reported unusual debris in July 1947 during the Roswell incident. It is usually identified as the debris-field site, distinct in many accounts from a separate alleged primary crash location.

## Role In Roswell Timeline

Military personnel were dispatched to the ranch after Brazel reported the material. Witness narratives describe a wide debris scatter area and recovery activity preceding the public press-release cycle on July 8, 1947.
