Foster Ranch is the ranch in New Mexico where Mac Brazel reported unusual debris in July 1947, during the Roswell incident. It is usually identified as the site of the debris field. Many accounts treat it as a different place from the alleged primary crash site.

## Role In Roswell Timeline

Military personnel were dispatched to the ranch after Brazel reported the material. Witness accounts describe debris scattered over a wide area, and a recovery effort that came before the public press releases of July 8, 1947.
