Fort Huachuca is a US Army installation in Cochise County, southern Arizona, founded in 1877. It is home to the Army's Intelligence Center of Excellence, the training centre for Army military intelligence. The fort also hosts the Joint Interoperability Test Command of the Defense Information Systems Agency, which the DoD counts among its major range and test facility bases. In UFO research, the fort is associated with Army intelligence and with Major General [[Albert Stubblebine]].

## Alleged UFO Connections

[[Philip J. Corso]] and [[Steven Greer]] have accused Stubblebine, who commanded [[Army INSCOM]] from 1981 to 1984, of taking part in UFO craft storage and material exploitation while he headed Army intelligence at Fort Huachuca. Gerb has repeated the accusation in several videos, and he estimates that the fort is heavily involved in UFO programs.

## On Hamilton's Map

In *Deep Underground Military Bases (D.U.M.Bs.) - UFO Legacy Programs*, Gerb notes that [[Bill Hamilton]]'s 1990 map of connected underground UFO facilities marks Fort Huachuca as a standalone [[Deep Underground Military Bases (DUMBs)|DUMB]], not connected to the rest of the alleged network. He says it is "really no surprise" to see the fort there. It also lines up with a 2019 DoD laboratory map that he overlays on Hamilton's.
