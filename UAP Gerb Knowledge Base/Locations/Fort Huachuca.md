Fort Huachuca is a US Army installation in Cochise County, southern Arizona, founded in 1877. It is home to the Army's Intelligence Center of Excellence, the training centre for Army military intelligence, and hosts the Joint Interoperability Test Command of the Defense Information Systems Agency, which the DoD counts among its major range and test facility bases. In UFO research it is associated with Army intelligence and with Major General [[Albert Stubblebine]].

## Alleged UFO Connections

[[Philip J. Corso]] and [[Steven Greer]] have accused Stubblebine, founder of [[Army INSCOM]], of taking part in UFO craft storage and material exploitation while heading Army intelligence at Fort Huachuca. UAP Gerb has repeated this accusation in several videos and estimates that the fort is heavily involved in UFO programs.

## On Hamilton's Map

In *Deep Underground Military Bases (D.U.M.Bs.) - UFO Legacy Programs*, UAP Gerb notes that [[Bill Hamilton]]'s 1990 map of connected underground UFO facilities marks Fort Huachuca as a standalone [[Deep Underground Military Bases (DUMBs)|DUMB]], not connected to the rest of the alleged network. He says it is "really no surprise" to see it there. The fort also lines up with a 2019 DoD laboratory map he overlays on Hamilton's.
