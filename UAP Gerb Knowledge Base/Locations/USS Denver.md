USS *Denver* (LPD-9) was an Austin-class amphibious transport dock of the United States Navy, commissioned on 26 October 1968 and decommissioned on 14 August 2014. In 2009 she was forward-deployed at Sasebo, Japan, with Marines of the [[31st Marine Expeditionary Unit]] embarked. She was sunk as a target during the RIMPAC 2022 exercise off Kauai, Hawaii, on 22 July 2022. In UAP research the ship is best known as the vessel that carried [[Michael Herrera]] to Indonesia in October 2009.

## 2009 Sumatra Relief

After the [[2009 Sumatra Earthquake]], the *Denver* and the destroyer USS *McCampbell* were sent to support international relief efforts near Padang in October 2009. The ship's heavy-lift CH-53 helicopters carried food, water and shelter supplies to remote villages whose roads had been cut by landslides. According to [[Richard B. Landolt]], who commanded the US joint task force, the helicopters came aboard on the morning of 9 October.

## The Herrera Account

[[Michael Herrera]] says he was aboard the *Denver* on officer's chow detail, having gone forward to Japan weeks before the main Marine element, when a female Marine major briefed volunteers in the ship's wardroom for a security mission ashore. He says the group flew by CH-53 to an airfield, then on to a landing zone in the hills, where six Marines came upon a large craft and eight armed operators, and that a CH-53 later returned them to the ship (see [[2009 Indonesia UFO Encounter (Herrera)]]). He recalls that the ship's phone banks were switched off for about two weeks, and that after the mission the group returned its loaned rifles and magazines to the ship's armory.
