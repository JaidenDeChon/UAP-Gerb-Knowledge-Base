The USS Denver (LPD-9) is an Austin-class amphibious transport dock that served as the primary US Navy vessel for Michael Herrera's Marine unit during the 2009 Indonesia humanitarian relief operation following the September 2009 earthquake. The ship arrived on approximately October 9, 2009, roughly 9-10 days after the earthquake, carrying CH-53 Super Stallion heavy lift helicopters tasked with delivering supplies to remote villages. After Herrera and his team's encounter with an unidentified craft and armed operators, they were extracted via CH-53 and returned to the Denver. On board, Herrera and his team returned their weapons and borrowed magazines to the ship's armory.
