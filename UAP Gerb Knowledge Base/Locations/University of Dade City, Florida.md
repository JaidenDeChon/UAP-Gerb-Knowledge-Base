UAP research records the University of Dade City, Florida as the place where [[Leonard Stringfield]], a pioneering researcher of UFO crash retrievals, met a former member of the alleged [[Blue Berets]] rapid-reaction unit during a public lecture. From that meeting came one of Stringfield's most detailed firsthand accounts of how the Blue Berets were organised and how they had operated.

## Stringfield's Encounter with "Cam"

During the Dade City lecture, Stringfield was approached by a man known by the pseudonym "Cam," who identified himself as a former Blue Beret. Cam told Stringfield the following about the unit:

- The Blue Berets were housed at select military bases in a state of constant readiness, so that they could be deployed quickly to UFO crash or retrieval sites
- Members received specialized training for covert operations and in diversionary tactics designed to secure crash sites and remove evidence without attracting public attention
- Cam had served with the unit in Vietnam himself, on covert missions behind enemy lines
- Cam said he had never personally taken part in a UFO crash retrieval, but he knew a fellow Blue Beret who had been directly involved in recovering a "**big head**" from an alien craft. That account is consistent with reports of non-human biological entities recovered at crash sites

## Significance

Stringfield documented Cam's testimony alongside accounts from other Blue Beret-connected sources, including [[Jeff Morse]] (who contacted Stringfield between 1985 and 1987) and multiple witnesses at the [[1965 Kecksburg, Pennsylvania Crash Retrieval|1965 Kecksburg UFO crash]] who described blue-bereted Air Force personnel arriving within minutes of the crash. Cam's account from Dade City is one of the earliest documented cases in Stringfield's research in which a self-identified former member of the alleged unit described its UAP-specific mission. Stringfield published this account in his *Crash Retrievals of the Third Kind: A Case Study of Alleged UFOs and Occupants in Military Custody*.
