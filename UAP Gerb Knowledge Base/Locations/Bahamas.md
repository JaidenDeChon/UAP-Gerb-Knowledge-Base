The Bahamas is an island nation in the western Atlantic Ocean. It is notable in UAP research for the deep water just off its continental shelf. The waters around the islands are shallow, but the ocean floor drops quickly to depths of 4,000 meters or more in the nearby Blake–Bahama Basin, and to roughly 2,000 meters in the "Tongue of the Ocean," a submarine trench that is home to the U.S. Navy's [[Atlantic Undersea Test and Evaluation Center]] (AUTEC). Gerb identifies the waters east of the Bahamas as the alleged operating area of the "[[Tic Tac Factory]]", an enormous unidentified submerged object that he says is monitored under a decades-old special access program run by [[MITRE Corporation|MITRE]] and the U.S. Navy.

## Geographic Significance

Gerb argues that the area's extreme depths, reached within a short distance, and its closeness to a major U.S. Navy undersea test range make it a plausible place for a large submerged object to operate largely undetected, and for the U.S. government to keep that object under sustained surveillance. Representative Tim Burchett has separately referred to the location, in different terms, in public comments about "deep underwater USO bases."
