The Bahamas is an island nation in the western Atlantic Ocean, notable in UAP research for the immediately adjacent deep-water geography off its continental shelf — despite the shallow waters surrounding the islands themselves, the ocean floor drops quickly to depths of 4,000 meters or greater in the nearby Blake–Bahama Basin, and to roughly 2,000 meters in the "Tongue of the Ocean," a submarine trench that is home to the U.S. Navy's [[Atlantic Undersea Test and Evaluation Center]] (AUTEC). In UAP Gerb's investigative framework, the waters east of the Bahamas are identified as the alleged operating area of the "[[Tic Tac Factory]]" — an enormous unidentified submerged object monitored under a decades-old [[MITRE Corporation|MITRE]]- and U.S. Navy-administered special access program.

## Geographic Significance

UAP Gerb argues that the area's combination of extreme, rapidly accessed depth and its proximity to a major U.S. Navy undersea test range makes it a geographically plausible location for both a large submerged object to operate largely undetected and for sustained U.S. government surveillance of that object to be conducted. The location has separately been referenced, in different terms, by Representative Tim Burchett in public comments about "deep underwater USO bases."
