The Puerto Rico Trench is an ocean trench north of Puerto Rico, on the boundary between the Caribbean and North American plates, and the deepest part of the Atlantic Ocean. It runs about 800 km (500 miles). Its deepest point, the Milwaukee Deep, is about 8,400 m (27,500 ft) down.

## In Gerb's videos

The trench comes up in Gerb's USO video through a story [[Luis Elizondo]] told astronaut Terry Virts in a 2022 interview (see [[Caribbean Island Cruise Missile Recovery USO Encounter]]). According to Elizondo's unnamed source, a Navy helicopter regularly flew from a Caribbean island to recover test-fired cruise missiles, which sink when their fuel runs out and later rise to be collected for their telemetry. On one recovery the crew saw something round, dark and "about the size of a small island" rise toward the surface without breaking it. A month later, as a frogman hung from a rope to latch onto another missile, the object rose again, the frogman scrambled back up the rope, and the missile was pulled under.

In the clip the recovery site is placed over the "Puerto Rican Trench", described as water 22,000 feet deep and "the second deepest part of the ocean". Gerb uses the story among his military accounts of [[Unidentified Submerged Object (USO)|unidentified submerged objects]].
