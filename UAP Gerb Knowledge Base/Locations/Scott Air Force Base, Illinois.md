**Scott Air Force Base** is a United States Air Force installation near [[St. Clair, Southern Illinois]], approximately 25 miles east of St. Louis. It is the headquarters of the Air Mobility Command (AMC), the primary Air Force organization responsible for global airlift, aerial refueling, and air mobility operations. In UAP research, the base is known as the military installation nearest to the site of the St. Clair Illinois Triangular Craft Sighting of January 5, 2000.

## UAP Significance

In the early morning hours of January 5, 2000, multiple police officers in the towns around St. Clair in Southern Illinois reported a massive triangular UAP operating close to Scott Air Force Base. The craft was described as an elongated black triangle approximately 75 feet long and 40 feet wide. It had a bright white light at each corner that varied in brightness, a small flashing red light under the middle of the object near the rear, and a white-lit rectangular section at the back with a band of multicolored light running lengthwise through it. Officer Martin reported that the object was 75 to 100 yards wide and flying at approximately 1,000 feet. Witnesses estimated that it loitered at roughly 15 mph before accelerating to approximately 100 mph.

A sighting this close to Scott AFB, the headquarters of Air Mobility Command, is consistent with [[Edgar Fouche|Edgar Fouché]]'s testimony about the [[TR-3B]]. Fouché described that aircraft as a logistics support and transport vehicle for a classified space command. Gerb presents the St. Clair sighting as a representative example of the large black triangular craft seen across Southern Illinois and around the world during the 1980s and 1990s. He notes that such craft were reportedly tested and operated over military bases during that era.

Because Scott AFB is the headquarters of Air Mobility Command, it may also be relevant to the logistics of classified UAP transport operations. AMC controls the air transport infrastructure, including the C-5 Galaxy, C-17 and C-130 aircraft that multiple UAP retrieval testimonies describe as carrying recovered craft and materials.
