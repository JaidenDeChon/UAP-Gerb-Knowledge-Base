Pucallpa is a city in the Ucayali Region of eastern Peru, about 300 miles from Lima, whose airbase was the primary acknowledged site of [[Operation Laser Strike]], the classified [[US SOUTHCOM|US Southern Command]] counter-narcotics operation of the mid-1990s. Since at least 2001 it has been publicly identified as a Laser Strike location. Transcripts of the channel's videos sometimes render the name "Bukalpa" or "Pukulpa".

## Connection to the Peru crash

Many researchers assume that Lance Corporal [[Jonathan Weygandt]] and the [[Marine Air Control Group 28]] Marines were stationed at Pucallpa during the 1997 [[Peru UFO Crash Incident]]. The channel's earlier treatment of the case in *The Marines Who Got too Close to UFOs* followed that view, placing him at Pucallpa Airbase from February 1997 alongside TPS-43 and relocatable over-the-horizon radars and CIA Cessna Citation spotter aircraft. One of the Marines the host later contacted insisted Weygandt never deployed there at all. UAP Gerb accepts that Pucallpa is possible but favours [[USAF Radar Detachment 5, Iquitos]] for three reasons:

- A C-130 from [[Lima, Peru|Lima]] reaches Pucallpa in under an hour, which does not fit Weygandt's description of a multi-hour flight with one stop.
- A Marine who served in the operation refused to discuss his base on classification grounds, although Pucallpa's role is already public.
- The base where Weygandt was held had permanent structures, unlike the makeshift forward operating base the host describes at Pucallpa.
