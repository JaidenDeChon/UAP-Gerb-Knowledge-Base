Pucallpa is a city in eastern Peru, approximately 300 miles from Lima, that served as the primary acknowledged location for [[Operation Laser Strike]], a classified [[US SOUTHCOM]] counter-narcotics operation in 1996–1997. Since at least 2001, Pucallpa has received publicity as a Laser Strike location.

While many researchers initially assumed that [[Jonathan Wagant]] and [[MAC G28]] Marines were stationed at Pucallpa during the 1997 [[Peru UFO Crash Incident]], UAP Gerb's investigation favors [[Iquitos, Peru]] as the more likely base of operations, based on the classified nature of the contact Marine's refusal to discuss the base location, and the fact that Pucallpa's distance from Lima (approximately 1 hour by C-130) does not match Wagant's description of a several-hour flight with one stop.
