Ascension Island is a remote British Overseas Territory in the South Atlantic Ocean, roughly midway between Africa and South America at about 7.9°S, 14.4°W. In UAP research, the island is known for the [[Ascension Island USO Sighting]] of June 25, 1960, one of the more anomalous cases in the maritime sighting record of [[Project Blue Book]]. Blue Book explained it as a flare, an explanation its own investigation had definitively ruled out.

## Strategic and Operational Context

Ascension Island has been a hub for British and American military and space operations since World War II, including missile tracking and test range work. In 1960 it was the site of the missile test recovery operation that produced the USO sighting. An operation range vessel code-named Whiskey was recovering a data cassette from missile test 1802 when several witnesses, among them an RCA photographer, missile recovery divers and aircraft crew, saw a steady white or yellowish light emerge from beneath the ocean surface near the target cassette. [[Project Blue Book]] attributed the sighting to a flare, but no flare was released at the location.

Gerb draws an explicit parallel between this case and an account related by [[Luis Elizondo]], in which a Navy helicopter crew encountered a massive [[Unidentified Submerged Object (USO)]] that emerged from depth during a similar cruise missile recovery operation. He suggests that missile recovery work may specifically attract USO observation, either through electromagnetic emissions or because of the operational value of the recovered technology.
