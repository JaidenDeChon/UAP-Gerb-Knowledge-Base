The Hoia Baciu Forest (Romanian: *Pădurea Hoia-Baciu*) is a forest near Cluj-Napoca, Romania, widely described in popular paranormal culture as "the most haunted forest in the world." It has been the site of reported paranormal phenomena including disappearances, unexplained physical sensations, electronic malfunctions, and UFO sightings dating to at least the 1960s. Reports from the 1960s include photographs of alleged craft over the forest's dense canopy.

## Description

The forest covers approximately 250 hectares and contains a large circular clearing where no vegetation grows. Locals report anxiety and nausea when they enter the clearing, and researchers who have brought sensitive instruments into the area have reported electromagnetic anomalies. The forest is named after a shepherd who disappeared with a flock of two hundred sheep within its boundaries.

## UFO Reports

Photographs taken in the 1960s claim to show UFO activity above the forest canopy, but their authenticity and provenance are contested. The forest has attracted UFO researchers and paranormal tourists in the decades since.
