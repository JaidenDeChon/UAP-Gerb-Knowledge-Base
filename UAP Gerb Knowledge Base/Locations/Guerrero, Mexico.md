Guerrero is a municipality in Coahuila, Mexico, directly across the Rio Grande from [[El Indio, Texas]]. The alleged [[Majestic 12]] Eisenhower Briefing Document refers to a UAP crash retrieval on December 6, 1950, reportedly in the area between El Indio and Guerrero.

## The 1950 Crash

According to the Eisenhower Briefing Document, a UFO of similar origin to the one in the 1947 [[Roswell Crash|Roswell Incident]] hit the Earth at high speed on December 6, 1950, in the area between El Indio, Texas and Guerrero. It struck so fast that the document describes the craft as "nearly obliterated" on impact. The recovered material was reportedly taken to the [[Atomic Energy Commission]]'s facility at [[Sandia National Laboratories]] in Albuquerque, New Mexico, for study.

A local witness, Senor Flores, reported seeing a ball of fire fall from the sky and crash on an adjoining ranch near El Indio, where it started a grass fire. A day or two later, a military contingent arrived from Piedras Negras and hauled something away by truck. Flores could not confirm whether the military vehicles were American, Mexican or both.

The Guerrero crash is a separate case from the 1955 crash near [[Langtry, Texas]], in a different place and context. UFO literature sometimes conflates the two, grouping them together as the "[[Del Rio, Texas]]" incidents.
