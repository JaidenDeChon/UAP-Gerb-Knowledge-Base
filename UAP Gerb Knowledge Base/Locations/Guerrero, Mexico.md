Guerrero is a municipality in Coahuila, Mexico, located directly across the Rio Grande from [[El Indio, Texas]]. The area between El Indio and Guerrero is the reported location of a December 6, 1950 UAP crash retrieval referenced in the alleged [[Majestic 12]] Eisenhower Briefing Document.

## The 1950 Crash

According to the Eisenhower Briefing Document, a UFO of similar origin to the 1947 [[Roswell Incident]] impacted the Earth at high speed on December 6, 1950 in the area between El Indio, Texas and Guerrero. The craft struck at sufficient velocity to be described as "nearly obliterated" upon impact. Recovered materials were reportedly transported to the [[Atomic Energy Commission]]'s facility at [[Sandia National Laboratories]] in Albuquerque, New Mexico, for study.

A local witness, Senor Flores, reported seeing a ball of fire fall from the sky and crash on an adjoining ranch near El Indio, igniting a grass fire. A day or two later, a military contingent arrived from Piedras Negras and hauled something away by truck. Flores could not confirm whether the military vehicles were American, Mexican, or a combination of both.

The Guerrero location is geographically and contextually distinct from the 1955 crash near [[Langtry, Texas]], though the two cases are sometimes conflated in UFO literature as the collective "[[Del Rio, Texas]]" incidents.
