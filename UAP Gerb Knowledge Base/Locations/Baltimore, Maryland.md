Baltimore, Maryland is a major port city and the largest city in Maryland. It comes up in UAP research through the 1948 Aztec, New Mexico crash retrieval case, as the city of a journalist's alleged employer. The journalist's claimed identity could not be verified during a government sting operation.

## The Aztec Photo Sting and the Baltimore Sun

On September 30, 1950, US Army CID and FBI agents staged a sting operation to intercept a sale of alleged photographs from the 1948 [[Aztec, New Mexico]] crash retrieval. It targeted people connected to oilman [[Silas Newton]], a primary source for [[Frank Scully]]'s 1950 book *Behind the Flying Saucers*.

One party to the alleged photo sale presented himself as a representative of the Baltimore Sun, the major newspaper based in Baltimore. The operative used the name "Mr. Klene", and Gerb's investigation of the Aztec case established that no one by that name worked for the Baltimore Sun at the time of the sting. The video uses this detail as evidence that the government built the sting itself. On this reading it was a staged scenario meant to identify, monitor or discredit the people circulating crash retrieval claims, and not a straightforward criminal investigation of fraud.

The Baltimore Sun's alleged involvement was a cover identity, with no genuine press inquiry behind it. That fits a wider pattern Gerb identifies in the Aztec case: government agencies, including the FBI and Army CID, took the crash retrieval claims seriously enough to run active counterintelligence operations to suppress them.
