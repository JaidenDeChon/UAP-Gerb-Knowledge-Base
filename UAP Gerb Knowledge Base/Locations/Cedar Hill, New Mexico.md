Cedar Hill is a small community in San Juan County, in northwestern New Mexico. In UAP accounts it is where Ken Farley was heading on the day he turned off instead to investigate anomalous activity at Hart Canyon Road near Aztec, the site of the alleged 1948 [[Aztec UFO Case|Aztec UFO crash]].

## Connection to the Aztec Case

Ken Farley, a local resident whose testimony about the Aztec crash is on record, was driving toward Cedar Hill when he noticed unusual activity and military vehicles and turned onto Hart Canyon Road instead. Cedar Hill is about 10 miles northwest of Aztec, close to the site of the alleged March 1948 crash retrieval, in which a large disc was reportedly recovered from the canyon floor.

## Geographic Context

Cedar Hill lies in the San Juan Basin of northwestern New Mexico, a region that also includes Farmington and Aztec. In the late 1940s the basin was the site of multiple reported UAP sightings and encounters. Because the area was fairly isolated and close to oil and gas infrastructure, unusual vehicle movements there could have come from either industrial or military operations.
