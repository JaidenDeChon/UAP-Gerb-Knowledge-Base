Magenta is a municipality in the Metropolitan City of Milan, in the Lombardy region of northern Italy, west of Milan. Among historical UAP claims, it is known as the reported site where an unidentified craft crashed or made a forced landing in June 1933. The fascist regime allegedly recovered the craft.

## Geographic Context

Magenta lies near [[Milan, Italy]], in a heavily connected corridor of industry and farmland. That location makes it operationally plausible for historical narratives that involve a rapid state response, controlled media directives and the transport of sensitive material.

## Role in the 1933 Case

In [[Videos/The 1933 Magenta, Italy UFO Crash/summary|The 1933 Magenta, Italy UFO Crash]], Magenta is presented as the place where the incident began. According to the video, the incident led to:

- suppression telegrams issued through [[Agenzia Stefani]]
- the creation of [[RS-33 (Gabinetto RS 33)]]
- the later movement of recovered material to facilities in [[Varese, Italy]]

The video frames the case as the earliest traceable European crash retrieval in the modern timeline of UAP narratives.

## Historical Status of the Claim

Magenta's history as a place is not in dispute. Whether a non-human craft was recovered there is still debated, and the answer depends on how period documents and later testimony are interpreted.

## Role in the Broader UFO Legacy Program Timeline

In his timeline video "80 Years of UFO Crash Retrieval and Reverse Engineering", Gerb deliberately treats Magenta as a "preliminary entry". It comes 14 years before the US legacy program timeline, which runs from 1947 onward. Gerb cites [[David Grusch]]'s statement in a 2023 News Nation interview that Roswell was not the first crash retrieval. Drawing on the Marconi family's own account, the video describes physicist [[Enrico Fermi]] as the person who actually led the scientific examination of the recovered craft under the [[RS-33 (Gabinetto RS 33)|RS-33]] commission, working alongside [[Guglielmo Marconi]].
