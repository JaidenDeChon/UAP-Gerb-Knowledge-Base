Magenta is a municipality in the Metropolitan City of Milan, in the Lombardy region of northern Italy, west of Milan. In UAP historical claims, it is notable as the reported site of a June 1933 crash or forced landing of an unidentified craft that was allegedly recovered by the fascist regime.

## Geographic Context

Magenta lies in a heavily connected industrial-agricultural corridor near [[Milan, Italy]], making it operationally plausible in historical narratives involving rapid state response, controlled media directives, and transport of sensitive material.

## Role in the 1933 Case

In [[Video - The 1933 Magenta, Italy UFO Crash]], Magenta is presented as the origin point of the incident that triggered:

- suppression telegrams issued through [[Agenzia Stefani]],
- creation of [[RS-33 (Gabinetto RS 33)]],
- and subsequent movement of recovered material toward facilities in [[Varese, Italy]].

The claim is framed as the earliest traceable European crash-retrieval episode in the modern UAP narrative timeline.

## Historical Status of the Claim

The location itself is historically uncontroversial, but the assertion that a non-human craft was recovered there remains debated and depends on interpretation of period documents and later testimony.

## Role in the Broader UFO Legacy Program Timeline

UAP Gerb's "80 Years of UFO Crash Retrieval and Reverse Engineering" timeline video treats Magenta as a deliberate "preliminary entry" predating the 1947-onward US legacy program timeline by 14 years, citing [[David Grusch]]'s 2023 News Nation interview statement that Roswell was not the first crash retrieval. Physicist [[Enrico Fermi]] is described, per the Marconi family's own account, as the figure who actually spearheaded scientific examination of the recovered craft under the [[RS-33 (Gabinetto RS 33)|RS-33]] commission, working alongside [[Guglielmo Marconi]].
