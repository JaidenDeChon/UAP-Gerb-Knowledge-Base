McLean, Virginia, is an unincorporated community in Fairfax County, Virginia, immediately adjacent to [[Crystal City, Virginia]] and near the [[Pentagon]]. In UAP research it is best known as the site of the [[MITRE Corporation]]'s Virginia headquarters and its National Security Engineering Center (NSEC), a Federally Funded Research and Development Center (FFRDC) sponsored by the Department of Defense. McLean is also home to CIA headquarters in nearby [[Langley, Virginia]]. That places it in the same institutional corridor as several of the intelligence and defense agencies that Gerb alleges are connected to UFO legacy program activity.

## MITRE's Alleged Hidden Security Office

Gerb alleges that MITRE has an unacknowledged "hidden" Special Security Office, an internal security unit alleged to administer MITRE's classified programs for reverse-engineering UFO technology and monitoring USOs. According to Gerb, this office is located at unmarked MITRE satellite offices in McLean near Crystal City. It is separate from the SSO that MITRE acknowledges publicly on the same campus.
