McLean, Virginia, is an unincorporated community in Fairfax County, Virginia, immediately adjacent to [[Crystal City, Virginia]] and near the [[Pentagon]], best known within UAP research as the site of the [[MITRE Corporation]]'s Virginia headquarters and its Department of Defense-sponsored National Security Engineering Center (NSEC) FFRDC. McLean also hosts CIA headquarters in nearby [[Langley, Virginia]], placing it within the same institutional corridor as several of the intelligence and defense agencies UAP Gerb alleges are connected to UFO legacy program activity.

## MITRE's Alleged Hidden Security Office

UAP Gerb alleges that MITRE's unacknowledged "hidden" Special Security Office — the internal security apparatus alleged to administer MITRE's classified UFO technology reverse-engineering and USO-monitoring programs — is located at unmarked MITRE satellite offices in McLean near Crystal City, distinct from MITRE's acknowledged, publicly known SSO on the same campus.
