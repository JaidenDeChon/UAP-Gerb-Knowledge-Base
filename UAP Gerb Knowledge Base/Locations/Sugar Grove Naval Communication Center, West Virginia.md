The Sugar Grove Naval Communication Center is a US Navy communications facility in the mountains near Sugar Grove, West Virginia, about 150 miles southwest of Washington, D.C. It houses an underground installation operated on behalf of the National Security Agency (NSA), which makes it one of the most prominent examples of an intelligence agency running a clandestine subterranean facility under the cover of a military base above ground.

## Intelligence Community Operations

[[Richard Sauder]] and other researchers have documented that the Navy facility at Sugar Grove includes a deep underground installation, run by the Navy for the NSA's signals intelligence collection. In this arrangement a naval base provides cover for an NSA-controlled underground facility. It is one of several documented instances of intelligence community agencies using military infrastructure as cover for independent covert operations.

Gerb cites the Sugar Grove installation, together with the [[Warrenton, Virginia|Warrenton Army Training Center]] (CIA), as a precedent for intelligence agencies operating hidden underground facilities beneath military installations on the surface. The pattern supports the analysis that UFO legacy programs could likewise occupy subterranean installations beneath military bases that appear conventional.
