Kirtland Air Force Base is a United States Air Force installation next to Albuquerque, New Mexico. It co-hosts Sandia National Laboratories, a Department of Energy nuclear weapons research center. Kirtland is considered one of the most important sites in the alleged UFO legacy program infrastructure. It has been identified as the home of the alleged Aphotech program and as a central location in the network that classifies UAP-related materials.

## Aphotech and UFO Legacy Program

UAP research has specifically identified Kirtland as the location of an entity called "Aphotech," described as a classified program or organizational layer connected to the UFO legacy program infrastructure. [[Sandia National Laboratories]] operates under Department of Energy sponsorship from facilities on Kirtland's grounds. Together with Sandia, the base is theorized to be a primary site for analyzing, storing and classifying retrieved UAP materials under the DOE's transclassified foreign nuclear information framework.

## DOE and Sandia Connection

UAP researchers give particular weight to the fact that Sandia National Laboratories is at Kirtland. The Atomic Energy Commission (AEC), the DOE's predecessor, has been cited in connection with the misclassification of UAP crash retrieval records as "trans-classified foreign nuclear material", a classification standard that places materials outside the reach of congressional oversight and standard declassification procedures. The AEC designation is referenced in the 2024 UAP NDAA legislation authored by Senator Chuck Schumer.

## Broader New Mexico Network

Kirtland is at the center of the cluster of military and research installations in New Mexico that form the alleged post-Roswell UAP research infrastructure. The cluster also includes [[Holloman Air Force Base]], [[White Sands Missile Range]], [[Roswell, New Mexico|Roswell]], and [[Albuquerque, New Mexico|Albuquerque]].
