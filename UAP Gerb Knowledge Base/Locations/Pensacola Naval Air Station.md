Naval Air Station Pensacola (NAS Pensacola) is a United States Navy installation located in Pensacola, Florida. It is known as the "Cradle of Naval Aviation" and serves as the home of the US Navy's flight training program, the Blue Angels flight demonstration team, and the National Naval Aviation Museum. In UAP research, NAS Pensacola is mentioned by [[Bob Echler]] as the location from which the Navy allegedly conducted EMP (electromagnetic pulse) experiments using onboard generators designated EMPRESS and EMPRESS 2, designed to disable and bring down UFOs for recovery operations.

## EMPRESS EMP Experiments

According to Echler — who recounted this claim in connection with his 1989 inquiry into recovered UFO vehicles — the Navy conducted experiments at NAS Pensacola using onboard EMP generators to forcibly disable UAP for retrieval. The generators were designated EMPRESS and EMPRESS 2. No independent corroboration of this specific claim has been identified in public records; however, the claim fits within a broader pattern of testimony regarding active UAP interdiction methods allegedly employed by US military forces to acquire non-human craft. Such methods, if accurate, would be consistent with the Navy's alleged interest in expanding its UAP collection capability as evidenced by the [[Deep Submergence Systems Project (DSSP)]], [[Sand Dollar]], and the [[National Underwater Reconnaissance Office]] (NURO).
