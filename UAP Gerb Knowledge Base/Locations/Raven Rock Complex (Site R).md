The Raven Rock Complex, officially designated Site R, is a classified deep underground command facility located beneath Raven Rock Mountain near Blue Ridge Summit, Pennsylvania, approximately 6 miles north of Camp David. Often described as the "Underground Pentagon" or the "Backup Pentagon," the complex was constructed during the Cold War as a continuity-of-government installation and emergency command center for the US military.

## Physical Description

The acknowledged portions of Raven Rock include accommodations for approximately 3,000 personnel, with five acknowledged three-story buildings. The facility has its own inbuilt medical and dental clinic, fire department, post office, and extensive communications infrastructure. It was designed to allow the Secretary of Defense and the Joint Chiefs to continue operating even after a nuclear strike on Washington, D.C.
