The Raven Rock Complex, officially designated Site R, is a classified deep underground command facility beneath Raven Rock Mountain near Blue Ridge Summit, Pennsylvania, about 6 miles north of Camp David. Often called the "Underground Pentagon" or the "Backup Pentagon," the complex was built during the Cold War as a continuity-of-government installation and emergency command center for the US military.

## Physical Description

The acknowledged parts of Raven Rock can accommodate about 3,000 personnel and include five acknowledged three-story buildings. The facility has its own medical and dental clinic, fire department and post office, along with extensive communications infrastructure. It was designed to allow the Secretary of Defense and the Joint Chiefs to continue operating even after a nuclear strike on Washington, D.C.

## Significance as an Acknowledged DUMB

Raven Rock is cited alongside the Cheyenne Mountain Complex as one of the most prominent examples of a confirmed Deep Underground Military Base (DUMB). Because its existence is acknowledged, it shows that self-contained underground military facilities can house thousands of personnel.
