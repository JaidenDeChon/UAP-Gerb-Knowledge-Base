Boulder is a city in Boulder County, Colorado, at the base of the Rocky Mountain foothills northwest of Denver. In this wiki it matters as the place where Gerb first met Dr. [[Steven Greer]] in person, at an event in 2017. That meeting was a formative moment in Gerb's involvement with the organized UAP disclosure community.

## The 2017 Boulder Meeting with Dr. Greer

Gerb describes the meeting in [[Videos/Michael Herrera - Insights into UAP Encounter and Black Program Insiders/summary|Michael Herrera - Insights into UAP Encounter and Black Program Insiders]]. In 2017 he attended an event in Boulder, Colorado, where he met Dr. [[Steven Greer]] in person for the first time. He went hoping to compare notes on his own UAP experience and told Greer his account directly. The encounter is cited as an example of how independent UAP researchers and content creators came to know established figures in the disclosure movement in the years before the congressional testimony of 2022–2023.

The meeting is one documented step in Gerb's path from personal UAP witness to investigative content creator. The same video also covers his later dealings with Greer. Both attended the 2023 National Press Club (NPC 2023) conference, where Greer showed Gerb an encrypted text message on his phone. It came from an alleged CIA Non-Official Cover operative who claimed to know about the classified UAP programs that whistleblower [[Michael Herrera]] had encountered in Indonesia in 2009.

## Context in the Disclosure Landscape

The Mountain West has other UAP and consciousness research activity, but in this wiki Boulder matters only for its place in Gerb's personal history. The 2017 meeting with Greer came several years before the more formal era of congressional whistleblower and AARO testimony. It belongs to an earlier phase of information exchange at the community level, which Gerb has documented as part of his channel's origin story.
