Crystal City is a densely developed neighborhood in Arlington County, Virginia, immediately adjacent to [[McLean, Virginia]] and the [[Pentagon]]. Eight of the United States' ten largest defense contractors have their headquarters or major offices there, including [[Northrop Grumman]], [[Lockheed Martin]], [[Boeing]], [[Raytheon]], [[General Dynamics]], L3Harris, Aerojet, and Rocketdyne, and roughly two-thirds of federal contracts by value are awarded through the neighborhood. In Gerb's investigative framework, Crystal City is "the beating heart of UFO legacy programs in the east of the United States," the administrative hub where contracting, program briefings, and security operations for alleged UFO legacy programs are carried out.

## Institutional Density

Besides the defense contractors, Crystal City has several federally funded research and development centers (FFRDCs) in or near it that Gerb alleges are involved in UFO-related research. They include the Center for Naval Analyses, the Institute for Defense Analyses, the [[Aerospace Corporation]], and the RAND Homeland Security Operations Analysis Center. Major Department of Defense research agencies are also based in the neighborhood, including [[DARPA]], the Office of Naval Research, and the Air Force Office of Scientific Research.

## MITRE Presence

The [[MITRE Corporation]] has its Virginia headquarters and its National Security Engineering Center in nearby [[McLean, Virginia]]. Gerb alleges that MITRE's unacknowledged "hidden" Special Security Office, the security apparatus alleged to administer MITRE's classified UFO reverse-engineering and USO-monitoring programs, is located at unmarked satellite offices in the Crystal City area. He also notes that the [[Air Force Office of Special Investigations]]' Office of Special Projects (AFOSI/PJ), a counterintelligence unit alleged to support MITRE's legacy programs, has permanent offices in Crystal City. Gerb quotes a former Special Access Program security specialist who told the channel: "The answer to everything you are looking for lies in Crystal City."
