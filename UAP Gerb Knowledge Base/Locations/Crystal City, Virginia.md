Crystal City is a densely developed neighborhood in Arlington County, Virginia, immediately adjacent to [[McLean, Virginia]] and the [[Pentagon]], home to the headquarters or major offices of eight of the United States' ten largest defense contractors — including [[Northrop Grumman]], [[Lockheed Martin]], [[Boeing]], [[Raytheon]], [[General Dynamics]], L3Harris, Aerojet, and Rocketdyne — and the site through which roughly two-thirds of federal contracts are awarded by value. In UAP Gerb's investigative framework, Crystal City is identified as "the beating heart of UFO legacy programs in the east of the United States," the administrative hub where contracting, program briefings, and security operations for alleged UFO legacy programs are conducted.

## Institutional Density

Beyond its concentration of major defense contractors, Crystal City is home to or in close proximity to several federally funded research and development centers (FFRDCs) UAP Gerb alleges are involved in UFO-related research, including the Center for Naval Analyses, the Institute for Defense Analyses, the [[Aerospace Corporation]], and the RAND Homeland Security Operations Analysis Center. The neighborhood also hosts major Department of Defense research agencies, including [[DARPA]], the Office of Naval Research, and the Air Force Office of Scientific Research.

## MITRE Presence

The [[MITRE Corporation]] maintains its Virginia headquarters and its National Security Engineering Center in nearby [[McLean, Virginia]], and UAP Gerb alleges that MITRE's unacknowledged "hidden" Special Security Office — the security apparatus alleged to administer MITRE's classified UFO reverse-engineering and USO-monitoring programs — is located at unmarked satellite offices in the Crystal City area. UAP Gerb further notes that the [[Air Force Office of Special Investigations]]' Office of Special Projects (AFOSI/PJ), a counterintelligence unit alleged to support MITRE's legacy programs, maintains permanent offices in Crystal City. A former Special Access Program security specialist is quoted by UAP Gerb as telling the channel: "The answer to everything you are looking for lies in Crystal City."
