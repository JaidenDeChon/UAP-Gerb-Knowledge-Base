Marine Corps Air Station Yuma (MCAS Yuma) is a United States Marine Corps installation in Yuma, Arizona. It is one of the busiest air stations in the Marine Corps and is home to numerous aviation squadrons. Its clear weather all year and the vast desert ranges around it make it a primary location for weapons and tactics training.

## UAP Connections

MCAS Yuma comes up in accounts of the 1997 [[Hunter Warrior Advanced Warfighting Experiment]], where it is named as one of the Marine Corps aviation installations whose squadrons took part in or supported the exercise at [[Marine Corps Air Ground Combat Center 29 Palms]]. Because the base is close to 29 Palms and supports Marine aviation exercises, it belongs to the group of Southwest US military installations where UAP-related incidents have been reported.
