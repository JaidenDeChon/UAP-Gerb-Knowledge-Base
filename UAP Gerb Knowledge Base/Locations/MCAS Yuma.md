Marine Corps Air Station Yuma (MCAS Yuma) is a United States Marine Corps installation located in Yuma, Arizona. MCAS Yuma serves as one of the busiest air stations in the Marine Corps, hosting numerous aviation squadrons and serving as a primary location for weapons and tactics training due to its year-round clear weather and vast surrounding desert ranges.

## UAP Connections

MCAS Yuma is referenced in the context of the 1997 [[Hunter Warrior Advanced Warfighting Experiment]] as one of the Marine Corps aviation installations whose squadrons participated in or supported the exercise at [[Marine Corps Air Ground Combat Center 29 Palms]]. The base's proximity to 29 Palms and its role in supporting Marine aviation exercises places it within the network of Southwest US military installations where UAP-related incidents have been reported.
