In the July 1947 reports about Roswell, Fort Worth, Texas was an important transfer point. Witness accounts and later affidavits say the press was handled at command level at Fort Worth Army Air Field. There, the office of Brigadier General Roger Ramey showed reporters debris from a weather balloon, after Roswell Army Air Field had announced the recovery of a "flying disc."

## Roswell Narrative Role

In Roswell timelines, Fort Worth is where the public explanation changed from the recovery of an anomalous craft to the identification of a weather balloon.
