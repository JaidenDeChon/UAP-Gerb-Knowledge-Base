Fort Worth, Texas is a key Roswell-linked transfer location in July 1947 reporting. Witness accounts and later affidavits place command-level media handling at Fort Worth Army Air Field, where Brigadier General Roger Ramey's office presented weather-balloon debris to the press after Roswell Army Air Field had announced recovery of a "flying disc."

## Roswell Narrative Role

In Roswell timelines, Fort Worth functions as the pivot point where the public explanation changed from anomalous-craft recovery to weather-balloon identification.
