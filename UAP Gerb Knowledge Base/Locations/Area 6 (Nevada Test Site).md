Area 6 is a numbered area of the [[Nevada National Security Site (Nevada Test Site)|Nevada National Security Site]], the former Nevada Test Site, located on Yucca Flat in Nye County, Nevada. Used since the 1950s for nuclear testing work, it now houses an Aerial Operations Facility for testing unmanned aerial vehicles and sensors, with a paved runway at Yucca Lake and a hangar complex. The site attracted press attention in 2016, when [[National Nuclear Security Administration]] spokesperson Darwin Morgan described its work to the *Las Vegas Review-Journal*.

## Facilities and Mission

According to Morgan, Area 6 hosts top secret research and development, mainly sensor and UAV testing. He said its partners include the Air Force, Army, Navy, [[DARPA]] and the [[Defense Threat Reduction Agency (DTRA)|Defense Threat Reduction Agency]], and that it uses advanced equipment that evolved from the nuclear testing program. In 2005 a dirt airstrip from the 1950s was rebuilt, with a runway and apron that cost $9.6 million. According to Gerb, they were built by Bechtel Nevada, the site's management contractor at the time. The hangars could hold 15 or more MQ-9 Reaper drones. The *Review-Journal* reported that Area 6 was funded through the NNSA Nevada Field Office's strategic partner program, which took about 13.5 percent of the office's $662 million budget in fiscal 2015.

A 2004 NNSA and DOE environmental assessment states that the Aerial Operations Facility was established in 2001 "to construct, operate, and test a variety of unmanned aerial vehicles", including airframe modifications, sensor operation and onboard computer development. The test site's resource management plan places it in a national security zone reserved for projects that need security, restricted access or a remote location. Gerb says the facility is operated by [[Lockheed Martin]]. According to the website Lazy G Ranch, the airfield, designated NVH1 and not listed in FAA records, was visited as recently as 2020 by N20RA, a Janet Airlines turboprop associated with [[Area 51]].

## Sandia Presence

Photographs taken by [[EG&G]]'s Nevada Test Site photo lab, which documented [[Sandia National Laboratories]]' unclassified test work from 1972 to 1992, show a Sandia compound at Area 6, including Sandia and Department of Defense control rooms photographed in 1974 and 1975.

## Alleged UFO Connections

In his investigation of Sandia, Gerb proposes Area 6 as a possible site of a UFO legacy program. His reasons are Sandia's long presence there, the NNSA's control of the area, and its location about 75 miles southeast of the [[Tonopah Test Range (TTR)|Tonopah Test Range]]'s main airfield. He also cites Lockheed Martin's roles as operator of the Aerial Operations Facility and as Sandia's manager from 1993 to 2017, and DARPA's presence among Area 6's customers. Gerb sets all this beside a 2021 source of [[Richard Dolan]]'s, who described DARPA-run programs that used recovered non-human technology flown out of Area 51 and Tonopah and were managed by an "Advanced Group Six". Without offering support, Gerb speculates that the group's name could refer to Area 6.
