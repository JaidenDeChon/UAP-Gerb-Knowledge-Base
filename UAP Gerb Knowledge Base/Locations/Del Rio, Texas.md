Del Rio is a city in Val Verde County, Texas, on the Rio Grande along the US-Mexico border, about 150 miles west of San Antonio. UAP researchers associate the region with two separate crash retrieval incidents from the 1950s. UFO literature often conflates them, although they differ in geography, timing, witness accounts and corroborating context. One is a near-incineration event on December 6, 1950 near [[El Indio, Texas]], 75 miles southeast of Del Rio. The other is a 1955 crash near [[Langtry, Texas]], 60 miles northwest of Del Rio. Together they are known as the "Del Rio cases."

## The 1950 El Indio Crash

The alleged [[Majestic 12]] Eisenhower Briefing Document refers to a UAP impact on December 6, 1950 between El Indio, Texas and Guerrero, Mexico, in which a craft traveling at high speed was reportedly nearly obliterated. The recovered material was described as having been taken to the Atomic Energy Commission's facility at [[Sandia National Laboratories]] in New Mexico. The same day, at 10:30 AM Eastern, a nationwide US alert was called over unidentified aircraft approaching the Northeast at altitude. [[General Ramey]] cancelled it at 1:16 PM. An FBI memo to [[J. Edgar Hoover]] dated December 8, 1950 placed Army intelligence on high alert for flying disc data. See [[El Indio, Texas]].

## The 1955 Langtry Crash

In spring 1955, USAF Reserve Colonel [[Robert Willingham]] claimed to have witnessed a UAP crash just south of the Rio Grande near [[Langtry, Texas]] while escorting bombers from [[Carswell Air Force Base]]. Several pilots saw the craft, and Distant Early Warning radar in Canada detected it. Willingham and his companion [[Jack Perkins]] flew privately to the crash site in a small aircraft. There they found Mexican soldiers guarding the perimeter while they waited for an American retrieval team. See [[Robert Willingham]].

## Significance in UFO Research

Researchers often cite the Del Rio cases as among the more credible mid-century crash retrieval accounts because of the corroborating context documented around them. This includes a Distant Early Warning (DEW) radar alert at the time and a nationwide military UFO alert on the date of the 1950 crash. It also includes congressional-level documentation in the alleged MJ-12 briefing materials, and physicist [[Eric Davis]]'s 2018 endorsement of the Del Rio area as a "really good bet" alongside Roswell in a *Coast to Coast AM* interview with [[George Knapp]]. The video draws connections between the 1950 El Indio debris reportedly sent to Sandia, [[AT&T]]'s management of Sandia from 1949 to 1993, alleged ET technology transfers to AT&T documented in [[Steven Greer]]'s Disclosure Project archives, and the handover of Sandia's management to [[Lockheed Martin]] in 1993.
