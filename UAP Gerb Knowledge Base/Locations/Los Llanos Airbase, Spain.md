Los Llanos Air Base is a Spanish Air Force base south-east of Albacete, in the Castilla–La Mancha region of central Spain. It shares its runway with Albacete Airport and is the home of the Air Force's 14th Wing (Ala 14), a fighter wing.

## The Manises incident

At about 00:40 on 12 November 1979, Captain [[Fernando Cámara]] took off from Los Llanos in a Mirage F1 fighter to identify the lights that had forced [[TAE (Trabajos Aéreos y Enlaces)|TAE]] flight JK 297 to divert to [[Manises Airport, Valencia, Spain|Manises Airport]] an hour earlier. His pursuit lasted roughly ninety minutes. During it he reported that his avionics were jammed and that his alert system warned of a missile-radar lock. This chase is the military half of the [[Manises UFO Incident]].
