Los Llanos Air Base is a Spanish Air Force base south-east of Albacete, in the Castilla–La Mancha region of central Spain, whose runway it shares with Albacete Airport. It is the home of the Air Force's 14th Wing (Ala 14), a fighter wing.

## The Manises incident

In the early hours of 12 November 1979, Los Llanos was the base from which Captain [[Fernando Cámara]] took off in a Mirage F1 fighter, at about 00:40, to identify the lights that had forced [[TAE (Trabajos Aéreos y Enlaces)|TAE]] flight JK 297 to divert to [[Manises Airport, Valencia, Spain|Manises Airport]] an hour earlier. His roughly ninety-minute pursuit, during which he reported that his avionics were jammed and that his alert system warned of a missile-radar lock, is the military half of the [[Manises UFO Incident]].
