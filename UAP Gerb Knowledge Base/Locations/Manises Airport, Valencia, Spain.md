Manises Airport in Valencia, Spain, is the site of the first recorded emergency landing of a commercial flight due to a UFO encounter. On November 11, 1979, [[TAE (Trabajos Aéreos y Enlaces)|TAE]] flight JK 297 made an unscheduled landing at the airport after encountering unidentified red lights over the Mediterranean Sea, an incident that became known as the [[Manises UFO Incident]].

## The Manises UFO Incident

Late on the night of 11 November 1979, Captain [[Francisco Javier Lerdo de Tejada]] landed his Super Caravelle, carrying 109 passengers, at Manises Airport. Two powerful red lights had followed each change in the aircraft's altitude and stayed close to it despite evasive maneuvers. [[Barcelona Air Traffic Control]] had confirmed that no other aircraft were in the area, so the captain decided that continuing the flight would violate aerial safety protocols.

Shortly before the emergency landing, the flight crew detected three more UFO radar signatures, each estimated at 200 meters across. Airport staff at Manises saw the anomalous moving lights independently, as did Marines stationed at the nearby [[Manises Air Force Base, Spain|Manises Air Force Base]]. When one of the radar signatures passed close to the airport runways, ground crew switched on the emergency lighting in case the object was an aircraft in technical difficulty.

## Military Response

In response to the incident, Spanish Air Force pilot [[Fernando Cámara]] was scrambled at about 00:40 from [[Los Llanos Airbase, Spain|Los Llanos Airbase]] in a Mirage F1 fighter jet to intercept and identify the UFOs. His pursuit lasted about 90 minutes. The jamming of his avionics during it was documented, and the unidentified craft appeared to use electronic countermeasures.

## Significance

Manises Airport is a significant location in UFO history. It is where a commercial flight was first grounded because of UFOs, and airport and military personnel independently corroborated the sighting. The incident reached the [[Spanish Parliament]] in September 1980, where it was officially dismissed as optical illusions despite several kinds of evidence.
