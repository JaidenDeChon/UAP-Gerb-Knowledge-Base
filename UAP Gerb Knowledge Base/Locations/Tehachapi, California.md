Tehachapi (also referred to as Tahon or Tejon Ranch by UFO researchers) is a region in Kern County, California, in the Antelope Valley area near [[Edwards Air Force Base]]. The Tehachapi Mountains contain a [[Northrop Grumman]] radar cross-section (RCS) test range and an alleged underground facility that multiple sources have linked to UFO legacy program operations.

## Tehachapi Triangle

In the spring of 1992, forensic illustrator [[Bill McDonald]] met with four engineers at a Denny's restaurant in Antelope Valley: two from [[Northrop Grumman]] and two from [[Lockheed Martin]]. The engineers, frustrated by extreme security impositions, described a triangular craft that residents had been reporting for years: the [[XF-131 Super Sentinel]]. The two Northrop employees worked at a facility called Tehachapi Ranch, while the two Lockheed employees worked for [[Ben Rich]] at Skunk Works and operated the radar cross-section range known as [[Helendale]]. From their testimony, McDonald produced a forensic illustration known as the "Tehachapi Triangle 1992".

According to McDonald, the facility was allegedly part of Antelope Valley's network of legacy program operations, along with Helendale and Edwards AFB.

## Underground Facility Claims

The Tehachapi Ranch facility is alleged to house an extensive underground complex known as the [[Anthill]]. Informants whose accounts [[Bill Hamilton]] compiled claimed that the tunnels beneath the facility had round doorways with red and green entry lights, and that staff on site rotated every 14–16 days. A source who claimed to work on "Project Star Talk" at the facility described a large underground building that used lasers to interact with or "bring in" UAP by means of scalar technology. A local property owner on 170th Street west of Tehachapi reportedly saw the ground open like a missile silo and a flying saucer emerge from it. Edwards AFB OSI personnel allegedly silenced this witness.

[[Bill Hamilton]] was deeply invested in the Tehachapi facility and compiled the largest body of firsthand testimony about its alleged underground operations. His assessment was that the Tehachapi Anthill was the primary underground node in a network of UFO legacy program facilities across the Antelope Valley. [[Richard Sauder]]'s documentary research, which confirmed an underground facility at the site, corroborated that assessment.
