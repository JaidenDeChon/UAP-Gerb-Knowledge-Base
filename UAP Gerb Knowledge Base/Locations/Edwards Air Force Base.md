Edwards Air Force Base is a United States Air Force installation in the Mojave Desert of Kern County, California, about 100 miles north of Los Angeles. It is the home of the [[Air Force Test Center]] and the [[412th Test Wing|Edwards 412th Test Wing]], the primary Air Force organization responsible for developmental flight testing of new aircraft, weapons systems, and technologies. Edwards also hosts the [[Air Force Test Pilot School]], the service's prestigious school for training military test pilots. Some of the most advanced aircraft in U.S. history were developed at the base, including classified programs run under Air Force Materiel Command ([[Air Force Materiel Command (AFMC)]]).

## UAP Significance

In "The Hidden Wing", Gerb identifies Edwards AFB as the alleged primary site for test and evaluation of [[Alien Reproduction Vehicle (ARV)|alien reproduction vehicles (ARVs)]] and derivative non-human airframes under the alleged [[Hidden Wing]] program portfolio. A witness known as [[Ed (Witness 11063)|"Ed"]], a retired Air Force officer, claimed he served on an ARV test and evaluation (T&E) program at the [[412th Test Wing|Edwards 412th Test Wing]]. He said the program involved both manned and unmanned reverse-engineered craft, flown by pilots trained at the [[Air Force Test Pilot School]]. Non-human and derivative craft are also allegedly transported to Edwards from [[Air Force Plant 42]] for advanced test and evaluation.

Gerb's "80 Years of UFO Crash Retrieval and Reverse Engineering" timeline video describes Edwards as "the test and evaluation hub" for piloting alleged NHI craft, ARVs and derivative airframes. The video traces this role through the full line of organizations that preceded Air Force Materiel Command (AFMC): Air Materiel Command, Air Research and Development Command, and Air Force Systems Command. Each is alleged to have kept a major presence at Edwards through the flight-test chain. The video ends the line with AFMC's modern-day Air Force Test Center (AFTC) and Air Force Research Laboratory (AFRL) elements, which operate out of the [[412th Test Wing|Edwards 412th Test Wing]] major range and test facility base (MRTFB). Gerb identifies Edwards and [[Wright-Patterson Air Force Base]] as the Air Force's two most critical legacy program locations.

## Connection to TR-3B and Edgar Fouché

[[Edgar Fouché]] was recruited to [[Area 51]] Groom Lake from his station at the Jet Propulsion Laboratory at Edwards Air Force Base, which he claimed also housed deep underground facilities. Fouché was assigned to Detachment 3 AFTC (Air Force Flight Test Center), the Edwards detachment that runs operations at Area 51. One of Fouché's five main sources, "Doc," was an SR-71 spy pilot and USAF test pilot at Edwards. Fouché claimed he saw triangular [[TR-3B]] prototypes high in the atmosphere over Edwards one night in 1975, and again in 1976 in the southern Nellis range and in 1979 at Groom Lake.

Edwards lies near [[Helendale]] (Lockheed's radar cross-section range), USAF [[Air Force Plant 42|Plant 42]] in Palmdale and the Northrop Grumman [[Tehachapi, California|Tehachapi]] Mountain facility, which puts it at the center of a cluster of alleged reverse engineering locations in the Antelope Valley region.

## Earlier accounts gathered by Gerb

In *UAP Reverse Engineering at Edwards Air Force Base*, Gerb tests Ed's account against older stories about the base:

- **1957.** Mercury astronaut [[Gordon Cooper]] said cameramen filming a precision landing facility at Edwards caught a silver saucer about 30 feet across landing on three landing gear on the dry lake bed; the film was sent to Washington by courier.
- **Late 1960s–early 1970s.** A physicist called Dr. Epigoni told [[Leonard Stringfield]] of [[Project Heaven]], in which a landed 40-foot saucer was kept in a guarded Edwards hangar and its occupants taken to a secure building.
- **1971.** [[Debbie Clayton]] described an acorn-shaped craft crashing about 15 miles outside the base and armed airmen clearing the site within 14 minutes.
- **1961–1963.** A witness known as MH said a recovered craft shipped from Edwards was test-flown at Area 51 under [[Project Redlight|Project Red Light]].
- **1972 and 1988.** [[Project Outgrowth]], from the Air Force Rocket Propulsion Laboratory, discussed an anti-gravity program, and a 1988 report for the base's astronautics laboratory explored the [[Biefeld-Brown Effect]].
- **1990.** Aviation Week reported sightings of craft accelerating from near-hover to supersonic speed over Edwards, George Air Force Base and China Lake.

Greer's sources also place one half of the [[Blackjack Team]] pairing, named in an alleged 1991 NRO document, at Edwards.

## Underground Facilities

Several informants whose accounts [[Bill Hamilton]] compiled described extraterrestrial discs stored underground at Edwards, and in particular a command center beneath Haystack Butte (the eastern test facilities area). [[Michael Wolf]] also claimed to have heard that live extraterrestrials were kept at a facility under Haystack Butte. The Air Force Research Laboratory's Aerospace Systems Directorate facilities are built into and around Haystack Butte, and rocket propulsion laboratory silos sit on Lumen Ridge. Both are pre-existing infrastructure that could accommodate deeper underground expansion. The Edwards/NTTR underground network, mentioned by witness "Ed," forms the core of the alleged southwestern deep underground military base (DUMB) complex centered on the Antelope Valley.
