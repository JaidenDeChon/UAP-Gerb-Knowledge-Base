Madison is the capital city of the US state of Wisconsin. In UFO research it is associated with a triangle sighting on 22 November 1985, when several witnesses in the countryside northwest of the city reported a large, dull triangular craft.

## The 1985 triangle

According to the account quoted by researcher [[Michael Schratt]], the craft was roughly 40 feet across, and one eyewitness said its underside resembled the back of a refrigerator, a collection of condensation pipes running back and forth. In *Alien Reproduction Vehicle - TR-3B and the Flying Triangles*, UAP Gerb groups the case with other oddly equipped triangles of the 1980s and 1990s, among them the 1993 [[Croton Falls Reservoir, New York|Croton Falls]] and 2000 [[St. Clair, Southern Illinois|St. Clair]] sightings, and asks whether it was a human-built [[Alien Reproduction Vehicle (ARV)|alien reproduction vehicle]] prototype whose propulsion needed a massive heat-exchanger apparatus.
