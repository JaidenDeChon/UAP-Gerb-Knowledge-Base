Tejon Ranch is a large private landholding in the Tehachapi Mountains of California, along the I-5 corridor approximately 60 miles north of Los Angeles. [[Northrop Grumman]] ran a radar cross-section (RCS) testing facility there, known variously as the Tehachapi RCS Range or the Tejon facility, which tested stealth aircraft including the B-2 from the 1980s. Northrop leased the land from the Tejon Ranch Company until 1998, when it bought the full 1,415 acres under the range. It officially closed the facility in 2011, saying nearby solar and wind developments interfered with testing. In November 2024 the property was sold to EDF Power Solutions and then to Scout Clean Energy, trading as Keyhole Wind LLC, for a wind project due to be completed by 2028.

## Alleged Underground Activity

Gerb places Tejon, with Lockheed's sister site at [[Helendale]], in an alleged network of underground UFO facilities beneath the Antelope Valley. Despite the official account of its closure, the channel alleges that the surface campus is a "staged dereliction", deliberately kept looking abandoned while underground operations continue. Among the evidence it cites, content creator Wasteland by Wednesday found the interior of the facility suspiciously clean and free of debris despite over a decade of alleged abandonment. Spent bullet casings were found inside buildings, consistent with active security operations. A locked generator building contained ventilation shafts leading underground. Copies of [[Project Blue Book]] documents were found scattered inside. A witness reported a Black Hawk-type helicopter landing near the main hangar, staying approximately 20 minutes and then leaving eastward. A person cycling near the security perimeter reported a burst of light, skin burning and nausea consistent with a directed energy weapon. Engineers also reportedly told researcher [[Bill Hamilton]] that they had been involved in digging a massive underground complex beneath the facility, which they called "the Anthill". In late 2025 Anders of the Uncanny Expeditions channel filmed the RCS runways and pylons by drone and found a pit whose ladder appeared to continue downward beneath a bolted cover. That civilian exploration followed the 2024 sale, which Gerb says "appears to be very above board".

Gerb says he remains skeptical of claims that Tejon has 42 underground levels, but maintains that the site was important to Western Range UFO programs and likely still is. In *Northrop Grumman & TRW* Gerb argues that if Northrop runs its own breakaway retrieval programs, a security chief like [[Terry Phillips]] would keep their material at sites like Tejon rather than in traditional government channels. The channel ties the site to Western Range research, development, test and evaluation of recovered non-human vehicles.
