Nellis Air Force Base is a United States Air Force base about 8 miles northeast of Las Vegas, Nevada. It manages the [[Nevada Test and Training Range (NTR)|Nevada Test and Training Range]] (NTTR), the largest contiguous air and ground space available for peacetime military operations in the free world. Nellis is connected to operations at [[Area 51]] (Groom Lake), and multiple UAP witnesses have identified it as a staging point for classified programs involving reverse-engineered craft.

## UAP Significance

[[Edgar Fouche|Edgar Fouché]] served at Nellis with top secret crypto access clearance before being recruited to [[Area 51]] in 1979. On Groom Lake assignments he left Nellis before sunrise on a blacked-out bus and came back after dark. Fouché claimed he saw a [[TR-3B]] prototype in 1976 in the southern part of the Nellis range.

[[Project Redlight]], the alleged program for testing NHI and reverse-engineered craft, reportedly operated on the Nellis test range after craft were shipped from [[Edwards Air Force Base]]. Witness [[Ed (Witness 11063)|"Ed"]] served at Nellis from 1999 to 2002. According to [[Steven Greer]]'s notes, Ed worked with [[Lockheed Martin]] as a range technical contractor and at [[Building 200]]. He was briefed on captured craft at Area 51's S4 site and saw man-made craft on the range, before moving to Hanscom and then Edwards. Greer's sources place Blackjack Control, the partner of the [[Blackjack Team]] named in an alleged 1991 National Reconnaissance Office (NRO) document, at Nellis.
