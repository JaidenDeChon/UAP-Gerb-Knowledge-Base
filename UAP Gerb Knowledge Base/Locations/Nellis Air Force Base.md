Nellis Air Force Base is a United States Air Force installation located approximately 8 miles northeast of Las Vegas, Nevada. The base manages the [[Nevada Test and Training Range (NTR)|Nevada Test and Training Range]] (NTTR), the largest contiguous air and ground space available for peacetime military operations in the free world. Nellis is connected to [[Area 51]] (Groom Lake) operations and has been identified by multiple UAP witnesses as a staging point for classified programs involving reverse-engineered craft.

## UAP Significance

[[Edgar Fouché]] served at Nellis with top secret crypto access clearance before being recruited to [[Area 51]] in 1979. His routine during Groom Lake assignments involved departing Nellis before sunrise on a blacked-out bus and returning after dark. Fouché claimed to have observed a [[TR-3B]] prototype in 1976 within the southern part of the Nellis range.

[[Project Redlight]], the alleged program for testing NHI and reverse-engineered craft, reportedly operated on the Nellis test range after craft were shipped from [[Edwards Air Force Base]]. Witness [[Ed (Witness 11063)|"Ed"]] served at Nellis from 1999 to 2002. According to [[Steven Greer]]'s notes, he worked with [[Lockheed Martin]] as a range technical contractor and at [[Building 200]], was briefed at Area 51's S4 site on captured craft, and saw man-made craft on the range before moving to Hanscom and then Edwards. Greer's sources place Blackjack Control, the partner of the [[Blackjack Team]] named in an alleged 1991 NRO document, at Nellis.
