El Segundo is a city in Los Angeles County, California, located adjacent to Los Angeles International Airport (LAX). The city has been a major hub for aerospace and defense industry operations since World War II, with numerous classified facilities operated by defense contractors and intelligence agencies.

## Defense Support Program Operations

El Segundo hosts (or hosted) highly classified facilities supporting [[Defense Support Program (DSP)]] satellite operations. These facilities are equipped with secure compartmented information facilities (SCIFs) requiring Top Secret/Sensitive Compartmented Information (TS/SCI) clearance for access.

## Bob Fish Testimony

In his 2015 email to [[John Podesta]], revealed through [[WikiLeaks]], [[Bob Fish]] described having lunch with DSP personnel "in the cafeteria of a highly classified organization in El Segundo, California" during preparations for [[Operation Desert Shield]] and [[Operation Desert Storm]] in the late 1980s and early 1990s.

Fish emphasized that **"no one could get into the cafeteria without TS/SCI clearance"** — indicating that everyone present was working on the most sensitive national security programs. It was in this setting that Fish first heard DSP operators discuss detecting a [[Fast Walkers|Fast Walker]] that had entered Earth's atmosphere from deep space and made a 30-degree course correction, indicating controlled flight.

## Aerospace Corporation Headquarters

[[Aerospace Corporation]], identified as a suspected UAP legacy program contractor, is headquartered in El Segundo. The corporation's documented role in analyzing DSP satellite data — including development of "hazard support systems" mentioned in official DSP fact sheets — connects its El Segundo operations to Fast Walker detection and analysis.

The proximity and organizational connections between Aerospace Corporation's headquarters and DSP operational facilities in El Segundo suggests potential integration of satellite detection data with the corporation's broader research activities.

## Defense Contractor Presence

El Segundo is home to numerous major defense contractors and classified facilities, including operations by [[Northrop Grumman]], [[Raytheon]], and other aerospace companies involved in satellite systems, space surveillance, and classified programs. The concentration of TS/SCI facilities in the city reflects its role as a major node in US military space operations and intelligence collection.
