El Segundo is a city in Los Angeles County, California, next to Los Angeles International Airport (LAX). It has been a major center of the aerospace and defense industry since World War II, and defense contractors and intelligence agencies run many classified facilities there.

## Defense Support Program Operations

El Segundo has (or had) highly classified facilities that support [[Defense Support Program (DSP)]] satellite operations. They contain secure compartmented information facilities (SCIFs), which only people with Top Secret/Sensitive Compartmented Information (TS/SCI) clearance can enter.

## Bob Fish Testimony

In his 2015 email to [[John Podesta]], made public through [[WikiLeaks]], [[Bob Fish]] described having lunch with DSP personnel "in the cafeteria of a highly classified organization in El Segundo, California" while [[Operation Desert Shield]] and [[Operation Desert Storm]] were being prepared in the late 1980s and early 1990s.

Fish stressed that "no one could get into the cafeteria without TS/SCI clearance", which meant that everyone present was working on the most sensitive national security programs. There Fish first heard DSP operators discuss detecting a [[Fast Walkers|Fast Walker]] that had entered Earth's atmosphere from deep space and made a 30-degree course correction, a sign of controlled flight.

## Aerospace Corporation Headquarters

[[Aerospace Corporation]], which has been identified as a suspected UAP legacy program contractor, has its headquarters in El Segundo. The corporation has a documented role in analyzing DSP satellite data, including developing the "hazard support systems" mentioned in official DSP fact sheets, and that work connects its El Segundo operations to the detection and analysis of Fast Walkers.

Aerospace Corporation's headquarters is close to the DSP operational facilities in El Segundo and has organizational ties to them. This suggests that the corporation's wider research may draw on satellite detection data.

## Defense Contractor Presence

Many major defense contractors and classified facilities are based in El Segundo, including operations run by [[Northrop Grumman]], [[Raytheon]], and other aerospace companies that work on satellite systems, space surveillance, and classified programs. The concentration of TS/SCI facilities in the city reflects its role as a major site for US military space operations and intelligence collection.
