Langley, Virginia, is the site of CIA Headquarters, formally known as the George Bush Center for Intelligence. Technically it is a census-designated place within McLean, Fairfax County. In research on UAP legacy programs, Langley matters mainly as the place where NASA mission specialist [[Bob Oechsler]] met [[Everett Heinman]], Deputy Director of the CIA Directorate of Science and Technology (DS&T), on or around August 10, 1989. The meeting was arranged at the direction of Admiral [[Bobby Ray Inman]], as part of an inquiry into whether recovered non-human vehicles could be made available for scientific research. Langley is also home to the headquarters of [[Air Combat Command]] (ACC), at the adjacent [[Langley Air Force Base]].

## The Oechsler-Heinman Meeting

In 1989 Oechsler, a NASA mission specialist who had been introduced to UAP-related channels through British military officer Lord Hill Norton, contacted retired Admiral [[Bobby Ray Inman]]. He asked about the possibility of gaining access to recovered UAP vehicles for scientific and technological research. Inman was a four-star Navy Admiral who had served as Director of Naval Intelligence, Director of the NSA, Deputy Director of the CIA, and Director of the classified [[National Underwater Reconnaissance Office|National Underwater Reconnaissance Office (NURO)]]. He told Oechsler that [[Everett Heinman]], then CIA DS&T Deputy Director, was "the best person to ask" about recovered craft in operational condition.

Oechsler then traveled to CIA Headquarters at Langley to meet Heinman, who denied any knowledge of UAP or UAP legacy programs during the meeting. In 2022 the Twitter user RGH_UFOs contacted Heinman and mentioned Inman's referral. This time Heinman did not deny it outright, saying only that he was "far removed from that area of work". UAP researchers treat this ambiguous, partial answer as meaningfully different from the categorical denial expected from someone with no knowledge of such programs.

The exchange matters for two reasons. First, it shows that Admiral Inman knew specifically which CIA officials managed recovered craft programs, a level of detail only possible if Inman himself was operationally familiar with how the program was structured. Second, it establishes CIA DS&T at Langley as a node in the alleged program management network, which supports the hypothesis that the CIA's science and technology directorate has historically been involved in exploiting UAP material.

## Langley Air Force Base

[[Langley Air Force Base]] is in Hampton, Virginia, next to Langley proper. It is home to the headquarters of [[Air Combat Command]] and to the 1st Fighter Wing. Witness [[Dylan Borland]] was stationed at Langley AFB around 2012 when he saw a large equilateral triangular craft with apparent electro-optic cloaking hovering silently over a NASA hangar on the base. UAP researchers cite his account in connection with alleged test and evaluation activities involving classified airframes.
