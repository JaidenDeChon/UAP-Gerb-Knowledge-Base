A municipality in Westmoreland County, Pennsylvania, located between Pittsburgh and the [[1965 Kecksburg, Pennsylvania Crash Retrieval|Kecksburg crash site]]. Murrysville is primarily relevant in the Kecksburg case as the 1965 residence of witness Jerry Betters, who provided testimony about the December 9, 1965 incident.

## Jerry Betters and the Kecksburg Case

Jerry Betters was a resident of Murrysville at the time of the Kecksburg incident and is among the civilian witnesses whose accounts were compiled in the investigation of the crash retrieval event. Murrysville lies approximately 20 miles west of Kecksburg along the Route 22 corridor, placing Betters within the region where the object's descent and the subsequent military response would have been observable or audible.

## Geographic Context

Murrysville sits between [[Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania|Pittsburgh]] (approximately 20 miles to the west) and [[Greensburg, Pennsylvania|Greensburg]] (approximately 10 miles to the east), along one of the main travel corridors connecting the Pittsburgh metropolitan area to the Kecksburg area. This placement would have made Murrysville residents potential witnesses to the fireball's descent path as well as to any military vehicle movements along Route 22 on the night of the incident.
