**Valentine** is a small town in Jeff Davis County in far West Texas, on US Route 90 between Van Horn and Marfa, south of the Davis Mountains. It appears in UAP literature as the reference point for a stopover in the [[1974 Coyame, Mexico UFO Crash Retrieval]].

## The Coyame Recovery

According to the [[Denb Report]], the American team that recovered a crashed disc from a Mexican military convoy near [[Coyame, Chihuahua, Mexico|Coyame]] in August 1974 flew it back across the border slung beneath a Sea Stallion helicopter. The helicopters then landed at a point in the Davis Mountains approximately 25 miles northeast of Valentine and waited there until 0225 hours the next morning. They then flew on to meet a small truck convoy on the road near Van Horn and Kent, Texas, where the disc was sealed into a truck bound for [[Atlanta, Georgia]].
