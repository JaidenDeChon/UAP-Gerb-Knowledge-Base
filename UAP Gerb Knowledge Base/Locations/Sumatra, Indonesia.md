**Sumatra** is the largest of the Indonesian islands and the approximate location of [[Michael Herrera]]'s 2009 UAP encounter, one of the most detailed alleged firsthand encounters by US military personnel with an unidentified craft and an associated armed paramilitary force. The exact landing zone is uncertain. The encounter took place in the island's jungle interior during a humanitarian mission after the [[2009 Sumatra Earthquake]].

## UAP Significance

In October 2009, 20-year-old [[Michael Herrera]], a USMC rifleman assigned to the [[USS Denver]] and the [[31st Marine Expeditionary Unit]], was deployed to Indonesia with the US military's humanitarian relief effort after the Sumatra earthquake and tsunami. His team flew in near Padang on CH-53 Super Stallion helicopters and climbed to a ridge to scout the area. At about 300 meters elevation, they saw an anomalous craft rotating just above the tree line in a jungle clearing below. Herrera said it "stuck out like a sore thumb."

According to the account, the craft was:
- about 300 feet in diameter and octagonal
- rotating clockwise and shifting between light matte gray and dark matte black
- covered in V-Banta black exterior panels, with a pyramid-type protrusion on top
- giving off an audible hum likened to the sound of a guitar amplifier
- floating 15 to 20 feet above the ground on an elevated platform

An armed force surrounded the craft. Its members spoke with American dialects, carried American gear and wore no rank or insignia. Gerb believes they were a rogue or compartmented black program unit, not a conventional US military element. Four F-350 trucks were staged nearby with weapon cases and what looked like oxygen or humidity control units. An alleged program insider later revealed that these containers had held human beings, apparently used as "psionic assets" to interface with the craft.

The force ambushed the Marines, disarmed them, photographed their military IDs and threatened them. The Marines were escorted back up the hillside and told to return to the landing zone (LZ) and say nothing about what they had seen. A platform below the craft then rose and joined it, and the two left together, instantly, heading west. There was no rotor wash, exhaust or sonic boom, and the surrounding vegetation was not disturbed.

Back on the USS Denver, an admiral debriefed Herrera. His camera battery and SD card, which held photos from the encounter, were confiscated, as were other Marines' cell phones. Later, in Okinawa, a USAF Lieutenant Colonel with no name tag told Herrera never to speak of the event and had him sign a non-disclosure agreement (NDA).

Herrera testified to [[AARO]] in April 2023. AARO's Historical Report Volume 1 mentioned his account but introduced two factual errors: it attributed to Herrera claims about "US Special Forces" and an "extraterrestrial spacecraft" that he says he never made.

## Geography

At the time of filming, the exact coordinates of the landing zone had not been made public. The encounter site was in jungle interior that could be reached only by helicopter. The team landed at a hasty LZ about 7-800 meters from the craft, with a hill in between. Padang, West Sumatra, was the coordination hub for the humanitarian relief operation.
