The underground facility at [[Naval Surface Warfare Center Crane]] (NSWC Crane) in Indiana is a classified subterranean complex. [[RA]], a firsthand witness, described it as strikingly modern next to the deliberately deteriorated buildings on the base's surface. It was reached by an elevator that went down to a noticeably colder level, where a guide from a private contractor called the area the "[[Off-World Technologies Division]]." The guide was likely an employee of [[SAIC]], given SAIC's dominant contracting presence at Crane. The facility houses, or housed as of about 2013–2015, at least two items described as non-human technology.

## Physical Description

NSWC Crane's surface campus is made up of 1970s-era buildings kept in a deliberately deteriorated or unremarkable condition, which is consistent with a security strategy of hiding things by making them look ordinary. RA described the difference between the surface and the underground levels as dramatic. After all electronics were surrendered at a checkpoint and the elevator went down, the air grew noticeably colder, and the interior was described as looking like a modern facility or, in RA's words, "like a spaceship."

The facility is described as heavily compartmentalized, with several security checkpoints between the entrance on the surface and the viewing area where the non-human technology was displayed. The visitors were given no documentation, and no explanation beyond what the contractor guide chose to tell them out loud.

## The Off-World Technologies Division

The contractor guide casually called the area the "off-world technologies division". RA and his fellow soldier both said the name immediately put them in a state of profound [[Ontological Shock]]. Staff told the soldiers that the items on display responded selectively to human consciousness, possibly depending on DNA or type of consciousness, and that some people could interact with the technology while others could not.

Two objects were shown to them:

- A seamless metallic sphere about the size of a basketball, with no visible manufacturing marks, joints, or welds. Its surface looked almost liquid while still clearly metallic, showing the "mother of pearl effect" that [[Jonathan Weygandt]] independently described after the [[1997 Peru UFO Crash]]. The sphere appeared to be repelled by its display podium, or to be floating above it.

- A gauntlet weapon system, a device worn on the arm. It was identified as a weapon because it had discharged an energy pulse while being recovered and injured a member of the recovery team. The device projected symbols that looked like hieroglyphs, with no visible means of projecting them. The symbols closely matched descriptions by attorney [[Daniel Sheehan]] of classified [[Project Blue Book]] photographs, which he reviewed at the Library of Congress in 1977 at President [[Jimmy Carter]]'s direction.

## SAIC and NSWC Crane

[[SAIC (Science Applications International Corporation)]] is a dominant contractor at NSWC Crane, with contracts that include weapons systems testing, electronic warfare, and classified advanced research. RA identified his guide as likely an SAIC employee because the contractor seemed familiar with the facility and its contents. NSWC Crane itself is responsible for a significant portion of the Navy's conventional weapons stockpile, energetics research, and electronic warfare systems. That legitimate work gives substantial cover to classified programs operating on the site.
