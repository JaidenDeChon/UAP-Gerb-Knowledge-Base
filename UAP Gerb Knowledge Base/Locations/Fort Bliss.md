**Fort Bliss** is a major United States Army installation in El Paso, Texas, next to the US-Mexico border and to White Sands Missile Range in New Mexico. It is one of the largest military complexes in the United States and has long been a training and staging ground for air defense and missile units.

## UAP Connections

### 1974 Coyame Recovery Operation

Fort Bliss is identified as the staging area for the CIA rapid recovery team dispatched to the [[1974 Coyame, Mexico UFO Crash Retrieval]]. According to the allegedly leaked [[Denb Report]], by 2100 hours on August 26, 1974, the CIA had assembled a recovery team with helicopters at Fort Bliss. Several helicopters, painted a neutral sand color and bearing no markings, were flown in from an unknown source and assembled in a secured area. The team's personnel stayed with their helicopters and had no contact with other Fort Bliss personnel, which suggests strict compartmentalization.

At 1438 hours the following day (the report gives only clock times, so the date is inferred), four helicopters (identified as three UH-1 Hueys and one larger helicopter, possibly a Sea Stallion) departed Fort Bliss, followed the border down to Presidio, Texas, then turned and entered Mexican airspace north of Candelaria. The team arrived at the crash site at 1653 hours, recovered a 16-foot-5-inch silver disc from a stopped Mexican convoy (all personnel dead), destroyed the site with high explosives, and returned to US territory with the object.

In the Coyame incident, a hazmat-equipped team arrived in unmarked military helicopters. The account has notable parallels with the [[Peru UFO Crash Incident|1997 Peru UFO Crash Retrieval]], where, according to the testimony of [[Jonathan Weygandt]], similar hazmat-equipped teams arrived at a UAP crash site in military helicopters.

### Roswell Debris Connection

Colonel [[Philip J. Corso]] mentions Fort Bliss in his book *The Day After Roswell*. In the Coyame video Gerb says that, according to Corso, debris and bodies from the 1947 [[Roswell Crash]] were shipped to Fort Bliss, Texas.[^gerb-corso-fort-bliss] In the book itself, crates Corso links to Roswell reach [[Fort Riley, Kansas]] from Fort Bliss on their way to Wright Field, which in his account made the base a transfer point for recovered non-human materials.

### El Paso Intelligence Center (EPIC)

Fort Bliss is also the home of the [[El Paso Intelligence Center]] (EPIC), which was established in 1974, the same year as the Coyame incident. EPIC coordinates intelligence gathering along the US-Mexico border and may have been involved in reconnaissance and intelligence on cross-border UAP incidents.

## Operational Significance

Its place on the US-Mexico border, its airlift capability as a major military installation, and the presence of EPIC make Fort Bliss an ideal forward operating base for covert cross-border UAP recovery operations. Unmarked helicopters, specialized personnel, and decontamination equipment were assembled there within about a day of the Coyame crash, which suggests Fort Bliss may have had standing or rehearsed protocols for UAP crash retrieval in the border region.
