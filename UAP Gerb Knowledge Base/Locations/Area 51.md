Area 51, also known as Groom Lake, Watertown, the Ranch, or Dreamland, is a highly classified United States Air Force facility in the Nevada Test and Training Range (NTTR), about 83 miles northwest of Las Vegas. It has been the main test site for the most advanced and classified aerospace programs in U.S. history, including the U-2, the SR-71 and the F-117A stealth fighter. Several UAP whistleblowers allege that the facility has been used to test reverse-engineered non-human craft, and Area 51 is central to their testimony.

## UAP Significance

[[Edgar Fouche|Edgar Fouché]], who was assigned to Detachment 3 AFFTC at Groom Lake, claimed that the facility housed the Defense Advanced Research Center (DARC), a 10-story underground laboratory near [[Papoose Lake]] where advanced digital technologies and reverse-engineered craft systems were developed. According to Fouché, a hangar built into the mountainside near Papoose stored [[TR-3B]] craft, with its entrance concealed by a holographic generator projecting the appearance of natural terrain. The top secret SR-75, SR-74 and TR-3B allegedly operated out of Groom Lake, and over the years people mistook them for UFOs.

In 1990, Jane's Defence writer [[James Goodall]] revealed his thesis that at least eight black programs were flying out of Area 51, among them a silent triangle that used unconventional technologies. One of Goodall's sources, who had been stationed at Groom Lake for 12 years, said that UAP positively exist at the base. Another said they had things "literally out of this world, better than Star Trek."

Fouché's sources claimed that as of 1992 the Air Force was carrying out a contingency plan to move exotic aircraft platforms out of Area 51 because of growing public scrutiny. The destinations allegedly included Utah ([[Dugway Proving Ground]]), Colorado, Alaska, Greenland, Diego Garcia, and south to Area 51 S4 at Papoose.

## Related Facilities
