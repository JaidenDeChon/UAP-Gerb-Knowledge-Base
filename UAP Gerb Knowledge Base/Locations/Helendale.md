Helendale is a [[Lockheed Martin]] radar cross-section measurement facility in unincorporated San Bernardino County, California, near Victorville in the Antelope Valley region. According to multiple UAP researchers and alleged insiders, the facility is a cover for testing, and possibly storing, reverse-engineered non-human craft.

## Alleged Dual Purpose

Forensic illustrator [[Bill McDonald]], drawing on testimony from Lockheed Martin engineers, stated that the Helendale facility was designed to be "hidden in plain sight," with a legitimate radar cross-section range as its cover. The storm drainage flood control channel on the facility's exterior is allegedly, in reality, a short runway or platform for testing electric or anti-gravity craft. Five years after McDonald identified the site, [[Steven Greer]] independently described it as an ongoing black-budget UAP reverse engineering location. Greer said that senior officials on the Senate intelligence and armed services committees had no knowledge of the activities there.

Helendale is close to [[Edwards Air Force Base]] and [[Air Force Plant 42]] in Palmdale. Gerb identifies both as locations connected to legacy reverse engineering programs.
