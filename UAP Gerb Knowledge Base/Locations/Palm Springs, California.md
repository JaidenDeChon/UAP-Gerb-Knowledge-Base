Palm Springs is a desert resort city in Riverside County, California, in the Coachella Valley east of Los Angeles. This wiki covers it as the place associated with [[Jeremy Corbell]]'s 2023 UFO footage, which Gerb cited in the debate over the flare explanation for the [[Phoenix Lights]].

## The Corbell 2023 Footage

In 2023, filmmaker and UAP media figure [[Jeremy Corbell]] released video of lights over the Palm Springs area, which he described as a UFO sighting. Gerb brought up this footage while discussing cases where the flare explanation may in fact apply. Flares are one of the U.S. Air Force's most commonly used explanations for formations of anomalous lights. The footage came up specifically in connection with the [[Phoenix Lights]] incident of March 13, 1997, when the Air Force attributed witness reports of a large delta-shaped craft to flares dropped by A-10 Thunderbolt II aircraft. Gerb's position on the Phoenix Lights, as discussed in [[Videos/The Alien and UFO Obscure Oddities Iceberg (Level 1)/summary|The Alien and UFO Obscure Oddities Iceberg (Level 1)]], is that the flare explanation cannot account for the many witnesses who described a coherent, solid, structured craft. He cites Arizona Governor [[Fife Symington]]'s firsthand description of a "dramatically large delta-shaped object with very distinctive leading edge and enormous lights." The Corbell Palm Springs footage was presented as the contrasting case, where the flare explanation may be more credible than it is for Phoenix.

## Context

This wiki catalogues the case as the [[Jeremy Corbell Palm Spring UFO Footage]] event. In Gerb's analysis it does not count as evidence of anomalous phenomena. He uses it instead as an example of how the same visual signature, lights in formation in the night sky, can lead to meaningfully different explanations depending on how many witnesses there were, what instruments recorded the lights, and how the lights behaved. Gerb is willing to suggest possible prosaic explanations where the evidence supports them, and this recurring habit sets the channel's analysis apart from uncritical promotion of all UFO claims.
