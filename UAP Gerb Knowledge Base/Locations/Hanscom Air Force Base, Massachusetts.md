Hanscom Air Force Base is a United States Air Force installation in Bedford, Massachusetts, about 17 miles northwest of Boston. It is home to the Air Force Research Laboratory's Information Directorate and hosts a range of research programs in defense electronics, command and control, and avionics. In UAP research, Hanscom appears as one of the postings in the career of witness "Ed," a redacted USAF officer. Gerb analyzes Ed's testimony in connection with alleged reverse engineering programs at [[Edwards Air Force Base]].

## Ed's Hanscom Period (2002–2005)

Witness "Ed" (Witness 11063 in [[Steven Greer]]'s Disclosure Project archive) is described as a US Air Force officer who held postings at Nellis Air Force Base, Hanscom, Edwards, and the Pentagon. Gerb's analysis of Ed's testimony describes the Hanscom period (about 2002–2005) as a phase in which Ed analyzed conventional foreign military assets in collaboration with MIT. MIT has close institutional ties with Hanscom through the Lincoln Laboratory, a federally funded research and development center on the base campus.

This phase is distinguished from his later Edwards posting because it involved terrestrial systems rather than direct testing of non-human technology. The Hanscom period is part of the professional biography that presents Ed as a credible Air Force officer with legitimate access to sensitive aerospace programs before his alleged read-in to the reverse engineering programs at Edwards.

## MITRE's First Program Office

Gerb's investigation into the [[MITRE Corporation]] identifies Hanscom as the base where MITRE opened its first program office after its founding in 1958, which reflects the organization's close working relationship with MIT Lincoln Laboratory during the SAGE era. Gerb also notes that the [[Air Force Office of Special Investigations]]' Office of Special Projects (AFOSI/PJ), which is alleged to have provided counterintelligence support for MITRE's legacy program activity in the past, has a permanent office at Hanscom as well as one in [[Crystal City, Virginia]].

## Institutional Context

Hanscom's research mission in electronics and systems, together with its close ties to MIT Lincoln Laboratory, makes it a plausible staging ground for personnel who would later move into more sensitive reverse engineering programs. The base's partnerships with the Air Force Research Laboratory and its specialized test and evaluation roles fit the network of Western Range facilities and personnel pipelines that Gerb describes in his analysis of the Hidden Wing program structure.
