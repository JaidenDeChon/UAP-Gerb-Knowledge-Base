The Utah Test and Training Range (UTTR, pronounced "udder") is a large U.S. Air Force test and training range in the western Utah desert, operated from Hill Air Force Base and one of the Air Force's [[Major Range and Test Facility Bases (MRTFBs)|major range and test facility bases]]. It shares [[Dugway Proving Ground]] with the Army's own MRTFB there, the [[West Desert Test Center]]. The [[388th Range Squadron]] at Hill runs the range's radars and instrumentation. Hill Air Force Base also hosts [[Air Force Materiel Command (AFMC)]]'s Ogden Air Logistics Complex, the worldwide manager for a wide range of aircraft, engines, missiles, software and avionics.

## In UAP research

UAP Gerb's *The Hidden Wing* counts the UTTR among the western ranges of his [[Hidden Wing]] thesis and theorizes that Hill Air Force Base takes part in joint Air Force and Army UFO legacy work at Dugway. He recalls contractor witness MS, who said he saw a seamless, flawless disc being dismantled in a hangar in Dugway's Avery area, next to Michael Army Airfield, which the UTTR and the 388th Range Squadron use. He also notes that the 388th's radars ring Granite Peak, where he alleges there is a deep underground base. AFOSI's Office of Special Projects keeps a permanent presence at Hill.
