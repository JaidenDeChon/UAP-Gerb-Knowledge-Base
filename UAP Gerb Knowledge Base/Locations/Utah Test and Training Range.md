The Utah Test and Training Range (UTTR, pronounced "udder") is a large U.S. Air Force test and training range in the western Utah desert, operated from Hill Air Force Base. It is one of the Air Force's [[Major Range and Test Facility Bases (MRTFBs)|major range and test facility bases]]. It shares [[Dugway Proving Ground]] with the [[West Desert Test Center]], the Army's own major range and test facility base there. The [[388th Range Squadron]] at Hill runs the range's radars and instrumentation. Hill Air Force Base is also home to [[Air Force Materiel Command (AFMC)]]'s Ogden Air Logistics Complex, which manages a wide range of aircraft, engines, missiles, software and avionics worldwide.

## In UAP research

In *The Hidden Wing*, Gerb counts the UTTR among the western ranges in his [[Hidden Wing]] thesis. He theorizes that Hill Air Force Base takes part in joint Air Force and Army UFO legacy work at Dugway. He recalls a contractor witness, MS, who said he saw a seamless, flawless disc being dismantled in a hangar in Dugway's Avery area. The Avery area is next to Michael Army Airfield, which the UTTR and the 388th Range Squadron use. Gerb also notes that the 388th's radars ring Granite Peak, where he alleges there is a deep underground base. The Office of Special Projects of the Air Force Office of Special Investigations (AFOSI) keeps a permanent presence at Hill.
