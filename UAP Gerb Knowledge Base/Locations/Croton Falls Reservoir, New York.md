A reservoir in Westchester County, New York, in the wider Hudson Valley region. It is the site of a 1993 sighting of a triangular UAP by a witness named Jim Cook. The sighting took place during the Hudson Valley UFO flap, a long wave of sightings of large structured craft documented across New York and Connecticut from the early 1980s through the mid-1990s.

## The 1993 Jim Cook Sighting

In 1993, Jim Cook saw a large triangular UAP over or near the Croton Falls Reservoir. This was in the later part of the Hudson Valley flap, when many people at a time reported slow-moving boomerang or V-shaped objects with multiple lights. Cook's sighting is part of the documented record of triangular craft seen in the Hudson Valley corridor.

## The Hudson Valley Flap

The Hudson Valley UFO flap is one of the most extensively documented regional UAP waves in American history. Hundreds of witnesses, among them police officers, civilian pilots and IBM engineers, reported large, silent, low-flying structured objects across Westchester and Dutchess counties in New York. Many of the sightings centred on the Croton Falls Reservoir and the surrounding reservoir system in the lower Hudson Valley, because the area has dark skies and few residents. The researchers J. Allen Hynek, Philip Imbrogno and Bob Pratt collected witness accounts in the 1987 book *Night Siege*.
