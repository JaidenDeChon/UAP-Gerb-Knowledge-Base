Green Bay is a city in north-eastern Wisconsin, at the mouth of the Fox River on the bay of Green Bay, an arm of Lake Michigan. In the UAP context it is known for a 2007 series of photographs of a disc-like craft taken in the Green Bay area, one of which UAP Gerb uses as his channel's profile image.

## The 2007 photographs

Physicist [[Kevin Knuth]] included one of the Green Bay images in the slide of seven UFO photographs that opened his 2023 [[Sol Foundation 2023 Presentation by Kevin Knuth|Sol Foundation talk]]. Gerb calls the series one of his favourites and notes its resemblance to the February 2003 Weyauwega, Wisconsin photographs, in which a mother sledding with her son near dusk photographed a disc passing overhead with colour-cycling lights. The two sets show a craft of almost identical shape and colour in the same part of Wisconsin; the difference is the lights, three triangular lights in 2003 against a circular pattern in 2007. Gerb says he plans to examine the series further.
