Green Bay is a city in north-eastern Wisconsin, at the mouth of the Fox River on the bay of Green Bay, an arm of Lake Michigan. In the UAP context it is known for a 2007 series of photographs of a disc-like craft taken in the Green Bay area. Gerb uses one of these photographs as his channel's profile image.

## The 2007 photographs

Physicist [[Kevin Knuth]] included one of the Green Bay images in the slide of seven UFO photographs that opened his 2023 [[Sol Foundation 2023 Presentation by Kevin Knuth|Sol Foundation talk]]. Gerb calls the series one of his favourites and notes that it resembles the Weyauwega, Wisconsin photographs of February 2003. In those, a mother sledding with her son near dusk photographed a disc with colour-cycling lights passing overhead. Both sets show a craft of almost identical shape and colour in the same part of Wisconsin. They differ in the lights: three triangular lights in 2003, and a circular pattern of lights in 2007. Gerb says he plans to examine the series further.
