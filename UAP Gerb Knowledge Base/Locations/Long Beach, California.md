Long Beach is a coastal city in Southern California, immediately south of Los Angeles. It has several major aerospace and defense industry facilities. The most notable is the main campus of [[Aerospace Corporation]], which has repeatedly been identified as having suspected ties to covert UAP research programs.

## Aerospace Corporation Campus

The Aerospace Corporation's Long Beach campus is known for its sprawling gardens and its distinctive architecture. [[Ross Coulthart]] reported in his book *In Plain Sight* that a source told him about "an aerospace company in Long Beach, California" with access to "the wreckage" from UFO crash retrievals. The source described the company as having "sprawling gardens". Among the major aerospace companies in Long Beach, only the Aerospace Corporation matches that description.

The campus is the corporation's primary research and engineering facility. It has access to classified programs and coordinates directly with the [[US Space Force]] and intelligence community partners. As a Federally Funded Research and Development Center (FFRDC), Aerospace Corporation has a level of access to Special Access Programs that typical defense contractors lack.

## UAP Legacy Program Identification

Researchers including [[Jacques Vallee]], [[Kit Green]], [[Hal Puthoff]], [[Eric Davis]], and Kristen B. Zimmerman identified the Aerospace Corporation, which is based at this Long Beach campus, as one of the suspected legacy private corporations engaged in "real UAP research," as documented in Vallée's *Hidden Science 5*.

The researchers who made this identification are senior figures with intelligence community backgrounds, and several other points fit with it:
- Coulthart's source described the Long Beach facility as having access to "wreckage".
- The corporation has a documented role in analyzing [[Defense Support Program (DSP)]] satellite data on [[Fast Walkers]].
- Its unique FFRDC status gives it access to classified programs.
- It descends institutionally from [[TRW]], a company repeatedly named in UAP program allegations.

Taken together with the researchers' identification, these points suggest the Long Beach campus may house some of the most sensitive UAP-related research conducted by private contractors under government oversight.

## Defense and Aerospace Industry Presence

Long Beach has historically been a major center for aerospace manufacturing and research. It was once home to the commercial aircraft production facilities of McDonnell Douglas (later Boeing). The Aerospace Corporation is now the most significant aerospace research presence left in the city.
