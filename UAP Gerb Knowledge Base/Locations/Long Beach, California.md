Long Beach is a coastal city in Southern California, immediately south of Los Angeles. It has several major aerospace and defense industry facilities. Gerb ties the city to the [[Aerospace Corporation]], which researchers have named as a suspected participant in covert UAP research programs.

## Aerospace Corporation Campus

[[Ross Coulthart]] reported in his book *In Plain Sight* that a source told him about "an aerospace company in Long Beach, California" with access to "the wreckage" from UFO crash retrievals. The source described the company as having "sprawling gardens". Gerb matches that company to the Aerospace Corporation, which he calls "the only major Aerospace company in Long Beach California that has this type of Campus". The corporation is headquartered elsewhere, and the Long Beach campus comes from Gerb's reading of Coulthart's source.

As a Federally Funded Research and Development Center (FFRDC), Aerospace Corporation has a level of access to Special Access Programs that typical defense contractors lack.

## UAP Legacy Program Identification

Researchers including [[Jacques Vallee]], [[Kit Green]], [[Hal Puthoff]], [[Eric Davis]], and Kristen B. Zimmerman identified the Aerospace Corporation as one of the suspected legacy private corporations engaged in "real UAP research," as recorded in Vallée's *Forbidden Science 5* (Gerb's video calls it *Hidden Science 5*). The researchers did not name Long Beach; that link is Gerb's.

The researchers who made this identification are senior figures with intelligence community backgrounds, and several other points fit with it:
- Coulthart's source described the Long Beach facility as having access to "wreckage".
- The corporation has a documented role in analyzing [[Defense Support Program (DSP)]] satellite data on [[Fast Walkers]].
- Its unique FFRDC status gives it access to classified programs.
- It descends institutionally from [[TRW]], a company repeatedly named in UAP program allegations.

Gerb says there is "much more work to be done investigating the Aerospace corporation".

## Defense and Aerospace Industry Presence

Long Beach has historically been a major center for aerospace manufacturing and research. It was once home to the commercial aircraft production facilities of McDonnell Douglas (later Boeing).
