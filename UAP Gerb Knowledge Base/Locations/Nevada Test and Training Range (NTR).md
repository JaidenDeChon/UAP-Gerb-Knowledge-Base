The Nevada Test and Training Range (NTTR, often "NTR" in UAP Gerb's videos) is the U.S. Air Force's large air and ground training and test range in southern Nevada, managed from [[Nellis Air Force Base]] near Las Vegas. It is one of the Air Force's [[Major Range and Test Facility Bases (MRTFBs)|major range and test facility bases]]. It surrounds or adjoins several of the most secretive sites in the United States, including [[Area 51]], the Department of Energy's [[Tonopah Test Range (TTR)|Tonopah Test Range]] (managed by [[Sandia National Laboratories]]), and the former Nevada Test Site, now the [[Nevada National Security Site (Nevada Test Site)|Nevada National Security Site]].

## History and scale

The range was run by the 98th Range Wing, which began operations in 2001 as the primary military training area for the Nellis complex and was later redesignated the Nevada Test and Training Range. The Air Force describes it as the largest contiguous air and ground space available for peacetime military operations in the free world: 2.9 million acres of land and 5,000 square miles of airspace closed to civilian overflight. It provides a battlespace for testing, tactics development and advanced training, supports Department of Energy testing, research and development, coordinates with other commands, services, DOE and the Department of the Interior, and acts as the single point of contact for range customers. Groom Lake ([[Area 51]]) lies within its South Range. [[Christopher Haave]] commanded the wing from 2005 to 2007.

## In UAP research

In *The Hidden Wing*, UAP Gerb places the NTR at the heart of the [[Hidden Wing]]. He says that most top-secret sites on the range, including Area 51 and Tonopah, are occupied by the Edwards [[412th Test Wing]]. His witness Ed described a derivative-technology program run from the 412th across Edwards, the NTR and other western-range installations. Gerb also recalls a source from his NRO investigation who claimed to have served on an NRO-led UFO crash-retrieval team based on the NTR in the 2000s. The video notes that AFOSI's Office of Special Projects keeps a permanent presence in Las Vegas, next to the range.

In *UAP Reverse Engineering at Edwards Air Force Base*, Greer's notes on witness [[Ed (Witness 11063)|Ed]] say he worked at [[Building 200]] under the range wing's first commander, whose name is redacted, and that he "saw man-made craft on the range" and was there often. UAP Gerb calls the range, with its mission of integrating partners in live and synthetic environments, an ideal place for the Department of Defense and contractors to test reverse-engineered technology, and concludes that the Edwards 412th Test Wing's program works in conjunction with it. Greer's sources place Blackjack Control, one half of the [[Blackjack Team]] pairing, at Nellis.
