The Nevada Test and Training Range (NTTR, often "NTR" in UAP Gerb's videos) is the U.S. Air Force's large air and ground training and test range in southern Nevada, managed from [[Nellis Air Force Base]] near Las Vegas. It is one of the Air Force's [[Major Range and Test Facility Bases (MRTFBs)|major range and test facility bases]]. It surrounds or adjoins several of the most secretive sites in the United States, including [[Area 51]], the Department of Energy's [[Tonopah Test Range (TTR)|Tonopah Test Range]] (managed by [[Sandia National Laboratories]]), and the former Nevada Test Site, now the [[Nevada National Security Site (Nevada Test Site)|Nevada National Security Site]].

## In UAP research

In *The Hidden Wing*, UAP Gerb places the NTR at the heart of the [[Hidden Wing]]. He says that most top-secret sites on the range, including Area 51 and Tonopah, are occupied by the Edwards [[412th Test Wing]]. His witness Ed described a derivative-technology program run from the 412th across Edwards, the NTR and other western-range installations. The host also recalls a source from his NRO investigation who claimed to have served on an NRO-led UFO crash-retrieval team based on the NTR in the 2000s. The video notes that AFOSI's Office of Special Projects keeps a permanent presence in Las Vegas, next to the range.
