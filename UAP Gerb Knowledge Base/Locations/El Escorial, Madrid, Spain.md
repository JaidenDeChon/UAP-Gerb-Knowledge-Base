El Escorial (also written L'Escorial or El Escorial) is a town and municipality in the Community of Madrid, Spain, located approximately 45 kilometers northwest of Madrid in the foothills of the Sierra de Guadarrama mountains. The town is most famous for the Royal Monastery of San Lorenzo de El Escorial, a UNESCO World Heritage Site and historical residence of Spanish royalty.

## UAP Activity

El Escorial is the reported location of the [[Metapod UFO Sighting]] of 6 September 2015, in which two friends filmed a small, silent, crescent-shaped craft (the [[Metapod]]) somewhere in the countryside near the town, in the direction of Las Navas del Marqués. The witness who emailed the footage to [[UFO VNI]], signing himself Modesto, withheld the exact site, offering the coordinates to the channel only for its own investigation. He described El Escorial as "a place where strange things happen", not in the town itself but in the nearby mountains.

According to the same email, the witness's companion said it was not the first time he had seen the object there, and that his late father had also seen it.
