Denver is the capital and most populous city of Colorado. On this wiki it comes up mainly in the [[Aztec UFO Crash and Recovery]] story. It is where [[Silas Newton]] gave his University of Denver lecture, where the Edetoise Bar sting operation took place, and where alleged fraud victim [[Herman Flader]] lived.

## Newton's University of Denver Lecture

[[Silas Newton]] gave a lecture at the University of Denver about the Aztec UFO crash story, which he claimed came from the group of scientists known as "Dr. G." Journalist [[J. P. Cahn]] later named [[Leo GeBauer]] as the sole Dr. G. Researcher [[William Steinman]] instead identified Denver geophysicist [[Carl A. Heiland]] as the scientist originally meant to give the lecture and as the source of the leak. In [[Videos/The 1948 Aztec, New Mexico UFO Crash Retrieval/summary|The 1948 Aztec, New Mexico UFO Crash Retrieval]], Gerb counts this lecture among the public occasions on which Newton spread the Aztec account. That fits the theory that elements of the military may have encouraged Newton to spread the story as part of a deliberate information management strategy.

## The Edetoise Bar Sting Operation

Denver was also the site of the Edetoise Bar sting operation, a law enforcement action against Newton over the fraud charges that eventually led to his 1953 conviction. Gerb places the sting within a wider pattern of federal pursuit of Newton, which he calls unusual: the FBI reportedly could not find a single federal judge in Arizona, Colorado, New Mexico, or Utah willing to hear a federal criminal case, and 32 of Newton's 33 investors reported that they were satisfied with their investments.

## Herman Flader

Denver was also the home of [[Herman Flader]], the only investor who filed a criminal complaint against Newton and GeBauer. He sought $250,000, which he claimed to have lost through the doodlebug oil-detecting device. Gerb's analysis notes that while Flader was pursuing his fraud complaint against them, he was also trying to sell Newton and GeBauer an interest in a nebulous "health machine". That detail complicates the prosecution's picture of Flader as a straightforward victim.

## Significance

In the Aztec story, Denver is a hub for Newton's network. The lecture hall, the sting and the complainant were all here, which concentrated the legal and social pressure on Newton in the Colorado region. The Aztec case itself took place in [[Aztec, New Mexico]]. In Gerb's broader argument, Denver was instead the administrative center of the reputational and legal campaign that, in his view, was used to suppress the case.
