Warrenton, Virginia is a town in Fauquier County, Northern Virginia. In UAP and intelligence research it is known as the location of the Warrenton US Army Training Center. The training center appears to be a standard Army installation, but it has been identified as a covert CIA facility with an underground installation used for purposes independent of the Army's public mission.

According to Fletcher Prouty's book *The Secret Team: The CIA and Its Allies in Control of the World*, the Warrenton US Army Training Center is a "highly secretive underground installation that serves as a Federal Relocation Center" operated by an unknown agency, which has since been identified as the CIA. An *Inquiry* magazine article by Robert Walters, titled "Going Underground", confirmed this, stating plainly that the Warrenton center is a CIA covert facility.

The same pattern appears at the Sugar Grove Naval Communications Center in West Virginia, where the Navy operates an NSA underground facility, and at the CIA's Camp Peary, located near Camp David in the mid-Atlantic region.
