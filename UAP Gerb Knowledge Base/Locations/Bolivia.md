Bolivia is a landlocked country in central South America, bordered by Brazil, Paraguay, Argentina, Chile, and Peru. Within UAP research, Bolivia is significant primarily as the site of the [[1978 Bolivia UFO Crash]] — one of the best-documented UFO crash incidents involving a confirmed US government response through [[Project Moon Dust]].

## 1978 UFO Crash Near El Taire

On May 6, 1978, thousands of people near El Taire, Bolivia witnessed a cylindrical object crash into a mountainside, creating a sonic boom heard up to 150 miles away. The US response was documented in State Department cables, including one from Secretary of State [[Cyrus Vance]], and two US Air Force officers, [[Colonel Robert Simmons]] and Major [[John Heise]], reached the area. Declassified documents confirm [[Project Moon Dust]] was the agency tasked with monitoring the situation. The [[UAP Caucus]] has brought this incident to modern Congressional attention as evidence of US government involvement in foreign crash retrieval operations.

## Other References

In testimony about the [[1997 Peru UFO Crash Retrieval - the Story of Jonathan Weygandt|1997 Peru crash]], witness Jonathan Weygandt described his radar base as being near the Bolivian border, though analysts believe he likely meant near the Colombian or Brazilian borders given the crash location in Peru.
