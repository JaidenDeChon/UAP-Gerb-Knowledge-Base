Bolivia is a landlocked country in central South America, bordered by Brazil, Paraguay, Argentina, Chile, and Peru. In UAP research, Bolivia is known mainly as the site of the [[1978 Bolivia UFO Crash]]. It is one of the best-documented UFO crash incidents, and the US government's response to it through [[Project Moon Dust]] is confirmed.

## 1978 UFO Crash Near El Taire

On May 6, 1978, thousands of people near El Taire, Bolivia witnessed a cylindrical object crash into a mountainside, with a sonic boom heard up to 150 miles away. The US response was documented in State Department cables, including one from Secretary of State [[Cyrus Vance]], and two US Air Force officers, [[Colonel Robert Simmons]] and Major [[John Heise]], reached the area. Declassified documents confirm that [[Project Moon Dust]] was the agency tasked with monitoring the situation. In recent years the [[UAP Caucus]] has raised the incident in Congress as evidence of US government involvement in foreign crash retrieval operations.

## Other References

In testimony about the [[1997 Peru UFO Crash Retrieval - the Story of Jonathan Weygandt|1997 Peru crash]], witness Jonathan Weygandt said his radar base was near the Bolivian border. Given where in Peru the crash happened, analysts believe he probably meant the Colombian or Brazilian border.
