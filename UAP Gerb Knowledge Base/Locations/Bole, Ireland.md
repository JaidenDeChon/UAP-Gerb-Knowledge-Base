**Bole, Ireland** is the alleged location of a 1996 UAP crash event in which local witnesses observed an aerial craft shear through trees and land in a lake, followed by approximately six months of substantial US military activity and a reported recovery of non-human occupants. The case is drawn from anonymous whistleblower testimony discussed in the context of US Special Forces UAP crash retrieval operations.

## UAP Significance

According to an anonymous whistleblower whose account was analyzed by UAP Gerb, in May 1996 local residents witnessed what appeared to be an aerial craft misjudging its approach, shearing trees as it descended, and coming to rest in a lake. A total of five crew members were aboard the craft; two survived with light bruising and three did not.

A recovery team — described as including American personnel — arrived and took the surviving occupants into custody. The operation was attributed to US forces under an informal agreement described by the witness as: "the price you pay for having a superpower watch your back" — meaning that in Europe, a default arrangement exists that any recovered UAP materials and occupants are transferred to the United States. Local police in the area were instructed to stay away from the site, and US military activity in the region was substantial for approximately six months following the event.

The recovered biologics were described as behaving like biological drones or androids with severely limited cognition: they stood in place wherever they were positioned, reacted only to basic stimuli such as bright lights, and did not resist medical examination. They were described as being "led out like children" from the craft, an observation that closely mirrors similar descriptions from the [[Camp Polk, Louisiana Egg-Shaped Craft Retrieval (1953)]] and other historical accounts. An ambassador doctor who spoke English, Chinese, Spanish, and Russian served as the lead medical contact with the occupants. Standard protocol specified that security personnel remain well away from the biologics at all times and that no weapons be leveled at them. The occupants died within days of their recovery.
