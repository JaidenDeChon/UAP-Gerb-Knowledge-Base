**Bole, Ireland** is the alleged site of the [[1996 Bole, Ireland Crash Retrieval|1996 UAP crash retrieval]]. In the alleged incident, local witnesses saw an aerial craft shear through trees and land in a lake. About six months of substantial US military activity followed, along with a reported recovery of non-human occupants. The case comes from anonymous whistleblower testimony discussed in the context of US Special Forces UAP crash retrieval operations.

## UAP Significance

According to the [[Infographic Show Whistleblower]], whose account Gerb analyzed, local residents in May 1996 saw what appeared to be an aerial craft misjudge its approach, shear through trees on the way down and come to rest in a lake. Five crew members were aboard. Two survived with light bruising, and three did not.

A recovery team, described as including American personnel, arrived and took the surviving occupants into custody. The operation was attributed to US forces under an informal agreement that the witness called "the price you pay for having a superpower watch your back". The phrase refers to a default arrangement in Europe under which any recovered UAP materials and occupants are transferred to the United States. Local police were told to stay away from the site, and US military activity in the region stayed substantial for about six months after the event.

The recovered biologics were described as behaving like biological drones or androids with severely limited cognition. They stood wherever they were placed, reacted only to basic stimuli such as bright lights, and did not resist medical examination. They were also described as being "led out like children" from the craft, which closely mirrors descriptions from the [[Camp Polk, Louisiana Egg-Shaped Craft Retrieval (1953)]] and other historical accounts. An ambassador doctor who spoke English, Chinese, Spanish, and Russian was the lead medical contact with the occupants. Under standard protocol (see [[Live Biologics Protocols]]), security personnel had to stay well away from the biologics at all times and were not to level weapons at them. The occupants died within days of their recovery.
