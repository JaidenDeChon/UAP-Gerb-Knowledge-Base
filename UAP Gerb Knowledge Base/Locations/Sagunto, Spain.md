Sagunto (Valencian: Sagunt) is a town on the Mediterranean coast of the province of Valencia, Spain, about 25 kilometres north of the city of Valencia.

## The Manises incident

During the [[Manises UFO Incident]] of 11–12 November 1979, Mirage F1 pilot [[Fernando Cámara]] was told of a new radar echo indicating a second object near Sagunto after the first object he had closed on disappeared from sight. According to the account Gerb relays, it was while chasing this second object, over the sea and the Spanish interior, that Cámara's avionics were jammed and his alert system warned that he was locked on by a continuous-wave missile radar.
