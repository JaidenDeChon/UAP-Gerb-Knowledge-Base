Sagunto (Valencian: Sagunt) is a town on the Mediterranean coast of the province of Valencia, Spain, about 25 kilometres north of the city of Valencia.

## The Manises incident

During the [[Manises UFO Incident]] of 11–12 November 1979, Mirage F1 pilot [[Fernando Cámara]] closed on a first object, which then disappeared from sight. He was then told of a new radar echo that indicated a second object near Sagunto. According to the account Gerb relays, while Cámara chased this second object over the sea and the Spanish interior, his avionics were jammed and his alert system warned that he was locked on by a continuous-wave missile radar.
