Langley Air Force Base (formally Joint Base Langley-Eustis since 2010) is a United States Air Force installation in Hampton, Virginia, next to the city of Newport News. It is the headquarters of Air Combat Command (ACC) and home to the 1st Fighter Wing, one of the oldest and best-known fighter wings in the Air Force. The base also hosts NASA Langley Research Center on adjacent grounds, with hangars and research facilities that have been in use since the early twentieth century. Its strategic location on the Virginia Peninsula and its role as ACC headquarters make Langley one of the most operationally important Air Force bases on the East Coast.
