Langley Air Force Base (formally Joint Base Langley-Eustis since 2010) is a United States Air Force installation located in Hampton, Virginia, adjacent to the city of Newport News. It is the headquarters of Air Combat Command (ACC) and home to the 1st Fighter Wing, one of the oldest and most storied fighter wings in the Air Force. The base also hosts NASA Langley Research Center on adjacent grounds, which includes hangars and research facilities that have been in operation since the early twentieth century. Langley's strategic location on the Virginia Peninsula and its status as an ACC headquarters make it one of the most operationally significant Air Force bases on the East Coast.
