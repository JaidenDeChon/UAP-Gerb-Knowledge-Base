Hangar 84 refers to a guarded hangar area at Roswell Army Air Field described in Roswell witness testimony as a temporary holding site for recovered material and bodies in July 1947. In the whistleblower narrative, it is presented as the location where Walter Haut said he viewed an egg-shaped craft and small covered bodies.

## Roswell Witness Context

Accounts linked to Walter Haut describe controlled access, interior and exterior guard presence, and temporary mortuary activity in or near this hangar space. These claims are disputed but remain central to Roswell crash-retrieval narratives.
