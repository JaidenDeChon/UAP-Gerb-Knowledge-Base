Hangar 84 (also called Building 84) is a B-29 hangar at [[Roswell Army Airfield]], New Mexico. In accounts of the July 1947 [[Roswell Crash]] it is described as the place where the recovered craft and bodies were held. It is best known from the posthumous affidavit of the base's public relations officer, [[Walter Haut]].

## In the Roswell accounts

According to UAP Gerb's summary of the Roswell timeline, debris and bodies were shipped out of Hangar 84 to [[Wright Field]] or [[Fort Worth, Texas]] as early as 5 July 1947. Haut's 2002 affidavit, released in 2007, says that on the morning of 8 July Colonel [[William Blanchard]] took him into the hangar, which was under heavy guard inside and out. There he saw a metallic, egg-shaped craft 12 to 15 feet long and about 6 feet in diameter, with no windows, portholes, wings, landing gear or tail section. Under a canvas tarp lay a couple of bodies about 4 feet tall, with larger-than-normal heads, and a temporary morgue had been set up.
