## Overview

This video analyzes a presentation by [[Karl Nell]] — retired U.S. Army Colonel, former Deputy CTO of [[Northrop Grumman]], and former DIA Technical Intelligence Operations Officer — delivered at the [[Sol Foundation]] Symposium in November 2023. [[UAP Gerb]] breaks down Nell's detailed five-phase UAP disclosure roadmap, legislative analysis of the Schumer Amendment, and broader analytical framework for understanding UAP and non-human intelligence in the context of modern disclosure efforts.

Nell is identified as one of the 40 firsthand witness whistleblowers interviewed by [[David Grusch]] over four years before Grusch's 2023 congressional testimony about a secret military program involving non-human craft and remains. Nell's presentation analyzes the Schumer Amendment's key legal definitions — including "non-human intelligence" (cited 18 times in the legislation) and "prosaic attribution" — and catalogues reasons UAP information has been withheld, ranging from national security considerations and covert NHI agreements to crimes allegedly committed to maintain secrecy.

The video establishes Nell's significance as a disclosure figure through both his credentials and his substance: his campaign plan maps disclosure across four channels (public sector, philosophical, scientific, and private sector) with phase targets extending from January 2024 through October 2034 and beyond. Phase 3, targeted for October 2030, is identified as a potential public disclosure inflection point. UAP Gerb also highlights Nell's broader analytical framework, including a proposed replacement for the Kardashev scale developed with [[Matthew Pines]], a three-category UAP taxonomy (physical, psychophysical, and metaphysical), and warnings about catastrophic uncontrolled disclosure versus a managed approach.

## Karl Nell's Background and Credentials

Karl Nell served as a U.S. Army Colonel, a DIA Technical Intelligence Operations Officer, and Deputy Chief Technology Officer at [[Northrop Grumman]]. He was the first DIA directorate representative deployed for multi-agency planning of Operation Iraqi Freedom, and served as CJ2 (Senior Intelligence Officer) for the Combined Joint Captured Material Exploitation Center in Iraq. He also served as the foreign materials program command representative to US CENTCOM CFLCC in Kuwait. After his military career, he became a Colonel in Army Futures Command.

His firsthand witness status — as one of the 40 witnesses interviewed by [[David Grusch]] over four years before Grusch's 2023 congressional testimony — is believed to derive from his Operation Iraqi Freedom role as a foreign materials program representative, which may have involved an encounter with a downed UAP. [[Leslie Kean]] published an article in The Debrief identifying Nell among Grusch's 40 witnesses.

## Legislative Analysis: The Schumer Amendment

Nell's presentation analyzes the Schumer Amendment to the NDAA as a landmark piece of UAP legislation. Key definitions he identifies:

- **UAP**: Defined as phenomena lacking "prosaic attribution" — the determination that something has a known human origin, whether foreign or domestic. Under this definition, UAP implicitly refers to non-human phenomena.
- **Temporarily Non-attributed Object (TNO)**: Objects temporarily resisting prosaic attribution due to observer or sensor limitations, but eventually identified as human-made.
- **Non-Human Intelligence (NHI)**: Any sentient, intelligent non-human life form regardless of nature or origin. The term appears 18 times in the Schumer Amendment — a frequency Nell argues reflects deliberate legal establishment of the NHI concept in federal law.
- **Legacy Programs**: All federal, state, local, commercial, academic, and private sector efforts to collect, exploit, reverse-engineer, or study UAP or NHI biological evidence.
- **Technology of Unknown Origin (TUO)**: Materials or technology associated with UAP that lack prosaic attribution or known means of human manufacturing.
- **Eminent Domain**: The Amendment grants the federal government authority to seize UAP materials and records from private corporations, which Nell identifies as a legally significant mechanism for recovering material held outside government control.

Nell acknowledges the Amendment was "watered down and gutted" from its original form. He notes [[AARO]] was conspicuously absent from his list of Phase Zero UAP narrative shapers despite its official oversight role — a slight UAP Gerb notes [[Sean Kirkpatrick]] was likely unhappy about.

## Reasons for Non-Disclosure

Nell catalogues the reasons UAP information has been withheld:
1. National security implications
2. Lack of a credible plan for managing disclosure
3. Risk of social disruption
4. Covert agreements with NHI (quid pro quo)
5. Cover for crimes committed to maintain secrecy — Nell references [[David Grusch]]'s indication that these crimes include murder, with "feudalistic dominance" as a motivation
6. Organizational inertia
7. Risk that the USA may be losing a UAP arms race with Russia or China

## Five-Phase UAP Disclosure Roadmap

Nell's campaign plan operates across four channels simultaneously: public sector, philosophical investigation, scientific research, and private sector. The roadmap:

| Phase | Target Date | Goal |
|-------|------------|------|
| Phase 0 | Pre-2024 | Narrative shaping (NY Times article, DoD video releases, AATIP, UAP Task Force) |
| Phase 1 | January 1, 2024 | Government acceptance; UAP as bipartisan issue |
| Phase 2 | January 1, 2026 | UAP signature correlation; harness academia |
| Phase 3 | October 2030 | **Public acceptance; potential disclosure inflection point for humanity** |
| Phase 4 | October 2034 | Answer the five W's of UAP; noted as off-target |
| Phase 5 | Indefinite | Interactive approach; integration of NHI and NHI technology with Earth civilization |

Phase 3 is identified by Nell as the critical threshold — the point at which public acceptance could enable genuine controlled disclosure versus the risk of catastrophic uncontrolled disclosure. Nell warns against a "catastrophic disclosure" scenario in which all UAP information is dumped without coordination, potentially by an adversary or by NHI themselves, causing severe social disruption.

A slide from Nell's presentation partially leaked in November 2023. The video's release followed the [[Sol Foundation]] symposium where Nell, [[David Grusch]], [[Chris Mellon]], [[Kevin Knuth]], [[Gary Nolan]], and [[Jacques Vallee]] all presented.

## Analytical Framework: Beyond the Kardashev Scale

Nell's backup slides, prepared with [[Matthew Pines]], propose replacing the Kardashev scale — which benchmarks civilizations by energy consumption (planetary, stellar, galactic) — with a "Universal Civilization Development Datum" using a multivariable system assessing what civilizations *are*, *know*, and *who they are*. End states in this framework include spacetime manipulation, understanding reality, and immortality/physical transcendence.

This framework is proposed as a tool for forecasting NHI motives and technological capabilities based on attributes rather than energy harnessing alone — a distinction Nell considers critical for preparing a meaningful post-disclosure policy response.

## UAP Taxonomy

Nell proposes classifying UAP and NHI hypotheses into three categories:
- **Physical**: Conventional extraterrestrial craft, natural plasma phenomena, advanced drones
- **Psychophysical**: Phenomena requiring consciousness interaction — interdimensional beings, beings from higher dimensions
- **Metaphysical**: Entities operating outside physical law — ascended masters, beings from colliding universes, pangalactic entanglement

The taxonomy is explicitly designed to be intellectually inclusive while allowing scientific investigation to narrow the field. Nell cites a newly published paper proposing some UAP are plasma-based pre-life forms as an example of the kind of reactive hypothesis generation occurring in Phase 1.
