## Summary

This video examines alleged connections between Science Applications International Corporation (SAIC) and covert UFO legacy programs, arguing that defense contractors and intelligence community insiders have systematically suppressed UAP-related information from congressional oversight. A central focus is Sean Kirkpatrick, former head of AARO, who is accused of lying to Senators Rubio, Warren, and Gillibrand during classified briefings, misrepresenting witness testimony in the AARO Historical Report Volume 1 (contradicted by Michael Herrera's official memorandum for record), and engaging DoD contractor SanCorp in 2022 to plug whistleblower leaks. The video also alleges a conflict of interest involving Ronald Moltry, who recommended AARO's formation while simultaneously serving on the board of Battelle Memorial Institute — a board membership he later allegedly removed from his LinkedIn profile. Drawing on the work of Catherine Austin Fitts and her 2004 "Black Budget of the United States" paper, the video further claims that $21 trillion in unauthorized DoD and HUD spending between 1995 and 2015 was funneled through defense contractors — including SAIC, Lockheed Martin Information Systems, IBM, and DynCorp — into clandestine UFO-related R&D programs, with former SAIC employee Denise McKenzie having testified in 2001 that billions were directed into fake budget entries to deceive government auditors.
