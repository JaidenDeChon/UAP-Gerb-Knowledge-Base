## The June 1933 Crash and Cover-Up

An unknown craft crashed or landed near [[Magenta, Italy]], west of Milan, in June 1933 — 14 years before the Roswell incident. The object was bell-shaped, approximately 10 meters in diameter, and resembled a combination of a flying saucer and Die Glocke (the legendary alleged Nazi wonder weapon). Mussolini initially believed it was an advanced terrestrial aircraft of British, French, or German origin.

The [[Agenzia Stefani]] state propaganda agency issued three classified telegrams on June 13, 1933 — all bearing "Lampo" (highest) priority and "Riservato" (confidential) classification — that document the systematic suppression:

1. **First telegram (morning):** Ordered "absolute silence" regarding the "presumed landing of an unknown aircraft on national soil," published a meteor cover story, and threatened maximum penalties including State Security Court prosecution for any journalist who violated the order.
2. **Second telegram (4:00 PM):** Directed that the craft be attributed to a meteor recognized by the [[Brera Astronomical Observatory]] and instructed media to minimize coverage.
3. **Third telegram (5:07 PM):** Ordered immediate cessation of all reporting and required newspapers to recast any leads already in print, again threatening maximum penalties.

This sequence — event, cover story deployment, total news blackout within a single day — closely mirrors the Roswell incident management in 1947. Italian newspaper archives from late June 1933 report severe lightning storms and hailstorms in the Magenta area the prior week, consistent with the mandated meteor cover story.

## The RS-33 Commission

Mussolini established the [[RS-33 (Gabinetto RS 33)]] — Gabinetto Ricerche Speciali 33 — immediately after the recovery to examine the craft and enforce secrecy. He headed the commission himself, with Foreign Minister [[Galeazzo Ciano]] and Air Marshal [[Italo Balbo]] providing political and military oversight. Scientific members included astronomer [[Gino Cecchini]] and possibly Nobel Prize-winning radio pioneer and Senator [[Guglielmo Marconi]], who reportedly resisted Mussolini's terrestrial-origin conclusion and argued the craft was extraterrestrial in nature — consistent with Marconi's publicly documented interest in contacting non-human civilizations via radio.

The crash retrieval protocol, addressed to a figure referred to as "Desanti" (almost certainly a pseudonym for Colonel Tomaso David, founder of the Vulpi intelligence spy group and later head of the Salo Secret Service), consisted of nine steps: immediate witness arrest; exclusive reporting to RS-33 through La Sapienza University in Rome; mandatory disinformation publication using pre-approved templates (RS-33 form FZ-4); strict exclusion of all scientific bodies including pontifical universities; and specific budget allocations through the R. Academia de Italia. The host draws direct structural comparisons between RS-33 protocols and [[USAF Regulation 200-2]] (1953) and the alleged [[Majestic 12]] operating framework, arguing RS-33 functioned as an Italian institutional precursor to the US crash retrieval apparatus.

## Document Authentication and Post-1933 Sightings

Pinotti applied forensic rigor before accepting the materials. A forensic expert confirmed paper and ink dated to the 1930s. Scientific consultant Antonio Agag stated publicly the documents "can be considered original and therefore authentic." An Italian researcher known online as "low lab 5185" confirmed the writing style matched fascist-era futurism communication conventions and provided matching template examples of authentic 1930s Italian government telegrams.

The document cache also included a series of handwritten notes bearing the Kingdom Senate seal, describing a 1936 sighting labeled "MRE" over Mestre. The notes — accompanied by a Stefani telegram demanding "absolute secrecy" — include a drawing of a cigar-shaped craft with an exhaust trail and two dome-topped saucers. The transcribed text describes a metallic disc 10–12 meters long, faster than any known balloon, generating no sound. An attached prefect's note states: "The Duce has expressed his worries because he says that if it were an English or French aircraft his foreign policy would have to start all over again." The video also references copies of telegrams from Italian prefects to the government between 1933 and 1938 mentioning "unconventional flying vehicles" over Albania.

## The Vatican Connection and US Recovery

The craft remained in Italian possession under [[SIAI-Marchetti Aircraft Company]] hangars in [[Varese, Italy]] during most of the fascist period. A fire at a warehouse in Varese on March 17, 1943 and the subsequent German takeover of Italian facilities under [[Operation Achse]] (rendered as "Operation Ox" in the video) complicated the craft's custody. It may have been moved to Mussolini's enormous bunker under [[Mount Soratte]] - 27 miles north of Rome, 3 miles long and 300 feet underground, the largest bunker in Europe at its 1937 construction.

According to journalist [[Christopher Sharp]]'s 2021 article "All UFO Disclosure Roads Lead to Rome" and David Grusch's testimony, Mussolini confided in Pope Pius XII about the craft. As Italy's Nazi alliance deepened, the Pope grew concerned and back-channeled intelligence to US authorities, reportedly resulting in Allied bombers sparing the facility and enabling a targeted OSS recovery in 1944–1945. The host acknowledges this portion rests on reporting and testimony rather than a hard paper trail, while noting Sharp is a well-regarded UAP journalist. Claims by William Brophy or his grandson that bodies were recovered at the Magenta site are explicitly excluded from the analysis as unsupported by original documents.
