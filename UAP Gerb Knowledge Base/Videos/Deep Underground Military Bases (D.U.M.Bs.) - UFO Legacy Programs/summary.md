## Overview

This video is UAP Gerb's first full investigation of [[Deep Underground Military Bases (DUMBs)|deep underground military bases]] (DUMBs) and their alleged role in [[Legacy Programs|UFO legacy programs]]: the black-budget programs that, in Gerb's framing, recover non-human craft, exploit technologies of unknown origin, reverse-engineer them and build [[Alien Reproduction Vehicle (ARV)|alien reproduction vehicles]]. Programs that Gerb, quoting [[David Grusch]], calls a "multi-decade secret Cold War" need extreme secrecy and strict need-to-know access for research, testing, and storing craft and possibly biologics. So, he argues, we must look underground. His starting point is that DUMBs are not controversial. Acknowledged sites such as the [[Cheyenne Mountain Complex]] and [[Raven Rock Complex (Site R)|Raven Rock]] exist, and a senior [[US Army Corps of Engineers]] official, [[Lloyd A. Duscha]], said publicly that several of the Corps' most interesting underground facilities "are still classified". The open questions are which sites engage with UFO programs, what they do, and whether decades of rumours of tunnels and trains linking them could be true.

The video leans throughout on the three books of Dr. [[Richard Sauder]], which build their case from public records, FOIA releases and declassified documents, and on the late researcher [[Bill Hamilton]]. It follows a documentary trail from Nazi underground factories and [[Operation Paperclip]], through RAND and Corps of Engineers studies of deep bases in the 1950s and 1960s, the proposed [[Deep Underground Command Center (DUCC)|Deep Underground Command Center]] beneath Washington, and a burst of tunneling studies in the early 1970s, to maglev research in the 1990s. Against that trail it sets witness testimony: [[Leonard Stringfield]]'s crash-retrieval files, Hamilton's informants in the [[Antelope Valley]], Sauder's sources at [[China Lake Naval Air Weapons Station|China Lake]] and [[Los Alamos National Laboratory|Los Alamos]], and Gerb's own conversations with nearly half a dozen former military personnel who say they have travelled to underground facilities. Gerb uses this testimony more than he usually does.

Gerb concludes that networks of UFO-related DUMBs probably exist in two regions. One runs through the American Southwest, across New Mexico, Arizona and Southern California and possibly up to [[Dugway Proving Ground|Dugway]] and Colorado, with the Antelope Valley at its heart. The other lies on the East Coast, around the Pentagon and [[Crystal City, Virginia|Crystal City]]. He proposes an order of operations: the bases were built from 1961, connective tunnels about a decade later, and train systems about a decade after that. The video is billed as part one of a series; part two will be an interview with Sauder about Hamilton's network map. Gerb deliberately leaves out [[Bob Lazar]]'s S4, the Dulce base and Phil Schneider as too controversial.

## Underground Bases Are Real

Gerb opens with official admissions. Lloyd A. Duscha, deputy director of engineering and construction for the Corps of Engineers, gave a talk titled "Underground Facilities for Defense: Experience and Lessons". The video dates it to 1989; the published text appears in 1987 conference proceedings. Duscha said that after World War II political and economic factors caused a renewed interest in thinking underground, and that the Corps became involved in "some very complex and interesting military projects". He then departed from his topic: several of the most interesting facilities the Corps had designed and built "are still classified". ## Nazi Origins and Paperclip

Gerb traces American DUMBs back to Nazi Germany. Colonel Robert S. Allen's *Lucky Forward*, a history of Patton's Third Army, describes four man-made underground installations in and around Ohrdruf as "literally subterranean towns", built with concentration-camp labour, with the spoil scattered into hills miles away. Some reached more than 50 feet down, with miles of two- and three-storey facilities laid out like wheels with spokes, and were meant to house the Nazi high command after Berlin was bombed. The Regenwurmlager, or "earthworm camp", beneath western Poland had miles of tunnels and electric train lines linking bunkers and fortifications. Gerb remarks that this "sounds familiar".

Two declassified 1947 Paperclip documents record an [[Air Materiel Command]] requirement for four German technicians to consult on a planned underground plant program. One was [[Xaver Dorsch]], director of the Organisation Todt, the Nazi counterpart of the Seabees or the Corps of Engineers, founded by Fritz Todt of Autobahn fame. Late in the war Dorsch helped build massive underground factories to escape Allied bombing, and he may have worked on the Regenwurmlager and Ohrdruf. He briefed the Americans after his capture in 1945 and again in 1949.

The second figure is SS engineer [[Hans Kammler]], who built concentration camps including Auschwitz and ran many of the Nazis' secret weapons projects. Gerb connects him to the [[1933 Magenta UFO CrashRetrieval|1933 Magenta, Italy crash]], a case David Grusch supports: the Nazis are said to have taken the lenticular disc from the Italians in 1943, before Americans recovered it after the war, and Kammler would have been one of the chief figures to study it. Kammler vanished at the end of the war, but, drawing on Dean Reuter's *The Hidden Nazi*, Gerb argues documents show he was taken alive into US custody. Albert Speer said that in April 1945 Kammler told him he had business to offer the Americans. OSS agent Donald W. Richardson said decades later that the Nazis had brought "treasure" to the US that served Cold War armament. Kammler reportedly hid documents from his think tank in an underground site in the woods of Czechoslovakia, and 44 years after the war a team from the Lawrence Livermore and Los Alamos laboratories went to a second, unemptied cavern to retrieve them. Gerb concludes that Kammler probably ran early iterations of UFO legacy programs underground, and that Dorsch and Kammler "could very well be the key architects of modern-day US UFO and DUMB legacy programs".

## The Planners: RAND and the Corps of Engineers

In the 1950s the United States proposed massive underground facilities that then disappeared from public view. Two 1957 articles reported an Army plan, proposed by American Machine and Foundry, Redstone Arsenal and the Army Ballistic Missile Agency, for a rocket factory inside Green Mountain outside Huntsville, Alabama, with a subterranean "junior Pentagon" to direct the defence of the southern US. Gerb reads this as early private-military cooperation.

The [[RAND Corporation]], established in 1948 out of the Army Air Forces' Project RAND, took up underground base construction with the Air Force in the 1950s. Its 1959 symposium *Deep Underground Construction* discussed protecting installations "deep underground or under mountains" in a nuclear war, and its chairman said RAND had been investigating since 1957 the need for "a small number of super hard deep underground centers". A 1960 RAND study for the Air Force examined 12 sites, all more than 1,000 feet deep. Site 3, near Santa Barbara, lies just over 100 miles from the Antelope Valley and under 100 miles from the laboratories of Los Angeles.

The Corps' five-part manual *Design of Underground Installations in Rock* (1959–1961) takes such facilities for granted: "vital government installations have been placed underground". It describes deep workings, "long and tunnel-like", with 50-by-50-foot tunnels and chambers up to 100 feet high, notes that "truck or rail traffic might be important", and advises that portals be as inconspicuous as possible, with camouflage considered. Gerb sets this beside witness MS's account of the Dugway facility's entrance, a small building in the middle of nowhere. A 1963 Corps manual covered self-contained, nuclear-powered underground installations with diesel backup. In 1964 the Corps proposed 12 massive structures up to 4,000 feet deep. Two matter here:

- **Below Yuma County, Arizona**, reached by vertical or incline shaft. Steven Greer's witness 10773 spoke of a massive research and storage facility for material of unknown origin at Yuma Proving Ground. Like Dugway, Yuma is an Army major range and test facility base run by the Army Test and Evaluation Command, so, Gerb reasons, if one has a stiff, the other likely does too.
- **In Inyo County, California**, reached by incline shaft, on the boundary of China Lake. A Navy contact told Sauder that the China Lake facility extends a mile deep and holds "weapons more powerful than nuclear weapons".

A 1972 Naval Facilities Engineering Command report planned administrative, medical, aircraft maintenance and ammunition facilities underground, and the Seabees are said to maintain underground installations between Camp David and the CIA's Camp Peary. From Air Materiel Command's 1947 request to Duscha's admission, Gerb infers that the stiffs of interest were built across a 42-year window, some before 1961. Groom Lake, built in the 1950s on a former boron mine, may be one: a 1991 DARPA report notes that secret cavities may be found alongside underground mines. Because the literature describes plans for massive bases after 1961, he places most construction between 1961 and 1989. The same 1991 DARPA and Army Missile Command paper, written to train inspectors hunting clandestine nuclear test complexes, states that buildings "can provide cover for shafts or other openings to a secret underground cavity".

Gerb has covered other alleged underground sites before: the 10-storey [[Defense Advanced Research Center (DARS)|Defense Advanced Research Center]] under [[Area 51]] described by [[Edgar Fouche]], the [[Underground Facility at NSWC Crane|Off-World Technologies Division]] under [[Naval Surface Warfare Center Crane]] described by [[Randy Anderson]], and the Dugway facility.

## The Trains

For decades, rumours of underground railways, especially [[Maglev Underground Transportation|maglev trains]], have been inseparable from accounts of DUMBs, sometimes described as pneumatic trains in partial-vacuum tubes. Maglevs levitate on electromagnets instead of wheels. They offer higher speeds, better acceleration and gradient handling, lower maintenance and less noise, at much greater cost, and have reached 375 mph. New York's first subway was Alfred Ely Beach's pneumatic tube line.

Gerb starts again with the Germans. [[Hermann Kemper]] began work in 1934 on the Rohrbahn, maglev cars sent through underground vacuum tunnels at hundreds or thousands of miles an hour; by 1938 he pictured Berlin linked to other cities at 600 to 1,000 mph. Whether Paperclip recruited him is unknown, but Gerb thinks he may have been contracted to help build early American systems. Maglev patents followed from the 1960s. By the early 1990s the acknowledged multi-agency [[National Maglev Initiative]], involving the DOE and the Corps of Engineers, was studying a national system for the year 2000. Its concept teams included Bechtel with Hughes Aircraft and MIT; Boeing and General Dynamics; and Grumman, Battelle and [[Honeywell]], all contractors Gerb suspects of decades of reverse-engineering work. By 2000 Lockheed Martin had agreements with Transrapid International USA. But Sauder and Hamilton first heard of clandestine systems in the 1980s. Sauder read of underground rail to Area 51 from Mercury, Nevada, and from the Naval Surface Warfare Center Corona Division, and the rumours reached online UFO forums around 1997.

Gerb finds the likely origins in the 1960s and 1970s. Engineers from the Office of Naval Research, the Army Research Office and Booz Allen Hamilton founded the International Society for Terrain-Vehicle Systems in the mid-1960s. Its working group coordinated research on high-speed underground tube trains, as did a Commerce Department panel whose 1967 report assumed "extensive tunneling will be involved". The high-speed ground transportation initiative ran through the 1960s and 1970s before fading in the 1980s, and involved TRW, General Dynamics, MIT, Southwest Research Institute, General Electric, NASA, the Army, the Navy and Battelle. TRW's 1970 study described tubes as deep as 3,000 feet and trains at about 450 mph.

The key document is [[Robert M. Salter]]'s 1972 RAND study, [[Very High Speed Transit System (VHST)|*The Very High Speed Transit System*]]. It described a continental deep-underground network of "electromagnetically levitated and propelled cars in an evacuated tunnel", examined speeds up to 14,000 mph (Los Angeles to New York in 21 minutes), and built on Robert Goddard's 1945 vacuum-tube concept and Michael Minovitch's deep underground tube shuttle. Salter recommended going deep to avoid grade crossings and weather, observed that deep tunneling was not much harder than shallow, and wrote that the first line would probably stop at "two intermediate staging points". Gerb reads this as the hub-and-spoke pattern he sees in DUMB networks: central stations joined to each other, with branch lines to small local sites. RAND's route map also matches Hamilton's network closely. No admission of maglevs connecting stiffs is public, Gerb concedes, but there is an overwhelming historical record of interest in underground transit at least as fast as an airliner.
