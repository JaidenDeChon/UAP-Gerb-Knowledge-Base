## Summary
The video, hosted by UAP researcher "UAP Gerb," presents an investigation into Dugway Proving Ground — a U.S. Army installation in Utah — as an alleged site of clandestine UFO Legacy Programs involving the retrieval and reverse engineering of Technologies of Unknown Origin (TUO). The central witness, an anonymous U.S. Navy veteran and former DOD contractor employed at Dugway from 2009 to 2013 through C Martin Company, claims to have accidentally entered an unmarked "ghost building" at Avery Technical Center, where he observed a hovering, seamless brushed-metal saucer craft approximately 10 feet tall and 20 feet in circumference, guarded by armed personnel and surrounded by lab technicians believed to be from Battelle Memorial Institute and ATCH. Following the encounter, the witness was interrogated for six hours by men in black suits at the Ditto Technical Center Garrison building, and was later told by a senior DOD chemist identified as "GP" that the craft was extraterrestrial, operated via gravity displacement, and was associated with a wormhole-based navigation system and an underground light rail network connecting Dugway's alleged Deep Underground Military Base (DUMB) to other classified facilities nationwide. The video also references alleged unauthorized transfers of $34–$40 billion from Air Force RDT&E funds in fiscal years 2013–2014, and notes that the Senate Select Committee on Intelligence approached the witness to testify, an offer he declined.
