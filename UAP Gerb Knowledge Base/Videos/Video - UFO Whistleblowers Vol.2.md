## Summary

"UFO Whistleblowers [Vol. 2]" examines multiple alleged firsthand and secondhand accounts of recovered non-human craft and government UFO secrecy, focusing primarily on whistleblower "RB," a former U.S. Marine corporal who claimed in 1963 he spent two weeks guarding a 40-foot seamless disc-shaped craft at an undisclosed military base, describing a strict color-coded badge access system, a white-taped restricted perimeter that even USAF generals and the Secretary of the Navy were barred from crossing, and a hull material that resisted drilling, cutting torches, and high-powered lasers. The video also details Senator Barry Goldwater's documented attempts to access the alleged "Blue Room" at Wright-Patterson Air Force Base — corroborated by multiple letters and a 1953 AFOSI document in which Master Sergeant Ralph Brown stated the base held three recovered flying saucers and alien bodies — and his angry refusal by General Curtis LeMay, with a FOIA response later confirming related film materials were destroyed on September 9, 1965. Additional claims covered include Steven Greer's alleged coordination of UAP briefing documents sent to French President Nicolas Sarkozy in 2009 via "Source One" Elaine Boder, and allegations that Frank Borman filmed six UFOs descending onto the lunar surface during the Apollo 8 mission in 1968. The video draws on archival materials accessed by researcher Michael Schratt from Leonard Stringfield's personal archives and positions RB as a credible, non-public whistleblower who never sought profit from his account.

## Concepts
- [[Blue Room]] — A classified room at Wright Patterson Air Force Base allegedly containing UFO-related information and materials; referenced by Barry Goldwater in multiple letters.
- [[Above Top Secret Classification]] — A classification level cited by Goldwater as applied to UFO-related materials at Wright Patterson, described as even higher than Top Secret.
- [[NHI (Non-Human Intelligence)]] — Term used to describe alleged non-human entities or craft, referenced in context of the alleged Apollo 8 UFO film.
- [[Bogey]] — Military aviation term for an unidentified object; used by Frank Borman during the Gemini 7 mission in 1965 to describe a sighted object, later attributed to the rocket's own booster.
- [[Non-Human Intelligence (NHI) Craft]] — Craft alleged to be of non-human origin, reportedly stored and studied at classified military facilities.
- [[Color-Coded Security Badges]] — A compartmentalized access control system used at the facility housing the disc-shaped craft, with red badges granting total access.
- [[Seamless Craft Hull]] — The whistleblower RB described the craft as having a nearly seamless surface that resisted drilling, torches, and high-powered lasers.
- [[Crash Retrieval]] — The alleged recovery and storage of non-human craft and biological remains by government or military entities.
- [[Ooid Cross-Section]] — A geometric shape described by whistleblower RB to characterize the cross-sectional profile of the disc-shaped craft he guarded.
- [[White Taped Circle Restricted Zone]] — A physical boundary surrounding the recovered craft that no unauthorized personnel, including USAF generals, could cross.
- [[Security Oath]] — A legally binding agreement signed by RB upon leaving the Marines, carrying a 10-year prison sentence and $110,000 fine for violations.
- [[UAP Whistleblowers]] — Individuals who claim firsthand or secondhand knowledge of UAP encounters, retrievals, or government programs and come forward publicly or privately.
- [[Project Aquarius]] — An alleged classified government program related to UAPs, described as extremely interesting alongside Dan Bish's claims.
