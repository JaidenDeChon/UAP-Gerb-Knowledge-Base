## Overview

This video analyzes claims made by a redacted witness known as [[Ed]] (Witness 11063) in [[Steven Greer]]'s [[Disclosure Project]] archive. Ed is described as a US Air Force officer who served at [[Nellis Air Force Base]], [[Hanscom Air Force Base, Massachusetts]], [[Edwards Air Force Base]], and the [[Pentagon]], and who allegedly worked on testing [[Reverse-Engineered Non-Human Technology]].

The central thesis is that a joint reverse engineering effort operated between [[Edwards Air Force Base]] and [[Area 51]] / [[S4]] via the [[Nevada Test and Training Range]], with operational ties to the [[412th Test Wing]] and selected defense contractors including [[Lockheed Martin]] and [[Boeing]]. The host presents Ed's testimony as one of the strongest claims that USAF test pilot and electronic warfare units directly supported man-made craft programs.

The video also links Ed's testimony to earlier crash retrieval literature, including reports by [[Leonard Stringfield]], and to broader claims about suppressed advanced propulsion and energy systems. It frames these allegations as part of a long-running legacy program ecosystem involving compartmentalized military units, contractors, and black-budget funding channels.

## Ed's Service Record and Testimony

### Nellis and NTTR Period (1999-2002)

According to the discussed documents, Ed served at [[Nellis Air Force Base]] and worked in and around [[Building 200]] while the [[998th Range Wing]] (later integrated into the [[Nevada Test and Training Range]]) was active. He allegedly worked with [[Lockheed Martin]] as a range technical contractor and had interactions with DOE-linked operations.

Greer notes claim Ed was briefed at [[S4]] on captured craft and later stated he had seen man-made craft on the range. The host treats this as an early-stage read-in before Ed's later formal assignment at Edwards.

### Hanscom Period (2002-2005)

Ed reportedly served at [[Hanscom Air Force Base, Massachusetts]] and worked with [[MIT]] on foreign military assets. The video states this period involved conventional or terrestrial systems rather than direct non-human technology testing.

### Edwards Period (2006-2010)

Ed was reportedly sent to [[Edwards Air Force Base]] in 2006 for test pilot school orientation and then moved into a leadership role in an [[Electronic Warfare]] group tied to the [[412th Test Wing]]. In this role, he allegedly coordinated between engineers and test pilots when man-made craft were being tested.

The host argues this is the most consequential part of the testimony: it places alleged reverse-engineering flight-test activity inside the same institutional ecosystem as Edwards test pilot operations.

### Go-Public Plan and Pentagon Transfer (2008-2015)

Greer's notes describe a proposed Air Force disclosure pathway around 2008 that was allegedly halted by a two-star general around 2009-2010. Ed then moved to the [[Pentagon]] and allegedly observed large funding transfers into non-blue authorization channels, interpreted in the video as black-budget support for unacknowledged programs.

## Historical Corroboration Threads

### Edwards Incident History

The host compares Ed's claims with older allegations:

- [[Gordon Cooper]]'s account of a filmed landed disc at Edwards in 1957.
- 1970s and 1980s anti-gravity and electrostatic propulsion research references linked to Edwards, including [[Project Outgrowth]] and the [[Byfield Brown effect]].
- [[Edgar Fouche]]'s later claims that triangular craft programs (including [[TR-3B]]) involved Edwards-trained pilot pipelines.

### Stringfield Material

The video draws heavily on [[Leonard Stringfield]] reporting, including:

- "[[Project Heaven]]" claims tied to a landed saucer and biological occupants at Edwards.
- [[Debbie Clayton]]'s 1971 acorn-shaped crash account near Edwards.
- "[[Project Red Light]]" claims that a recovered craft shipped from Edwards was tested at Area 51.

The host presents these as overlapping narratives rather than independently verified facts.

## 1991 NRO Document and Blackjack Claims

A purported 1991 NRO leak is discussed as a potential node linking Nellis, Groom and Area 51 infrastructure, and a "[[Blackjack Team]]" construct. The video cites Greer's claim of two connected entities:

- [[Blackjack Control]] (allegedly Nellis-associated)
- [[Blackjack Team]] (allegedly Edwards-associated)

The host notes unresolved inconsistencies in sourcing and nomenclature but treats the Blackjack references as a major lead for future research.

- A programmatic link existed between [[Edwards Air Force Base]] and [[Area 51]] / [[S4]] through range and test structures.
- [[412th Test Wing]]-adjacent units allegedly supported testing of man-made craft derived from non-human technology.
- A planned disclosure effort around 2008 was allegedly terminated by senior command authority.
