## Summary
The video examines claims that Lockheed Martin, through its Skunk Works division, has long been in possession of retrieved UAP crash materials and has engaged in reverse engineering efforts within classified black programs, with custody allegedly held by private aerospace contractors rather than the U.S. government directly. Former Senate Majority Leader Harry Reid stated in a 2021 New York Times interview that he was told for decades Lockheed possessed such materials and was personally denied Pentagon clearance to view them. The video also highlights statements attributed to Skunk Works director Ben Rich, including a 1986 letter to researcher John Alexander confirming his belief in both man-made and extraterrestrial UFO categories, and alleged remarks at a 1993 UCLA conference — corroborated by Mufon director Jan Harzan — that humanity already possesses technology to "take ET home," though no audio or video evidence of those quotes exists. Additional claims include that the CIA was involved as a government customer in a high-security custodial arrangement for Lockheed's UAP materials, and that UAP whistleblower David Grusch and scientist Gary Nolan have also named Lockheed Martin in connection with non-human retrieved materials.
