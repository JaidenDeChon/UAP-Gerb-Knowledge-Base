## Overview

This video is one of Gerb's footage analyses. Its subject is the [[Metapod]], a short, close-range clip of a small crescent-shaped craft said to have been filmed near [[El Escorial, Madrid, Spain]] on 6 September 2015. Gerb calls it one of his all-time favourite pieces of alleged UFO footage and, if genuine, one of the best close-up views of a UFO ever recorded. Most UFO footage, he notes at the start, is grainy, distant or a handful of pixels on a degraded infrared (FLIR) display; the Metapod shows a structured object in daylight, near enough to see what it is made of.

The analysis moves in four steps. First Gerb traces the footage to its source, which he stresses he could not do for the [[Flyby Footage]] he analysed earlier: an email sent to the YouTube channel [[UFO VNI]] the day after the sighting by a witness signing himself Modesto. He then follows how the clip travelled online, through a Portuguese forum and the site [[Third Phase of the Sun]] within five days, and explains why he works from a stabilized copy by the channel [[ITBS]]. Second, he describes the craft (a reflective metallic back, a transparent glass-like front) and measures its flight against the [[Five UAP Characteristics (AATIP)|five UAP observables]] associated with [[AATIP (Advanced Aerospace Threat Identification Program)|AATIP]] and [[Luis Elizondo]], finding three. Third, he takes up the debunks (a balloon, an object on a string, CGI) and finds none of them persuasive. Fourth, he sets the clip beside five other recordings from 1998 to 2023, from Peru, Mexico, Vancouver, an alleged US Navy [[US Navy Blue Angels|Blue Angels]] cockpit leak and Texas, that he argues show the same craft.

Gerb's conclusion is cautious but favourable. He is no visual-effects artist and cannot rule on artefacts, but the footage "passes the simple eye test" for him, and he hypothesizes that the Metapod may be a real single-occupant observation craft whose transparent face is a window. He closes by correcting a rumour that the clip was filmed in Denver, his own city, and by announcing his next video, a review of the [[Sol Foundation Interview Release|interviews the Sol Foundation had just released]].

## Tracing the Footage

### The email

Unlike the Flyby clip, Gerb says, the Metapod can be followed back to its first appearance. On 7 September 2015 a man signing himself Modesto emailed [[UFO VNI]], having read that the channel would publish viewers' UFO footage. Gerb shows the original Spanish email and reads it in English translation. Its main points:

- The video was taken "the day before yesterday", 6 September 2015, by the writer and a friend he calls Mo.
- The object made no sound. The writer, who has spent years in the countryside and knows the sound of birds' wings in flight, says it descended vertically and then moved away.
- He admits he is somewhat short-sighted, but his friend has exceptional eyesight, and the friend said he saw something moving inside the UFO: "something alive with limbs".
- For personal reasons the pair would not give the exact location, only that it was near El Escorial, in the direction of Las Navas del Marqués. El Escorial, he writes, is a place where strange things happen, not in the town but in the nearby mountains.
- The writer had never believed in UFOs, but from that moment he knew the object "wasn't human". His friend, more taciturn and from the town, did not seem surprised. Seeing the video, the friend said "here again", and admitted it was not the first time he had seen the object, and that his late father had seen it too.
- He would give the GPS data and exact location to the channel only for its own investigation, not for publication (the place name and coordinates are redacted in the copy Gerb shows), asked for his personal details to be kept private, and handed over the video for the channel to use as it pleased, offering to send the micro SD card if preferred.

Gerb flags one inconsistency. The writer introduces himself as Modesto and his friend as Mo, but later refers to "my friend Modesto". Gerb wonders whether this is a careless slip by someone using pseudonyms, since the sender was already unwilling to reveal the location. He does not know why UFO VNI later took the video down, perhaps at Modesto's request, and says he has contacted the channel to learn more.

### How the clip travelled

Posts about the footage appeared almost at once. A Portuguese forum, since taken down, discussed it on 8 September 2015: it could not tell whether the video was real or fake but was impressed by its realistic zoom, focus and blur, by the shape of the object, and by its similarity to the 2006 [[Bruja de Monterrey]] footage from Mexico. [[Third Phase of the Sun]] followed on 11 September with a post that also detailed UFO VNI's correspondence with Modesto. Both of the clip's main YouTube homes have since gone: UFO VNI's original upload and a 2017 re-upload by [[UFO Invasions]]. Gerb therefore works from a stabilized version made by the channel [[ITBS]], which he credits in the video description.

### Against the AATIP observables

Gerb measures the pod against the [[Five UAP Characteristics (AATIP)|five characteristics of UAP]] listed by [[AATIP (Advanced Aerospace Threat Identification Program)|AATIP]] and [[Luis Elizondo]], and finds three of them:

- Instantaneous acceleration, in the pod's sudden drop in altitude.
- Anti-gravity, or no visible signs of propulsion.
- Low observability.

He does not claim the other two, trans-medium travel and hypersonic velocity. Beyond the five, he points to what he calls intelligent flight characteristics: the sudden altitude drop; holding still in a wind that can be heard buffeting the camera's microphone; a straight-line traverse away from the person filming; and a variable but stable rotation about its long axis. He asks viewers to watch in particular for the moment its rotation reverses from counterclockwise to clockwise, accelerating variably in both directions.

On the soundtrack, Gerb overlays a translation of the witnesses' voices made by a Reddit user, and passes on another user's observation that the audio seems to have gone through a noise-reduction filter, applied either in post-processing or by the camera itself to cut the wind.

The two earliest recordings frame the Spanish clip in time. The 1998 footage from Peru is the first appearance of the shape that Gerb can track. The [[Bruja de Monterrey]], filmed in Mexico in 2006, is the one the Portuguese forum had already compared the Metapod to in 2015, and Gerb reads it as the craft's non-transparent back moving away from the camera.

The Vancouver footage from 2021 comes with an account from the person who posted it: three objects about 20 feet apart moved north on a horizontal plane; two then climbed fast while the third carried on north out of sight; and of the two, one went north and the other west until they disappeared.

Gerb calls the 2022 frames, allegedly leaked from a [[US Navy Blue Angels]] cockpit recording, possibly the most crucial corroboration of all. Like the Flyby clip, they are a second-generation recording (the mouse cursor on screen gives it away). He steps through them: in three frames the pod descends rapidly in a horizontal orientation, its transparent face turned away from its path, with four other signatures alongside; in another, its back faces the ground and the transparent face the sky. If the leak is genuine, he says, the Metapod shape is a legitimate form of UAP, though he is still trying to get to the bottom of it. Some compare the Metapod to the Navy's Gimbal video, and the outline does match, but Gerb holds back: a FLIR image does not necessarily show an object's true shape, so he keeps the comparison in mind without claiming it as fact.

Last is a hang glider filmed over Texas in 2023, apparently observing the pod. Its early and late frames show the vertical pod accurately, but the front-facing view does not match the Metapod in the other recordings, and Gerb judges this clip the one most likely to be something prosaic or misidentified, such as a balloon.

## Gerb's Assessment

Gerb acknowledges the jokes: the pod has been compared to the "show me what you got" heads from *Rick and Morty*. He still finds that the craft "looks like the real deal". What strikes him most is that it resembles a single-occupant observation craft, with a window of what seems to be glass, and he says he does not know where to begin with the implications of the craft and of whatever pilots it, especially given its unobservable means of propulsion.

He also corrects the record on where the clip came from. When the footage first surfaced, accounts on Reddit and other platforms re-uploaded it with the claim that it was filmed in Denver, his own city. Gerb calls this a fallacy of unknown origin: the footage comes from Spain. He jokes about spending a 15 to 20 minute video on a clip of a minute or two, but says he won't settle for less than a full analysis.

The video ends with a note on timing. Recording on what he believes is 11 February 2024, Gerb says the [[Sol Foundation]] has just released a set of interviews and panels with [[David Grusch]], [[Chris Mellon]], [[Kevin Knuth]] and [[Hal Puthoff]], which he plans to review in his next video (see [[Sol Foundation Interview Release]]).

## Key Claims

- The Metapod footage was allegedly filmed near El Escorial, Madrid, Spain, on 6 September 2015, in the direction of Las Navas del Marqués. Gerb calls it, if genuine, one of the best close-up views of a UFO ever recorded.
- A witness signing himself Modesto emailed the clip to the YouTube channel UFO VNI on 7 September 2015, withholding the exact location and asking for anonymity. Gerb notes that the email calls the friend both "Mo" and "Modesto", perhaps a slip in the use of pseudonyms.
- According to the email, the object made no noise, descended vertically and moved away, and the writer's sharp-eyed friend saw something alive with limbs moving inside it. The friend reportedly said it was not the first time he had seen the object, and that his late father had seen it too.
- The clip was discussed on a Portuguese forum on 8 September 2015 and posted by Third Phase of the Sun on 11 September. UFO VNI's original and a 2017 re-upload by UFO Invasions have both been taken down, so Gerb works from ITBS's stabilized version.
- The craft has a solid, metallic, reflective back and a transparent, glass-like front face. Gerb attributes the "bean" some viewers see inside it to sunlight reflecting off that face.
- In Gerb's reading the Metapod shows three of AATIP's five UAP observables (instantaneous acceleration, anti-gravity or no visible propulsion, and low observability), along with intelligent flight characteristics, including a rotation that reverses from counterclockwise to clockwise.
- Gerb rejects the balloon, string and CGI explanations. He notes that balloon proponents on Metabunk themselves say the jitter cited as evidence of CGI is consistent with a physical object in air currents.
- Gerb argues that the same craft appears in footage from Peru (1998), Monterrey, Mexico (2006), Vancouver (2021), an alleged 2022 US Navy Blue Angels cockpit leak and Texas (2023). He considers the Blue Angels frames potentially the most important, and the Texas footage the likeliest to be prosaic.
- Gerb hypothesizes that the Metapod is a single-occupant observation craft, and states that the footage comes from Spain, not Denver as some re-uploads claimed.
