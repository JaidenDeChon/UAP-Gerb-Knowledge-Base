## Summary

The video, hosted by YouTube creator UAP Gerb, provides a comprehensive historical survey of Unidentified Submerged Object (USO) sightings spanning from Christopher Columbus's era through the late 20th century, drawing on maritime logs, military records, and Project Blue Book files. Key cases examined include the 1879 HMS Vulture sighting of two counter-rotating luminous wheel-shaped objects in the Persian Gulf, the 1887 SS Siberian fireball emergence off Newfoundland, the 1902 Fort Salisbury encounter with a ~200-meter cigar-shaped object submerging in the Gulf of Guinea, and multiple mid-20th century cases where Blue Book explanations — such as meteor, flare, and satellite decay — are argued to be inadequate. The video also highlights that of 258 ship-based Blue Book sightings, only a small number qualified as credible USO reports, a gap the creator attributes to suppressive reporting directives OPNAV 3820 and JANAP 146C. UAP Gerb concludes that the volume and consistency of credible historical USO reports, particularly the recurring "maritime light wheel" phenomenon across the Eastern Hemisphere, suggests the oceans may be central to understanding the broader UAP phenomenon.

## Concepts
- [[USO (Unidentified Submerged Object)]] — Unidentified objects observed entering, exiting, or operating underwater; a subset of UAP phenomena.
- [[Trans Medium Object]] — Objects capable of moving through multiple mediums such as air and water, transitioning between them.
- [[OPNAV Directive 3820]] — A 1952 US Navy directive restricting the sharing of Navy UFO sightings to only specific authorized intelligence entities.
- [[Blue Book Unidentified Classification]] — A designation within Project Blue Book for cases that could not be explained by conventional or prosaic explanations.
- [[Maritime UFO and USO Research]] — A field of UAP study focused on sightings occurring at sea, including both aerial and submerged unidentified objects.
- [[Maritime Light Wheel]] — A recurring phenomenon observed by sailors in the Eastern Hemisphere featuring wheel-shaped structures with spokes of brilliant white light appearing below or above the water surface.
- [[Non-interaction with fluid]] — A reported characteristic of some USOs where they submerge without disturbing the water surface, suggesting unconventional interaction with physical matter.
- [[Bioluminescent algae pressure wave hypothesis]] — A proposed natural explanation for maritime light wheel phenomena involving the interaction of bioluminescent algae with pressure waves in calm water, though lacking scientific consensus.
- [[Ball lightning]] — A rare electrical phenomenon sometimes cited to explain USO fireballs, though no documented cases of ball lightning exiting the sea are noted.
- [[Transmedium craft]] — A craft capable of operating across multiple physical mediums such as air, water, and potentially space.
- [[Maritime Light Wheels]] — Historical reports of large, spoke-like rotating wheels of light observed on or beneath ocean surfaces, documented across multiple centuries and locations.
- [[Trans-Medium Objects]] — Craft or phenomena capable of operating both in the air and underwater without apparent transition effects.
- [[Luminous WavesPulsations]] — Reported underwater light movements described as moving at great speed beneath naval vessels in the 19th century.
