## Summary

This video features an in-depth interview with UAP researcher Ryan S. Wood examining the authenticity and contents of the Majestic 12 (MJ-12) documents, particularly the MJ-12 Special Operations Manual (SOM 1-01), which was anonymously mailed to researcher Don Berliner in 1994 on undeveloped 35mm film authenticated to 1954 and classified "Top Secret MAGIC Eyes Only." Document authenticator Robert Wood details his forensic analysis — including typographic artifacts such as raised-Z hot lead press characters, period-accurate abbreviations, and formatting conventions — which led him to conclude the CIA likely printed the manual, with document control traced to Kirtland Air Force Base, New Mexico. The manual itself describes MJ-12 as a covert group established September 24, 1947, responsible for crash retrieval operations, and contains detailed classifications of extraterrestrial craft shapes, two types of extraterrestrial biological entities, a disposition table routing intact craft to Area 51 S4 and deceased entities to the Wright Patterson Blue Room, and a public disclosure contingency plan also referenced in the Eisenhower Briefing Document. The video also addresses skeptical claims — notably Colonel John B. Alexander's assertion that SOM 1-01 was a Soviet disinformation operation — which Ryan Wood rebuts by citing corroborating witnesses such as Navy yeoman Dale Bailey, investigative identification of document control signatories near Kirtland AFB, and cross-referencing with separately FOIA-obtained documents that precisely match the manual's table of contents.
