## Summary

This video examines alleged U.S. Navy involvement in UAP crash retrieval and reverse engineering programs, focusing on the classified National Underwater Reconnaissance Program (NURO) and its connections to senior intelligence officials. Central to the video is Admiral Bobby Ray Inman, who allegedly confirmed in a 1989 recorded phone call with NASA mission specialist Bob Echler that the U.S. government possessed recovered UAP hardware in operational condition, directing Echler to CIA DS&T Deputy Director Everett Heinman for further inquiry; former Director of Naval Intelligence Sumar Shapiro separately told Echler that factions within the U.S. possessed extraterrestrial vehicles he had personally studied. The video also details an alleged 1991 incident in which a U.S. Navy DSRV crew recovered a triangular, 68–70-foot craft with no seams, rivets, or windows—bearing hieroglyphic-style geometric markings—from nearly 1.5 miles underwater approximately 250 nautical miles off Aberdeen, Scotland, with a marine archaeologist estimating the craft had rested there 30 to 40 years. Additional claims include the existence of a classified Navy program called Sand Dollar focused on seafloor UAP retrieval, the use of EMP generators to down UAPs for recovery, and testimony from former Navy Director of Science and Technology Development Nat Kobitz, who told journalist Ross Coulthart before his death that he had been read into an active Navy UAP crash retrieval and reverse engineering program.
