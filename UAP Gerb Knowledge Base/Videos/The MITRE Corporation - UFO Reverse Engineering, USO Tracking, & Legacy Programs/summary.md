## Overview

This video presents UAP Gerb's investigation into the [[MITRE Corporation]], a nonprofit [[Federally Funded Research and Development Center (FFRDC)|FFRDC]] founded in 1958 and spun off from [[MIT Lincoln Laboratory]], which the presenter identifies as the "apex" science and technology institution within the alleged UFO legacy program architecture. The video's central thesis, developed over roughly two years of research and testimony from several MITRE engineers and program managers, is that MITRE has served since its founding as R&D lead and program manager for two distinct, Navy-administered special access programs: a decades-old technology reverse-engineering effort tracing to material recovered at the 1947 [[Roswell Crash]], and a separate, similarly old program monitoring an enormous unidentified submerged object in the Atlantic Ocean nicknamed the "[[Tic Tac Factory]]." The video opens with an extended primer on the FFRDC model itself — describing FFRDCs as government-owned, contractor-operated institutions trusted with more sensitive materials and information than ordinary defense contractors while remaining outside standard federal oversight and FOIA obligations — before applying that framework specifically to MITRE.

The presenter traces MITRE's founding to Project SAGE, the United States' first computer-based national air defense system, while arguing that MITRE's creation roughly ten years after Roswell was not coincidental: that MITRE's founding scientific personnel, many with Naval Special Projects backgrounds including first technical director [[Robert R. Everett]], were tasked from 1958 with reverse engineering exploitable technology from Roswell wreckage held at [[Wright-Patterson Air Force Base]]. This reverse-engineering program is alleged to be administered by the U.S. Navy — via the [[Office of Naval Intelligence]] and [[Naval Air Systems Command]] — rather than the Air Force that formally chartered MITRE, with [[Naval Air Station Patuxent River]] as its operational nexus. The video separately details the "Tic Tac Factory" USO program, an enormous object alleged to be stationed near [[Bahamas|the Bahamas]] that deploys and recovers smaller transmedium craft, corroborated in part by an anonymous 2023 4chan testimony and Commander [[David Fravor]]'s 2004 USS Nimitz encounter.

A major secondary thread concerns MITRE's internal security architecture, which a source described to the presenter as the literal origin of the "onion" model of nested secrecy used throughout the wider [[Legacy Program Onion Model|legacy program]] apparatus. The video details an alleged "hidden" MITRE Special Security Office using nonofficial-cover personnel, a 2011 CIA-driven clampdown on historical program briefings, and an alleged 2017 loss of MITRE's control over the reverse-engineering program coinciding with [[James Clapper]]'s tenure as Director of National Intelligence. The video closes by cataloguing a pattern of senior former intelligence and acquisition officials — including [[Donald Kerr]], [[Susan Gordon]], [[Don Meyer]], [[Paul Kaminski]], [[John Deutsch|John M. Deutsch]], [[Linton Wells II]], and [[William LaPlante]] — who held senior MITRE board or leadership positions, and ties the entire investigation to a 2025 congressional legal interrogative letter sent to MITRE by Representative [[Eric Burlison]] and whistleblower [[David Grusch]].

## FFRDCs as the "Missing Link" in Legacy Programs

The presenter opens with an extended argument that federally funded research and development centers are the structurally ideal — and historically overlooked — vehicle for classified UFO technology exploitation. See [[Federally Funded Research and Development Center (FFRDC)]] for full detail. In brief: when a Navy sponsor determines that a recovered vehicle's systems are exploitable, transferring the material directly to a private contractor such as Lockheed Martin would require ceding physical custody to a comparatively insecure environment. FFRDCs resolve this tension by being technically outside government while remaining under such strict programmatic control — rooted, for DOE FFRDCs, in the [[Atomic Energy Act of 1954]] — that materials, technology, and even the true origin of derived capabilities can be kept almost entirely in-house. Because FFRDCs are only semi-government-operated, they also fall outside ordinary FOIA obligations, making funding footprints easier to obscure. The presenter credits [[Vannevar Bush]] as the "grandfather" of the entire FFRDC/GOCO model and, by direct institutional lineage through MIT Lincoln Laboratory, as MITRE's own grandfather.

## MITRE's Founding and Modern Structure

MITRE was spun off from MIT Lincoln Laboratory in 1958 at Air Force request to provide systems-engineering leadership for [[Semi-Automatic Ground Environment (SAGE)|Project SAGE]], modeled directly on the [[RAND Corporation]]. Sixth Secretary of Defense [[Neil McElroy]] personally asked MITRE's founders to build institutional "flexibility" into the new organization. From its earliest years MITRE was caught in a roles-and-missions power struggle among the Army, Navy, and Air Force, a dynamic the video argues is foundational to MITRE's later legacy program compartmentation culture. The modern MITRE Corporation operates six FFRDCs — most relevantly the DoD-sponsored National Security Engineering Center (NSEC) at its [[McLean, Virginia]] campus, administered through the [[Air Force Life Cycle Management Center (AFLCMC)|Air Force Life Cycle Management Center]] under Lieutenant General [[Shipton|Donna D. Shipton]] — plus 15 internal "MITRE Labs" innovation centers. MITRE's Virginia presence sits immediately beside [[Crystal City, Virginia]], which the presenter describes as "the beating heart of UFO legacy programs in the east of the United States" given its concentration of major defense contractors and additional FFRDCs including the [[Aerospace Corporation]].

## Program One: Roswell-Derived Reverse Engineering

The presenter alleges MITRE's founding scientific mandate secretly extended beyond SAGE to reverse-engineering "war-altering" capabilities from Roswell wreckage, drawing a direct parallel to [[David Grusch]]'s public description of the wider reverse-engineering effort as a program that has "inspired other innovations" without directly copying recovered technology, and to Lieutenant Colonel [[Philip J. Corso]]'s account of a parallel, more aggressive Army technology-seeding effort under General [[Arthur Trudeau]]'s [[U.S. Army Foreign Technology Division]] beginning the same year. Since 1958 this program has allegedly been administered by the Navy rather than the Air Force, with [[Office of Naval Intelligence|ONI]] and [[Naval Air Systems Command|NAVAIR]] as primary mission partners, counterintelligence support from the [[Naval Criminal Investigative Service]] and [[Air Force Office of Special Investigations]] (historically under [[Terry Phillips]]), and [[Naval Air Station Patuxent River]] as the operational nexus, supported by [[Naval Surface Warfare Center Dahlgren]], [[Naval Surface Warfare Center Crane]], and [[Naval Undersea Warfare Center Keyport]]. Alleged contractors include [[Raytheon]], L3Harris, and [[Lockheed Martin]].

## Program Two: The Tic Tac Factory

The video's second program is a decades-old USO monitoring effort focused on an enormous, roughly ten-city-block object stationed in the Atlantic near the [[Bahamas]] and the [[Atlantic Undersea Test and Evaluation Center]], alleged to deploy and recover smaller transmedium subordinate craft. ## MITRE's Security Architecture and the 2011–2017 Transition

MITRE's alleged legacy program security runs through a "hidden" Special Security Office distinct from its acknowledged SSO, using nonofficial-cover personnel and a "lollipop manufacturers" cover story for auditors — an apparatus a source described to the presenter as the literal origin of the [[Legacy Program Onion Model]]. The video alleges a 2011 clampdown on historical program briefings, coinciding with the introduction of CIA partners, followed by an alleged 2017 loss of MITRE's control over the reverse-engineering program coinciding with [[James Clapper]]'s DNI tenure and possibly connected to the 2017 alleged program [[Immaculate Constellation (IMCON)]].

## Key Claims

- MITRE's 1958 founding, publicly attributed solely to Project SAGE, secretly included a Navy-administered mandate to reverse-engineer technology recovered from the 1947 Roswell crash, according to unnamed MITRE engineers and program managers the presenter states it spent roughly two years vetting.

- MITRE is separately alleged to be R&D lead on a decades-old Navy program monitoring an enormous USO near the Bahamas, nicknamed the "Tic Tac Factory," which deploys and recovers smaller transmedium subordinate craft.
- MITRE's legacy program security is alleged to run through an unacknowledged "hidden" Special Security Office at its McLean, Virginia campus, using nonofficial-cover personnel and funding skimmed from acknowledged MITRE and Navy projects.

- Representative Eric Burlison and David Grusch sent a detailed, ten-plus-page legal interrogative letter to MITRE senior vice president Christopher Land in 2025, establishing a records preservation obligation and demanding an accounting of MITRE's alleged legacy program involvement.
- The presenter alleges MITRE has periodically destroyed legacy program records at the Fairfax County, Virginia Waste Management Depot, including around 2011 and 2017.
