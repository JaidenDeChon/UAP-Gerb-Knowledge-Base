## Overview

This video presents the firsthand testimony of an anonymous US Army Green Beret identified as "[[RA]]," who claims that between 2013 and 2015 he was escorted into a classified underground facility at [[Naval Surface Warfare Center Crane]] in Indiana explicitly labeled the "[[Off-World Technologies Division]]." There, RA states he observed two objects described as non-human technology: a seamless, levitating metallic sphere that interacted selectively with human consciousness, and a gauntlet-like device identified as a weapon system that had discharged an energy pulse during recovery. RA holds an active TS/SCI clearance and continues performing opposing force training at [[Area 51]], lending institutional credibility to his account.

The video contextualizes RA's testimony within a broader investigative framework. [[UAP Gerb]] verifies RA's military credentials through his DD214 and service records, then layers the testimony against NSWC Crane's documented history of reverse engineering unknown technology, [[SAIC]]'s massive and partially concealed defense contracts with the facility, and historical precedents including [[Philip J. Corso]]'s Roswell reverse engineering claims. A second witness — a figure named [[Martin]] who participated in remote viewing programs — independently describes a nearly identical consciousness-interactive sphere, presented by scientists in a briefcase. Attorney [[Daniel Sheehan]]'s 1977 account of classified [[Project Blue Book]] photographs provides an additional corroborating thread: the symbols RA saw projected by the gauntlet device closely match symbols Sheehan described from a crashed saucer in Blue Book files, which he reviewed under orders from President [[Jimmy Carter]].

The host also examines [[SAIC]] as the probable private-sector conduit for the Off-World Technologies Division, citing former SAIC employee [[Denise McKenzie]]'s 2001 testimony at [[Steven Greer]]'s Disclosure Project briefing that black budget money is systematically hidden within legitimate SAIC government contracts. Multiple SAIC contract announcements for Crane — totaling hundreds of millions of dollars — have since been removed from the company's public website, a detail the video presents as evidence of deliberate concealment.

## Witness: RA and the Off-World Technologies Division

### Credentials and Placement

RA served in the [[First Special Forces Airborne Command]] as an 18 Bravo weapons sergeant, one of the most highly specialized combat roles in the US Army. His credentials — including SERE Level C training completion, a 12-year active TS/SCI clearance, and full DD214 documentation — were independently verified by UAP Gerb. Between 2013 and 2015, RA attended a TS/SCI-level weapons training course at NSWC Crane covering new minigun systems, SCAR rifle iterations, and foreign adversarial weapons delivered to Crane via CIA logistics channels.

Out of approximately 10 soldiers in the training class, only RA and one other soldier held TS/SCI clearance. These two were separated from their class and escorted deep underground through multiple security checkpoints after surrendering all electronics. The elevator descent brought them to a noticeably colder environment that RA described as strikingly modern and advanced in contrast to the deliberately deteriorated surface buildings — "like a spaceship" compared to the 1970s-era structures above.

### The Technology

A private contractor guide — likely an SAIC employee based on contractor demographics at Crane — casually referred to the area as the "off-world technologies division," a phrase that induced immediate ontological shock in both soldiers. In a viewing room setup apparently designed for observation access, two pieces of alleged non-human technology were displayed:

**The Seamless Metallic Sphere**: A basketball-sized object with no visible joints, welds, or manufacturing marks. Its surface appeared almost liquid yet clearly metallic, exhibiting what RA compared to the "mother of pearl effect" described by [[Jonathan Wagant]] from the [[1997 Peru UFO Crash]]. The sphere appeared to float or be repelled off its podium by an unknown force, and featured an indentation on top roughly the size of a hand. Staff informed RA that both objects interacted with human consciousness — responding to some individuals and not others, possibly based on DNA or consciousness type.

**The Gauntlet/Weapon System**: A device resembling a gauntlet or arm-worn tablet, identified as a weapon system because it had discharged an energy pulse during recovery, injuring at least one person. Most significantly, the device projected hieroglyphic-like symbols with no visible projection mechanism — causing RA to speculate the characters were being displayed directly into his mind. These symbols closely matched those described by [[Daniel Sheehan]] from classified Blue Book photographs.

RA's normally photographic military memory becomes "real fuzzy" specifically when attempting to recall the underground facility — an effect he considers unnatural and possibly induced by the objects or external means. Researcher [[Gary Nolan]] has documented similar cognitive effects in individuals exposed to UAP phenomena.

### Post-Encounter and Current Status

After leaving the facility, both soldiers experienced severe ontological shock, repeatedly questioning reality. RA lost contact with his fellow witness, who was assigned to a different unit; he later heard unconfirmed rumors the soldier was killed in a combat operation. RA signed what were likely NDAs, though the exact moment is unclear given routine military NDA signing. He continues performing OP4 opposing force training for special forces at Area 51 under DoD and Department of Energy contracts, and deliberately chose not to contact AARO, [[Luis Elizondo]], [[David Grusch]], or [[Chris Mellon]] after his experience.

## Corroborating Accounts

### Daniel Sheehan and Project Blue Book

In 1977, attorney [[Daniel Sheehan]] was tasked by President [[Jimmy Carter]] to review classified sections of [[Project Blue Book]] at the Library of Congress. Sheehan claims to have discovered five or six photographs of a downed saucer-shaped craft in a snowy field, with symbols around the base of its dome. When UAP Gerb showed RA a reference to these symbols, RA identified them as closely matching the hieroglyphics projected by the gauntlet device at the Off-World Technologies Division. The video suggests the 1952 Spitsbergen, Norway disc crash — a 47-meter disc that attracted CIA interest — as a possible candidate for the snowy recovery event depicted in the photographs.

### Martin's Consciousness-Interactive Sphere

A witness named [[Martin]] who came forward through researcher Courtney claims to have participated in remote viewing programs during which eight scientists presented a basketball-sized transparent sphere in a briefcase. Martin states the sphere "chose" who it interacted with — a description functionally identical to RA's account of the NSWC Crane sphere. UAP Gerb was actively communicating with Martin to investigate potential connections to the Off-World Technologies Division.

### SAIC's Consciousness Research

SAIC conducted extensive research into anomalous cognition (extrasensory perception) and anomalous perturbation (psychokinesis) through its Cognitive Science Laboratory, including evaluating the CIA's Stargate Program in 1995. A 2012 Office of Naval Research paper discussed "human sixth sense" research. These documented institutional interests align precisely with the consciousness-interactive properties of the technology RA observed at the Navy's Crane facility.

## NSWC Crane and SAIC: Investigative Analysis

### Crane's Documented Reverse Engineering Capability

NSWC Crane has a publicly acknowledged history of reverse engineering unknown technology. Electromagnetic warfare experts at Crane successfully reverse engineered an unknown component of the AN/SLQ-32 (Slick 32) shipboard electronic warfare system — a device initially described as unknown in construction, function, and repair. This established the facility's institutional capability for analyzing technology whose origins and operating principles are initially incomprehensible.

Crane receives foreign weapon systems captured by CIA operatives via the same logistics channels RA describes for technology deliveries. The DoD maintains guidebooks for "situations such as this" — explicitly referencing guidance for reverse engineering unknown recovered technology.
