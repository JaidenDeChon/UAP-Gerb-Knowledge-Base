Jose Ramon Zarazua Ingore (also spelled ZaZa Ngore) was the co-pilot of [[TAE (Trabajos Aéreos y Enlaces)|TAE]] flight JK 297 during the [[Manises UFO Incident]] of November 11, 1979. He served alongside Captain [[Javier Leo de Taha]] and flight mechanic [[Francisco Javier Rodriguez]] when the crew encountered two powerful red lights over the [[Mediterranean Sea]] that led to the first recorded emergency landing of a commercial flight due to a UFO.

Zarazua Ingore supported Captain Javier's decision to abort the flight path and perform an emergency landing at [[Manises Airport, Valencia, Spain|Manises Airport]] in Valencia after the unidentified objects matched the aircraft's altitude changes and maintained close proximity despite evasive maneuvers. The incident occurred on a flight carrying 109 passengers from [[Salzburg, Austria|Salzburg]] to [[Las Palmas, Spain|Las Palmas]] with a refueling stop at [[Mallorca, Spain|Mallorca]].
