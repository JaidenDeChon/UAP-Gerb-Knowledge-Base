Mary Evans was an Aurora, Texas resident whose testimony was gathered during the 1973 [[MUFON]] investigation of the [[Aurora Texas UFO Crash]] led by [[Bill Case]]. Evans told investigators that her parents, who had refused to allow her to visit the crash site at the time of the alleged April 1897 incident, had gone there themselves and personally discovered an alien body. Her account represents one of the few second-generation eyewitness testimonies to the Aurora crash with specific content about what was found at the scene.
