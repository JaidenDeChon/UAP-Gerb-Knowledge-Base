Modesto is the pseudonymous Spanish witness who filmed and submitted the "Metapod" UAP footage to YouTube channel UFO VNI on September 7, 2015, one day after the sighting occurred near [[El Escorial, Madrid, Spain]]. The footage, captured on September 6, 2015, shows a structured crescent-shaped craft with a metallic reflective back and transparent glass-like front face descending vertically and moving silently through the air.

## The Sighting and Submission

Modesto was accompanied by a friend identified as "[[Mo]]" during the observation. In his email to UFO VNI, Modesto described being nearsighted himself but noted that his companion Mo "can see hairs and quailes in the same way as finding a needle in a hay stack." Mo reported observing something with limbs moving inside the craft through the transparent face.

Modesto stated he had spent many years in the countryside and was familiar with the sounds of birds in flight, emphasizing that the observed craft made no noise whatsoever. He described it as descending vertically before moving away and stated unequivocally: "what we saw yesterday I sincerely know was not from here."

## Prior Familiarity

In his correspondence with UFO VNI, Modesto revealed that his friend (also referred to as "Modesta" in what appears to be a pseudonym confusion in the email) was not surprised by the sighting, as it was "not the first time he has seen it" and that his deceased father had also observed the same or similar craft. Modesto characterized his companion as "a little more tack turn and from the town" than himself and noted the companion's cryptic remark "here again" after the sighting.

## Anonymity and Location

Modesto requested strict anonymity from UFO VNI, stating "due to personal reasons and the location of the sighting we do not want to give the exact location." He specified the general area as near El Escorial, direction [[Las Navas Del Marquez]], and noted that "El escorial is a place where strange things happen but not in the city but in the nearby mountains." He offered to provide GPS coordinates privately for investigation purposes only, on the condition they not be made public or associated with the video.

## Post-Release Confusion

Following the video's release, some social media accounts incorrectly claimed the footage was filmed in Denver, Colorado. This misinformation was noted and corrected by UAP analysts confirming the Spanish origin of the footage.
