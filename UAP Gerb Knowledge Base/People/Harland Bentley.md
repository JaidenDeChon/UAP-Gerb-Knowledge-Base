Harland F. Bentley is a UAP whistleblower who claimed to have witnessed a UFO crash and recovery operation in May 1958 near a Nike Ajax missile site outside Washington, D.C. He also claimed to have later overheard communications between Houston Mission Control and the Apollo 8 crew describing an encounter with a saucer-like craft. Bentley stated that he held an electrical engineering degree, with graduate work in nuclear engineering, and that he served in the U.S. Army as a private first class and radar operator. He first appeared publicly at [[Steven Greer]]'s 2001 [[Disclosure Project]] event. Gerb could not verify Bentley's military records or educational credentials, so his claims have to be judged on their own merit.

## 1958 Nike Ajax Missile Site UFO Encounter

Bentley claimed to have been stationed at a Nike Ajax missile facility north of Washington, D.C., near Olney, Maryland, likely either Site W-92 (Gaithersburg) or W-93 (Olney). The Nike Ajax program, led by Bell Labs, deployed surface-to-air missile systems across the U.S. and [[NATO]] during the Cold War to defend against enemy bomber aircraft.

Bentley claimed that at approximately 6:00 a.m. in May 1958 he saw a disc-shaped craft hit the ground in a civilian cornfield roughly 2,000 yards from his position and then immediately take flight again. He described it as roughly 30 feet in diameter, with a cake-like structure on top and circular, ball-like pulsating lights that cycled between orange, red, and white. A crashed craft taking flight again is noted as unusual in crash retrieval accounts. Metallic debris was left at the impact site, including a washing machine-sized piece glowing white hot. Soon afterwards, [[US Air Force]] personnel in radiation protection suits arrived with long poles and a lead-lined truck to collect the material. Bentley did not learn where the debris was taken.

The following evening, a nearby Gaithersburg facility contacted Bentley's site to report 12 to 15 UFOs of varying shapes hovering 50 to 100 feet off the ground. The M-33 radar system at Bentley's facility tracked the objects, and all of the signatures left at the same moment, within a single sweep. A calculation on an analog computer, based on the M-33's scan rate of 33.33 RPM, put their speed at more than 177,000 mph. A presiding general debriefed the men and attributed the contacts to Navy-Army-Air Force maneuver training exercises. Bentley found that explanation implausible, and Gerb found no documentation to support it.

A relevant case occurred in October 1958, fewer than 50 miles from Olney. In the Loch Raven Reservoir incident, documented in [[J. Allen Hynek]]'s *The UFO Experience*, two men's car stalled within 75 feet of a 100-foot egg-shaped UFO hovering over a bridge. They were struck by a beam of white light and were later treated for sunburns. In a separate case at the W-93 Nike Ajax site, on September 29, 1958, Private Jerome Scanland reportedly saw a teardrop-shaped object land approximately 1.5 miles away. Broken branches and scorched earth were found where it landed.

## Apollo 8 Communication Claim

Bentley claimed that while doing classified NASA contract work at a California facility in approximately 1967–1968, he overheard Houston Mission Control communicate with the Apollo 8 crew about a "bogey coming in at 11:00." The crew requested and received permission to perform collision avoidance. The unidentified craft was described as saucer-like and flying parallel to the capsule, with visible portals and movement inside that suggested occupants. The crew reportedly stated "they are now paralleling our course" before the craft departed. A colleague immediately told Bentley: "You did not hear anything."

Gerb also investigated a separate claim about the same mission. A Disclosure Project witness (number 101179), identified as [[Alain Boudier]], claimed to have seen an uncut [[NASA]] film, taken by astronaut [[Frank Borman]] during Apollo 8, that showed six UFOs descending onto the lunar surface. Bentley's account and Boudier's claim are treated as parallel threads, both unverified and not connected to each other.
