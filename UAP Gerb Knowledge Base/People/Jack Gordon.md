Jack Gordon was a senior executive at [[Lockheed Martin Skunk Works]] who left the Palmdale facility in the late 1990s. He is known mainly for an encounter with author [[Nick Cook]], who saw Gordon become visibly flustered when asked about an entry labeled "Astra" on a chart of Skunk Works aircraft. The chart listed Astra, a triangular craft, after the Darkstar reconnaissance aircraft.

## Encounter with Nick Cook

Nick Cook, author of *The Hunt for Zero Point*, was visiting Lockheed's Palmdale facility. As he was leaving with Gordon, he noticed a large chart on the wall showing the full lineage of every Skunk Works aircraft since the XP-80. After the Darkstar, the aircraft featured prominently in the film *Top Gun: Maverick*, the chart listed something called "Astra," shown as an ultra-high-speed reconnaissance craft that appeared triangular. When Cook asked Gordon about it, Gordon became flustered and dismissed it as "an old airliner."

The chart sighting is treated as indirect evidence that [[Aurora Program|Project Astra]] was part of Lockheed's official aircraft lineage but was deliberately kept off public records. Gordon's visible discomfort at the question is taken as a sign that the subject was sensitive, and not just obscure.
