"Ed Doll" is the informal name for [[Edward Bushnell Doll]], a physicist and the project director for [[Operation Upshot-Knothole]] at the [[Nevada National Security Site (Nevada Test Site)|Nevada Proving Ground]] in 1953. In [[Arthur Stansel Jr.]]'s account of the [[1953 Kingman, Arizona Crash Retrieval]], Doll is the man who telephoned Stansel on the evening of May 20, 1953, to tell him about a special assignment for the next day.

His full biography is on the [[Edward Bushnell Doll]] page.
