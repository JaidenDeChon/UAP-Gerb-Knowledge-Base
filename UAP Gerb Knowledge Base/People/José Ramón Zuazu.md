José Ramón Zuazu was the co-pilot of [[TAE (Trabajos Aéreos y Enlaces)|TAE]] flight JK 297, the Super Caravelle that diverted to [[Manises Airport, Valencia, Spain|Manises Airport]] in Valencia on 11 November 1979 after two red lights closed on it over the Mediterranean, in the [[Manises UFO Incident]]. In Gerb's video the transcript renders his name "Jose Ramon ZaZa Ngore".

## The Manises incident

Zuazu sat beside Captain [[Francisco Javier Lerdo de Tejada]], with flight mechanic [[Francisco Javier Rodriguez]] behind them, on the leg from [[Mallorca, Spain|Mallorca]] toward [[Las Palmas, Spain|Las Palmas]] with 109 passengers aboard. After Rodriguez spotted the lights at about 23:05, the crew watched them mirror the aircraft's change of altitude and stay between half a mile and five miles away, and the captain chose to land at Manises. Gerb points out that Zuazu and Rodriguez backed that decision, which is why he rejects the later suggestion that the captain simply suffered an anxiety attack: the whole cockpit would have had to share it.
