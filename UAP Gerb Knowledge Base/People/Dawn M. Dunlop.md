Dawn M. Dunlop is a retired U.S. Air Force major general and test pilot who served from 1988 to 2021. She was among the first American women selected to fly combat aircraft, was the first woman to fly the F-22, and was the first woman to command an Air Force test wing. She commanded the [[412th Test Wing]] at [[Edwards Air Force Base]] from August 2009 to March 2012, after serving as its vice commander. She later commanded the NATO Airborne Early Warning and Control Force (2015-2018) and directed the Department of Defense Special Access Program Central Office (SAPCO) in 2018-2019. She left SAPCO in 2019 amid inspector general investigations into complaints of a toxic work environment. Her final post was Director of Operational Capability Requirements in the Deputy Chief of Staff for Strategy, Integration and Requirements at Headquarters Air Force, and she retired in November 2021.
