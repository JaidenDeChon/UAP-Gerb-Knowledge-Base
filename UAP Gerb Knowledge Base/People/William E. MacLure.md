William E. MacLure is a member of the Senior Executive Service who served as Director of Security, Special Program Oversight and Information Protection ([[SAFAAZ|SAF/AAZ]]) in the Office of the Administrative Assistant to the Secretary of the Air Force at the Pentagon. The directorate is the Air Force's Special Access Program Central Office and the home of its insider-threat program. In that post he provided security leadership, policy direction, integration and oversight for the Air Force special access program and information protection enterprise, and was functional manager of a security community of more than 2,000 people. UAP Gerb's *The Hidden Wing* spells his name "McClure", as does his LinkedIn profile ("Bill McClure") according to Gerb.

## Legacy activity architecture

UAP Gerb's *Special Access Required Vol.2* names MacLure among a small group of Senior Executive Service officials, with [[Randall G. Walden]], [[Terry Phillips]] and [[Lee M. Russ]], whom Gerb alleges "operate key positions in legacy activity architecture." That video points to a tension in the role. SAF/AAZ is nominally the Air Force component Special Access Program Central Office (SAPCO), with oversight of Air Force special access programs. Yet Gerb alleges that the Air Force's UFO-related "outside activities", set up under [[Edward C. Aldridge]], were historically routed directly under SAF/AA in a way that bypassed SAF/AAZ's own oversight. On this view, whether a given SAF/AAZ director is read into legacy activities depends on that director and their personal history in the programs.

## Alleged gatekeeper candidate

The video dates MacLure's tenure at SAF/AAZ to 2019-2021 and describes him as an SES tier 2 official. Gerb proposes him as the alternative to [[Russell E. Wiler]] for the redacted "SES2 who's the Air Force gatekeeper" in the 2020 Signal exchange that [[Chris Mellon]] released on 23 April 2024. Researcher Alex Catz had derived a 12-character name from the redaction. Gerb concedes that "William McClure" (14) and "Will McClure" (11) miss that count, but suggests an error margin of about two characters and notes that "Bill E. McClure" gives exactly 12. He leans roughly 60-40 toward Wiler, but says both men have been involved in Air Force legacy programs. He urges the administration to serve MacLure with interrogatories or a subpoena and lists him among five "hostile witnesses", with [[Terry Phillips]], [[Lee M. Russ]], Wiler and [[Randall G. Walden]].
