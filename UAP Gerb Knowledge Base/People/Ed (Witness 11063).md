"Ed" is the name UAP Gerb gives to witness 11063, a redacted entry on the witness list that [[Steven Greer]]'s [[Disclosure Project]] published in 2024. According to Greer's files, Ed is a retired US Air Force officer who served at [[Nellis Air Force Base]], [[Hanscom Air Force Base, Massachusetts|Hanscom]], [[Edwards Air Force Base]] and the [[Pentagon]], and who claims to have tested craft reverse-engineered from non-human technology as a test director in an electronic warfare group of the Edwards [[412th Test Wing]]. He is one of the most frequently cited witnesses on the UAP Gerb channel, where his account underpins Gerb's theory of the [[Hidden Wing]].

UAP Gerb says he has worked out Ed's identity and confirmed his postings at Nellis, Edwards and the Pentagon from public sources, but withholds his name for Ed's operational security and his family's safety.

## The Disclosure Project documents

Greer's witness list describes 11063 as a career Air Force officer at Nellis and Edwards, test director of an electronic warfare group who tested reverse-engineered technology, with Pentagon and Department of Energy service, a wing commander at Edwards, who contacted the Disclosure Project in 2023. In late summer 2024 the archive added Module 3, "Covert Government Retrieval and Handling ET Vehicles", which describes a lieutenant colonel who managed and trained pilots of man-made UFOs at Edwards. It says Greer introduced him to the Senate intelligence and armed services committees, and that he is ready to testify under oath in an open hearing. Greer then uploaded two documents under his number:

- an internal Disclosure Project email giving Ed's service as 1999 to an early retirement in 2015, with three years at Nellis, three at Hanscom, five at Edwards and seven at the Pentagon, and an engineering background. It says Ed agrees with Greer on suppressed free-energy systems and would testify to [[AARO]] only if [[Sean Kirkpatrick]] were removed.
- two pages of notes from Greer's phone call with Ed on 25 November 2023, which break his career down by year and station.

## Alleged career

- **Nellis (1999–2002).** As a first lieutenant, Ed worked with [[Lockheed Martin]] as a range technical contractor, and at [[Building 200]] under the first commander of the range wing that became the [[Nevada Test and Training Range (NTR)|Nevada Test and Training Range]]. He was allegedly briefed at [[Area 51]]'s S4 site, where a major said they had "caught/captured lots of aircraft", by a 30-minute video of essentially invisible "inspection craft". He says he saw man-made craft on the range.
- **Hanscom (2002–2005).** He worked with [[MIT]] on foreign military assets but saw only terrestrial craft.
- **Edwards (2006–2010).** Sent to Edwards for range orientation and the [[Air Force Test Pilot School]], he says pilots there are trained for unacknowledged special access programs using reverse-engineered non-human technology. He headed an electronic warfare group of the 412th Test Wing, acting as intermediary between the engineers who reported to him and test pilots when "they had a man-craft" to test. He was formally read into several "advanced aircraft programs".
- **The go-public plan (2008–2010).** Greer writes that Ed, as director of a redacted program, was part of an Air Force plan to go public in 2008 that a two-star general killed in 2009–2010.
- **Pentagon (2010–2015).** He worked within a research and development panel chain. Greer's notes record "$34 billion then $40 billion to non-Air Force authorization, non-blue TOA", which UAP Gerb reads as transfers into black programs.

Ed also told Greer that [[Wright-Patterson Air Force Base]] has close ties to Edwards, guessed that its [[Hangar 6]] is significant, and has threatened legal action against Lockheed and [[Boeing]].

## In UAP Gerb's research

In *UAP Reverse Engineering at Edwards Air Force Base*, UAP Gerb concludes from Ed's account that a joint reverse-engineering program for craft of non-human origin, centred on [[Alien Reproduction Vehicle (ARV)|alien reproduction vehicles]], runs between Edwards and Nellis, Area 51 and S4 out of the 412th Test Wing. In *The Hidden Wing* he says his confidence in Ed has grown after seeing his records, including letters of commendation signed by [[Dawn M. Dunlop]], and relays Ed's claim that the program answers to [[Air Force Test and Evaluation (AF-TE)]]. Later videos return to him on the Department of Energy's role, on [[Project Redlight]], and on ARVs such as those described by [[Mark McCandlish]] and [[Edgar Fouche|Edgar Fouché]].

By the time of the Edwards video, Gerb says, Ed had gone "off books" and could not be reached, and he asked whether Greer's claim that Ed had testified might be an embellishment.
