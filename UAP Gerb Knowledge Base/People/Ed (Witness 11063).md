"Ed" is the name Gerb gives to witness 11063, a redacted entry on the witness list that [[Steven Greer]]'s [[Disclosure Project]] published in 2024. According to Greer's files, Ed is a retired US Air Force officer who served at [[Nellis Air Force Base]], [[Hanscom Air Force Base, Massachusetts|Hanscom]], [[Edwards Air Force Base]] and the [[Pentagon]], and who claims to have tested craft reverse-engineered from non-human technology as a test director in an electronic warfare group of the Edwards [[412th Test Wing]]. He is one of the most frequently cited witnesses on the UAP Gerb channel, where his account underpins Gerb's theory of the [[Hidden Wing]].

Gerb says he has worked out Ed's identity and confirmed his postings at Nellis, Edwards and the Pentagon from public sources. He does not publish the name, to protect Ed's operational security and his family's safety.

## The Disclosure Project documents

Greer's witness list describes 11063 as a career Air Force officer at Nellis and Edwards who was test director of an electronic warfare group and tested reverse-engineered technology. The entry also lists service at the Pentagon and the Department of Energy and a post as a wing commander at Edwards, and says he contacted the Disclosure Project in 2023. In late summer 2024 the archive added Module 3, "Covert Government Retrieval and Handling ET Vehicles", which describes a lieutenant colonel who managed and trained pilots of man-made UFOs at Edwards. It says Greer introduced him to the Senate intelligence and armed services committees, that he is eager to testify openly before the House Oversight Committee, and that he left the Air Force in 2009, a date that differs from the 2015 retirement in Greer's other documents. Greer then uploaded two documents under his number:

- an internal Disclosure Project email giving Ed's service as 1999 to an early retirement in 2015, with three years at Nellis, three at Hanscom, five at Edwards and seven at the Pentagon, and an engineering background. It says Ed agrees with Greer on suppressed free-energy systems and would testify to [[AARO]] only if [[Sean Kirkpatrick]] were removed.
- two pages of notes from Greer's phone call with Ed on 25 November 2023, which break his career down by year and station.

## Alleged career

- At Nellis (1999–2002), Ed was a first lieutenant, read into UAP programs there. He worked with [[Lockheed Martin]], a range technical contractor at Nellis, and at [[Building 200]] under the first commander of the range wing that became the [[Nevada Test and Training Range (NTR)|Nevada Test and Training Range]]. He was allegedly shown a 30-minute video of essentially invisible "inspection craft" at a briefing at [[Area 51]]'s S4 site, where a major said they had "caught/captured lots of aircraft". He says he saw man-made craft on the range.
- At Hanscom (2002–2005) he worked with [[MIT]] on foreign military assets but saw only terrestrial craft.
- At Edwards (2006–2010), where he was sent for range orientation and graduated from the [[Air Force Test Pilot School]], he says pilots are trained for unacknowledged special access programs that use reverse-engineered non-human technology. He headed an electronic warfare group of the 412th Test Wing and was the go-between for the engineers who reported to him and the test pilots when "they had a man-craft" to test. He was formally read into several "advanced aircraft programs".
- In 2008–2010, Greer writes, Ed was director of a redacted program and part of an Air Force plan to go public in 2008, which a two-star general killed in 2009–2010.
- At the Pentagon (2010–2015) he worked within a research and development panel chain. Greer's notes record "$34 billion then $40 billion to non-Air Force authorization, non-blue TOA", which Gerb reads as transfers of authority into black programs. In his Dugway video Gerb places the two sums in fiscal years 2013 and 2014, "I think", and in *The Hidden Wing* he describes Ed's Pentagon post as connected to the Air Force acquisition office, [[SAFAQ|SAF/AQ]], where, Ed says, 30 to 40 percent of Air Force funding was pulled into the black budget before the financial management office could review it.

Ed also told Greer that [[Wright-Patterson Air Force Base]] has close ties to Edwards, guessed that its [[Hangar 6]] is significant, and has threatened legal action against Lockheed and [[Boeing]].

## In UAP Gerb's research

In *UAP Reverse Engineering at Edwards Air Force Base*, Gerb concludes from Ed's account that a joint reverse-engineering program for craft of non-human origin, centred on [[Alien Reproduction Vehicle (ARV)|alien reproduction vehicles]], runs between Edwards and Nellis, Area 51 and S4 out of the 412th Test Wing. In *The Hidden Wing* he says his confidence in Ed has "grown 10,000-fold" and that he has held Ed's military records in his own hands. The records name the Air Staff's [[Air Force Test and Evaluation (AF-TE)|test and evaluation directorate (AF/TE)]] as Ed's office of primary responsibility at the 412th, and include letters of commendation signed by [[Dawn M. Dunlop]], who commanded the wing from 2009 to 2012. Ed says the program tested manned and unmanned reverse-engineered vehicles, and that the Department of Energy was "intimately involved with every layer of the program", from the Air Force Test Center to Air Force acquisition. Gerb counts Ed among the whistleblowers he stands with, alongside [[David Grusch]] and [[Dylan Borland]]. Later videos return to him on the Department of Energy's role, on [[Project Redlight]], and on ARVs such as those described by [[Mark McCandlish]] and [[Edgar Fouche|Edgar Fouché]].

By the time of the Edwards video, Gerb says, Ed had gone "off books" and could not be reached. Gerb also asked whether Greer's claim that Ed had testified might be an embellishment.
