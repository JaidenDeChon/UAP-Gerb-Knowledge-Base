Alain Boudier is a French aerospace figure who in May 2008 became the first president of the 3AF-PAN commission, the body set up by the Association Aéronautique et Astronautique de France (3AF) to study unidentified aerospace phenomena (*phénomènes aérospatiaux non identifiés*, PAN); the commission was renamed SIGMA during 2009. He appears in [[Steven Greer]]'s [[Disclosure Project]] archives, where UAP Gerb identified him as the source Greer's notes label "AB" and his emails call "Source One." In the video the name is spoken as "Elaine Boder"; the 3AF's own records give it as Alain Boudier.

## Role in the Greer Network

In Greer's Disclosure Project witness list, a redacted entry numbered 101179 describes an individual in government who claimed to have seen the official, uncut version of a [[NASA]] film showing six UFOs descending onto the lunar surface, allegedly taken by astronaut [[Frank Borman]] during Apollo 8 in 1968. Greer's files show this source travelling from France, to be accompanied by a high-profile French astronaut, with a phone call planned for late February 2009 and a meeting for April 2009.

Greer's handwritten note of 24 February 2009 records the two discussing hoaxes, truth, and (redacted) names of trustworthy and untrustworthy people; NASA comes up only when "AB" asks whether the agency can be trusted, and Greer replies that most NASA employees know nothing. On 28 February 2009 the source forwarded Greer an article from the French magazine *L'Express* whose section "a pilot from NASA" discussed the White House's search for a new NASA administrator, naming [[Charles Bolden]] and his four Space Shuttle flights. In early March 2009 Greer's email chain about getting briefing documents to French President [[Nicholas Sarkozy]] names Boudier ("Source One") as the man carrying them.

Using the context of these emails, UAP Gerb matched the source to Boudier, who had been appointed to lead the 3AF's UAP commission at around that time. UAP Gerb said it had contacted him about the alleged Apollo 8 film and had found no other reference to the film anywhere.
