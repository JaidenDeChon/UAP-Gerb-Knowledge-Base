M. Harmon is a name appearing in an alleged leaked classified UAP document that purportedly represents a section from a longer classified appendix of a UAP Task Force report. The document surfaced online around 2021-2022 and lists Harmon alongside [[K Shakoya]], [[S. McCaron]], and [[Hillary S. McCaron]] as contributors to a "Behavioral Data Analysis" section examining UAP and USO (Unidentified Aerial Submerged Phenomena) characteristics.
