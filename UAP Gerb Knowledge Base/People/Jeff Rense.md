Jeff Rense is an American radio host and media personality known for conducting interviews on fringe and alternative topics including UAP, government conspiracies, and anomalous phenomena. In the UAP research community, Rense is noted for a July 24, 2012 interview with Colonel [[Robert Willingham]] in which Willingham described observing three to four non-human bodies at the 1955 crash site near [[Langtry, Texas]] — directly contradicting Willingham's earlier written account in *The Other Roswell* (2008), in which he specifically stated he never saw bodies.

The Rense interview is cited by UAP Gerb as the most significant credibility issue in Willingham's account, representing a factual contradiction the host declines to attribute to faulty memory.
