Jeff Rense is an American radio host and media personality known for interviews on fringe and alternative topics, including UAP, government conspiracies, and anomalous phenomena. In the UAP research community, Rense is noted for a July 24, 2012 interview with Colonel [[Robert Willingham]]. In it, Willingham described seeing three to four non-human bodies at the 1955 crash site near [[Langtry, Texas]]. That directly contradicts his earlier written account in *The Other Roswell* (2008), in which he specifically stated that he never saw bodies.

Gerb cites the Rense interview as the most significant credibility issue in Willingham's account. He treats it as a factual contradiction and declines to attribute it to faulty memory.
