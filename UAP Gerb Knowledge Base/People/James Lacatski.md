James T. Lacatski is a former Defense Intelligence Agency rocket scientist who co-founded and ran the DIA side of [[AAWSAP]], the Advanced Aerospace Weapon System Applications Program started by Senator [[Harry Reid]] in 2007 and 2008 and contracted to [[Bigelow Aerospace Advanced Space Studies]]. He co-wrote *Skinwalkers at the Pentagon* (2021) with Colm Kelleher and George Knapp, and later books on the program.

## Statements

Lacatski has written that the United States had possession of a craft of unknown origin and had gained access to its interior. By his account, the craft had a streamlined shape suited to aerodynamic flight but no intakes, exhaust, wings or control surfaces. In an interview clip that Gerb plays in his video *Lockheed Martin - UFO Reverse Engineering, Material Exploitation, & Legacy Programs*, Lacatski declines to go beyond what he wrote, says that going to Congress "would be a red line" for him, and answers a question about a subpoena with "let's cross that bridge when we come to it". Gerb cites this among the reasons he doubts subpoenas would get answers from figures such as Lacatski, [[Glenn Gaffney]] or [[Sean Kirkpatrick]].

## In the NASIC video

In [[Videos/NASIC - Air Force UFO Crash Retrieval & Reverse Engineering/summary|NASIC - Air Force UFO Crash Retrieval & Reverse Engineering]], Gerb plays the interview in which Lacatski confirms that he is officially allowed to say the United States had in its possession a craft of unknown origin and had accessed its interior. Gerb places that work within the off-books part of the [[Foreign Material Program (FMP)|Foreign Materiel Program]] under the [[Defense Intelligence Agency]], and cites Lacatski's DIA role against accounts of the legacy programs that leave the DIA out.
