Oak Shannon is a former manager of special projects at [[Los Alamos National Laboratories]] and a former Department of Energy nuclear physicist. He helped facilitate the October 16, 2002 meeting between Vice Admiral [[Thomas Wilson]] and physicist [[Eric Davis]] that is documented in the [[Wilson-Davis Memo]].

## Role in the Wilson-Davis Meeting

According to the Wilson-Davis memo, Shannon asked Wilson to speak to Davis about what Wilson had told Commander [[Will Miller]] about crashed or retrieved UFO craft. His involvement suggests he knew of Wilson's 1997 investigation into crash retrieval programs and had stayed in contact with Wilson after the admiral retired from the DIA.

## Confirmation of Authenticity

Shannon later confirmed the authenticity of the Wilson-Davis memo in an interview with Jay Anderson for the Project Unity podcast. His confirmation carries significant weight for two reasons. He managed special projects at Los Alamos National Laboratories, one of the most sensitive research facilities in the United States, and he was directly involved in setting up the meeting.

## Notes from the 1985 Advanced Theoretical Working Group

Shannon's personal notes from the 1985 Advanced Theoretical Working Group, held at BDM International's McLean secure facility and classified using DOE controls, were published in 2022. Besides their bearing on the Wilson-Davis Memo, the notes contain intelligence about USOs (unidentified submerged objects). Shannon recorded that the US Navy held intelligence on an underwater UFO hotspot near Gulfoss San Matias off the coast of Argentina. The notes also reference UAP hotspots including Mount Hayes, Alaska.

Gerb cites the Argentina underwater hotspot as part of the growing evidence for a classified Navy maritime UAP tracking program. He points to Shannon's connection to highly sensitive government research as a factor that lends weight to the claim.
