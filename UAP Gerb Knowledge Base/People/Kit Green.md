Dr. Christopher "Kit" Green is a former CIA medical and scientific intelligence officer who has been involved in government and private UAP research for decades. Green has worked with multiple research groups investigating the structure of alleged covert UAP programs and the private contractors suspected of involvement in crash retrieval and reverse engineering efforts.

## Aerospace Corporation Identification

Working alongside [[Jacques Vallee]], [[Hal Puthoff]], [[Eric Davis]], and Kristen B. Zimmerman, Green co-authored research identifying [[The Aerospace Corporation]] in [[Long Beach, California]] as one of the suspected legacy private corporations engaged in "real UAP research." This analysis was documented in Vallee's journal compilations published as *Hidden Science 5*, covering the period from the 2000s to 2009.

The research group's identification of Aerospace Corporation aligns with multiple lines of evidence:
- The corporation's documented work with [[Defense Support Program (DSP)]] satellites analyzing [[Fast Walkers]]
- The 2014 DSP fact sheet mentioning Aerospace Corporation's development of "hazard support systems"
- [[Ross Coulthart]]'s reporting on Aerospace Corporation's campus in Long Beach matching descriptions from anonymous sources about facilities with "access to the wreckage"

## Intelligence Background

Green's CIA background in scientific intelligence and his long-standing involvement in UAP research lend significant weight to his assessments of which private corporations are likely involved in classified UAP-related work. His expertise spans both the intelligence community's structure and the scientific/technical aspects of analyzing anomalous phenomena.
