Sergeant Allen is a former United States Marine Corps non-commissioned officer who served with [[Marine Air Control Group 28|Air Control Group 28]] during [[Operation Laser Strike]] in Peru in 1997. Along with [[Sergeant Atkins]], Allen was one of the first Marines to observe a crashed [[Egg-shaped UAP|egg-shaped craft]] embedded in a cliff face in the Peruvian jungle. [[Jonathan Weygandt]] later testified publicly about the incident.

## Role in the Peru Incident

Allen was one of the personnel who told Weygandt about a downed aircraft that needed securing, and he accompanied the vanguard of 8–10 Marines sent to the crash site in Humvees. Allen, Atkins, and Weygandt formed the point team. They reached the ridge and climbed down to observe the craft. Weygandt went closest, while Allen and Atkins stayed at a distance and shouted at him not to get closer. Armed men in all-black fatigues with no insignia then intercepted, disarmed, and detained both sergeants. Allen was separated from Weygandt after the [[Department of Energy]] retrieval team arrived.

## Post-Incident Career

Allen went on to a long and accomplished career in the Marine Corps and retired after reaching a prestigious rank. Gerb identified Allen as a real USMC veteran and wrote to him, along with Montil and Atkins, during his 2025 investigation. Only one of the three replied, and Gerb has kept that Marine anonymous.
