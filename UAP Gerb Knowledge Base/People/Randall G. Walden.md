Randall G. Walden is a member of the Senior Executive Service who serves as Director and Program Executive Officer (PEO) of the Department of the Air Force [[Rapid Capabilities Office (RCO)]]. In this role he oversees the RCO's mission of accelerating the development, acquisition, and fielding of critical Air Force capabilities through streamlined acquisition processes that can bypass standard congressional and service-branch oversight. The RCO reports to a board chaired by the [[Under Secretary of Defense for Acquisition and Sustainment (USD A&S)]], giving it a uniquely short chain of command relative to most acquisition programs.

## Role in UAP Programs

The presenter of "The Hidden Wing" identifies Walden as one of the most important current Air Force officials to question about alleged UFO legacy program operations, given the [[Rapid Capabilities Office (RCO)|RCO]]'s theorized role as the execution arm for legacy program acquisition. The presenter specifically calls on the current presidential administration to question Walden directly, arguing that the RCO's structure — its narrow oversight chain, ability to minimize read-in personnel, and access to carve-out contracting mechanisms — makes it a plausible vehicle for managing [[Waived Unacknowledged Special Access Programs (USAPs)]] related to non-human technology exploitation.
