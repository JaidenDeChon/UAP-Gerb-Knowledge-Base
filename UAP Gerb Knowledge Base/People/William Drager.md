William Drager is a former US Air Force member who served as a Spanish-language interpreter and provided a formal affidavit corroborating elements of the 1955 [[Robert Willingham]] UAP crash account near [[Langtry, Texas]]. His testimony first appears in researcher [[Leonard Stringfield]]'s *UFO Crash Retrievals* reports, in an entry labeled "Del Rio, Texas, 1950". The date in that label is a discrepancy consistent with the broader conflation of two separate Del Rio-area incidents in UFO literature.

## Role in the Japanese Documentary

Drager was hired as an interpreter for a Japanese documentary crew investigating a UAP crash involving a colonel near the US-Mexico border. For the production, Drager contacted a Mexican general who commanded the border region that included the alleged crash site. In private, the general acknowledged: "Yes, I know about that. I don't have any papers or documents to prove it, but due to my position I know about that." On camera, however, the same general denied all knowledge and evaded questions about the incident and UFOs. Drager described the general as "beyond reproach" and a "military celebrity" in Mexico.

Drager, [[Robert Willingham]], and the film crew met the general together. The documentary has not been publicly identified or located, though screenshots from the production appear in [[Noe Torres]] and [[Ruben Uriarte]]'s 2008 book *The Other Roswell: UFO Crash on the Texas Border*.
