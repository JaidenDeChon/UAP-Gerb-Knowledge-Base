John B. Alexander is a retired US Army Colonel who served in a variety of special operations and intelligence roles and became a prominent figure in government-affiliated UAP research during the 1980s and 1990s. He is best known for spearheading the classified "Advanced Theoretical Physics Working Group" — a restricted group that met at BDM International's McLean, Virginia facility and discussed UFO legacy programs, reverse engineering, and advanced physics at the highest levels of the US defense and intelligence community.

## Advanced Theoretical Physics Conference

From 1982 to 1983, Alexander reported directly to [[Albert Stubblebine]] at US Army INSCOM. He is credited by UAP Gerb with spearheading the Advanced Theoretical Physics Working Group, which held a classified conference at a secure BDM International facility in McLean, Virginia on 20–25 May 1985. Notes from the conference were released by attendee Oke Shannon, formerly a manager for special projects at Los Alamos National Laboratory, and reveal that the group discussed multiple aspects of UFOs, including legacy programs and a major engineering project under [[Bobby Ray Inman]]. The conference used Department of Energy security controls.

The Advanced Theoretical Physics Conference is considered by UAP Gerb to be one of the most significant documented gatherings of senior intelligence and defense figures explicitly discussing UFO programs, establishing a direct link between Army INSCOM leadership, BDM International, Los Alamos, and UAP program knowledge.

## Philip J. Corso Manuscript

Alexander was privy to [[Philip J. Corso]]'s original manuscript before its publication. He reportedly remarked on sensational details in the published book *The Day After Roswell* that were absent from the original manuscript, suggesting significant editorial additions or embellishments were made between the manuscript stage and publication.
