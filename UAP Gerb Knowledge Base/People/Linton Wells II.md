Linton Wells II is a retired U.S. Navy officer with 26 years of naval service and a 14-year career within the Senior Executive Service in the Office of the Secretary of Defense (OSD). He served as a member of the Special Access Program Oversight Committee's Senior Review Group (SAPOC SRG) — the body established in 1994 to serve as the primary gatekeeping structure for access to the Department of Defense's most sensitive Special Access Programs (see [[Special Access Programs (SAPs)]]).

## Role in UAP Programs

UAP Gerb states it is aware — though Wells was not publicly employed by [[MITRE Corporation|MITRE]] — that Wells was briefed into MITRE's alleged UFO legacy programs at least in the mid-to-late 2000s. UAP Gerb cites Wells's SAPOC SRG membership and his contemporaneous MITRE briefing as further evidence of the overlap between the Pentagon's formal Special Access Program gatekeeping structure and senior personnel with knowledge of MITRE's alleged reverse-engineering and USO-monitoring programs, a pattern the channel also attributes to [[Paul Kaminski]] and [[John Deutsch|John M.
