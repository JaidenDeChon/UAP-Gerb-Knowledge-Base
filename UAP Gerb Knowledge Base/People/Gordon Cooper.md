L. Gordon Cooper Jr. was a US Air Force pilot and NASA Mercury astronaut. In UFO and UAP discourse, he is frequently cited for statements about unusual aerial events he said were observed and filmed during his military service period around [[Edwards Air Force Base]].

In this knowledge base, Cooper is referenced as a historical witness whose account is used to contextualize later claims about Edwards as a recurring site in UAP narratives. His testimony appears in many secondary discussions and remains contested in interpretation.

## Relevance in This Knowledge Base

- Cited in relation to a reported 1957 landed-disc filming incident at Edwards.
- Used as historical context for claims of long-duration restricted aerospace activity around Edwards test infrastructure.
