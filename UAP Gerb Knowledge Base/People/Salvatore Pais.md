Salvatore Cezar Pais is an American aerospace engineer, with a PhD in mechanical and aerospace engineering, who worked for the US Navy's Naval Air Warfare Center Aircraft Division at [[Naval Air Station Patuxent River]] in Maryland. He is best known for a series of Navy-assigned patents, among them the "Craft Using an Inertial Mass Reduction Device," filed in 2016 and granted in 2018 (US 10,144,532), which describes a craft that generates and manipulates fields around itself to reduce its inertial mass. The patents are widely nicknamed the Navy's "UFO patents."

## The Craft Patent

The patent describes a triangle- or diamond-shaped hybrid aerospace-undersea craft that manipulates electromagnetic fields and the quantum vacuum around its hull to achieve propulsion. Pais calls the underlying mechanism the "Pais Effect." The Navy's application was supported inside NAVAIR by its chief technology officer, James Sheehy, and was approved after internal review.

In *[[Off-World Technologies Division – UAP Technology Reverse Engineering]]*, UAP Gerb dates the filing to 2017 at Pax River (the application was published that year) and observes that the craft design strongly resembles the alleged reverse-engineered [[TR-3B]] triangle craft. Gerb cites the account in [[Ross Coulthart]]'s book *In Plain Sight* of former Navy science and technology official [[Nat Kobitz]], who said he made discreet inquiries about the patent at NAVAIR and Pax River and found that none of his Navy colleagues knew anything about Pais or his patents. Kobitz is quoted as saying that if anything near a working anti-gravity craft existed, it had been kept very quiet.

The video raises Pais and Pax River as a possible parallel to the alleged [[Off-World Technologies Division]] at [[Naval Surface Warfare Center Crane]], suggesting that similar non-human technology reverse-engineering work may be carried out at Crane's sister facility. Gerb also recommends Pais's interview on Curt Jaimungal's *Theories of Everything*.
