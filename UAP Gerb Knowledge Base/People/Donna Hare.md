Donna Hare is a former NASA contractor who worked as a photographic specialist in the Space Shuttle program at Johnson Space Center in Houston, Texas. She is best known for alleging that Building 8 of Johnson Space Center housed a dedicated laboratory where technicians airbrushed unidentified objects out of high-resolution NASA satellite and aerial imagery before the images were released to the public. She made the allegation publicly at the 2001 [[Disclosure Project]] press conference and in later interviews.

## Allegation About Building 8

Hare stated that she saw a colleague working on a photograph of a round object above the Earth, which was being removed from the image before distribution. The colleague was under security clearance obligations that Hare herself did not share. She alleged that Building 8 had a systematic process for finding and removing anomalous objects from NASA imagery, and that the staff involved were sworn to secrecy.

Her account directly informed [[Gary McKinnon]]'s decision to target Building 8 servers during his 2001–2002 hack of 97 US military and NASA computer systems. McKinnon claims he found folders titled "raw" and "processed" on Building 8 machines, holding image files of unusually large size. That fits Hare's description of high-resolution source material being processed for public release.

[[Chris Mellon]] has separately said that classified 4K satellite footage of UAPs exists but is suppressed or altered, which gives indirect support to Hare's account.
