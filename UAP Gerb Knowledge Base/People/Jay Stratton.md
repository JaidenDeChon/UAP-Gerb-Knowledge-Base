Jay Stratton is a U.S. defense intelligence official. He is best known publicly as the first director of the Unidentified Aerial Phenomena Task Force (UAPTF), which he led from 2019 to 2022. The UAPTF is a Department of Defense office established in 2020 to standardize the collection and reporting of UAP incursions into military-controlled airspace. Stratton also served as Chief of Air and Space Warfare in the Defense Intelligence Agency's Defense Warning Office. He was an early participant in the DIA's [[AAWSAP]] effort, beginning around 2008, and was a featured participant in the 2025 documentary *Age of Disclosure* alongside [[Luis Elizondo]].

## Role in UAP Programs

Gerb's video *Special Access Required Vol.2* plays a clip of Elizondo describing the years-long UAP investigation effort, which Elizondo says was funded by then-Senate Majority Leader [[Harry Reid]]. In the clip, Elizondo says he, Stratton and a wider team carried it out "together". Stratton's name comes up in the AAWSAP/[[AATIP (Advanced Aerospace Threat Identification Program)|ATIP]] era and again later, with the official UAPTF directorship and *Age of Disclosure*. Gerb treats this as evidence that Stratton stayed close to official and semi-official UAP program structures for more than a decade. The video criticizes how *Age of Disclosure* presents the organizational structure of the legacy program, in which Stratton and Elizondo appear together, and calls the documentary's chart of program authority self-serving.
