Steven M. Greer is an American physician turned UFO researcher. He founded the Center for the Study of Extraterrestrial Intelligence (CSETI) and the Disclosure Project. Greer is one of the most prominent and controversial figures in the UAP field. He is known for organizing the Disclosure Project's 2001 event at the National Press Club, where over 20 military, intelligence and government witnesses gave testimony. He has claimed that Lockheed Skunk Works and Northrop operate out of Helendale and Tehachapi as part of a UFO legacy program network. He has also claimed that [[Michael Herrera]]'s octagon craft and the Nimitz Tic Tac are connected to Skunk Works.

## Role in Jonathan Weygandt's Testimony

Greer played a major part in first bringing the testimony of [[Jonathan Weygandt]] to public attention. Weygandt contacted Greer's team in August 2000, and about two months later Greer recorded his testimony on tape for the Disclosure Project archive. The version of the interview Greer published was edited, however, and left out important details. The most notable omission is Weygandt's description of a four-fingered arm hanging from a partly open hatch on the crashed [[Egg-shaped UAP|egg-shaped craft]] during the 1997 [[Peru UFO Crash Incident]]. Questions that filmmaker [[James Fox]] asked during the recording session were also cut. For his comprehensive 2025 analysis, Gerb obtained the tape files closest to uncut from Greer's DPI archive, but the fully unedited original tape has not been found.

## Involvement with Michael Herrera  

Greer played a major part in putting Michael Herrera in contact with sources inside classified UAP programs. In messages Greer was referred to as 'Dr. G' or 'Dr. Gri'. He discussed Herrera's 2009 Indonesia sighting with an insider source and helped arrange contact. At the June 2023 National Press Club (NPC) event, Greer pulled Herrera aside after his presentation and showed him an encrypted text message from a CIA Non-Official Cover operative who claimed to know what Herrera had witnessed. Before that press conference, Greer had also helped set up Herrera's Daily Mail interview with Josh Boswell.

## Connection to the Apollo 8 Film Allegation

Greer's Disclosure Project witness archive includes a redacted entry, number 101179, about a person who claimed to have seen an uncut [[NASA]] film showing six UFOs descending onto the lunar surface. The film was allegedly shot by astronaut [[Frank Borman]] during the Apollo 8 mission in 1968. Greer's 2009 emails and handwritten notes, which Gerb reviewed, describe this source as traveling from France to meet Greer in April 2009, together with a high-profile French astronaut. Gerb identified the source as [[Alain Boudier]], at that time president of the French national aeronautical and astronautical Association UAP division. By early March 2009, Greer's emails refer to Boudier, called "Source One", as the courier delivering UAP briefing documents to French President [[Nicholas Sarkozy]]. When the video was made, Gerb had been unable to find any independent documentation of the alleged Apollo 8 UFO film.
