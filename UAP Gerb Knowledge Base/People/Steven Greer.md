Steven M. Greer is an American physician turned UFO researcher. He founded the Center for the Study of Extraterrestrial Intelligence (CSETI) and the [[Disclosure Project]]. Greer is one of the most prominent and controversial figures in the UAP field. He is known for organizing the Disclosure Project's 2001 event at the [[National Press Club]], where over 20 military, intelligence and government witnesses gave testimony. In Gerb's videos he matters above all as a gatekeeper of witnesses: many of the whistleblowers Gerb covers, from [[Jonathan Weygandt]] to [[Michael Herrera]], first went public through Greer, and Gerb mines the witness list and files Greer released in 2024 for redacted testimony. Greer has claimed that [[Lockheed Martin Skunk Works|Lockheed Skunk Works]] and Northrop operate out of [[Helendale]] and Tehachapi as part of a UFO legacy program network. He has also claimed that Herrera's octagon craft and the Nimitz Tic Tac are connected to Skunk Works.

## Career

Greer was born on 20 October 1955 in Charlotte, North Carolina. He took his medical degree in 1987 at East Tennessee State University's Quillen College of Medicine and worked as an emergency physician, becoming chairman of the department of emergency medicine at Caldwell Memorial Hospital in Lenoir, North Carolina. He founded CSETI in 1990, to seek peaceful contact with extraterrestrial civilizations, and the Disclosure Project in 1993, to press the US government to disclose what it allegedly knows about UFOs and suppressed energy and propulsion technology. In 1998 he retired from medicine to work on them full time.

## Gerb's View of Greer

Gerb is openly critical of Greer. He calls him egotistical, money-driven and "very problematic", is scathing about his later contact work, and notes that Greer either embellished or failed to check [[Steven Lovekin]]'s "brigadier general" title, which came from a volunteer state militia. He still credits Greer with important whistleblower work around 2001, when, Gerb says, Greer was doing largely respectable work. His standing rule is to separate witnesses from "the stain of Greer": men such as [[Bill Uhouse]] and [[Michael Herrera]] went through Greer because they knew no other route, and many have regretted it. Gerb admits he first brushed Herrera off partly by "buying into the Greer hate", and says the claim that Greer and Herrera are "in cahoots" is false. He also corrects the record on what Greer originated: the term ARV and the [[Helendale]] site were in circulation before Greer, through [[Bill Hamilton]], [[Brad Sorenson]] and [[Bill McDonald]], and it was Hamilton who passed Sorenson and [[Mark McCandlish]] to Greer.

## Role in Jonathan Weygandt's Testimony

Greer played a major part in first bringing the testimony of [[Jonathan Weygandt]] to public attention. Weygandt wrote to Greer's team on 1 August 2000, and about two months later Greer recorded his testimony on tape for the Disclosure Project archive. The version of the interview Greer published was edited, however, and left out important details. The most notable omission is Weygandt's description of a four-fingered arm hanging from a partly open hatch on the crashed [[Egg-shaped UAP|egg-shaped craft]] during the 1997 [[Peru UFO Crash Incident]]; Weygandt confirmed to Gerb that he had told Greer about it. Questions that filmmaker [[James Fox]] asked during the recording session were also cut. For his comprehensive 2025 analysis, Gerb used the rougher tape files from Greer's DPI archive, which are the closest to uncut, but he has been unable to obtain the fully unedited original tape.

## Involvement with Michael Herrera

Greer played a major part in putting [[Michael Herrera]] in contact with sources inside classified UAP programs. Herrera first met him in [[Boulder, Colorado|Boulder]] in 2017 and now volunteers as his security. In June 2023 Greer set up Herrera's Daily Mail interview with reporter Josh Boswell and his appearance at the National Press Club on 12 June, preceded by a two-day event. After Herrera presented his theory that the containers he saw held drugs, Greer pulled him aside in the green room and showed him an encrypted message from an insider in attendance: "I don't want to leave Michael hanging, but I know what Michael saw." The insider asked Greer to make the connection, and Greer later texted Herrera, at 2 a.m., the number of a man Herrera describes as a CIA [[Non-Official Cover (NOC)|non-official cover]] officer. Herrera himself says Greer has embellished details of his story: Greer has said guns were put to the Marines' heads, while Herrera says the operators held them from a distance.

## Witness Files and Locations

Much of what Gerb draws from Greer comes from the Disclosure Project's files rather than from Greer's own claims. In the briefing document and the 2024 archive Gerb finds:

- **Dugway.** Insider emails to Greer from 1996 (relayed by his then webmaster AJ Kadok) and March 1998 (from Steve Jones) pointed to [[Dugway Proving Ground]] before any public claim about it, and the Boeing insider AH, whom Gerb identifies as [[Arnold House]], said in 2000 that 35 to 40 percent of [[Area 51]] had moved to an underground Utah base. Greer published the location of an alleged Dugway underground entrance, which is why Gerb was willing to discuss it. The witness [[MS (Dugway Witness)|MS]], whose story Greer told in 2023, is witness 10655 in the archive.
- **The Antelope Valley.** Greer says he has flown over the [[Helendale]] and [[Tejon Ranch|Tejon]] radar cross-section ranges and seen "diamond" pads where man-made electrogravitic craft, built since 1954 or 1955, hover and are struck with electromagnetic weapons to test their hardening. He also says he knows the entrance to Tejon's underground "anthill".
- **Edwards.** Greer lists [[Edwards Air Force Base]] as a chief facility with "a whole constellation" around it, in a list he says he updated "for the president in '09", and says his sources split a [[Blackjack Team]] and a Blackjack Control between Edwards and Nellis. Witness 11063, [[Ed (Witness 11063)|Ed]], is the subject of Gerb's second Redacted List video.
- **Corporate witnesses.** Former SAIC employee [[Denise McKenzie]], Lockheed's [[Don Phillips]] and two redacted entries tying AT&T to "ET technology transfer" all come from Greer's files.
- **Army INSCOM.** Greer says [[Albert Stubblebine]] offered him $2 billion to stop his work and join his team after a contact event near Pensacola, and Stubblebine's own entry in Greer's witness log places stored craft and bodies at Fort Huachuca.

## Connection to the Apollo 8 Film Allegation

Greer's Disclosure Project witness archive includes a redacted entry, number 101179, about a person who claimed to have seen an uncut [[NASA]] film showing six UFOs descending onto the lunar surface. The film was allegedly shot by astronaut [[Frank Borman]] during the Apollo 8 mission in 1968. Greer's 2009 emails and handwritten notes, which Gerb reviewed, describe this source as traveling from France to meet Greer in April 2009, together with a high-profile French astronaut. Gerb identified the source as [[Alain Boudier]], who around that time became president of the UAP division of the French aeronautics and astronautics association (3AF). By early March 2009, Greer's emails refer to Boudier, called "Source One", as the courier delivering UAP briefing documents to French President [[Nicholas Sarkozy]]. When the video was made, Gerb had been unable to find any independent documentation of the alleged Apollo 8 UFO film.
