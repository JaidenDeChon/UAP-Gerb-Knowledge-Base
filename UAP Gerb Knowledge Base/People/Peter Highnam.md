Dr. Peter Highnam is a senior U.S. defense science and technology official who is currently Principal Deputy Assistant Secretary of Defense for Critical Technologies in the Office of the Under Secretary of Defense for Research and Engineering (USD R&E). He holds a Ph.D. in computer science from Carnegie Mellon University and worked in industry at Schlumberger before joining the government as a DARPA program manager. He was then the second Director of the Intelligence Advanced Research Projects Activity (IARPA, 2012–2015) and later Director of Research at the National Geospatial-Intelligence Agency (NGA). He returned to DARPA as Deputy Director from approximately 2018 to 2022, including periods as acting director.

## Role in UAP Programs

UAP Gerb's *Special Access Required Vol.2* names Highnam as a senior figure in the reporting chain that runs from [[DARPA]]'s [[DARPA Security and Intelligence Directorate (SID)|Security and Intelligence Directorate]] (SID) to the Office of the Under Secretary of Defense for Research and Engineering. The video identifies that office, per DoD Instruction 5205.11, as responsible for cultivating cutting-edge technology development programs that require Special Access Program protection. Gerb alleges that Highnam has been "integral to coordinating legacy efforts between DARPA and USD R&E" for the past several years. His reasons are that DARPA SID reports administratively up through USD(R&E), and that Highnam's career has spanned DARPA's deputy directorship, IARPA's directorship, and NGA's research directorate. Gerb also describes Highnam's current "critical technologies" portfolio as a marker that, the video argues, often correlates with proximity to classified legacy program activity. The video says that as an early DARPA program manager Highnam focused on electronic warfare and airborne communications, but this detail comes from the video's own narration and has not been independently corroborated.
