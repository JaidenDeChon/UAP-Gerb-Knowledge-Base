Michael Shermer is a prominent skeptic and the founder and head of [[Skeptic Magazine]]. He is known for offering prosaic explanations for anomalous phenomena, including UAP encounters. For the March 5, 2004 [[Mexico UFO Incident]], Shermer proposed that the 11 infrared-detected objects filmed by the [[Mexican Air Force]] over [[Campeche, Mexico|Campeche]] were burnoff flares from an offshore oil platform in the [[Gulf of Mexico]].

## Mexico UFO Incident Analysis

After the Mexican Air Force released infrared footage of 11 unidentified objects, three of which also appeared on radar, Shermer suggested the lights were conventional burnoff flares from offshore oil drilling platforms in the Gulf of Mexico. The explanation has been considered but not conclusively confirmed. To hold up, it has to account for the way the objects seemed to move around the aircraft, the pilots' sense that the objects were reacting to the pursuit, and the radar returns that corroborated three of the objects at altitude.

Gerb introduces Shermer as someone viewers may know from "getting cooked" in a debate with [[Graham Hancock]] on The Joe Rogan Experience. He first says he chooses to believe the military pilots over Shermer, and later that, although the Mexico case has the highest probability of a prosaic explanation of the four he covers, he is not sure the objects were flares and more analysis is needed.
