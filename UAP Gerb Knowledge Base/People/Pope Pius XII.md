**Pope Pius XII** (born Eugenio Maria Giuseppe Giovanni Pacelli, 1876–1958) was head of the Catholic Church and sovereign of Vatican City State from 1939 until his death in 1958. His papacy spanned World War II and the early Cold War, a period of complex diplomatic relationships with both the Axis and the Allied powers. His wartime conduct, particularly toward the Holocaust and in his relationship with Nazi Germany, remains controversial among historians.

## Alleged Role in 1933 Magenta UFO Craft Transfer

According to UAP whistleblower [[David Grusch]]'s public testimony, and to journalist [[Christopher Sharp]]'s 2021 article "All UFO Disclosure Roads Lead to Rome," Pope Pius XII played a secret intelligence role in moving the craft recovered near [[Magenta, Italy]] in 1933 from Italian to American possession.

Sharp reports that relations between [[Benito Mussolini]] and the Catholic priesthood were at first positive, and that the dictator confided in Pope Pius XII about the retrieved unknown craft, which the Italian government had controlled since June 1933 through the secret [[RS-33 (Gabinetto RS 33)]] group. As Italy formalized its alliance with Nazi Germany in the late 1930s and early 1940s, the Pope allegedly grew uneasy about the craft staying in fascist Italian hands.

Worried about the strategic implications of advanced technology under Axis control, Pius XII reportedly passed information about the craft's existence and location to the United States through back channels. This intelligence allowed U.S. [[Office of Strategic Services (OSS)]] agents to plan and carry out the craft's recovery during or right after the fall of the Italian fascist state in 1944–1945. According to Grusch's account, the facility housing the craft, possibly Mussolini's bunker under [[Mount Soratte Bunker|Mount Soratte]], was spared from Allied bombing because the Pope had earlier shared intelligence with American authorities.

## Historical Context

The alleged Vatican involvement is consistent with documented cases of Pope Pius XII passing intelligence to the Allies during World War II, including back-channel communications through various intermediaries. The Vatican was formally neutral in diplomacy during the war but carried out covert intelligence activities on several fronts.
