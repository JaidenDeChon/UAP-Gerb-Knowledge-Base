**Tommaso David** (1875–1959) was an Italian military officer and intelligence chief who, under the cover name "Dott. De Santis", commanded the counter-intelligence service of Mussolini's Italian Social Republic (the Salò republic) in 1943–1945 and founded its "Volpi Argentate" (Silver Foxes) espionage unit. In UAP research he is notable as the probable real identity of "De Santis", the man to whom the fascist-era crash-recovery protocol of the [[RS-33 (Gabinetto RS 33)|RS-33]] commission was addressed, according to documents tied to the [[1933 Magenta UFO CrashRetrieval|1933 Magenta, Italy crash]].

## Career

Born in Esperia, in the province of Frosinone, on 28 February 1875, David served in the Regia Marina and later in anti-communist militia units, commanding an anti-partisan band in Dalmatia between 1941 and 1943. After the armistice of September 1943 he joined the Guardia Nazionale Repubblicana of the Salò republic, where [[Benito Mussolini]] placed him in command of the "Gruppo Servizi Autonomi", its counter-intelligence service. Under the name "Dott. De Santis" he set up the "Allevamento volpi argentate", a largely female unit that carried out espionage and sabotage behind Allied lines. In April 1945 Mussolini reportedly entrusted him with papers including his wartime correspondence with Winston Churchill. David hid after the war under the false identity "Professor Luigi Grossi" and died in Genoa on 11 November 1959.

## The RS-33 protocol

Among the documents that the anonymous source [[Mr. X]] gave Italian researcher [[Roberto Pinotti]] in 1996 is a cover letter, stamped with the seal of the Kingdom Senate, that introduces a nine-point protocol for handling the Magenta craft. It opens "Dear Desanti" in the English translation Gerb reads, and tells the recipient to make no copies, not to mention the note even to his deputy, and to bring any matter concerning the RS-33 office to the writer first. In the video "The 1933 Magenta, Italy UFO Crash", Gerb states that this "De Santis" was almost certainly a pseudonym for Tommaso David, an identification made by Italian historian Arrigo Petacco. The protocol addressed to him orders the arrest of all witnesses by special RS-33 sections of the OVRA secret police, routes all reports to the central meteorological office at La Sapienza University in Rome, and requires a meteor or other celestial explanation to be published in the press.
