Richard Bissell Jr. was a senior Central Intelligence Agency official. He was the CIA's Deputy Director for Plans (the clandestine services), and when the [[NRO (National Reconnaissance Office)]] was founded in 1961 he was one of its two co-directors. Bissell was a central architect of the CIA's early technical intelligence programs, including the U-2 spy plane and the Corona film-return satellite system, which made him a foundational figure in overhead reconnaissance. According to UAP claims, he was also a foundational figure in the early institutional management of UAP-related programs.

## Role in UAP Programs

The economist and senior government adviser [[Harold Malmgren]] claims that Bissell confirmed to him that a UFO crash occurred in Magenta, Italy, in 1933, and briefed him on "otherworldly technologies" associated with that crash. Gerb presents this claimed disclosure as evidence that the NRO's founding leaders knew of, and were connected to, UFO recovery programs that already existed when the organization was created in 1961. Because Bissell was an NRO co-director, his alleged knowledge of such programs would put the NRO at the center of oversight of UAP legacy programs from the very beginning.
