Dennis Stacy is an American journalist and UFO researcher who contributed to *Omni* magazine. He spent several years investigating the alleged 1950 UAP crash near [[El Indio, Texas]], and published his findings in a 1995 *Omni* article titled "Crash at El Indio: Alleged UFO Crash in Mexico."

## El Indio Investigation

From 1990 to 1994, Stacy tried to find out whether the El Indio crash described in the alleged [[Majestic 12]] Eisenhower Briefing Document referred to an actual event. During his fieldwork he found a local resident, Senor Flores. Flores had been working on his family's land north of El Indio, on the US side of the Rio Grande, when a ball of fire fell from the sky, crashed on the neighbouring ranch and started a grass fire. A day or two later, a military unit arrived from Piedras Negras, blocked off the site and took something away by truck. Flores could not confirm whether the vehicles were American, Mexican or a mix of both.

Stacy also considered whether the account might instead describe a Civil Air Patrol (CAP) aircraft crash in the region on January 16, 1944, but he did not confirm it.
