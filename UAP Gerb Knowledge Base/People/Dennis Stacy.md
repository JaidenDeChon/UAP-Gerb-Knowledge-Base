Dennis Stacy is an American journalist and UFO researcher who contributed to *Omni* magazine. He conducted a multi-year investigation into the alleged 1950 UAP crash near [[El Indio, Texas]], publishing his findings in a 1995 *Omni* article titled "Crash at El Indio: Alleged UFO Crash in Mexico."

## El Indio Investigation

From 1990 to 1994, Stacy worked to determine whether the El Indio crash described in the alleged [[Majestic 12]] Eisenhower Briefing Document referred to an actual event. During his fieldwork, he located a local resident, Senor Flores, who had been working on his family's land north of El Indio on the US side of the Rio Grande when a ball of fire fell from the sky, crashed on an adjoining ranch, and ignited a grass fire. A day or two later, a military contingent arrived from Piedras Negras, blocked off the location, and hauled something away by truck. Flores could not confirm whether the vehicles were American, Mexican, or a mixed contingent.

Stacy considered but did not confirm the possibility that the incident might instead describe a Civil Air Patrol (CAP) aircraft crash that occurred on January 16, 1944 in the region.
