Jake Barber is a former US Air Force airman and combat controller who says he later worked as a contract helicopter pilot for a program that retrieved downed craft, some of which he believes are of non-human origin. He went public in January 2025 in a NewsNation interview with [[Ross Coulthart]], describing among other things the recovery of a smooth white egg-shaped object the size of an SUV, and he is associated with the [[Skywatchers|Skywatcher]] group. Gerb draws on Barber's account in several videos: for weapons said to disable craft, for the idea that some craft are controlled by thought ([[Psionics|psionics]]), and for his roman à clef *[[Sentinels of Ether]]*.

## Background

Barber served in the Air Force as an aerospace mechanic in the late 1990s before joining its combat control special operations field, where he earned a NATO top-secret clearance through service in Bosnia. He says he went on to work for the Department of Defense and the intelligence community in a non-official capacity, including as a contracted helicopter pilot on retrieval missions. According to Gerb, Barber was in the audience at [[Steven Greer]]'s June 2023 whistleblower symposium at the National Press Club, the event that featured the first public disclosures of [[Michael Herrera]].

## Weapons to Disable Craft

In a clip Gerb plays in his [[Deep Underground Military Bases (DUMBs)|underground bases]] video, Barber says that during a deployment he met people from an aerospace company he will not name, who were "supporting us with some new weapons technology that could be used to disable vehicles and aircraft". He agrees that pulsed microwave weapons were in use at the time, and says "there were some early prototypes" being used. Those relationships, he says, led to opportunities "in the California desert". Gerb sets this beside an anonymous 1974 letter to [[Leonard Stringfield]] about the "Blue Boys", which described helicopters carrying "a new type of missile" used on UFOs and an "electronic warfare system designed to disable UFOs under controlled conditions". Gerb also connects Barber with programs said to use anomalous cognition to lure craft in and bring them down with EMP weapons.

## Sentinels of Ether

Gerb calls *Sentinels of Ether* Jake Barber's "roman à clef": a story told as fiction but said to draw on real events. It describes a 2004 blue-on-blue firefight around a UFO retrieval, in which a clandestine team flies into a disguised base hidden in the mountains of Chihuahua, Mexico. In his underground bases video, Gerb notes that the map [[Bill Hamilton]] received from a source in 1990, published in *Cosmic Top Secret* in 1992, marks an installation in Chihuahua, and that the subject did not come up again until Barber's story.

## Egg-Shaped Craft and Psionics

Gerb credits Barber's 2025 testimony, together with Skywatcher, with making egg-shaped retrievals and psionic control of craft a live topic. In his video on [[Jonathan Weygandt]]'s 1997 Peru account, he contrasts Barber's smooth white egg the size of an SUV with the far larger craft Weygandt described, with hatches, a band of light around it and gill-like vents, and notes that Weygandt spoke of a thought-controlled craft as early as 2000. In his SAIC video he shows that the idea of craft flown by cognition is much older than Barber, going back to the 1950s and to the claims of [[Philip J. Corso]]. In his Northrop Grumman video, a clip of Barber on anti-gravity and drawing energy from the quantum vacuum leads Gerb to say that [[Steve Wilson]]'s claims of reverse-engineered anti-gravity vehicles "maybe" are not so far-fetched.
