Jake Barber is a US military special operations veteran who has publicly claimed involvement in UAP crash retrieval operations. His testimony connects him to clandestine recovery units and advanced aerospace weapons programs. Given in 2025, it is unusually specific about electromagnetic weapons used to disable UAP, and it corroborates decades-old anonymous testimony compiled by researcher [[Leonard Stringfield]].

## EMP-Type Weapons and UAP Disabling

One of Barber's most significant disclosures is that he was shown "new weapons technology" at an undisclosed location, provided by an unnamed aerospace company. He described it as pulse microwave weapons: early prototypes of systems designed to disable vehicles and aircraft, potentially including UAP. He met people representing the companies that provided these services to the government, and those relationships later led to "opportunities in the California desert", where he began working on the UAP retrieval program.

This testimony directly echoes an anonymous letter Stringfield received in 1974, which described an "electronic warfare system designed to disable UFOs under controlled conditions" and a "new type of missile" used against UAP. Gerb cites the agreement between these two accounts, which come from entirely independent sources more than 50 years apart, as one of the more compelling corroborations in research on deep underground military bases (DUMBs) and retrieval operations.

## Chihuahua Operation (Sentinels of Ether)

Barber's account, which Gerb calls "Sentinels of Ether" or "Roman clay," describes a 2004 blue-on-blue UAP retrieval operation in which a clandestine team flew into a disguised base hidden in the mountains of Chihuahua, Mexico. The account is of particular interest because [[Bill Hamilton]]'s 1990 map of deep underground military bases (DUMBs) independently identifies an installation in Chihuahua, a match in location that Hamilton's map could not have anticipated.
