Robert R. Everett was an American computer scientist and systems engineer who served as the first technical director of the [[MITRE Corporation]] upon its founding in 1958. Prior to MITRE, Everett worked on sensitive MIT Navy-funded research, including Project Whirlwind — an early real-time digital computing project that became foundational to the later SAGE air defense system (see [[Semi-Automatic Ground Environment (SAGE)]]).

## Naval Background and MITRE's Founding

Everett's arrival at MITRE from Naval Special Projects research is cited by UAP Gerb as an early example of the organization's deep — if not publicly emphasized — Navy institutional lineage, despite MITRE having been formally chartered by the U.S. Air Force. UAP Gerb argues this Navy connection took on particular significance decades later, when the U.S. Navy — rather than the Air Force — emerged as the alleged administering sponsor for MITRE's reverse-engineering and USO-monitoring programs.
