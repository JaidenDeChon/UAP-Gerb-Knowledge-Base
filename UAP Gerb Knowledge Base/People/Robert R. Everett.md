Robert R. Everett was an American computer scientist and systems engineer who was the first technical director of the [[MITRE Corporation]] when it was founded in 1958. Before joining MITRE, Everett worked on sensitive Navy-funded research at MIT, including Project Whirlwind, an early real-time digital computing project that became a foundation of the later SAGE air defense system (see [[Semi-Automatic Ground Environment (SAGE)]]).

## Naval Background and MITRE's Founding

Gerb cites Everett's move to MITRE from Naval Special Projects research as an early example of the organization's deep Navy institutional lineage, which it did not publicly emphasize, even though the U.S. Air Force formally chartered MITRE. Gerb argues that this Navy connection took on particular significance decades later, when the U.S. Navy, rather than the Air Force, emerged as the alleged administering sponsor of MITRE's reverse-engineering and USO-monitoring programs.
