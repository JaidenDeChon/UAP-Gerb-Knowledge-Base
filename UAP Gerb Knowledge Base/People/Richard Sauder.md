Richard Sauder is an American researcher and author who investigates deep underground military bases, secret underground tunnels and clandestine subterranean installations operated by the US government and military. His rigorous method combines Freedom of Information Act requests, declassified documents, publicly available defense records and firsthand witness testimony. It has produced what Gerb describes as three "dense masterpieces": *Underground Bases and Tunnels: What Is the Government Trying to Hide?*, *Hidden in Plain Sight: Beyond the X-Files* and *Underground and Underwater Bases*. These books are the primary reference material for Gerb's investigation into deep underground military bases (DUMBs) and UFO legacy programs.

## Methodology and Research

Sauder bases his research on DUMBs on publicly available documentation instead of speculation. He has obtained FOIA documents confirming underground facilities beneath locations such as Manzano Mountain at Kirtland Air Force Base, and he checks witness testimony against official records to establish a factual baseline. His research into Los Alamos National Laboratory's underground installations is an example. The DOE repeatedly denied having any underground facilities there, but Sauder eventually obtained a badly blurred copy of an article describing an underground facility built in the late 1940s, which confirmed that the earlier denials were false.

Sauder has also documented the testimony of multiple military and government insiders. For example, a Navy veteran stationed at China Lake confirmed to Sauder that the facility extends one mile deep and contains "weapons more powerful than nuclear weapons." Through informants, Sauder also learned that some Bureau of Reclamation tunnels in the American Southwest have served as cover for clandestine secondary tunnel boring operations.

## Bill Hamilton Connection

Sauder spoke at length with researcher [[Bill Hamilton]] about Hamilton's network map of underground UAP facilities across the American West and Southwest. By the time of Gerb's DUMBs video (circa 2024–2025), Sauder had spoken with Gerb for approximately two to three hours and had agreed to appear on a future collaborative project. He has also made statements to Gerb about specific installations that he had not previously disclosed in public.

## Key Findings on Tunneling and Transport

Sauder's research into underground transportation includes documentation of high-speed rail systems allegedly connecting classified facilities. He referenced reports of underground rail systems connecting Area 51 from Mercury, Nevada, and the Naval Surface Warfare Center Corona Division. An informant told Sauder that approximately 10 men are needed to operate a tunnel boring machine (TBM), and that in good rock conditions a TBM can bore up to 10 miles per year. At that rate, facilities such as China Lake and Edwards AFB could be connected by tunnel in as little as five and a half years.

A FOIA document Sauder obtained from the military-industrial complex gave hard estimates of the personnel needed to build and operate a deeply buried tunnel system hundreds of miles long: 7,800 to 8,700 people, at a total cost estimated at around $17 billion over a 10-year period.
