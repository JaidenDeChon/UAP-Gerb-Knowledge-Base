Eric Wayne Burlison (born October 2, 1976) is a Republican member of the U.S. House of Representatives, representing Missouri's 7th congressional district since 2023. He is a member of the bipartisan **House UAP Caucus** alongside Representatives [[Tim Burchett]], [[Anna Paulina Luna]], and [[Representative Moskowitz|Jared Moskowitz]], and has been an active participant in congressional UAP oversight, including questioning [[David Grusch]] and [[Luis Elizondo]] during House Oversight Committee hearings and supporting efforts to establish a dedicated UAP oversight subcommittee.

## UAP Caucus Membership and Legislative Activity

As a member of the UAP Caucus, Burlison has supported efforts to:

- Bring historical UFO crash retrieval events (including the [[1978 Bolivia UFO Crash]]) to congressional investigation
- Support legislative provisions requiring UAP program transparency in the National Defense Authorization Act (NDAA)
- Coordinate congressional hearings featuring UAP whistleblower testimony
- Advocate for legal protections for whistleblowers disclosing classified UAP information
- Push for declassification of historical programs such as [[Project Moondust|Project Moon Dust]]

In a September 2025 hearing, Burlison presented military video footage as part of the caucus's ongoing UAP oversight work.

## MITRE Interrogative Letter

Burlison, working with [[David Grusch]], sent a detailed legal interrogative letter of more than ten pages, dated 22 May 2026, to the [[MITRE Corporation]], received by MITRE senior vice president [[Christopher Land]]. The letter establishes a preservation obligation on MITRE covering contracts and sponsor relationships, classified Special Access Program oversight visibility, sensor and domain-awareness data related to UAP/USO detection, materials and technology exploitation records, secure facilities, third-party contractor relationships, and budgeting and funding controls, in connection with UAP Gerb's allegations that MITRE has managed a decades-old Navy technology reverse-engineering program and a USO-monitoring program nicknamed the "[[Tic Tac Factory]]."

## Naval Whistleblower Meeting

Burlison has described meeting, alongside fellow UAP Caucus member [[Anna Paulina Luna]], with a whistleblower from a "nautical source" — a meeting that required a full day of travel. Burlison described the whistleblower's account of underwater activity as reminiscent of the film *The Abyss*, referencing a structure in the deep ocean rather than any depiction of aquatic beings from the film. UAP Gerb connects this account to its own reporting on an alleged MITRE-administered USO monitoring program in the Atlantic.

## Pax River Site Visit

Burlison conducted a site visit to [[Naval Air Station Patuxent River|Naval Air Station Patuxent River (Pax River)]], accompanied by White House staff, specifically looking for hangar facilities that could have been used to store recovered non-human vehicles. He reported not finding direct evidence of such storage but stated the facility would be, logistically, "the perfect venue" for a vehicle transfer — referencing infrastructure reportedly considered for a transfer of material from Lockheed Martin to Bigelow Aerospace.

## Immaculate Constellation and ARV Terminology

UAP Gerb states that Burlison confirmed to the channel he had previously heard the term "ARV" (alien reproduction vehicle) used within the UFO research community, in the context of discussing the [[Immaculate Constellation (IMCON)]] report's alleged designation of certain triangular UAP as ARVs.
