Eric Wayne Burlison (born October 2, 1976) is a Republican member of the U.S. House of Representatives who has represented Missouri's 7th congressional district since 2023. He belongs to the bipartisan House UAP Caucus with Representatives [[Tim Burchett]], [[Anna Paulina Luna]], and [[Representative Moskowitz|Jared Moskowitz]]. He has taken an active part in congressional UAP oversight: he questioned [[David Grusch]] and [[Luis Elizondo]] at House Oversight Committee hearings, and he has supported efforts to set up a dedicated UAP oversight subcommittee.

## UAP Caucus Membership and Legislative Activity

As a member of the UAP Caucus, Burlison has supported efforts to:

- Bring historical UFO crash retrieval events, including the [[1978 Bolivia UFO Crash]], under congressional investigation
- Add provisions to the National Defense Authorization Act (NDAA) that require transparency about UAP programs
- Coordinate congressional hearings with testimony from UAP whistleblowers
- Secure legal protections for whistleblowers who disclose classified UAP information
- Declassify historical programs such as [[Project Moon Dust]]

In a September 2025 hearing, Burlison presented military video footage as part of the caucus's ongoing UAP oversight work.

## MITRE Interrogative Letter

Working with [[David Grusch]], Burlison sent the [[MITRE Corporation]] a detailed legal interrogative letter of more than ten pages, dated 22 May 2026. MITRE senior vice president [[Christopher Land]] received it. The letter places a preservation obligation on MITRE covering contracts and sponsor relationships, classified Special Access Program oversight visibility, sensor and domain-awareness data related to UAP/USO detection, materials and technology exploitation records, secure facilities, third-party contractor relationships, and budgeting and funding controls. It was sent in connection with UAP Gerb's allegations that MITRE has managed a decades-old Navy technology reverse-engineering program and a USO-monitoring program nicknamed the "[[Tic Tac Factory]]."

## Naval Whistleblower Meeting

Burlison has described a meeting that he and fellow UAP Caucus member [[Anna Paulina Luna]] held with a whistleblower from a "nautical source". Getting to the meeting took a full day of travel. Burlison said the whistleblower's account of underwater activity was reminiscent of the film *The Abyss*. He was referring to a structure in the deep ocean, not to the film's aquatic beings. UAP Gerb connects this account to its own reporting on an alleged MITRE-administered USO monitoring program in the Atlantic.

## Pax River Site Visit

Burlison visited [[Naval Air Station Patuxent River|Naval Air Station Patuxent River (Pax River)]] with White House staff to look for hangar facilities that could have been used to store recovered non-human vehicles. He reported that he found no direct evidence of such storage, but said that logistically the facility would be "the perfect venue" for a vehicle transfer. He was referring to infrastructure that was reportedly considered for a transfer of material from Lockheed Martin to Bigelow Aerospace.

## Immaculate Constellation and ARV Terminology

Gerb states that Burlison confirmed to the channel that he had already heard the term "ARV" (alien reproduction vehicle) used in the UFO research community. The subject came up in a discussion of the [[Immaculate Constellation (IMCON)]] report, which allegedly labels certain triangular UAP as ARVs.
