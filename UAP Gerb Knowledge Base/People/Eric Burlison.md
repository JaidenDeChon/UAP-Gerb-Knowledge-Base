Eric Wayne Burlison (born October 2, 1976) is a Republican member of the U.S. House of Representatives who has represented Missouri's 7th congressional district since 2023. He belongs to the bipartisan [[UAP Caucus|House UAP Caucus]] with Representatives [[Tim Burchett]], [[Anna Paulina Luna]], and [[Representative Moskowitz|Jared Moskowitz]]. He has taken an active part in congressional UAP oversight: he questioned [[David Grusch]] and [[Luis Elizondo]] at House Oversight Committee hearings, took Grusch on as a special adviser, and has sent legal interrogatories, with Grusch's help, to agencies and contractors that Gerb ties to UFO legacy programs. In Gerb's videos he is the lawmaker whose letters and remarks Gerb most often builds on, above all the 2026 interrogatory to the [[MITRE Corporation]].

## Career

Burlison was born in Springfield, Missouri. He was elected to the Missouri House of Representatives in 2008 and served there until the end of 2016, then sat in the Missouri Senate from 2019 to 2023. He took his seat in the U.S. House on 3 January 2023, where he sits on the House Oversight Committee and its Task Force on the Declassification of Federal Secrets. In April 2025 he appointed David Grusch as his special adviser on UAP for four months.

## UAP Caucus Membership and Legislative Activity

Burlison is a member of the UAP Caucus, the group of lawmakers whose work, Gerb says, first brought the [[1978 Bolivia UFO Crash]] to his attention; Gerb covers it in his video on [[Project Moon Dust]]. In a September 2025 hearing of the House Oversight Committee's Task Force on the Declassification of Federal Secrets, Burlison presented military video footage of a Hellfire missile striking an object off Yemen.

On the SecoActivo podcast, Burlison said he had sent legal interrogatories to the CIA, the [[Aerospace Corporation]] and the Navy, and that Grusch would keep helping with more. The CIA declined to answer and referred him to [[AARO]]. The Aerospace Corporation gave a briefing in a secure setting which, Burlison said, "really didn't turn up" anything. Gerb takes the fact that an interrogatory was aimed at the Aerospace Corporation as evidence that it is deep in the programs.

Burlison has also named the 1952 film briefing *Flying Saucer Talk* (AF-ATIC-film 0352), given by Captain [[Edward J. Ruppelt]], as held at [[MIT Lincoln Laboratory]]. And, according to Burlison, Air Force intelligence officer [[Matthew Sullivan]] died of what was called an accidental overdose two weeks before he was due to make protected disclosures about legacy programs.

## MITRE Interrogative Letter

Working with [[David Grusch]], Burlison sent the [[MITRE Corporation]] a detailed legal interrogative letter of more than ten pages in May 2026. MITRE senior vice president [[Christopher Land]] received it. The letter places a preservation obligation on MITRE covering contracts and sponsor relationships, classified Special Access Program oversight visibility, sensor and domain-awareness data related to UAP/USO detection, materials and technology exploitation records, secure facilities, third-party contractor relationships, and budgeting and funding controls. Gerb's video on MITRE sets out his allegations that the company has managed a decades-old Navy technology reverse-engineering program and a USO-monitoring program nicknamed the "[[Tic Tac Factory]]", and offers its findings to Land as help in answering the letter.

## Naval Whistleblower Meeting

Burlison has described a meeting that he and fellow UAP Caucus member [[Anna Paulina Luna]] held with a whistleblower from a "nautical source". Getting to the meeting took a full day of travel. Burlison said the whistleblower's account of underwater activity was reminiscent of the film *The Abyss*. He was referring to a structure in the deep ocean, not to the film's aquatic beings. Gerb connects this account to his own reporting on an alleged MITRE-administered USO monitoring program in the Atlantic.

## Pax River Site Visit

Burlison visited [[Naval Air Station Patuxent River|Naval Air Station Patuxent River (Pax River)]] with White House staff to look for hangar facilities that could have been used to store recovered non-human vehicles. He reported that he found no direct evidence of such storage, but said that logistically the facility would be "the perfect venue" for a vehicle transfer. He was referring to the facility that was reportedly to be used for a transfer of material from Lockheed Martin to Bigelow Aerospace.

## Immaculate Constellation and ARV Terminology

Gerb states that Burlison confirmed to the channel that he had already heard the term "ARV" (alien reproduction vehicle) used in the UFO research community. The subject came up in a discussion of the [[Immaculate Constellation (IMCON)]] report, which allegedly labels certain triangular UAP as ARVs.

## Rodrik Castle

In early 2025 former Marine [[Rodrik Castle]], who describes a 1997 encounter with a triangular craft at 29 Palms, approached David Grusch offering sworn testimony and then [[Steven Greer]], who brought him to Burlison. A photograph of Castle with Greer, Burlison, [[Michael Herrera]] and others circulated in mid-2025. Gerb, who had been vetting Castle since the spring, hopes to present Castle's testimony and his own thesis to members of Congress, perhaps Burlison.
