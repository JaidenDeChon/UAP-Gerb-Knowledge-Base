**Valentine Archuleta** was a rancher near [[Aztec, New Mexico]] who said he watched a large disc-shaped craft in trouble strike a cliff near his land. His account is part of the eyewitness record of the [[Aztec UFO Crash and Recovery]]. He first told his story to researcher [[William Steinman]] in 1982 under the pseudonym "VA," and his account was later developed by [[Scott and Suzanne Ramsey]]. Video captions render his name "Valentin Archeletta."

## Background

Archuleta served in the Second World War in the Philippines, where he was captured and survived Japanese prisoner-of-war camps. After the war he ranched near Aztec.

## His Account

Early one morning while doing chores, some time between 1948 and 1950 by his own recollection, Archuleta heard a large explosion. He then saw a huge disc, larger than his house and with a dome on top, pass overhead within about 200 yards. It appeared to be in distress, skimming as low as 100 feet and wobbling as it went. He watched it strike the corner of a cliff face about 150 feet high, throwing sparks and rocks in every direction. The Ramseys believe they have found the spot. Archuleta telephoned the military in Albuquerque, which Gerb takes to mean [[Kirtland Air Force Base, New Mexico|Kirtland Air Force Base]]. He asked to be called back with an explanation but never heard from them again.
