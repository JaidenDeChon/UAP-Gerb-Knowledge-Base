Hillary S. McCaron is a research neuroscientist at the [[US Army Medical Research Institute of Chemical Defense]], according to her LinkedIn profile. Gerb identified her as a possible match for "S. McCaron", one of three names that appear on a page of the [[Alleged Leaked UAP Task Force Report]].

## Possible link to the alleged report

The page in question, from a section titled "Behavioral Data Analysis", names [[M. Harmon]], [[S. McCaron]] and [[K Shakoya]] and presents conclusions about the behaviour of UAP and unidentified submerged phenomena. McCaron's own name does not appear on it. In his analysis of the [[Flyby Footage]], Gerb said that his exhaustive search for the names had turned up almost nothing, and that his only lead was a Hillary S. McCaron working as a research neuroscientist at the institute. He remarked that a neuroscientist's involvement in a page studying UFO behaviour "fits", and said he would investigate further.
