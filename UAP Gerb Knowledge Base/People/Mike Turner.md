Michael Ray Turner (born 1960) is a Republican member of the US House of Representatives from Ohio, representing the 3rd congressional district from 2003 to 2013 and the 10th district, which includes [[Wright-Patterson Air Force Base]], since 2013. He was mayor of Dayton from 1994 to 2002 and chaired the House Permanent Select Committee on Intelligence from 2023 to 2025. In UAP Gerb's coverage he is cast as the leading congressional opponent of UAP disclosure legislation.

## Opposition to the Schumer amendment

In "The Alien and UFO Obscure Oddities Iceberg (Level 2)", Gerb names Turner as the "biggest proponent" of blocking the [[Schumer Amendment (NDAA)|Schumer amendment]] to the fiscal year 2024 National Defense Authorization Act, which would have required legacy programs, and any program studying or holding UAP, to hand over their materials and findings. Gerb stresses that Turner's district is the one that contains Wright-Patterson, the base he treats as a hub of the [[Legacy Programs|UFO legacy programs]] from the 1947 [[Roswell Crash]] onward.

In "Special Access Required - the Secrecy of UFO Crash Retrieval Programs Vol.2", discussing how special access programs are reported to Congress, Gerb goes further: he alleges that legacy programs are not reported to congressional elements, "bar a few Congress people here and there on the payroll of legacy programs", and calls Turner likely "one of these thug enforcers".
