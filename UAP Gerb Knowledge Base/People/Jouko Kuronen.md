Jouko Kuronen was the second Finnish Air Force pilot-in-training to witness the April 12, 1969 [[Pori Airport UFO Incident]] over [[Pori Airport]], during a Fouga Magister jet training mission. [[Gerb]] rendered his name "Jau Corona" in the video; it is usually given as Jouko Kuronen.

## Role in the Pori Incident

A [[Finnish Defense Force]] flight controller had sent the first pilot, [[Tarmo Tukeva]], to investigate seven presumed balloons above the airport. Tukeva reported pale yellow, disc-shaped objects "like balls with no extremities" that accelerated away from him against a 180 km/h headwind. Kuronen, according to Gerb, observed the objects as well, confirming their anomalous shape and speed. Radar at [[Vaasa, Finland|Vaasa]], 200 km away, then reportedly detected the seven objects at an estimated 3,218 metres per second.

The incident remains the only UFO observation acknowledged by the [[Finnish Air Force]].
