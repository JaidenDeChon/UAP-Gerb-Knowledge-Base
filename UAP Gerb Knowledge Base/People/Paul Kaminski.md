Paul G. Kaminski is a former senior U.S. defense acquisition official. He was Under Secretary of Defense for Acquisition and Technology from October 3, 1994, to May 16, 1997. UAP research calls this office "Ostat" or "OUSD(AT)," and according to the [[Wilson-Davis Memo]], it is the specific office Vice Admiral [[Thomas Wilson]] investigated in 1997. Kaminski holds an aeronautics doctorate and degrees from the U.S. Air Force Academy, MIT, and Stanford. In his OUSD(AT) role he was a permanent member of the Special Access Program Oversight Committee (SAPOC). More recently he sat on the board of directors of [[MITRE Corporation]] from 2017 to 2025, and he has also served on the boards of Axient and Johns Hopkins Applied Physics Laboratory.

## Role in UAP Programs

UAP Gerb's *Special Access Required Vol.2* identifies Kaminski as the Ostat/OUSD(AT) official in office when Wilson investigated a crash retrieval and reverse engineering program in 1997. Gerb states that he has "direct knowledge" that Kaminski was "a very senior individual within the UFO legacy program portfolio." The video ties Kaminski's SAPOC membership and his later MITRE board seat to its broader thesis that senior officials who oversaw DoD Special Access Program governance in the 1990s went on to hold influential positions at federally funded research and development centers (FFRDCs) alleged to be involved in non-human technology exploitation.
