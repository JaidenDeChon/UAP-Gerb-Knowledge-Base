Donald B. Rice is an American defense official and executive who served as US Secretary of the Air Force from 1989 to 1993, having earlier been president of the RAND Corporation. In UAP research he is remembered for his public denials that the rumored [[Aurora Program|Aurora]] aircraft existed.

## Aurora

In "UFOs in the Private Sector - Lockheed Martin", Gerb cites a 22 December 1992 letter from the British Embassy in Washington to the Assistant Chief of the Air Staff in London about the US reaction to triangular UAP sightings, including one made about three years earlier by a member of the Royal Observer Corps. British officials monitoring that reaction wrote that the inquiries had caused "considerable irritation", and that Rice was, to say the least, incensed by renewed speculation that he had lied to Congress in stating that Aurora did not exist. Gerb uses the letter as one strand of the paper trail he believes links triangular craft to [[Lockheed Martin]] and the US Air Force.
