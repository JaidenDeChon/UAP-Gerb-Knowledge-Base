Vice Admiral Robert Spain is a retired United States Navy officer who served as Commander, Naval Air Force, US Pacific Fleet (COMNAVAIRPAC). Spain authored a pivotal 1995 report proposing that naval carrier tactical aviation could provide critical support to Marine ground forces — a concept that directly contributed to the planning of the 1997 [[Hunter Warrior Advanced Warfighting Experiment]].

## Role in Hunter Warrior

Spain's 1995 report established the doctrinal foundation for integrating Navy strike aviation with Marine expeditionary operations. When the [[Marine Corps Warfighting Laboratory]] organized the Hunter Warrior experiment at [[Marine Corps Air Ground Combat Center 29 Palms]], Spain's concept was tested in practice: naval air crews from [[NAS Lemoore|Naval Air Station Lemoore]] and the [[Naval Aviation Warfare Development Center (NAWDC)]] were brought in as a late adjunct to the primarily Marine exercise, a decision UAP Gerb considers significant because it provided justification for Navy and naval intelligence assets to operate alongside Marine units in the field.

General [[Charles Krulak]], the 31st Commandant of the Marine Corps, was the senior officer who authorized Hunter Warrior. Vice Admiral [[Jay Johnson]] reportedly helped push the naval aviation component into the experiment based on Spain's recommendations.
