Rear Admiral Sumner Shapiro was a senior US Navy intelligence officer who served as Director of Naval Intelligence and, after retiring, joined [[BDM International]]. BDM was a defense contractor that hosted classified Advanced Theoretical Physics working groups, and it was eventually absorbed into [[Northrop Grumman]] through [[TRW|TRW Inc]]. In UAP research Shapiro is known for a 1989 disclosure to NASA mission specialist [[Bob Echler]], in which he said that the United States possessed extraterrestrial vehicles he had personally studied at close quarters and described in detail how those craft were handled.

## 1989 Disclosure to Bob Echler

In 1989, [[Bob Echler]] was introduced to Shapiro by Admiral [[Bobby Ray Inman]], who had served as Director of Naval Intelligence, Director of the NSA, Deputy Director of the CIA, and Director of the classified [[National Underwater Reconnaissance Office]] (NURO). Inman sent Echler to Shapiro under the pretense that Echler was interested in working with the group that managed UFO information. By then Shapiro had retired as Director of Naval Intelligence and was an executive with BDM International.

Shapiro told Echler that factions within the US government and military possessed extraterrestrial vehicles and that he had personally studied one at close quarters. He described in concrete terms how recovered craft were handled:

- Teams would disassemble the craft
- The disassembled components would be packed up and shipped across the country to different laboratories for study
- The craft had unique interlocking components that had to be disassembled in an exact sequence to be put back together
- Shapiro was deliberately careful about what he divulged, and declined to say where vehicles and technology were taken in order to protect classified information

## Second Meeting (1990)

A second meeting was held at Shapiro's home in Virginia in 1990. It ended abruptly when Echler displayed a small hologram of an alien head, trying to draw a link between the technology and its non-human source. Shapiro became visibly upset and paced about his living room. He said he was worried that he had been unable to reach Inman to confirm the purpose of the meeting, and, suspecting that Echler was there under false pretenses, he decided to end the visit. According to Echler, as relayed by author Timothy Good, Shapiro also wondered aloud why Inman had not directed him to the current Director of Naval Intelligence rather than to Shapiro himself.

## Significance

Shapiro's seniority in Naval intelligence and his membership of the BDM board placed him in two overlapping institutional networks that have each been independently implicated in UAP legacy programs: military intelligence and the defense contractor world. Inman's introduction links three figures who are each associated with alleged awareness of UAP programs: Inman (NURO director, SAIC board); Shapiro (Director of Naval Intelligence, BDM); and Echler (NASA mission specialist). The specific operational detail about sequential disassembly of recovered craft is consistent with other witness descriptions of non-human vehicle construction. In the 1980s BDM International, where Shapiro was an executive, hosted a series of Advanced Theoretical Physics working group meetings in which [[Eric A. Walker]] and other senior figures allegedly took part and discussed UFO propulsion physics.
