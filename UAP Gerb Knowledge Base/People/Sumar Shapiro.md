**Rear Admiral Sumner Shapiro** was a senior US Navy intelligence officer who served as Director of Naval Intelligence and later joined the board of directors of [[BDM International]], a defense contractor that hosted classified Advanced Theoretical Physics conferences and was eventually absorbed into [[Northrop Grumman]] through [[TRW Inc]]. He is notable in UAP research for his 1989 disclosure to NASA mission specialist [[Robert Oechsler]] that the United States possesses extraterrestrial vehicles and that he had personally studied one at close quarters.

## 1989 Disclosure to Robert Oechsler

In 1989, NASA mission specialist [[Robert Oechsler]] was introduced to Shapiro by [[Bobby Ray Inman]] — then a board member of [[SAIC]] and former NSA Director and CIA Deputy Director. Shapiro told Oechsler that factions within the US government and military possessed extraterrestrial vehicles and that he had studied one at close quarters. According to Shapiro, teams would disassemble the craft, pack them up, and ship them across the country to different locations. The craft featured unique interlocking components that had to be disassembled in an exact sequence to be put back together. Shapiro was serving as a [[BDM International]] board member at the time of this disclosure.

## Significance

Shapiro's account is significant for several reasons. His Naval intelligence seniority and BDM board membership positioned him within two overlapping institutional networks — military intelligence and the defense contractor world — implicated in UAP legacy programs. His introduction to Oechsler through Inman connects three figures each associated separately with alleged UAP program awareness: Inman (SAIC board, former NSA/CIA; referenced in [[Colonel Steve Wilson]]'s "Star Wars City" org chart as a UAP program manager), Shapiro (Director of Naval Intelligence, BDM), and Oechsler (NASA). The detail about sequential disassembly of recovered craft is consistent with other witness descriptions of non-human vehicle construction.
