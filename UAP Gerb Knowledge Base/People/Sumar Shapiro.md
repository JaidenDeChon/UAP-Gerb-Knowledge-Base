Rear Admiral Sumner Shapiro was a senior [[US Navy]] intelligence officer who served as Director of Naval Intelligence from 1978 to 1982 and, after retiring, joined [[BDM International]]. BDM was a defense contractor that hosted classified Advanced Theoretical Physics working groups, and it was eventually absorbed into [[Northrop Grumman]] through [[TRW|TRW Inc]]. In UAP research Shapiro is known for a 1989 disclosure to NASA mission specialist [[Bob Echler]], in which he reportedly said that the United States possessed extraterrestrial vehicles he had personally studied at close quarters and described in detail how those craft were handled. Gerb's captions and the vault's page title spell his first name "Sumar"; his name was Sumner.

## Career

Shapiro was born in Nashua, New Hampshire, in 1926. He graduated from the US Naval Academy in 1949 and served in the Navy until 1982, rising to rear admiral. A Russian specialist, he directed the [[Office of Naval Intelligence]] from 1978 to 1982, one of the longest tenures in that post; Gerb places his term in the Cold War reorganization era of his legacy-program timeline. In retirement he was vice president for advanced planning at BDM International in McLean, Virginia, and Gerb also describes him as a member of BDM's board. He died at his home in McLean on 14 November 2006, aged 80.

## 1989 Disclosure to Bob Echler

In 1989, [[Bob Echler]] was put in touch with Shapiro by Admiral [[Bobby Ray Inman]], a fellow former Director of Naval Intelligence who had also been Director of the NSA, Deputy Director of the CIA and, by his own admission, Director of the classified [[National Underwater Reconnaissance Office]] (NURO). In the same recorded 1989 phone call, Inman pointed Echler to [[Everett Heinman|R. Everett Heinman]] of the CIA's Directorate of Science and Technology. Echler met Shapiro under the pretense that he was interested in working with the "group" that managed UFO information. By then Shapiro had retired as Director of Naval Intelligence and was an executive with BDM International. Gerb notes that it is unknown whether Shapiro, like Inman, served at NURO.

Shapiro told Echler that factions within the US government possessed extraterrestrial vehicles and that he had personally studied one at close quarters. He described in concrete terms how recovered craft were handled:

- Teams would take the craft apart
- The components would be packed up and shipped around the country to different laboratories for study
- The craft had unique interlocking components that had to be disassembled in an exact sequence to be put back together
- Shapiro was careful about what he divulged, and never said where vehicles and technology were taken, to protect classified information

## Second Meeting (1990)

A second meeting was held at Shapiro's home in Virginia in 1990. It ended abruptly when Echler displayed a small hologram of an alien head, trying to draw a link between the technology and its non-human source. According to Echler, as relayed by author Timothy Good, Shapiro became visibly upset and paced about his living room. He voiced concern that he had been unable to reach Inman to confirm the recommended meeting, wondered aloud why Inman had not directed Echler to the current Director of Naval Intelligence instead, and ended the visit, suspecting that Echler was there under false pretenses.

## Significance

Shapiro's seniority in naval intelligence and his place at BDM put him in two overlapping networks that Gerb ties to UAP legacy programs: military intelligence and the defense contractor world. BDM hosted an advanced theoretical physics conference in 1985, which Gerb says discussed UFO reverse engineering, and [[Albert Stubblebine]] became a BDM vice president after leaving INSCOM. TRW bought BDM in 1997 and Northrop Grumman bought TRW in 2002, a chain Gerb follows in his Northrop Grumman videos. Inman's introductions link three figures each associated with alleged awareness of UAP programs: Inman (NURO director, SAIC and Wackenhut board member), Shapiro (Director of Naval Intelligence, BDM) and Echler (NASA mission specialist).

Gerb repeatedly compares Shapiro's account of sequential disassembly to the 1948 [[Aztec UFO Crash and Recovery|Aztec crash]], where the craft was allegedly taken apart into sections joined by interlocking keys and trucked to Los Alamos, and asks whether Shapiro was describing Aztec. In his Off-World Technologies Division video he also asks whether [[Naval Surface Warfare Center Crane|NSWC Crane]] received one of the shipped components. Gerb says he has tried to contact Shapiro's family, without a reply.
