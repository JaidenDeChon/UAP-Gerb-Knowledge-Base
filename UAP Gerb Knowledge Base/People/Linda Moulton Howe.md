Linda Moulton Howe is an American investigative journalist and documentary producer known for her long-running reporting on UFOs, animal mutilations and related anomalies, beginning with her 1980 documentary *A Strange Harvest* and continuing through her Earthfiles website. She is a frequent speaker at UFO conferences and a recurring secondary source in accounts of alleged government and contractor UFO programs.

## Ben Rich's UCLA remarks

Howe is the principal recorder of the best-known words attributed to [[Ben Rich]], the second director of [[Lockheed Martin Skunk Works]], from his 1993 talk at [[UCLA (University of California, Los Angeles)|UCLA]]: that the means to travel among the stars already exist but are locked up in black projects. In "UFOs in the Private Sector - Lockheed Martin", Gerb notes that no audio or video of Rich saying this exists, that the quote rests on Howe's record, and that she is known to take some reportorial liberties. He adds that the same quote appears in a 2010 [[MUFON]] article by Tom Keller, and that MUFON director [[Jan Harzan]], who attended the talk, confirmed that Rich ended it with the line "we now have the technology to take ET home."

## J. Andrew Kissner's statement

Howe also presented publicly, in 2004, the statement of former New Mexico state representative [[Andrew Kissner]] on UFO crash retrieval and reverse engineering from 1947 to 1994.
