**Glenn Theodore Seaborg** (1912–1999) was an American nuclear chemist who shared the 1951 Nobel Prize in Chemistry for discoveries in the chemistry of the transuranium elements, including plutonium. He chaired the [[Atomic Energy Commission]] from 1961 to 1971 under Presidents Kennedy, Johnson and Nixon.

## In UAP research

UAP Gerb lists Seaborg among the officials summoned to [[Lyndon B. Johnson]]'s Texas residence on 10 December 1965, the day after the [[1965 Kecksburg, Pennsylvania Crash Retrieval|Kecksburg crash]]. The host says he glosses over Seaborg's significance for lack of time, but counts him among the guests whose presence he argues may point to a day of briefings on the crash.
