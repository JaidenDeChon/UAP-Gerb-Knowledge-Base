UAP Gerp (stylized as "UAP gerp" or "UAP Gerb" in some contexts) is a YouTube content creator and UAP researcher who produces detailed analytical videos examining UFO footage, witness testimony, and alleged government UFO programs. The channel focuses on in-depth investigations of both historical and contemporary UAP cases, often spending 15-20+ minutes analyzing brief pieces of footage to provide comprehensive context, corroboration, and technical assessment.

## Analytical Approach

UAP Gerp's methodology emphasizes:
- **Footage authentication and sourcing** — Tracing videos back to original uploaders, submission emails, and witness statements
- **Cross-case corroboration** — Identifying visual similarities and patterns across multiple unrelated sightings
- **Technical analysis** — Evaluating flight characteristics, physics, and craft structures against known aerospace capabilities
- **Skeptical engagement** — Addressing debunk arguments directly and assessing prosaic explanations fairly
- **AATIP framework application** — Using [[Luis Elizondo]]'s five UAP observables as an analytical framework for evaluating footage

## Metapod Investigation

In the Metapod video, UAP Gerp conducted a comprehensive analysis including:
- Tracing the footage origin to [[Modesto]]'s September 7, 2015 email to UFO VNI
- Translating and presenting the full Portuguese forum post from September 8, 2015
- Identifying and cataloging six other global sightings of the same or similar crescent-shaped craft (Peru 1998, Mexico 2006, Vancouver 2021, Texas 2023, alleged 2022 U.S. Navy Blue Angels leak)
- Evaluating the footage against three of AATIP's five UAP characteristics
- Addressing and rebutting multiple debunk theories (balloon, CGI, string-suspended object)
- Correcting misinformation about the footage's origin (Denver vs. Spain)

UAP Gerp characterized the Metapod as "one of my all-time favorite pieces of alleged footage" and expressed fascination with its "shocking connections to other sightings and footage worldwide," while remaining measured about its authenticity and acknowledging it is "too anomalous too clear and too too strange to possibly be true" on first viewing.

## Research Philosophy

UAP Gerp states: "I won't settle for anything less than a full analysis," reflecting a commitment to thoroughness over brevity. The channel aims to contribute to UFO disclosure efforts inspired by figures like [[David Grusch]], with UAP Gerp explicitly citing Grusch's 2023 public revelations as part of a "mission he aims to contribute to."
