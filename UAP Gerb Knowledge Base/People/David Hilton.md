David Hilton is a YouTuber who posted a video in 2013 titled "Edgar Fouche Fake Documents," alleging that [[Edgar Fouché]] fabricated military documents supporting his [[TR-3B]] claims. Hilton stated that after knowing Fouché personally since February 2012, it was his opinion that Fouché "made up/lied about everything." The video was subsequently removed or unlisted from YouTube, along with related videos by [[Jeremy Rys]].

In a June 2012 Skype conversation with UFO researcher [[Dan Benkert]], Hilton made startling remarks claiming "I work for an agency that monitors things you shouldn't do" and "I now control Ed, not you — funny how things work out, isn't it? The exposer becomes a tool of control." Hilton also obtained Fouché's military service documents through FOIA requests, which paradoxically corroborated key elements of Fouché's service history.
