David Hilton is a YouTuber who posted a video in 2013 titled "Edgar Fouche Fake Documents," in which he alleged that [[Edgar Fouché]] fabricated military documents to support his [[TR-3B]] claims. Hilton said he had known Fouché personally since February 2012 and that, in his opinion, Fouché "made up/lied about everything." The video was later removed from YouTube or unlisted, as were related videos by [[Jeremy Rys]].

In a June 2012 Skype conversation with UFO researcher [[Dan Benkert]], Hilton claimed "I work for an agency that monitors things you shouldn't do" and said "I now control Ed, not you — funny how things work out, isn't it? The exposer becomes a tool of control." Hilton also obtained Fouché's military service documents through FOIA requests, although those records ended up corroborating important parts of Fouché's service history.
