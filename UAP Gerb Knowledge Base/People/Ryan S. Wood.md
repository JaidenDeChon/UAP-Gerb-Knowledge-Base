**Ryan S. Wood** is an American author and UAP researcher who has worked extensively on [[Majestic 12]] document authentication and UFO crash retrieval case documentation. He is the author of *Magic Eyes Only*, a comprehensive catalog of alleged UFO crash retrieval incidents, which documents numerous cases including a 1994 crash retrieval at [[Boscombe Down Incident|Boscombe Down]], UK. Wood has collaborated with his father [[Robert Wood]] on MJ-12 research and uses forensic document analysis to assess the authenticity of alleged classified documents.

## Work on MJ-12 Documents

Wood considers the [[Majestic 12 Special Operations Manual]] to be among the most crucial pieces of the MJ-12 document set, alongside crash retrieval case documentation. His forensic document analysis methodology examines paper stock, typewriter characteristics, terminology consistency, and historical context to evaluate whether purported classified documents are authentic or fabricated.

## Coyame Case Assessment

In *Magic Eyes Only*, Wood rates the [[1974 Coyame, Mexico UFO Crash Retrieval]] case as medium to high authenticity based on the quality of the [[Denb Report]], its use of correct military terminology, operational detail consistent with known crash retrieval protocols, and the distinction between hard evidence and speculation. Wood has referenced Tim Cooper's father's citation in MJ-12 research work.
