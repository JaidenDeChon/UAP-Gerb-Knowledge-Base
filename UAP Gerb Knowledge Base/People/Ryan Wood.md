Ryan Wood is an American author and UAP researcher who has worked extensively on [[Majestic 12]] document authentication and UFO crash retrieval case documentation. Wood authored *Magic Eyes Only*, a comprehensive catalog of alleged UFO crash retrieval incidents, which documents a 1994 crash retrieval case at [[Boscombe Down Incident|Boscombe Down]], UK. He has collaborated with his father [[Robert Wood]] on MJ-12 research and referenced Tim Cooper's father's citation in their work.
