Paul Stonehill is a Ukrainian-born UAP researcher and author who has specialized in documenting Soviet and post-Soviet UFO and USO reports that Western researchers could not reach during and after the Cold War. He is best known as co-author, with [[Philip Mantle]], of *Russia's USO Secrets*, the primary English-language compilation of Soviet [[Unidentified Submerged Object (USO)]] cases.

## Russia's USO Secrets

*Russia's USO Secrets*, co-authored with Philip Mantle, is the main published source Gerb uses for Soviet USO cases in his USO case book video. The book compiles case logs from Soviet naval records, scientific expedition reports, and firsthand accounts that were not subject to Western Freedom of Information processes. Among the cases Gerb takes from the book are the 1977 sighting by the Soviet submarine repair ship Vulga in the Barents Sea and the 1970 Kamchatka Lake USO encounter involving Soviet hydrologists.

Stonehill's work matters to UAP researchers because the stigma around UFOs in the Soviet Union was historically as suppressive as American institutional culture, or more so. Without him, the cases he recovered would remain entirely unknown to researchers who do not read Russian.
