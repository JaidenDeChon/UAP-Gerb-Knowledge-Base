Hans Adam II is the Crown Prince of Liechtenstein who has been linked to UAP-related intelligence activities through his alleged funding of [[TREAT]] (Tactical Reconnaissance Engineering Assessment Team). A letter dated August 31, 1997, sent to Dr. [[Steven Greer]] from an unknown sender, discussed Hans Adam's mention in a book by author Tom Bowers titled *Nazi Gold*. The Crown Prince had previously spoken and collaborated with Greer on UAP issues to an unknown degree.

The letter's sender directly accused Hans Adam of supporting counterintelligence operations against the American people on the subject of UFOs, and specifically accused him of funding TREAT. The letter stated: "Adam's funding should be focused on his support for TREAT and its GRD, who is wed to a former US Army General, ex-head of Army Intelligence and Security Command." The general referenced is [[Albert Stubblebine]], founder of the US Army Intelligence and Security Command (INSCOM).
