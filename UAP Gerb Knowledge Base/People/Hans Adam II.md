Hans Adam II has been the reigning Prince of Liechtenstein since November 1989; Gerb calls him the Crown Prince. Gerb links him to UAP-related intelligence activities through his alleged funding of [[TREAT]], which Gerb takes to be the Tactical Reconnaissance Engineering Assessment Team. A letter dated August 31, 1997, sent to Dr. [[Steven Greer]] by an unknown sender, discussed a mention of Hans Adam in *Nazi Gold*, a book by Tom Bower. Hans Adam had earlier spoken and worked with Greer on UAP issues, though to what extent is unknown.

> [!edit] Attribution clarified 17 October 2026
> Earlier versions said as fact that he was linked to intelligence work through funding the Tactical Reconnaissance Engineering Assessment Team. That link is Gerb's reading of the 1997 letter, whose TREAT may instead be Rima Laibow's abduction-trauma conferences.

> [!edit] Corrected 17 October 2026
> Earlier versions gave the author of *Nazi Gold* (1997) as Tom Bowers. The book is by the British journalist Tom Bower.

The letter's sender directly accused Hans Adam of supporting counterintelligence operations against the American people on the subject of UFOs, and specifically of funding TREAT. The letter stated: "Adam's funding should be focused on his support for TREAT and its GRD, who is wed to a former US Army General, ex-head of Army Intelligence and Security Command." Gerb identifies the general as [[Albert Stubblebine]], whom he calls the founder of the US Army Intelligence and Security Command (INSCOM), and reads the letter's TREAT as Edgar Fouché's covert team. The wording also fits a different TREAT: the "Treatment and Research of Experienced Anomalous Trauma" conferences on alien abduction, founded in 1989 by the psychiatrist Rima Laibow, who later married Stubblebine. Hans Adam is reported to have been the major backer of at least one of those conferences.

> [!edit] Attribution clarified 17 October 2026
> Earlier versions stated as fact that the letter referred to Stubblebine and to Fouché's TREAT. Both are Gerb's identifications; Laibow's conferences fit the letter as well (*Baltimore Sun*, 15 April 1992; ISGP's study of Greer's Disclosure Project).
