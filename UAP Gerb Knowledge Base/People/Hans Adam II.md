Hans Adam II is the Crown Prince of Liechtenstein. He has been linked to UAP-related intelligence activities through his alleged funding of [[TREAT]] (Tactical Reconnaissance Engineering Assessment Team). A letter dated August 31, 1997, sent to Dr. [[Steven Greer]] by an unknown sender, discussed a mention of Hans Adam in *Nazi Gold*, a book by Tom Bowers. The Crown Prince had earlier spoken and worked with Greer on UAP issues, though to what extent is unknown.

The letter's sender directly accused Hans Adam of supporting counterintelligence operations against the American people on the subject of UFOs, and specifically of funding TREAT. The letter stated: "Adam's funding should be focused on his support for TREAT and its GRD, who is wed to a former US Army General, ex-head of Army Intelligence and Security Command." The general it refers to is [[Albert Stubblebine]], founder of the US Army Intelligence and Security Command (INSCOM).
