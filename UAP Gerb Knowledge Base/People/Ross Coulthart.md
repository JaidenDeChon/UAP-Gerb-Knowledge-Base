Ross Coulthart is an award-winning Australian investigative journalist and author known for his extensive reporting on unidentified aerial phenomena and alleged government UAP programs. He is a former reporter for Australia's *60 Minutes* and for *The Feed* on SBS, and the author of *In Plain Sight: An Investigation into UFOs and Impossible Science* (2021), which provides a comprehensive overview of UAP evidence and government secrecy.

## UAP Reporting

Coulthart has conducted many interviews with military and intelligence personnel about UAP encounters and legacy programs. He has reported claims that the "Tic Tac" UAP that [[David Fravor]] observed during the 2004 [[2004 Nimitz UAP Encounter (Tic Tac)|Nimitz encounter]] may have originated from [[Lockheed Martin|Lockheed Martin Skunk Works]]. If true, that claim would place the Tic Tac in the category of [[Alien Reproduction Vehicle (ARV)|alien reproduction vehicles]] rather than non-human-origin craft.

Coulthart has also reported on the broader infrastructure of UAP secrecy, including the roles of defense contractors and intelligence agencies in maintaining classification of recovered materials and reverse-engineering programs.

## Vetting of Bob Fish

Coulthart has confirmed that he vetted [[Bob Fish]], the former USG contractor who disclosed information about [[Defense Support Program (DSP)]] satellites tracking [[Fast Walkers]]. Coulthart's vetting lends credibility to Fish's claims about DSP satellites collecting data on objects entering and leaving Earth's atmosphere from deep space.
