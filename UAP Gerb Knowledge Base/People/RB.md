RB is the pseudonym of a former U.S. Marine Corporal who claimed that in December 1963 he spent two weeks guarding a recovered disc-shaped craft of non-human origin at an undisclosed military base. His account is documented in [[Leonard Stringfield]]'s *UFO Crash/Retrieval Status Report 5* under the case title "Marine Guards a Disc-Shaped Vehicle, 1963." RB served in the [[United States Marine Corps]] from 1960 to 1966 as a weapons technician, held an interim Top Secret security clearance, and has never gone public with his account or sought profit from it.

## Account

RB's story came to [[Leonard Stringfield]] through UFO researcher [[Michael Johnstone]], who arranged contact between the two. In the 2000s, aerospace historian [[Michael Schratt]] was granted access to Stringfield's personal archives at [[MUFON]] in Cincinnati, Ohio, and reconstructed the full account. Schratt interviewed Johnstone again in 2015 to gather additional details.

In December 1963, RB boarded a windowless plane at Cherry Point, North Carolina Marine Corps Air Station and was flown approximately three hours to an undisclosed location — a range that researchers have noted is consistent with [[Wright-Patterson Air Force Base]]. At the destination, a large metal building housed the craft.

### Craft Description

RB described the vehicle as:

- 40 feet in diameter, totally symmetrical, with an ooid (lens-like) cross-section
- Silverish in appearance, resembling polished aluminum
- Completely seamless except for a hatch-like seam on the lower surface — "you could not get a razor blade in the crack"
- Elevated five feet off the ground on scaffolding
- No visible landing gear, fins, or propulsion system
- Apparent smoked-glass windows spaced one foot apart around the rim, opaque even at close range

A technical crew was actively attempting to gain entry. RB observed drilling and torch cutting near the hatch that left the hull entirely undamaged: "Metal got hot under the torch but when it cooled you could wipe off the soot and the surface remained clean and undamaged." A high-powered laser powered by two semi-sized trailer generator vans also completely deflected off the craft, damaging the ceiling of the building. Civilian scientists working around the craft made references to "aliens," leading RB to conclude it was not of human manufacture. The construction material was described as seamless in a manner compared to the ancient stonework at Machu Picchu, where a razor blade cannot be inserted between joints.

### Security Protocols

Access to the craft was controlled through a color-coded badge system. Different colors granted access to designated sections of the vehicle; a red badge conferred total access. A white-taped circle surrounded the craft that no unauthorized person — including USAF generals — could cross. RB stated he personally stopped the Secretary of the Navy from crossing this perimeter during his guard duty. Three to four other guards shared the post with him.

### Other Details

During his two-week assignment, RB used a small German pocket camera to photograph the craft. The image was later reported lost in a flood around 1983. On one occasion, he observed the craft being placed on a flatbed trailer for transport to another base for "security reasons." RB heard secondhand accounts from others that recovered non-human bodies — approximately 39 inches tall with no thumbs — were stored in an underground base in a western U.S. state; RB made no direct claims of seeing bodies himself.

Upon leaving the Marines, RB signed a security oath carrying a ten-year prison sentence and $110,000 fine for violations.

## Connection to Other Evidence

[[Barry Goldwater]]'s documented attempts to access the "[[Blue Room]]" at [[Wright-Patterson Air Force Base]] — a classified facility alleged to store recovered UFO materials — are presented in conjunction with RB's account. UAP Gerb speculates that the facility RB guarded may be the same location Goldwater was denied access to by General [[Curtis LeMay]].

RB is believed to correspond to redacted witness number 10138 on [[Steven Greer]]'s Disclosure Project witness list. Whether RB ever spoke directly with Greer is unknown. RB's wife reportedly did not believe his account until she personally saw the photograph of the craft, before it was lost.
