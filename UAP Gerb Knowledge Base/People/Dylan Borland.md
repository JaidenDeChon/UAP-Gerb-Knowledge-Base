Dylan Borland is a U.S. Air Force veteran and UAP whistleblower. A geospatial intelligence specialist in the Air Force from 2010 to 2013, he later worked as an analyst at the [[Defense Threat Reduction Agency (DTRA)|Defense Threat Reduction Agency]]. On 9 September 2025 he testified under oath to a House Oversight task force that in 2012, while stationed at [[Langley Air Force Base]], he watched a silent triangular craft hover about 100 feet above him ([[Borland's Triangular Craft Sighting]]). He has said that reporting it cost him his career in intelligence. Gerb calls him a "whistleblower and American hero", and in his *Special Access Required* video declares: "I stand with Dylan Borland."

## Career

Borland served in the [[US Air Force]] from 2010 to 2013 as a 1N1 geospatial intelligence specialist (imagery analyst) and held a top-secret clearance. He was living in the barracks at Langley, on three months of night work, at the time of the 2012 sighting. He later went to DTRA, where, he told the *Weaponized* podcast, he worked for a while as an all-source analyst. He says that after he reported the sighting he was blocked from clearances and shut out of intelligence work for more than ten years.

## The Langley Triangle

Borland testified that he was on the telephone at a smoking area outside the barracks, looking toward the flight line, when a white light rose and stopped about 100 feet in the air. As he walked toward it the light flew across the base toward him, and "a triangle manifests around the light". He said he could not tell whether this was active camouflage. The craft stopped about 100 feet in front of him and about 100 feet above him, and his phone became extremely hot and froze. It was an equilateral triangle one to two stories thick, with a light on each corner and a larger light in the centre, two to three times the size of the others. Its body was "black metallic flake paint", with "this gold, lava, plasma, some type of fluid going over and around the craft". After two to three minutes the centre light flashed two or three times and the craft shot up without a sound to at least the height of a commercial jet. He felt static electricity all over his body and smelled what he described as the smell after a strong summer thunderstorm.

Asked whether the triangle was US government technology, he said he had once thought so, but "knowing what I know now, I'll have to answer that question in a SCIF probably", and that whoever wants the answer should go to [[AARO]]. He said he did not believe it belonged to a foreign government.

## Gerb's Reading

Gerb notes that Borland was asked about the craft's *technology*, not the airframe. In his NRO video he argues that the triangle was a human-built airframe, perhaps inspired by non-human flight principles, carrying an electro-optic cloaking system derived from non-human craft, with the flowing gold surface a by-product of that cloaking. He compares it with the shimmering field [[Jonathan Weygandt]] described around the craft in Peru in 1997, and with the electrochromatic coating [[Edgar Fouche]] attributed to the [[TR-3B]], and asks whether such a triangle flies for the CIA, NSA and NRO. In his [[Hidden Wing]] video he places the sighting over the NASA hangar at Langley, home of [[Air Combat Command]], and suggests Borland saw elements of Air Combat Command testing either an alien reproduction vehicle or a human-made triangle with a derivative cloaking system. He also suggests that the operation could fall under the Air Force's [[SAFAHA|SAF/AAH]] (Sensitive Activities) office.

## DTRA and the Atomic Energy Commission Files

Borland's account of old [[Atomic Energy Commission]] files at DTRA led Gerb to look into the agency's ancestry. In his Sandia video he traces DTRA back to the [[Armed Forces Special Weapons Project (AFSWP)]], which sat at Sandia Base and which the disputed IPU documents place at the 1947 recoveries, and argues that this makes Borland's files less surprising. In his TRW video he wonders whether [[Edward Bushnell Doll]], who was technical director for AFSWP programs, and the 1953 Kingman case appear in them. In *Manhattan Project 2.0* he cites the files as the kind of record, classified under the [[Atomic Energy Act of 1954]], that a presidential order to release UFO files would not reach.

## Reprisals

Gerb counts Borland among the whistleblowers who have faced what he calls administrative terrorism. In his Hidden Wing video he says he strongly suspects that reprisals against Borland, like those against [[David Grusch]], came at least partly from program protection offices such as the DARPA Security and Intelligence Directorate and the AFOSI Office of Special Projects (PJ) under [[Terry Phillips]].
