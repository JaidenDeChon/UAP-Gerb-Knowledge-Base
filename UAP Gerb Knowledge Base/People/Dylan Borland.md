Dylan Borland is a U.S. Air Force veteran and military whistleblower who publicly testified to witnessing an anomalous triangular craft while stationed at [[Langley Air Force Base]] around 2012. His is one of the more detailed first-person accounts from a military witness in UAP disclosure, with specific descriptions of the craft's appearance, how it flew and what appeared to be its technology. Borland has also said he believes [[AARO]] would have answers about where the craft came from and what it was.

## Role in UAP Programs

Borland testified that while on duty at Langley AFB he saw a large, equilateral triangular craft with black metallic flake paint and a gold, lava-like plasma fluid flowing over its surface. The craft flew silently, hovered over a NASA hangar on the base, and appeared to use an electro-optic cloaking system. When it left, Borland reported feeling static electricity and noticing a strong odor like that of a thunderstorm. In "The Hidden Wing", Gerb cites Borland's account as consistent with alleged test and evaluation of derivative or reverse-engineered non-human airframes under the [[Hidden Wing]] program portfolio, possibly by assets operating out of the [[412th Test Wing|Edwards 412th Test Wing]].
