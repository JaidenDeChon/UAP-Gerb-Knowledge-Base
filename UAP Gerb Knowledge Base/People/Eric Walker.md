Dr. Eric A. Walker was an American engineer and administrator who served as president of Penn State University and is an alleged member of [[Majestic 12]] (MJ-12). Walker self-admitted in recorded phone conversations that he oversaw the crash site of the 1965 [[Kecksburg UFO Incident|Kecksburg crash]], one of the most well-documented UFO retrieval cases in American history.

Walker had previously established Penn State's University Affiliated Research Center (UARC), the Applied Research Laboratory, at the behest of the US Navy. This connection between UARCs and UFO crash retrievals was highlighted by UAP Gerb when noting that [[AARO]], under the leadership of Dr. Klosi, admitted to working alongside UARCs and [[FFRDC (Federally Funded Research and Development Centers)|FFRDCs]] — the same categories of institutions implicated in the management of UFO legacy programs.
