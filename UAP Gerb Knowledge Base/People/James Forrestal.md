**James Vincent Forrestal** (1892–1949) was the last cabinet-level Secretary of the Navy and the first United States Secretary of Defense, serving from September 1947 until March 1949. He was a founding member of the [[National Security Council]] created by the 1947 National Security Act. In UFO research he is best known as an alleged original member of [[Majestic 12]], the secret group said to have been created to manage recovered craft after the [[Roswell Crash]], and for his death on 22 May 1949 after a fall from Bethesda Naval Hospital, which the official record calls suicide and some UFO researchers, including UAP Gerb, suspect was murder.

## Role in the early legacy programs

On 29 January 1947, as Navy Secretary, Forrestal and Secretary of War Robert P. Patterson established the [[Armed Forces Special Weapons Project (AFSWP)|Armed Forces Special Weapons Project]], a joint Army and Navy body to manage nuclear weapons after the [[Atomic Energy Commission]] succeeded the [[Manhattan Project]]. UAP Gerb argues AFSWP was dual-hatted between the atomic program and the early UFO effort he calls the [[Manhattan Project 2.0]].

The Majestic 12 documents place Forrestal at the group's founding. The Eisenhower Briefing Document, dated 18 November 1952, includes a memo from [[Harry Truman]] dated 24 September 1947 authorizing Forrestal and [[Vannevar Bush]] to proceed with "Operation Majestic 12"; Truman's appointment calendar confirms that Truman, Forrestal and Bush met that day. An alleged Interplanetary Phenomenon Unit report of 22 July 1947 says Forrestal was to be briefed on aspects of the crashed craft, though [[George C. Marshall]] was the only cabinet member who knew fully.

## Death

Forrestal left office in March 1949 and died on 22 May 1949 after falling from a window of Bethesda Naval Hospital, where he was being treated for depression. The Eisenhower Briefing Document says General Walter Bedell Smith replaced him in Majestic 12 on 1 August 1950, and Truman's calendar records an off-record meeting with Smith that day. Some researchers allege Forrestal was pressing for openness about UFOs; a Majestic document is said to describe his death as "necessary and regrettable," and [[Ryan S. Wood]]'s book *Magic Eyes Only* is dedicated in part to him.
