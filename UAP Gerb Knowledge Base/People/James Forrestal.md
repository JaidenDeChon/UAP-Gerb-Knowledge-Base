**James Vincent Forrestal** (1892–1949) was the last cabinet-level Secretary of the Navy and the first United States Secretary of Defense, serving from September 1947 until March 1949. He was a founding member of the [[National Security Council]] created by the 1947 National Security Act. In UFO research he is best known as an alleged original member of [[Majestic 12]], the secret group said to have been created to manage recovered craft after the [[Roswell Crash]]. He is also known for his death on 22 May 1949 after a fall from Bethesda Naval Hospital. The official record calls it suicide; some UFO researchers suspect murder, and Gerb calls him a "likely murder victim".

## Career

Forrestal was born on 15 February 1892. After Princeton he joined the Wall Street firm that became Dillon, Read & Co. in 1916, served as a naval aviator in the First World War, and became the firm's president in 1937. He entered government in 1940 as Under Secretary of the Navy, and on the death of Frank Knox in 1944 became Secretary of the Navy. When the National Security Act created the new defense establishment in 1947, [[Harry Truman]] made him its first Secretary of Defense. He resigned in March 1949 and died two months later.

## Role in the Early Legacy Programs

On 29 January 1947, as Navy Secretary, Forrestal and Secretary of War Robert P. Patterson established the [[Armed Forces Special Weapons Project (AFSWP)|Armed Forces Special Weapons Project]], a joint Army and Navy body to manage nuclear weapons after the [[Atomic Energy Commission]] succeeded the [[Manhattan Project]]. AFSWP and the Los Alamos weapons division set up at Sandia Base, now part of [[Sandia National Laboratories|Sandia]]'s home at Kirtland. Gerb argues that AFSWP served both the atomic program and the early UFO effort he calls the [[Manhattan Project 2.0]], and notes that AFSWP recurs in every early crash-retrieval source he examines. In his video on the [[Aztec UFO Crash and Recovery|Aztec crash]], he floats the idea that AFSWP handled the Roswell and Aztec wreckage.

## Majestic 12

The [[Majestic 12]] documents place Forrestal at the group's founding. The [[Eisenhower Briefing Document]], dated 18 November 1952, says Operation Majestic 12 was set up by a classified executive order of [[Harry Truman]] on 24 September 1947, on the recommendation of [[Vannevar Bush]] and Forrestal. It includes a Truman memo of that date authorizing Forrestal and Bush to proceed. Truman's appointment records show that he did meet Forrestal and Bush that day, at Forrestal's suggestion, one of several parallels with the historical record that make the document "somewhat convincing" to Gerb. [[Stanton Friedman]] judged the Truman–Forrestal memo "almost certainly genuine". [[Ryan S. Wood]] says the documents show it was really Forrestal and Bush who came together to form the group.

An alleged [[Interplanetary Phenomenon Unit]] report of 22 July 1947 says [[George C. Marshall]] was the only cabinet member who knew of the crashed UFOs, but that Forrestal would be briefed on certain aspects.

## Death

Forrestal left office in March 1949 and died on 22 May 1949 after falling from a window of Bethesda Naval Hospital, where he was being treated for depression. The Eisenhower Briefing Document says General [[Walter Bedell Smith]] replaced him in Majestic 12 on 1 August 1950, and Truman's calendar records an off-the-record meeting with Smith that day. According to Wood, Forrestal favoured more openness on the subject, and one Majestic document calls his death "necessary and regrettable". Wood's book *Magic Eyes Only* carries a dedication to those who lost their lives for the sake of UFO secrecy, and Wood counts Forrestal among them. Gerb says much more work remains to be done on Forrestal's death and Majestic 12.
