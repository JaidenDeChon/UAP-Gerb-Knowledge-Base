Karl Nell is a retired United States Army Colonel who also served as Deputy Chief Technology Officer at [[Northrop Grumman]] and later as a Colonel in Army Futures Command. He has emerged as a prominent and credentialed voice in the UAP disclosure movement, having testified before Congress and made statements publicly affirming the reality of non-human intelligence and the existence of classified UAP programs.

## Key Statements on UAP Physics

Nell has publicly rejected the mainstream scientific consensus that faster-than-light travel is impossible, stating: "The second point is faster-than-light travel. Everybody says faster-than-light travel is not possible. This is false." He referenced the work of Miguel Alcubierre — a postgraduate student at the University of Mexico who in 1994 solved Einstein's equations for an effective faster-than-light travel mechanism (the Alcubierre warp drive) — noting that NASA has investigated this solution and that it is widely accepted as mathematically valid by physicists. Nell's statement that faster-than-light travel is theoretically achievable through spacetime manipulation is cited by [[Mark McCandlish]] and UAP Gerb as consistent with the claimed capabilities of [[Alien Reproduction Vehicle (ARV)|ARV Flux Liner craft]] described at the 1988 Norton Air Force Base exhibit.

## Role in UAP Disclosure

Nell has been associated with the [[Sol Foundation]], an organization bridging scientific and policy communities around UAP disclosure. His background at Northrop Grumman — a company UAP researchers have directly implicated in the construction of classified anti-gravity craft including the [[TR-3B]] and the Great Pumpkin anti-gravity disc — lends particular weight to his statements.
