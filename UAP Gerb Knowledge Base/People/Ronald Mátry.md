Ronald Mátry served as Under Secretary of Defense for Intelligence and Security (USD I&S) in the Biden Administration, a senior position overseeing the All-domain Anomaly Resolution Office ([[AARO]]) and its director [[Sean Kirkpatrick]]. UAP researchers have identified Mátry as a central figure in the US government's effort to deny and minimize UAP crash retrieval claims, characterizing him as a "gatekeeper" of UAP legacy programs.

## Role in UAP Denial Effort

At the 2022 Congressional UAP hearings, Mátry and Kirkpatrick denied the existence of UAP crash recoveries and reverse engineering programs. UAP Gerb and other researchers have characterized these denials as coordinated disinformation. Mátry has been described as operating in concert with Kirkpatrick to spread misinformation about UAP at official government hearings.

## Connections to the Carlyle Group

Mátry has notable ties to the [[Carlyle Group]], a private equity firm with extensive defense industry investments and a former ownership stake in [[EG&G]]:
- He served as a board member of iCapital, a Carlyle-invested group.
- He served on the board of directors for the Better Angels Society, an organization funded by Carlyle Group founder David Rubenstein.

These connections link Mátry to the same private equity network that, under [[Frank Carlucci]]'s chairmanship, oversaw EG&G during the same period that EG&G's parking lot hosted the Wilson-Davis meeting — a document central to UAP legacy program claims.
