Richard Mingus is a former Federal Services security guard who worked at the [[Nevada Test Site]] and frequently served at [[Area 51|Groom Lake (Area 51)]]. In UAP lore he is known for his firsthand account of a 1982 security incident at Area 6 of the Nevada Test Site that was escalated all the way to the President of the United States.

## 1982 Area 6 Incident

In 1982, while an underground nuclear test area at Area 6 of the Nevada Test Site was being secured, the base came under attack by armed combatants at a moment when a live nuclear weapon was exposed above ground. The security breach was treated as a critical national security event and was escalated through the chain of command to President Ronald Reagan. It was later determined that [[Wackenhut Corporation|Wackenhut]] personnel had caused the incident by conducting an unauthorized or poorly coordinated security drill. The finding exposed the sometimes dangerous overlap between Wackenhut's private security operations and active nuclear weapons handling at the Test Site.

## Significance

Mingus's account shows the complex security arrangements at Nevada Test Site facilities, where private contractors such as Wackenhut worked alongside government personnel near nuclear materials and, allegedly, near UFO legacy program operations. His testimony has been cited in discussions of how private security forces operated with significant autonomy at classified installations.
