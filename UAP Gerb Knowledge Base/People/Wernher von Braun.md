Wernher von Braun (1912–1977) was a German-American aerospace engineer who led the development of the V-2 rocket for Nazi Germany during World War II and was subsequently transferred to the United States under [[Operation Paperclip]]. He commanded the Army Ballistic Missile Agency, which according to a 1973 declassified ARPA historical evaluation was considered for transfer into the [[Defense Advanced Research Center (DARS)|Defense Advanced Research Center (DARC)]] — the same underground facility at [[Area 51]] where [[Edgar Fouché]] later worked on reverse-engineered craft technologies.

Von Braun's mentor was [[Hermann Oberth]], the father of German rocketry, who performed extensive study on UFOs. The connection between von Braun's Army Ballistic Missile Agency and the proposed DARC suggests institutional continuity between early US rocketry programs and later classified aerospace efforts.
