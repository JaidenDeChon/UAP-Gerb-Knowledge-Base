Arthur McQuitty was managing editor of the Roswell Morning Dispatch at the time of the 1947 Roswell incident. He matters to Roswell historiography because he later signed an affidavit confirming receipt of Lieutenant Walter Haut's original press release announcing the recovery of a "flying disc," followed by a call from military authorities retracting it.

## Press-Chain Corroboration

McQuitty's account supports the claim that the official Roswell story changed within hours. The military first issued a statement implying it had recovered an anomalous craft, then, in a coordinated reversal, explained the object as a weather balloon.

## Historical Importance

Because McQuitty was a civilian in a newsroom and not a military insider, his account corroborates the testimony of Walter Haut, Jesse Marcel, and Thomas J. DuBose from outside the military.
