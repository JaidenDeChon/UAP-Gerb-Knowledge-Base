Hillary Clinton is an American politician and former First Lady. She represented New York in the US Senate from 2001 to 2009 and was Secretary of State from 2009 to 2013. She won the Democratic presidential nomination in July 2016 and lost the general election to Donald Trump on 8 November 2016. Gerb's videos mention her mainly as the candidate he believes a group inside the government meant to present as a "disclosure president".

## The "disclosure president" effort

In Gerb's account, the [[AATIP (Advanced Aerospace Threat Identification Program)|AATIP]] effort that ran until 2017 was partly a cover for National Security Council work on a partial disclosure of the UFO subject, with "top cover" from Director of National Intelligence [[James Clapper]]. The planned disclosure would have framed UFOs as a national security issue and acknowledged that "we are not alone", while saying nothing about crash retrievals, reverse engineering or the legacy programs. Its aim, Gerb says, was to establish Clinton as the disclosure president.

Gerb names [[Tom DeLonge]]'s [[To The Stars Academy]] as the public vehicle for this effort. [[Luis Elizondo]] joined it in 2017, and its advisers included her campaign chairman [[John Podesta]], [[Chris Mellon]], [[Hal Puthoff]] and Major General [[Neil McCasland]]. Gerb also quotes a message from DeLonge to Podesta saying that McCasland would publicly present himself as a skeptic while being more open in private.

When [[David Grusch]] spoke in January 2026 of Obama-era steps to help a particular presidential candidate if the subject became normal to talk about, Gerb identified that candidate as Clinton and said he believed Clapper, [[Stephanie O'Sullivan]] and Elizondo were involved. In Gerb's telling, Clinton's defeat ended the plan: "Hillary Clinton wasn't elected. TTSA failed." He says the effort later changed shape and came back as *[[The Age of Disclosure]]*.

## Campaign emails

Gerb also draws on the campaign emails released by [[WikiLeaks]] in 2016. One of them is the March 2015 message from government contractor [[Bob Fish]] to Podesta stating that "one of the government programs that collects hard data on unidentified flying objects is the USAF DSP satellite program" (see [[Bob Fish Email to John Podesta]] and [[Defense Support Program (DSP)]]).
