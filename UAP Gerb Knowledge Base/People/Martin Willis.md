Martin Willis is an American podcast host who covers UAP and paranormal topics. In 2023, Willis hosted [[Jonathan Wagant]] on his program for Wagant's second public appearance discussing his 1997 [[Peru UFO Crash Incident]] encounter — the first since Wagant's original 2000 testimony with [[Steven Greer]]. However, the 2023 appearance reportedly explored little new ground beyond the original testimony, and Wagant appeared uncomfortable on camera. UAP Gerb noted that the interview did not shed significant new light on the details of the encounter.
