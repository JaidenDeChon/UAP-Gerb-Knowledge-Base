Martin Willis is an American podcast host who covers UAP and paranormal topics. In 2023 Willis hosted [[Jonathan Weygandt]] on his program. It was Weygandt's second public appearance discussing his 1997 [[Peru UFO Crash Incident]] encounter, and his first since his original 2000 testimony with [[Steven Greer]]. The 2023 appearance reportedly covered little new ground beyond the original testimony, and Weygandt appeared uncomfortable on camera. Gerb noted that the interview did not shed significant new light on the details of the encounter.
