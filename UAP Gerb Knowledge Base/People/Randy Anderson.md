Randy Anderson is a former 18 Bravo US Army Special Forces (Green Beret) weapons sergeant and a personal acquaintance of Gerb. He is cited in connection with alleged off-world technology programs at underground facilities. His testimony concerns the [[Naval Surface Warfare Center Crane|Naval Surface Warfare Center (NSWC) Crane]] in Indiana, where he alleged that an "Off-World Technologies Division" exists in an underground facility beneath the base. This testimony is one of the primary sources for Gerb's investigation into the off-world technologies operations at NSWC Crane.

An earlier description of Anderson as having "seen triangular craft at Area 51" may reflect a separate part of his witness testimony or a misattribution from earlier research. UAP Gerb's DUMBs video places his most significant disclosure at NSWC Crane, not Area 51.

## NSWC Crane Underground Facility Account

In the early-to-mid 2010s, Anderson was sent to NSWC Crane for an advanced weapons training course, where he carried out munitions testing involving experimental Russian ballistics and other advanced weaponry. Anderson stated that during this assignment an unnamed contractor took him and another highly cleared Green Beret to an underground location beneath NSWC Crane, a working site the contractor called the "Off-World Technologies Division."

While underground, Anderson reported seeing a spherical object roughly the size of a basketball that was said to interact with consciousness, and a gauntlet-type device covered in glyphic writing. Contractor personnel told Anderson the objects had been recovered, and that the gauntlet in particular was considered a weapon because of an energetic discharge that occurred during its recovery. Gerb assesses that the unnamed contractor was likely [[SAIC]], and ties this to SAIC's broader alleged role in absorbing legacy reverse-engineering research portfolios.

Gerb has stated that around the time of his first interview with Anderson, individuals identifying themselves as CIA personnel reached out to warn him to be wary of discussing NSWC Crane.
