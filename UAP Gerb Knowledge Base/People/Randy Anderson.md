Randy Anderson is a UAP witness cited by UAP Gerb in connection with alleged off-world technology programs at underground facilities. Anderson's testimony specifically concerns the [[Naval Surface Warfare Center Crane, Indiana|Naval Surface Warfare Center (NSWC) Crane]] in Indiana, where he alleged the existence of an "Off-World Technologies Division" housed in an underground facility beneath the base. This testimony is one of the primary sources for UAP Gerb's investigation into the off-world technologies operations at NSWC Crane.

Anderson's earlier characterization as having "seen triangular craft at Area 51" may reflect a separate component of his witness testimony or a misattribution from earlier research — UAP Gerb's DUMBs video places his most significant disclosure at NSWC Crane rather than Area 51.
