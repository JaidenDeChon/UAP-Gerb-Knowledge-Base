Randy Anderson is a former US Army Special Forces (Green Beret) 18 Bravo weapons sergeant and a personal friend of Gerb. He says that during an advanced weapons course at [[Naval Surface Warfare Center Crane|Naval Surface Warfare Center (NSWC) Crane]] in Indiana, a contractor took him to an underground site called the "[[Off-World Technologies Division]]" and showed him two recovered objects. His account is one of the main sources for Gerb's investigations into NSWC Crane and into [[SAIC]], which Gerb believes ran the division. Anderson has also told Gerb that he saw triangular craft operating on Department of Energy land near [[Area 51]].

Gerb first presented his testimony on 17 July 2024 without naming him, as "[[RA]]", and his Dugway Proving Ground video calls it "the testimony of RA or we now know Randy Anderson". The RA page covers that anonymous account. Anderson later told his story under his own name to [[Jesse Michels]] on the *American Alchemy* channel, and Gerb took his first video down so that the interview could reach a wider audience.

## NSWC Crane Underground Facility Account

In the early-to-mid 2010s (between 2013 and 2015 in Gerb's first video, 2012 to 2015 in his later SAIC video), Anderson was sent to NSWC Crane with fellow Green Berets for an advanced weapons training course, where he carried out munitions testing involving experimental Russian ballistics and other advanced foreign and domestic weaponry. Of the roughly ten soldiers in the class, he and one other held TS/SCI clearances. Anderson states that the contractor leading the course, a former military man in civilian clothes, took the two of them to a pristine underground location beneath NSWC Crane and told them "nonchalantly" that they were entering the "off-world technologies division". Gerb says he vetted Anderson's service through his certifications and DD214.

Instead of a firing range, the site was an observation room above a laboratory, where one other contractor was dressed like a lab technician. Anderson reported seeing a seamless metal sphere roughly the size of a basketball that was said to interact with consciousness, and a gauntlet-type device projecting glyph-like writing; he could not tell whether the projection was physical or in his mind, and he has drawn the writing for Gerb. Contractor personnel told Anderson the objects had been recovered, and that the gauntlet in particular was considered a weapon because of an energetic discharge that occurred during its recovery. Anderson still describes a mental haze and memory problems around the visit. Gerb thinks the unnamed contractor was probably [[SAIC]], and ties this to SAIC's alleged wider role in absorbing legacy reverse-engineering research portfolios. He later compared the gauntlet's treatment as a weapon with the "Red Team" handling of functional devices in the Majestic-12 documents (in [[Videos/The Majestic-12 Documents With Ryan S. Wood/summary|his video with Ryan S. Wood]]), and in his [[Sandia National Laboratories|Sandia]] video with the 1947 classification of the special weapons aboard flying discs as enemy weapon systems.

Gerb has stated that around the time of his first interview with Anderson, "some CIA spooks" reached out to warn him to be wary of discussing the naval support activity. He was also criticized for naming a possible underground site, which is why his later [[Dugway Proving Ground]] video stresses that it uses only public information. Crane recurs in Gerb's later work: his [[Videos/Deep Underground Military Bases (D.U.M.Bs.) - UFO Legacy Programs/summary|DUMBs]], US Navy and [[MITRE Corporation|MITRE]] videos all return to Anderson's account, the last as one of the sites Gerb says holds and analyses hardware for the MITRE program.

## Triangles near Area 51

In an interview with Gerb, Anderson said that while carrying out operations at [[Nellis Air Force Base|Nellis]] and on nearby land owned by the Department of Energy around Area 51, he saw triangular craft that Gerb describes as running on electrogravitic propulsion. Gerb cites this in his videos on [[Mark McCandlish]], on [[Rodrik Castle]]'s 1997 encounter at 29 Palms, and on Sandia, where he reasons that the DOE land was the Nevada Test Site, much of it operated by Sandia, and that the sightings lend some weight to [[Edgar Fouche]]'s claims about the [[TR-3B]].
