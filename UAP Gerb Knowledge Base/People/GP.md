"GP" is the pseudonym of a senior Department of Defense chemist working in legacy UAP operations at Dugway Proving Ground, both in above-ground facilities and within the alleged deep underground military base (DUMB). GP served as a critical source for witness "MS" following MS's 2009 encounter with an alleged extraterrestrial craft at Avery Technical Center, confirming the craft's extraterrestrial origin, explaining its gravity displacement propulsion system and wormhole navigation capabilities, and disclosing extensive details about Dugway's 1,300-square-mile subterranean facility connected to other underground installations nationwide via maglev rail systems. GP was a DOD chemist by degree who was promoted and transferred to Dugway specifically to work in UAP legacy programs. MS lost contact with GP when MS left C Martin Company in 2014, though efforts to reconnect are ongoing.

## Background and Role at Dugway

GP holds a doctorate (or advanced degree) in chemistry and served in Department of Defense chemical research before being promoted and moved to Dugway Proving Ground to work in classified UAP legacy programs. His designation as a "senior chemist" suggests significant experience and expertise in chemical analysis, materials science, or related disciplines critical to understanding extraterrestrial technology and materials.

According to MS, GP worked "in Legacy operations at Dugway" with access to both above-ground facilities and the alleged deep underground military base beneath Granite Peak. GP's role as a chemist in UAP legacy programs is particularly notable given Dugway's designation as the nation's premier chemical and biological weapons testing facility, suggesting his expertise may relate to:

- Chemical composition and analysis of non-human craft materials and alloys
- Propulsion system chemistry (analyzing the conical "reactor" components observed by MS)
- Biological or chemical aspects of extraterrestrial technology
- Chemical processes involved in gravity displacement or exotic propulsion
- Analysis of non-human biologics (if present at Dugway)

GP's position within legacy operations indicates he was read into highly classified Special Access Programs (SAPs) or compartmented programs related to technologies of unknown origin.

## Contact with MS and Disclosures

MS states he "reached out to him and got him to talk about my situation by accident," suggesting MS made contact with GP through Dugway-affiliated channels following his 2009 encounter at Avery Technical Center. GP's willingness to discuss classified UAP programs with MS—even after a six-hour interrogation by men in black suits—indicates either significant trust in MS or recognition that MS had already been exposed to information that could not be "un-seen."

**Confirmations and Technical Disclosures to MS:**

**Craft Origin and Propulsion:** GP confirmed the disc craft MS observed was extraterrestrial in origin and operated via "gravity displacement" propulsion. This propulsion mechanism aligns with numerous alleged UAP propulsion theories involving manipulation of gravitational fields rather than conventional thrust-based systems.

**Wormhole Navigation System:** GP explained the flat-screen electronic mapping display MS observed near the craft. According to GP, the system operates by allowing operators to input two reference points (coordinates), which the craft then uses to "generate a wormhole and drop through"—enabling instantaneous or near-instantaneous point-to-point travel across interstellar distances. GP stated: "that's where he says that interstellar travel is how they get from point A to point B so quickly."

This description suggests GP had direct knowledge of not only the craft's propulsion but also its navigation and translocation capabilities—highly compartmented information unlikely to be known by peripheral personnel.

**Deep Underground Military Base:** GP disclosed that a massive subterranean facility exists beneath Dugway Proving Ground, spanning approximately 1,300 square miles (matching the surface facility's footprint) at an undisclosed depth, located primarily beneath Granite Peak. 

**Underground Transportation Network:** GP revealed that a light rail system connects the Dugway DUMB to "every base in the nation," operating via magnetic levitation (maglev) technology. The tunnels are wide enough to transport enormous aircraft including a Boeing 747. GP specifically stated the rail system "can transport the president's plane basically anywhere they need to without putting it in the air"—suggesting continuity of government (COG) functionality and that the underground network serves strategic defense purposes beyond UAP operations.

**Entrance Location:** GP showed MS the location of the DUMB entrance—a small, inconspicuous building in the middle of the West Desert Test Center near German Village, functioning solely as an elevator shaft for personnel access. GP explained that all logistics, supplies, equipment, and materiel for the underground facility are supplied internally through tunnel connections to other facilities, not through the surface entrance.

The depth of technical and operational detail GP provided to MS—including specific propulsion mechanisms, navigation technology, facility dimensions, transport infrastructure specifics—indicates GP held a senior position within the program with broad access to multiple aspects of the operation.

## Significance as a Chemist in UAP Programs

GP's role as a senior chemist in UAP legacy operations is particularly significant given that most public discourse around reverse engineering programs focuses on aviation engineers, physicists, and aerospace specialists. The presence of high-level DOD chemists at Dugway—the nation's premier chemical and biological weapons facility—suggests several possibilities:

**Materials Analysis:** Studying the chemical composition, molecular structure, and material properties of non-human alloys and craft components.

**Propulsion Chemistry:** Analyzing chemical or exotic-matter propulsion systems, particularly the floating conical "reactor" section MS observed.

**Biologics Study:** Given Dugway's biochemical specialization and Fort Detrick's alleged role in storing non-human biologics (where Battelle also operates), GP may have been involved in studying biological materials of extraterrestrial origin.

**Exotic Materials Synthesis:** Attempting to replicate or synthesize materials with properties observed in recovered craft.

The fact that Dugway's West Desert Test Center director Ryan W. Harris is also a chemical engineer who previously worked for Battelle Memorial Institute as a project manager—and that multiple WDTC leadership figures have Battelle backgrounds—suggests a concentrated effort to recruit top chemistry and chemical engineering talent for classified programs at Dugway.

## Loss of Contact and Ongoing Search

MS lost contact with GP when MS departed C Martin Company in 2014. According to UAP Gerb's investigation, efforts are currently underway to locate and re-establish contact with GP to potentially secure additional testimony and technical details about Dugway's UAP legacy programs.

Given GP's seniority, technical expertise, and direct involvement in both above-ground and underground operations, his testimony—if obtained—would be highly corroborative of MS's account and could provide unprecedented technical insight into reverse engineering operations, propulsion mechanisms, and the scale of underground UAP program infrastructure.
