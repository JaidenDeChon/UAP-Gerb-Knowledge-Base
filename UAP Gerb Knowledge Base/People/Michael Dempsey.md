Michael P. Dempsey is a former US intelligence official who served from 2014 to 2017 as Deputy Director of National Intelligence for Intelligence Integration under [[James Clapper]], leading the integration of the intelligence community's agencies and acting as President Barack Obama's primary intelligence briefer. He was acting Director of National Intelligence from January to March 2017. Earlier, at the CIA, he was deputy associate director for military affairs, deputy CIA representative to the Joint Chiefs of Staff, and deputy director of the Weapons Intelligence, Nonproliferation and Arms Control Center (WINPAC). He later became vice president for government affairs at [[Northrop Grumman]].
