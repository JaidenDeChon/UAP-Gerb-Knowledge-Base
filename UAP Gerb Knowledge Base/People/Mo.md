Mo is the pseudonymous co-witness who accompanied [[Modesto]] during the September 6, 2015 Metapod UAP sighting near [[El Escorial, Madrid, Spain]]. Modesto characterized Mo as having exceptionally sharp eyesight, stating he "can see hairs and quailes in the same way as finding a needle in a hay stack."

## Observation Claims

Mo reported seeing something alive with limbs moving inside the craft through what appeared to be a transparent glass-like front face. This detail, relayed by Modesto in his email to UFO VNI, suggests Mo observed potential occupants or internal mechanisms within the structured craft during the sighting.

## Prior Experience

According to Modesto's account, Mo did not appear surprised by the sighting and remarked cryptically "here again" in reference to the object. When pressed about the meaning of this expression, Mo was reluctant to elaborate but confirmed it was not the first time he had seen the craft. Mo's deceased father had also reportedly observed the same or similar object in the area, suggesting a pattern of sightings in the El Escorial mountain region spanning at least two generations.

Modesto described Mo as being "a little more tack turn and from the town" than himself, suggesting Mo may have roots in the local area and greater familiarity with unusual phenomena reported in the region.
