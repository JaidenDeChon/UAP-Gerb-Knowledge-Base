Jan Harzan is a nuclear engineer and a former director of the Mutual UFO Network ([[MUFON]]). As an alumnus, he attended [[Ben Rich]]'s 1993 lecture at [[UCLA]], and his eyewitness account corroborates Rich's statements about extraterrestrial technology. Harzan was later removed as MUFON director after he faced criminal charges.

## Corroboration of Ben Rich's UCLA Lecture

Harzan attended the 1993 UCLA conference where [[Ben Rich]], the second director of [[Lockheed Martin Skunk Works]], gave what is alleged to have been an extraordinary disclosure. Harzan confirmed that Rich ended his formal talk with a slide of a black disc zipping into outer space, along with the statement: "We now have the technology to take ET home." According to Harzan, most of the audience missed the literal meaning of the statement.

After the lecture, Harzan was one of approximately 20–25 attendees who gathered around Rich to ask informal questions. When someone asked how "getting ET home" worked, Rich at first deflected. He then asked an engineer in the group: "Do you think it's possible to travel to the Stars?" When the engineer said it would take a very long time, Rich replied: "No, it wouldn't. We found an error in the equations and we now know how to travel to the stars." Rich also posed a question about how ESP works. When Harzan offered the answer "all points in time and space are connected", Rich reportedly looked him in the eye and confirmed: "That's how it works."

Harzan's account corroborates that of [[Tom Keller]], who also attended the lecture and published a 2010 MUFON article confirming Ben Rich's statements. Gerb notes, however, that Harzan's credibility as a witness was later undermined when he was charged with criminal offenses involving a minor, which led to his removal as MUFON director.
