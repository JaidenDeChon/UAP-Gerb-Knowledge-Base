Don Schmidt is an American UFO researcher and author, best known for his work with Kevin Randle on the [[Roswell UFO Crash]]. He co-wrote several books on Roswell and has been a prominent figure among crash retrieval researchers.

In the case of the [[1953 Kingman, Arizona Crash Retrieval]], Schmidt received testimony from [[Judy Wolcott]]. She claimed that her husband, a Vietnam-era military officer, had witnessed the Kingman crash and the recovery of non-human biologics. Author [[Nick Redfern]] later exposed Wolcott's account as fabricated.
