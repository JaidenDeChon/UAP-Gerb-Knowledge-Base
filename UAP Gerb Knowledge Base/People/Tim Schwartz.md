Tim Schwartz is an author and researcher who wrote about classified US spy satellite programs and their role in detecting UFOs. His work is not widely preserved, but insiders cited it as containing accurate information about space-based UAP detection systems.

## "Top Secret Spy Satellite Spot UFOs" Blog

Schwartz wrote a now-defunct blog post titled "Top Secret Spy Satellite Spot UFOs" that described in detail how [[Defense Support Program (DSP)]] satellites and other classified space-based sensor systems detect and track unidentified objects. The blog has since been removed from the internet and can only be read through web archives.

## Bob Fish Confirmation

[[Bob Fish]], a former USG contractor with direct access to DSP program personnel, specifically referred to Schwartz's blog in his 2015 email to [[John Podesta]] and stated that "much of the information on this site is correct." Because it came from a cleared insider with firsthand knowledge of DSP operations, this endorsement lent significant credibility to Schwartz's research and his conclusions about satellite-based UFO tracking.
