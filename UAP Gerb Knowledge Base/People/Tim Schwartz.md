Tim Schwartz is an author and researcher who wrote about classified US spy satellite programs and their role in detecting UFOs. His work, while not widely preserved, was cited by insiders as containing accurate information about space-based UAP detection systems.

## "Top Secret Spy Satellite Spot UFOs" Blog

Schwartz authored a now-defunct blog post titled "Top Secret Spy Satellite Spot UFOs" which detailed how [[Defense Support Program (DSP)]] satellites and other classified space-based sensor systems detect and track unidentified objects. The blog has since been removed from the internet and is only accessible through web archives.

## Bob Fish Confirmation

In his 2015 email to [[John Podesta]], [[Bob Fish]] — a former USG contractor with direct access to DSP program personnel — specifically referenced Schwartz's blog and stated that "much of the information on this site is correct." This endorsement from a cleared insider with firsthand knowledge of DSP operations lent significant credibility to Schwartz's research and conclusions about satellite-based UFO tracking.

Fish's confirmation suggested that Schwartz had accurately described aspects of how DSP infrared sensors detect [[Fast Walkers]] and how that data is processed and classified — information that would typically be protected under national security protocols.
