Joey Is Not My Name (Reddit handle: u/joeyisnotmyname) is a private UAP researcher and investigator who extensively researched Michael Herrera's Indonesia UAP encounter claims through open-source intelligence methods. Joey conducted detailed verification of the 2009 humanitarian mission details, cross-referenced military records, and using objective data such as flight records, claims to have independently verified that Herrera was taken to a secure facility by an anonymous program insider. Joey helped network Michael with key figures in the disclosure movement including David Grusch and has been described as one of the first to seriously investigate and corroborate verifiable elements of Herrera's testimony.

## Investigation Methods

Joey employed 3D terrain modeling, photojournalism analysis, military operation documentation, and flight record tracking to corroborate details of Herrera's account that could be verified through public sources.
