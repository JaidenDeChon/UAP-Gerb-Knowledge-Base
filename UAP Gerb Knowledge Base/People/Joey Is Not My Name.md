Joey Is Not My Name (Reddit handle u/joeyisnotmyname) is a private researcher known for investigating the account of [[Michael Herrera]], the former Marine who says he encountered a large craft and armed, unmarked operators in Indonesia in 2009. Joey matched the details of Herrera's relief mission against the public record, and says that objective data, including flight records, proves Herrera was flown to a secure facility in 2023 to meet an anonymous program insider ([[The Insider]]). He first appeared on Gerb's channel in March 2024 and co-hosted Gerb's in-person interview with Herrera that June. He still goes by his Reddit handle, but showed his face on camera for the first time in that June interview, having earlier revealed his identity only to journalists and podcasters he trusts.

## Verifying the Mission

Joey says he took up the case because of the reaction to Herrera's 2023 appearance at [[Steven Greer]]'s [[National Press Club]] event. People dismissed Herrera for what Joey considered flimsy reasons, such as his team having no radios or his misnaming a rifle. Because a humanitarian operation leaves a trail of photojournalism, press coverage and military reports, Joey reasoned that any lie about the mission could be exposed. His Reddit post "Verifying the events around Michael Herrera's UFO encounter, part one" found none. It matched four details of the setting: the [[USS Denver]] as the sole ship assigned to support Indonesia, the presence of the [[31st Marine Expeditionary Unit]], CH-53 Super Stallions delivering aid, and hasty landing zones for reaching remote areas. Joey stresses that this corroborates only the operation, and that the sighting is a separate matter.

Drawing on his background in 3D modelling, he planned a second part that would locate the landing zone and model the terrain in 3D software to check Herrera's estimates of distance, height and scale. By his reckoning the landing zone lay some 700 to 800 m from the craft, with a hill in between. When he emailed Herrera, Herrera called him the same day, and the two went on to speak weekly.

Joey has also spoken to members of Herrera's platoon. He says every one of them who would talk believes Herrera is lying (the one exception declined to answer and asked to be left out of everything), but that those he pressed admitted they were in a different squad or never left the ship. The critic he finds most credible is Nathan, Herrera's team leader, who produced a photograph he says shows Herrera flying into Indonesia without a rifle. Herrera denies being in it. Joey also reports that the Marines he spoke to agree the first wave was armed and later flights were not.

## The Insider Meeting

Joey was skeptical when Herrera began describing an insider who had approached him through Steven Greer, and says he swung between thinking it was all real and thinking it was a setup. He realized Herrera was using him as a [[Dead Man Switch|dead man switch]] by telling him the meeting's time, date and place. The day before the meeting he recorded a call with Herrera without Herrera's knowledge, as proof in case Herrera disappeared, and later told him about it. Using the details Herrera gave, he says he tracked the trip through flight records and other data. In a June 2024 interview he said he had tracked the helicopter flight on radar and matched its take-off time to Herrera's account. He calls this the turning point in his own belief: when he later asked Herrera what time he had taken off, without saying he had tracked the flight, the answer matched the radar exactly. He has shown a censored version of this evidence to Gerb, who says he is highly confident the meeting took place. Joey says he knows the insider exists and that Herrera was taken to the facility, but not whether anything the insider says is true. He found the insider's manuscript, *[[Sentinels of Ether]]*, unconvincing as corroboration.

## The June 2024 Interview

When Gerb sat down with Herrera in person in June 2024, Joey joined as co-interviewer. He set Herrera's account against the public record, reading Rear Admiral [[Richard B. Landolt]]'s press briefing that the [[USS Denver]]'s CH-53s came aboard on the morning of 9 October 2009 and first flew survey teams inland, and showing photographs of Marines with rifles guarding relief helicopters, which he cites as support for an armed security mission. He argued that much of the confusion around the account comes from not understanding how a non-combat deployment works, and rejected comparisons with the [[Paul Bennewitz]] case: Bennewitz had only radio intercepts that [[Richard Doty]] encouraged him to read as extraterrestrial, whereas Herrera describes a sighting at close range.

## Criticism

Joey has been called Herrera's shill, his publicist, or even Herrera under a pseudonym, and has been criticized for keeping his evidence private. He says he is "just a random guy", unpaid and not a publicist, though he did suggest a few interviewers, such as Chris Lehto, and make the first contact. He accepts the criticism of his secrecy and says he is honouring requests from people involved behind the scenes, but hopes to make more public in time.
