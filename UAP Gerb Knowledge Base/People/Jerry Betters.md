Jerry Betters is a prominent Pittsburgh jazz trombonist. On December 9, 1965, he saw a military roadblock and the transport of an acorn-shaped object from the [[1965 Kecksburg, Pennsylvania Crash Retrieval|1965 Kecksburg UFO crash]] site. His testimony is significant because he saw the craft on a flatbed truck from a distance before military personnel blocked his way. That gives independent corroboration of the descriptions of the object from the witnesses [[Jim Romansky]] and [[Bill Bully Bush]].

## The Sighting

On the evening of December 9, 1965, Betters was a young jazz musician returning from a gig in Pittsburgh. As he drove through the area near [[Kecksburg, Pennsylvania]], he encountered a military roadblock. Before he was stopped, Betters glimpsed a large acorn-shaped object sitting on a flatbed truck. He described it as metallic with a bronze or copper color.

Military personnel turned Betters away at once and did not let him approach the object. He saw soldiers and other personnel surrounding the truck and securing the area. The object matched descriptions given by civilian witnesses who had seen the craft in the woods shortly after it crashed.

## Credibility

In the decades that followed, Jerry Betters became a well-known and respected figure in the Pittsburgh jazz scene, which lends credibility to his account. He gave his testimony independently of other witnesses, and it was consistent with the physical descriptions of the craft recorded by [[Stan Gordon]] and other investigators.

Betters is one of the few witnesses who saw the object *after* it had been loaded for transport. His account is evidence that a physical craft was indeed recovered from the Kecksburg woods and removed under military escort.
