**Arnold House** is a pseudonym for a Boeing Aerospace surface technician who passed on disclosures about UAP programs that he received from a network of contacts. They included NSA, CIA, NASA, JPL, Office of Naval Intelligence, [[Area 51]], USAF, Northrop (now [[Northrop Grumman]]) and [[Boeing]] personnel, as well as multiple generals. [[Steven Greer]]'s 2001 Disclosure Project briefing document identifies him as "AH," and Gerb believes this corresponds to the real individual "Arnold House". House's testimony is explicitly secondhand: he never directly took part in UAP program operations.

> [!edit] Corrected 17 October 2026
> Earlier versions listed Northrop Grumman among House's contacts. Gerb says "Northrup"; Northrop only became Northrop Grumman when it bought Grumman in 1994 ([Northrop Grumman 10-K](https://investor.northropgrumman.com/node/21306/html)).

According to Greer, House briefed Bill Clinton in 1990, when Clinton was still governor of Arkansas; he became president in 1993. Gerb says he has no evidence to confirm the briefing. The only possible link he found is a 9 July 2000 letter from Greer to Clinton, which asks for disclosure of UFO-related USAPs through their "mutual friend." House and Greer corresponded from December 2000 to as recently as 2018, which suggests House was one of Greer's main long-term research sources.

> [!edit] Attribution clarified 17 October 2026
> Earlier versions said House allegedly briefed "President Clinton" in 1990. The claim is Greer's, as Gerb reports it, and Clinton was governor of Arkansas in 1990, not president ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Governorships_of_Bill_Clinton); [Encyclopedia of Arkansas](https://encyclopediaofarkansas.net/entries/bill-clinton-95/)).

## The Northrop Connection

A friend of House's in the Army Criminal Investigation Division (CID), who had worked on UFO crash recoveries since 1947, handpicked one of House's Boeing colleagues to join a crash recovery team in Northern New Mexico, starting in 1980. This Boeing source spent 17 years (1980–1997) in UAP legacy program operations at [[Area 51]]. His paychecks during that time came directly from Northrop (Northrop Corporation, which became [[Northrop Grumman]] in 1994), which indicates he was on the company's payroll rather than employed by the government. He told House that Northrop, [[Lockheed Martin]] and Boeing all worked on reverse engineering UFOs at Area 51, and that the facility held extensive extraterrestrial debris in a containment area.

> [!edit] Corrected 17 October 2026
> Earlier versions said the source's paychecks from 1980 came from Northrop Grumman, and named Northrop Grumman among the companies he described. Gerb says "Northrup", and Northrop Grumman was not formed until 1994, so most of the 1980–1997 period predates that name.

## Project Red Light

House's source named the Area 51 program that tested non-human vehicles and exploited recovered materials as [[Project Redlight|Project Red Light]]. He described "a base near Lancaster in Palmdale area" with an underground area near Northrop, which is consistent with independent descriptions of the [[Tejon Ranch]] facility. He also mentioned underground research at Anza, California and at March Air Force Base. According to the source, 35–40% of Area 51's UAP operations were moved to Utah (identified as [[Dugway Proving Ground]]) in the early 1990s. This corroborates the identical claim made independently by [[Edgar Fouche|Edgar Fuché]].

## Additional Disclosures

House said he learned the following from his contacts:
- [[Curtis LeMay|General Curtis LeMay]] personally confirmed the [[Roswell Crash|1947 Roswell Crash]] at a private meeting around 1987
- Henry Kissinger, George H.W. Bush, Ronald Reagan, and Mikhail Gorbachev were all aware of the UAP subject
- CIA and USAF had downed non-human craft
- FBI radar testing had interfered with some craft, causing them to crash, an account that echoes [[Philip J. Corso|Lieutenant Colonel Philip J. Corso]]'s Roswell analysis
- Underground bases testing and maintaining TUOs existed at [[Dugway Proving Ground]], [[Palmdale, California|Palmdale]], [[Edwards Air Force Base]], Eglin Air Force Base, and others
