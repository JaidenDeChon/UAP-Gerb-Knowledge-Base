**Arnold House** is a pseudonym (identified in [[Steven Greer]]'s 2001 Disclosure Project briefing document as "AH," and believed by UAP Gerb to correspond to the real individual "Arnold House") for a Boeing Aerospace surface technician who became a conduit for UAP program disclosures received through a network of contacts including NSA, CIA, NASA, JPL, Office of Naval Intelligence, [[Area 51]], USAF, [[Northrop Grumman]], and [[Boeing]] personnel, as well as multiple generals. House's testimony is explicitly secondhand — he never directly participated in UAP program operations.

House allegedly briefed President Clinton in 1990, though the only corroborating documentation is a July 2000 letter from Greer to Clinton requesting disclosure of UFO-related USAPs through their "mutual friend." Correspondences between House and Greer span from December 2000 to as recently as 2018, suggesting House served as one of Greer's primary long-term research sources.

## The Northrop Connection

A friend of House's in the Army Criminal Investigation Division (CID), a veteran of UFO crash recoveries since 1947, handpicked one of House's Boeing colleagues to join a crash recovery team in Northern New Mexico starting in 1980. This Boeing source spent 17 years (1980–1997) in UAP legacy program operations at [[Area 51]]. During this period, his paychecks came directly from [[Northrop Grumman]], indicating direct corporate payroll involvement rather than government employment. The source told House that Northrop Grumman, [[Lockheed Martin]], and Boeing all engaged in UFO reverse engineering efforts at Area 51, with the facility containing extensive extraterrestrial debris in a containment area.

## Project Red Light

House's source identified the Area 51 program testing non-human vehicles and exploiting recovered materials as [[Project Red Light]]. The source noted "a base near Lancaster in Palmdale area" with an underground area near Northrop — consistent with independent descriptions of the [[Tejon Ranch]] facility — as well as underground research at Anza, California and [[March Air Force Base]]. The source also stated that 35–40% of Area 51's UAP operations were relocated to Utah (identified as [[Dugway Proving Ground]]) in the early 1990s, corroborating the identical claim made independently by [[Edgar Fuché]].

## Additional Disclosures

House stated he learned from his contacts:
- [[General Curtis LeMay]] personally confirmed the [[1947 Roswell Crash]] at a private meeting around 1987
- Henry Kissinger, George H.W. Bush, Ronald Reagan, and Mikhail Gorbachev were all aware of the UAP subject
- CIA and USAF had downed non-human craft
- FBI radar testing had caused interference with some craft, resulting in crashes — an account echoing [[Lieutenant Colonel Philip J. Corso]]'s Roswell analysis
- Underground bases testing and maintaining TUOs existed at [[Dugway Proving Ground]], [[Palmdale]], [[Edwards Air Force Base]], [[Eglin Air Force Base]], and others
