**Harold Keith Johnson** (1912–1983) was a United States Army general who served as Chief of Staff of the Army from 1964 to 1968. A survivor of the Bataan Death March and three years as a Japanese prisoner of war, he fought in Korea with the 1st Cavalry Division. He commanded the 3rd Battalion, 8th Cavalry Regiment on the Pusan Perimeter, winning the Distinguished Service Cross, and later commanded the 5th and 8th Cavalry Regiments.
