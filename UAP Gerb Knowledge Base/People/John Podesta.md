John Podesta is an American political consultant who has held senior roles in several Democratic administrations and campaigns. He was White House Chief of Staff under President Bill Clinton (1998-2001), Counselor to President [[Barack Obama]] (2014-2015), and chairman of Hillary Clinton's 2016 presidential campaign. Podesta has spoken publicly about his interest in government transparency on UFO/UAP information and has called for the declassification of UFO-related documents. In Gerb's videos he appears mainly as the recipient of two UFO emails that [[WikiLeaks]] later published, one from [[Bob Fish]] and one from [[Tom DeLonge]], and as a figure Gerb ties to an alleged effort to make Hillary Clinton the "disclosure president".

## Career

Podesta was born in Chicago in 1949. In the Clinton White House he rose from Staff Secretary (1993-1995) to Deputy Chief of Staff (1997-1998) and then Chief of Staff, from October 1998 until the end of the administration in January 2001. He founded the Center for American Progress, a Washington think tank, in October 2003. He returned to the White House as Counselor to President Obama from January 2014 to February 2015, and left to lead Hillary Clinton's presidential campaign, which he chaired formally from its launch in April 2015 through the 2016 election. Under President Joe Biden he was senior adviser for clean energy innovation and implementation from September 2022, and from 2024 to January 2025 senior adviser for international climate policy.

His public interest in UFOs predates Gerb's coverage. At an October 2002 National Press Club event launching the Coalition for Freedom of Information, he called on the government to declassify UFO records more than 25 years old. On his last day as Obama's counselor in February 2015, he tweeted that his "biggest failure of 2014" was "once again not securing the #disclosure of the UFO files."

## WikiLeaks Email Disclosure on DSP and Fast Walkers

In March 2015, weeks after leaving the White House to lead Hillary Clinton's campaign, Podesta received a detailed [[Bob Fish Email to John Podesta|email]] from [[Bob Fish]], a former US government contractor who had managed highly classified government communications systems. [[WikiLeaks]] later published it with the other leaked Clinton campaign emails. In it, Fish claimed that US government satellites were tracking UFOs.

Fish's email stated that "one of the government programs that collects hard data on unidentified flying objects is the USAF [[Defense Support Program (DSP)|DSP]] satellite program" and described a [[Fast Walkers|Fast Walker]] incident in which a DSP satellite detected an object entering from deep space that made a 30-degree course correction, indicating controlled flight.

Fish was a cleared contractor with direct access to DSP personnel, and he put the claim on the record to one of the most senior political figures in the United States. Because the campaign kept the email and WikiLeaks later released it, it is one of the few documented cases of UAP-related information reaching the highest levels of US political leadership. Gerb returns to it in his video on the [[Peru UFO Crash Incident|1997 Peru crash]], where he argues that NRO-managed DSP satellites likely gave the retrieval team about an hour's warning.

## DeLonge's Message About General McCasland

The leaked emails also include a message from [[Tom DeLonge]] to Podesta about retired Major General [[Neil McCasland]]. According to Gerb, DeLonge wrote that McCasland would publicly position himself as a skeptic of the UFO phenomenon but was much more open behind closed doors: "He just has to say that out loud, but he is very, very aware as he was in charge of all the stuff." DeLonge went on to say that when Roswell crashed, the material was shipped to the laboratory at [[Wright-Patterson Air Force Base|Wright-Patterson]] that McCasland later commanded as head of the Air Force Research Laboratory. Gerb uses the message in *The Hidden Wing* to trace that custody back through the predecessors of [[Air Force Materiel Command (AFMC)|Air Force Materiel Command]].

## Alleged "Disclosure President" Effort

Gerb connects Podesta to an alleged effort to position [[Hillary Clinton]] as a "disclosure president" if she won the 2016 presidential election. In Gerb's account, [[AATIP (Advanced Aerospace Threat Identification Program)|AATIP]] was cover for National Security Council work on a partial-disclosure initiative, which DNI [[James Clapper]] gave "top cover", and that work led to the attempts with Podesta. Gerb describes the effort as connected to [[To The Stars Academy]], alongside Podesta, Major General Neil McCasland, Lockheed Skunk Works personnel and possibly intelligence officials, and says it allegedly sought to coordinate narratives and release partial information while protecting the legacy programs. He also finds [[Andrew Kissner]]'s mention of the Rockefeller Institute intriguing, since the Rockefellers seemed to play a hand in Podesta's initiative. When Clinton lost and To The Stars Academy faltered, Gerb says, the effort changed shape and resurfaced as *The Age of Disclosure*.

## To The Stars Academy

On the Joe Rogan episode released on 26 October 2017, Tom DeLonge named Podesta among the people advising To The Stars Academy of Arts & Science, the organization DeLonge founded that helped bring Navy UAP encounter footage to public attention. The others he named included Major Generals Neil McCasland and Michael Carey, [[Hal Puthoff]], [[Chris Mellon]], [[Luis Elizondo]] and two Lockheed Martin Skunk Works veterans.
