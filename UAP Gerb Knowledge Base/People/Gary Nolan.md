Dr. Garry Nolan (usually spelled "Gary" in Gerb's videos and in this vault's page title) is the Rachford and Carlota A. Harris Professor of Pathology at Stanford University School of Medicine and one of the few mainstream scientists who study UAP openly. He researches how close encounters with UAP affect the bodies and minds of witnesses, and in 2023 he co-founded the [[Sol Foundation]], whose November 2023 symposium was held at his laboratory at Stanford. In Gerb's videos he appears in four roles: as a source naming aerospace contractors as holders of crash material, as a voice for the idea that UAP occupants are biological androids, as a researcher of UAP effects on human cognition, and as a figure in the Sol Foundation.

## Academic Background

Nolan is a mainstream scientist with strong credentials. According to his Stanford biography he has published more than 300 research articles, holds dozens of US patents and has helped found several biotechnology companies. Gerb counts him, with [[David Grusch]], among the "credible individuals" whose statements give weight to the subject, and his standing as a tenured Stanford professor is part of why his involvement draws attention.

## UAP Biological Effects Research

Nolan's UAP work began with medical cases: brain scans of people, many from military and intelligence backgrounds, who reported health problems after close encounters with UAP. He has described damage to white matter in some of these patients, resembling the scarring seen in multiple sclerosis, and unusual features in the caudate nucleus and putamen, brain structures he and colleagues have since studied for their role in intuition and planning. He has said this about the phenomenon:

> "Whatever the phenomenon is, it's capable of manipulating human perception... human consciousness."

Gerb draws on this work twice. In his video on the [[Off-World Technologies Division]], he links it to the Green Beret [[RA]], who says his near-photographic memory goes "real fuzzy" only when he recalls the [[Consciousness-Interactive Technology|consciousness-interactive objects]] he was shown. In his video on the [[Infographic Show Whistleblower]], he links it to the whistleblower's claim that recovery teams fixed mental "anchors", simple pictures of the scene, to hold on to when a craft distorted their perception. Gerb notes that this kind of effect also appears on slide 9 of an AATIP presentation leaked from [[Chris Mellon]]'s website. In his breakdown of [[Karl Nell]]'s Sol Foundation talk, Gerb suspects Nolan was behind the sixth UAP characteristic now cited in legislation, "physical or invasive biological effects", which he links to Havana syndrome and to the fear witnesses report near a craft.

## Biological Android and Avatar Hypothesis

In public statements and interviews, Nolan has put forward the hypothesis that most UAP occupants people have seen are not the intelligence behind the phenomenon. In his view they are biological intermediaries, avatars or biological robots sent to deal with humans in place of the intelligence itself. In a clip Gerb plays, he gives his reasoning this way: "If you're an intelligence, are you going to go down on a planet with a bunch of angry monkeys who might kill you? No. You'll send some intermediary. And what kind of intermediary are you going to send? You're going to send something that maybe almost looks like them, but isn't them."

Gerb sets this beside the account of the anonymous whistleblower analyzed in "US Special Forces Confession - I Recovered Crashed UFOs - Fact or Fiction". The whistleblower describes colleagues in the program concluding that the biological entities recovered from crash sites were biological androids or drones rather than naturally evolved life forms: beings with crude digestive systems who lived at most a few days after recovery. "Like Gary Nolan said," the whistleblower says, whatever makes these craft "just creates a biological crew when it needs one".

## Crash Material and the Contractors

Gerb's video on [[Lockheed Martin]] opens with a clip in which Nolan says he has heard from very well-informed people that crash materials are held not by the US government but by its aerospace and defense contractors, naming McDonnell Douglas and Lockheed. Gerb counts him, with Grusch and [[Harry Reid]], among those who name Lockheed as a holder of retrieved material.

## The Sol Foundation

Nolan co-founded the Sol Foundation in 2023 with Peter Skafish to build a base for scientific and policy research on UAP. Gerb describes it as a group of scientists, academics, military personnel and policy makers working to give UAP academic legitimacy and to prepare for a world after disclosure. Its November 2023 symposium at Nolan's Stanford laboratory heard talks from Karl Nell, Grusch, [[Kevin Knuth]], [[Chris Mellon]], [[Jacques Vallee]] and Nolan himself (see [[SOL Foundation Symposium]]).
