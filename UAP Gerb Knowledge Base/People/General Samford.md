Major General John A. Samford was Director of Air Force Intelligence. In UAP history he is known for holding the largest US Air Force press conference since World War II, after the [[1952 UFOs Over Washington DC|1952 Washington DC UFO sightings]]. In July 1952 Samford and USAF Director of Operations [[Roger Ramey]] faced the press at the Pentagon to address the wave of UFO reports over the nation's capital.

## The 1952 Pentagon Press Conference

At the press conference, Samford acknowledged that a percentage of credible observers had reported "relatively incredible things". In the same breath he attributed the majority of UFO reports "to their own satisfaction" to hoaxes, misidentified aircraft or meteorological phenomena. He stated specifically that the 1952 Washington DC sightings were "in no way related to any secret development by any Department of the United States."

Samford's position admitted some credible unknowns while officially dismissing the bulk of the reports. Six months later the CIA reversed it by forming the [[Robertson Panel]]. The panel concluded that UFOs should be stripped of "special status" entirely and that public interest in UFOs should be actively reduced through a media management campaign.
