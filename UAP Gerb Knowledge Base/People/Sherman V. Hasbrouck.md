Sherman V. Hasbrouck was a US Army officer who held senior posts in the early postwar nuclear weapons establishment. As a colonel he served as chief of staff to General Kenneth D. Nichols and helped shape the [[Armed Forces Special Weapons Project (AFSWP)|Armed Forces Special Weapons Project]], the joint Army and Navy body created in January 1947 to take over the military side of the [[Manhattan Project]], including the assembly of nuclear weapons and surveillance of the stockpile. He worked closely with [[Leslie Groves]], the project's first chief. In UFO research his name is best known from the disputed [[Interplanetary Phenomenon Unit]] documents.

## The IPU Report

The Interplanetary Phenomenon Unit report, dated July 1947 and given to researcher [[Tim Cooper|Timothy Cooper]] in 1995, describes the recovery of multiple fallen objects in New Mexico between 4 and 6 July 1947, which is generally read as the [[Roswell Crash]]. It says a special radiobiological team, a Special Engineer Detachment (a Manhattan Project term for technical personnel) and a security detail were sent under orders from Colonel Hasbrouck of AFSWP, and that Sandia and AFSWP personnel secured the immediate area around the crash sites. The report adds that several military policemen suffered breakdowns and that three technicians from Sandia died of an unexplained contamination.

## Assessment in UAP Gerb's Work

In his investigation of [[Sandia National Laboratories]], UAP Gerb notes that Hasbrouck was a real officer who helped build AFSWP and whom he can place at Sandia Base, where AFSWP was headquartered beside Los Alamos' Z Division. The host treats this, together with former New Mexico state representative [[Andrew Kissner]]'s independent 1994 account of AFSWP support for the early recovery effort, as the second source to put AFSWP at Sandia Base into the 1947 retrievals. Because Kissner finished his statement in 1994 and the IPU documents surfaced in 1995, the host suggests that the documents could have drawn on Kissner rather than the reverse. He also links AFSWP's successor, the [[Defense Threat Reduction Agency (DTRA)|Defense Threat Reduction Agency]], to [[Dylan Borland]]'s account of old Atomic Energy Commission UFO files.
