Jaime Shandera is an American television producer, based in Los Angeles, who in December 1984 anonymously received a roll of undeveloped 35 mm film. It contained the [[Eisenhower Briefing Document]], the first of the [[MJ-12 Documents]]. That delivery began a series of leaks that, by [[Ryan S. Wood]]'s count, reached about 3,500 pages from seven sources by 1999.

Shandera worked with researcher [[Bill Moore]] and shared the film with physicist [[Stanton Friedman]], who spent years trying to authenticate it and the [[Majestic 12]] members it names. When the document was later submitted to the FBI and circulated among intelligence agencies, none claimed it, and the FBI declared it bogus.
