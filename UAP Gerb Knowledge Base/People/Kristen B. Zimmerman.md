Kristen B. Zimmerman is a researcher who, alongside [[Jacques Vallee]], [[Kit Green]], [[Hal Puthoff]], and [[Eric Davis]], identified [[Aerospace Corporation]], [[Lockheed Martin]], and [[Northrop Grumman]] as suspected legacy private corporations engaged in "real UAP research."

This identification appears in Vallée's book *Hidden Science 5*, which documents journals from the 2000s to 2009. The research group's analysis of defense contractor involvement in UAP programs aligns with subsequent disclosures by [[Bob Fish]] and [[Ross Coulthart]] regarding the Aerospace Corporation's role in analyzing [[Defense Support Program (DSP)]] satellite data on [[Fast Walkers]] and possible access to recovered UAP materials.

## Significance

Zimmerman's participation in the research group that identified specific defense contractors as UAP legacy program participants demonstrates that multiple independent researchers with intelligence community backgrounds arrived at similar conclusions about which organizations possess access to classified UAP materials and research programs.
