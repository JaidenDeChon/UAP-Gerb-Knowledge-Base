Kristen B. Zimmerman is a researcher who, alongside [[Jacques Vallee]], [[Kit Green]], [[Hal Puthoff]], and [[Eric Davis]], identified [[Aerospace Corporation]], [[Lockheed Martin]], and [[Northrop Grumman]] as suspected legacy private corporations engaged in "real UAP research."

This identification appears in Vallee's *Forbidden Science 5: Pacific Heights*, his published journals for 2000 to 2009 (rendered "Hidden Science 5" in the video's transcript). The group's analysis of defense contractor involvement in UAP programs fits later disclosures by [[Bob Fish]] and [[Ross Coulthart]] about the Aerospace Corporation's role in analyzing [[Defense Support Program (DSP)]] satellite data on [[Fast Walkers]] and possible access to recovered UAP materials.

## Significance

Zimmerman was part of the research group that identified specific defense contractors as participants in UAP legacy programs. That participation shows that several independent researchers with intelligence community backgrounds reached similar conclusions about which organizations have access to classified UAP materials and research programs.
