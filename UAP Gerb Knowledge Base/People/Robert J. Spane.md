Vice Admiral Robert J. "Rocky" Spane is a retired United States Navy aviator who served as Commander, Naval Air Force, US Pacific Fleet (COMNAVAIRPAC) from 1993 until his retirement in 1996. His earlier commands included Attack Squadron 37, the amphibious assault ship USS *Tripoli*, the carrier USS *Enterprise* and Carrier Group Eight. In UAP research he is known for a December 1995 study that helped bring Navy carrier aviation into the Marine Corps' 1997 [[Hunter Warrior Advanced Warfighting Experiment]]. The UAP Gerb video on the experiment renders his name as "Robert Spain".

## The 1995 fire-support study

In December 1995, while still at COMNAVAIRPAC, Spane put his name to a report titled *An Operational Examination of Sea-Based Aerial Fire Support for Engaged Troops*. It proposed a new mission for carrier tactical aviation: supporting troops ashore on a nonlinear battlefield, with new training procedures and employment concepts for aircrews flying from carriers in close concert with ground forces.

According to the video, the report led, at the behest of Chief of Naval Operations Admiral [[Jay L. Johnson]], to a late-1996 request by Commandant of the Marine Corps General [[Charles Krulak]]. Krulak asked the Naval Strike and Air Warfare Center at Fallon, Nevada (today's [[Naval Aviation Warfare Development Center (NAWDC)]]) to assemble aircrews from Fallon and [[Naval Air Station Lemoore (NAS Lemoore)|Naval Air Station Lemoore]] to explore a "Hunter Air" concept during Hunter Warrior. That naval element was an adjunct bolted onto the experiment late in its planning.

## UAP significance

UAP Gerb treats the Spane study as the paper trail that explains why Navy units were present at all during Hunter Warrior, the exercise in which Marine Sergeant [[Rodrik Castle]] says he saw a large black triangular craft and an armed, unmarked ground team near [[Emerson Dry Lake]]. Gerb theorizes that the late naval adjunct let Navy (and allied Air Force) programs place an [[Alien Reproduction Vehicle (ARV)|alien reproduction vehicle]] and a special mission unit inside the exercise's area of operations, to practise alongside unwitting "blue" forces. Spane himself is not alleged to have had any part in that.
