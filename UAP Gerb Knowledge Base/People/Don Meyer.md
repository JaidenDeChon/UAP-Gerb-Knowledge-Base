Don Meyer is a former senior U.S. intelligence acquisition official who served as Assistant Director of National Intelligence for Acquisition, Technology, and Facilities (ADNI/ATNF) from 2009 to 2013 — a position UAP Gerb identifies as a direct counterpart to the Department of Defense's Under Secretary of Defense for Acquisition and Sustainment (USD A&S) role, and important to legacy program oversight in part through its relationship to National Reconnaissance Office acquisitions. Earlier in his career, Meyer held technical staff and program management roles at TRW and the Jet Propulsion Laboratory, and served as chief technology officer of the Defense Information Systems Agency. Following his ADNI/ATNF tenure, Meyer served as deputy director of the CIA's Directorate of Science and Technology (DS&T) from 2013 to 2021.

## MITRE Corporate Board

Immediately following his service as CIA DS&T deputy director, Meyer joined the corporate board of the [[MITRE Corporation]]. UAP Gerb cites this trajectory — from a senior national intelligence acquisition post, through the CIA's most technically sensitive directorate, and directly into MITRE governance — as part of a broader pattern of former senior intelligence officials with alleged UFO legacy program proximity moving into MITRE leadership roles, alongside [[Donald Kerr]] and [[Susan Gordon]].
