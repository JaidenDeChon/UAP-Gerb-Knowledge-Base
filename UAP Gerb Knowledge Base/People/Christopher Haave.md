Christopher Haave is a retired US Air Force colonel and fighter pilot who commanded the 98th Range Wing, the unit that ran the [[Nevada Test and Training Range (NTR)|Nevada Test and Training Range]] from [[Nellis Air Force Base]], from July 2005 to July 2007. He then served as special adviser for national security affairs to Vice President [[Dick Cheney]] from 2007 to January 2009, and later held government-relations and trade-control posts in the defense industry: director of global trade controls, licensing and policy at [[Boeing]], and vice president for international operations at Raytheon.

## In UAP research

UAP Gerb examines Haave in *UAP Reverse Engineering at Edwards Air Force Base*. Greer's notes on witness [[Ed (Witness 11063)|Ed]] say Ed worked at [[Building 200]] at Nellis under the range wing's first commander, whose name is redacted, and the host suspected that the post might carry oversight of reverse-engineering work on the range. Unable to identify the first commander, he looks instead at Haave, the 2005–2007 commander. He highlights Haave's 26-year Air Force career in fighter aviation; his Boeing role handling trade policy and technology licensing with major government departments; his work at Raytheon managing relationships with the Office of the Secretary of Defense and the military departments; and his time advising Cheney, whom rumours have linked to senior roles in UFO programs. The host presents him as a possible reverse-engineering program official. He does not claim Haave has any known UAP involvement.
