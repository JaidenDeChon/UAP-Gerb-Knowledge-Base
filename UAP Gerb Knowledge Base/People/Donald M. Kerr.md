Donald M. Kerr (1939–2025) was a US physicist and senior national-laboratory, intelligence and defense-industry official whose career ran through an unusual number of the institutions UAP Gerb associates with the UFO legacy programs. Holding a PhD in plasma physics and microwave electronics (1966), Kerr was director of Los Alamos National Laboratory (1979–1985), president of [[EG&G]] (1989–1992), an executive at [[SAIC]], Deputy Director for Science and Technology at the CIA, heading the [[CIA Directorate of Science and Technology]] (2001–2005), director of the [[NRO (National Reconnaissance Office)|National Reconnaissance Office]] (2005–2007), and Principal Deputy Director of National Intelligence under Mike McConnell (2007–2009). He joined the [[MITRE Corporation]] board of trustees in 2009 and later chaired it.

## Alleged Legacy Program Role

UAP Gerb describes Kerr as one of the most senior legacy program leads of his era: in Majestic 12 terminology, an "MJ-1" equivalent. The channel says Kerr, before his death, helped bring some transparency to the topic. Its SAIC investigation cites his career as a case of contractor leadership and intelligence science portfolios overlapping in one person.

## SAIC and the gatekeeper case

UAP Gerb's SAIC investigation says Kerr joined [[SAIC]] in 1993 as a corporate executive vice president and director, between his EG&G presidency and his CIA appointment. The host notes that the [[CIA Office of Global Access]], which he accuses of foreign UFO crash retrievals with JSOC, was established within the CIA Directorate of Science and Technology while Kerr was its deputy director. He also cites the notes of [[Oak Shannon|Oke Shannon]] from the 1985 Advanced Theoretical Physics working group at [[BDM International|BDM]]'s facility in McLean, Virginia, which include a plan to approach Donald Kerr for "influence and money", and a separate entry reading "Don K/ET". The host names Kerr among the gatekeepers he believes Congress should have questioned, saying his CV (Los Alamos, EG&G, SAIC, CIA science and technology, the NRO and MITRE) "would lend" more than anyone's to legacy program work.

## MITRE Corporation

In its MITRE investigation, UAP Gerb places Kerr first among the former senior officials who took leadership roles at MITRE after government service, giving his tenure as trustee and chairman as 2009–2021. The channel also notes that Kerr served a second stint on the Defense Science Board (from 2010 into the early 2020s), where it says he would have crossed paths with [[William LaPlante]]. It argues that the pattern of Principal Deputy DNIs with strong intelligence-community and FFRDC ties (Kerr, then [[Stephanie O'Sullivan]] and [[Susan Gordon]]) is consistent with its thesis that MITRE has long been R&D lead on classified, Navy-administered UFO reverse-engineering and USO-monitoring programs.
