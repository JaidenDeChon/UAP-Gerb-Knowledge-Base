Donald M. Kerr (1939–2025) was a US physicist who held senior posts at a national laboratory, in intelligence and in the defense industry. His career ran through an unusual number of the institutions Gerb associates with the UFO legacy programs. Kerr received a PhD in plasma physics and microwave electronics in 1966. He was director of Los Alamos National Laboratory (1979–1985), president of [[EG&G]] (1989–1992) and an executive at [[SAIC]]. At the CIA he was Deputy Director for Science and Technology, heading the [[CIA Directorate of Science and Technology]] (2001–2005). He went on to direct the [[NRO (National Reconnaissance Office)|National Reconnaissance Office]] (2005–2007) and to serve as Principal Deputy Director of National Intelligence under Mike McConnell (2007–2009). He joined the [[MITRE Corporation]] board of trustees in 2009 and later chaired it.

## Alleged Legacy Program Role

Gerb describes Kerr as one of the most senior legacy program leads of his era, the equivalent of "MJ-1" in Majestic 12 terminology. The channel says Kerr, before his death, helped bring some transparency to the topic. The channel's SAIC investigation cites his career as an example of one person combining contractor leadership with intelligence science portfolios.

## SAIC and the gatekeeper case

The same investigation says Kerr joined [[SAIC]] in 1993 as a corporate executive vice president and director, between his EG&G presidency and his CIA appointment. Gerb notes that the [[CIA Office of Global Access]], which he accuses of carrying out foreign UFO crash retrievals with JSOC, was established within the CIA Directorate of Science and Technology while Kerr was its deputy director. He also cites the notes of [[Oak Shannon|Oke Shannon]] from the 1985 Advanced Theoretical Physics working group at [[BDM International|BDM]]'s facility in McLean, Virginia, which include a plan to approach Donald Kerr for "influence and money", and a separate entry reading "Don K/ET". Gerb names Kerr among the gatekeepers he believes Congress should have questioned, saying his CV (Los Alamos, EG&G, SAIC, CIA science and technology, the NRO and MITRE) "would lend" more than anyone's to legacy program work.

## MITRE Corporation

In the channel's MITRE investigation, Gerb places Kerr first among the former senior officials who took leadership roles at MITRE after government service, giving his tenure as trustee and chairman as 2009–2021. The channel also notes that Kerr served a second stint on the Defense Science Board (from 2010 into the early 2020s), where it says he would have crossed paths with [[William LaPlante]]. It argues that the pattern of Principal Deputy DNIs with strong intelligence-community and FFRDC ties (Kerr, then [[Stephanie O'Sullivan]] and [[Susan Gordon]]) is consistent with its thesis that MITRE has long been the R&D lead on classified, Navy-administered UFO reverse-engineering and USO-monitoring programs.
