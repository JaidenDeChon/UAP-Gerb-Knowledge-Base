Roscoe Henry Hillenkoetter (1897–1982) was a US Navy officer and naval intelligence specialist who served as the third Director of Central Intelligence from 1947 to 1950, and as such the first director of the newly created [[CIA]]. In the [[MJ-12 Documents]] he is listed as MJ-1, a founding member of [[Majestic 12]], and as the briefing officer named in the [[Eisenhower Briefing Document]] of 18 November 1952.

## In the Majestic documents

Several documents give Hillenkoetter a central role. One is a 19 September 1947 memo to the Joint Intelligence Committee requesting a classified intelligence project after the recovery of a craft. Another is an 11 February 1948 "Majestic Black Book" summary for [[Harry Truman]]. A third is a later memo congratulating fellow alleged member [[Donald Menzel]] on a book, which Gerb reads as praise for successful obfuscation. [[Ryan S. Wood]] and Gerb say these still need further research.

Critics of the Eisenhower Briefing Document argued that Hillenkoetter was in Korea in late 1952 and could not have given the briefing. [[Stanton Friedman]] established that he was in New York at the time as commander of the Third Naval District, within reach of Washington.
