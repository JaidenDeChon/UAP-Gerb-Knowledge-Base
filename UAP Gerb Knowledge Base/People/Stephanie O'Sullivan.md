Stephanie O'Sullivan was Principal Deputy Director of National Intelligence (PDDNI) from 2011 to 2017, recruited to the role by Director of National Intelligence [[James Clapper]]. Before joining the [[Office of the Director of National Intelligence (ODNI)]], O'Sullivan was Deputy Director of the CIA's Directorate of Science and Technology (DS&T), one of the most technically sensitive positions in the U.S. Intelligence Community. Earlier in her career, she worked at [[TRW]], the defense and aerospace contractor later acquired by [[Northrop Grumman]] in 2002. Her career ran from technical work at TRW, through science and technology leadership at the CIA, to the second-highest position in the ODNI. That path matches the alleged TRW-to-ODNI personnel pipeline described in analysis by UAP researchers.

## Role in UAP Programs

O'Sullivan is alleged to have co-managed UAP legacy programs alongside [[James Clapper]], with her earlier TRW employment and CIA DS&T role allegedly giving her the need-to-know access such work required. Testimony cited in the video claims that O'Sullivan and Clapper held informal dual-hatted roles, managing UFO crash retrieval programs at the same time as they held their official ODNI positions. She is also alleged to have participated in an Obama-era initiative, alongside Clapper and [[Luis Elizondo]], that positioned [[Hillary Clinton]] as a potential disclosure president.

## TRW Years and Later Boards

Gerb's video *Northrop Grumman & TRW* says O'Sullivan joined TRW around 1982; her Battelle board biography says that there she provided oversight for "a series of sensitive projects". The video says she later worked on sensitive research and development programs at the [[Office of Naval Intelligence]] before joining the CIA in 1995. She overlapped at TRW with [[Dawn Meyerriecks]]. Gerb calls the thin public record of these years "data masking", meaning classified work obscured on a CV. Before becoming PDDNI she was CIA Deputy Director and, for four years, deputy director of the [[CIA Directorate of Science and Technology]].

After leaving the ODNI she joined the board of the [[Aerospace Corporation]] in 2017 and became its chair in 2021. She also sits or sat on the boards of [[Battelle Memorial Institute]], Booz Allen Hamilton and Huntington Ingalls Industries, on the advisory board of [[Oak Ridge National Laboratory]], among the trustees of In-Q-Tel (where [[Glenn Gaffney]] was an executive) and, from about 2018, on the board of Peraton alongside [[Robert Cardillo]]. The video cites [[David Grusch]]'s remarks on Megyn Kelly's show in January 2026, in which he called on Clapper, O'Sullivan and others "in rooms discussing this issue" to come forward. Gerb believes Clapper recruited her to share dual-hatted ODNI and legacy-program roles.

## MITRE Timing Coincidence

Gerb's investigation of [[MITRE Corporation|MITRE]] notes that O'Sullivan's term as Principal Deputy Director of National Intelligence (2011–2017) under [[James Clapper]] lines up exactly with a period in which Gerb alleges MITRE saw a major security clampdown. It began with a 2011 restriction on historical briefings for new legacy program personnel and was followed by MITRE's alleged loss of control, in 2017, over a Navy-administered program to reverse-engineer UFO technology. Gerb considers the overlap unlikely to be a coincidence. He describes O'Sullivan as one of a small number of senior officials, along with Clapper and [[Susan Gordon]], who held Principal Deputy DNI-level positions during the exact window of MITRE's alleged legacy program clampdown.
