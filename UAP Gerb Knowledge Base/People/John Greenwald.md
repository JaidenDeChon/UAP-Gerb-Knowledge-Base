John Greenwald is the founder of [[The Black Vault]], one of the largest privately-run online repositories of declassified US government documents obtained through Freedom of Information Act (FOIA) requests. Greenwald has filed thousands of FOIA requests over decades, focusing heavily on UFO/UAP-related documents and has become one of the foremost experts on navigating government classification systems and disclosure processes.

## Fast Walker FOIA Requests

Greenwald has been a persistent voice demanding transparency on [[Fast Walkers]] — the military designation for objects detected by satellite entering or leaving Earth's atmosphere at extreme speeds. He has submitted multiple FOIA requests specifically seeking information and reports on Fast Walkers and slow Walkers, spanning more than a decade.

### 2013 US Northern Command / NORAD Denial

In 2013, Greenwald submitted a FOIA request to [[US Northern Command]] (which oversees [[NORAD]]) seeking Fast Walker documentation. The command responded that such documentation is "currently and properly classified" under Executive Order 13526, stating it is "considered critical to National Defense or foreign policy and is thus controlled by the US government."

However, NORTHCOM did provide two non-classified research papers:
1. "Orbit Determination of Sunlit Illuminated Objects Detected by Overhead Platforms" (1989)
2. "Space-Based Satellite Tracking and Characterization Utilizing Non-Imaging Passive Sensors" (2008)

Both papers confirmed that [[Defense Support Program (DSP)]] satellites have been detecting and cataloging Fast Walkers since 1972.

### 2023 US Space Force Final Denial

In late 2023, Greenwald received a decisive and final denial from [[US Space Force]] (which absorbed the Air Force space division overseeing DSP operations). Space Force issued a full denial under FOIA Exception One, which covers information "to be kept secret in the interest of National Defense or foreign policy." This represented the government's most recent and unequivocal refusal to disclose any Fast Walker data.

## Significance

Greenwald's work has established the clear timeline and pattern of government secrecy surrounding space-based UFO detection. His documentation of repeated classification invocations proves that Fast Walker data is treated as among the most sensitive information held by the US military — more protected than many traditional defense programs.
