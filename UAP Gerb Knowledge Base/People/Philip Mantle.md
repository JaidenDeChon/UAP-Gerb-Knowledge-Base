Philip Mantle is a British UAP researcher, author, and publisher who has worked extensively on Soviet and Russian UFO documentation. He is co-author, with [[Paul Stonehill]], of *Russia's USO Secrets*, the primary English-language compilation of Soviet [[Unidentified Submerged Object (USO)]] cases, and has contributed to making formerly inaccessible Eastern Bloc UAP records available to Western researchers.

## Russia's USO Secrets

*Russia's USO Secrets*, co-authored with Paul Stonehill, serves as the main documentary source for Soviet USO cases discussed in UAP Gerb's video survey of historical USO sightings. The book draws on Soviet naval records, scientific expedition logs, and firsthand testimony from witnesses who operated within a state apparatus that officially discouraged UFO reporting — making the cases preserved in the book an especially difficult-to-access category of primary source material.
