Philip Mantle is a British UAP researcher, author and publisher who has worked extensively on Soviet and Russian UFO documentation. With [[Paul Stonehill]], he co-wrote *Russia's USO Secrets*, the primary English-language compilation of Soviet [[Unidentified Submerged Object (USO)]] cases. He has also helped make formerly inaccessible Eastern Bloc UAP records available to Western researchers.

## Russia's USO Secrets

*Russia's USO Secrets*, co-written with Paul Stonehill, is the main documentary source for the Soviet USO cases in Gerb's video survey of historical USO sightings. The book draws on Soviet naval records, scientific expedition logs and firsthand testimony from witnesses who worked within a state apparatus that officially discouraged UFO reporting. That makes the cases it preserves a kind of primary source material that is especially difficult to access.
