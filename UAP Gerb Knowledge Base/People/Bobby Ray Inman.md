Bobby Ray Inman is a retired four-star US Navy Admiral and career intelligence official who held some of the most senior positions in American intelligence: Director of Naval Intelligence, Director of the National Security Agency, Deputy Director of the Central Intelligence Agency, and Director of the classified [[National Underwater Reconnaissance Office (NURO)]]. His career gave him simultaneous or sequential access to the NSA, CIA, NRO, and Navy intelligence apparatus — a breadth of cross-agency authority cited by UAP researchers as characteristic of a legacy program gatekeeper. He later served on the board of Wackenhut Services, a private security firm associated with several classified government installations.

## Role in UAP Programs

In 1989, Inman received a telephone inquiry from NASA mission specialist [[Bob Echler]], facilitated by British Admiral Lord Hill Norton, regarding the possibility of making recovered UFO vehicles available for scientific research. Inman told Echler that CIA DS&T Deputy Director [[Everett Heinman]] was "the best person to ask" about recovered craft in operational condition — a referral treated by UAP researchers as evidence that Inman possessed specific knowledge of which CIA officials managed recovered craft programs. UAP researcher Gerb identifies Inman as one of the central alleged gatekeepers of the UFO legacy program infrastructure, noting his ties to the [[NRO (National Reconnaissance Office)]], CIA, NSA, and private intelligence networks.
