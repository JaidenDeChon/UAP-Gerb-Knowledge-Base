Joseph Stafula is a journalist and former agent of the United States Army Criminal Investigation Command (CID) who has passed on anonymous insider testimony about UAP detection by US military satellite systems. An anonymous source gave Stafula claims about a 1984 incident in which a [[Defense Support Program (DSP)]] satellite detected a UAP traveling toward Earth at approximately 22,000 mph. According to these claims, the object passed within 1.5 miles of the DSP satellite and then abruptly changed course, a maneuver inconsistent with any known natural or man-made object.

## Significance

Stafula's reporting links the US military's space-based early warning system to UAP tracking. The DSP satellite network, managed by the [[NRO (National Reconnaissance Office)]], was designed to detect ballistic missile launches with infrared sensors. According to Stafula's sources, these satellites also routinely detected anomalous objects entering and leaving Earth's atmosphere, which [[NORAD]] and US Northern Command designated as "[[Fast Walkers]]".
