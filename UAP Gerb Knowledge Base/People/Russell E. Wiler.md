Russell E. Wiler is a member of the Defense Intelligence Senior Executive Service (DISES) who directed the Air Force Sensitive Activities Office ([[SAFAHA|SAF/AAH]]), part of the Office of the Administrative Assistant to the Secretary of the Air Force at Headquarters Air Force in Arlington, Virginia, from 2009 to 2024. He came to the post after a 36-year Army career that ended with his retirement in 2009. His official biography says he was designated the Air Force focal point for immediate, near-term and emerging needs of the combat air forces, combat support agencies and combatant commanders involving specialized low-density expertise and sensitive activities managed by other government agencies. That language closely follows SAF/AAH's mission under classified DoD directive S-5210.36. When the Department of the Air Force folded sensitive activities into the new Office of Competitive Activities (SAF/OC) in 2024, Wiler became its Director of Plans and Effects (SAF/OCX). A since-deleted 2025 job posting for his successor suggests he retired around mid-2025.

## Alleged Air Force gatekeeper

In *The Hidden Wing*, Gerb presents Wiler as a leading candidate for the redacted "SES2 who's the Air Force gatekeeper" in a 2020 Signal exchange that [[Chris Mellon]] posted on 23 April 2024. The unknown correspondent in that exchange said their group had deciphered the crash-retrieval portfolio's management structure, security controls and ownership, was dealing with the craft recovered at Kingman, Arizona, in 1953, and knew of a still-classified 1950s Secretary of the Air Force memo that keeps the cover on UAPs. Researcher Alex Catz looked at SES-2 officials in senior Air Force posts around 2020 and derived a 12-character name from the redaction. "Russell Wiler" fits that count exactly.

Catz's saved copy of the 2025 job posting is the video's evidence that Wiler held SES tier 2. The posting advertised his former SAF/OCX post as open only to tier 2 SES and DISES candidates, which establishes the position's grade. Gerb notes that Wiler has largely scrubbed himself from the internet. His name nevertheless appears on the Headquarters Air Force organizational chart. Gerb offers [[William E. MacLure]] of [[SAFAAZ|SAF/AAZ]] as the other possible gatekeeper, leans about 60-40 toward Wiler, and lists Wiler among five "hostile witnesses" whom the administration should subpoena or serve with interrogatories.
