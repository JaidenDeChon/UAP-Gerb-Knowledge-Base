Russell E. Wyler is a member of the Defense Intelligence Senior Executive Service (DISES) who directed the Air Force Sensitive Activities Office ([[SAFAHA|SAF/AAH]]), part of the Office of the Administrative Assistant to the Secretary of the Air Force at Headquarters Air Force in Arlington, Virginia, from 2009 to 2024. His official Air Force biography spells the surname Wyler; the captions of Gerb's video, and so this page's title, spell it Wiler. In *The Hidden Wing* Gerb presents him as a leading candidate for the redacted "SES2 Air Force gatekeeper" in a Signal exchange that [[Chris Mellon]] made public.

## Career

Wyler came to the Air Force after a 36-year career in the [[US Army]], where he served as both a noncommissioned and a commissioned officer in command and staff posts in the Army and the joint special operations community, and from which he retired in January 2009. He became director of the Sensitive Activities Office in February 2009. His biography says he was designated the Air Force focal point for immediate, near-term and emerging needs of the combat air forces, combat support agencies and combatant commanders involving specialized low-density expertise and sensitive activities managed by other government agencies. Gerb notes that this language closely follows SAF/AAH's mission under the classified DoD directive S-5210.36. When the Department of the Air Force folded sensitive activities into its new Office of Competitive Activities (SAF/OC), Wyler became its Director of Plans and Effects (SAF/OCX) in December 2024. A since-deleted 2025 job posting for his successor suggests to Gerb that he retired around mid-2025.

## Alleged Air Force gatekeeper

On 23 April 2024 [[Chris Mellon]] posted partly redacted screenshots of a 2020 Signal exchange with an unknown correspondent. The correspondent said their group was making progress in accessing the crash-retrieval portfolio, was dealing with the craft recovered at Kingman, Arizona, in 1953, and knew of a still-classified 1950s Secretary of the Air Force memo that keeps the cover on UAPs. The exchange refers to a redacted "SES2 who's the Air Force gatekeeper". Researcher Alex Catz looked at SES tier 2 officials in senior Air Force posts around 2020 and measured the redacted name at 12 characters. "Russell Wiler" fits that count exactly, as does the biography's spelling, "Russell Wyler".

Catz's saved copy of the 2025 job posting is the video's evidence of Wyler's grade: it advertised his former SAF/OCX post as open only to tier 2 SES and DISES candidates. Gerb notes that Wyler has largely scrubbed himself from the internet, though his name appears on the Headquarters Air Force organizational chart. He offers [[William E. MacLure]] of [[SAFAAZ|SAF/AAZ]] as the other possible gatekeeper, leans about 60-40 toward Wyler, and lists Wyler among five "hostile witnesses" whom the administration should subpoena or serve with interrogatories. If Wyler is the gatekeeper, Gerb suggests, he may have been protecting the history of the Kingman crash.
