Robert D. Morning Star is an American UAP researcher and independent commentator known for his claims about the circumstances of [[Mark McCandlish]]'s death and McCandlish's alleged Congressional testimony. Morning Star publicly stated that McCandlish had been in contact with Senator [[Marco Rubio]] and was scheduled to testify before elements of the US Senate on UAP matters in June 2021. That testimony never took place, because McCandlish died on April 13, 2021, from an apparent self-inflicted gunshot wound. If the claim is accurate, McCandlish died not long before his most consequential planned act of disclosure.

Gerb and other researchers have cited Morning Star's claim as a central element in questioning whether McCandlish's death was self-inflicted. They suggest that his move from informal public advocacy (speaking at conferences and in documentaries) to sworn Senate testimony may have been a triggering factor. Brad Sorenson's comment that he had "protected [McCandlish] until he could not" is also interpreted in light of this alleged testimony timeline.
