The Insider is an anonymous man who, according to [[Michael Herrera]], has spent decades inside a classified program that recovers and reverse-engineers non-human craft, and who calls himself an advocate for disclosure. He first reached Herrera in June 2023, after Herrera's appearance at [[Steven Greer]]'s [[National Press Club]] event, and later had him flown to a secure facility to meet in person. Herrera describes the man he met as a CIA [[Non-Official Cover (NOC)|non-official cover]] officer. The Insider's allegations reach the public only through Herrera and the researcher [[Joey Is Not My Name]], and neither of them vouches for what he says.

## Contact With Herrera

At Greer's two-day event before the National Press Club appearance on 12 June 2023, Herrera presented his theory that the containers he had seen in Indonesia in 2009 carried drugs. Afterwards, in the green room, Greer showed him an encrypted message from someone in attendance: "I don't want to leave Michael hanging, but I know what Michael saw." The sender, Herrera says, knew what the containers were for, and asked Greer to connect him with Herrera. Greer later texted Herrera, at 2 a.m., with the man's number.

Herrera was then flown out to meet him. By Herrera's account, the man had checked every detail of his background to be sure he was not a plant, carried a device that detects implanted chips, handed Herrera a pistol as a gesture of trust, and apologized for the harassment Herrera had suffered. He took Herrera underground to prove who he was. Herrera will not say what he saw there, citing a non-disclosure agreement, but says that if politicians were given free access to the facility it "would single-handedly blow the doors off" the secrecy. It was through these insiders, Herrera says, that he learned the containers held people. He stresses that the insiders he now works with are not the men who held his unit at gunpoint in 2009.

## Alleged Disclosures

Herrera may not describe what he was shown, but the Insider allowed him to share what he was told. According to Herrera's account, as relayed in a Reddit post by Joey Is Not My Name, the Insider alleges:

- Two groups study UAP within the US government: a "blue group" of programs with congressional oversight, such as [[AARO]], [[AATIP (Advanced Aerospace Threat Identification Program)|AATIP]] and [[Project Blue Book]], and a "black group", an organization founded in the 1940s to reverse-engineer non-human technology. Gerb says the black group's lack of oversight mirrors the [[Wilson-Davis Memo]].
- The black group has reverse-engineered non-human craft to an unknown degree and uses them in operations, though the Insider calls them "boring tech" beside other technologies.
- [[EMP-like Weaponry|EMP-like weapons]] can disable UAP, and [[Crash Retrieval|crash retrieval]] programs lure craft to places where they can be brought down and recovered.
- The program recruits people with psionic abilities ([[Psionic Abilities (P3)|"P3"]]), often from developing countries, to interface with non-human technology, and the operation Herrera came upon in Indonesia in 2009 was one such recruitment.

In a later interview Herrera adds that the insiders have been in the program for thirty years or more, are "pro-disclosure to a certain extent", and describe a program split into warring factions that treats the people it takes as "[[Pink Assets|pink assets]]".

## Book Project

A few weeks after the meeting, the Insider told Herrera he was writing a book, *[[Sentinels of Ether]]*, with a ghostwriter, mixing truth and fiction in the way of [[Tom DeLonge]]'s *Sekret Machines*. The two chapters he handed over concern a 2004 event in Mexico, not Herrera's encounter, and were under review by the [[DOPSR (Defense Office of Prepublication and Security Review)|Defense Office of Prepublication and Security Review]]. In the Insider's own words he is playing "[[4D Chess on DOPSR|4D chess]]" with DOPSR, deliberately including deep-black material to see whether the reviewers catch it and treating their response as intelligence.

## Credibility

Joey Is Not My Name says objective data, including the helicopter flight he tracked on radar, proves that Herrera was flown to a secure facility for the meeting, and that others have confirmed the Insider's existence to him. Gerb, who has seen a censored version of that evidence, is highly confident the meeting took place. Neither man vouches for what the Insider said. Gerb weighs two readings without settling on either: that the Insider is a pro-disclosure dissenter using Herrera as a messenger, or that he is feeding Herrera disinformation, as Gerb says the CIA did to [[Paul Bennewitz]] in the 1980s. Joey rejects the Bennewitz comparison, arguing that if Herrera had invented his encounter, an insider claiming to know about it would plainly be lying. Gerb counts the *Sentinels of Ether* manuscript and the "4D chess" with DOPSR among his reasons for doubt.
