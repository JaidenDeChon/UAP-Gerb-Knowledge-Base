Admiral Jay L. Johnson is a retired United States Navy aviator who served as the 26th Chief of Naval Operations (CNO) from 1996 to 2000. He later became an executive in the defense industry, including chairman and chief executive of General Dynamics. In UAP research he appears as the senior Navy officer behind the naval aviation adjunct to the Marine Corps' 1997 [[Hunter Warrior Advanced Warfighting Experiment]].

## Hunter Warrior

UAP Gerb's video on the testimony of Sergeant [[Rodrik Castle]] says that Commandant of the Marine Corps General [[Charles Krulak]] acted "at the behest of" Admiral Johnson as CNO. In late 1996, Krulak asked the Naval Strike and Air Warfare Center at Fallon, Nevada (today's [[Naval Aviation Warfare Development Center (NAWDC)]]) and [[Naval Air Station Lemoore (NAS Lemoore)|Naval Air Station Lemoore]] to supply aircrews to test a "Hunter Air" concept during the exercise. The idea built on Vice Admiral [[Robert J. Spane]]'s 1995 study of carrier aviation supporting troops ashore. The video, which calls him "Admiral J. Johnson", cites that chain of requests as documented evidence that the Navy was integral to Hunter Warrior, though not prominently acknowledged. It then argues that the adjunct gave cover to a clandestine triangular craft and ground team. Johnson is not alleged to have known of any such activity.
