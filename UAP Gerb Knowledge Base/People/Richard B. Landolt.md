Richard B. Landolt is a retired United States Navy rear admiral who commanded the Amphibious Force of the US 7th Fleet (Task Force 76), headquartered in Okinawa, Japan. In that post he served as commander of the US joint task force that delivered relief after the [[2009 Sumatra Earthquake|September 2009 earthquake]] near Padang, Sumatra, an operation in which the [[USS Denver]] and its embarked Marines flew supplies to villages cut off by landslides. He retired from the Navy in February 2014.

## Role in the Herrera Account

In *[[Michael Herrera - Insights into UAP Encounter and Black Program Insiders]]*, the researcher [[Joey Is Not My Name]] identifies Landolt as the rear admiral who debriefed [[Michael Herrera]]'s group aboard the Denver after its mission ashore. Joey reads from a press briefing in which Landolt said the ship's CH-53 helicopters came aboard on the morning of 9 October 2009 and were first used to fly survey teams into the countryside to pick cargo drop sites, and that crowd control was the first concern once the helicopters began arriving with cargo. The interviewers cite the quote as support for Herrera's claim that armed Marines were sent ashore on a humanitarian mission to provide security.
