Art Bell (1945–2018) was an American radio broadcaster and the founder and longtime host of *Coast to Coast AM*, a late-night talk radio program that became the largest overnight radio broadcast in the United States. Bell pioneered the genre of late-night paranormal and conspiracy talk radio, regularly featuring guests discussing UFOs, government cover-ups, remote viewing, time travel, and related subjects. He reached millions of listeners each night and provided a platform for many UAP witnesses and researchers who would not otherwise have reached a mainstream audience.

In UAP research, Bell is notable as the host who broadcast the alleged 1991 North Atlantic USO retrieval testimony. On 19 April 2002, a caller identifying himself as a retired Marine Corps Lieutenant Colonel named Mark described participating in a classified US Navy Deep Submergence Rescue Vehicle (DSRV) operation approximately 250 nautical miles west of Aberdeen, Scotland, in which his crew recovered a triangular, 68–70-foot non-human craft from nearly 1.5 miles of depth. Bell received original sketches of the craft from the naval lieutenant commander involved in the operation and was the intended point of contact to connect Gerb's investigators with Mark. Bell passed away in 2018 before further investigation could be pursued through him.

Bell's program also hosted many other significant UAP-related testimonies and discussions, including appearances by [[Steven Greer]], researchers who discussed the Majestic-12 documents, and witnesses to various crash retrieval incidents documented by [[Leonard Stringfield]].
