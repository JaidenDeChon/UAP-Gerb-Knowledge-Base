Captain Plandowski was an officer of the [[Air Force Office of Special Investigations]] (AFOSI) who, on April 27, 1953, conducted an interview with Master Sergeant [[Ralph Brown]] that produced a document now cited in UAP research. Brown relayed to Plandowski information from a redacted source stating that [[Wright-Patterson Air Force Base]] possessed three flying saucers — one damaged and two functional — and the bodies of their pilots. Plandowski's role in the incident is limited to conducting and recording the interview; the AFOSI document bearing his name is cited as early institutional evidence of UFO-related materials being held at Wright-Patterson.
