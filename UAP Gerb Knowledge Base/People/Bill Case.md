Bill Case was a [[MUFON]] (Mutual UFO Network) investigator who in 1973 led the organization's official investigation into the [[Aurora Texas UFO Crash]] of April 1897. The investigation took place 76 years after the alleged incident. It turned up new eyewitness testimony, and physical anomalies at the supposed burial site that MUFON thought worth further inquiry, although the organization's final report was inconclusive.

## The Aurora Investigation

Case's 1973 investigation identified two previously undocumented witnesses to the alleged 1897 crash:

- [[Mary Evans]], whose parents visited the crash site and told her they had discovered an alien body.
- [[Charlie Stevens]], who at ten years old saw an airship trailing smoke as it headed toward Aurora. His father later went into town to see the wreckage for himself.

Case's team examined the Aurora Cemetery, where the alleged craft's pilot had reportedly been buried with a stone slab. They found:

- A grave marker that appeared to bear an engraved image of a flying saucer.
- Anomalous metal detector readings at the gravesite, which suggested that metal had been buried with the remains.

MUFON formally asked for permission to exhume the site, and the cemetery refused. After the investigation ended, the grave marker disappeared from the cemetery and a pipe was placed over the gravesite. Later metal detector sweeps there found nothing unusual, so Case and MUFON suspected that the material beneath the grave may have been removed.

Case's report called the investigation inconclusive and explicitly declined to rule out a hoax.
