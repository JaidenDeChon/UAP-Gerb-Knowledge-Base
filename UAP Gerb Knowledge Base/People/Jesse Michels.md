Jesse Michels is a YouTuber and UAP interviewer known for research-oriented content on UFO-related topics. Gerb's captions spell his surname "Michaels". Gerb has recommended Michels's video on [[T. Townsend Brown]] and [[Electrogravitics]] as essential viewing for understanding the scientific lineage behind claims of electrogravitic propulsion. Gerb appeared on a show with Michels and has cited his work as complementary to the investigations into the [[Alien Reproduction Vehicle (ARV)]] and the [[TR-3B]].
