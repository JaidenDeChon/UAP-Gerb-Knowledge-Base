J. Robert Beyster (1924-2014) was an American physicist and entrepreneur who founded [[SAIC]] in 1969. A former U.S. Navy officer and nuclear physicist, he built SAIC around an employee-ownership model and a high-clearance technical workforce that became deeply embedded in U.S. defense and intelligence contracting.

## Role in SAIC's Growth

Beyster designed SAIC as a science-and-systems contractor capable of serving classified government missions at scale. Under his leadership, the company rapidly expanded into defense analysis, information systems, and mission support contracts tied to the Department of Defense and intelligence community.
