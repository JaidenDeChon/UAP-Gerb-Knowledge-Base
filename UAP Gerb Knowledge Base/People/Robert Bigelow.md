Robert Bigelow is an American businessman who founded the Budget Suites of America hotel chain and the space habitat company Bigelow Aerospace, and who has funded UFO and paranormal research for decades, including the National Institute for Discovery Science and the study of Skinwalker Ranch in Utah. His company [[Bigelow Aerospace Advanced Space Studies]] (BAASS) held the Defense Intelligence Agency contract for [[AAWSAP]], the program Senator [[Harry Reid]] started in 2007 and 2008.

## The Lockheed material

In the account UAP Gerb assembles in *Lockheed Martin - UFO Reverse Engineering, Material Exploitation, & Legacy Programs*, Bigelow's BAASS was the intended recipient of recovered non-human material that [[Lockheed Martin]] tried to divest between 2008 and 2011. BAASS designed a 5,000 square foot underground vault in 2009 for recovered hardware and biological samples "from the corporate world", and Lockheed vice president [[James T. Ryder]] proposed transferring 1950s crash retrieval material into the prospective DHS program [[Kona Blue]]. The CIA's [[Glenn Gaffney]] is alleged to have blocked the transfer.
