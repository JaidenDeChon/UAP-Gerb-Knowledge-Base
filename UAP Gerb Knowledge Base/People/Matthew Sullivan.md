Matthew Sullivan, call sign "Quake," was a decorated U.S. Air Force intelligence officer who served within Air Force Intelligence, the National Security Agency, and the National Air and Space Intelligence Center (NASIC). He earned a Bronze Star for Valor for his service in Operation Enduring Freedom. Sullivan died in 2023, in what his family and associates have described as an "accidental overdose," shortly before he was reportedly set to travel to Capitol Hill to testify about his knowledge of UAP-related activities.

## Role in UAP Programs

[[David Grusch]] has publicly identified Sullivan as a personal friend, a fellow Air Force intelligence officer he served alongside, and one of the sources for his UAP-related investigation. In a Fox News interview cited in Gerb's *Special Access Required Vol.2*, Grusch said that Sullivan "passed away mysteriously before he was willing... to come to the Hill to testify about his knowledge of these activities," and that his death remains an ongoing FBI matter. Grusch declined to characterize it further. In an earlier remark, Grusch had referred to an unnamed NASIC analyst who allegedly did UAP-related work covertly, without informing his chain of command: "the night job of a certain analyst at NASC". Because Sullivan was assigned to NASIC, Gerb links that remark to him. Gerb presents Sullivan's death as a possible example of the "wet works" and program-protection activity that, the video alleges, legacy UFO programs have used against prospective whistleblowers. He also notes that the matter remains an open federal investigation.
