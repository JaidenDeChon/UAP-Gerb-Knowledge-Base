Michael B. Johnstone is a UFO researcher who conducted the interview that the case of the anonymous Marine whistleblower known as "[[RB]]" rests on, and who put RB in touch with researcher [[Leonard Stringfield]]. Stringfield later published the record of Johnstone's 1986 interview in *UFO Crash/Retrieval Status Report 5* under the case title "Marine Guards a Disc-Shaped Vehicle, 1963."

## Role in the RB Case

Johnstone put Stringfield in touch with RB after learning of the former Marine's account. In 1986 he interviewed RB in detail and recorded his claims that in December 1963 he had guarded a 40-foot disc-shaped craft of non-human origin at an undisclosed military base. Stringfield used this interview as the primary source for his status report.

In the 2000s, aerospace historian [[Michael Schratt]] was granted access to Stringfield's personal archives at [[MUFON]] in Cincinnati, where he found letters between Stringfield and Johnstone among Stringfield's notes. In 2015 Schratt interviewed Johnstone again to gather more details about RB's account for his own published research.
