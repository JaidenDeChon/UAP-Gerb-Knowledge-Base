John Ramirez is a former CIA operative who has made public claims about the US intelligence community's involvement with UAP and unidentified submerged object (USO) phenomena. He became known for statements about a "galactic federation" and other fringe UAP claims. He has also made more specific, though unverified, claims about institutional relationships between naval and CIA bodies involved in recovering USO material.

## WINPAC and Office of Naval Research Claims (2022)

In 2022, Ramirez claimed he had heard rumors of a relationship between the [[Office of Naval Research]] and the CIA's Weapons Intelligence, Nonproliferation, and Arms Control Center (WINPAC), through which USO-related materials recovered from the ocean are transferred to the CIA. Gerb cites the claim when noting that USO research overlaps with the CIA's institutional interests in ways that are still poorly documented. The claims are described as unverified, and as part of a wider pattern of figures close to the intelligence community making incremental disclosures about UAP/USO programs.

Gerb says he intends to cover the CIA's UAP connections more thoroughly in a video of their own.

## Galactic Federation Claims

The transcript describes Ramirez as the person who "made the galactic federations claims famous". Those sensational UAP claims drew significant public attention, but Gerb's analysis does not treat them as credible. His WINPAC claims are treated as a separate matter and as more plausible.
