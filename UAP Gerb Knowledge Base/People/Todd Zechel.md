Todd Zechel is an American UFO researcher and an investigator for the National Investigation Committee on Aerial Phenomena (NICAP). He is known for archival and field research into mid-century UAP cases, including tracking down obscure witnesses and obtaining signed affidavits.

## Willingham Affidavit

In NICAP's files, Zechel found clippings of a 1965 *Mechanicsburg* (Pennsylvania) newspaper article. The article was the first public appearance of Colonel [[Robert Willingham]]'s account of a 1955 UAP crash near [[Langtry, Texas]]. In 1977, twelve years after it was published, Zechel tracked down Willingham and obtained a signed [[Willingham Affidavit|affidavit]] from him. The affidavit disagrees with Willingham's later account in places, including the aircraft type and base (an F-94 at Dyess Air Force Base in the affidavit, an F-86 out of Carswell in the 2008 book). It gives no year; the 1948 date is from the newspaper story, and the book gives 1955. Zechel himself dated the crash to December 1950. Gerb cites these discrepancies as significant credibility concerns.

> [!edit] Corrected 16 October 2026
> Earlier versions said the affidavit dates the incident to 1948. In Kevin Randle's transcription it gives no date; the 1948 date comes from the earlier newspaper telling.

Zechel also reportedly obtained a declassified USAF document stating the military had recovered a foreign object on December 6 or 7, 1950, and transported it to [[Carswell Air Force Base]]. The document may be connected to the El Indio 1950 crash case. No proof of it has been produced publicly, however, so it cannot be added to the evidentiary record.
