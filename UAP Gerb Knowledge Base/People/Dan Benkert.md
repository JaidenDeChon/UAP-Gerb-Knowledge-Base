Dan Benkert is a UFO researcher who has co-authored work with [[Michael Schratt]] and has been a staunch supporter of [[Edgar Fouché]]'s [[TR-3B]] testimony. Benkert participated in group email discussions organized by [[David Hilton]] regarding the authenticity of Fouché's claims and military documents. He offered a counter-explanation to a March 19, 2016, article titled "Edgar Fuché's TR-3B Is Most Likely a Hoax."

In a June 2012 Skype conversation, Hilton made disturbing remarks to Benkert, claiming to work for a monitoring agency and asserting control over Fouché.
