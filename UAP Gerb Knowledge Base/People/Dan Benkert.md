Dan Benkert is a UFO researcher who has co-authored work with [[Michael Schratt]] and has strongly supported [[Edgar Fouché]]'s [[TR-3B]] testimony. Benkert took part in group email discussions, organized by [[David Hilton]], about whether Fouché's claims and military documents were authentic. He also offered a counter-explanation to a March 19, 2016, article titled "Edgar Fuché's TR-3B Is Most Likely a Hoax."

In a June 2012 Skype conversation, Hilton made disturbing remarks to Benkert: he claimed to work for a monitoring agency and said he had control over Fouché.
