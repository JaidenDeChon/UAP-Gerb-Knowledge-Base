Eric Taber is a former aerospace defense contractor and UAP whistleblower who gave testimony to [[AARO]] (the All-domain Anomaly Resolution Office) in 2023 and signed a Memorandum for Record of it. In it he passes on what his late great-uncle told him: that an intact, egg-shaped non-human craft found in the Nevada desert was kept at [[Area 51]]. His account became public in December 2023, and Gerb returns to it in four videos as a reference point for egg-shaped craft.

## AARO Testimony

Taber's testimony centers on what his great-uncle, Sam Urquhart, told him. Urquhart worked at [[Area 51]] as a contractor and was head of security for an engineering group, the radar cross-section training team. According to Taber, his great-uncle said that while working there he learned the base housed an egg-shaped UFO that had been found intact in the Nevada desert. Gerb shows a project patch from Area 51 that Taber supplied and that, Taber says, belonged to his great-uncle. Gerb calls the name "a little tough to spell"; the spelling Urquhart is the one given in the December 2023 press reports of the account.

Gerb stresses that Taber put the account in a legal document to AARO, the office Congress set up through the National Defense Authorization Acts for 2022 and 2023: "true or not this is extremely interesting as Taber has submitted a legal document to Arrow testifying that this is in fact true."

## The egg-shaped craft

As Gerb summarises Taber's account, the craft was kept at Area 51 in the 1980s. It was smooth, with no rivets, no welded seams and no visible entry points. Gerb sets it beside several other accounts (see [[Egg-shaped UAP]]):

- the egg-shaped craft [[Jonathan Weygandt]] says he found at the 1997 [[Peru UFO Crash Incident|Peru crash site]], which differed mainly in having gill-like vents. Gerb cites both as technology he doubts humans have been able to reverse-engineer;
- the egg-shaped craft, with no windows, wings or landing gear, that [[Walter Haut]]'s 2002 affidavit places in Building 84 at Roswell;
- the egg made from one piece of metal that the [[Infographic Show Whistleblower]] says was recovered after a crash in Vietnam.

Because Taber's egg was found untouched in the desert, Gerb also ties it to the [[Craft Donation Theory]], the idea that some craft were left to be recovered.
