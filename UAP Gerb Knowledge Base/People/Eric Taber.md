Eric Taber is a UAP whistleblower who submitted a Memorandum for Record to AARO (the All-domain Anomaly Resolution Office). In it he passes on what a family member told him: that a recovered non-human craft was kept at [[Area 51]]. His submission is one of the AARO testimonies known to have been made public.

## AARO Testimony

Taber's testimony centers on what his great-uncle told him. The great-uncle was a contractor at [[Area 51]], where he was head of security for an Engineering Group known internally as the radar cross-section training team. According to Taber, his great-uncle said that this role gave him access to information that the base housed an egg-shaped UFO, found intact in the Nevada desert. Along with his memorandum, Taber gave AARO a project patch from Area 51 that he says his great-uncle gave him.

Because the memorandum is a legal document submitted to a federal office, Taber's account falls under the formal congressional and executive framework for UAP disclosure set up by the National Defense Authorization Acts of 2022 and 2023.

## The egg-shaped craft

As Gerb summarises Taber's account, the craft at Area 51 was smooth, with no rivets, no welded seams and no visible entry points. Gerb compares it with the egg-shaped craft [[Jonathan Weygandt]] says he found at the 1997 [[Peru UFO Crash Incident|Peru crash site]], which differed mainly in having gill-like vents. He cites both as examples of technology he doubts humans have been able to reverse-engineer. See [[Egg-shaped UAP]].
