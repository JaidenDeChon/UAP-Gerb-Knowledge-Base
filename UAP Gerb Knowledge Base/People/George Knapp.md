George Knapp is an American investigative journalist and television news anchor based in Las Vegas, Nevada, widely regarded as one of the leading mainstream journalists covering UAP and related phenomena. He is a host of *Coast to Coast AM*, the syndicated overnight radio program, where he has interviewed many prominent UAP witnesses and researchers.

## Key Interviews and Investigations

In June 2018, Knapp interviewed physicist and UAP researcher [[Eric Davis]] on *Coast to Coast AM*. Davis endorsed the Del Rio, Texas region as a particularly credible UAP crash retrieval case, saying "your bets really good" on Del Rio as well as Roswell. He also mentioned other cases that he declined to identify because they were classified. Gerb uses this interview to open his video [[Videos/The 1950s Del Rio, Texas UFO Crashes/summary|The 1950s Del Rio, Texas UFO Crashes]].

Knapp also recorded a long interview with [[Philip J. Corso]], later featured on *Weaponized*, in which Corso discussed non-human encounters and the reverse engineering of recovered UAP technology.

### Alfred O'Donnell and the Needles Crash

In 1990, while researching Area 51, S-4 and [[EG&G]], Knapp was tipped off by a relative of senior EG&G manager [[Alfred O'Donnell]]. O'Donnell told him a flying saucer recovered in New Mexico, with a live being, was held at [[Indian Springs Air Force Base]] until 1955 and then moved beside [[Area 51]]; he later gave Annie Jacobsen a very different account. Knapp's I-Team also investigated the [[2008 Needles UFO Crash Retrieval|2008 Needles crash]]. Witnesses there described military convoys and personnel with Nevada Test Site uniforms or NNSA badges. Knapp's reporting identified the convoys as the [[Office of Secure Transportation (OST)|Office of Secure Transportation]]. Gerb draws on both stories in his investigation of [[Sandia National Laboratories]].
