George Knapp is an American investigative journalist and television news anchor based in Las Vegas, Nevada. He is widely regarded as one of the leading mainstream journalists covering UAP and related phenomena. As a host of *Coast to Coast AM*, the syndicated overnight radio program, he has interviewed many well-known UAP witnesses and researchers. Gerb's videos cite him as a source more than they discuss him. He is the journalist who heard [[Alfred O'Donnell]]'s account of a saucer held by [[EG&G]], who reported the [[2008 Needles UFO Crash Retrieval|2008 Needles crash]], who published the list of [[AATIP (Advanced Aerospace Threat Identification Program)|AATIP]] studies, and who drew Del Rio out of [[Eric Davis]] on the radio.

## Career

Knapp was born on 18 April 1952 in Woodbury, New Jersey, and grew up in Northern California. He studied communication at the University of West Georgia and the University of the Pacific. He joined KLAS-TV, Channel 8 in Las Vegas, as a general assignment reporter in 1981, and since 1995 has been the chief reporter of its I-Team investigative unit. He brought [[Bob Lazar]] to public attention in 1989. His work has won Peabody, Edward R. Murrow and regional Emmy awards. He is also a frequent host of *Coast to Coast AM* and is involved with the *Weaponized* podcast.

## Key Interviews and Investigations

In June 2018, Knapp interviewed physicist and UAP researcher [[Eric Davis]] on *Coast to Coast AM*. Davis endorsed the Del Rio, Texas region as a particularly credible UAP crash retrieval case, saying "your bets really good" on Del Rio as well as Roswell. He also mentioned other cases that he declined to identify because they were classified. Gerb uses this interview to open his video [[Videos/The 1950s Del Rio, Texas UFO Crashes/summary|The 1950s Del Rio, Texas UFO Crashes]].

[[Philip J. Corso]] told Knapp, as he told [[John B. Alexander]] and [[Jacques Vallee]], about his claimed encounter with a non-human being at White Sands. A long-lost interview in which Corso draws the being was later shown on *Weaponized*.

According to Gerb, in July 2018 Knapp released a leaked list of the 38 Defense Intelligence Reference Documents commissioned under [[AATIP (Advanced Aerospace Threat Identification Program)|AATIP]]. FOIA requests by [[John Greenewald]] of The Black Vault later confirmed the list. Gerb credits Eric Davis with the papers in it on traversable wormholes, anti-gravity and energy from the quantum vacuum. Gerb has also said he would like to ask Knapp whether the Russian UFO files Knapp reportedly smuggled out of Russia include material on unidentified submerged objects.

### Alfred O'Donnell and the Needles Crash

In 1990, while researching Area 51, S-4 and [[EG&G]], Knapp was tipped off by a relative of senior EG&G manager [[Alfred O'Donnell]]. O'Donnell told him a flying saucer recovered in New Mexico, with a live being, was held at [[Indian Springs Air Force Base]] until 1955 and then moved beside [[Area 51]]. Knapp believes the saucer came from Roswell. After an EG&G co-founder approved a meeting between Knapp, O'Donnell and congressional staffer Dick D'Amato, who oversaw special access programs, O'Donnell stopped talking about UFOs; he later gave Annie Jacobsen a very different account.

Knapp's I-Team also investigated the [[2008 Needles UFO Crash Retrieval|2008 Needles crash]], reporting on it in 2010 and again in a 14 May 2020 article. Witnesses there described military convoys and personnel with Nevada Test Site uniforms or NNSA badges. Knapp's reporting identified the convoys as the [[Office of Secure Transportation (OST)|Office of Secure Transportation]]. Gerb draws on both stories in his investigation of [[Sandia National Laboratories]].
