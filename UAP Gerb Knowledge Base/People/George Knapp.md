George Knapp is an American investigative journalist and television news anchor based in Las Vegas, Nevada, widely regarded as one of the foremost mainstream media figures covering UAP and related phenomena. He serves as a host on *Coast to Coast AM*, the syndicated overnight radio program, through which he has conducted numerous interviews with prominent UAP witnesses and researchers.

## Key Interviews and Investigations

In June 2018, Knapp interviewed physicist and UAP researcher [[Eric Davis]] on *Coast to Coast AM*. During this conversation, Davis endorsed the Del Rio, Texas region as a particularly credible UAP crash retrieval case — specifically stating "your bets really good" on Del Rio alongside Roswell — and referenced additional cases he declined to identify due to their classified status. The interview is cited by UAP Gerb as the opening context for the [[Video - The 1950s Del Rio, Texas UFO Crashes]].

Knapp also conducted a long-form interview with [[Philip J. Corso]] that was later featured on *Weaponized*, in which Corso discussed non-human encounters and the reverse engineering of recovered UAP technology.
