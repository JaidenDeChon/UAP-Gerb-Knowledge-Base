Harry Mason Reid (1939-2021) was a Democratic United States Senator from Nevada from 1987 to 2017 and Senate Majority Leader from 2007 to 2015. As majority leader he was a member of the congressional "Gang of Eight," the small group of leaders briefed on the most sensitive intelligence matters. In UFO research he is best known as the sponsor of the Defense Intelligence Agency's [[AAWSAP]], and for later saying he believed the government held UFO material that he had been denied access to.

## AAWSAP and Kona Blue

Reid secured about $22 million for AAWSAP, which the [[Defense Intelligence Agency]] ran from 2008 to 2012 through a contract with Bigelow Aerospace Advanced Space Studies. The program is often confused with the informal [[AATIP (Advanced Aerospace Threat Identification Program)|AATIP]] associated with [[Luis Elizondo]], which Elizondo describes as funded by Reid. According to [[David Grusch]] and reporting cited by UAP Gerb, one purpose of AAWSAP was to receive recovered material from [[Lockheed Martin]] through [[Kona Blue]], a proposed waived special access program that Reid championed and that was blocked by intelligence officials. Reid's petition to give AATIP special access program status was reportedly denied by the Defense Department.

## "I was denied access for decades"

In *Special Access Required Vol.2*, UAP Gerb plays David Grusch describing a meeting with Reid in Reid's living room about nine months before his death, with other witnesses present. Grusch wanted to learn what Reid, as a former Gang of Eight member, actually knew and why he had funded AAWSAP. According to Grusch, Reid told him: "Yeah, I knew we had UFO material. I was denied access for decades." UAP Gerb uses the account to argue that even the Gang of Eight, which should be told of waived unacknowledged programs, was kept out of the legacy portfolio.
