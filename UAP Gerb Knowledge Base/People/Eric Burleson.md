Eric Burleson serves as a United States Representative from Missouri and is a member of the bipartisan [[UAP Caucus]], a legislative group focused on UAP transparency and disclosure. Burleson has worked alongside fellow caucus members to investigate alleged UFO crash retrieval programs and push for declassification of historical UAP materials.

## UAP Caucus Membership

As a member of the [[UAP Caucus]] alongside Representatives [[Tim Burchett]], [[Anna Paulina Luna]], and [[Jared Moskowitz]], Burleson has supported efforts to:

- Bring historical UFO crash retrieval events (including the [[1978 Bolivia UFO Crash]]) to Congressional investigation
- Support legislative provisions requiring UAP program transparency in the National Defense Authorization Act (NDAA)
- Coordinate Congressional hearings featuring UAP whistleblower testimony
- Advocate for legal protections for whistleblowers disclosing classified UAP information

## Legislative Objectives

Burleson's participation in UAP oversight reflects Congressional interest in:

- Investigating Special Access Programs that allegedly operate beyond normal Congressional authority
- Examining defense contractor involvement in UAP crash retrieval and reverse engineering
- Mandating declassification of historical UAP programs such as [[Project Moondust]]
- Addressing Constitutional questions regarding unacknowledged programs that evade legislative oversight

## Role in Disclosure Movement

Burleson has supported the UAP Caucus's efforts to highlight documentary evidence of government UFO programs, including Project Moon Dust, which operated as a classified crash retrieval program from at least 1961 through the mid-1990s. The caucus has used declassified documents obtained through Freedom of Information Act requests to challenge official denials and force acknowledgment of historical programs.

## Bipartisan Approach

Burleson's involvement demonstrates that UAP transparency has bipartisan support in Congress, with Republicans and Democrats working together through the UAP Caucus to overcome institutional resistance to disclosure from defense and intelligence agencies.
