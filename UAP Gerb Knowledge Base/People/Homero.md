Lieutenant Homero was a Brazilian military officer who witnessed the famous [[Trinidade Island UFO Photographs|1958 Trinidade Island UFO sighting]] alongside photographer [[Aliro Barana]] and 46 other passengers aboard the vessel *Almirante Saldanha*.

## Trinidade Island Sighting (1958)

Homero was among 48 total witnesses who observed a metallic disc-shaped craft near [[Trinidade Island]], located 600 miles off the Brazilian mainland. The craft—which Barana successfully photographed—was described as:

- Gray metallic and solid-looking
- Surrounded by a greenish haze or mist
- Featuring a ring running through its midsection
- Resembling a flattened Saturn

The UFO flew behind Dado Peak on the island, then reversed direction and approached the vessel closer and at faster speeds. Homero's presence as a military lieutenant adds institutional credibility to the sighting, establishing the incident as a military-documented case rather than purely civilian testimony.

## Significance

Homero's role as a named military witness elevates the Trinidade photographs beyond anonymous testimony. His status as a lieutenant provides:

1. **Institutional credibility** — Military training in observation and reporting
2. **Named corroboration** — Specific identified witness rather than generic "crew members"
3. **Multi-witness documentation** — One of 48 observers, establishing the sighting as mass-witnessed

The Trinidade case is referenced by [[Kevin Knuth]] in his academic presentations on UAP physics as an example of well-documented historical UFO encounters with strong evidentiary support from credible witnesses.
