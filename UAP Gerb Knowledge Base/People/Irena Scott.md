Dr. Irena McCammon Scott is an Ohio-based scientist and UFO researcher. She served on the board of directors of the [[MUFON|Mutual UFO Network]] (MUFON) from 1993 to 2000 and edited the *Ohio UFO Notebook*, the publication of the Mid-Ohio Research Associates (MORA), a group of which she was a founding member. She holds a PhD from the University of Missouri College of Veterinary Medicine and has worked at [[Battelle Memorial Institute]] and, as a PhD-level physical scientist, for the Defense Intelligence Agency. In UAP research she is best known for recording the account of Battelle chemical engineer [[EJ Center]], who allegedly said he had studied parts of a crashed flying saucer. The transcript of Gerb's video spells her name "Irene Scott".

## The EJ Center Account

In 1992 a man who had attended North High School in Columbus, Ohio, in the late 1950s came forward to MUFON. In 1958, he said, he had dated Center's daughter, and one night at her home her father told them that while working at Battelle he had been "responsible for a project which required him to study Parts retrieved from a flying saucer." Scott published the account in the summer of 1994 in the *Ohio UFO Notebook*, in a piece titled "The Ohio UFO Crash Connection and Other Stories", under a section headed "Decoding the I-Beam". The heading refers to the marked I-beam [[Jesse Marcel]] described among the [[Roswell Crash|Roswell]] debris.

Gerb gives the account weight because of its timing. Center's 1949 Battelle work on titanium alloys, including [[Nitinol (Nickel-Titanium Alloy)|nitinol]], for [[Wright-Patterson Air Force Base]] was not declassified until 2010, sixteen years after Scott's publication, so the account could not have been built from those documents.

## Other Work

Scott has written several books on the subject, including *Sacred Corridors: Secrets Behind the Real Project Blue Book*, which covers Wright-Patterson, Roswell, Battelle and memory metal, and *UFOs Today: 70 Years of Lies, Disinformation, and Government Cover-Up*. She has also written for the MUFON UFO Journal, the International UFO Reporter and *FATE* magazine.
