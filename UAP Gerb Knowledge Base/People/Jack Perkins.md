Jack Perkins was a Pennsylvania electronics engineer who served in the Civil Air Patrol with Colonel [[Robert Willingham]]. Willingham had seen a disc-shaped craft crash during a Cold War simulation mission from [[Carswell Air Force Base]]. Afterwards, in spring 1955, Perkins went with him on a private flight to investigate the alleged UAP crash site near [[Langtry, Texas]], in a single-engine 1947 Aeronca Champion.

At the crash site, Mexican soldiers guarding the perimeter would not let Perkins near the wreckage. Only Willingham was allowed limited access: the offer came from an officer, Lieutenant Martinez, because Willingham was wearing USAF fatigues. Perkins watched from a distance and could see the disintegrated craft spread across a 300-foot swath on the Mexican side of the Rio Grande.

Perkins' identity was withheld during Willingham's lifetime and was only revealed publicly after Perkins' death in 2002.
