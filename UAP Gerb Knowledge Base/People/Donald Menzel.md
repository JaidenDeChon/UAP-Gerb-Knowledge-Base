Donald Howard Menzel (1901–1976) was an American astronomer and director of the Harvard College Observatory. He was among the best-known scientific sceptics of UFOs in the 1950s and 1960s and wrote several books explaining sightings as natural phenomena. The [[Eisenhower Briefing Document]] nonetheless lists him as an original member of [[Majestic 12]], and the video describes him as a high-security consultant to the NSA and CIA.

## In the Majestic documents

According to the Eisenhower Briefing Document, Menzel was part of the team that concluded the craft recovered at [[Roswell Crash|Roswell]] in 1947 was of non-human origin and probably came from a distant solar system. A memo from [[Roscoe H. Hillenkoetter]] among the Majestic documents congratulates him on a book. Gerb reads the memo as praise for obscuring the subject. On the [[Bowen Manuscript]], a handwritten note attributed to [[Vannevar Bush]] asks "what is Don Menzel doing?"

[[Stanton Friedman]] found Menzel's inclusion on the list especially surprising, given his public role as a debunker. Friedman's archival research turned up Menzel's classified consulting work and extensive correspondence between him and other alleged members.
