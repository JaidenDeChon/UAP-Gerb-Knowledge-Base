Betty Jean Sapp (born 1955; spelled "Betty Sap" in some UAP Gerb captions) is a former US Air Force officer and CIA officer. She was Principal Deputy Director of the [[NRO (National Reconnaissance Office)|National Reconnaissance Office]] from April 2009 and then the NRO's 18th Director from July 2012 to April 2019, the first woman to hold that post. She joined the CIA in 1997 after a career as an Air Force officer, and she holds a bachelor's degree and an MBA from the University of Missouri.

## Role in UFO Legacy Program Research

In his account of the "modern era" of UFO legacy programs, Gerb names Sapp, along with Director of National Intelligence [[James Clapper]] and his deputies [[Stephanie O'Sullivan]] and Michael Dempsey, as part of the diminished leadership close to the National Security Council that took shape after [[Dick Cheney]] left government in January 2009. The video describes that period as lacking anyone with the centralized authority Cheney once held over the portfolio of legacy programs. Gerb also links Sapp, as NRO director, to [[Immaculate Constellation (IMCON)]], an unacknowledged special access program from 2017 that is theorized to have drawn on NRO satellite collection to build a centralized repository of UAP data.
