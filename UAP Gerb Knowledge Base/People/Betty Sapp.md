Betty Jean Sapp (born 1955; referred to as "Betty Sap" in some UAP Gerb captions) is a former US Air Force officer and CIA officer who served as Principal Deputy Director of the [[NRO (National Reconnaissance Office)|National Reconnaissance Office]] from April 2009 before becoming the NRO's 18th Director from July 2012 to April 2019 — the first woman to hold the position. She joined the CIA in 1997 after a career as an Air Force officer, and holds a bachelor's degree and an MBA from the University of Missouri.

## Role in UFO Legacy Program Research

UAP Gerb's account of the "modern era" of UFO legacy programs identifies Sapp, alongside Director of National Intelligence [[James Clapper]] and his deputies [[Stephanie O'Sullivan]] and Michael Dempsey, as part of the diminished National Security Council-adjacent leadership that emerged after [[Dick Cheney]]'s departure from government in January 2009 — a period the video characterizes as lacking any figure with Cheney's former centralized authority over the legacy program portfolio. As NRO director, Sapp is separately linked by UAP Gerb to the 2017 [[Immaculate Constellation (IMCON)]] unacknowledged special access program, theorized to have drawn on NRO satellite collection to build a centralized UAP data repository.
