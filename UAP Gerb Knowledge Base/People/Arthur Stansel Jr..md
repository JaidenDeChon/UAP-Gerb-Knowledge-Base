Arthur Stansel Jr. was an engineer employed under the Air Research and Development Command (ARDC) in the early 1950s. He is the primary known witness to the alleged [[1953 Kingman, Arizona Crash Retrieval]], claiming he was summoned on 21 May 1953 to evaluate a crashed unknown vehicle found near [[Kingman, Arizona]]. Stansel described the craft as an oval-shaped object approximately 30 feet in diameter, made of a brushed aluminum-like material, and reported seeing two small bodies in flight suits in a nearby tent at the recovery site. Stansel initially came forward anonymously under the pseudonym "Fritz Werner" — a name borrowed from a German rocket scientist — when he disclosed his account to UFO researcher [[Raymond Fowler]] in 1973, later allowing his real name to be published.

## Role in UAP Programs

Stansel identified [[Edward Bushnell Doll]] as the official who coordinated his assignment to the Kingman crash site. His testimony is treated by UAP researchers as a founding data point in the alleged chain of non-human craft exploitation that passed through TRW-connected networks and ultimately to [[Northrop Grumman]]. The Kingman case, centering on Stansel's account, is cited in the video as the earliest documented link between the personnel network later associated with [[TRW]] and clandestine UAP material exploitation.
