"Thomas Cantwheel" (given in full as Thomas Cryll "Cy" Cantwheel) is the pseudonym of the main alleged source of the [[MJ-12 Documents]] sent to researcher [[Tim Cooper]] in the 1990s. From 1992 he took responsibility for many of the mailbox drops of Majestic material, among them the [[Interplanetary Phenomenon Unit]] Report and Field Order, the Hillenkoetter MAJIC memo, the First Annual Report, and a Kennedy-to-CIA-director document. Near death from cancer, he wrote to Cooper describing contacts with [[Leonard Stringfield]], [[Stanton Friedman]] and intelligence personnel, and enclosed a sketch of the [[Roswell Crash|Roswell]] craft.

## Claimed background

Cooper set out Cantwheel's account in a five-page affidavit shared with Friedman and [[Robert Wood]]. Cantwheel said he had served in the Counter Intelligence Corps' Scientific and Technical Branch, [[Interplanetary Phenomenon Unit]], from 1942 to 1958, and in the CIC until 1980. He also said he had worked in CIA covert operations in the 1960s, had contacts in the FBI, KGB and GRU, and had retired as a lieutenant colonel after involvement with the "Majestic community" and the 5412 group. A woman calling herself "Selena", said to be his daughter, sent Cooper ten further short memos, the Twining "White Hot" Report, an Eisenhower-to-Oppenheimer memo and a Twining-to-Truman directive.

## Identification

Reddit researcher "Harry is White Hot" has proposed that Cantwheel was Boris Tarasoff, a Russian-born US Army Counter Intelligence Corps officer (1942–1945). Tarasoff trained at Camp Ritchie, Maryland, attended the advanced CIC school in Oberammergau, Germany, and then spent 1945–1956 at the American Red Cross in Cleveland, Ohio. The researcher suggests that job was cover for IPU work near [[Wright Field]]. Tarasoff worked for the CIA from 1956 to 1968, including a 1963 posting in Mexico City translating surveillance traffic used as evidence of Lee Harvey Oswald's visits to Soviet compounds. A CIA personal history released in 1998 placed him in San Leandro, California, where Cooper believed Cantwheel lived, and he would have been about 88 at the time of Cooper's investigation.
