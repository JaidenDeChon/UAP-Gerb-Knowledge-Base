Rodrik John Castle III (the video's title spells his first name "Roderick") is a veteran and former sergeant (E-5) of the [[United States Marine Corps]] who served from 1994 to 1999. His primary military occupational specialty (MOS) was 6531, aircraft ordnance technician, and he served with [[VMA-513|VMA-513 Flying Nightmares]], an AV-8B Harrier attack squadron stationed out of [[MCAS Yuma]], Arizona. Castle led a team of six to eight Marines in aviation ordnance and also trained as a helicopter flight medic. He is now a prominent UAP witness. His testimony describes a firsthand encounter with an enormous triangular craft and a clandestine armed unit during the 1997 [[Hunter Warrior Advanced Warfighting Experiment]] at [[Marine Corps Air Ground Combat Center 29 Palms]].

## Military Service

Castle served at Marine Aircraft Group 13 (MAG-13) of the 3rd Marine Aircraft Wing (3rd MAW) at MCAS Yuma. His squadron, VMA-513 "The Flying Nightmares," operated AV-8B Harrier jets. These are STOVL (short takeoff and vertical landing) aircraft with Rolls-Royce Pegasus engines and four rotating nozzles that can direct thrust for vertical landing and for hovering in place. Castle's familiarity with VTOL and STOVL technology, helicopter morphology, and Harrier flight dynamics left him well equipped to distinguish the craft he encountered from any known aircraft.

## The 1997 Encounter

During the [[Hunter Warrior Advanced Warfighting Experiment]], a 12-day operation running from approximately March 2–14, 1997, Castle and his team were attached to an experimental special-purpose marine air-ground task force (SPMAGTF-X). One night during the exercise, Castle's team was performing overnight crash retrieval duties for Harrier jets when they received a radio call about unexplained or unauthorized flare activity approximately three to five miles out in the desert.

### The Craft

Driving in a Humvee with fire suppression equipment, Castle's team crested a hill near [[Emerson Dry Lake]] and saw an enormous craft shaped like an equilateral triangle, hovering silently about 150–200 feet in the air. It was an estimated 200–300 feet long. Its features included:

- A matte black surface, darker than the night sky and with no observable reflectivity, that blocked out the stars and sky behind it
- Three embedded spherical lights at each vertex of the triangle, emitting a yellowish-white-gold color that cast no shadow onto the ground
- No seams, rivets, bolts, exhaust ports, or visible propulsion systems
- Silent hovering with no wobbling, bobbling, or weaving, unlike any Harrier or helicopter Castle had observed
- Slow counterclockwise rotation while stationary
- A concave dome-like structure (unlit) in the center of the underside
- Ridges or vents visible between each spherical light along the edges

The encounter took place in the FAA restricted airspace that the video identifies as R2501C, within a 7.5-mile radius of Emerson Dry Lake, approximately 90 miles southeast of [[Edwards Air Force Base]].

### The Operators

Below the craft, approximately 30 armed men in unmarked, all-black uniforms occupied five to six all-black tactical vehicles resembling Ford F-150s to F-350s. The men wore no insignia, name tags, flags, or identifying markings. Their vehicles were all matte black with no logos, chrome, or metallic reflections, and some had canopies and antennas. The operators spoke with American dialects and carried submachine guns (possibly MP5-type weapons). About half of them, roughly 15, ran toward Castle's team in V-formation with weapons drawn, using harsh language and forcing the Marines to keep their heads down.

### Departure and Aftermath

After approximately two to three minutes, the triangle ceased its counterclockwise rotation, climbed at an angle, and departed to the northwest at an extremely high speed, toward [[R2508 Complex|R2508 airspace]] and [[Edwards Air Force Base]]. As it left, it showed no visible, auditory, or optical signs of propulsion or thrust. The black operators immediately sprinted to their vehicles and drove off in the same northwest direction at high speed.

Castle's Humvee failed to start for approximately five minutes after the craft departed, which is consistent with the electromagnetic vehicle interference documented in other UAP encounters. When the team returned to their Quonset hut, their gunnery sergeant and a warrant officer visited them and implied the incident was merely "flare activity" and the Marines were to remain silent.

### Post-Encounter Events

The following day, Castle and his team were called to medical and given an anthrax booster, despite having already received the anthrax vaccine approximately a year prior. Only the Marines who witnessed the incident received the booster, and an armed guard was present. [[Michael Herrera]] and [[Jonathan Weygandt]], two other Marines with similar UAP encounters, also reported receiving anthrax boosters immediately after their own incidents.

## UAP Gerb's Analysis

In Gerb's 2025 investigation built around his testimony, Castle is treated as a witness rather than a whistleblower. What he saw is presented as a rare, precisely located look at a UFO legacy program operation on US soil. Gerb's main arguments are these:

- The craft was a triangular [[Alien Reproduction Vehicle (ARV)|alien reproduction vehicle]], most likely [[Edgar Fouche|Edgar Fouché]]'s [[TR-3B]]. Its size, rounded corners, concave centre, non-flush vertex lights and ridges between the lights all resemble Fouché's description. A less likely alternative is the [[XF-131 Super Sentinel]] associated with the [[Tejon Ranch]] and [[Helendale]] radar cross-section sites.
- The sponsor was the Navy, with Air Force support, not the Marine Corps. The [[Naval Aviation Warfare Development Center (NAWDC)]] and [[Naval Air Station Lemoore (NAS Lemoore)|NAS Lemoore]] joined Hunter Warrior late as a "Hunter Air" adjunct, and the craft fled northwest toward the [[R2508 Complex]] and [[Edwards Air Force Base]].
- The ground team was possibly a [[DOE Special Response Teams (SRTs)|Department of Energy Special Response Team]] trained and staffed by [[Wackenhut Services|Wackenhut]]. Gerb bases this on the MP5-type weapons, the lack of insignia and Wackenhut's attempt to recruit Castle in 2002.

## Wackenhut Recruitment

In 2002, three years after leaving service, Castle was contacted by [[Wackenhut Services|Wackenhut Corporation (now G4S)]] to join their helicopter teams. Wackenhut appeared to have detailed knowledge of Castle's military service record, including his training as a helicopter flight medic, which was not part of his official MOS. Castle speculates that the recruitment attempt may have been an effort to bring him into clandestine operations, possibly related to the "Blue Boys", the alleged UFO crash retrieval rapid response teams.

## Disclosure Efforts

Castle kept his experience largely private for decades. In early 2025, he contacted [[David Grusch]] and said he was willing to testify before Congress. He later connected with [[Steven Greer|Dr. Steven Greer]] and was photographed with Greer, Representative [[Eric Burlison]], and [[Michael Herrera]], among others. Castle is committed to giving sworn testimony about his encounter.
