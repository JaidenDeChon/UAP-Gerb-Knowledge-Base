Rodrik John Castle III (the video's title spells his first name "Roderick") is a veteran and former sergeant (E-5) of the [[United States Marine Corps]] who served from 1994 to 1999. His primary military occupational specialty (MOS) was 6531, aircraft ordnance technician, and he served with [[VMA-513|VMA-513 Flying Nightmares]], an AV-8B Harrier attack squadron stationed out of [[MCAS Yuma]], Arizona. Castle led a team of six to eight Marines in aviation ordnance and also trained as a helicopter flight medic. He is now a prominent UAP witness. His testimony describes a firsthand encounter with an enormous triangular craft and a clandestine armed unit during the 1997 [[Hunter Warrior Advanced Warfighting Experiment]] at [[Marine Corps Air Ground Combat Center 29 Palms]] (see [[Rodrik Castle UAP Encounter]]). Gerb's 2025 video, built around a long interview with him, treats him as a witness rather than a whistleblower. Castle now works with veterans suffering from PTSD.

## Career

According to Castle, he entered the Marine Corps in 1994 and left in 1999 as a sergeant. He later served in the New York National Guard, where he says he left ordnance work behind. He served at Marine Aircraft Group 13 (MAG-13) of the 3rd Marine Aircraft Wing (3rd MAW) at MCAS Yuma. His squadron, VMA-513 "The Flying Nightmares," operated AV-8B Harrier jets. These are STOVL (short takeoff and vertical landing) aircraft with Rolls-Royce Pegasus engines and four rotating nozzles that can direct thrust for vertical landing and for hovering in place. Castle's familiarity with VTOL and STOVL technology, helicopter morphology, and Harrier flight dynamics left him well equipped to distinguish the craft he encountered from any known aircraft.

## The 1997 Encounter

During the [[Hunter Warrior Advanced Warfighting Experiment]], which the video dates to 2–14 March 1997 (official accounts give 1–15 March), Castle and his team were attached to an experimental special-purpose marine air-ground task force (SPMAGTF-X). One night during the exercise, Castle's team was performing overnight crash retrieval duties for Harrier jets when they received a radio call about unexplained or unauthorized flare activity approximately three to five miles out in the desert.

### The Craft

Driving in a Humvee with fire suppression equipment, Castle's team crested a hill near [[Emerson Dry Lake]] and saw an enormous craft shaped like an equilateral triangle, hovering silently about 150–200 feet in the air. It was an estimated 200–300 feet long. Its features included:

- A matte black surface, darker than the night sky and with no observable reflectivity, that blocked out the stars and sky behind it
- Three embedded spherical lights at each vertex of the triangle, emitting a yellowish-white-gold color that cast no shadow onto the ground
- No seams, rivets, bolts, exhaust ports, or visible propulsion systems
- Silent hovering with no wobbling, bobbling, or weaving, unlike any Harrier or helicopter Castle had observed
- Slow counterclockwise rotation while stationary
- A concave dome-like structure (unlit) in the center of the underside
- Ridges or vents visible between each spherical light along the edges

The encounter took place in the FAA restricted airspace that the video identifies as R2501C, within a 7.5-mile radius of Emerson Dry Lake, approximately 90 miles southeast of [[Edwards Air Force Base]].

### The Operators

Below the craft, approximately 30 armed men in unmarked, all-black uniforms occupied five to six all-black tactical vehicles resembling Ford F-150s to F-350s. The men wore no insignia, name tags, flags, or identifying markings. Their vehicles were all matte black with no logos, chrome, or metallic reflections, and some had canopies and antennas. The operators spoke with American dialects and carried submachine guns (possibly MP5-type weapons). About half of them, roughly 15, ran toward Castle's team in V-formation with weapons drawn, using harsh language and forcing the Marines to keep their heads down.

### Departure and Aftermath

About two minutes after Castle's team first saw it, while they were still being held by the operators, the triangle ceased its counterclockwise rotation, climbed at an angle, and departed to the northwest at an extremely high speed, toward [[R2508 Complex|R2508 airspace]] and [[Edwards Air Force Base]]. As it left, it showed no visible, auditory, or optical signs of propulsion or thrust. The black operators immediately sprinted to their vehicles and drove off in the same northwest direction at high speed.

Castle's Humvee failed to start for approximately five minutes after the craft departed, which is consistent with the electromagnetic vehicle interference documented in other UAP encounters. When the team returned to their Quonset hut, their gunnery sergeant and a warrant officer asked what they had seen and implied that it was merely "flare activity" and that the Marines were to keep quiet. "It was implied," Castle says. "This is flares. You guys stumbled across something you shouldn't have seen." Gerb believes the two men had not been briefed on the triangle or the black team.

### Post-Encounter Events

The following day, Castle and his team were called to medical and given an anthrax booster, although as far as they knew they had already been given the anthrax vaccine about a year earlier. Only the Marines who witnessed the incident received the booster, and an armed guard was present. Gerb leaves the purpose of the booster open, but calls it a data point that cannot be ignored. [[Michael Herrera]] and [[Jonathan Weygandt]], two other Marines with similar UAP encounters, also reported receiving anthrax boosters immediately after their own incidents.

## UAP Gerb's Analysis

In Gerb's 2025 investigation built around his testimony, Castle is treated as a witness rather than a whistleblower. What he saw is presented as a rare, precisely located look at a UFO legacy program operation on US soil. Gerb's main arguments are these:

- The craft was a triangular [[Alien Reproduction Vehicle (ARV)|alien reproduction vehicle]], most likely [[Edgar Fouche|Edgar Fouché]]'s [[TR-3B]]. Its size, rounded corners, concave centre, non-flush vertex lights and ridges between the lights all resemble Fouché's description. A less likely alternative is the [[XF-131 Super Sentinel]] associated with the [[Tejon Ranch]] and [[Helendale]] radar cross-section sites.
- The sponsor was the Navy, with Air Force support, not the Marine Corps. The [[Naval Aviation Warfare Development Center (NAWDC)]] and [[Naval Air Station Lemoore (NAS Lemoore)|NAS Lemoore]] joined Hunter Warrior late as a "Hunter Air" adjunct, and the craft fled northwest toward the [[R2508 Complex]] and [[Edwards Air Force Base]].
- The ground team was possibly a [[DOE Special Response Teams (SRTs)|Department of Energy Special Response Team]] trained and staffed by [[Wackenhut Services|Wackenhut]]. Gerb bases this on the MP5-type weapons, the lack of insignia and Wackenhut's attempt to recruit Castle in 2002.

## Wackenhut Recruitment

In 2002, three years after he left the Marines and within three months of leaving the reserves, Castle received a letter from [[Wackenhut Services|Wackenhut Corporation (now G4S)]] inviting him to a recruiting event for its helicopter teams. Wackenhut appeared to know about his training as a helicopter flight medic, which was not part of his official MOS. At the time Castle assumed he was simply on a list of veterans; looking back, he says, "it was an attempt to pull me in as an asset." Gerb goes further and theorizes that Wackenhut may have been recruiting him for clandestine teams, possibly a group like the "Blue Boys", the alleged UFO crash retrieval rapid response teams.

## Disclosure Efforts

Castle kept his experience largely private for decades. In early 2025, around the time Gerb began vetting him in the spring, he contacted [[David Grusch]] and said he was willing to testify before Congress. He then reached out to [[Steven Greer|Dr. Steven Greer]], who brought him to Representative [[Eric Burlison]]; a photograph circulating in UFO circles, which Gerb believes dates from late July 2025, shows Castle with Greer, Burlison and [[Michael Herrera]], among others. Castle is committed to giving sworn testimony about his encounter.

## In Gerb's Other Investigations

Castle's encounter recurs in Gerb's later work. In his video on [[Sandia National Laboratories]], Gerb theorizes with medium to high confidence that a [[DOE Special Response Teams (SRTs)|DOE Special Response Team]] was the ground element beneath the triangle, as he does in the [[Manhattan Project 2.0]] investigation. In *The Hidden Wing*, the triangle's departure toward the R-2508 complex and [[Edwards Air Force Base]] supports his thesis of an Air Force reverse-engineering wing in the Antelope Valley. In his Lockheed Martin video, he wagers that the craft belonged to the same alien reproduction vehicle program described by Ed, a retired Air Force lieutenant colonel and Edwards test pilot.
