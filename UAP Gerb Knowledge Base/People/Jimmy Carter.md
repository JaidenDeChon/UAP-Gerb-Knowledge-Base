James Earl "Jimmy" Carter Jr. (1924-2024) was the 39th President of the United States, serving from 1977 to 1981, and before that Governor of Georgia. He is one of the few US presidents on record as having reported a UFO sighting. As governor-elect in 1973, he filed a report of a sighting he made in Leary, Georgia in 1969. During his 1976 campaign he said he would make government UFO information public.

## Role in UAP Accounts

Attorney [[Daniel Sheehan]] says that in 1977, under orders from President Carter, he was allowed to review classified portions of [[Project Blue Book]]. Sheehan says he found five or six photographs of a downed saucer-shaped craft in a snowy field, with symbols running around the base of its dome.

In *[[Off-World Technologies Division – UAP Technology Reverse Engineering]]*, UAP Gerb notes that Carter had a verifiable interest in UAP. Gerb describes how Green Beret witness [[RA]] identified Sheehan's symbols as closely matching the characters projected by a gauntlet-like device he says he saw in the alleged [[Off-World Technologies Division]] at [[Naval Surface Warfare Center Crane]].
