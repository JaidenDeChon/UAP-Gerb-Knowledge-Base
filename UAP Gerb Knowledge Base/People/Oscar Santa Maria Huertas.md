Óscar Santa María Huertas is a former [[Peruvian Air Force]] (FAP) pilot who, as a 23-year-old lieutenant on April 11, 1980, took off from [[La Joya Airbase, Arequipa, Peru|La Joya Airbase]] in a Sukhoi Su-22 to intercept an unidentified silvery object and fired a burst of 64 30mm shells at it, with no effect. The encounter, the [[La Joya Airbase UFO Intercept]], was recorded in a [[DOD Joint Chiefs]] briefing document, and he is often described as the only pilot known to have opened fire on a UFO. Gerb rendered his surname "Hueras" in the video; Peruvian press and his own interviews give it as Huertas.

## The La Joya Intercept

As Gerb tells it in [[Video - Global Air Force UFO Encounters You've Probably Never Heard of|Global Air Force UFO Encounters You've Probably Never Heard of]], Santa María was ordered up early in the morning to intercept a strange silvery object spotted floating near the end of the runway. He described it as being "in restricted airspace without authorization, representing a grave challenge to national sovereignty", at a time of high concern about espionage in Peru.

He climbed to 2,500 metres and made an attack run on what he assumed was a balloon: "I reached the necessary distance and shot a burst of 64 30mm shells, which created a cone-shaped wall of fire that would normally obliterate anything in its path." He expected to see the balloon torn to shreds; the barrage had no effect.

The object then shot skyward. Santa María lit his afterburners and chased it at Mach 1.6 for 84 kilometres, until it stopped dead and he had to turn sharply to avoid a collision. He re-engaged and locked on, but "at that moment the object made another fast climb evading the attack. I was left underneath it. It broke the attack." Trying, like [[George F. Gorman]] in 1948, to get above it, he climbed after the object, which shadowed him from 14,000 feet all the way up to 19,200, in Gerb's figures.

## The Close Look

Low on fuel, he closed to within 100 metres and saw that "the balloon was not a balloon at all": an object about 10 metres across, with a shiny cream-coloured dome on top, "similar to a light bulb cut in half", on a wide, circular, silver metallic base. It had no wings, propulsion jets, exhaust, windows or antennae, and no visible propulsion system. Realising he had engaged a UFO, and with his fuel low, he was, in Gerb's words, paralysed with fear, and zigzagged away so that his aircraft would be hard to hit.

## Corroboration

On his return to base, multiple eyewitnesses and base personnel also observed the object. The official investigation that followed is recorded in the DOD Joint Chiefs briefing, which, per Gerb, corroborates that he fired 30mm rounds at the object without damaging it and that it went on to perform remarkable manoeuvres. Gerb found the case through that document, the same one that describes the [[1968 Nepal Circular Disc Crash]], while researching [[Project Moon Dust]]. He ranks the La Joya intercept, with the [[Gorman Dogfight]], as the most compelling of the four cases in the video.
