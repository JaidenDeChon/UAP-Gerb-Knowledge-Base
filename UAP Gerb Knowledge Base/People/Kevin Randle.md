Kevin Randle is an American UFO researcher and author who co-wrote *Crash: When UFOs Fall from the Sky* with [[Don Schmidt]]. He is among the researchers who have publicly questioned or doubted [[Robert Willingham]]'s claimed Air Force service record and the credibility of Willingham's 1955 UAP crash account near [[Langtry, Texas]].

## Skepticism of the Willingham Case

Randle's doubts about Willingham are frequently cited in discussions of the [[Del Rio, Texas]] crash cases, especially the fact that the National Personnel Records Service in St. Louis, Missouri, could not retrieve Willingham's service record. Gerb notes, however, that a catastrophic fire at that facility on July 12, 1973 destroyed most military records for personnel who served between 1947 and 1963. The missing record is therefore weaker as disqualifying evidence. [[Noe Torres]], a co-author of *The Other Roswell*, claims to hold over 50 documents that corroborate Willingham's service.

Randle's assessment of the Willingham case remains a significant counterpoint when weighing the credibility of the 1955 crash retrieval account.
