Kevin Randle is an American UFO researcher and author who co-wrote *Crash: When UFOs Fall from the Sky* with [[Don Schmidt]]. Randle is among the researchers who have publicly questioned or expressed doubt regarding [[Robert Willingham]]'s claimed Air Force service record and the credibility of Willingham's 1955 UAP crash account near [[Langtry, Texas]].

## Skepticism of the Willingham Case

Randle's doubts about Willingham are frequently cited in discussions of the [[Del Rio, Texas]] crash cases, particularly regarding the inability of the National Personnel Records Service in St. Louis, Missouri to retrieve Willingham's service record. However, UAP Gerb notes that a catastrophic July 12, 1973 fire at that facility destroyed most military records for personnel who served between 1947 and 1963, weakening the absence of records as disqualifying evidence. Co-author [[Noah Torres]] of *The Other Roswell* claims to hold over 50 documents corroborating Willingham's service.

Randle's assessment of the Willingham case remains a significant counterpoint in evaluating the credibility of the 1955 crash retrieval account.
