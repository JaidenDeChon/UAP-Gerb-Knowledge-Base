Jonathan Wagant is a former United States Marine Corps Lance Corporal who testified about encountering a crashed unidentified aerial object during Operation Laser Strike, an aerial counter-narcotics operation conducted in [[Peru]] in 1997. His testimony is one of the most detailed first-hand accounts of an alleged UAP crash retrieval event involving US military personnel. The name also appears in some UAP research sources as "Jonathan Weygandt."

## Role in UAP Programs

Wagant testified that during Operation Laser Strike, Marines encountered a large egg-shaped craft embedded in rock at a crash site in Peru. The craft was roughly the size of a sedan, oblong and spherical in shape, with a shimmering green and purple "mother of pearl" field around its exterior; it reportedly absorbed light rather than reflecting it and cast no shadow in direct sunlight. A "soupy air effect" near the craft was described as the air feeling like the space between two magnets. Personnel from the [[Department of Energy]] wearing MOPP gear (rubber suits and gas masks) secured the site and conducted CBR risk assessments, while Marines were held at gunpoint by men in black fatigues, believed to be from the 7th Army. Wagant's account is corroborated in several physical details — particularly the nesting doll internal structure and the oblong spherical shape — by subsequent independent witness accounts of other alleged retrievals.
