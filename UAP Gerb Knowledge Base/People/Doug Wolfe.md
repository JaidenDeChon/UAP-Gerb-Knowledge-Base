Douglas "Doug" Wolfe is a retired U.S. intelligence official with a 33-year career at the [[CIA]], where his final post was Associate Deputy Director of the CIA's Directorate of Science and Technology (DS&T). He became CIA Chief Information Officer in 2013. Separately, he was Deputy Director of National Intelligence for Acquisition, Technology and Facilities (DDNI/ATNF) at the Office of the Director of National Intelligence. He retired from government service in December 2017.

## Role in UAP Programs

In *Special Access Required Vol.2*, Gerb names Wolfe, along with [[Donald M. Kerr|Don Kerr]], as one of several people in the National Reconnaissance Office's acquisitions leadership chain who also held deputy director posts in the CIA Directorate of Science and Technology. The acquisitions post in question is the DDNI/ATNF role, which is overseen jointly with the Under Secretary of Defense for Acquisition and Sustainment (USD A&S). Gerb cites this overlap between NRO/IC acquisitions leadership and CIA DS&T leadership as evidence of a small, recurring pool of "legacy gatekeepers" who move between these offices. He also says of Wolfe, "Doug Wolfe is a very bad man", but gives no evidence for the claim and does not explain it further in the video.
