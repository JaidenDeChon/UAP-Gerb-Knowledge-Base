Brian Parks is a UFO researcher who in 1991 submitted a Freedom of Information Act (FOIA) request about the alleged "[[Blue Room]]" at [[Wright-Patterson Air Force Base]], the classified facility that Senator [[Barry Goldwater]] had sought access to in the 1970s and early 1980s. USAF Colonel [[Eddie L. Anderson]] answered Parks' request and confirmed that materials related to the Blue Room, including film, had been destroyed on September 9, 1965. The reply is one of the few official government acknowledgments that the Blue Room existed, though only as a records-management matter confirming that its contents were destroyed.

## In the NASIC video

In [[Videos/NASIC - Air Force UFO Crash Retrieval & Reverse Engineering/summary|NASIC - Air Force UFO Crash Retrieval & Reverse Engineering]], Gerb places Parks's request in a longer trail of Blue Room FOIA requests. Parks asked specifically for the 35 mm film "Blue Room Radar Scope, Wright-Patterson Air Force Base, 1955", listed on an Air Force Form 1350 record that surfaced after [[Bill Moore]]'s 1980 request; the Air Force replied that the DoD Motion Media Records Center at [[Norton Air Force Base]] had been searched and the film destroyed on 9 September 1965. In 2012 researcher Anthony Bragalia used Parks's request as the basis for his own, and was told that the office of record for the Blue Room is the [[National Air and Space Intelligence Center (NASIC)|National Air and Space Intelligence Center]].
