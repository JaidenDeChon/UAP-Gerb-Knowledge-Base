Tom DeLonge is an American musician (co-founder of Blink-182) and the founder of [[To The Stars Academy]] (TTSA), an organization set up to research and disclose advanced aerospace phenomena. DeLonge has spoken publicly about his connections to government officials, [[Lockheed Martin Skunk Works]] executives, and intelligence community figures regarding UAP programs.

With author [[AJ Hartley]], DeLonge co-wrote *Secret Machines*, a novel series that mixes fiction with what is alleged to be real UAP-related information from military insiders. The novel *Locust* describes a triangular craft navigated with a red trackball sphere. The same detail appears independently in [[Brad Sorenson]]'s description of the [[Alien Reproduction Vehicle (ARV)]] control system and in [[Edgar Fouche|Edgar Fouché]]'s account of the [[TR-3B]] cockpit interface. [[Eric Schrock]] was allegedly an adviser who gave DeLonge factual information for the series.

## In the NASIC video

In [[Videos/NASIC - Air Force UFO Crash Retrieval & Reverse Engineering/summary|NASIC - Air Force UFO Crash Retrieval & Reverse Engineering]], Gerb replays DeLonge's account that one of his advisers, high up in the National Reconnaissance Office, described satellites tracking craft entering and leaving the atmosphere by programmed heat and motion signatures, "episodic visits", and a paper in which a DoD physicist worked out an algorithm for when and where "mother ships" would collect smaller craft, tested successfully. Gerb reads this as measurement and signature intelligence of the kind the [[National Air and Space Intelligence Center (NASIC)|National Air and Space Intelligence Center]] would perform.
