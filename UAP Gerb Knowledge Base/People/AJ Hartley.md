AJ Hartley is an American author who co-wrote the *Secret Machines* novel series with [[Tom DeLonge]], blending fiction with alleged real UAP-related information sourced from military and intelligence insiders. Notably, the novel *Locust* describes a triangular craft navigated via a red trackball sphere — a detail that independently mirrors [[Brad Sorenson]]'s description of the [[Alien Reproduction Vehicle (ARV)]] control system and [[Edgar Fouché]]'s account of the [[TR-3B]] cockpit interface. [[Eric Schrock]], a [[Lockheed Martin Skunk Works]] official, allegedly served as an adviser providing factual information for the series.
