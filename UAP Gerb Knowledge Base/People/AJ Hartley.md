AJ Hartley is an American author who co-wrote the *Secret Machines* novel series with [[Tom DeLonge]]. The books mix fiction with alleged real UAP-related information sourced from military and intelligence insiders. The novel *Locust* describes a triangular craft navigated with a red trackball sphere, a detail that independently mirrors [[Brad Sorenson]]'s description of the [[Alien Reproduction Vehicle (ARV)]] control system and [[Edgar Fouché]]'s account of the [[TR-3B]] cockpit interface. [[Eric Schrock]], a [[Lockheed Martin Skunk Works]] official, was allegedly an adviser who provided factual information for the series.
