Eric Schrock is a [[Lockheed Martin Skunk Works]] official who allegedly served as an adviser providing factual UAP-related information to [[Tom DeLonge]] during the writing of the *Secret Machines* novel series (co-authored with [[AJ Hartley]]). The inclusion of a red trackball sphere as the navigation mechanism for a triangular craft in the novel *Locust* — a detail independently corroborated by [[Brad Sorenson]]'s description of the [[Alien Reproduction Vehicle (ARV)]] and [[Edgar Fouché]]'s [[TR-3B]] testimony — suggests Schrock may have provided technical details drawn from classified aerospace programs.
