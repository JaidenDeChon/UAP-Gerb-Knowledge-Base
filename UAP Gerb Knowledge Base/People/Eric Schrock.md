Eric Schrock is a [[Lockheed Martin Skunk Works]] official who was allegedly an adviser to [[Tom DeLonge]], giving him factual UAP-related information while he wrote the *Secret Machines* novel series (co-authored with [[AJ Hartley]]). In the novel *Locust*, a triangular craft is navigated with a red trackball sphere. [[Brad Sorenson]]'s description of the [[Alien Reproduction Vehicle (ARV)]] and [[Edgar Fouché]]'s [[TR-3B]] testimony independently corroborate this detail, which suggests that Schrock may have provided technical details drawn from classified aerospace programs.
