Francisco Javier Rodriguez was the flight mechanic aboard [[TAE (Trabajos Aéreos y Enlaces)|TAE]] flight JK 297 during the [[Manises UFO Incident]] of 11 November 1979. At approximately 23:05 he was the first crew member to point out two powerful red lights ahead and to the left of the aircraft, alerting Captain [[Francisco Javier Lerdo de Tejada]] and co-pilot [[José Ramón Zuazu]].

The Super Caravelle was then cruising over the Mediterranean Sea at 23,000 feet, flying 109 passengers from [[Salzburg, Austria|Salzburg]] to [[Las Palmas, Spain|Las Palmas]]. His warning led the captain to contact [[Barcelona Air Traffic Control]], and in the end to an emergency landing at [[Manises Airport, Valencia, Spain|Manises Airport]] in Valencia, the first recorded emergency landing of a commercial flight because of a UFO encounter.
