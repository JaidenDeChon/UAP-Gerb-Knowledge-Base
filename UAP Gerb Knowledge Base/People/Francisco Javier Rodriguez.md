Francisco Javier Rodriguez was the flight mechanic aboard [[TAE (Trabajos Aéreos y Enlaces)|TAE]] flight JK 297 during the [[Manises UFO Incident]] of November 11, 1979. He was the first crew member to alert Captain [[Javier Leo de Taha]] and co-pilot [[Jose Ramon Zarazua Ingore]] to the presence of two powerful red lights visible to the front left of the aircraft at approximately 23:05 hours.

Rodriguez's initial observation occurred while the Super Caravelle was cruising over the [[Mediterranean Sea]] at 23,000 feet on a flight from [[Salzburg, Austria|Salzburg]] to [[Las Palmas, Spain|Las Palmas]] with 109 passengers aboard. His warning prompted the captain to contact [[Barcelona Air Traffic Control]] and ultimately led to the first recorded emergency landing of a commercial flight due to a UFO encounter at [[Manises Airport, Valencia, Spain|Manises Airport]] in Valencia.
