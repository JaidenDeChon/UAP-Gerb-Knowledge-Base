Jacques Vallée is a French-American computer scientist and astronomer, and a prominent UFO researcher whose work has significantly influenced the study of unidentified aerial phenomena. A former colleague of Dr. [[J. Allen Hynek]], Vallée has written numerous books and papers on UFO cases, anomalous phenomena, and where science meets the paranormal.

## Canadian Air Force 1953 UFO Case

Vallée summarized and reported on the Canadian Air Force's 1953 UFO sighting and its photographic evidence, adding to the record of early military UFO encounters.

## Aerospace Corporation Connection

In his book *Hidden Science 5*, which covers his journals from the 2000s to 2009, Vallée and [[Kit Green]], [[Hal Puthoff]], [[Eric Davis]], and Kristen B. Zimmerman identified [[Aerospace Corporation]] in [[Long Beach, California]] as one of the suspected legacy private corporations engaged in "real UAP research." The group's analysis is consistent with claims by [[Bob Fish]] and [[Ross Coulthart]] that the Aerospace Corporation was involved in [[Defense Support Program (DSP)]] data analysis and may have had access to recovered UAP materials.

## Battelle Identification on Joe Rogan Experience

On December 4, 2020, in Joe Rogan Experience episode #1574 with documentary filmmaker [[James Fox]], Vallée referred to a private contractor that helps control the UFO phenomenon through intelligence agencies and holds recovered UFO materials. He said he was unwilling to name the organization. Fox then identified it indirectly by mentioning its involvement with the January 1953 [[Robertson Panel]]. That pointer is consistent specifically with [[Battelle Memorial Institute]], the contractor running the parallel [[Project Stork]] investigation at the time. Gerb cited this part of the episode (starting about one hour and forty-two minutes in) as corroboration of the Battelle material recovery hypothesis.

## USO and Norton Air Force Base (1985)

In his journal collection *Forbidden Science: Pacific Heights*, Vallée records a 1985 claim from a source at NARCAT (the National Aviation Reporting Center on Anomalous Phenomena) that a large collection of UFO and USO (unidentified submerged object) files was being stored at [[Norton Air Force Base]]. The claim puts USO files at Norton along with other UAP material that other researchers have linked to the base, including [[Steven Greer]] in his alleged 1996 congressional briefing.

## UFO Chronicles of the Soviet Union

Vallée's book *UFO Chronicles of the Soviet Union: A Cosmic Samizdat* documents Soviet UFO and USO cases, including the [[Soviet Submarine Repair Ship Vulga Sighting]] of October 7, 1977. Pages 29–30 of the book are cited as a source for the aftermath of that case. They describe [[Fleet Admiral Nikolai Smirnov]]'s mandatory UFO reporting directive for Soviet hydrographic and reconnaissance ships, authorized by naval officer and researcher [[Vladimir Azhazha]]. The words "cosmic samizdat" in the subtitle refer to the Soviet underground self-publishing tradition (*samizdat*) and present Soviet UFO research as inherently dissident activity under a state apparatus that suppressed the subject. Gerb cites this work alongside *Russia's USO Secrets* by [[Paul Stonehill]] and [[Philip Mantle]] as the primary Western documentary sources for Soviet USO cases.
