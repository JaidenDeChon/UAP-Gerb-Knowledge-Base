Walter Bedell Smith (1895–1961) was a US Army general who served as [[Dwight D. Eisenhower]]'s chief of staff in the Second World War. He was later US ambassador to the Soviet Union (1946–1949), Director of Central Intelligence (1950–1953) and Under Secretary of State. The [[Eisenhower Briefing Document]] names "General Walter B. Smith" as a member of [[Majestic 12]], appointed to replace the deceased [[James Forrestal]] on 1 August 1950.

## In the Majestic documents

The video points out that [[Harry Truman]]'s appointment records show an off-the-record meeting with Smith on 1 August 1950, the date the briefing document gives for his appointment. Supporters of the documents cite this match, along with the Truman–Bush–Forrestal meeting of 24 September 1947, as evidence that the author had accurate inside knowledge.
