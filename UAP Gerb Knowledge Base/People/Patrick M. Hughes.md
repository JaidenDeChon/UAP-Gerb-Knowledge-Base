Patrick M. Hughes (1942-2024) was a US Army lieutenant general who served as the twelfth Director of the [[Defense Intelligence Agency]] from February 1996 to July 1999. Before that he was Director of Intelligence (J-2) for the Joint Chiefs of Staff (1994-1996) and Director of Intelligence at US Central Command (1992-1994). He retired from the Army in October 1999.

## Role in the Wilson-Davis story

Hughes was DIA director in 1997, when his future successor, Vice Admiral [[Thomas Wilson]], was the Joint Staff's J-2. In an interview with researcher Joe Murgia, retired Navy Commander [[Will Miller]] said Hughes had also requested a UFO briefing from Miller's group and wanted it expedited. Miller placed him among the officials briefed alongside Wilson, [[Edgar Mitchell]] and [[Steven Greer]] in the period covered by the [[Wilson-Davis Memo]]. Transcripts of the interview sometimes render his name as "Dia Hughes", a run-together form of "DIA Hughes".
