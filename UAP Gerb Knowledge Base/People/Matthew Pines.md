Matthew Pines is an American national security analyst: director of intelligence at the cybersecurity company SentinelOne and a national security fellow at the Bitcoin Policy Institute. He comments on UAP policy and the disclosure effort. In February 2024 he publicly shared two backup slides from [[Karl Nell]]'s November 2023 [[SOL Foundation Symposium]] presentation that were not shown in the recorded talk.

## The Nell backup slides

The slides Pines shared are:

- A proposed replacement for the [[Kardashev Scale]], the [[Universal Civilization Development Datum]], which would gauge civilizations by what they are, what they know and who they are rather than by the energy they harness.
- A "proposed taxonomy of UAP origin hypotheses" that sorts explanations into physical, psychophysical and metaphysical categories (see [[UAP Taxonomy]]).

In *SOL Foundation: Karl Nell - A Key Figure in UAP Disclosure*, Gerb walks through both slides and credits Pines with providing them.
