Michael Shellenberger is an American investigative journalist, author, and founder of the online publication Public. In the UAP research community he is known mainly for an October 2024 article that made a new UAP whistleblower's testimony public and described the alleged existence of the "Immaculate Constellation", a classified US government program for surveilling non-human craft and technologies derived from them.

## The Immaculate Constellation Article (October 2024)

Gerb called Shellenberger's October 2024 article "groundbreaking" because of one sentence in it: the report's conclusion that "the existence of Immaculate Constellation demonstrates the extent and capability to detect, quarantine, and transfer UAP and ARV — Alien Reproduction Vehicle — collection incidents before they are observed and circulated within the military intelligence enterprise." This explicit use of the term "ARV (Alien Reproduction Vehicle)" in a published whistleblower report was the first known appearance of that technical designation in a formal disclosure. It corroborated [[Mark McCandlish]], who had used the same term for decades.

The article describes [[Immaculate Constellation (IMCON)]] as an unacknowledged Special Access Program (USAP). According to the article, the program was designed to gather UAP observations from both tasked and untasked collection platforms in one place, and to quarantine and manage ARV-related collection incidents before they spread through the wider military intelligence enterprise.
