Ed is a pseudonymous individual identified in UAP Gerb's research as an electronics warfare director associated with [[Edwards Air Force Base]] and the [[412th Test Wing]]. According to [[Edgar Fouche]], Ed served as an intermediary or coordinator involved in the operational infrastructure surrounding reverse-engineered craft, including the [[TR-3B]].

## Role and Significance

Ed's position as an electronics warfare director placed him at the intersection of advanced aerospace testing and classified programs at Edwards Air Force Base — a facility long associated with experimental aircraft development and, in UAP lore, with the testing of reverse-engineered alien reproduction vehicles. Fouché identified Ed as having knowledge of or involvement in the logistics of TR-3B operations, particularly regarding pilot selection and craft deployment.

Edwards Air Force Base houses the [[Air Force Test Center]] and the 412th Test Wing, which oversees all flight testing at the base. Fouché claimed that TR-3B pilots were drawn from elite aviators at both the Air Force Test Pilot School at Edwards and the Navy's [[Naval Aviation Warfare Development Center (NAWDC)|Top Gun]] school — organizations where Ed's electronics warfare expertise would have been directly relevant.
