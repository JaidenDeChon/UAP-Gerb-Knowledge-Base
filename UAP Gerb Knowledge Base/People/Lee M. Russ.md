Lee M. Russ is a member of the Senior Executive Service who serves as Executive Director of the [[Air Force Office of Special Investigations|Air Force Office of Special Investigations']] Office of Special Projects, designated AFOSI/PJ — the unit responsible for safeguarding the Air Force's most sensitive Special Access Programs — and as Director of the Air Force's departmentwide SAP Security enterprise. He succeeded [[Terry Phillips]] in these roles following Phillips's retirement from federal service around January 2022, after Phillips had held the position for approximately three decades. Russ has more recently been named to a Pentagon-wide role as one of the Department of Defense's first Executive Directors of Counterintelligence and Global SAP Security.

## Role in UAP Programs

UAP Gerb's *Special Access Required Vol.2* names Russ as one of two individuals — alongside his predecessor [[Terry Phillips]] — Gerb identifies as "paramount gatekeepers to the UFO topic" within AFOSI/PJ, stating he would "not be surprised if documents are ever released that directly state Russ has been involved in UFO narrative shaping." The video frames AFOSI/PJ, under both Phillips and Russ, as a "program protection" office deputized to enforce insider- and outsider-threat protection for Air Force Special Access Programs, characterizing such offices collectively as the "antibodies" of the [[Legacy Program Onion Model|legacy program onion]].

UAP Gerb's *The Hidden Wing* calls on the administration to put to Russ the same questions it proposes for Phillips: whether PJ, under his leadership, engaged in reprisals against UFO whistleblowers. It lists him among five "hostile witnesses" alongside [[Terry Phillips]], [[William E. MacLure]], [[Russell E. Wiler]] and [[Randall G. Walden]].
