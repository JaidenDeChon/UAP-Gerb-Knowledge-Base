Lee M. Russ is a member of the Senior Executive Service. He is Executive Director of the [[Air Force Office of Special Investigations|Air Force Office of Special Investigations']] Office of Special Projects, designated AFOSI/PJ, the unit responsible for protecting the Air Force's most sensitive Special Access Programs (SAPs). He is also Director of the Air Force's departmentwide SAP Security enterprise. He took over both roles from [[Terry Phillips]], who had held the position for about three decades and retired from federal service around January 2022. More recently, Russ was named to a Pentagon-wide role as one of the Department of Defense's first Executive Directors of Counterintelligence and Global SAP Security.

## Role in UAP Programs

In UAP Gerb's *Special Access Required Vol.2*, Gerb names Russ and his predecessor [[Terry Phillips]] as the two people within AFOSI/PJ he considers "paramount gatekeepers to the UFO topic". He says he would "not be surprised if documents are ever released that directly state Russ has been involved in UFO narrative shaping." The video describes AFOSI/PJ, under both Phillips and Russ, as a "program protection" office deputized to enforce protection against insider and outsider threats for Air Force Special Access Programs. It calls such offices, taken together, the "antibodies" of the [[Legacy Program Onion Model|legacy program onion]].

UAP Gerb's *The Hidden Wing* calls on the administration to put to Russ the same questions it proposes for Phillips: whether PJ, under his leadership, engaged in reprisals against UFO whistleblowers. It lists him among five "hostile witnesses" alongside [[Terry Phillips]], [[William E. MacLure]], [[Russell E. Wiler]] and [[Randall G. Walden]].
