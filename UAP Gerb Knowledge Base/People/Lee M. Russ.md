Special Agent Lee M. Russ is a member of the Senior Executive Service and the Executive Director of the [[Air Force Office of Special Investigations|Air Force Office of Special Investigations']] Office of Special Projects, designated AFOSI/PJ, the office that safeguards the Air Force's most sensitive Special Access Programs (SAPs). He is also the Department of the Air Force's Special Access Program Security Director. He succeeded [[Terry Phillips]] in both roles after Phillips left federal service in 2021. Gerb names him, with Phillips, as one of the two people at PJ he considers "paramount gatekeepers to the UFO topic".

## Career

Russ is a career OSI special agent. He served on active duty as an OSI special agent from 2001 to 2006, then continued as a civilian; until the end of 2020 he was a supervisory special agent and deputy director of the Office of Special Projects. In January 2021 he moved to the Pentagon as the first Director of Counterintelligence and Global Special Access Program Security at the Department of Defense Special Access Program Central Office (SAPCO). In 2022 he was promoted to the Senior Executive Service and returned to PJ as its executive director, where his Air Force biography credits him with directing 427 people at 52 field offices who provide security, investigative, counterintelligence, polygraph and program security support to all programs protected under special access controls.

## Role in UAP Programs

In *Special Access Required Vol.2*, Gerb describes PJ as one of the "program protection" offices deputized to enforce insider and outsider threat protection for the legacy UFO programs: the "antibodies" that swarm to neutralize threats to the core of the [[Legacy Program Onion Model|legacy program onion]]. He says he is aware of specific instances in which Phillips and Russ "have acted as attack dogs for the UFO legacy architecture", and that Russ "picked up right where Phillips left off within federal service". He adds that he "would not be surprised if documents are ever released that directly state Russ has been involved in UFO narrative shaping." He first signals the theme at the end of *The Manhattan Project 2.0*, Vol.1 of the same series: "Never forget the name Terry Phillips. Never forget his successor Lee M. Russ."

In *The Hidden Wing*, Gerb says there is "not much here regarding Russ", but calls on the administration to put to him the same questions he proposes for Phillips: whether, as head of PJ and Air Force SAP security, he engaged in reprisals against UFO whistleblowers. Russ is one of five officials Gerb calls "hostile witnesses" and asks the administration to subpoena or serve with interrogatories, alongside [[Terry Phillips]], [[William E. MacLure]], [[Russell E. Wiler]] and [[Randall G. Walden]].
