**Manuel Sandoval** was a law enforcement officer from [[Cuba, New Mexico]], identified by researchers as one of the two policemen at the scene of the [[Aztec UFO Crash and Recovery|1948 Aztec crash]]. According to the accounts compiled by [[Scott and Suzanne Ramsey]], he arrived saying he had followed the low-flying disc in a northerly direction and described it, like rancher [[Valentine Archuleta]], as wobbly and "fluttering like a leaf." He ordered civilians off the craft. A second, local officer then arrived and told him the military already knew about the craft and that everyone should leave at once. Other witnesses, including [[Doug Noland]] and [[Ken Farley]], mention two police officers clearing the site. The Ramseys did not interview Sandoval themselves; his account reached them through a relative.
