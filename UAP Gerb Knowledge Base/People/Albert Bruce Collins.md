Albert Bruce Collins (died December 30, 1990), also known by the pseudonym "Barnabas," was an American who claimed to be a retired metallurgical engineer and government employee. He said that from 1942 through the late 1950s he developed metal alloys for electromagnetic propagation and magnetic field propulsion in an official capacity. UFO researcher [[Tim Cooper]] recorded his testimony on November 20, 1990, shortly before Collins died.

## Claimed Observations

Collins disclosed that in 1947 he watched a UAP being backed into a large warehouse at the [[University of California, Berkeley]] on a long flatbed trailer under a green tarp. From about 100 feet away, he saw an oval craft "like an egg with the shell cracked and the yolk still inside." It was metallic, with a silvery, polished-aluminum finish and a seam running along its edge. The craft was badly damaged: a large section had been ripped off, and a huge gash on top ran below the seam. Collins saw a multi-layered honeycomb skin in the bulkhead and, at the center of the craft, a shiny sphere surrounded by another bulkhead. The craft measured 30–40 feet in diameter and about 15 feet high.

## Alleged Knowledge of UAP Programs

Collins claimed that around 1949 he studied metal of unknown composition and origin in an official capacity, and in doing so learned of multiple agencies involved in [[Technologies of Unknown Origin (TUO)]] research. These included the intelligence offices of the US Army, Air Force, and Navy, the [[CIA]], the [[RAND Corporation]], and reportedly the [[Vatican]]. He described several alleged joint projects:

- Project Archangel, between the CIA, RAND, and the Vatican
- Project Black Book, under the Air Force
- Project Blue Book, under the Air Force
- Project White Book, between the CIA and the Vatican
- Project Yellow Book, a scientific panel of an unknown government review board

Collins also said that [[Los Alamos National Labs]] had been studying green fireballs and strange debris found in the desert. The claim is consistent with Project Twinkle, a real Atomic Energy Commission investigation of green fireballs at Los Alamos, Sandia, White Sands, and Holloman Air Force Base in the late 1940s and early 1950s.

## Verification

Collins's obituary confirms that he was a veteran and a member of the NRA and a nature conservancy. His claimed career as a metallurgical engineer on classified programs has not been independently verified. The joint UAP programs with the Vatican that he described are noteworthy given the [[1933 Magenta, Italy UFO Crash]], in which Vatican intelligence allegedly alerted the United States to a UAP in Axis possession.
