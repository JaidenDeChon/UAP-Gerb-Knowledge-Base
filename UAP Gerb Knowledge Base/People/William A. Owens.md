William A. Owens is a retired U.S. Navy admiral who served as Vice Chairman of the Joint Chiefs of Staff in the mid-1990s and then moved into the defense and technology industry, including a senior executive role at [[SAIC]]. In UAP Gerb's reading of the [[Wilson-Davis Memo|Wilson-Davis notes]], Owens was one of the permanent members of the restructured [[SAPOC (Special Access Program Oversight Committee)|Special Access Program Oversight Committee]] that allegedly kept Vice Admiral [[Thomas Wilson]] out of a UFO legacy program, and one of two SAIC alumni among the six SAPOC figures Wilson dealt with.

## Career

Before becoming Vice Chairman, Owens served as senior military assistant to Secretary of Defense [[Dick Cheney]]. After leaving the Navy he joined SAIC; the UAP Gerb video describes him as the company's chief executive.

## Alleged role as a gatekeeper

UAP Gerb's SAIC investigation places Owens on SAPOC after the January 1994 restructuring of special access program oversight, alongside chairman [[John Deutsch]] (also a former SAIC director), [[Paul Kaminski]], Brigadier General Michael Kostelnik and [[Linton Wells II]]. Because two of those six figures later or earlier served SAIC, the host wagers that the large defense contractor Wilson said worked alongside SAPOC to protect the program was SAIC. He names Owens among the officials he believes should be subpoenaed by Congress, and cites him as an example of senior officers who "trade in their stripes and stars for a pinstriped suit".
