Matt Brown is a UAP researcher and whistleblower advocate. He wrote the publicly circulated report describing the alleged [[Immaculate Constellation (IMCON)]] surveillance program, which referenced [[NRO Sentient]] as an important underlying intelligence resource. In interviews he has discussed the security architecture of alleged legacy UFO programs, and he publicly supports whistleblowers featured on the UAP Gerb channel, including [[David Grusch]] and [[Dylan Borland]].

## Role in UAP Programs

In an interview clip played in Gerb's *Special Access Required Vol.2*, Brown talks about an unnamed individual, shown only in a redacted photograph and identified solely by the title "deputy SAPCO for DARPA" at [[Northrop Grumman]]. He describes this person as "a nexus point for legacy security" and part of what he calls "the dirty tricks department," and alleges that the person matches the names of prospective whistleblowers to personnel capable of carrying out reprisal campaigns. The result, Brown says, is "an ecosystem flooded with disinformation and psychological operations and targeted attacks against whistleblowers and their allies." Brown says he cannot reveal the person's identity because he is relaying the information on behalf of others. Gerb cites Brown's account, alongside statements from [[David Grusch]], as an example of the "administrative terrorism" that the video alleges legacy program protection agencies use against whistleblowers.
