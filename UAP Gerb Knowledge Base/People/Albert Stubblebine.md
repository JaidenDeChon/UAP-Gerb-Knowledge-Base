Major General Albert Stubblebine III (1930–2017) was a United States Army officer who served as the commanding general of the US Army Intelligence and Security Command (INSCOM) from 1981 to 1984. He is the founder of INSCOM and is notable in UAP research for his connections to [[TREAT]] (Tactical Reconnaissance Engineering Assessment Team) and alleged UFO-related intelligence operations.

A 1997 letter to Dr. [[Steven Greer]] accused Crown Prince [[Hans Adam II]] of Liechtenstein of funding TREAT and identified Stubblebine as connected to the organization through his marriage — the letter stated TREAT's "GRD" was "wed to a former US Army General, ex-head of Army Intelligence and Security Command," directly referencing Stubblebine. UAP Gerb has stated there is good reason to believe Army INSCOM is likely associated with UFO crash retrievals, though this will be explored in a future project.

Stubblebine was also known for his interest in parapsychology and psychic phenomena during his tenure at INSCOM, as documented in Jon Ronson's *The Men Who Stare at Goats*.
