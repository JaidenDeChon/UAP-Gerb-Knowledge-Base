Philip Corso Jr. is the son of retired U.S. Army Lieutenant Colonel [[Philip J. Corso]]. Years after his father's death in 1998, he released his father's original, unfiltered manuscript, *Dawn of a New Age*, in Italy in 2003 and in the United States in 2011. He did so to correct inaccuracies and embellishments in the bestselling book *[[The Day After Roswell]]*, which had made Corso Sr. furious during his lifetime.

## Release of Dawn of a New Age

According to Corso Jr., he at first had no plans to make his father's manuscript public. Having seen how much his father disliked *The Day After Roswell*, co-written with [[Bill Burns]], he felt he had to publish the original material to set the record straight.

Corso Jr. revealed that his father went through *The Day After Roswell* and highlighted everything he did not say or did not like in the published version. Corso Sr. gave up halfway through the book, frustrated by the many inaccuracies, creative liberties and sensational details that co-author Burns had added and that were not in the original manuscript.

*Dawn of a New Age* is Corso Sr.'s own, unfiltered account of his experiences with UAP crash retrieval materials, the [[U.S. Army Foreign Technology Division]] and [[Technology Seeding]] programs. It does not have the 130 extra pages of embellishments found in the bestselling book.

## Preserving His Father's Legacy

Corso Jr. released the manuscript so that researchers and the public could read what his father actually claimed and wrote, apart from the dramatized and possibly fabricated material added when the book was published commercially. The difference is now critical for researchers judging the credibility of the elder Corso's testimony, because the manuscript lets them assess his claims directly, without the distortions Burns introduced.
