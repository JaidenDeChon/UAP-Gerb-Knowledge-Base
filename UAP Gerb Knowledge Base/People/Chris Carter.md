Chris Carter is an American television writer, director, and producer best known as the creator of *The X-Files* (1993–2018). In UAP research, Carter is known for contracting to use forensic illustrator [[Bill McDonald]]'s Tehachapi Triangle design in the first regular season episode of *The X-Files*, titled "Deep Throat." The design is an illustration of the [[XF-131 Super Sentinel]] based on testimony from real [[Northrop Grumman]] and [[Lockheed Martin]] engineers. Fox Network produced a computer render of McDonald's vehicle for the episode.

Rumors persist that Carter was approached by unnamed military brass who asked how he knew so much about the subjects depicted in the show, though no credible source for this claim has been identified. Former FBI agent [[John DeSouza]] has separately claimed that the first season of *The X-Files* was based on true cases he investigated.
