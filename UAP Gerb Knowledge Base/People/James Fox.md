James Fox is an American documentary filmmaker specializing in the subject of unidentified aerial phenomena. He has produced several notable documentaries on the topic and is recognized as one of the leading documentary voices in the UAP field.

## Role in Jonathan Wagant's Testimony

Fox was present during the year-2000 tape-recording session in which [[Jonathan Weygandt]] provided his testimony to [[Steven Greer]]'s Disclosure Project. Fox asked questions during the recording that were subsequently cut from the published interview, along with other details including Wagant's description of a four-fingered arm visible in the crashed craft. UAP Gerb's 2025 investigation sourced alternative tape files from Greer's archive that preserved some of Fox's questions and other details omitted from the official release.

## Battelle Identification on Joe Rogan Experience

On December 4, 2020 (Joe Rogan Experience episode #1574), Fox appeared alongside [[Jacques Vallee]], who referenced a private contractor holding recovered UFO materials but declined to name the organization. Fox intervened to identify the organization indirectly by referencing its involvement with the 1953 [[Robertson Panel]] — a reference that points specifically to [[Battelle Memorial Institute]], which was the sole contractor conducting the parallel [[Project Stork]] investigation at the time of the panel. UAP Gerb cited this exchange as documentary evidence corroborating the Battelle hypothesis.
