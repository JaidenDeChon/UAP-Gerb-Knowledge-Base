James Fox is an American documentary filmmaker who specializes in unidentified aerial phenomena. He has made several well-known documentaries on the subject and is regarded as one of the leading documentary filmmakers in the UAP field.

## Role in Jonathan Weygandt's Testimony

Fox was present in 2000 when [[Jonathan Weygandt]] recorded his testimony on tape for [[Steven Greer]]'s Disclosure Project. Fox asked questions during the recording, and they were later cut from the published interview. Other details were cut too, including Weygandt's description of a four-fingered arm visible in the crashed craft. For his 2025 investigation, Gerb obtained other tape files from Greer's archive that preserve some of Fox's questions and other details left out of the official release.

## Battelle Identification on Joe Rogan Experience

On December 4, 2020, Fox appeared with [[Jacques Vallee]] on episode #1574 of the Joe Rogan Experience. Vallee mentioned a private contractor that holds recovered UFO materials but declined to name the organization. Fox stepped in and identified it indirectly by mentioning its involvement with the 1953 [[Robertson Panel]]. That reference points specifically to [[Battelle Memorial Institute]], the only contractor conducting the parallel [[Project Stork]] investigation at the time of the panel. Gerb cited the exchange as documentary evidence corroborating the hypothesis that Battelle is the contractor Vallee described.
