James Fox is a documentary filmmaker who specializes in unidentified aerial phenomena. He has made several well-known documentaries on the subject, including *The Phenomenon* (2020), and is regarded as one of the leading documentary filmmakers in the UAP field. In Gerb's videos he turns up three times: asking questions during [[Jonathan Weygandt]]'s 2000 testimony, pointing toward [[Battelle Memorial Institute]] on the Joe Rogan Experience, and retelling the story of a film of a landing at Holloman Air Force Base.

## Career

Fox's first film on the subject was *UFOs: 50 Years of Denial?* (1997). It was followed by *Out of the Blue* (2003), *I Know What I Saw* (2009) and *The Phenomenon* (2020). *Moment of Contact* (2022) examines the 1996 [[Varginha, Brazil|Varginha]] case, and *The Program* (2024) follows the bipartisan effort in Congress to find out what the intelligence agencies know about UAP. Gerb recommends both *The Phenomenon* and *Moment of Contact*.

## Role in Jonathan Weygandt's Testimony

Fox asked questions during the 2000 taping of [[Jonathan Weygandt]]'s testimony for [[Steven Greer]]'s Disclosure Project, and his questions were cut from the published interview. Other details were cut too, including Weygandt's description of a four-fingered arm visible in the crashed craft. For his 2025 investigation, Gerb obtained rougher source files from Greer's archive that preserve some of Fox's questions and other details left out of the official release.

## Battelle Identification on Joe Rogan Experience

On December 4, 2020, Fox appeared with [[Jacques Vallee]] on episode #1574 of the Joe Rogan Experience. Vallee said a private contractor helps control the study of UFOs within the intelligence agencies, but declined to name the organization that holds recovered UFO materials. Fox stepped in and identified it indirectly by mentioning its involvement with the 1953 [[Robertson Panel]]. Gerb reads that reference as pointing to [[Battelle Memorial Institute]], which was running the parallel [[Project Stork]] investigation at the time of the panel, and cites the exchange as support for the view that Battelle is the contractor Vallee described.

## The Holloman Film

In an interview with [[Jesse Michaels]], Fox described a film of a UFO landing at Holloman Air Force Base, which his friend Allan Sandler had seen. In the 1970s Sandler and his co-producer Robert Emenegger, then working on a UAP documentary, were shown the film by two Air Force men and licensed to use it, but the licence was soon withdrawn. Gerb's video on Dugway picks up the story because one of the two men, Colonel Weinbrenner, reportedly said on his deathbed that Utah is where the alien bodies are stored.
