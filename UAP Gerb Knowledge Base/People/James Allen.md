James Allen was an American documentary filmmaker who produced the documentary "Zero Point: The Story of Mark McCandlish and the Flux Liner" (released in 2013; Gerb dates it to 2014). The film investigates in depth [[Mark McCandlish]]'s claims about the [[Alien Reproduction Vehicle (ARV)|Alien Reproduction Vehicle (Flux Liner)]]. Allen died approximately one month before his documentary was scheduled to be released, under circumstances that UAP researchers have described as highly suspicious.

## Death and Alleged Radioisotope Poisoning

Allen fell ill suddenly and was hospitalized. He died within approximately two and a half months of the illness beginning. Because his illness was unusual, a pathology analysis was done on his remains. The results allegedly showed elevated levels of multiple radioisotopes that would not naturally occur in his system, including uranium, thorium, beryllium, and cadmium. McCandlish publicly stated that the pathology indicated Allen had been poisoned with radioisotopes. He dedicated his own 2015 Secret Space Program Conference presentation to Allen's memory.

## Documentary

Allen's film told the full story of McCandlish's research into the Alien Reproduction Vehicle (ARV). It drew on McCandlish's own testimony, the accounts of [[Brad Sorenson]], and the wider context of alleged ARV programs and related phenomena. Gerb calls the film essential viewing for understanding the ARV story, alongside Steven Greer's 2001 National Press Club conference segment titled "Blueprint for a UFO."

## Pattern of Suspicious Deaths

Allen's death is cited as part of an alleged pattern of suspicious deaths among researchers and advocates in the free energy and ARV disclosure fields. The other deaths cited with it are McCandlish's own in 2021, physicist Stefan Marinov (fell from a window in 1997), cold fusion researcher Eugene Mallove (beaten to death in 2004), zero point field investor Arie DeGeus (found dead in his car in 2007), "star drive" inventor Mark Tomion (died unexpectedly in 2009), and Dr. [[Amy Eskridge]] (died 2022).
