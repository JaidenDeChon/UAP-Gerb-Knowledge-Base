Rick Price was a personal friend and associate of aerospace illustrator and ARV researcher [[Mark McCandlish]]. Price spoke with McCandlish by telephone on April 13, 2021, the day McCandlish died, and McCandlish told him he would call back later. He never made that call. UAP researchers have cited the missed call, together with McCandlish's separately expressed intention to visit his girlfriend at her animal shelter that day, as evidence that McCandlish showed no indication of suicidal intent on the day he was found dead from an apparent self-inflicted gunshot wound. Price's account of this last conversation is one of the main points raised by those questioning the official suicide determination.
