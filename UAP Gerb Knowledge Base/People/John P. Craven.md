Dr. John Piña Craven (1924–2015) was an American scientist and engineer. During the Cold War he was Chief Scientist of the US Navy's Special Projects Office, the office behind the Polaris submarine-launched ballistic missile, and from 1964 he led the [[Deep Submergence Systems Project (DSSP)]]. In his book *The Silent War: The Cold War Battle Beneath the Sea* (2001) he disclosed [[Project Sanddollar]], a highly compartmented program for recovering objects from the seafloor that was nested inside the Polaris program. Gerb returns to Craven in many videos for two reasons. His account is an independent record of how secret programs were hidden inside one another, and his "seventh veil" is, for Gerb, an early statement of the [[Legacy Program Onion Model]]. Gerb also asks whether Sand Dollar was the Navy's first UFO legacy program.

## Career

Craven was born on 30 October 1924. He was Chief Scientist of the Navy's Special Projects Office from 1959 to 1969, and in that role he worked on the Polaris program, led the Deep Submergence Systems Project and later directed the search that located the lost submarine USS *Scorpion*. In 1970 he moved to Honolulu as the University of Hawaii's dean of marine programs. He published *The Silent War* in 2001 and died at his home in Honolulu on 12 February 2015, aged 90.

## Deep Submergence Systems Project

In 1964, under [[Lyndon B. Johnson]]'s administration, Craven was put in charge of the DSSP. Its purpose was to develop technology that would let the Navy carry out deep ocean engineering (search, rescue, salvage and recovery) at much greater depths than had been possible before, and it was prompted in part by the 1963 loss of the USS *Thresher*. The same year, the body that controlled legacy programs was renamed the "303 Committee". Gerb's wider timeline of legacy programs places Craven's briefing on [[Project Sanddollar|Project Sand Dollar]] a year later, in 1965. The DSSP's products include the [[Deep Submergence Rescue Vehicle (DSRV)|Deep Submergence Rescue Vehicles]] (DSRVs), Mystic and Avalon, built by Lockheed Missiles and Space Company to rescue stranded submarines, and deep clandestine craft such as NR-1. Search vehicles rated to 20,000 feet were also planned, but were never officially built.

Craven was explicit that the rescue submersibles had a second purpose: "a DSRV designed, constructed, and deployed for every conceivable rescue mission would also be available for the intelligence 'mission impossible' that were sure to occur". Gerb's thesis is that Sand Dollar found objects, the DSSP built the craft to recover them, and the submarines of the [[National Underwater Reconnaissance Office]], among them *Halibut* and *Seawolf*, carried those craft. He notes that the DSSP began just two years after the 1962 [[Bluegill Triple Prime UFO Recovery|Bluegill Triple Prime]] recovery.

Witnesses have described DSRVs being sent on clandestine missions beyond their acknowledged purposes. One case is the [[1991 North Atlantic USO Retrieval]]: a retired Marine lieutenant colonel known as "Mark" says his DSRV crew was sent to a radioactive seafloor contact about 250 nautical miles from Aberdeen, Scotland, and helped retrieve a seamless triangular craft with repeating geometric markings.

## Project Sanddollar Disclosure

In chapter 10 of *The Silent War*, titled "Spooked", Craven disclosed that the DSSP had been set up to meet the requirements of Project Sanddollar, which Gerb describes as "an extremely compartmented and top secret program meant to identify and retrieve items of national security interest on the seafloor." Craven wrote that the program was still classified at the time of writing. He was shown an inventory of items known to be on the seabed and a map of where they lay around the world, "not just in the Bermuda Triangle", split evenly between the continental shelf and the deep ocean. He asked himself whether he could organize such a recovery program in complete secrecy, so that "my family and friends and closest professional colleagues would have no idea that I was involved in such a project."

The 1965 briefing was so secret that not even Craven's staff knew where he was going or whom he was meeting. He was taken into a secure facility by a well-known submarine officer from the Polaris program, and learned in the briefing that the officer's involvement with Polaris was itself a cover for Sanddollar. Sanddollar sat inside a web of programs hidden under Polaris, separated from it by several layers of cover programs. Gerb compares the structure to the SAP umbrellas, compartments and sub-compartments of DoD Directive 5205.07, and wagers that UFO activities sat in the same way under the [[Yellow Fruit]] umbrella. Polaris was led by Admiral [[William F. Raborn]], who later held senior roles at SAIC.

## The Seventh Veil

Craven described what he called the "seventh veil": an apparatus of secrecy around top-secret unacknowledged programs, in which no outsider, and possibly no insider, could ever be certain of having reached the innermost layer of compartmentation. He also described the principle behind it. Every special intelligence project required a real cover project, most of the people working on the cover did not know it was a cover, and the only links between the two levels were one or more individuals cleared for both.

Gerb calls the seventh veil "a much earlier version of my onion theory", the [[Legacy Program Onion Model]]: the layered, nested structure of secrecy allegedly used for UFO crash retrieval and exploitation programs. He applies it beyond the Navy, for example to the way [[MITRE Corporation|MITRE]] allegedly compartmented its own programs, so that engineers on cover tasks had no idea of the legacy work underneath.
