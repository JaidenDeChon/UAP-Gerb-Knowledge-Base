Dr. John P. Craven was an American engineer and oceanographer. During the Cold War he was Chief Scientist of the US Navy's Special Projects Office, where he oversaw the development of deep ocean engineering technologies, including the [[Deep Submergence Systems Project (DSSP)]]. In his book *The Silent War: The Cold War Battle Beneath the Sea* (2001) he disclosed the existence of [[Project Sanddollar]], a highly compartmented program for recovering objects from the seafloor that was nested inside the Polaris submarine-launched ballistic missile program. His disclosures matter to UAP research for two reasons. They are an independent historical record of how SAPs were nested inside one another to conceal sensitive recovery programs, and they corroborate the framework Gerb calls the Legacy Program Onion Model.

## Deep Submergence Systems Project

In 1964, under [[Lyndon B. Johnson]]'s administration, Craven was tasked with leading the DSSP. Its purpose was to develop technology that would let the Navy carry out deep ocean engineering (search, rescue, salvage and recovery) at much greater depths than had been possible before. The same year, the body that controlled legacy programs was renamed the "303 Committee". Gerb's wider timeline of legacy programs places Craven's briefing on [[Project Sanddollar|Project Sand Dollar]] a year later, in 1965. The DSSP's products include the [[Deep Submergence Rescue Vehicle (DSRV)|Deep Submergence Rescue Vehicles]] (DSRVs), namely the Lockheed Martin Mystic and Avalon, which were designed to rescue stranded submarines. A companion vehicle, the Deep Submergence Search Vehicle (DSSV), was never built on an acknowledged basis. It was designed to operate at much greater depths, with expanded salvage and recovery capabilities.

Witnesses have described these vessels, or similar ones, being sent on clandestine missions beyond their acknowledged purposes. One case is the [[1991 North Atlantic USO Retrieval]], a 1991 incident off Aberdeen, Scotland. A crew member of a DSRV or DSSV claimed to have taken part in retrieving an exotic triangular craft with glyphic writing from the ocean floor.

## Project Sanddollar Disclosure

In chapter 10 of *The Silent War*, titled "Spooked", Craven disclosed that the DSSP had been set up to meet the requirements of Project Sanddollar, which is described as "an extremely compartmented and top secret program meant to identify and retrieve items of national security interest on the seafloor." Craven stated that he was shown an inventory of items known to be on the seabed, along with a map of where they lay around the world. He was asked to organize a recovery program using DSSP assets in complete secrecy, set up so that "my family and friends and closest professional colleagues would have no idea that I was involved in such a project."

Craven learned that Sanddollar was nested inside the Polaris program behind several layers of cover programs. He also learned that the submarine officer who briefed him was in fact covering for Sanddollar. Until then, Craven had accepted the officer's involvement with Polaris at face value.

## The Seventh Veil

Craven described what he called the "seventh veil": an apparatus of secrecy around top-secret unacknowledged programs, in which no outsider, and possibly no insider, could ever be certain of having reached the innermost layer of compartmentation. He also described the principle behind it. Every special intelligence project required a real cover project, most of the people working on the cover did not know it was a cover, and the only links between the two levels were one or more individuals cleared for both.

His description is one of the earliest and most credible independent statements of what Gerb calls the Legacy Program Onion Model: the layered, nested structure of secrecy allegedly used for UFO crash retrieval and exploitation programs.
