William A. LaPlante (referred to in some UAP Gerb captions as "Lelant") is a senior U.S. defense acquisition official who served as Under Secretary of Defense for Acquisition and Sustainment (USD A&S) from 2022 to 2025 during the Biden administration. Earlier in his career, he held a research role at the Johns Hopkins University Applied Physics Laboratory (APL), a university-affiliated research center (UARC), and served on the Defense Science Board around 2013 alongside [[Donald M. Kerr]]. LaPlante has also served on the corporate board of the [[Aerospace Corporation]].

## MITRE Career and Dual-Hatted Air Force Role

LaPlante joined the [[MITRE Corporation]] in 2011 as missile defense portfolio director. He left MITRE in 2013 to become principal deputy in Air Force acquisition, and the Senate confirmed him as Assistant Secretary of the Air Force for Acquisition (ASAF/AT&L) on 12 February 2014. Gerb identifies this Air Force acquisition office as central to alleged Air Force UFO legacy program administration (see [[Hidden Wing]]). The Air Force announced on 18 November 2015 that he was leaving to return to MITRE as senior vice president and general manager of the National Security Engineering Center (NSEC), MITRE's Department of Defense-sponsored FFRDC. Gerb gives his Air Force tenure as 2014 to 2017 and says he held both positions at once from 2015 to 2017; the Air Force, contemporary news reports and his Wikipedia entry all have him leaving the Air Force post when he rejoined MITRE, so the public record shows no overlap. (A Catholic University alumni page also gives 2014 to 2017.) Gerb cites this dual-hatted arrangement, which joined senior Air Force acquisition leadership to MITRE's defense-sponsored FFRDC, as a further example of the personnel overlap that the channel alleges exists between government oversight roles and MITRE's alleged legacy program activity, comparable to that of [[Paul Kaminski]] and [[John Deutch|John M. Deutch]].

> Earlier versions said LaPlante held the Air Force acquisition post until 2017, overlapping with MITRE from 2015. The Air Force, UPI and Wikipedia have him confirmed in February 2014 and leaving for MITRE in November 2015; Gerb's 2014 to 2017 dates are now attributed to him.
