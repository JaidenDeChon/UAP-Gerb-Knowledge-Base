William A. LaPlante (referred to in some UAP Gerb captions as "Lelant") is a senior U.S. defense acquisition official who served as Under Secretary of Defense for Acquisition and Sustainment (USD A&S) from 2022 to 2025 during the Biden administration. Earlier in his career, he held a research role at the Johns Hopkins University Applied Physics Laboratory (APL), a university-affiliated research center (UARC), and served on the Defense Science Board around 2013 alongside [[Donald M. Kerr]]. LaPlante has also served on the corporate board of the [[Aerospace Corporation]].

## MITRE Career and Dual-Hatted Air Force Role

LaPlante joined the [[MITRE Corporation]] in 2011 as missile defense portfolio director. He left MITRE in 2013 to become Assistant Secretary of the Air Force for Acquisition, Technology and Logistics (ASAF/AT&L), serving in that role until 2017 — UAP Gerb identifies this Air Force acquisition office as central to alleged Air Force UFO legacy program administration (see [[Hidden Wing]]). In 2015, while still serving as ASAF/AT&L, LaPlante returned to MITRE as senior vice president and general manager of the National Security Engineering Center (NSEC) — MITRE's Department of Defense-sponsored FFRDC — holding both positions simultaneously from 2015 to 2017. UAP Gerb cites this dual-hatted arrangement between senior Air Force acquisition leadership and MITRE's defense-sponsored FFRDC as a further example of the personnel overlap the channel alleges exists between government oversight roles and MITRE's alleged legacy program activity, comparable to that of [[Paul Kaminski]] and [[John Deutsch|John M. Deutsch]].
