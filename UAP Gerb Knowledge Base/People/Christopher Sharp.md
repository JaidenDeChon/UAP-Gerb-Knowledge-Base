**Christopher Sharp** is a UAP investigative journalist and founder/editor of *Liberation Times*, an independent publication focused on government transparency regarding unidentified aerial phenomena, national security, and classified crash retrieval programs.

## "All UFO Disclosure Roads Lead to Rome" (2021)

In 2021, Sharp authored a significant article titled "All UFO Disclosure Roads Lead to Rome," which examined the [[Vatican]]'s alleged role in the [[1933 Magenta UFO Crash/Retrieval|1933 Magenta, Italy UFO crash/retrieval]] case. The article predated [[David Grusch]]'s public whistleblower testimony by two years, establishing early investigative groundwork connecting [[Pope Pius XII]] to the transfer of the Italian-held craft to U.S. possession.

Sharp's reporting detailed how initial relationships between [[Benito Mussolini]] and the Catholic Church were cooperative, with the Italian dictator allegedly confiding in Pope Pius XII about the recovered craft examined by the secret [[RS-33 (Gabinetto RS 33)]] group. According to Sharp's sources, the Pope became uneasy as Italy deepened its alliance with Nazi Germany, leading him to back-channel intelligence about the craft to the United States — ultimately facilitating the [[Office of Strategic Services (OSS)]] recovery operation in 1944-1945.

## UAP Crash Retrieval Reporting (2024)

Sharp authored a September 2024 article on UFO transparency legislation and UAP crash retrieval team programs, initially redacting certain agency names from the published version due to fears of professional or personal reprisals — an indication of the sensitivity surrounding active crash retrieval program reporting.
