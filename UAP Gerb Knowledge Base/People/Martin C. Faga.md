Martin C. Faga is a former US intelligence official and engineer who served as Director of the [[NRO (National Reconnaissance Office)|National Reconnaissance Office]] from 1989 to 1993 while also serving as Assistant Secretary of the Air Force for Space. In September 1992 he oversaw the official declassification of the NRO's existence, which had been secret since its founding in 1961. After leaving government he led the [[MITRE Corporation]] as its president and chief executive.

## Career

Faga left the Air Force for MITRE, where he worked on ground sensors used in Vietnam. After meeting CIA engineers doing similar work, he joined the NRO side of the CIA's Office of Development and Engineering. There he worked under the NRO's CIA-run Program B, and he later called those years the best engineering years of his career.
