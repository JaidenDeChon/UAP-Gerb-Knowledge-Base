Martin C. Faga is a former US intelligence official and engineer who served as Director of the [[NRO (National Reconnaissance Office)|National Reconnaissance Office]] from 1989 to 1993, concurrently as Assistant Secretary of the Air Force for Space. In September 1992 he oversaw the official declassification of the NRO's existence, which had been secret since its founding in 1961. After leaving government he led the [[MITRE Corporation]], serving as its president and chief executive.

## Career

Faga left the Air Force for MITRE, where he worked on ground sensors used in Vietnam. Meeting CIA engineers doing similar work, he joined the NRO side of the CIA's Office of Development and Engineering, working under the NRO's CIA-run Program B, which he later called the best engineering years of his career.

## Alleged Role in UAP Programs

UAP Gerb counts Faga among thirteen officials with NRO ties he considers likely legacy-program members or gatekeepers. He points to the Program B lineage Faga shares with [[Everett Heinman]], to his repeated movement between MITRE and the intelligence community, and to a quotation in the NRO's *Leaders of the NRO* series that the host paraphrases as praising intelligence gathered with ideas that do not conform to known physics.
