"MS" is the pseudonym of a former US Navy serviceman and Department of Defense contractor who witnessed an alleged extraterrestrial disc craft at Dugway Proving Ground's Avery Technical Center in 2009. MS served in the US Navy from 1990-2000 in the auxiliary division of the USS Teddy Roosevelt before entering government contracting work. From 2009-2013, he worked as a maintenance painter and sign technician for C Martin Company at Dugway, holding a level five proximity badge (non-security-clearance access credential) allowing entry to numerous facilities across the installation. MS's firsthand testimony of encountering a hovering, seamless disc craft surrounded by scientists and armed guards led to his being approached by the Senate Select Committee on Intelligence to provide Congressional testimony, though he declined due to work obligations as a current railroad employee. His account was featured prominently by Dr. Steven Greer at the June 2023 National Press Club whistleblower symposium.

## Background and Employment at Dugway

MS served in the United States Navy from 1990 to 2000, stationed in the auxiliary division aboard the USS Teddy Roosevelt. Following his military service, he entered government contracting work. In 2009, MS secured employment with C Martin Company, a DOD contractor specializing in facility operations, maintenance, logistics, and range operations at Dugway Proving Ground.

As a maintenance painter and sign technician, MS was issued a level five proximity badge—effectively an access credential that permitted him to enter numerous facilities across Dugway for contractor work, though this was not a security clearance. MS frequently traveled to Dugway from Kearsville (Kville), Utah in a van with other contractors who held top secret clearances. His primary responsibilities included facilities maintenance, painting, and signage across the 1,250-square-mile installation.

## 2009 Encounter at Avery Technical Center

Approximately four months into his employment at Dugway, MS was assigned to read electrical meters across roughly 15 facilities as a temporary substitute for his apprentice who had called in sick. This task was performed to enable the US government to bill the various contractors working on site—approximately 52% of Dugway operations being private contractor-controlled meant proper meter reading was critical for government accounting.

This routine assignment led MS to the Avery Technical Center, where he entered an unmarked, unnumbered building referred to as the "ghost building" (believed to be Building 1010 - Test Operations). The building had no identifying numbers or insignia on its exterior.

**The Encounter:**

Upon opening the door, MS was immediately confronted by armed guards who pointed an M16 at his chest. He later stated that his level five badge was "the only thing that saved my tail that day." Inside what MS described as a clean room area, he observed:

- Approximately six scientists in white coats (MS later learned these were likely ATCH and Battelle Memorial Institute personnel)
- A disc-shaped craft approximately 10 feet tall and 20 feet in circumference
- The craft hovering 10-12 inches off the ground under its own power with no audible noise
- A brushed metal appearance with absolutely no seams, fasteners, or visible joints—appearing to be constructed from a single seamless piece
- A conical bottom section that appeared to be floating independently from the main craft, suggesting it had been removed during dismantling
- A flat-screen electronic display near the craft showing what appeared to be a mapping or navigation system

MS's description of the craft's construction—"not a seam, not anything...almost like it was just cast...it's flawless...there's no way to replicate it for us"—emphasized the seemingly impossible manufacturing technique. He observed that the craft appeared to be in the process of being dismantled or studied, with the bottom section opened to reveal internal components.

MS later stated: "Everything happened very very quickly and just trying to take in everything as fast as I could...that [conical] section is basically doing its own thing...it's floating...like it's generating a power I don't know."

**Immediate Detention and Interrogation:**

MS was immediately detained by the guards and transported to the Garrison building at Ditto Technical Center. There, he was interrogated for approximately six hours by two men in black suits—MS speculated these were FBI, CIA, or NSA personnel. Throughout the interrogation, MS maintained that he had no idea what he had seen and was simply performing his assigned duties as a contractor. He was eventually released and continued working at Dugway.

## Post-Encounter Investigation and Contact with "GP"

In the months and years following his encounter, MS discussed the incident with family, friends, and colleagues, attempting to understand what he had witnessed. He reached out to various individuals affiliated with Dugway operations.

MS made contact with a senior DOD chemist working in legacy operations at Dugway, identified by the pseudonym "GP." MS states he "reached out to him and got him to talk about my situation by accident." GP worked in Dugway's UAP legacy programs both above ground and in the alleged subterranean facility.

**GP's Disclosures to MS:**

GP confirmed several critical details about what MS had witnessed:

- The craft was extraterrestrial in origin
- It operated via gravity displacement propulsion
- The mapping/navigation display MS observed could generate two reference points to create a wormhole, enabling point-to-point in terstellar travel ("that's where he says that interstellar travel is how they get from point A to point B so quickly")
- A deep underground military base (DUMB) existed beneath Dugway, spanning approximately 1,300 square miles
- An underground light rail system connected the Dugway DUMB to numerous other facilities nationwide, using magnetic levitation (maglev) technology
- The tunnel system was wide enough to transport enormous aircraft like a 747
- The rail network could "transport the president's plane basically anywhere they need to without putting it in the air"

GP was described by MS as a DOD chemist by degree who was promoted and moved to Dugway specifically to work in UAP legacy programs. MS lost contact with GP when he left C Martin in 2014, though efforts to reconnect are ongoing.

**Additional Corroboration:**

MS also learned from a current railroad co-worker that the underground facility exists and that this individual had assisted in digging out the tunneling system beneath Dugway Proving Ground. This co-worker is being contacted for further testimony.

## Senate Select Committee on Intelligence Outreach

MS was approached by elements of the Senate Select Committee on Intelligence (SSCI) to testify regarding his claims about UAP legacy programs at Dugway. MS has shared screenshots of this outreach as evidence. The SSCI offered to pay for his travel and accommodate his testimony schedule.

However, MS declined the invitation due to work obligations—as a current railroad employee, he was unable to take a week or more away from work, even with the committee covering expenses. MS's decision to decline Congressional testimony despite official government interest underscores his priority on maintaining his current employment rather than seeking publicity or attention.

## Public Disclosure and Steven Greer Symposium

MS's testimony was featured by Dr. Steven Greer at the June 2023 National Press Club whistleblower symposium, the first such event since Greer's landmark 2001 panel. Though MS was not physically present at the event, Greer presented his story in detail, including:

- The encounter at Avery Technical Center with the extraterrestrial vehicle
- The location of the alleged DUMB entrance near German Village in the "Avery sector"
- GP's disclosures about the underground facility's scale and purpose
- The deep underground complex with capacity for parking Air Force One

This symposium marked the first major public disclosure of MS's testimony, bringing widespread attention to Dugway Proving Ground as an alleged UAP legacy program location.

## Credibility Assessment

Several factors support MS's credibility as a witness:

**Documentary Evidence:** MS has provided screenshots of Senate Select Committee on Intelligence outreach, verifying that Congressional investigators found his testimony credible enough to pursue official testimony.

**Verifiable Employment:** MS's employment at C Martin Company from 2009-2013 can be verified, as can C Martin's presence at Dugway Proving Ground near the administration center.

**Consistent Testimony:** MS's account has remained consistent across multiple tellings, with specific technical details about the craft's appearance, the facility layout, and the sequence of events.

**Corroborating Witnesses:** GP (senior DOD chemist) and MS's railroad co-worker both allegedly corroborate key aspects of his claims about underground facilities at Dugway.

**Lack of Profit Motive:** MS declined Congressional testimony due to work obligations and has not sought financial gain from his story, suggesting genuine testimony rather than fabrication for attention or profit.

**Technical Plausibility:** Details about Avery Technical Center's internal rail system, Building 1010's purpose, and the facility's classified nature align with publicly available documentation.
