Daniel Sheehan is a prominent constitutional attorney and activist, known for high-profile cases and for his role as general counsel for UAP whistleblower [[Luis Elizondo]]. In 1977, under orders from President [[Jimmy Carter]], Sheehan was tasked with reviewing classified portions of [[Project Blue Book]] at the Library of Congress. He claims that during the review he found five or six photographs of a crashed saucer-shaped UAP in snowy terrain, with distinctive symbols around the base of the craft's dome. In 2023 the Green Beret witness [[RA]] independently identified those symbols as matching the hieroglyphic-like characters projected by recovered technology at the [[Off-World Technologies Division]].

## Background and Legal Career

Daniel Sheehan has built a career around constitutional law, civil rights and high-profile national security cases. His legal work has touched on government secrecy, national security operations and whistleblower protection, which made him a natural fit as counsel for people involved in UAP disclosure.

## 1977 Project Blue Book Review Under Carter

### Presidential Assignment

In 1977, attorney Daniel Sheehan claimed that he was assigned, under orders from President [[Jimmy Carter]], to search classified portions of the [[Project Blue Book]] files held at the Library of Congress. Carter had a documented personal interest in UAP and had filed his own UFO sighting report.

Carter's interest in UAP is on record. He campaigned on a promise to release UFO information, and his own sighting is well documented. The assignment to Sheehan fits Carter's known attempts to access classified UAP information during his presidency.

### Discovery of Classified Photographs

Among the classified materials, Sheehan claims to have found a series of five or six photographs of a downed saucer-shaped UAP.

The craft had crashed in a snowy field, plowing a visible ditch through the ground, and was stuck in the side of an embankment. U.S. Air Force personnel can be seen in the photographs, recognisable by their fur-hooded cold-weather jackets. From their body language as they photographed the craft, they appeared "really excited".

The craft was saucer-shaped, with a semicircular dome. Symbols ran along the base of the dome, all the way around its bottom edge.

### The Symbols

Sheehan described the markings on the craft in some detail:

- "Straight lines with dashes and dots and little horseshoe shapes"
- Arranged in sequence around the base of the craft's dome
- Clearly different from the Roswell symbols. Sheehan said explicitly that "it didn't look like the ones at Roswell. These are different."
- He could not recall the exact sequence, but he remembered the shapes precisely.

In Sheehan's words: *"Here's the semicircle of the craft like this and the edges here, and they were a series of these straight lines with dashes and dots and little horseshoe shapes like this. And they went all the way around the bottom. But that's exactly what they looked like. And it didn't look like the ones at Roswell. These are different."*

### RA's Independent Corroboration (2023)

During his interviews with Gerb, Green Beret [[RA]] was shown various examples of alleged UAP iconography, including the well-known [[Roswell I-Beam]] symbols. He found no match until he was shown a recreation of the symbols Sheehan had described.

RA said the symbols Sheehan drew "look quite close to the symbols projected from this anomalous technology". These were the symbols RA saw projected, with no visible projection mechanism, from the gauntlet-like device at the [[Off-World Technologies Division]]. He made the match blind: he was not told whose description he was looking at.

The two witnesses had no connection to each other, and their accounts are 46 years apart (1977 and 2023), which makes the match strong independent corroboration.

## Possible Source Incident: 1952 Spitsbergen, Norway

### Timeline Analysis

[[Project Blue Book]] ran from 1952 to 1969. Sheehan's photographs came from classified sections of Blue Book, and they showed:

- U.S. Air Force personnel
- Snow and cold-weather gear
- Excitement suggesting an active recovery operation

UAP researchers have tried to identify the incident from these details. One candidate is the 1952 event at Spitsbergen, Norway:

- A 47-meter disc reportedly crashed in Norway
- The CIA took a documented interest in the case
- The date falls within the classified Blue Book period
- The location matches the snowy terrain
- However, the case is confused, possibly because of disinformation

Apart from the Norway case, few documented UAP incidents from the Blue Book era match every condition (snow, USAF involvement, saucer shape, classified status). This suggests the photographed incident may have been a retrieval operation kept entirely secret, which never entered public UFO case databases.

## Connection to Radiance Technologies and Hypersonics

In 2023, speaking at UFO conferences, Sheehan made further claims linking defense contractor [[Radiance Technologies]] to UAP reverse engineering. He alleged that Radiance's Prompt Global Strike hypersonic missile program drew on reverse-engineered UAP technologies.

The claim stands out because [[SAIC]] and [[Sandia National Laboratories]], both historically implicated in UAP programs, are connected to [[Naval Surface Warfare Center Crane]]'s hypersonics programs. It suggests a possible technology transfer pipeline: crash retrieval, then analysis at facilities like Crane, then use in advanced weapons programs.

## Significance in UAP Research

Daniel Sheehan's testimony matters for several reasons:

1. The assignment came from Carter, which gives it a high-level government connection.
2. RA's 2023 identification of matching symbols is blind corroboration from 46 years later.
3. Because the symbols differ from those at Roswell, the account establishes more than one crash retrieval with different symbol sets, suggesting multiple non-human sources or craft types.
4. The classified Blue Book photographs were physical documentation, and they may still exist in government archives.
5. If the Spitsbergen, Norway connection is accurate, it extends confirmed U.S. recovery operations to incidents outside the United States.
