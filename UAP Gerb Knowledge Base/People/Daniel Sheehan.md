Daniel Sheehan is an American constitutional and public interest attorney and activist, known for high-profile cases from the Pentagon Papers to Iran-Contra and, in the UAP field, as the attorney for whistleblower [[Luis Elizondo]]. He says that in 1977, under orders from President [[Jimmy Carter]], he reviewed classified portions of [[Project Blue Book]] and found five or six photographs of a crashed saucer-shaped UAP in snowy terrain, with symbols around the base of the craft's dome. In Gerb's coverage, the Green Beret witness [[RA]] picked out those symbols as close to the characters he saw projected by recovered technology at the [[Off-World Technologies Division]].

## Background and Legal Career

Sheehan was born on 9 April 1945 in Glens Falls, New York, and grew up in nearby Warrensburg. He graduated from Harvard College in 1967 and from Harvard Law School in 1970, and later also studied at Harvard Divinity School. In 1971 he was co-counsel defending *The New York Times*'s right to publish the Pentagon Papers. He went on to work on the Watergate break-in case, the Karen Silkwood case against Kerr-McGee, the Greensboro massacre case and the La Penca bombing case. He co-founded two non-profit public policy centres: the Christic Institute (in 1980), which he led as president and chief counsel and through which he brought the Iran-Contra lawsuit, and the Romero Institute. His autobiography is *The People's Advocate*. His recent work centres on UAP disclosure through the New Paradigm Institute.

Much of his legal work has dealt with government secrecy, national security operations and whistleblower protection, and that work drew him to represent people involved in UAP disclosure.

## 1977 Project Blue Book Review Under Carter

### Presidential Assignment

At UFO Megacon in 2021, Sheehan said that in 1977 he was assigned, under orders from President [[Jimmy Carter]], to search classified portions of the [[Project Blue Book]] files. Gerb notes that Carter "did have a verifiable interest in UAP": he had filed a report of his own UFO sighting and had campaigned on a promise to release UFO information. Gerb sets aside the question of classified programs running parallel to Blue Book, which he has covered elsewhere, but says the account immediately recalls the testimony of [[Steven Lovekin]].

### The Photographs

Among the classified materials, Sheehan says he found a series of five or six photographs of a downed saucer-shaped UAP. The craft had hit a snowy field, ploughed a ditch through it and lodged in the side of an embankment. He took the men in the photographs for U.S. Air Force personnel, "because it was snowing and they had these big furry hoods on with their jackets", and "you could tell that they were really excited" as they photographed it. The craft was saucer-shaped, with a semicircular dome, and symbols ran all the way around the base of the dome.

### The Symbols

Sheehan described the markings in some detail:

- "straight lines with dashes and dots and little horseshoe shapes"
- arranged in sequence all the way around the base of the dome
- different from the Roswell symbols: "it didn't look like the ones at Roswell. These are different."
- he could not recall the order of the symbols, only their shapes: "I don't remember in which order they were or the exact sequence, but that's exactly what the shapes were."

In Sheehan's words: *"Here's the semicircle of the craft like this and the edges here, and they were a series of these straight lines with dashes and dots and little horseshoe shapes like this. And they went all the way around the bottom."*

### RA's Identification

During his conversations with Green Beret [[RA]], Gerb showed him many of the best-known symbols in ufology, including the [[Roswell I-Beam]]. RA recognised none of them until Gerb reached Sheehan's description, a relatively obscure reference. RA said the symbols drawn from it "look quite close to the symbols projected from this anomalous technology": the characters he says were projected, with no visible projection mechanism, from the gauntlet-like device at the [[Off-World Technologies Division]]. Gerb calls the connection "truly truly fantastic stuff."

### A Possible Source Incident: Spitsbergen, 1952

Because Blue Book ran from 1952 to 1969 and the photographs show U.S. airmen in snow, Gerb briefly considers which recovery they might show. His one candidate is the 1952 [[Spitsbergen, Norway]] case, a reported crash of a 47-metre disc that drew CIA interest, though its story has "some confusing pathways and possible misinformation." Apart from the Norway case, he finds few incidents that fit, and suggests the recovery may have been kept under wraps.

### The Symbols in Gerb's Other Cases

Gerb returns to Sheehan's description when other witnesses describe writing on a craft. In his Kecksburg video it is one of three parallels he draws for the hieroglyph-like band witnesses saw around the [[1965 Kecksburg, Pennsylvania Crash Retrieval|Kecksburg]] craft, alongside the writing [[Jesse Marcel]] described on the Roswell debris and the characters RA saw. In his video on Navy crash retrievals he sets it beside the geometric, hieroglyphic-style writing on a triangular craft a DSRV crew says it found on the seabed, noting that such writing "is not rare, but not common" in crash retrieval accounts.

## Philip Corso's Film

Sheehan says he was shown a posthumous 8mm home movie that [[Philip J. Corso]] made for his grandchildren, and calls it "extraordinarily persuasive." Because of it, he gives Corso's account of Roswell technology "a considerable amount of credibility," and he says the aerospace companies' own histories of the silicon chip and fiber optics are "exactly what they would do" to obscure where the technology came from. Sheehan places Corso's encounter with a landed craft at Alamogordo in July 1945. Gerb says Sheehan "is indeed incorrect about the 1945 date": Corso set the encounter at the Red Canyon missile range, part of White Sands, where he was commander in 1957 and 1958.

## Connection to Radiance Technologies and Hypersonics

In 2023 Sheehan alleged that the defense contractor [[Radiance Technologies]] was involved in UAP reverse engineering, and that its prompt global strike program used reverse-engineered UAP technology in developing hypersonic missiles. Gerb raises the claim alongside a 2023 hypersonics technology transfer between [[Naval Surface Warfare Center Crane]], [[Sandia National Laboratories]] and other participants, because [[SAIC]] has ties to both, though he allows that there is "probably not much of a connection here."

## Role as Luis Elizondo's Attorney

Sheehan represents former AATIP director [[Luis Elizondo]]. In May 2021, with Sheehan as his attorney, Elizondo filed a complaint with the Department of Defense Inspector General alleging that Defense Department officials were trying to discredit him after he went public. Sheehan told reporters that those officials were "using the color of authority of their offices to disparage him and discredit him."
