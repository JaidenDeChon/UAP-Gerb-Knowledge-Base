Daniel Sheehan is a prominent constitutional attorney and activist known for his involvement in high-profile cases and his role as general counsel for UAP whistleblower [[Luis Elizondo]]. In 1977, under orders from President [[Jimmy Carter]], Sheehan was tasked with reviewing classified portions of [[Project Blue Book]] at the Library of Congress, during which he claims to have discovered five or six photographs of a crashed saucer-shaped UAP in snowy terrain featuring distinctive symbols around the base of the craft's dome — symbols that Green Beret witness [[RA]] independently identified in 2023 as matching the hieroglyphic-like characters projected by recovered technology at the [[Off-World Technologies Division]].

## Background and Legal Career

Daniel Sheehan has built a career around constitutional law, civil rights, and high-profile national security cases. His legal work has intersected with government secrecy, national security operations, and whistleblower protection — making him a natural fit as counsel for UAP disclosure figures.

## 1977 Project Blue Book Review Under Carter

### Presidential Assignment

In 1977, attorney Daniel Sheehan claimed that he was assigned under orders from President [[Jimmy Carter]] — who had a documented personal interest in UAP and had filed his own UFO sighting report — to scour classified portions of [[Project Blue Book]] files housed at the Library of Congress.

President Carter's interest in UAP is on record: he campaigned on a promise to release UFO information and had a well-documented sighting of his own. The assignment to Sheehan fits Carter's known attempts to access classified UAP information during his presidency.

### Discovery of Classified Photographs

Among the classified materials, Sheehan claims to have found a series of five or six photographs depicting a downed saucer-shaped UAP:

**Physical Scene:**
- Crashed in a snowy field
- Plowed through the terrain creating a visible ditch
- Stuck in the side of an embankment
- U.S. Air Force personnel visible in the photographs, identifiable by their fur-hooded jackets appropriate for cold weather
- Personnel appeared "really excited" based on their body language while photographing the craft

**Craft Description:**
- Saucer-shaped with a semicircular dome structure
- Symbols running along the base of the dome, all the way around the bottom perimeter

### The Symbols

Sheehan provided specific details about the iconography on the craft:

**Symbol Characteristics:**
- "Straight lines with dashes and dots and little horseshoe shapes"
- Arranged in sequence around the base of the craft's dome
- **Distinctly different from Roswell symbols**: Sheehan explicitly stated "it didn't look like the ones at Roswell. These are different."
- Sheehan could not recall the exact sequence but remembered the shapes precisely

Sheehan's description: *"Here's the semicircle of the craft like this and the edges here, and they were a series of these straight lines with dashes and dots and little horseshoe shapes like this. And they went all the way around the bottom. But that's exactly what they looked like. And it didn't look like the ones at Roswell. These are different."*

### RA's Independent Corroboration (2023)

When Green Beret [[RA]] was shown various examples of alleged UAP iconography during his interviews with [[UAP Gerb]] — including the famous [[Roswell I-Beam]] symbols — he identified **no matches** until shown a recreation of the symbols Sheehan had described.

**RA's reaction:**
- The symbols Sheehan drew "look quite close to the symbols projected from this anomalous technology"
- These were the symbols RA saw projected (without visible projection mechanism) from the gauntlet-like device at the [[Off-World Technologies Division]]
- The match was identified blind — RA was not told whose description he was viewing

This independent corroboration across 46 years (1977 → 2023) and between two unconnected witnesses represents significant evidential convergence.

## Possible Source Incident: 1952 Spitsbergen, Norway

### Timeline Analysis

[[Project Blue Book]] ran from 1952 to 1969. Since Sheehan reviewed photographs from **classified** sections of Blue Book, and the images showed:

- U.S. Air Force personnel  
- Snow and cold-weather gear
- Excitement suggesting an active recovery operation

UAP researchers have attempted to identify the specific incident. One candidate is the **1952 Spitsbergen, Norway** event:

- Reported crash of a 47-meter disc in Norway
- CIA took documented interest in the case
- Timeline fits the classified Blue Book period
- Geographic location matches snowy terrain
- However, confusion exists around this case with possible disinformation pathways

Besides the Norway case, few documented UAP incidents from the Blue Book era match all conditions (snow, USAF involvement, saucer shape, classified status). This suggests the photographed incident may have been a retrieval operation kept entirely under wraps, never entering public UFO case databases.
