**Robert Oechsler** is a former NASA mission specialist and robotics engineer who became a UAP researcher following a 1989 introduction to senior national security figures who privately confirmed US possession of extraterrestrial technology. His accounts document direct conversations with individuals at the nexus of US Navy intelligence and defense contractor circles.

## 1989 Introduction by Bobby Ray Inman

In 1989, Oechsler was introduced to [[Rear Admiral Sumner Shapiro]] — former Director of Naval Intelligence — by [[Bobby Ray Inman]], then a board member of [[SAIC]] and former NSA Director and CIA Deputy Director. Shapiro told Oechsler that factions within the US possess extraterrestrial vehicles and that he had personally studied one at close quarters. Shapiro described teams disassembling craft, packing them, and shipping them to different facilities. He stated the craft featured unique interlocking components requiring disassembly in an exact sequence. Shapiro was serving as a [[BDM International]] board member at the time — a company later acquired by [[TRW Inc]] and through TRW by [[Northrop Grumman]].

This chain — Inman to Shapiro to Oechsler — represents one of the few documented instances of US Navy intelligence seniority privately confirming the existence and physical characteristics of recovered non-human vehicles.
