Michael Herrera is a former United States Marine Corps infantryman and SAW (Squad Automatic Weapon) gunner who witnessed a large octagonal craft and armed operators during a humanitarian relief mission in Indonesia in October 2009. His encounter occurred approximately 9-10 days after a September 2009 earthquake, when he was assigned to officer's chow detail aboard the USS Denver and volunteered for a security mission. Herrera has become a prominent UAP whistleblower, testifying to AARO in April 2023 and speaking at the National Press Club in June 2023, and claims to have been contacted by insiders from classified UAP programs who provided him with additional context about what he witnessed.

## 2009 Indonesia Encounter

### Mission Context

In October 2009, Herrera was deployed aboard the [[USS Denver]] as part of a humanitarian relief operation following a major earthquake in Indonesia. He was assigned to officer's chow detail with fellow Marines when a female USMC major briefed volunteers in the wardroom for a security mission, showing pictures of Barack Obama as a child alongside references to his time in Indonesia. Herrera and his team were issued M16 rifles (Herrera typically carried a SAW) and transported via CH-53 Super Stallion helicopter first to Tobing Airport (described as having distinctive blue roofs), then onward to a hasty landing zone in heavily vegetated terrain with hills.

The mission was described as providing security for supply deliveries to remote villages, justified by the region's reputation as the second-largest terrorist capital in the world. However, Herrera's team was dropped at a LZ with no other personnel present, no radio communications, and no clear extraction timeline — an unusual circumstance for a routine security detail.

### The Craft and Observation

After landing, Herrera's team of six Marines moved to an elevated position on a hill overlooking the LZ. From this vantage point, they observed what appeared to be a structure changing colors in the distance, partially obscured by vegetation. The decision was made to investigate despite abandoning their security post. As they descended and approached, they observed:

- **An octagonal-shaped craft** approximately the size of a football field, with a pyramid-like top section
- **Surface characteristics**: The craft appeared to absorb light, described as "the blackest, darkest thing" Herrera had ever seen, with a scale-like pattern across its surface
- **Corner vents** positioned along the edges of the craft
- **Color-changing capability**: The craft changed from light matte gray to dark matte black
- **An elevating platform**: Beneath the craft was a raised platform roughly the same length as the craft itself, with pointed features. This platform slowly raised approximately 10 feet off the ground before the craft departed
- **Silent operation**: The craft emitted only a low hum similar to a guitar amplifier or transformer, with no rotor wash or disturbance to surrounding vegetation
- **Colored lights**: Upon takeoff, lights at the craft's corners activated in red, yellow, green, and blue

### The Transport Operation

Concurrent with the craft observation, Herrera's team witnessed a logistics operation involving:

- **Four black up-armored trucks** resembling Ford F-350s with aggressive off-road tires and reinforced armor similar to Secret Service vehicles
- **Pelican cases** (weapons cases) in each truck bed
- **Trailers with black shipping containers**, each bearing a large black cylinder spanning the full width of the container
- **Night vision equipment** mounted on the sides of trucks, suggesting nighttime operations
- Trucks moving onto the elevating platform beneath the craft

Herrera initially testified to AARO that he believed the cylinders were humidity control equipment possibly for drug trafficking. He later learned from insiders that the cylinders were oxygen supply systems and the containers held human beings — allegedly individuals recruited from disaster zones under false pretenses to serve as "pink assets" (biological subjects) in classified programs.

### Armed Interception

As Herrera's team broke into the clearing, they were immediately intercepted by **eight armed operators** in unmarked black uniforms using a tactical formation called a "nosbed" (interlocking fields of fire from two flanking groups). The c operators:

- Wore all-black gear with no identifying markings or insignia
- Carried M4-style rifles with ACOGs and PEQ-16 illuminators
- Were not identifiable as US Navy SEALs (who Herrera had been briefed might be in the area) or any conventional military unit
- Demonstrated professional tactics consistent with former military training
- **Disarmed the Marines methodically**, removing magazines and slinging weapons uncomfortably tight on the Marines' backs
- **Scanned military IDs** using advanced thin devices resembling modern smartphones (more advanced than 2009-era BlackBerrys or Sony Ericsson phones), after first attempting to use a BATS-like biometric system
- Threatened the Marines, stating "we will fucking kill you" and "it's easy to get lost out here"
- One operator maintained a standoff position with a radio, appearing to coordinate with others

The operators knew exactly where the Marines' IDs were located, suggesting they were former US military personnel. After searching the team and confiscating their camera documentation, the operators returned the Marines' weapons but loaded in a deliberately difficult-to-use configuration, then ordered them to leave and not look back.

### Return and Aftermath

Upon returning to the LZ, Herrera's team encountered an unknown Gunnery Sergeant whom Herrera had never seen before — not wearing red patches indicating ship assignment and of unclear origin. The Gunnery Sergeant appeared upset about the Marines' weapons being improperly slung. A CH-53 helicopter arrived shortly after radio communication and extracted the team back to the USS Denver, where they returned weapons and borrowed magazines to the armory.

Back at Subic Bay, Herrera asked a fellow Marine if he would tell anyone about the incident. The Marine responded "absolutely not," and the team remained silent. None of the Marines have come forward publicly, and one later sent a text message published in the Daily Mail stating "leave me out of that" when asked by Senate Intelligence Committee representatives (via Herrera) to provide anonymous testimony.

## Whistleblower Journey

### Coming Forward

For approximately 14 years, Herrera remained silent about his encounter, bound by a non-disclosure agreement that allegedly threatened his life. His motivation for coming forward stemmed from recently passed whistleblower protection laws that rendered certain NDAs illegal, combined with a desire to help others who may have had similar experiences and to contribute to public understanding of classified UAP programs. Herrera states he was not a believer in UAPs prior to his experience and understands why others are skeptical.

### AARO Testimony (April 2023)

In late April 2023, Herrera testified to [[AARO]] (All-domain Anomaly Resolution Office) in a secure facility (SKIF). He describes the experience as interrogation-like and states that [[Sean Kirkpatrick]], AARO's director, showed little interest, asking only three repetitive questions focused solely on identifying the operators Herrera encountered. Kirkpatrick stated AARO had access to satellite imagery of the incident location and promised to follow up, but after more than a year, Herrera says he was never contacted again to review the imagery.

Critically, Herrera alleges that **AARO's Historical Report Volume 1 deliberately misrepresented his testimony**, falsely attributing statements that he saw an "extraterrestrial vehicle" and that the operators were "US Special Forces" — claims Herrera adamantly denies making. He asserts this was a purposeful omission and distortion, not an innocent error, noting that another individual present in the SKIF can corroborate that he never made those statements.

### National Press Club (June 2023)

Herrera spoke publicly at the National Press Club in June 2023, providing his account of the 2009 encounter. This appearance was preceded by a two-day disclosure event on June 10-11 where Herrera presented his hypothesis that the operation involved drug trafficking (based on his interpretation of the cylindrical containers). Dr. [[Steven Greer]], who helped arrange Herrera's Daily Mail interview and National Press Club appearance, facilitated much of Herrera's public disclosure efforts.

### Contact with Insiders

Following his public testimony, Herrera was approached by individuals claiming to be insiders within classified UAP programs, facilitated initially through Dr. Greer's contacts. Key developments included:

**Encrypted Communication**: An insider at the June 2023 NPC event sent Dr. Greer an encrypted text message (via a platform described as more sophisticated than Signal) stating they knew what Herrera had witnessed and wanted to make contact. Greer showed Herrera this message in a green room, with Herrera's wife present.

**CIA NOC Operative**: Herrera received contact information for a CIA Non-Official Cover (NOC) operative who offered to meet and provide documentation. After deliberation, Herrera called and arranged to be flown to an undisclosed location for a face-to-face meeting. The operative:
- Conducted the meeting with extensive security measures, including chip-detection devices
- Provided Herrera with a loaded sidearm as a trust-building gesture
- Appeared nervous and fidgety, which Herrera interpreted as genuine concern
- Apologized to Herrera on behalf of the organization for the harassment and trauma caused by the 2009 encounter
- Stated he would "never kill a patriot" or innocent people, though acknowledged that people have been murdered to protect program secrecy
- Took Herrera to an underground facility to prove his identity and the existence of the programs

**Information Revealed**: Through these insider contacts, Herrera learned:
- The operation he witnessed was not drug trafficking but **human trafficking for classified programs**
- Individuals are recruited from disaster zones under false pretenses and used as "pink assets" (biological subjects/equipment)
- Participants are heavily drugged and do not fully understand or recall their involvement
- Within the USG, there are two groups studying UAP: the **"Blue Group"** (programs with congressional oversight like AARO, AATIP, and Project Blue Book) and the **"Black Group"** (an organization founded in the 1940s to reverse engineer non-human intelligence technology, operating without oversight in a manner mirroring the [[Wilson Davis Memo]])
- The Black Group has successfully reverse-engineered NHI craft to an unknown degree and utilizes these craft for operations; flying craft are considered "boring tech" compared to other technologies
- **EMP-like weaponry** has been developed that can target, disrupt, and disable UAP for crash retrieval purposes
- The program recruits individuals with **psionic abilities** (referred to as "P3") often from third-world nations, as these individuals are needed to interface with and control NHI technology
- Recruits are bargained with in exchange for security and safety of their families, likely due to permanent or semi-permanent isolation
- The Indonesia operation Herrera witnessed involved this P3 recruitment and transport
- Reverse-engineered craft (ARVs) have advanced to the point where insiders cannot distinguish them from genuine non-human intelligence craft
- There are **internal factions** within these programs: "good guys" who want disclosure and Congressional oversight, versus "bad guys" who want to maintain secrecy, commit crimes against humanity, and retain power
- The covert group functions as an enforcement arm for political corruption with assets embedded in government, law enforcement, and military institutions
- Civilian aircraft accidentally entering Temporary Flight Restricted Areas (TFRs) near classified installations are allegedly targeted with undisclosed weapon systems
- Non-human intelligence craft are being shot down in desert locations worldwide, risking provocation of an advanced species

**Sentinels of Ether Book Project**: The Insider told Herrera he is writing a book titled "Sentinels of Ether" through a ghost writer, mixing truth and fiction similar to Tom DeLonge's "Secret Machines." The book describes an event in Mexico in 2004 where a paramilitary black program team wiped out a JSOC team during an operation involving transporting humans in shipping containers to a hidden mountain base. The Insider provided Herrera with a two-chapter manuscript currently under DOPSR (Defense Office of Pre-publication and Security Review) review. The Insider claims to be playing "4D chess on DOPSR" by intentionally including deep black classified information to test whether pre-publication reviewers will flag it, using the government's response as inverse intelligence. This strategy raised credibility concerns for Joey Is Not My Name, who questioned how a fictional book could serve as corroborating evidence when it describes a completely different event.

**Verification**: Reddit investigator [[Joey Is Not My Name]] conducted extensive open-source verification of Herrera's account, independently confirming key operational details including the USS Denver's sole assignment to the West Coast of Indonesia, the 31st Marine Expeditionary Unit's presence, the use of CH-53 Super Stallion helicopters, and the establishment of hasty landing zones in remote terrain. Joey's research corroborated all verifiable military operation details surrounding Herrera's testimony.

More significantly, Joey independently verified through objective data (flight records and other intelligence) that Herrera was indeed transported to a secure facility for a meeting with an insider. Prior to the meeting, Joey recorded a phone conversation with Herrera in which Herrera described his apprehension and the meeting details, serving as documentary evidence and a "dead man switch" in case Herrera disappeared. Joey's verification did not rely on trusting Herrera's word but on tracking the movements and verifying the facility location objectively.

Herrera states he has been in contact with David Grusch and has successfully connected insiders with prominent figures in the disclosure movement. He describes ongoing harassment as validation that he is revealing accurate information.

## Parallels with Other Witnesses

Herrera's account shares striking similarities with other UAP encounter testimonies documented by UAP Gerb:

- **Unmarked armed operators**: Like [[Rodrik Castle]] and [[Jonathan Weygandt]], Herrera encountered personnel in all-black or unmarked gear who were not identifiable as conventional military — consistent with UAP Gerb's theory that these were [[Wackenhut Corporation|Wackenhut]]-trained [[DOE Special Response Teams|DOE Special Response Teams (SRTs)]]
- **Detention and intimidation**: All three witnesses were detained, debriefed, and warned against disclosure
- **Anthrax booster**: Following his encounter, Herrera received an anthrax booster shot — the same post-encounter medical protocol administered to Castle and Wagant, which UAP Gerb theorizes may be connected to proximity to nuclear-powered craft or materials associated with the [[Atomic Energy Act of 1954]]
- **Active-duty Marines**: All three witnesses were active-duty Marines at the time of their encounters

## Controversy and Credibility

### Nathan's Contradictory Account

A fellow Marine named Nathan, who was Herrera's team leader at a different point during the deployment, came forward claiming Herrera fabricated the UAP encounter. However, Nathan acknowledged he was **not present during the specific mission where Herrera claims the UAP observation occurred**. Nathan provided a photograph of two Marines on a helicopter allegedly being flown into Indonesia, claiming one was Herrera and asserting the Marines in the photo did not have rifles — contradicting Herrera's account that his team was armed. Herrera denies being in the photograph and provided the names of the two individuals he believes are shown.

The rifle question is significant because humanitarian missions typically do not arm personnel. However, Joey Is Not My Name's investigation revealed that the first wave of Marines in the Indonesia relief operation were in fact armed with M16 A4s due to heavy terrorist presence in the region, but subsequent deployments were ordered to stop carrying rifles after an Air Force Colonel objected due to bad optics. This corroborates Herrera's claim of being armed while creating ambiguity about which deployment wave Nathan photographed.

Herrera notes that Nathan's testimony is paradoxical: if Herrera were fabricating the story, he could have simply acknowledged going on a separate mission with Nathan while maintaining his UAP account. Instead, Herrera denies being in Nathan's photograph, which would be a strange choice for a fabricator seeking to cover all bases.

### Documentation and Evidence

- Herrera captured photographs and video of the craft and operation using a camera stored in his dump pouch
- These materials were confiscated by the operators during the encounter
- Herrera provided names of all fellow Marines involved to AARO, the Pentagon, and the Senate Intelligence Committee
- None of the fellow Marines have come forward; one actively refused when contacted by Senate representatives
- No documentation or confirmation from other witnesses has emerged publicly

### Motivation and Financial Interests

Herrera repeatedly emphasizes that he is not profiting financially from his disclosure and that his primary motivation is truth and helping others potentially affected by these programs. He runs his own business separate from his UAP advocacy and does not accept payment for interviews or appearances.

### Disinformation Concerns

The credibility of The Insider's disclosures to Herrera remains unverified. Investigators have noted parallels to the CIA's 1989 disinformation campaign against [[Paul Benowitz]], in which Benowitz was fed false information about extraterrestrials and underground bases, eventually driving him to psychological breakdown. If The Insider is conducting a similar psychological operation, this would not invalidate Herrera's 2009 Indonesia UAP encounter, which occurred before any insider contact and has been independently corroborated in its verifiable operational details. However, it raises questions about the truthfulness of insider claims regarding the Black Group, P3 recruitment, and other program details.

## Assessment

Herrera's testimony is highly detailed and internally consistent across multiple interviews and public appearances. The existence of insider contacts provides additional context unavailable to other witnesses, though the insiders themselves remain anonymous. The apparent misrepresentation of his testimony in AARO's historical report, if accurate, suggests institutional resistance to his account. The refusal of fellow Marines to corroborate publicly, while understandable given potential career and personal risks, leaves Herrera as a sole public witness to an extraordinary claim.
