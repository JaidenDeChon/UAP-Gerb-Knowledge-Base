Michael Herrera is a former United States Marine Corps infantryman who claims to have witnessed a large octagonal craft during a humanitarian aid mission in Sumatra, Indonesia, in 2009. The encounter reportedly involved armed operators in unmarked gear who detained Herrera and his team, a pattern that closely parallels the experiences of [[Rodrik Castle]] in 1997 and [[Jonathan Wagant]] in Peru the same year.

## 2009 Sumatra Encounter

While deployed on a humanitarian mission following a natural disaster in Sumatra, Herrera and members of his unit reportedly came upon a large octagonal-shaped craft in a jungle clearing. According to Herrera, armed individuals in unmarked uniforms — not identifiable as any known military branch — were present at the site and detained the Marines, confiscating their communications equipment and warning them against discussing what they had seen.

The craft Herrera described was octagonal in shape, distinct from the triangular [[TR-3B]]-type vehicles described by other witnesses but consistent with the broader category of alleged [[Alien Reproduction Vehicle (ARV)|alien reproduction vehicles]]. [[Steven Greer]] has linked the craft Herrera described to [[Lockheed Martin|Lockheed Martin Skunk Works]] programs.

## Parallels with Other Witnesses

Herrera's account shares striking similarities with other UAP encounter testimonies documented by UAP Gerb:

- **Unmarked armed operators**: Like Castle and Wagant, Herrera encountered personnel in all-black or unmarked gear who were not identifiable as conventional military — consistent with UAP Gerb's theory that these were [[Wackenhut Corporation|Wackenhut]]-trained [[DOE Special Response Teams|DOE Special Response Teams (SRTs)]]
- **Detention and intimidation**: All three witnesses were detained, debriefed, and warned against disclosure
- **Anthrax booster**: Following his encounter, Herrera received an anthrax booster shot — the same post-encounter medical protocol administered to Castle and Wagant, which UAP Gerb theorizes may be connected to proximity to nuclear-powered craft or materials associated with the [[Atomic Energy Act of 1954]]
- **Active-duty Marines**: All three witnesses were active-duty Marines at the time of their encounters

## Disclosure Efforts

Herrera has become a public whistleblower, sharing his account through various media appearances and UAP disclosure channels. His testimony has been cited alongside Castle's and Wagant's as evidence of a pattern of clandestine craft operations conducted in proximity to conventional military exercises or deployments.
