Major John Heise was a United States Air Force officer who, with [[Colonel Robert Simmons]], went to the area of the [[1978 Bolivia UFO Crash]] in May 1978, in connection with [[Project Moon Dust]]. In [[Videos/MOON DUST - The Pentagon's Secret UFO Programs/summary|UAP Gerb's video on Moon Dust]] his name is rendered "Major Jesse Haaste"; the State Department documents on the case, as researchers have reported them, name him as Maj. John Heise.

## The 1978 Bolivia crash

On 6 May 1978, thousands of people near El Taire, Bolivia, reportedly saw a cylindrical object crash into a mountainside. Bolivian soldiers and police reached a "dull metallic cylinder 12 feet long with a few dents" and said they were waiting for technical commissions. According to the video, Simmons and Heise "arrived subsequently", while State Department cables, including one from Secretary of State [[Cyrus Vance]], said the appropriate government agencies had been contacted. Gerb identifies that agency as Moon Dust, which received the Bolivian military's film of the object and was tasked with monitoring the situation.

According to the released State Department documents as reported by researchers, Simmons and Heise were assigned to the military attaché's office at the US Embassy in La Paz, and flew to Tarija with a Bolivian Air Force officer in connection with Project Moon Dust. The documentary trail ends there: Gerb says he could not find out whether anything was retrieved.
