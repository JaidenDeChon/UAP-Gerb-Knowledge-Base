Hans Kammler (born 1901) was an SS general and civil engineer. He directed construction for the SS in Nazi Germany, including concentration camps such as Auschwitz, and late in World War II took charge of the V-weapons and other secret weapons programs. Many of these programs were housed in underground tunnels and factories built with slave labour. He was officially declared dead in 1945, but his fate has long been disputed. In UFO research he is alleged to have been taken into US custody and to have brought secret technology, possibly including a recovered craft, to the United States.

## Disputed Fate

Kammler disappeared near the end of the war. The 2019 book *The Hidden Nazi* by Dean Reuter, Colm Lowery and Keith Chester argues from US documents that he was in American custody for months after his reported suicide. Albert Speer, the Reich armaments minister, said that in April 1945 Kammler told him he had business to offer the Americans. According to Gerb, OSS agent Donald W. Richardson said some 70 years later that he still could not fully discuss interrogating Kammler, but that the Nazis had brought "treasure" to the US that "served the development of armament platforms for the Cold War and for the future".

Kammler is said to have hidden documents from his think tank in an underground site in the woods of Czechoslovakia. Gerb relates that 44 years after the war, a team from the Lawrence Livermore and Los Alamos laboratories went to a second, unemptied underground cavern to retrieve material that was "the key to what was hidden at the previous site".

## Alleged UFO Connections

In *Deep Underground Military Bases (D.U.M.Bs.) - UFO Legacy Programs*, Gerb connects Kammler to the [[1933 Magenta UFO CrashRetrieval|1933 Magenta, Italy crash]]. The Nazis purportedly took the lenticular disc from the Italians in 1943. Given his command of Nazi secret weapons programs, Kammler would have been one of the chief figures to study it before Americans recovered it after the war. Gerb argues that Kammler probably ran early versions of UFO legacy programs in his underground facilities. He calls Kammler, along with [[Xaver Dorsch]], a possible key architect of modern US UFO and [[Deep Underground Military Bases (DUMBs)|DUMB]] legacy programs. Kammler, he says, was brought over secretly and outside [[Operation Paperclip]]'s formal channels. Gerb also recommends the documentary *The Two Lives of Hans Kammler*.
