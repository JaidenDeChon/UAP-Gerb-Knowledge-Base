**John Murphy** was a news director at WHJB radio in Greensburg, Pennsylvania, who became one of the main investigators and documenters of the [[1965 Kecksburg, Pennsylvania Crash Retrieval|1965 Kecksburg UFO crash]]. His attempt to make an honest documentary about the incident led to heavy censorship, the intimidation of witnesses and, shortly after the event, his own tragic death.

## Investigation of the Kecksburg Crash

On the evening of December 9, 1965, a distressed mother phoned Murphy at WHJB. She said she had seen a fiery object fall into the woods near [[Kecksburg, Pennsylvania]], with blue smoke rising from where it hit. Murphy was a veteran news director, used to telling credible reports from pranks, and he judged her sincere. He drove straight to the scene to investigate.

At the woods, Murphy spent a good deal of time investigating before state police and military personnel arrived. State Police Fire Marshal [[Carl Mets]] went into the woods with another investigator. When he came back, he told Murphy cryptically: "You better get your information from the Army." At the state police barracks in Greensburg, Murphy saw Army and Air Force personnel swarming the building. The sight was unprecedented, and it convinced him that something of major military significance had been found.

## "Object in the Woods" Documentary

Murphy went on to interview many witnesses from the Kecksburg area about what they had seen and experienced. From these interviews and his own investigation he began producing a radio documentary called "Object in the Woods", but it never aired in its original form.

According to Murphy's wife and other WHJB employees, unidentified men approached Murphy at the radio station, and their visit led to radical changes to the program. The version that finally aired included:

- A defensive disclaimer stating: "This station has not been contacted by any official agency of the state federal or local government. We have received very good cooperation with the state police and with the military and we were able to receive all the information we wanted. This past week we have not had any political or otherwise influence put on us concerning this program whatsoever."
- Testimony from witnesses who had withdrawn their accounts, with Murphy saying on air: "Yesterday afternoon a woman from Gettysburg area recorded for us what she saw... Late this afternoon she notified this radio station that she was afraid of getting into trouble and she did not want her description of the object used on the air."
- References to other witnesses who refused to go public because they feared reprisals from the U.S. Army or State Police

Researcher [[Stan Gordon]] later received notarized documents from WHJB station employees claiming that U.S. government elements confiscated news tapes of eyewitness accounts and prevented them from being broadcast.

## Witness to the Object

In 2003, Murphy's wife revealed that he had told her privately that he went into the woods on December 9, 1965, and saw a strange metallic object on the ground. Murphy never made this claim in public before his death, likely because of the intense pressure and intimidation surrounding the case.

## Tragic Death

John Murphy died in a hit-and-run incident shortly after the Kecksburg event. Researchers have speculated about the circumstances of his death, though no evidence has emerged that definitively links it to his investigation of the crash.

## Legacy

Murphy's censored documentary, together with his widow's revelation that he saw the object himself, makes him a central figure in the Kecksburg case. What happened to him is an example of the [[Media Censorship of UAP Events]] and [[Witness Suppression]] that marked the official response to the incident.
