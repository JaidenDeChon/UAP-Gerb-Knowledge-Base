Parviz Jafari was an Imperial Iranian Air Force fighter pilot who, as a major, flew the second F-4 Phantom II scrambled against a brilliant unidentified object over Tehran on the night of 19 September 1976, the [[1976 Tehran UFO Incident]]. His account of an orb separating from the main object and closing on his jet, while his weapons control and communications failed, made him one of the best-known military UFO witnesses of the Cold War, and the case is a standard example of electromagnetic interference near UAP.

## The Tehran encounter

Vectored toward the large object, Jafari reported that a smaller, brightly lit orb detached from it and came toward his aircraft. When he tried to fire an AIM-9 Sidewinder, his weapons control panel went dead and his radio was jammed; the systems came back once the orb withdrew to the main object.

## In UAP Gerb's coverage

In UAP Gerb's video on physicist [[Kevin Knuth]]'s 2023 [[Sol Foundation 2023 Presentation by Kevin Knuth|Sol Foundation talk]], Jafari's scrambled avionics are the first of Knuth's examples of electronics failing close to a craft, evidence Knuth takes for strong electric and magnetic fields around UAP.
