Barack Hussein Obama II was the 44th President of the United States, serving from January 20, 2009, to January 20, 2017. He was the first African American to hold the office. Before his presidency, Obama served as a U.S. Senator from Illinois and as an Illinois State Senator.

## Childhood in Indonesia

Obama lived in Indonesia as a child from about 1967 to 1971, between the ages of 6 and 10. He spent roughly four years in Jakarta with his mother, Ann Dunham, and his Indonesian stepfather, Lolo Soetoro, attending local Indonesian schools and learning to speak Indonesian. He wrote about this period in his memoir *Dreams from My Father*, and it was a matter of public record long before his presidency.

## Connection to 2009 Indonesia Relief Mission

The October 2009 [[USS Denver]] humanitarian relief operation in Indonesia was the same mission during which [[Michael Herrera]] witnessed an octagonal craft and an alleged human trafficking operation. On that mission, a female USMC major gave the volunteer briefing in the wardroom. She showed the Marines pictures of Obama as a child with his family members and referred explicitly to his time in Indonesia.

Several things make this stand out. Obama was already president at the time of the mission, having been elected in 2008 and inaugurated in January 2009. How his childhood related to the mission was unclear: Herrera and the other Marines were being briefed for a security detail during humanitarian supply deliveries, yet a significant amount of time went to Obama's childhood connection to Indonesia. Briefings of this kind usually cover only operational details, threat assessments and tactical considerations. The major gave no operational context and did not explain why Obama's childhood in Indonesia mattered to a routine security mission.

Why this material was part of the mission briefing has not been explained. Possible interpretations include:
- Obama may have had a personal interest in the Indonesia relief operation because of his childhood connection to the country.
- The Marines were being prepared for possible media coverage of the President's connection to the region (a public relations purpose).
- The material provided cover or misdirection for the actual nature of the operation, as a form of compartmentalization.
- The briefing gave the Marines cultural context about the President's familiarity with Indonesia in order to stress the mission's importance.

The mission ultimately involved Herrera's encounter with what insiders later described as a human trafficking operation for classified UAP programs. That raises the question of whether the Obama material reflected routine presidential interest in a humanitarian mission, or served another purpose in the operation's security or information compartmentalization.
