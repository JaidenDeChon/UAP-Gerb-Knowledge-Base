Michael Schratt is an aerospace historian and UAP researcher who has collaborated extensively with [[Dan Benkert]] on investigations into triangular craft sightings and the credibility of [[Edgar Fouché]]'s [[TR-3B]] testimony. Schratt gained access to the long-lost files of [[Leonard Stringfield]] in Mufon, Ohio, publishing cases from Stringfield's archives that had never appeared in the *Crash Retrieval Status Reports 1–7*.

## Key Contributions

Schratt co-authored work with Benkert that included the extraction of eyewitness quotes from the 1985 Mad, Wisconsin triangular craft sighting — a case significant for its early date and the witness's description of a large, silent triangle. Additionally, Schratt hosts a YouTube channel where he published a four-part series titled "Roswell Revisited" featuring the forensic illustrations and research of [[Bill McDonald]].
