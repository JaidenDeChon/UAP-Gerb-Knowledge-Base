Michael Schratt is an aerospace historian and UAP researcher. He has worked extensively with [[Dan Benkert]], investigating sightings of triangular craft and the credibility of [[Edgar Fouché]]'s [[TR-3B]] testimony. Schratt gained access to the long-lost files of [[Leonard Stringfield]] in Mufon, Ohio, and published cases from Stringfield's archives that had never appeared in the *Crash Retrieval Status Reports 1–7*.

## Key Contributions

Work that Schratt co-authored with Benkert included extracting eyewitness quotes from the 1985 triangular craft sighting in Mad, Wisconsin. The case stands out for its early date and for the witness's description of a large, silent triangle. Schratt also runs a YouTube channel, where he published a four-part series titled "Roswell Revisited" on the forensic illustrations and research of [[Bill McDonald]].

## Investigation of the RB Case

Schratt was granted access to Stringfield's personal archives, held at MUFON's headquarters in Cincinnati, Ohio. There he found letters between Stringfield and UFO researcher [[Michael Johnstone]] about an anonymous Marine whistleblower known as "[[RB]]." Stringfield had published a brief account of RB's claims in *UFO Crash/Retrieval Status Report 5* under the title "Marine Guards a Disc-Shaped Vehicle, 1963," but Schratt's archival research uncovered the full documentary record. In 2015, Schratt interviewed Johnstone directly to gather more details about RB's account for his own research and publications. In the RB case, a former Marine Corporal claims that in December 1963 he guarded a 40-foot disc-shaped craft of non-human origin at an undisclosed military base and watched failed attempts to breach its hull with drills, cutting torches, and high-powered lasers.
