**Ralph Blumenthal** is an American journalist and author who spent most of his career as a reporter for *The New York Times*. With [[Leslie Kean]] and Helene Cooper, he co-wrote the Times article of 16 December 2017 that revealed the Pentagon's [[AATIP (Advanced Aerospace Threat Identification Program)|Advanced Aerospace Threat Identification Program]] and published two of the Navy videos now known as the [[Pentagon UFO Videos]]. The article is widely treated as the start of the modern era of UAP disclosure.

## UAP reporting

Blumenthal and Kean went on to report on UAP together for several years. In the first episode of his iceberg series, Gerb credits the public release of the three videos, labelled FLIR, Go Fast and Gimbal, to the efforts of [[Luis Elizondo]] and [[Chris Mellon]], with publication through *The New York Times* by Kean and Blumenthal.

In June 2023, in *The Debrief*, Blumenthal and Kean published the first report of [[David Grusch]]'s allegations of a decades-long program to retrieve and reverse-engineer craft of non-human origin. That article also quoted [[Karl Nell]] backing Grusch's account.

Blumenthal is also the author of *The Believer: Alien Encounters, Hard Science, and the Passion of John Mack* (2021), a biography of the Harvard psychiatrist who studied people reporting alien abduction.
