Major Dewey J. Fournet Jr. was a United States Air Force officer who spent one year, around 1952–1953, as the Pentagon's UFO desk coordinator, handling UFO affairs for the Pentagon. He personally supported the extraterrestrial hypothesis as the explanation for some unidentified cases. He is known for his testimony before the [[Robertson Panel]] and for his work with civilian UFO researchers.

## Robertson Panel Testimony

Fournet was invited to speak before the [[Robertson Panel]] at its fourth and final formal session in January 1953. He told the panel that the extraterrestrial hypothesis best explained some unexplained UFO cases. That view ran directly against the panel's predetermined directive to debunk every case put before it. According to the video, the panel spent the rest of that fourth day discussing Fournet's briefing instead of case files, which suggests his arguments caused considerable debate among its members. Even so, the panel's final report ignored his position entirely.
