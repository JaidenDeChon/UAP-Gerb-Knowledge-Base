**Ken Farley** was a witness to the [[Aztec UFO Crash and Recovery|1948 Aztec crash]] site who described what he saw to [[Scott and Suzanne Ramsey]] on his deathbed in 1999. He had recently left the military and was visiting an aunt in [[Durango, Colorado]]. On the morning of 25 March 1948 he was on his way to see a friend in [[Cedar Hill, New Mexico]], and the friend suggested they check out a lot of activity on a nearby dirt road. The two followed the dust of other cars to Hart Canyon Road and walked up onto the mesa.

They saw "a large disc-shaped object, aluminum in color, but not really very shiny, sort of dull aluminum," with no visible doors or windows. It was undamaged but lay "at a funny angle," about 12 degrees, resting on its belly. It had a bubble on top and a smaller one underneath, "like two pie pans." Oil-field workers were climbing on it while an older couple, probably the ranchers Mr. and Mrs. Knight, shouted at them to get off. Two police officers, probably [[Manuel Sandoval]] and a local officer, were asking people to leave, and disgruntled military personnel soon arrived and cleared everyone out. UAP Gerb stresses that Farley's description closely matches [[Doug Noland]]'s although the two had no apparent contact.
