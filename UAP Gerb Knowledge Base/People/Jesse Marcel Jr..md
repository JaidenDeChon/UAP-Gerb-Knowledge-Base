Jesse Marcel Jr. was the son of Major Jesse Marcel. He saw for himself the materials his father brought home during the Roswell recovery in July 1947, and his testimony is often cited as second-generation corroboration of the debris's unusual characteristics.

## Witness Account

According to his account, he saw lightweight metallic debris and pieces marked with unusual symbols that did not look like ordinary military hardware of the time. His descriptions overlap with later statements attributed to Walter Haut and Steven Lovekin about marked or "instruction-like" symbols.

## Relevance To Roswell Evidence

Marcel Jr. did not take part in the recovery operations himself, but researchers use his timeline and his descriptions of the material to support the argument that the weather balloon explanation did not match what some participants and witnesses saw.
