**Grant Cameron** is a Canadian UFO researcher and author from Winnipeg, best known for decades of work on what US presidents knew about UFOs, published through his long-running Presidents UFO research and in books on disclosure and consciousness. He has filed many Freedom of Information requests with presidential libraries and government agencies, and has archived the resulting documents.

## Research files and Kecksburg

In 2024 Cameron released his research files publicly. UAP Gerb draws on them in *The 1965 Kecksburg, Pennsylvania UFO Crash*, which leans on two items from the [[Lyndon B. Johnson]] Presidential Library. The first is Johnson's schedule for 9 to 11 December 1965. It shows a leisurely evening at the Texas White House after the [[1965 Kecksburg, Pennsylvania Crash Retrieval|Kecksburg crash]], then a day of meetings from 9:50 a.m. on 10 December with the Joint Chiefs, Defense Secretary [[Robert McNamara]], NASA's [[James E. Webb]], science adviser [[Donald Hornig]] and others. The second is a record of an off-record meeting on 13 December 1965 between [[Joseph Califano]] and Buford Ellington, director of the Office of Emergency Planning. Gerb recommends reading Cameron's Kecksburg files in full, noting they run to many more pages than the video could cover.
