"Fritz Warner" is the pseudonym under which [[Arthur Stansel Jr.]] publicly disclosed his account of the [[1953 Kingman, Arizona Crash Retrieval]]. Stansel took the name from a real German rocket engineer. He used it when he came forward to UFO researcher [[Raymond Fowler]] in 1973, and he signed a sworn affidavit under it. Fowler later confirmed and published Stansel's real identity.

Everything else about him is on the [[Arthur Stansel Jr.]] page.
