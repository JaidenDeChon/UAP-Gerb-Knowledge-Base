Edgar Fouché was a United States Air Force Master Sergeant who publicly disclosed alleged details of the [[TR-3B]], a classified triangular stealth reconnaissance platform he described as the most exotic and classified aerospace vehicle ever built. Fouché first revealed his claims in 1998, drawing on his own service career and testimony from five close friends and sources within the US military-industrial complex. He has since passed away.

## TR-3B Disclosures

Fouché claimed the TR-3B saw prototype flights beginning in the early 1970s with successful implementation of gravity-warping systems. By 1994, three operational prototype systems were reportedly flying — triangular craft approximately 250 feet across — along with an enormous 600-foot operational model. According to Fouché, the craft's key technology was the [[Magnetic Field Disruptor (MFD)]], a circular plasma field accelerator ring surrounding a rotatable crew compartment that used mercury-based plasma pressurized at 250,000 atmospheres at 150 degrees Kelvin, accelerated to 60,000 RPM to generate a superconductive plasma capable of reducing the gravitational effect on mass within proximity by 89%.

Fouché stated the engineering of TR-3B was performed by [[Lockheed Martin]], [[Boeing]], [[Northrop Grumman]], Teledyne Ryan, and others, while the program was managed by three of the US "Big Six" intelligence agencies: the [[NRO (National Reconnaissance Office)|NRO]], [[NSA]], and [[CIA]]. The gravity-warping propulsion systems were allegedly reverse engineered from nonhuman technology at [[Sandia National Laboratories]] and [[Lawrence Livermore National Laboratory]], both [[FFRDC (Federally Funded Research and Development Centers)|Department of Energy FFRDCs]].

## Pilot Claims

Fouché stated that TR-3B was operated by both military and contractor personnel, with military pilots being elite aviators from the Air Force Test Pilot School at [[Edwards Air Force Base]] (AFTTC) and the Navy's Top Gun school at [[NAWDC]] in Fallon, Nevada. This claim connects TR-3B operations to installations that UAP Gerb has independently linked to alien reproduction vehicle testing and UFO legacy program operations.

## Significance

Although Fouché has been criticized for lack of physical evidence, his detailed and specific disclosures about the TR-3B's propulsion, engineering contractors, managing agencies, pilot sourcing, and operational characteristics have found corroboration in other witness testimonies. The craft described by [[Rodrik Castle]] during the 1997 [[Hunter Warrior Advanced Warfighting Experiment]] — a 200–300 foot silent black triangle with embedded spherical lights at each vertex, rounded corners, possible venting or ridges, and a concave center structure — closely parallels Fouché's TR-3B descriptions, including the size range, the non-flush lights (which Fouché described as hydrogen-oxygen vectored rocket engines), and the circular MFD feature on the underside.
