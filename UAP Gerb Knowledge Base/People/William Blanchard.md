Colonel William H. Blanchard commanded Roswell Army Air Field and the [[509th Bomb Group]] during the July 1947 [[Roswell Crash]]. According to his public relations officer, [[Walter Haut]], Blanchard dictated the 8 July 1947 press release announcing that the base had recovered a "flying disc." Within hours that story was replaced by General [[General Ramey|Roger Ramey]]'s weather balloon explanation.

## Role in Roswell

Haut's 1993 affidavit says Blanchard dictated the release almost verbatim from a scratch pad and ordered it delivered to Roswell's newspapers and radio stations immediately. Haut's 2002 affidavit, released in 2007, adds that Blanchard attended a morning staff meeting on 8 July with Ramey and Colonel [[Thomas J. DuBose]], at which wreckage samples were handled and Ramey proposed steering attention to the debris field. After dictating the release, Blanchard allegedly took Haut into [[Hangar 84]] to see an egg-shaped craft and bodies. Haut said he swore to Blanchard never to reveal what he saw in his lifetime.

After the retraction Blanchard went on leave. Months later he told his friend [[Arthur McQuitty]], managing editor of the Roswell Morning Dispatch, that he would say nothing more than this: the material he saw was unlike anything he had seen anywhere else in his life.
