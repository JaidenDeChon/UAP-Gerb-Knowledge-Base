**Detlev W. Bronk** (1897–1975) was an American physiologist and biophysicist, widely regarded as a founder of biophysics as a discipline. He was president of the National Academy of Sciences (1950–1962), of Johns Hopkins University, and of the Rockefeller Institute (later Rockefeller University). In UFO research he is named in the [[MJ-12 Documents|Majestic documents]] as an original member of [[Majestic 12]] and some accounts put him in charge of examining recovered biologics.

## Alleged Role in Crash Retrievals

According to [[William Steinman]]'s 1986 account of the [[Aztec UFO Crash and Recovery|1948 Aztec crash]], Bronk was one of the scientists [[Vannevar Bush]] brought to the site. As a physiologist he ordered the bodies placed on dry ice, and cryogenics specialist Paul A. Scherer arranged freezing equipment through the AiResearch company. Bronk is then said to have led full autopsies, whose results were reportedly published in an elusive Project Sign/Blue Book "Report 13." These described beings of about 40 pounds with large heads, slanted eyes, webbed fingers, no digestive or reproductive systems, and a colourless, ozone-smelling fluid in place of blood.

The disputed 22 July 1947 [[Interplanetary Phenomenon Unit]] report among the Majestic documents also places Bronk in charge of biologic autopsies after the [[Roswell Crash]]. Gerb points out that this matches Steinman's account, which was published about nine years before that document surfaced.
