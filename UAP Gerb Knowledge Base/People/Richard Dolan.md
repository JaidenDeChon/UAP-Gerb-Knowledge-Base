Richard Dolan is an American historian, author and UFO researcher, best known for his book series *UFOs and the National Security State*, a chronological history of how the US military and intelligence agencies have dealt with UFOs since the Second World War. Gerb's videos draw on him as a source. [[James Rigney]] explained where the [[Wilson-Davis Memo]] came from on Dolan's podcast, Dolan read out a leak in 2021 about exotic propulsion projects at [[Tonopah Test Range (TTR)|Tonopah]], and he says he identified the people behind the Zodiac crash retrieval story. Gerb calls him "the legend Richard Dolan" and "respected UFO historian Richard Dolan".

## Career

Dolan was born in Brooklyn, New York, in 1962 and grew up on Long Island. He took a BA in English and history at Alfred University in 1984, studied at Oxford, and earned an MA in history from the University of Rochester in 1995. He became interested in UFOs in 1994. The first volume of *UFOs and the National Security State* came out in 2000 and the second in 2009. He has since hosted his own show and podcast and founded Richard Dolan Press.

## The Wilson-Davis Memo

[[James Rigney]] copied the [[Wilson-Davis Memo]] from the estate of Apollo 14 astronaut [[Edgar Mitchell]] after Mitchell's death in 2016. The notes record Vice Admiral [[Thomas Wilson]]'s alleged discovery in 1997 of a crash retrieval program, and his denial of access to it. Rigney's name became public when he appeared on Dolan's podcast to discuss the memo's origin and contents. Gerb also names Dolan, with [[Chris Mellon]], [[Ross Coulthart]] and [[Luis Elizondo|Lou Elizondo]], among those who have confirmed the document's authenticity. It was entered into the US Congressional Record in 2022.

## The Advanced Group Six Leak

In 2021 Dolan read out information leaked to him by a source familiar with the classified [[UAP Task Force]] report presented to Congress. It listed energy pulse, ion, anti-gravity, antimatter, ramjet hydrogen and compressed nuclear propulsion, and advanced uses of exotic elements from recovered non-human technology, in projects controlled by [[DARPA]], managed by a group called "Advanced Group Six" and paid for from black intelligence funds and partly by contractors. Prototypes were said to fly only at [[Area 51]] and Tonopah. Gerb says Dolan is the only researcher he has seen link Tonopah to legacy programs. Because [[Lockheed Martin]] ran [[Sandia National Laboratories]], and through it Tonopah, until 2017, Gerb reads the leak as implying Lockheed's involvement. In his Sandia video he wonders whether the Nevada Test Site's Area 6 is tied to the same programs, and suggests, without offering support, that "Advanced Group Six" takes its name from Area 6.

## The Zodiac Story

In Gerb's TRW videos, Dolan is the researcher who has done most on [[Operation Zodiac|Zodiac]], a crash retrieval program described in a three-part story printed in *UFO Magazine* in 1998 under the pseudonym "Greg Halifax", about the experiences of a pseudonymous "Sage Masters". Researchers such as Dolan describe [[TRW]] as its main contractor. Dolan has said he identified the author as TRW attorney Jeffrey W. Griffith and the participant as Mary Elizabeth Elliott. Gerb has said he would like to work with Dolan someday to revisit Zodiac.

## The Black Budget

In clips Gerb plays from Dark Journalist, [[Catherine Austin Fitts]] calls *UFOs and the National Security State* "the best book in terms of describing the mechanisms put in place after World War II to create... a far expanded infrastructure for what I call the black budget."

## Dugway

Gerb's video on [[Dugway Proving Ground]] starts from a Quest TV documentary in which Nick Pope and Dolan discuss Dugway as the new Area 51. Gerb notes that the documentary presents no testimony or evidence, which is part of why he made the video.
