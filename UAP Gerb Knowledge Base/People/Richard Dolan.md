Richard Dolan is a historian, author and prominent UFO researcher, best known for documenting the institutions behind UFO secrecy and black budget programs. His book series *UFOs and the National Security State* is widely regarded as the best account of how the black budget system has worked since World War II, and of where UFOs and the military and intelligence agencies meet.

## Role in Wilson-Davis Memo Disclosure

Dolan played a significant part in drawing attention to the [[Wilson-Davis Memo]] by hosting [[James Rigny]] on his podcast. On the show, Rigny discussed where the 15-page memo came from and what it says. The memo documents Vice Admiral [[Thomas Wilson]]'s alleged discovery of a crash retrieval program in 1997, and his denial of access to it. Rigny had obtained the memo in 2016 while helping disperse the estate of Apollo 14 astronaut [[Edgar Mitchell]].

Dolan's platform helped spread awareness of the memo. It later became one of the most significant documents in the modern debate over UAP disclosure, and in 2022 it was entered into the official U.S. Congressional Record.

## Expertise in Black Budget Infrastructure

Dolan's work looks at how black budget programs operate outside normal oversight, which institutions keep UFO secrecy in place, and what the historical record shows about military and intelligence encounters with UFOs. His research helps explain how a program like the one Wilson allegedly discovered could exist and stay hidden even from senior officials with the right clearances.
