Bob Echler is a former NASA mission specialist who, in 1989, conducted a personal inquiry into the possibility of making recovered UFO vehicles available for civilian scientific and technological research. Echler's inquiry, facilitated by British Admiral Lord Hill Norton who provided an introduction to senior US intelligence figures, led to a significant telephone exchange with retired Admiral [[Bobby Ray Inman]] and a subsequent face-to-face meeting at CIA Langley with CIA DS&T Deputy Director [[Everett Heinman]].

## Role in UAP Programs

In 1989, Echler called [[Bobby Ray Inman]] to ask whether recovered UFO vehicles could be made available for scientific research. Inman told Echler that CIA DS&T Deputy Director [[Everett Heinman]] was "the best person to ask" about such vehicles — a specific referral UAP researchers treat as evidence that Inman had knowledge of the program structure surrounding recovered craft. Echler subsequently met Heinman at CIA Langley, where Heinman denied any knowledge of UFOs or related programs. The [[Inman-Echler Telephone Call]] and the subsequent Heinman meeting are treated as among the clearest documented instances of a senior intelligence figure pointing toward the CIA DS&T as a management node for alleged UAP materials.
