Ellen M. Lord is a former US defense official and industry executive who served as the first Under Secretary of Defense for Acquisition and Sustainment from 2018 to 2021, after the Under Secretary for Acquisition, Technology and Logistics was split in two. Before government she was chief executive of Textron Systems.

## Alleged Role in UAP Programs

UAP Gerb credits Lord with establishing the Department of Defense's corporate portfolio program in 2020. He describes it as giving defense-industrial-base corporations access to DoD executives, scientists, FFRDCs and management with broad access to special access program portfolios, and he calls it a major milestone in the evolution of the UFO legacy programs because it tightened the bond between program administrators, [[Federally Funded Research and Development Center (FFRDC)|FFRDCs]] and contractors. Because she held the office descended from the acquisition under secretary's office named in the [[Wilson-Davis Memo]] (today the [[Under Secretary of Defense for Acquisition and Sustainment (USD A&S)]]), which shares milestone authority over NRO acquisitions, the host lists her among thirteen alleged gatekeepers with NRO-adjacent ties.

## SAIC Strategic Advisory Board

After leaving the Pentagon in 2021, Lord joined the Strategic Advisory Board of [[SAIC]]. In *[[Off-World Technologies Division – UAP Technology Reverse Engineering]]*, UAP Gerb links this move to the Corporate Portfolio Program she created. SAIC is eligible for the program, and the host argues that it gives the company access to DoD special access program portfolios. He places that access alongside SAIC's large contracts at [[Naval Surface Warfare Center Crane]], the alleged home of the [[Off-World Technologies Division]], among them a 2020 $133 million award that reaches Crane's special capabilities branch.
