Nicolas Sarkozy served as President of France from 2007 to 2012. He appears in UAP research as the intended recipient of UAP briefing documents coordinated by [[Steven Greer]] and delivered around early March 2009 by [[Alain Boudier]] — designated "Source One" in Greer's email communications. Boudier, at that time president of the French national aeronautical and astronautical Association UAP division ([[3af-p (French national aeronautical and astronautical Association UAP division)|3af-p]]), reportedly brought the briefing documents to Sarkozy as part of Greer's effort to inform allied heads of government about UAP-related matters. There is no public record of Sarkozy's response to or acknowledgment of these materials.
