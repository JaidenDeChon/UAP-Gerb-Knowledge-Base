Nicolas Sarkozy was President of France from 2007 to 2012. In UAP research he appears as the intended recipient of UAP briefing documents that [[Steven Greer]] coordinated and that [[Alain Boudier]] delivered around early March 2009. Greer's emails refer to Boudier as "Source One". Boudier, at that time president of the French national aeronautical and astronautical Association UAP division ([[3af-p (French national aeronautical and astronautical Association UAP division)|3af-p]]), reportedly brought the briefing documents to Sarkozy as part of Greer's effort to inform allied heads of government about UAP-related matters. There is no public record that Sarkozy responded to or acknowledged these materials.
