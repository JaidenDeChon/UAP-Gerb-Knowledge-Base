Lee Graham is a UFO researcher who worked as a satellite sensor technician at [[Aerojet]] in California, on the [[Defense Support Program (DSP)|Defense Support Program]] satellites that scan the Earth for missile launches. With his Aerojet colleague [[Ron Regehr]] he researched [[Fast Walkers]], unidentified objects that DSP satellites detect entering or leaving Earth's atmosphere, and the two say a DSP printout recorded the [[1976 Tehran UFO Incident]]. Gerb's videos also cite a 1981 letter Senator [[Barry Goldwater]] wrote to him about the "Blue Room" at Wright-Patterson, and the Aquarius document he brought forward.

## Fast Walker Research

Graham and Regehr say that a US military DSP satellite tracked the 1976 Tehran UFO incident. They located a computer printout from the time of the Iranian overflight which, they say, shows the satellite detecting an "anomalous object" in Iranian airspace. The printout reportedly carries the notation "238 scans possible SR". At one scan every ten seconds, Gerb works this out as about 39.7 minutes of tracking, and he suggests that "SR" may stand for strategic reconnaissance.

Gerb spent a long time trying to confirm the claim. He found little beyond a declassified paper Regehr often cites, "Now You See It, Now You Don't" by USAF Captain Henry S. Shields, before tracing the printout through dead web pages to an out-of-focus image posted in 1997. Researcher [[Brad Sparks]] had questioned whether the satellite caught the jets scrambled to pursue the object rather than the object itself. Beyond the scan count and the term "UCF", Gerb finds the image illegible, calls the case a dead end for now, and asks viewers to help him read it.

## Barry Goldwater Letter

On 19 October 1981 Senator Barry Goldwater wrote to Graham about the alleged "Blue Room" at [[Wright-Patterson Air Force Base]], said to hold UFO information. Goldwater, who had been refused entry to the room years earlier, told him: "I don't know of anyone who has access to the blue room nor am I aware of its content and I am not aware of anything having been relocated. This thing has gotten so highly classified, even though I will admit there is a lot of it that has been released, it is just impossible to get anything on it."

## The Aquarius Document

In his interview with [[Ryan S. Wood]] on the [[Majestic 12]] documents, Gerb notes that the Aquarius document package, allegedly signed during the Carter administration, "was brought forth by UFO researcher Lee Graham." Wood calls it "informed fluff": with no specifics that can be checked, he keeps it in a "neutral basket" until more evidence appears.

## Gerb's Contact Attempt

Gerb has tried to reach Graham on Facebook to learn more about his DSP research. He says there are "a lot of interesting things surrounding Graham and some claims he made", and that he has heard some people liken him to a 1980s UFO whistleblower.
