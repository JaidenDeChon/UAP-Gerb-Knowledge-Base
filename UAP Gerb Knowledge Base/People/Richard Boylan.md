Richard Boylan (often referred to as Dr. Richard Boylan) is a UFO researcher who has reported claims from military officials regarding [[Electrogravitics|electrogravitic]] systems on US military aircraft. Specifically, Boylan reported that three-star generals disclosed the [[Northrop Grumman]] B-2 Spirit stealth bomber carries onboard electrogravitic systems that achieve internal mass reduction on the wings and fuselage — a capability allegedly contributing to the aircraft's over $1 billion per-unit cost.
