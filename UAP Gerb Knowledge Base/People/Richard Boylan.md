Richard Boylan (often referred to as Dr. Richard Boylan) is a UFO researcher who has reported claims from military officials about [[Electrogravitics|electrogravitic]] systems on US military aircraft. Boylan reported that three-star generals disclosed that the [[Northrop Grumman]] B-2 Spirit stealth bomber carries electrogravitic systems on board that reduce the internal mass of the wings and fuselage. This capability allegedly contributes to the aircraft's cost of over $1 billion per unit.
