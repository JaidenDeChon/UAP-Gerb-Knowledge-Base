John F. Kennedy was the 35th President of the United States, from 1961 to 1963. In UAP-related histories, his presidency falls within the period when military and intelligence compartmentalization deepened after the Roswell era.

## Relevance In This Knowledge Base

In the whistleblower accounts Gerb covers, Steven Lovekin's service is described as spanning both the Eisenhower and Kennedy administrations. That places Kennedy-era White House communications within the same wider timeline of restricted handling of UFO information.
