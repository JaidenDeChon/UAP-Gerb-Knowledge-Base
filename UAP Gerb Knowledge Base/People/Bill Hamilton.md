Bill Hamilton (died before 2024) was an American UFO researcher, widely credited as the first person to publicly define the Alien Reproduction Vehicle (ARV) concept and to produce an image of it. He introduced important witnesses, including [[Mark McCandlish]] and [[Bill Uhouse]], to researcher [[Steven Greer]]. He also compiled some of the earliest systematic records of alleged underground UAP facilities in the American Southwest. His 1992 book *Cosmic Top Secret* is a foundational reference for research into ARVs and deep underground military bases (DUMBs).

## ARV Research and *Cosmic Top Secret*

Hamilton coined the term "Alien Reproduction Vehicle" and produced the first public conceptual image of the craft, based on what his own informants told him. The image came before [[Brad Sorenson]]'s testimony at Norton AFB and before [[Mark McCandlish]]'s later detailed diagram. Later researchers built on the terms and the picture of the craft that Hamilton's early ARV work set out. Gerb has called his 1992 book *Cosmic Top Secret* "the most underappreciated book in all of UFOlogy". It contains what is likely the first published use of the term "ARV", although Hamilton at first spelled the acronym out as "alien replicated vehicle". In the early 1990s Hamilton discussed Sorenson's fluxliner testimony with [[William B. Scott]] of [[Aviation Week and Space Technology]]. He then passed both Sorenson and forensic illustrator [[Bill McDonald]] on to [[Steven Greer]], who featured them at the 2001 Disclosure Project event.

## Underground Base Investigation and the Antelope Valley Network

From the late 1980s, Hamilton built up a large network of informants who gave testimony about alleged underground UFO facilities in California's [[Antelope Valley]]. His research concentrated on the [[Tehachapi, California|Tehachapi Ranch (Northrop) facility]], sometimes called the "Anthill" because of its purported network of underground tunnels. Among the significant testimonies Hamilton collected:

- A local property owner near Tehachapi reported seeing the ground open like a missile silo and a flying saucer take off from it. OSI personnel from Edwards AFB later silenced the witness.
- A contractor named Chuck described digging underground tunnels beneath the Anthill. He claimed the tunnels had round doorways with few doors, and red and green ID/entry lights.
- A source who claimed to have worked on "Project Star Talk" at Tehachapi described a large underground building that used lasers to "bring in UFOs" by means of scalar technology.
- An informant known as "Gober" claimed to work with alien technology that "deeply frightened him" at Tehachapi, Helendale, and the Nevada Test and Training Range. He reported that staff on site rotated every 14–16 days, and that there was a command center under Haystack Butte.
- Several informants described ground vehicles and maglev train systems inside DUMBs, linking installations such as Tehachapi, McDonald Douglas, Helendale, George AFB, Edwards AFB, the NASA rocket test site, and China Lake.

From these sources, Hamilton concluded that a huge complex of underground facilities in the Antelope Valley, all linked by tunnels and transport systems, was engaged in UFO testing, storage, and material exploitation.

## The DUMB Network Map

Hamilton's most significant contribution to underground base research is a map of alleged connected underground UFO facilities and transport links across the American West, compiled from informants including "TA" (1990) and Thomas Castello. Gerb has compared the map with the locations of DOD laboratories and with RAND Corporation's 1972 Very High Speed Transit (VHST) feasibility study, and found that they match geographically to a significant degree, especially along the southwestern routes through Arizona and New Mexico. The map marks sites at Edwards AFB, Tehachapi, Helendale, McDonald Douglas, George AFB, China Lake, Area 51, Tonopah, Los Alamos, Sandia/Kirtland, Cheyenne Mountain, and elsewhere. Many are labeled R&D sites rather than full DUMBs.

[[Richard Sauder]] spoke with Hamilton at length about this map and, at the time of Gerb's DUMBs video, planned to discuss it further in a future collaborative project.
