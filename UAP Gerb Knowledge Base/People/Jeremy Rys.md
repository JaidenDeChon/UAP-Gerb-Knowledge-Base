Jeremy Rys, known online as Alien Scientist, is a UFO researcher and YouTuber who became one of the most prominent critics of [[Edgar Fouché]]'s [[TR-3B]] testimony. Rys first met Fouché in 2009 and initially considered him a close friend and legitimate whistleblower, before publishing damaging criticism alleging Fouché fabricated his disclosures.

## Criticism of Fouché

On his website alienscientist.com, Rys posted an analysis concluding that Fouché's TR-3B testimony had "no technical value" and was "likely fabricated by Ed in an effort to combine stories about the Nazi Bell device with the [[Belgian UFO Wave]] of triangular UFO sightings so that his story would be more believable." Rys additionally accused Fouché of fabricating military documents and using a stolen F-117A photograph. He described Fouché as "one of the most deceitful and manipulative people that I have ever met," attributing this behavior to PTSD-driven alcoholism.

Rys's email to the photographer of the alleged Area 51 F-117 photo contained leading statements against Fouché, potentially undermining the objectivity of the photographer's response. Both Rys's and [[David Hilton]]'s critical videos were subsequently removed or unlisted from YouTube.
