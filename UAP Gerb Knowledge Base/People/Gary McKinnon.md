Gary McKinnon (born 10 February 1966 in Glasgow) is a Scottish systems administrator. Between February 2001 and March 2002 he carried out what a US prosecutor called the biggest military computer hack of all time, breaking into 97 US military and [[NASA]] computers to look for evidence of a UFO cover-up and suppressed free-energy technology. He was indicted in the United States, and the extradition proceedings against him lasted a decade until the UK government blocked them in 2012. Gerb covers his claims twice: as an entry in his obscure-oddities iceberg, and as a parallel to the secret space program described by [[Mark McCandlish]] and [[Edgar Fouche|Edgar Fouché]].

## The Hack and Alleged Discoveries

Gerb covers two of McKinnon's claimed finds from among mostly mundane files.

McKinnon acted on a claim by [[Donna Hare]], a former NASA photographic specialist, that a laboratory in Building 8 of [[Johnson Space Center]] airbrushed UFOs out of high-resolution satellite imagery. He says he reached Building 8's machines and found folders named "raw" and "processed". In the "raw" folder he began downloading an image of a blue-and-white planet with a cigar-shaped, metallic white craft superimposed on it, in near-Earth orbit. It had domes around its middle and at its ends, and no rivets or seams. Then his cursor began to move on its own, and he was caught.

He also says he found lists of officers who serve off the planet. In the McCandlish video Gerb describes a document on a NASA server titled "Non-Terrestrial Officers", listing the names and ranks of US Air Force personnel, with tabs for "Material Transfer Between Ships". In the iceberg video he describes Pentagon files on [[Solar Warden]]: a ledger of 300 military personnel in what McKinnon calls a US military battalion in space, run by the US Navy's Network and Space Operations Command. Those files spoke of "extraterrestrial officers" and "contact between fleets", which McKinnon explained meant officers who serve off-planet, not aliens. They named two ships, the USSS *LeMay* and USSS *Hillenkoetter*; Gerb suggests the third "S" may stand for "space". McKinnon took these findings as evidence of a classified US space fleet.

Gerb has "no doubt" that McKinnon believes he is telling the truth, and thinks there "could be some credibility" to his claims, especially the airbrushed imagery. He notes that [[Chris Mellon]] has said 4K satellite footage of UAP exists but is classified or has been tampered with.

## Secret Space Program Parallels

In his video on Mark McCandlish's testimony, Gerb sets McKinnon beside Fouché before playing McCandlish's own account of vehicles he believes the secret space program uses. Fouché claimed that the triangular [[TR-3B]] provided logistics and transport for a secret space fleet. Gerb says McKinnon's list of off-planet USAF officers and its tabs for transfers between ships "already draws parallels" to Fouché's logistics and personnel carriers, so "maybe this idea of a black budget hidden space program isn't so fantastical".

## Legal Battle

McKinnon was first arrested in the UK in 2002, and in November 2002 a federal grand jury in Virginia indicted him on seven counts. He fought extradition to the United States for a decade, during which he was diagnosed with Asperger syndrome. In October 2012 UK Home Secretary Theresa May blocked his extradition on human rights grounds, and in December 2012 the Crown Prosecution Service announced he would not be prosecuted in the United Kingdom either.
