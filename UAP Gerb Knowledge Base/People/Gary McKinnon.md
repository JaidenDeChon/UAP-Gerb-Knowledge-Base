Gary McKinnon (born 1966) is a Scottish systems administrator. Between 2001 and 2002 he carried out what US prosecutors called the largest military computer hack in history, breaking into 97 US Army and NASA computer systems to look for evidence of UFO cover-ups and suppressed free energy technology. He was indicted in the United States, and the extradition proceedings against him lasted nearly a decade until the UK government blocked them in 2012.

## The Hack and Alleged Discoveries

Among mostly mundane files, McKinnon claimed to have found several extraordinary items:

- At the NASA Johnson Space Center, an image file that allegedly showed a large cigar-shaped object in near-Earth orbit, with no visible seams, rivets or propulsion systems.
- A document on a NASA server titled "Non-Terrestrial Officers," which McKinnon described as containing the names and ranks of US Air Force personnel assigned to vessels not in the US Navy's known ship registry. The document also contained tabs for "Material Transfer Between Ships."

McKinnon took these findings as evidence of a classified US Space Fleet operating off-planet. [[Mark McCandlish]] cited McKinnon's alleged discoveries as corroboration for [[Edgar Fouché]]'s claims that the [[TR-3B]] was a logistics and personnel transport craft for a secret space program. McCandlish noted that both pointed to off-planet USAF operations and to transfers of material between vessels.

## Legal Battle

McKinnon, who is autistic, faced extradition to the United States for nearly a decade. In 2012, UK Home Secretary Theresa May blocked his extradition on human rights grounds. McKinnon was never extradited, and in the end he was not prosecuted in the United Kingdom either.
