Neil H. McElroy served as the sixth United States Secretary of Defense under President Dwight D. Eisenhower, from 1957 to 1959. During his tenure, McElroy is recorded as having personally asked the founders of the newly created [[MITRE Corporation]] to build "flexibility to take on the Department of Defense's requirements as we develop them" — a mandate UAP Gerb cites as evidence that MITRE, though chartered around the Air Force's SAGE air defense program, was from its 1958 founding envisioned as a broader, open-ended national security research and engineering resource for the Department of Defense.

## Role in UAP Research

UAP Gerb argues McElroy's request for institutional "flexibility" is consistent with the channel's broader allegation that MITRE's founding purpose extended beyond its publicly acknowledged SAGE mission to include reverse-engineering work on technology recovered from the 1947 [[Roswell Crash]].
