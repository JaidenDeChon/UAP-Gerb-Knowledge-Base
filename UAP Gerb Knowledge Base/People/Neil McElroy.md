Neil H. McElroy was the sixth United States Secretary of Defense, serving under President Dwight D. Eisenhower from 1957 to 1959. While in office, McElroy is recorded as having personally asked the founders of the newly created [[MITRE Corporation]] to build "flexibility to take on the Department of Defense's requirements as we develop them". Gerb cites this mandate as evidence that MITRE, though chartered around the Air Force's SAGE air defense program, was envisioned from its founding in 1958 as a broader, open-ended national security research and engineering resource for the Department of Defense.

## Role in UAP Research

Gerb argues that McElroy's request for institutional "flexibility" is consistent with the channel's broader allegation that MITRE's founding purpose went beyond its publicly acknowledged SAGE mission and included reverse-engineering work on technology recovered from the 1947 [[Roswell Crash]].
