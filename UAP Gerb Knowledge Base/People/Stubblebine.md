Major General Albert N. Stubblebine III (1930–2017) was a United States Army officer who served as the Commanding General of the [[Army INSCOM|US Army Intelligence and Security Command]] (INSCOM). Stubblebine is a notable figure in both intelligence history and UAP lore for his keen interest in utilizing unconventional phenomena — including remote viewing and other psychic ("psi") phenomena — for military intelligence applications.

## UAP Connections

Stubblebine has been accused by multiple individuals, including [[Philip J. Corso]] and [[Steven Greer]], of participating directly in UFO legacy program operations at Fort Huachuca, Arizona. His role is significant in the context of the 1997 [[Peru UFO Crash Incident]] because INSCOM originally housed the Intelligence Support Activity (ISA, also known as "Task Force Orange") — the most secretive unit within [[JSOC (Joint Special Operations Command)|JSOC]]. The ISA is cited by researchers as potentially involved in crash retrieval intelligence operations.

Stubblebine has also been referenced in connection with a possible program called "TREATS," described as resembling a Men in Black-style unit.
