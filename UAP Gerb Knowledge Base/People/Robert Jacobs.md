Robert "Bob" Jacobs is a former US Air Force Lieutenant who testified about UFO activity near nuclear test sites and specifically witnessed UAP interference with dummy ICBM flights. Jacobs is best known for his eyewitness account of the "Big Sur UFO" incident from 1964, in which he filmed a disc-shaped craft apparently interacting with a test warhead during a missile launch from Vandenberg Air Force Base.

## Warhead Shootdown Footage

Jacobs described filming a saucer-shaped craft with a distinctive upper protrusion — characterized as resembling "half of a ping-pong ball" — that appeared to maneuver around and seemingly disable a test warhead in flight. The craft's physical description closely matches both the disc-shaped object depicted in the [[Flyby Footage]] and the famous [[McMinnville Photos]], particularly the prominent dome or protrusion on the upper surface of the disc. This consistency across independent accounts and photographic evidence has been cited as lending credibility to the general characteristics of classic "flying saucer" morphology.

## Testimony on UAP Interest in Nuclear Systems

Jacobs's testimony forms part of a broader pattern of reported UAP interest in nuclear weapons systems, with his account documenting apparent UFO interference with ICBM test flights. His role as the camera operator who directly filmed the incident places him among the most significant military UAP witnesses, particularly regarding the intersection of UAP activity and nuclear defense systems.
