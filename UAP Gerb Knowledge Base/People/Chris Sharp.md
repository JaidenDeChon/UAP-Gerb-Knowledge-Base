Chris Sharp is an investigative journalist writing for Liberation Times, an outlet focused on UAP-related reporting. Sharp authored an article titled "UFO allegedly stored at East Coast Naval Facility as material transfer claims resurface," in which sources described a vehicle or vehicles of unknown origin as having been, or currently being, stored at [[Naval Air Station Patuxent River|Naval Air Station Patuxent River (Pax River)]].

## Role in UAP Research

UAP Gerb cites Sharp's reporting as independent corroboration of Pax River's significance within the alleged UFO legacy program apparatus, noting that Representative [[Eric Burlison]] separately conducted a 2025 site visit to Pax River — accompanied by White House staff — specifically to look for hangar facilities that could have stored recovered vehicles, including infrastructure reportedly considered for a transfer from Lockheed Martin to Bigelow Aerospace.
