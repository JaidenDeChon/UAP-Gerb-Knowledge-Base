Tim Burchett (born 25 August 1964) is a Republican United States Representative for Tennessee's 2nd congressional district, in office since January 2019, and one of the most outspoken members of Congress on UAP. He was one of the House Oversight Committee members who pushed for the July 2023 hearing at which [[David Grusch]] testified, and in August 2023 he launched the bipartisan [[UAP Caucus]]. In Gerb's videos he appears four times: as a member of the caucus whose work drew Gerb's attention to a Moon Dust case, as one of the Oversight members an Edwards Air Force Base witness hoped would hear him, as the congressman who entered the Kona Blue documents into the record of the November 2024 UAP hearing, and as the source of claims about fast, enormous craft deep under the ocean.

## Career

Burchett served in the Tennessee House of Representatives from 1995 to 1999 and in the Tennessee Senate from 1999 to 2010. He was then Mayor of Knox County for two terms, from 2010 to 2018, before winning election to the U.S. House.

## UAP Oversight

After Grusch's claims became public in June 2023, Burchett and Representative [[Anna Paulina Luna]], both Republicans on the House Oversight Committee, pushed for a hearing on them. It was held on 26 July 2023 by an Oversight subcommittee, with Grusch, [[David Fravor]] and [[Ryan Graves]] testifying. In August 2023 Burchett launched the UAP Caucus and led five colleagues, including Luna, [[Eric Burlison]] and [[Representative Moskowitz|Jared Moskowitz]], in a letter to the Inspector General of the Intelligence Community asking for follow-up on Grusch's testimony. Gerb names Burchett, Luna, Burlison and Moskowitz as members of Congress behind the caucus, and says its work brought the [[1978 Bolivia UFO Crash]], his favourite [[Project Moon Dust]] case, to his attention.

A 2024 intelligence briefing module in [[Steven Greer]]'s archive describes a retired Air Force lieutenant colonel, known in the vault as [[Ed (Witness 11063)]], who managed pilots at Edwards Air Force Base and says he trained those who flew man-made UFOs. Greer had introduced him to the Senate intelligence and armed services committees, and he was eager to testify openly before the House Oversight Committee, though two key members, Luna and Burchett, had not yet replied.

## Kona Blue Documents

In his Lockheed Martin video, Gerb says that UK journalist [[Christopher Sharp]]'s reporting of two attempts to move crash-retrieval material out of [[Lockheed Martin]] to [[AAWSAP]] "became a reality" in the packet Burchett entered into the record of the November 2024 House Oversight subcommittee hearing on UAP. The second attempt was to run through [[Kona Blue]], a prospective special access program at the Department of Homeland Security. Gerb stresses that the packet's author or compiler is unknown, but still finds the documents worth citing.

## Underwater Craft

In his MITRE video, Gerb suggests that Burchett "was right on the money, but with the wrong terminology" when he talked about deep underwater USO bases. Gerb places the enormous USO nicknamed the [[Tic Tac Factory]] in the deep water of the Blake Bahama Basin, just east of the Bahamas. He plays Burchett describing sonar contacts of craft "as big as a football field" moving underwater at "something over 200 miles an hour", against the "upper 30 mph" that Burchett says is the best the United States can do. Burchett has said elsewhere that an admiral, whom he has not named, told him of the contact. See [[Unidentified Submerged Object (USO)]].
