Tim Burchett is a United States Representative from Tennessee's 2nd congressional district and a prominent member of the bipartisan [[UAP Caucus]], a legislative group focused on UAP transparency and government disclosure. He has been one of the most vocal advocates of UAP transparency in Congress, and frequently appears in the media to discuss the need for government disclosure and accountability over alleged UFO crash retrieval programs.

## UAP Caucus Leadership

As a founding member of the [[UAP Caucus]] alongside Representatives [[Anna Paulina Luna]], [[Eric Burlison]], and [[Jared Moskowitz]], Burchett has worked to:

- Get Congress to investigate historical UFO crash retrieval events, including the [[1978 Bolivia UFO Crash]]
- Support legislative efforts for UAP declassification through provisions in the National Defense Authorization Act (NDAA)
- Coordinate Congressional hearings featuring whistleblower testimony from figures such as [[David Grusch]]
- Advocate for the protection of UAP whistleblowers who come forward with classified information

## Public Advocacy

Many members of Congress avoid the topic, but Burchett has been unusually outspoken about UAP issues. He has:

- Stated publicly that he believes the government possesses recovered non-human technology
- Called for transparency and declassification of historical UAP programs
- Criticized what he characterizes as government obstruction and stonewalling on UAP disclosure
- Drawn attention to the constitutional implications of unacknowledged programs operating beyond Congressional oversight

## Legislative Actions

Burchett has been instrumental in:

- Submitting UAP-related documents into the Congressional record
- Pushing for amendments to defense authorization bills that would require UAP program transparency
- Coordinating with other UAP Caucus members to keep up bipartisan pressure for disclosure
- Putting Congressional investigators in touch with UAP whistleblowers

## Significance

Burchett's willingness to advocate publicly for UAP transparency is a shift in how Congress engages with the topic. His leadership in the UAP Caucus has helped make serious Congressional discussion of UFO crash retrieval programs and government secrecy normal, and has moved the subject from fringe speculation into mainstream legislative oversight.
