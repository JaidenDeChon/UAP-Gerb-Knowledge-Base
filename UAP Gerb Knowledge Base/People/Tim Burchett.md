Tim Burchett serves as a United States Representative from Tennessee's 2nd congressional district and is a prominent member of the bipartisan [[UAP Caucus]], a legislative group focused on UAP transparency and government disclosure. Burchett has been one of the most vocal Congressional advocates for UAP transparency, frequently appearing in media to discuss the need for government disclosure and accountability regarding alleged UFO crash retrieval programs.

## UAP Caucus Leadership

As a founding member of the [[UAP Caucus]] alongside Representatives [[Anna Paulina Luna]], [[Eric Burlison]], and [[Jared Moskowitz]], Burchett has worked to:

- Bring historical UFO crash retrieval events (including the [[1978 Bolivia UFO Crash]]) to Congressional investigation
- Support legislative efforts for UAP declassification through National Defense Authorization Act (NDAA) provisions
- Coordinate Congressional hearings featuring whistleblower testimony from figures such as [[David Grusch]]
- Advocate for protection of UAP whistleblowers coming forward with classified information

## Public Advocacy

Unlike many Congressional representatives who avoid the topic, Burchett has been unusually outspoken about UAP issues:

- Publicly stating his belief that the government possesses recovered non-human technology
- Calling for transparency and declassification of historical UAP programs
- Criticizing what he characterizes as government obstruction and stonewalling on UAP disclosure
- Highlighting the constitutional implications of unacknowledged programs operating beyond Congressional oversight

## Legislative Actions

Burchett has been instrumental in:

- Submitting UAP-related documents into the Congressional record
- Pushing for amendments to defense authorization bills requiring UAP program transparency
- Coordinating with other UAP Caucus members to maintain bipartisan pressure for disclosure
- Facilitating connections between Congressional investigators and UAP whistleblowers

## Significance

Representative Burchett's willingness to publicly advocate for UAP transparency represents a shift in Congressional engagement with the topic. His leadership in the UAP Caucus has helped normalize serious Congressional discussion of UFO crash retrieval programs and government secrecy, moving the conversation from fringe speculation to mainstream legislative oversight.
