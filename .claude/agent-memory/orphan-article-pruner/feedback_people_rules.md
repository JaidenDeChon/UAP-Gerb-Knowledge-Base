---
name: People Article Deletion Rules
description: Specific rules for evaluating People folder articles in the UAP KB
type: feedback
---

**Always delete:**
- Fictional characters (Pete Mitchell/Top Gun, Allan/film, Moulder/X-Files)
- Source-link-only entries (no prose body)
- Single first names with no anchoring fact (generic "mentioned in comparison," "referenced in context")
- Anyone described purely as "the witness," "the subject," "the speaker" with no further identity anchors
- Sports figures/entertainers mentioned only as pop culture references (Diego Lopez/UFC, Sully Erna/Godsmack)
- Content creators mentioned only by first name with no last name and no specific UAP role

**Keep even with only a single sentence if:**
- The person is associated with a specific named event (Kecksburg, Aztec, Roswell, Operation Laser Strike, etc.)
- They have a specific dated role (e.g., "testified to AARO prior to June 2023")
- They have a specific institutional position or rank with named unit/base
- They performed a specific documented action (authored paper, confiscated flight logs, signed NDA, etc.)
- They are identified by initials only but have a specific fact (e.g., "RA, witnessed at Off-World Technologies Division")

**Why:** Single-name entries with no anchoring are unverifiable and untraceable. The KB exists to support research into specific verified incidents, not to document anonymous participants.
